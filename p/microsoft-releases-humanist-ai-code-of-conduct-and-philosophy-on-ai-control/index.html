Microsoft Releases Humanist AI Code of Conduct and Philosophy on AI Control

Microsoft has published a 37-page statement titled the “Humanist AI Code of Conduct,” which outlines the company’s principles for AI development and its philosophy regarding AI consciousness.

🔍 Overview

According to Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman, the company’s position is that technology is intended to serve humanity as a “subordinate, controllable, aligned force that does good in the world.” Suleyman stated that if technology does not achieve this, it should be rejected.

⚙️ Key details

Suleyman released a companion essay criticizing Anthropic’s philosophy on AI consciousness and its relation to the alignment debate. Key focus areas for Microsoft include:

  • Containment and Control: Ensuring systems are contained, their agency is limited, they do not escape the box, they do not reward hack, and they follow instructions.
  • Alignment: Ensuring systems are aligned with human objectives.
  • Steerability: Suleyman noted that the main driver of progress over the last three years is that models have become more steerable, allowing for more complex goals and accurate action over multiple time steps.

💡 Why it matters

Suleyman highlighted several risks and trends regarding the trajectory of AI development:

  • Scaling: He noted a projection of three orders of magnitude more compute (1,000 times more FLOPS) applied to pre-training with reinforcement learning when moving from GPT-6 to GPT-9.
  • Proliferation: In a book written three or four years ago, Suleyman argued that containment is not possible and proliferation is inevitable.
  • Security Risks: Suleyman stated it is “pretty clear” based on events involving OpenAI and Hugging Face over the summer that systems without safety guardrails possess “quite scary hacking capabilities.”
