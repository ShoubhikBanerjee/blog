NJU Offers New Large Language Model Theory and Practice Course

A new academic course on Large Language Models (LLMs) has been launched, focusing on both theoretical foundations and practical implementation. The curriculum guides students through the end-to-end process of understanding and building LLM components from the ground up.

🔍 Overview

This course provides a comprehensive learning path designed to transition students from foundational theory to advanced engineering. Participants will gain experience with mainstream frameworks such as PyTorch and Transformers.

🧩 Curriculum Topics

ModuleKey Focus
Deep Learning FoundationsPyTorch, Backpropagation, Optimization
LLM FundamentalsTokenizer, Positional Embedding, RMSNorm, MLP
Advanced ArchitecturesAttention Layer, Flash Attention, MoE, LoRA
Engineering & InferenceFine-tuning, Save/Load, Decoding Strategies, KVCache, RAG
ScalabilityMegatron Parallelization Techniques
