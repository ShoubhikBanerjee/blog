Nvidia Q3 Revenue Doubles to $115.8B as CEO Huang Resigns Amid Scandal

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, more than double the prior year, while its stock plunged nearly 40 percent from an October high following the resignation of cofounder and CEO Jensen Huang amid allegations of extramarital affairs and a Chinese espionage plot.

📊 Q3 Financial Results

  • Revenue more than doubled year over year to $115.8 billion
  • Data center revenue increased 110 percent
  • Results exceeded Wall Street expectations
  • Nearly $20 billion in revenue came from the Vera Rubin platform

🖥️ Vera Rubin Platform

  • Shipped during the quarter
  • Described as the “fastest product ramp in company history” by Kress
  • Generated nearly $20 billion in revenue

📉 Stock Price Volatility

  • All-time high reached on October 6
  • October 29: closed at $213.66, down 0.8 percent
  • October 30: closed with a 4 percent drop as Nasdaq fell 2.9 percent
  • November 4: continued decline amid midterm election concerns
  • November 5: fell 9.2 percent, largest single-day drop in at least a year
  • November 6: rose 2.8 percent to $186.64
  • November 9: tumbled 7 percent at open, volume surged past three-month average
  • November 10: closed at $168.89, nearly 10 percent below last trading day of 2025
  • November 11: rebounded slightly then plunged again on ouster reports
  • November 12: fell nearly 14 percent at open, closed with 4.3 percent dip, intraday low $148.80 (lowest in a year)
  • By Thursday (November 12) hovering around $150, wiping out post-earnings pop from Tuesday night

🧑‍💼 Leadership Crisis

  • October 29: The New York Times reported U.S. intelligence believed Huang was target of Chinese espionage plot
  • Plot centered on “close relationship” with Alice Huang, Chinese national and executive at Megaspeed (Singapore data center company, Nvidia client)
  • November 8: Huang sent company-wide memo apologizing for extramarital affair
  • Memo coincided with independent board investigation into alleged misconduct and potential compromise of company secrets or national security
  • November 11: media reports indicated board considering ousting Huang; second alleged affair with Nvidia employee emerged
  • November 12: Nvidia announced Huang’s departure
  • Harvey Jones appointed chairman of the board; stated company “does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO” and retained executive search firm for replacement
  • Huang cofounded Nvidia in 1993 and led it without a succession plan

🏛️ Congressional Hearings

  • December 1: Huang scheduled for transcribed interviews before House Foreign Affairs Committee, Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party, and House Intelligence Committee
  • December 2: Nvidia general counsel scheduled for same committees

🤖 Anthropic Debut

  • Anthropic, a major Nvidia customer, made Nasdaq debut at $111.50 per share — 34 percent higher than expected
