Trump Signs Executive Order Replacing AI Terminology with Super Intelligence

President Donald Trump has signed an executive order directing the US executive branch to stop acknowledging the existence of “artificial intelligence” and instead use the term “Super Intelligence.”

⚙️ Key details

  • Scope of Change: Official policy websites, press releases, and policy documents will now refer only to “Super Intelligence.”
  • Usage Requirements: US officials are expected to not acknowledge the use of “AI” or “artificial intelligence” in relevant settings.
  • Regulatory Status: Executive agencies are not required to change past documents or regulations.
  • Terminology: While “superintelligence” is an industry term for powerful versions of AI, the administration is using it as a replacement for the broader statutory definition of AI.

💡 Why it matters

President Trump stated the rebrand aims to quell fears regarding safety risks and the speed of AI advancement. Regarding the terminology, he noted:

  • “The word super is the best word of all, and it’s the simplest”
  • “We don’t want to hear artificial. Because it’s not artificial. It’s very powerful, it’s very brilliant.”
  • He compared the word “artificial” to “fake news.”

🔍 Overview

The order follows a White House luncheon with government officials and tech CEOs, including Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk. During these discussions, the following points were addressed:

  • Regulation: Trump mentioned a belief in “tremendous self-regulation” and noted existing regulation through the FBI and Department of Justice.
  • Infrastructure: To address local hurdles in building massive data centers, Trump stated firms will “work to make the community happy” via economic support and resources, adding that “data centers are going to be very popular.”
  • Usage: Trump stated the technology “is going to mostly be put to good use and we’ll stop the bad use.”
