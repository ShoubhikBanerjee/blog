Thariq Shihipar Discusses AI Coding Agents and Moves to OpenAI

At AI Engineer CODE, Thariq Shihipar presented an existing development regarding the coexistence of high-volume software production and careful craft, and subsequently announced his move to OpenAI after the episode was recorded.

🎙️ Overview

The discussion centered on how AI can make software cheap enough to produce by the thousand while still giving developers more time to make one thing feel right. Thariq demonstrated how these two futures can coexist through specific examples involving coding agents and decision models.

🧩 How it works

Thariq illustrated these concepts through several practical applications:

  • Fighting Game Development: He edited the size of a rock in the /human folder and immediately tested the throw mechanism.
  • Mac Dock Project: He built a Mac Dock that switched modes, used it, and realized he cared more about noticing when he got distracted than the switching itself.
  • Decision Models: He explained how a decision model can choose from actions a designer defines, raising the question of who gets to decide which experiments deserve the compute.
