OpenAI Claims Exact Value of Matrix Multiplication Exponent ω = 9/4 with Matching Lower Bound

📚 Mathematical Milestones in AI-Assisted Research

During a single dinner conversation, Scott Aaronson was digestively processing two proofs of VP ≠ VNP when OpenAI announced a breakthrough in computational complexity. Before dessert, an OpenAI paper claimed that the square matrix multiplication exponent ω is exactly 9/4. During dessert, another paper claimed matching upper and lower bounds for the finite Fourier transform.

⚙️ Key Details

The lower bound matching the October upper bound leaves only one possible value: ω = 9/4. Previously, no nontrivial lower bound was known. The result reportedly comes through an explicit superlinear circuit lower bound. Expert Omri Weinstein called it ’truly surreal’ if correct, since most experts expected ω = 2.

  • Matrix multiplication exponent: ω = exactly 9/4
  • VP ≠ VNP proofs: Two distinct proofs emerged in October and November
  • Finite Fourier transform: Matching upper and lower bounds claimed
  • Lower bound significance: Described as ’the biggest news'
  • Mechanism: Explicit superlinear circuit lower bound
