Meta to Launch Muse Charm Handheld AI Device in December

Meta is expanding its AI offerings with the Muse Charm, a handheld gadget housing the Muse AI agent. The device is scheduled to launch later this year.

⚙️ Key details

Hardware Specifications

  • Screen: Two-inch OLED touchscreen
  • Connectivity: Built-in 5G modem for interaction with Muse AI without Wi-Fi
  • Inputs/Outputs: Fingerprint sensor, microphones, speakers, and a USB-C charging port
  • Imaging: Cameras for taking photos, videos, and providing visual information of the surrounding environment to Muse

System and Software

  • Operates on a new operating system
  • Setup is managed through Meta’s Muse app on Android or iOS
  • Supports music and video playback

🧩 How it works

  • AI Interaction: Users can use the fingerprint sensor to start interacting with the Muse AI agent.
  • Connectivity: The 5G modem allows for AI interaction independent of Wi-Fi connections.
  • Inter-device Communication: The Muse Charm will be able to recognize and interact with other nearby Charms.

🚀 Availability

  • Release Date: Zuckerberg says Meta plans to launch the device “in time for the holidays in December.”
  • Pricing: While no official pricing has been confirmed, sources tell Bloomberg the device will likely cost as much as a smartwatch. Mobile carrier fees for 5G access have not yet been determined.
