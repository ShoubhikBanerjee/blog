New Technical Project Manager Role Opens in Oxfordshire

A new permanent, full-time Technical Project Manager position has been posted in Oxfordshire, offering a hybrid working model and a salary of 65,000. Posted on 10/09/2026, the role involves leading project delivery from design and development through to testing, deployment, and customer handover.

🔍 Overview

This role combines technical expertise, customer engagement, and project leadership. The position requires working within Agile delivery frameworks to ensure solutions are delivered efficiently, meet customer requirements, and provide measurable business value. The manager will work closely with customers, technology partners, and internal teams to deliver integrated solutions spanning:

  • Software
  • Infrastructure
  • Sensor technologies
  • Security systems
  • Advanced operational platforms

⚙️ Key details

  • Salary: 65,000
  • Location: Oxfordshire (Hybrid Working)
  • Employment Type: Permanent, Full time
  • Date Posted: 10/09/2026
  • Key Tools: Agile project management tools such as Azure DevOps, Jira, or similar platforms

🧩 How it works

The Technical Project Manager is responsible for overseeing the full lifecycle of solution delivery. Key duties include:

  • Leading the end-to-end delivery of complex technical solutions to ensure they are completed on time, within budget, and to agreed quality standards.
  • Acting as the primary customer contact throughout the project lifecycle to manage stakeholder expectations.
  • Providing technical leadership across solution design, systems integration, software development, infrastructure deployment, and testing activities.
  • Leading and motivating cross-functional Agile project teams, facilitating delivery ceremonies, and promoting collaboration.
  • Developing and managing project plans, product backlogs, release schedules, resources, and dependencies.
  • Identifying, managing, and mitigating project risks, issues, and changes.
  • Overseeing quality assurance, governance, reporting, and project documentation.

🚀 Availability

Candidates can search and apply for this and other verified opportunities in Oxfordshire on the IT Job Board by location, salary band, industry sector, and contract type. Requirements for this role include:

  • A degree-level qualification in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related discipline (or equivalent experience).
  • Demonstrable experience delivering complex technical projects using Agile methodologies, with AgilePM (DSDM) or similar frameworks being advantageous.
  • A strong technical background covering software development, systems engineering, infrastructure, or technology solution delivery.
  • Proven ability to lead multidisciplinary teams and manage stakeholders at all organisational levels.
  • Solid understanding of project governance, risk management, quality assurance, and change control practices.
