Campbell’s Details New Agentic AI Engineer Role to Build Autonomous AI Systems

Campbell’s, the company behind brands such as the iconic Campbell’s, Pepperidge Farm, and Rao’s Homemade, has outlined the details and requirements for an Agentic AI Engineer position. The role is focused on designing, building, and deploying autonomous AI systems that can reason, plan, use tools, and execute multi-step workflows with minimal human intervention.

🔍 Overview

Unlike traditional AI/ML engineers who focus on model training and prediction, an Agentic AI Engineer orchestrates goal-driven workflows. These workflows integrate models, tools, memory, and business logic to dynamically achieve objectives.

⚙️ Key Responsibilities

The role involves designing, developing, and deploying generative AI and agentic systems. Below are the key responsibility areas for the position:

Responsibility AreaDescription
Design & Develop Agentic SystemsBuild intelligent agents capable of autonomous planning, reasoning, and task execution, often using LLMs (e.g., GPT-class, LLaMA), multi-modal models, and autonomous workflows.
Orchestration & FrameworksImplement agent orchestration using frameworks like LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel, or custom solutions.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)Design and optimize RAG pipelines for enhanced reasoning with external knowledge, including document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector stores, and retrieval ranking.
Tool & Memory IntegrationDevelop agents that call APIs, databases, and other tools, maintain memory, and adapt based on outcomes.
Evaluation & MonitoringCreate evaluation frameworks for accuracy, grounding, latency, and cost; build observability for agent behavior and failure modes.
Model AdaptationFine-tune or adapt foundation models (e.g., via LoRA, adapters) for domain-specific use cases.
Production DeploymentDeploy GenAI/agentic systems in cloud-native environments with CI/CD, versioning, and runtime safeguards.
Cross-Functional CollaborationWork with data scientists, ML engineers, product teams, and governance/compliance stakeholders.

📋 Candidate Requirements

Candidates applying for the position must meet the following criteria:

  • 2+ years of experience in AI/ML system design, deployment, or autonomous agent development.
  • Proficiency in Python (and sometimes Java, C#) for AI/ML solution development.
  • Experience with agent orchestration frameworks and multi-agent communication protocols.
  • Hands-on experience with RAG architectures, evaluation methodologies, and LLM integration.
  • Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., Azure, AWS) and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Understanding of responsible AI, security, and compliance in regulated domains such as retail.

💼 Company Benefits

Campbell’s offers a comprehensive support package for employees starting on day one:

  • Medical, dental, short and long-term disability, AD&D, and life insurance for individuals, families, and domestic partners beginning on day one.
  • Immediate vesting and eligibility to enroll in a matching 401(k) plan on the first day of employment.
  • Unlimited sick time alongside paid time off and holiday pay.
  • Free access to the fitness center for employees located at World Headquarters (WHQ).
  • On-site day care operated by Bright Horizons and access to a company store.
  • Donation and volunteer matching up to $1,500 annually through the “Campbell’s Cares” program.
  • Support from a variety of Employee Resource Groups (ERGs).
