BioDuro appoints Greg Makara to lead AI‑enabled discovery chemistry

BioDuro, a global integrated contract research, development and manufacturing organization (CRDMO), announced on October 1 2026 that it has appointed Greg Makara, Ph.D., as Senior Vice President Lead Discovery Chemistry to strengthen early drug discovery capabilities.

🔎 Overview

  • The appointment supports BioDuro’s continued efforts to build a more integrated, data‑driven drug discovery platform.
  • It is part of BioDuro’s continued efforts to strengthen its early‑phase drug discovery and medicinal chemistry capabilities and integrate modern computational approaches more deeply across its discovery platform.

📅 Announcement Details

  • Location: Irvine, Calif.
  • Date: October 1 2026
  • Quote from BioDuro CEO Dr. Armin Spura: “This appointment builds on BioDuro’s broader efforts to incorporate AI and advanced computational tools into our drug discovery services.”

👨‍🔬 Leadership Background

Dr. Makara brings more than two decades of experience in drug discovery and chemistry leadership across pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and technology companies. His career highlights include:

PositionOrganization
Founder & CEOChemPass
CEO & CTO (co‑founded chemistry service business)ComInnex, Inc.
Site HeadAMRI Hungary
Leader, Target Validation Chemistry groupMerck & Co., Rahway
Director (participated in ASMS screening technology ALIS)Neogenesis Pharmaceuticals

He has experience spanning library development, hit‑to‑lead and lead optimization, computer‑aided drug discovery and AI‑assisted drug discovery.

🤖 AI & Computational Chemistry Focus

In his new role, Dr. Makara will:

  • Lead the expansion of BioDuro’s computational chemistry, AI, and lead discovery capabilities.
  • Work across the discovery organization to support lead discovery projects and expand computational approaches, including:
    • Molecular modeling
    • Structure‑based drug design
    • Fragment‑based discovery
    • Virtual screening
    • Molecular design
    • Compound prioritization
  • Complement BioDuro’s experimental chemistry, biology, and DMPK expertise to help clients accelerate hit identification, lead generation, and optimization while making more informed decisions throughout early drug discovery.

Quote from Dr. Greg Makara: “BioDuro has built a strong foundation in chemistry, biology, DMPK, and integrated drug discovery, and I am excited to help further connect these capabilities with computational and medicinal chemistry. The combination of computational and experimental approaches can create a more efficient and informed discovery process. I look forward to working with BioDuro’s scientists and clients to apply these approaches to challenging drug discovery programs and accelerate the path from hits to optimized leads.”

🌐 Strategic Impact

  • The hire strengthens BioDuro’s integrated discovery platform with more powerful, data‑driven approaches.
  • Clients gain access to combined computational and experimental methods designed to speed early‑stage drug discovery.
