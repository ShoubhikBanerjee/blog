🚀 Model Introduction

LongCat-Video represents a breakthrough in AI video generation technology. This foundational video generation model with 13.6B parameters delivers exceptional performance across Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, and Video-Continuation generation tasks. The model particularly excels in efficient and high-quality long video generation, marking a significant step toward world models.

⭐ Key Features

  • 🌟 Unified architecture for multiple tasks: LongCat-Video unifies Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, and Video-Continuation tasks within a single video generation framework. It natively supports all these tasks with a single model and consistently delivers strong performance across each individual task.

  • 🌟 Long video generation: LongCat-Video is natively pretrained on Video-Continuation tasks, enabling it to produce minutes-long videos without color drifting or quality degradation.

  • 🌟 Efficient inference: LongCat-Video generates 720p, 30fps videos within minutes by employing a coarse-to-fine generation strategy along both the temporal and spatial axes. Block Sparse Attention further enhances efficiency, particularly at high resolutions.

  • 🌟 Strong performance with multi-reward RLHF: Powered by multi-reward Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), comprehensive evaluations on both internal and public benchmarks demonstrate that LongCat-Video achieves performance comparable to leading open-source video generation models as well as the latest commercial solutions.

For comprehensive details, please refer to the LongCat-Video Technical Report.

🏁 Conclusion

LongCat-Video represents a significant advancement in AI-powered video generation technology. With its unified architecture supporting multiple video generation tasks, efficient inference capabilities, and competitive performance metrics, it offers developers and researchers an accessible, open-source solution for high-quality video generation. The model’s ability to generate long-form videos without quality degradation, combined with its 13.6B parameter efficiency, positions it as a compelling alternative to proprietary solutions in the rapidly evolving field of AI video generation.

The comprehensive evaluation results demonstrate that LongCat-Video delivers performance comparable to leading commercial solutions while maintaining the transparency and accessibility benefits of open-source development. This makes it an invaluable resource for advancing research and practical applications in video generation technology.
