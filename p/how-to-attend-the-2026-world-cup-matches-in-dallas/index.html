How to Attend the 2026 World Cup Matches in Dallas

If you missed your chance to buy tickets for the World Cup’s Dallas matches, there are still a few ways to get in before the action kicks off at AT&T Stadium.

📅 Overview

  • The information reflects the situation as of Apr 20, 2026.
  • Event inquiries are handled Monday‑Friday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
  • Phone contact: (214) 741‑8328.

🎟️ Ticket & Reservation Details

  • Reservations must be confirmed at the time of purchase.
  • A valid photo ID is required for ticket pickup.
  • A call‑window will be located at the main entrance.
  • For groups of eight or more, a reservation is recommended.
  • Cancellations require a 24‑hour notice.

🚗 Parking & Transportation

  • Limited on‑site parking is available.
  • Street parking is offered on surrounding streets.
  • Nearby paid parking lots serve the downtown Dallas area.
  • Metered parking is within 1–2 blocks of the venue.
  • Public transportation or ride‑share services are recommended.

♿ Accessibility

  • ADA‑accessible seating is provided.
  • Wheelchair‑accessible restrooms are available.
  • Main‑floor accessibility and elevator access to upper levels (if applicable) are in place.
  • Contact the venue in advance for specific accessibility needs.

👥 Age, Dress & Policies

  • Age restriction: 21+ for some events; evening events and alcohol‑service areas also have age limits.
  • Dress code: Business casual is recommended.
  • No outside beverages are permitted.
  • Photography policy: Inquire with the venue.

👶 Family & Children

  • Children are permitted during daytime hours (before 5 PM).
  • Accompanied minors are welcome at family‑oriented events.
  • High chairs are available upon request.
  • Contact the venue for special children’s event arrangements.

📞 Contact & Additional Information

  • For event inquiries, call during business hours (Monday‑Friday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM).
  • Phone: (214) 741‑8328.
  • Verify reservation confirmation at purchase and bring a valid photo ID for pickup.
  • Ask about photography rules, dress expectations, and any specific accessibility or family needs ahead of time.
