Big Tech AI CapEx Hits $720B Annual Spend, $900B Future Compute

Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta announced they will collectively spend at least $720 billion on capital expenditures this year, adding to more than $1 trillion of spending commitments arranged for the coming years.

🔍 Overview

  • The AI‑focused build‑out is described as a “once in a lifetime opportunity” for Amazon to become a $1 trillion company and as a “significant” increase for Alphabet in 2027.
  • Analysts note that “Big Tech will not be slowing down, at least not on its own.”

💰 CapEx Commitments

  • Alphabet: $195 billion‑$205 billion this year; plans to increase spending “significantly” in 2027 (CFO Anat Ashkenazi).
  • Amazon: $220 billion this year; intends to spend “meaningfully more” in 2027 (CEO Andy Jassy).
  • Meta: $135 billion‑$145 billion this year; future‑year amount not disclosed, but financial institutions expect an increase; Mark Zuckerberg said compute efficiency is expected to rise “tenfold” next year, requiring “significant investment.”
  • Microsoft: Included in the $720 billion aggregate figure (specific amount not broken out).

📈 Performance Highlights

  • Amazon reported its fastest quarterly growth in five years.
  • Meta’s ad revenue is “surging.”
  • Google Cloud revenue more than doubled in a year.
  • Google Search ads revenue grew 12 % in Q3, down from 17 % growth in the same quarter last year.

📊 Market Reaction

  • Alphabet’s stock experienced its worst one‑day drop in history, sinking 13 % in a single day.
  • The broader “big AI buildout” spooked Wall Street, triggering a sharp sell‑off in tech stocks that later tapered off as investors focused on the midterm elections.
  • Some analysts see uncertainty around data‑center regulation as potentially beneficial, noting tech stocks rose again on Tuesday.

🏗️ Compute & Infrastructure

  • Anthropic: Q3 revenue $17 billion (up >1,000 % YoY); compute costs $17.5 billion; committed nearly $900 billion to future compute and infrastructure; will begin building its own data centers.
  • Part of the $900 billion commitment will go to Google, which is selling TPU chips to Anthropic.
  • Executives were pressed on the TPU business during Google’s earnings call.
  • Philipp Schindler (Alphabet SVP & CBO) said strong growth in AI Mode queries reduced monetization in Search but affirmed confidence in the AI trajectory and promising ad‑experiment results.

📅 Outlook

  • Both Amazon and Alphabet signal “significant” or “meaningfully more” CapEx in 2027, underscoring a multi‑year acceleration of AI infrastructure.
  • Meta aims for a tenfold boost in compute efficiency next year, implying large capital outlays.
  • The industry narrative stresses that the AI build‑out “will not end until someone stops it.”
