Apple Unveils iPhone Duo Foldable and iPhone 18 Pro Lineup

At the “Surprise and Shine” event in Cupertino on September 9, Apple officially unveiled a new generation of hardware, including its first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro series, Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and AirPods 5. AI serves as the common thread across this new ecosystem.

📱 iPhone Duo

The iPhone Duo is Apple’s first foldable iPhone, featuring a Grade 5 titanium build and a precision-engineered hinge with more than 100 components.

  • Displays: A 7.6-inch Super Retina XDR inner display (more than 50% larger than the iPhone 18 Pro Max) and a 5.4-inch external display.
  • Durability: IP68 rating and Ceramic Shield 2 on the outer display, which Apple claims has up to three times greater scratch resistance than the previous generation.
  • Performance: Powered by the A20 Pro chip, utilizing a dual-battery architecture and a vapor-chamber cooling system.
  • Display Tech: Supports ProMotion and Always-On capabilities with a peak outdoor brightness of 3,000 nits.
  • Expanded Utility: Support for Apple Pencil USB-C is coming later this year for sketching, note-taking, and document annotation.

📸 iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max

These models focus on AI, battery life, performance, and camera capabilities, powered by the A20 Pro chip and a new-generation vapor-chamber cooling system for high sustained performance.

  • Camera System: Features a 48MP Fusion main camera with variable aperture. The system can automatically optimize aperture for depth of field and lighting, or users can manually select from four levels.
  • Video and Audio: Supports 4K Dolby Vision HDR time-lapse and cinematic effects applied after recording at up to 60 fps. Audio Mix has been improved for voice processing.
  • Imaging: Additional Photographic Styles controls for image grain and texture.

⌚ Apple Watch Series 12

Powered by the S11 chip, the Series 12 introduces a Health Sensing System combining electrical and optical sensors to measure heart rate variability (HRV) and heart rate every five seconds.

  • AI Integration: watchOS 27 brings Siri AI to the device to combine personal context with broader knowledge. New Audio Intelligence capabilities will arrive later this year.
  • Materials: Available in aluminum, titanium, and a ceramic option (available in Night and Pearl White).
