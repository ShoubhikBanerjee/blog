🛠️ Getting Started with Hi3DGen

Setting up Hi3DGen is surprisingly straightforward, making it accessible even to those with minimal technical background:

  1. Access the Platform 🔑 Visit the official interface through Hugging Face or other supported platforms. The web-based interface eliminates the need for heavy software installation.

  2. Craft Your Prompt ✨ The magic begins with your description. Be specific and detailed about what you want to create. For example, instead of “a chair,” try “a mid-century modern armchair with walnut frame and cream upholstery.”

  3. Fine-tune Parameters 🎛️ Hi3DGen allows you to adjust various generation parameters:

    • Resolution quality (higher for more detail)
    • Generation steps (more steps = more refined results)
    • Style guidance (how closely to follow artistic references)

  4. Export & Use 📤 Once generated, your 3D models can be exported in standard formats compatible with major 3D software including Blender, Maya, and Unity. This seamless workflow integration makes Hi3DGen particularly valuable for professional pipelines.
