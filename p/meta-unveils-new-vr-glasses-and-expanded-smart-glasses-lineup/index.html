Meta Unveils New VR Glasses and Expanded Smart Glasses Lineup

During the annual Meta Connect showcase on Wednesday, Meta announced a slate of new wearables, including VR glasses, updated smart glasses, and a dedicated hardware device for its Muse AI agent.

👓 Meta VR Glasses

Meta is launching new VR hardware that packs virtual reality tech into a lightweight frame that sits on the ears like typical glasses rather than being strapped over the head.

  • Weight: 100g
  • Design: Uses a tethered computing puck in the pocket to distribute components.
  • Capabilities: Immersive displays for playing games, watching movies, and getting work done.
  • Content: Meta promises “hundreds” of movies in the US and “thousands of titles internationally” upon launch.

🕶️ Smart Glasses Lineup

Meta announced new screen-free smart glasses and updates to existing lines.

Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3

  • Styles: Includes a new aviator style with transition lenses and a geometric cat-eye frame called Zena.
  • Hardware: Features six microphones for clearer calls (up from five in the second generation).
  • Audio: All sound is captured in Dolby Atmos for spatial audio.
  • Battery: Nine hours of battery life.
  • Options: 27 color and lens combinations, plus three seasonal options.
  • Price: Starts at $449.

Ray-Ban Meta Audio Glasses

  • Features: Infused with Meta AI but contains no camera for recording others.
  • Weight: Audio-only models start at 43 grams.
  • Styles: Available in Clubmaster and Burbank styles.
  • Options: 23 color and lens combinations; supports the same wider prescription range as the Ray-Ban Meta Optics lineup.
  • Battery: Estimated 12 hours on a single charge.
  • Price: Starting price of $349.
  • Capabilities: Interacting with Meta AI, taking phone calls, and listening to podcasts or music.

Meta Glasses (Entry-Level)

  • New Styles: Capri and Nova.
  • Special Editions:
    • Kylie Jenner pair (ivory-colored): $399
    • Lisa (Blackpink) pair: Pink lenses and a blind-box-type charm styled after Lisa’s pets; starts at $399.
  • Standard Version: Non-celebrity versions start at $249.

🧩 AI and Accessibility Features

Meta is integrating the Muse AI agent into its smart glasses, allowing users to activate it by name for tasks such as logging meals, guiding workouts, or shopping assistance. Other updates include:

  • Hearing Enhancement: FDA-cleared software for adults with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss, available as a $149 add-on or via Meta One subscription.
  • Audio Customization: Users can shift volume between left and right ears, including sending all audio to one side.
  • Meta AI: Ability to provide “more context-rich answers” regarding what users are looking at.

⚙️ Muse Charm

Meta is building a dedicated hardware device for the Muse AI agent called Muse Charm.

  • Appearance: A large screen with a lanyard, resembling a chunky smart watch without the strap.
  • Interface: A fingerprint sensor on the top right corner to activate the agent.
  • Hardware: Includes a small camera and at least three microphones on the front.
