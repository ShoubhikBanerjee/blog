King Charles Issues Formal Clarification on Private Status of Duke and Duchess of Sussex

King Charles has issued a formal letter clarifying that the Duke and Duchess of Sussex will not carry out official engagements or act as working royals following their return to the UK. The message, sent by the Lord Chamberlain, confirms that Prince Harry and Meghan remain private citizens and are no longer permitted to use the titles of “His and Her Royal Highness.”

🔍 Overview

  • The Duke and Duchess moved back to the UK from California last month with their children, Archie and Lilibet.
  • The King’s letter was distributed to government departments, military officials, Lord Lieutenants, and Prince Harry’s team.
  • The correspondence emphasizes that the 2020 Sandringham Agreement remains in effect, which prevents a “half-in and half-out” status where the couple could pursue commercial interests while representing the Sovereign.
  • Palace sources indicated the letter was made public to ensure transparency and avoid potential leaks.

⚙️ Key details

  • Official Status: The Duke and Duchess are no longer working members of the Royal Family and will not represent the Royal Family at engagements.
  • Charitable Work: Supporting their charities is considered a personal matter and is undertaken in their private capacity.
  • Financial Protocols: Questions regarding the reception of the Sussexes, especially those involving public funds, must be directed to Buckingham Palace.
  • Security: Operational security matters are handled by the Royal and VIP Executive Committee (Ravec) and relevant police authorities.
  • Upcoming Appearances: Prince Harry is expected to support the Invictus Games in Birmingham next year, and public appearances for other charities are likely this autumn.
