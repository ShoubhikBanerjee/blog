Onekey Launches AI Agent that Answers Firmware Security Queries with Verified Data

Onekey introduced the Onekey AI Agent, an AI‑driven assistant that returns security‑focused answers grounded in the platform’s own analysis results.

🔍 Overview

  • The AI is uniquely enriched with analysis results generated by the Onekey Platform.
  • Users can query security information and firmware analysis results in natural language and receive answers based on the platform’s actual findings.
  • Developed entirely by Onekey, the technology improves accuracy, transparency, and traceability of AI‑generated responses.

🧩 How it works

  • The AI operates directly on the platform’s analysis results, including:
    • Firmware extraction
    • Binary inspection
    • Software Bills of Materials (SBOMs)
    • Component analysis
    • Vulnerability intelligence
  • Natural‑language queries are answered with verifiable data from these analyses.
  • The platform also supports Bring Your Own Model (BYOM) and Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) approaches, letting organisations tailor AI deployments to specific business, industry, and regulatory requirements.

⚙️ Key details

  • Technical focus – “The goal in product cyber security isn’t to generate answers that merely sound convincing. What matters is technical accuracy and reliability,” said Jan Wendenburg, CEO of Onekey.
  • Integrated intelligence – “The Onekey AI Agent combines the power of modern AI with the deep analysis capabilities of our platform. Every response is grounded in verifiable security data.”
  • Contextual awareness – “The second key differentiator is contextual awareness,” Wendenburg added.
  • Beyond simple chatbots – “The market is currently focused on AI copilots, chatbots, and agentic AI. However, in cyber security, simply layering a language model on top of existing workflows is not enough,” Wendenburg explained.
  • Roadmap – The agent is the first milestone in a four‑stage AI roadmap that will evolve the platform into a comprehensive product security assistant, eventually supporting complete product security workflows.

🚀 Availability

  • After a beta programme, the initial release is scheduled for September and will continuously expand.

💡 Why it matters

  • Professional security environments demand responses that are transparent, verifiable, and based on concrete technical evidence.
  • “Our AI roadmap demonstrates how Decision Intelligence becomes reality step by step—as a trusted assistant for those who make security‑critical decisions every day,” said Wendenburg.
  • The goal is to help security teams move more quickly to transform highly complex firmware data into actionable insights and informed decisions.
