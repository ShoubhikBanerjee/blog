Enterprise AI Engineer Role Emphasizes Generative AI and Healthcare Solutions

The organization announced an opening for an AI/ML Engineer tasked with building and deploying enterprise‑scale AI solutions that serve both healthcare and business operations.

🔍 Overview

  • Position focuses on Generative AI, AI Copilots, Conversational AI, Intelligent Search, Summarization, Workflow Automation, and Predictive Analytics.
  • Solutions are embedded directly into clinical and business workflows and operate at production scale.

⚙️ Core Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and deploy AI‑powered applications using the AI Development Lifecycle (AIDLC) covering ideation, prototyping, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring.
  • Build capabilities with Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG), vector search, semantic retrieval, prompt engineering, and agentic AI workflows.
  • Create AI copilots, intelligent assistants, chatbots, recommendation engines, and workflow automation that integrate into existing enterprise applications.
  • Establish evaluation frameworks for LLM output quality, hallucination detection, and human‑in‑the‑loop review processes.
  • Develop APIs and scalable backend services in Python.
  • Implement MLOps, LLMOps, CI/CD, observability, and deployment automation practices.
  • Ensure compliance with Responsible AI, security, privacy, and governance standards.
  • Collaborate with cloud, platform, and modernization teams to integrate AI capabilities into cloud‑native, enterprise‑scale solutions.
  • Exercise independent judgment to prioritize, plan, and manage AI‑related engineering activities, dependencies, risks, and delivery commitments.
  • Communicate complex AI/ML concepts, solution options, technical trade‑offs, risks, and results to technical teams, business stakeholders, and senior leadership.
  • Evaluate emerging AI/ML technologies, frameworks, patterns, and industry trends to inform solution design and drive practical enterprise innovation.
  • Mentor junior engineers and contribute to AI engineering best practices.

🛠️ Technical Focus Areas

  • Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Vector search & semantic retrieval
  • Prompt engineering
  • Agentic AI workflows
  • AI copilots & conversational agents
  • Intelligent search & recommendation engines
  • Workflow automation solutions

📚 Required & Preferred Qualifications

  • 6–10 years of software engineering, AI/ML engineering, or data science experience.
  • Strong proficiency in Python.
  • Familiarity with emerging agent‑interoperability protocols such as Model Context Protocol (MCP) (preferred).
  • Experience building AI copilots, conversational AI, intelligent search, or workflow automation solutions (preferred).
  • Experience working with healthcare, clinical, consumer, or other regulated domain data (preferred).
  • Knowledge of Responsible AI, model evaluation, and AI governance frameworks (preferred).
