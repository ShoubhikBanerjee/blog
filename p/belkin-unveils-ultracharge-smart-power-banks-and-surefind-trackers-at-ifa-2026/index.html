Belkin Unveils UltraCharge Smart Power Banks and SureFind Trackers at IFA 2026

Belkin, a global leader in consumer electronics for over 40 years, announced a new collection of connected accessories, device protection and lifestyle solutions at IFA 2026.

🔍 Overview

  • The new portfolio includes the SureFind collection, the BoostCharge Slim Magnetic Ring Stand, UltraCharge Smart Display Power Banks and fresh colorways of Belkin’s popular BoostCharge Compact USB‑C Charger 30W.
  • The products will debut at IFA 2026, Exhibit Hall 3.2, booth #117.
  • Designed for professionals, frequent travelers and power users, the additions aim to deliver greater confidence, convenience, and flexibility throughout the day.

⚡ UltraCharge Smart Display Power Banks

  • Powered by reliable cylindrical lithium‑ion cells.
  • Both models feature a 70 cm retractable USB‑C cable that stores neatly when not in use, eliminating the need for extra cables.
  • Integrated loop cord makes the power banks easy to grab and carry; the slim design slips alongside a laptop in a backpack, briefcase or carry‑on.
  • Large digital smart display shows real‑time battery percentage, current wattage output, charging direction and temperature control status.
  • TSA carry‑on compliant, suitable for business trips, holidays and daily commuting.

📍 SureFind Tracking Collection

  • Every product in the SureFind family is certified for both Apple Find My and Google Find Hub, allowing location across the two largest mobile ecosystems.
  • SureFind Spot – designed for keys, backpacks, luggage and other personal belongings; provides an easy way to keep everyday essentials within reach. Price starts at $14.99 USD.
  • SureFind Card – built for wallets, passport holders and other slim carry items; ultra‑thin with powerful location tracking and an IP68 rating (dust‑tight and water‑submersible). Price starts at $26.99 USD.
  • SureFind Loop Cable Tag – combines reliable item‑finding technology with a built‑in USB‑C to USB‑C cable.
  • All SureFind items are available beginning September 2026 at belkin.com, Amazon, and select retailers worldwide.

💡 Why it matters

  • The integrated retractable cable and loop cord reduce cable clutter and simplify travel packing.
  • Real‑time smart display gives users visibility into charging status and temperature, increasing confidence in power usage.
  • TSA‑compliant designs enable hassle‑free travel for business and leisure.
  • Cross‑platform Find My and Find Hub certification lets users locate assets regardless of their smartphone ecosystem.
