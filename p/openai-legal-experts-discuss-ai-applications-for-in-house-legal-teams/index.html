OpenAI, Legal Experts Discuss AI Applications for In-House Legal Teams

OpenAI hosted a discussion featuring Stacey Wang and Craig Bruney, Mary O’Carroll of LECG, and Cooley’s Richard Segal on how AI can assist in-house legal teams in connecting business context, building reusable workflows, and leveraging outside counsel more effectively.

📚 Participants

  • Stacey Wang (OpenAI)
  • Craig Bruney (OpenAI)
  • Mary O’Carroll (LECG)
  • Richard Segal (Cooley)

🧩 AI Applications in Legal Practice

The discussion explored several areas where AI can enhance legal operations:

  • Connecting business context within legal workflows
  • Building reusable workflows for legal processes
  • Getting more value from outside counsel relationships

💼 Why It Matters

The conversation highlighted AI’s potential to streamline legal operations by:

  • Helping legal teams better align with business objectives
  • Creating scalable and efficient legal processes
  • Optimizing the use of external legal resources

The session emphasized practical applications of AI in in-house legal environments rather than theoretical capabilities.
