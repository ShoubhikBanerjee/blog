Zacks Rank System and Adobe Stock Analysis

The Zacks Rank serves as a timeliness indicator for stocks over the next 1 to 3 months. It is a complementary set of indicators to use alongside the Style Scores.

⚙️ How it works

The scores are based on the trading styles of Value, Growth, and Momentum. There is a VGM Score (‘V’ for Value, ‘G’ for Growth and ‘M’ for Momentum), which combines the weighted average of the individual style scores into one score. Within each Score, stocks are graded into five groups: A, B, C, D and F.

📊 Industry Analysis

The Zacks Industry Rank assigns a rating to each of the 265 X (Expanded) Industries based on their average Zacks Rank. The industry with the best average Zacks Rank would be considered the top industry (1 out of 265), which would place it in the top 1% of Zacks Ranked Industries. Conversely, the industry with the worst average Zacks Rank (265 out of 265) would place in the bottom 1%.

📈 Adobe Stock Performance

For their last quarter, Adobe (ADBE) reported earnings of $6.13 per share, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $6.08 per share. This reflects a positive earnings surprise of 0.82%.

In the earnings report for the quarter ending in August 2024, Adobe Inc. (ADBE) announced earnings of $4.65 per share versus the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $4.53 per share, representing a surprise of 2.65%.

Look out for ADBE’s next earnings release expected on December 09, 2026. For the next earning release, we expect the company to report earnings of $6.30 per share, reflecting a year-over-year increase of 14.55%.

📚 Research Tools

The Zacks Equity Research reports, or ZER for short, are our in-house, independently produced research reports. The ever popular one-page Snapshot reports are generated for virtually every single Zacks Ranked stock. The detailed multi-page Analyst report does an even deeper dive on the company’s vital statistics.

The report also visually displays the four components of the Zacks Rank (Agreement, Magnitude, Upside and Surprise).
