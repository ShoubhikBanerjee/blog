US District Judge dismisses sexual abuse lawsuit against Sam Altman

On Tuesday, US District Judge Zachary Bluestone dismissed a lawsuit filed last year by Ann Altman against her brother, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman. The lawsuit, which alleged that Sam Altman had sexually abused her throughout her childhood, was dismissed with prejudice, meaning the case cannot be refiled in that court.

⚙️ Key details

  • The dismissal occurred because Ann Altman failed to appear for an independent medical examination over the past year.
  • The plaintiff did not meet other court deadlines despite receiving warnings that failure to do so would result in the case being dismissed.
  • Judge Bluestone granted the motion to dismiss with prejudice but denied Sam Altman’s request for attorney fees and costs.
