Canva Adds AI Off Button and Library Content Controls

Canva has introduced a new toggle that allows users to turn off its generative AI features. This update responds to user requests for control over the platform’s expanding array of AI-powered tools.

🔍 Overview

The new feature provides a way to disable Canva’s generative AI capabilities, including its chatbot for image generation and specific editing tools. Users can also adjust their content library preferences to prioritize human-created designs over AI-generated ones.

🧩 How it works

To turn off generative AI features, users navigate to Profile > Settings > Permission and toggle off Enable generative AI features.

Separately, users can choose to see less AI-generated content by going to Profile > Your account > AI personalization and turning on the option to show less AI content. When this option is enabled, human-created content appears first when searching through Canva’s library for templates, graphics, and other design elements.

⚙️ Key details

  • Disabled Features: The toggle turns off many AI features, including:
    • Magic Write
    • Magic Design
    • Magic Layers
    • Magic Edit
    • Dream Lab
    • Canva Code
    • AI Shapes
    • Magic Expand (AI photo-extending)
    • Image generation via Canva chatbot
  • Enabled Features: Some AI-powered tools remain active because they “make everyday tasks faster without generating anything new.” These include:
    • Background remover
    • Magic Grab tool (lets you select an object in an image and move it somewhere else)
  • Content Library: Canva states that a “small portion” of its library consists of AI-generated content. Any design using these elements will have an AI label in its metadata.

💡 Why it matters

Since Canva’s significant push into AI in 2023, some users have expressed frustration with the influx of AI features and requested ways to disable them. Canva has continued to expand its AI-powered image-editing suite, including a prompt-powered editing update in April and the launch of its first in-house AI model, Canva Craft, last month. This new control allows users to manage their experience with these tools.
