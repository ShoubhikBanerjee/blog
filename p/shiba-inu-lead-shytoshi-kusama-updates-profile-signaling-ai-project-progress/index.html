Shiba Inu Lead Shytoshi Kusama Updates Profile, Signaling AI Project Progress

Shiba Inu lead ambassador Shytoshi Kusama has updated his X profile, marking changes to his project’s public status. The Shiba Inu community spotted the updates to his location and bio, which previously detailed final preparations for an AI application.

⚙️ Key details

The updates to Shytoshi Kusama’s X profile include the following changes:

  • Location: Changed to “close” from the previous “backlog, Marketing and positioning.”
  • Bio: Changed to “Polish.” from the previous ‘Pure focus: AI App/site: ROS Finishing touches: Beta website complete. Final bug pass’.
  • Social Media Activity: Kusama continues to maintain a relatively low profile on social media, with his last post or interaction on X occurring on May 13, 2026.

🧩 How it works

In a February 2026 livestream, Kusama unveiled an AI-powered relationship platform. The platform is designed to:

  • Help couples identify behavioral patterns.
  • Find friction points.
  • Determine long-term compatibility risks.

Kusama’s last X update before this transition suggested his project was in the final stages of a beta launch and undergoing a bug vetting process, though he did not specify which project was being finalized.

💡 Why it matters

These profile updates occurred alongside a recovery in the Shiba Inu (SHIB) market:

  • SHIB rose 1.14% over a 24-hour period, reversing a drop from the prior day as August jobs data came in far stronger than expected, with 162,000 jobs added.
  • Shiba Inu is up almost 6% weekly, broadly bouncing back since the start of September from its August 30-day low of $0.00000488.
