Claude and OpenAI Publish Proofs of VP ≠ VNP, GPT-6 Advances in Science and Code

An internal Claude research model reportedly provided a proof that VP ≠ VNP, which Anthropic shared in a GitHub repository. OpenAI subsequently posted its own proof, claiming it was ‘obtained on November 6, 2026’. The result does not imply P ≠ NP, which still requires general Boolean circuit lower bounds. Levent Alpöge noted that the ‘250,000+ pages’ claim was a joke.

OpenAI released a preprint showing GPT-6 models designed new protein shapes and sequences through reasoning and code, without specialized bio models. In one run, GPT-6 Cyber helped validate 948 Linux kernel bugs that could grant local root and generated patches for maintainer review. A historian reported that GPT-6.2 Astra identified the vicomte de Chabot as the artist of an anonymous 1808 Yale sketchbook in 22 minutes, and it also surfaced an obscure microfilmed diary whose handwriting and itinerary matched the sketches. Additionally, one 13.8-day signal was independently confirmed by a SETI astronomer, and 49 TESS candidates were flagged.

GPT-6.2 Astra on Codex scored 97% on RoboDojo-RC Tier 1 across 120 real-robot trials, with RoboDojo being retired. Einsia released RoboUniverse-10K, a set of 10,807 verifiable Isaac Sim tasks built from real manipulation videos, with a top frontier score of 85.8% and 7 of 11 models scoring ≤10%. Neon set a Day-1 usage record on Google’s paid APIs, and Antigravity tokens/day rose 82% since Argon launched six weeks ago. Neon is now available to Google Workspace customers through the Gemini app, and Perplexity has passed 120B Neon tokens, doubling daily volume in 3 days with 36B on Monday.

One estimate places Neon xhigh roughly equal to Opus Max at $6.44 vs $10.04 per Artificial Analysis task. Several users report heavy ‘glazing’ and overcorrection when Neon is critiqued. Users describe Opus 5.6 being A/B-tested in Claude Code and chat as ‘quite a jump’ over 5.5. The model is pure-RL trained on Kimi K3 and reportedly approaches frontier models on FrontierCode 2.0 at max reasoning, and is free on Devin paid tiers for two weeks.

GLM-5.4 creates five looped models in Raschka’s architecture gallery. The model runs its middle 24 of 32 layers twice and beats Qwen3.8-27B on GAIA (69.90 vs 66.99). It is about 4.9 GB at 4-bit and released under Apache 2.0. LoopCD contrasts loop pass 3 against pass 1 with no training, lifting AA-Omniscience from -9.0 to -3.4. Reported scores include #1 under 32B on DeepSWE (48.2%) and 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified. Unsloth fine-tuning of the model is up to 3x faster with 50% less VRAM.
