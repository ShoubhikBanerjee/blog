MindTopo Benchmark Evaluates Foundation Model Reasoning and Planning in Topological Space

MindTopo is a new benchmark designed to measure topological intuition across five properties grounded in cognitive science and formal topology. The benchmark evaluates foundation models by testing their ability to reason and plan within topological environments.

🔍 Overview

The benchmark contains 11,030 instances across 13 procedurally generated task types with controllable difficulty. It evaluates properties through two cognitive levels:

  • Reasoning: Asking a model to identify topological relations or infer how they change.
  • Planning: Instantiating a foundation model as a closed-loop agent whose policy selects environment actions.

⚙️ Key details

MindTopo focuses on five specific topological properties:

PropertyBasis
ContinuityCognitive science and formal topology
SeparationCognitive science and formal topology
OrderCognitive science and formal topology
EnclosureCognitive science and formal topology
KnotsCognitive science and formal topology

Researchers benchmarked 14 Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). These evaluations included agent configurations augmented with image and video generation, utilizing three specific video generative models in planning settings.

📊 Performance Findings

The study reveals significant gaps between current model capabilities and human performance:

  • Every MLLM performs better on reasoning tasks than on planning tasks.
  • The best-performing model remains far below observed human performance.
  • In tests using Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct, supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning improved the model’s reasoning capabilities more than its planning capabilities.
