Trump Defends US AI Guardrails and Anthropic Blacklist in FOX Interview

In a FOX News interview published Friday, President Donald Trump defended his administration’s approach to artificial intelligence regulation.

🔍 Overview

  • Trump asserted that Washington has established the necessary guardrails for AI.
  • He claimed the United States is ahead of China in the AI race.
  • He described AI as “bigger than the internet” and “probably” bigger than the Industrial Revolution.

🛡️ Guardrails

  • “We have guardrails. The biggest guardrails are having people that are just as smart because nobody understands it except you have to be extremely high IQ.”
  • He emphasized that the guardrails are meant to protect against misuse of the technology.

⚖️ Enforcement Action: Anthropic

  • The administration blacklisted AI company Anthropic as a security risk earlier this year and barred it from government contracts.
  • Trump pointed to this as evidence of swift enforcement, saying the firm “crossed red lines three months prior.”
  • Quote from the interview: “They did something that was very bad. Three months ago. And we shut them down. We shut them down fast.”
  • A federal judge ruled the ban illegal late last month.

🌏 US vs China

  • Trump contrasted the U.S. approach with China’s, stating, “China doesn’t have guardrails, by the way … President Xi is the guardrail. I mean, you can do anything in China.”
  • He reiterated that the United States is winning against China with respect to AI.

📈 Stakes

  • Trump framed AI as a transformative technology, saying it is larger in impact than the internet and likely surpasses the Industrial Revolution in significance.
