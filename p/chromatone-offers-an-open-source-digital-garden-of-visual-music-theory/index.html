Chromatone Offers an Open-Source Digital Garden of Visual Music Theory

Chromatone has been introduced as an independent open-source research project to use colors, shapes, and motion to learn, play, and communicate melody, harmony, and rhythms in live music performance environments. It functions as both a digital garden of visual music theory and a collection of visual music instruments.

🔍 Overview

Chromatone provides scientific research on many aspects of music theory as cards and short overviews focusing on the physics and physiology of vision and hearing, alongside their intersection in visual music research, exploration, practice, and self-expression. The project consists of progressive web-apps designed to be carried in a pocket for everyday music practice, including open-source web-experiments featuring interactive combinations of sounds and colors.

🧩 Key Features

  • Interactive Experiments: Open-source tools for sound analysis and synthesis, allowing users to experiment with various media instruments.
  • Modular Music Theory Data: Neatly stored lists of intervals, chords, scales, and rhythmic patterns from different cultures provided in YAML format.
  • Developer Integration: Everything is available as a treeshakeable NPM package that can be integrated into any JS project on both the server-side (SSR) and the client-side.
  • Educational Utility: The project abstracts low-level details into simple building blocks, making it a functional tool for prototyping and concept verification by audio researchers, educators, and students.
