Nvidia CEO Return and OpenAI Pretraining Loss Milestones

Nvidia announced the return of Jensen Huang as CEO eleven days after his resignation, while OpenAI published its pretraining loss curve, identifying a capability jump at a specific log-perplexity threshold.

🏢 Nvidia CEO Return

Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO, president, and a member of the Board of Directors. The board voted unanimously for this decision after holding additional meetings with Huang on Sunday. The company stated that it received input from senior U.S. officials, including the Treasury Secretary and the President, who dialed into the board meeting. Colette Kress remains the CFO, and the independent directors’ investigation continues. Additionally, Nvidia formed a board committee dedicated to national security and export control. Huang’s Senate hearing is still scheduled to take place in eight days.

📉 OpenAI Pretraining Loss Curve

OpenAI’s Chief Scientist, Jakub Pachocki, published a plot of base-model log-perplexity on their internal held-out coding dataset, a metric representing how surprised the model is by the next token (where lower values indicate better performance).

ModelLog-Perplexity
GPT-3.50.99
GPT-40.91
MAI-2 Base0.819
GPT-6 Astra0.76

Pachocki noted that when a model reached the 0.76 log-perplexity level (GPT-6 Astra), hundreds of frontier math problems began to be solved quickly. He extrapolates that the next significant jump in capabilities could occur around a log-perplexity of 0.68–0.70.

🤝 Proposed Self-Regulatory Limits

Pachocki proposed that AI labs agree on a limit to development and define the conditions required to cross it, such as the creation of a successor to chain-of-thought monitoring. Microsoft’s Mustafa Suleyman participated in this effort by running MAI-2 Base on OpenAI’s evaluation set, reporting a score of 0.819, and cosigning the proposal. The evidence notes that at least two more top labs, most notably Anthropic, need to join for this to become a substantive self-regulatory limit.

📈 Other Industry Updates

Sierra tripled its previous $10B valuation. Z.ai released GLM 5.4, described as their first looped transformer, which followed the release of Thinking Machine’s Inkling Mini 2 the previous week. Backblaze B2, which manages over six exabytes of capacity tier storage, emphasized that growth should not be a concern for their customers, who include leading neoclouds, labs, and generative AI companies.
