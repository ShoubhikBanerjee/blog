DIY Muse Gadget gets Claude upgrades for voice, camera and minigame

Meta’s Muse AI can now live on a $50 pocket‑sized pendant, thanks to a series of Claude‑generated code patches that add voice, richer avatars and remote‑camera functions.

🔍 Overview

Meta officially sanctions a “Muse Gadget” – a device that uses Meta’s open‑source code and tokens to connect directly to its Muse AI cloud. One hobbyist bought a Waveshare ESP32‑S3 1.75C touchscreen pendant for $50 and turned it into a working Muse Charm.

🛠️ Hardware

  • Model: Waveshare ESP32‑S3 1.75C (1.75‑inch touchscreen computer pendant)\
  • Cost: $50 (drop‑shipped from China)\
  • Form factor: Pocket‑watch size, doubles as a remote camera viewfinder\
  • Features out of the box: No cameras, no 5G cellular radio, can theoretically control smart‑home devices\
  • Official status: One of Meta’s first suggested hardware for building a Muse Gadget

⚙️ Original Muse Charm experience

  • Setup required plugging into a PC, pointing Claude at Meta’s GitHub, and pairing with the Muse phone app.\
  • Initial AI behavior treated push‑to‑talk audio as transcription requests rather than commands.\
  • Responses were slow, displayed only a few words at a time, and were not spoken aloud.\
  • The UI showed a single “Jolly” avatar that stayed idle.\
  • No automatic discovery of Amazon Echo, Google Home, or other smart‑home gadgets.\
  • Sample app from Meta engineers Anant Narayanan and Jeremy Payne could stream video but lacked a remote shutter button, making photo capture impractical.

🚀 Current capabilities

  • Push‑to‑talk AI with spoken answers via ElevenLabs voice.\
  • Full conversation history readable on the pendant.\
  • Multiple customizable avatars and a functional Tamagotchi‑style pet game.\
  • Remote photo capture with live preview and Bluetooth preview mode.\
  • Pocket‑watch display that shows accurate time.\
  • Embedded video player for Muse‑related clips.\
  • Still subject to occasional latency from Meta’s servers.

📦 Availability & concerns

  • The Waveshare ESP32‑S3 pendant can be purchased for $50 and is officially sanctioned by Meta for building a Muse Gadget.\
  • The author doesn’t trust Meta or its Muse agent with personal email or home‑network access after a reported leak of a reviewer’s home address.\
  • Shipping dates for the official Muse Charm remain unannounced.
