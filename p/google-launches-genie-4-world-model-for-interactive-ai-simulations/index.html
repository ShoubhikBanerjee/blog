Google launches Genie 4 world model for interactive AI simulations

Google announced today its Genie 4 world model for generating interactive experiences using AI.

🔍 Overview

  • Genie 4 is the next iteration of Google’s world‑model line, described as a model that “unlocks unprecedented realism and improves real‑time interactive simulation.”
  • The company positions the model as useful for pre‑visualization for filmmakers, prototyping game ideas, and creating training scenarios for robots.
  • Google warns that, as simulations run longer, details and object permanence can shift over time.

🧩 How it works

  • The model runs in real time and is natively multimodal, generating visual scenes together with audio such as music, ambient noises, and interactive dialogue.
  • It relies on several Gemini family components:
ComponentRole
Gemini OmniAnchors simulations in real‑world appearance and behavior
Other Gemini modelsProvide complex reasoning to maintain simulation coherence and continuity
Gemini 3.8 FlashActs as a guiding agent that continuously observes the scene, breaks goals into steps, tracks progress, and adapts plans when the world or user input changes
  • Users can steer a simulation with natural‑language prompts, changing weather, camera angle, or adding objects and characters. Gemini adapts the prompt to match the surrounding scene and monitors the world between inputs to keep it cohesive.
  • Physics is modeled with an advanced physical understanding, supporting scenarios such as aerodynamics and collisions.
  • People are simulated with natural movement, organic reactions, and real‑time interactive dialogue that adapts on the fly.

⚙️ Key details

  • Realism: More realistic worlds than the previous Genie 3 model.
  • Audio: First real‑time world model to generate audio natively; each world includes ambient sound (e.g., wind, waves) and interactive speech.
  • Duration: Experiences can now run about two minutes, up from one minute in Genie 3.
  • Real‑time updates: Natural‑language prompts can modify simulations instantly.
  • Safety and variability: Designed to create safe, infinitely variable simulations for robot and agent training.

🚀 Availability

  • Genie 4 is being released first as a research preview for “trusted testers.”
  • Google is gathering feedback before a broader consumer and developer release.
  • The model will be coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie, with a waitlist already open.
  • Early‑access partners include those using the Gemini API and Google AI Studio.

💡 Why it matters

  • Filmmakers can pre‑visualize scenes with realistic physics and audio.
  • Game designers can prototype ideas and iterate quickly using natural‑language control.
  • Robotics teams can build and test training environments for robots and AI agents before deployment in the real world.
  • The integration of Gemini reasoning allows testing of complex, multi‑step tasks within a simulated environment.
