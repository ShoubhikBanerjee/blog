Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 Lead New Wave of Frontier AI Models

🎉 One Year Later: Claude Opus 4.5 Anniversary

Today, November 24th 2026, marks the one year anniversary of the release of Claude Opus 4.5.

🧪 Testing Breakthroughs

  • Claude Opus 4.5 handles ambiguity and reasons about tradeoffs without hand-holding.
  • When pointed at a complex, multi-system bug, Opus 4.5 figures out the fix.
  • Tasks that were near-impossible for Sonnet 4.5 just a few weeks ago are now within reach.
  • Opus 4.5 was the first model I had used with Claude Code where the default result was generally the right thing.

⚙️ Claude Code Evolution

  • Claude Code was 9 months old at that point, having been introduced alongside Claude 3.7 Sonnet on February 24th, 2025.

📸 OpenAI’s GPT-5.1 Family

  • In November, OpenAI released GPT-5.1 Instant, GPT-5.1 Thinking, GPT-5.1-Codex, and GPT-5.1-Codex-Max.
  • These had a similar effect on their Codex coding agent: it became reliably useful for many of my coding tasks.

🔐 Claude Fable: Cybersecurity Breakthrough

  • We got our first hint of Claude Fable in April, when Anthropic announced Claude Mythos and said that it was too dangerous to release, due to its improved cybersecurity capabilities.
  • Mythos was a model that could not just find security vulnerabilities, but it could also figure out how to exploit them.
  • In June we got to see how good that model was for ourselves, with the release of Claude Fable 5—a defanged Mythos, that would solve most problems but would actively block you from using it for security work.
  • After three glorious days… the US government took it away from us.
  • Fable 5 was placed under export controls that effectively banned the model.
  • We wouldn’t get it back until July 1st.

🛠️ Revolutionary Automation

  • In June I wrote about how Claude Fable is relentlessly proactive, after I asked it to help fix a display glitch in Safari and it spun up its own pyobjc-framework-Quartz screenshot mechanism and a CORS-enabled localhost logging server to help resolve the bug.
  • A more recent example of the power of Fable class models is my mysterious beep finder from a couple of weeks ago.
  • I gave Opus 5.5 and then Fable 5.2 access to five WebAudio microphones in a room full of boxes and it was able to triangulate the source of an infuriating mysterious beep to within about 40cm.

🎮 3D Modeling Renaissance

  • I’d currently class Claude Fable 5, 5.1, 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 as Fable class (I think of Opus 5.5 as “cheap Fable”).
  • GPT-6 Astra got there for me too, and I think the new Gemini 4 Neon may count as well.
  • Something interesting happened with frontier LLMs over just the past four months: they got really good at 3D modeling.
  • I first noticed this with Claude Fable 5, but it really kicked into gear with Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, and most recently the Gemini 4 family.

🎲 Game Development Breakthrough

  • Back in August I experimented with having Fable 5 build me a raccoon heist game.
  • It had 3D graphics, looked like a game, and played really poorly.
  • GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra did a much better job but the game itself was still pretty dull after the first 90 seconds of play.
  • I tried telling Claude Opus 5.5 “This game isn’t fun enough yet. Make it a LOT more fun.”
  • My Command & Conquer-style RTS went from boring to deeply entertaining overnight, after Opus spent 12 hours playtesting new features and rebalancing the gameplay.
  • Opus 5.5 came out on September 22nd, 2026.

⚔️ Indie Games Culture War

  • The indie games scene has descended into a full-blown culture war over AI-assisted programming.
