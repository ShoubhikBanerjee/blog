AI Scribes Enter Dental Practices to Automate Notes and Voice Perio Charting

Dental practices are adopting AI scribes to automate the creation of exam notes and complete periodontic charts. These tools allow dental professionals to document patient visits and update records hands-free, directly addressing administrative burdens and documentation gaps.

🔍 Overview

An AI scribe for dentistry drafts exam notes and fills perio charts while the clinician stays with the patient. Historically, keeping complete records has been a challenge; a 2016 study summarized earlier research showing that dental patient information was absent anywhere from 9.4% to 87.1% of the time. Modern dental AI scribes integrate directly with Practice Management Systems (PMS) to write back clinical notes and perio data in real time.

⚙️ Key features

  • Voice Perio Charting: Seven out of eight reviewed tools support voice perio charting. The hygienist calls out values, and the PMS perio chart updates while charting is still underway.
  • System Write-Back: Four of the eight tools support write-back integrations for Dentrix, Open Dental, and Eaglesoft. Clinicians should confirm write-back compatibility for their exact practice management system first, as it determines where notes land.
  • Automated Note Drafting: These systems record the visit, recognize speakers, and generate structured clinical notes or SOAP drafts.

💡 Why it matters

Dental practices are currently facing severe staffing and operational pressures. According to polling by the American Dental Association (ADA), most dentists recruiting hygienists find the process very or extremely challenging. Additionally, a study published in 2024 revealed that 44.1% of dentists surveyed at one large US dental group reported experiencing burnout. By taking over the documentation process, AI scribes aim to relieve clinical workloads and improve charting accuracy.

🚀 Availability

  • Avora: Designed for hygiene-heavy practices. Offers a free two-week trial under a limited-time offer, with pricing quoted upon request. Security claims like SOC 2 Type II are stated in its own materials rather than a viewable report.
  • Bola AI: Well-suited for Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, and Eaglesoft offices. It powers Henry Schein One’s Dentrix Ascend Voice. On Dentrix Ascend, a Chrome extension saves to the cloud record in real time, while on desktop Dentrix, the note goes into the patient chart. Users can download a business associate agreement (BAA) and patient consent form before the first visit, though the privacy policy does not specify how long audio is kept or if it trains models.
  • Denti.AI: Suitable for practices that want to price a tool without a sales call.
