Granite Switch 4.1 Unifies Chat, Decision‑Making and Speech on a Single Endpoint

Granite Switch 4.1 introduces a family of models that pack chat, decision‑making and speech transcription capabilities into a single endpoint while using roughly the same GPU memory as the base model alone.

🔍 Overview

  • New model variants: Granite‑Switch‑4.1‑8B and Granite‑Switch‑4.1‑30B.
  • Each variant bundles three activated LoRA (aLoRA) adapters:
    • a Decision aLoRA for general‑purpose decision tasks (e.g., v1/systemone).
    • a Guardian aLoRA that, on average, outperforms Jev 1.13, Microsoft‑Decision‑1, GPT‑Luna‑Decisions and Clef‑Flash on safety‑related decision tasks.
    • Granite‑Speech‑5.0‑Turbo‑CTC, the fastest ASR model on the OpenASR Leaderboard, used for transcription.
  • The combined model occupies approximately the same VRAM as the underlying base model, delivering “more capabilities with fewer GPUs.”

🧩 How it works

  • The adapters are built as cache‑efficient Activated LoRAs. The KV cache generated by the base model during one generation can be reused for any subsequent adapter‑based request.
  • Recent vLLM contributions accelerate low‑rank adapter inference.
  • A co‑located ASR encoder enables the model to switch between chat, decision and spoken requests with “blazing fast speeds.”
  • An example Space – Speech‑to‑Decisions – shows granite-switch-4.1-8b transcribing audio, generating summaries, screening for harmful content and evaluating 15 user‑defined flags in real time on a single endpoint.

⚙️ Key details

Granite‑Switch‑4.1‑8B8 B parametersDecision aLoRA, Guardian aLoRA, Granite‑Speech‑5.0‑Turbo‑CTC
Granite‑Switch‑4.1‑30B30 B parametersDecision aLoRA, Guardian aLoRA, Granite‑Speech‑5.0‑Turbo‑CTC
  • Performance: In a “race” against Jev‑27B (frozen Qwen base + 1 generic decision LoRA), Granite‑Switch‑4.1‑30B completed a real‑world, 5‑step RAG workflow faster.
  • Custom use case: A 3 B‑parameter Granite Switch model navigated the classic Doom game at 60–100 decisions / second, compared with Jev’s reported 10 decisions / second, and could also narrate and answer questions while playing.
  • Granite Libraries provide a repository of specialized adapters that can be mixed with the general‑purpose decision adapter at little extra cost because they share the base model’s KV cache.

🚀 Availability

  • The 8B and 30B models are released with “batteries included”, i.e., pre‑compiled with a helpful set of adapters.
  • Users may compose their own Granite Switch models by mixing adapters from Granite Libraries.
  • An Adapter SDK for training adapters in Mellea, along with tutorials and support, is planned for future release.

💡 Why it matters

  • Consolidates three major AI functionalities—chat, decision‑making and speech—into one model, simplifying deployment and reducing hardware requirements.
  • Cache‑efficient design and vLLM acceleration make multi‑modal switching real‑time, opening possibilities for interactive applications such as live transcription with instant safety screening.
  • The open‑adapter ecosystem encourages community extensions and custom task specialization without large memory overhead.
