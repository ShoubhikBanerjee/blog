Board Intelligence launches IQ Entities, an AI‑powered multi‑jurisdiction entity governance platform

Board Intelligence introduced IQ Entities, an AI‑driven solution designed for governance teams that manage entities across multiple jurisdictions.

🧩 Overview

  • IQ Entities is built for governance teams running entities across several jurisdictions.
  • The AI reads your paperwork, prepares record updates and filings, and answers questions about any entity in the group.
  • It redraws the group‑structure chart as records change and exports it to PowerPoint and Word as editable shapes.

⚙️ Core AI Capabilities

  • Document ingestion – Bulk‑upload existing documents; the AI reads them and proposes each change with a citation to the source page. A named person accepts or rejects each proposal before it becomes the live record.
  • Automation of filings – Files directly with Companies House in the UK under your presenter account, which you connect during onboarding.
  • Public‑register verification – Checks your records against the public register so you can fix differences before they are discovered.
  • In‑app assistant – Finance or tax colleagues with read‑only access can ask the assistant about an entity, including who the ultimate beneficial owners are.

🌍 Jurisdiction Handling

  • Company secretaries see the right fields for each jurisdiction.
  • German entities – Options offered: GmbH, AG, UG.
  • Delaware entities – Options offered: Corporation, LLC.
  • Configure a jurisdiction once; IQ Entities then applies that jurisdiction’s rules as dated tasks against every entity in it.
  • Jurisdiction‑aware records include mapped obligations such as beneficial‑ownership reporting (e.g., the UK PSC register).
  • Board Intelligence is adding further registry integrations.

📊 Tasks and Obligations Automation

  • Map an obligation once (e.g., a new director appointment triggering an AP01 within 14 days). IQ Entities raises the task, assigns an owner, and starts the clock.
  • From April 2028, UK Companies House will require annual accounts in iXBRL format using commercial software; web and paper routes for accounts filings will close.
  • Web filing will remain open for confirmation statements and director‑detail updates.

🔒 Security and Data Handling

  • IQ Entities holds a single structured register for ownership, directors, officers, and roles, with granular permissions layered on top.
  • Permissions can be tied to a position, automatically granting or revoking access as colleagues move between roles.
  • ISO 27001‑audited, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and is independently penetration‑tested.
  • Board Intelligence applies zero data retention and never uses customer data to train AI models.

🚀 Availability

  • The platform is live for governance teams seeking to streamline multi‑jurisdiction entity management and regulatory compliance.
