AI Surveillance Towers Deployed for Border Monitoring in Southern California

The U.S. border in Southern California has seen a proliferation of surveillance towers equipped with artificial intelligence designed to track movements and distinguish border-crossers from animals and other people.

🧩 How it works

Contractors have implemented newer camera systems using AI to monitor the border. Key technical capabilities include:

  • Hardware: Towers such as those made by General Dynamics feature two sets of electro-optical and infrared cameras.
  • Range: Long-range lenses are capable of tracking targets 5 to 7 miles away.
  • Network Integration: These towers are part of a broader network including trail cameras, radar, infrared sensors, drones, blimps, and satellites (as directed by the Trump administration in March 2025).

⚙️ Key details

  • Cost: The overall cost of these AI surveillance systems exceeds one billion dollars.
  • Scale: More than 120 surveillance towers are located along the California border.

💡 Why it matters

An analysis by the Times of San Diego and an investigation by MIT Technology Review have examined the effectiveness of these systems in relation to migrant deaths:

  • Analysis: At least 138 cases in the past four years involved migrants’ remains found within the range of the cameras.
  • Visibility: An MIT Technology Review topographical analysis estimated more than half of these remains were located in spots where a camera would have a clear line of sight.
