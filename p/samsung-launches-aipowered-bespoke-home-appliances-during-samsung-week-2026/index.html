Samsung Launches AI‑Powered Bespoke Home Appliances During Samsung Week 2026

From October 12 to November 8, Samsung’s global Samsung Week 2026 promotion highlighted the company’s newest AI‑enabled Bespoke appliances, offering up to 34% off plus freebies and exclusive benefits on Samsung.com.

🔍 Overview

  • Samsung Week 2026 runs from October 12 to November 08 in over 60 countries and is Samsung’s largest global promotion of the year.
  • The event celebrates Samsung’s anniversary and thanks loyal customers with discounts, rewards points, trade‑in options, and free delivery.
  • Among the highlights are the Bespoke AI appliances that bring intelligence into the kitchen and laundry.

🛠️ AI Features in Bespoke Appliances

  • AI Vision Inside – Identifies ingredients in the Bespoke AI Refrigerator and suggests recipes.
  • AI Pro Cooking – Recognizes ingredients, optimizes cooking settings, and monitors food in the Bespoke AI Oven.
  • AI OptiWash – Detects load weight, fabric type, and soil level to optimize wash cycles in the Bespoke AI Laundry Combo.

🎁 Offers and Benefits

  • Up to 34% off plus freebies during Samsung Week 2026.
  • 1.5× Samsung Rewards discount when points are used toward purchases, including select accessories and digital content.
  • Trade‑in Program, Samsung Care+, flexible finance options, and free delivery.
  • Additional perks: 60 days of Strava and iFIT with the Galaxy Watch Ultra2, and six months of Google AI Pro with the latest Galaxy Z Series, Galaxy S26 FE, or Galaxy Tab S12 Series.

📅 Availability

  • The promotions and new AI‑powered Bespoke appliances are available through Samsung.com from October 12 to November 8, 2026.
  • Visit the Samsung Week 2026 offer page for details: https://www.samsung.com/ph/offer/samsung-week

💡 Why It Matters

  • Integrating AI into everyday appliances makes cooking and laundry more intuitive and efficient.
  • By automatically recognizing ingredients and adjusting settings, the devices reduce the need for manual input and help users get better results with less effort.
  • The launch demonstrates Samsung’s broader strategy of embedding AI across its product ecosystem to support productivity at home and in the office.
