Meta Introduces Muse Personal AI Agent for Everyday Tasks

Meta has launched Muse, a personal AI agent powered by the in-house Muse Spark model. The product is part of a multi-billion dollar strategy overhaul intended to revitalize Meta’s position in the AI race and catch up to rivals such as Google, Anthropic, and OpenAI.

🔍 Overview

Muse is described by Meta as a “personal AI agent” designed to put AI in the hands of virtually anyone. While Meta pitches it as “the world’s first personal AI agent built for everyone,” it is similar to various AI assistants and teammates offered by competitors.

🧩 How it works

Muse is designed to handle everyday tasks and projects. Its capabilities include:

  • Autonomous Action: Once given a goal, Muse can open a browser, fill out forms, and negotiate on behalf of users.
  • Background Processing: For lengthy tasks, Muse continues working after the app is closed and returns to the user if it requires approval (such as for a purchase) or if something changes.
  • Contextual Memory: The agent remembers details shared by users, even if mentioned only once, to provide unprompted suggestions.
  • Simple Interface: Users can communicate with the assistant via the Muse app or WhatsApp.

⚙️ Key details

Meta has focused on ease of use and privacy to distinguish Muse from other tools, many of which target technically-minded individuals or businesses.

Privacy and Security:

  • Data Control: Users can opt out of their interactions being used to train Meta’s AI models and can instruct Muse to “forget” specific learned information.
  • Virtual Isolation: Muse runs on a virtual computer in the cloud to keep data separate from other agents.
  • Sentinel: A separate AI agent named Sentinel patrols the virtual machine to ensure Muse cannot reach the internet without approval.
  • Payment Security: Muse has no visibility into payment method passwords and currently supports secure checkout via Stripe’s Link, with 1Password and Shop Pay support coming soon.
  • Future Encryption: Meta plans to introduce a “confidential” version of the virtual machine later this year that is encrypted so even Meta cannot access it.

🚀 Availability

Muse will roll out in the US across the following platforms:

  • iOS
  • Android
  • muse.ai
  • AI glasses (coming soon)

The assistant will be free for most users, although Meta stated there will be paid subscriptions “for people who want to do more,” though costs and specific limits were not outlined.
