Nvidia Launches DLSS 5 for RTX 50-Series GPUs and NBA 2K27

Nvidia is officially launching DLSS 5 this week, an AI upscaling technology the company described as its most significant breakthrough in computer graphics since real-time ray tracing debuted in 2018.

🧩 How it works

Nvidia stated that DLSS 5 provides developers with controls and sliders to determine how changes appear in their games. Capabilities include:

  • The ability to choose which parts of a scene to enhance.
  • The option to enhance specific elements like foliage, food, or silverware while leaving characters’ faces alone.
  • Support for 6x frame generation, where the game produces one “real” frame for every six frames displayed.

⚙️ Key details

While initial demos required two RTX 5090 cards, Nvidia claims a single midrange RTX 5060 can run NBA 2K27 at 1080p with ray tracing and ultra spec DLSS 5, provided it is paired with one of the fastest gaming CPUs and 6x frame generation is enabled.

However, the technology carries a significant performance cost:

  • Nvidia estimates a performance hit of 50 to 60 percent.
  • An unofficial mod in Shadow of the Tomb Raider showed performance being cut in half.

🚀 Availability

DLSS 5 is available on RTX 50-series desktop and laptop GPUs and through GeForce Now cloud gaming.
