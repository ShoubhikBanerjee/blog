AI Engineer Role Focused on LangChain, RAG, and Model Context Protocols

A pioneer in smart connected technology, precision agriculture, and autonomous machinery is opening a 12‑month contract for an AI Engineer to join its Enterprise Data Science & AI Enablement team.

🔍 Overview

  • The position is centered on translating departmental AI use cases into scalable, reusable AI agents, Model Context Protocol (MCP) connections, Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, and modular starter kits.

⚙️ Role & Responsibilities

  • Architect, build, and maintain a central repository of reusable AI skills, agentic workflows, prompt templates, and developer starter kits.
  • Build and deploy functional AI agents, tool‑calling chains, and conversational copilots using Python and frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or AutoGen.
  • Implement and standardize Model Context Protocols (MCPs) and custom tool harnesses to connect large language models (LLMs) securely with internal databases, APIs, and document stores.
  • Design and optimize RAG pipelines, embedding strategies, semantic search indices, and vector‑database storage for contextual reasoning.
  • Partner with embedded AI coaches across departments to package one‑off solutions into reusable, well‑documented enterprise knowledge assets.
  • Establish automated testing harnesses, evaluate agent performance and output quality, track adoption metrics, and manage version‑controlled releases via GitHub.

📍 Location & Work Structure

  • Locations: Austin, TX 78704; Chicago, IL; Urbandale, IA – open to 100 % fully remote work.
  • Shift: First Shift, standard daytime business hours (≈ 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM), Monday through Friday.

💰 Compensation & Contract

  • Pay Rate: $56.00 – $60.00 per hour on a W‑2 basis.
  • Contract Duration: 12 months with the possibility of extension.
  • Seniority Level: Mid‑Senior.
  • Employment Type: Full‑time.
  • Industries: Motor Vehicle Manufacturing, Industrial Machinery Manufacturing, and Manufacturing.

🛠️ Required Skills & Technologies

  • 4 + years of software/data engineering experience with generative AI systems, conversational agents, or LLM‑powered applications.
  • Expert‑level Python development and familiarity with standard data/AI libraries.
  • Hands‑on experience orchestrating LLMs using LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, AutoGen, or comparable agent frameworks.\n- Practical understanding of Model Context Protocols (MCPs), API tool calling, and dynamic context injection.
  • Experience building end‑to‑end RAG pipelines and working with vector databases such as Pinecone, Milvus, Chroma, Weaviate, Qdrant, or pgvector.
  • Advanced GitHub workflow, CI/CD automation, and test‑harness engineering.
  • Cloud engineering experience on AWS (Bedrock, SageMaker, Lambda, S3).
  • Experience with complex multi‑agent systems, big‑data platforms (Databricks, Spark, Delta Lake), and developer documentation.

📅 Application Process

  • A mandatory Glider AI/Coding assessment must be completed before client submission.
  • Interested candidates should contact the recruiter at jevin@cube-hub.com.
