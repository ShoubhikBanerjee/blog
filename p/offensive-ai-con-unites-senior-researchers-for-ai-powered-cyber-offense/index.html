Offensive AI Con Unites Senior Researchers for AI-Powered Cyber Offense

Offensive AI Con (OAIC) is an invite‑only technical conference that examined the intersection of artificial intelligence and offensive cybersecurity. Held in Oceanside, San Diego, the event gathered senior security researchers, AI/ML engineers, and government defense representatives to discuss AI‑driven vulnerability discovery and exploit development.

🔍 Overview

  • Invite‑only, non‑commercial format prioritizing research collaboration over vendor presentations
  • Hosted in Oceanside, San Diego
  • Structured around technical talks, confidential breakout sessions, and networking receptions
  • Targets senior technical professionals working in offensive security and AI research

🎯 Topics Covered

  • AI‑based vulnerability research
  • Autonomous and semi‑autonomous offensive cyber operations
  • Methods for accelerating exploit development
  • Specialized datasets, model training strategies, scaffolding techniques, and binary analysis
  • OODA (observe, orient, decide, act) workflows and reducing cognitive strain for security operators
  • Multi‑agent strategies and AI‑based target identification

⚖️ Ethics & Policy

  • Responsible‑use frameworks for offensive AI capabilities
  • Discussion of unintended acceleration of cyber‑warfare capabilities
  • Strategic approaches to safeguarding organizations
  • Policy sessions with industry and government perspectives to examine how offensive AI research should be conducted and governed
  • Efforts to establish norms and coordination mechanisms as AI capabilities in offensive security advance

👥 Attendee Profile

  • Heads of security research, CTOs, research engineers, distinguished engineers, and technical staff from AI labs
  • Government and defense representatives from the Department of Defense and intelligence community organizations
  • Practitioners and policymakers interested in AI/ML applications in security contexts
