Autonomous Frameworks ScientistTwo and CDA Advance Enterprise Model Discovery and Scientific Research

Researchers have announced developments in autonomous discovery with the introduction of ScientistTwo and the Continual Discovery Agent (CDA). ScientistTwo is a multi-agent framework designed to automate the scientific research cycle, while CDA focuses on discovering and revising world models within enterprise systems. These developments aim to create agents that can learn complex rules and generate expert-level outputs without human intervention.

🔍 Overview

The research focuses on two primary systems designed for autonomous learning and discovery:

  • ScientistTwo: A fully autonomous multi-agent framework that establishes baselines, formulates hypotheses, and coordinates specialized agents for end-to-end research cycles.
  • Continual Discovery Agent (CDA): An agent that starts without knowledge of business rules in an enterprise system and discovers them by interacting with records and observing outcomes.

🧩 How it works

ScientistTwo utilizes a closed-loop system to manage scientific tasks:

  • Takes an initial problem as input and coordinates specialized agents to orchestrate an end-to-end discovery cycle.
  • Conducts experiments using diverse datasets and metrics.
  • Refines methodologies through automated ablation studies.
  • Validates findings via a closed-loop simulated peer-review rebuttal engine.

CDA operates within enterprise environments to build and maintain world models:

  • Observes effects where updating one field can set another, create a record, or start an approval.
  • Builds a world model from observations and revises it as rules change.
  • Predicts the effects of hidden rules without querying the running system.

⚙️ Key details

To evaluate these systems, researchers established specific benchmarks and environments:

ComponentDetail
EnterpriseWorldShiftA live ServiceNow environment with nine tables and 25 hidden rules.
Evaluation Actions600 actions used to test discovery, revision, extension, and retirement.
Performance MetricCDA predicts hidden rule effects up to 8.98 IoU points more accurately than lookup-based approaches.
Scientific BenchmarksScientistTwo is tested against papers from conferences such as ICLR, ICML, and NeurIPS.

💡 Why it matters

ScientistTwo produces expert-level, publishable papers and fully verified, executable codebases. Its solutions consistently outperform human state-of-the-art models and achieve higher average review ratings than human-authored papers when evaluated by automated AI review agents. In enterprise contexts, CDA demonstrates the ability to carry a learned world model across different versions of a system, accurately predicting the effects of business rules that are revised, added, or removed over time.
