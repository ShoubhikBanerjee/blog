Suno launches Speech: AI voices with built‑in music in public beta

Suno, known for its AI‑generated music, has added a new Speech feature that creates spoken voiceovers from scripts or descriptive prompts and can blend them with AI‑generated background music. The feature is available now in public beta on Suno’s web and mobile platforms.

🔍 Overview

  • Suno is branching out from the world of AI music, launching a new feature that generates spoken voices based on scripts or prompted descriptions.
  • The new Speech feature provides synthetic voiceovers embellished with AI background music.
  • “Music will always be at the heart of Suno and what we build. At the same time, our vision has always extended to other forms of human expression,” said Suno chief product officer Jack Brody.
  • “Today, we’re expanding what’s possible in Suno with Speech: the first audio model that generates voice and music together as one cohesive track.”

🧩 How it works

  • To use the feature, select the Create tab and navigate to the Speech option.
  • Two creation modes are offered:
    ModeWhat you do
    SimpleDescribe what you want in the prompt box (e.g., “a pirate captain rallying his crew”).
    AdvancedProvide a custom script and adjust advanced settings such as gender, speech style, and voice‑generation variety.
  • Advanced settings let you tweak:
    • Gender of the AI voice
    • Speech style
    • Variety of each voice generation
  • Speech tracks can be up to eight minutes long.

⚙️ Features

  • Synthetic voiceovers generated from text or descriptive prompts.
  • Optional AI‑generated background music that can be toggled on or off.
  • Music can complement use cases like calming soundtracks for poems or energetic scores for dramatic voiceovers and encouraging speeches.
  • Public beta across both web and mobile platforms.
  • Users can turn off background music with a simple toggle if they only need clean speech.

🚀 Availability

  • Speech is now in public beta on Suno’s web and mobile platforms.
  • The feature is still being refined; Suno admits it is “far from perfect,” and promises to improve it based on user feedback.
  • “Beta really does mean beta,” said Brody.
  • Known quirks include: “Occasionally, British accents can wander off to Australia and back. Dramatic pauses may be very dramatic. You will almost certainly discover uses for this that never occurred to us.”

💡 Why it matters

  • AI‑generated speech is a mature field, with existing tools from DeepMind, Adobe, and ElevenLabs. Suno’s entry pairs its music‑generation capability with voice synthesis, offering a unique spin on text‑to‑speech tools.
  • By combining voice and music in a single cohesive track, Suno aims to diversify its platform beyond music, especially as its music generator has attracted legal challenges.
  • The optional music layer and flexible prompt modes open new creative possibilities for creators looking to add narrated audio with tailored soundscapes.
