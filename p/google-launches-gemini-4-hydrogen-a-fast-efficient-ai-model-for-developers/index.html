Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast, efficient AI model for developers

Today Google announced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a new model in the Gemini 4 family built for rapid, efficient execution of everyday coding and agentic workflows.

🔍 Overview

  • Hydrogen targets “rapid execution across diverse agentic workflows” while Carbon and Argon remain the frontier models for deep, long‑horizon reasoning.
  • It runs on a flat‑rate pricing of $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1 million‑token context window (both input and output).
  • Four thinking levels – low, medium, high (default), and xhigh – let users trade quality, latency, and cost predictably.
  • The model pairs native 1 M‑token input with up to 1 M‑token output to synthesize dense PDFs, spreadsheets, charts, audio, and video into cited reports.

🧩 How it works

  • Hydrogen batches file‑read and search calls in parallel, enabling 2×‑2.8× faster repository exploration and 12%‑23% lower token usage compared with Gemini 3.8 Flash.
  • In multi‑step research or code‑base navigation it ranks #5 on Agent Arena and leads its class on HLE‑Diamond (58.8%), Vals Tax Agent Bench (52.3% #1), Vals Legal Research Bench (55.8% tied #1), and AutomationBench‑AA.
  • On the new Multimodal Knowledge Agent benchmark it scores 53.4%, and on Agents’ Last Exam v1.1 it scores 71.6%.
  • For developer‑centric tasks like live UI prototyping, design‑to‑code iteration, and interactive app building, Hydrogen generates more functional layouts with fewer prompts.

⚙️ Key details

ModelPrimary strength
Gemini 4 CarbonDeep, long‑horizon reasoning
Gemini 4 ArgonDeep, long‑horizon reasoning
Gemini 4 HydrogenRapid, high‑throughput execution
  • Orchestrator (in Google Antigravity) automatically switches between models in a session. On Terminal‑Bench 4.0 it achieved near‑Carbon success rates while using 24% less quota and finishing 19% faster.
  • In multi‑agent workflows Carbon can coordinate while Hydrogen powers worker and verifier agents, allowing parallel development across isolated workspaces.
  • Compared with Gemini 3.8 Flash, Hydrogen finishes tasks 2.5× faster at its high default setting, more than triples Terminal‑Bench 4.1 scores (57% vs 18.2%), and reduces fabricated claims by >5×.

🚀 Availability

  • Hydrogen is available to all tiers of Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for Business, the Gemini app, Google Search, and to paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity.

💡 Why it matters

  • Teams can keep Carbon or Argon for deep architectural judgment while using Hydrogen as a disciplined execution engine for code writing, testing, and verification.
  • The lower latency and token‑efficient batching cut costs and speed up shipping of complex projects.
  • Improved instruction following, tone adherence, and tool‑calling make Hydrogen a reliable everyday assistant for drafting emails, study guides, and other documents.
