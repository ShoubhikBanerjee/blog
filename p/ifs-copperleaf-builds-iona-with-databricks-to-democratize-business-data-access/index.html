IFS Copperleaf Builds IONA with Databricks to Democratize Business Data Access

IFS Copperleaf, a provider of Asset Investment Planning software, partnered with Databricks to build IONA, a platform designed to democratize access to business data and advanced analytics.

🤝 The Partnership

IFS Copperleaf and Databricks share a vision to rethink how employees interact with business data and make advanced analytics accessible to everyone. The team explored the Databricks Genie product family, specifically looking for AI cowork and conversational analytics features. IFS Copperleaf became a voice for advanced customers, sharing prototypes and feedback to help shape the future of Genie One, Genie Agents, and Genie Ontology.

“We would have moved on to build our own thing. But we made this partnership happen because we both had the same vision.”

🎯 The Challenge

IFS Copperleaf runs on many systems, from CRM and marketing platforms to support, contract and finance tools, each owned by a different part of the business. Lisa Richter, Director of Technology and AI at IFS Copperleaf, notes that even at a simplistic level, questions like “how many customers do we have?” are hard to answer due to siloed systems and inconsistent definitions.

🛠️ The Solution (IONA)

The goal was to create a foundation that is standardized, governed, and usable by business teams without waiting for a specialist. The team held it to three outcomes: faster decisions, grounded in trusted data, and no longer dependent on a specialist.

Instead of the textbook approach of cleaning every table, the team flipped the process. Finance and the marketing-to-sales funnel are fully modeled, while everything else answers directly on raw data. This allows data to become useful months sooner.

📈 Outcomes

Business users were immediately relieved and excited to have democratized access to data that had previously been limited to formal analysis or technical practitioners. Unlike generic AI tools, IONA, built on Genie and grounded in Genie Ontology, was designed to self-improve and prompt for additional information when needed.

As employees began working with IONA to refine its understanding of their business, IFS Copperleaf brought that real-world feedback to the Databricks team. This collaboration helped shape the future of the Genie platform.
