China Telecom’s TeleAgent reaches 1.5 million users and launches private‑deployment edition

China Telecom’s office AI agent, TeleAgent, announced that it has grown to 1.5 million registered users and over 200,000 daily active users – a sharp rise from just 27,000 registered users in mid‑June. The milestone was revealed by Liu Guiqing at the opening of PT Expo China 2026, after the product’s first unveiling at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) 2026.

🔍 Overview

  • User growth: 1.5 M registered users, >200 K DAU (up from 27 K in mid‑June).\
  • Announcement: Liu Guiqing, Executive Director, President and COO of China Telecommunications Corporation, at PT Expo China 2026.\
  • First reveal: WAIC 2026.

🧩 How it works

  • Built by China Telecom AI Technology Co. (TeleAI) as a native workplace AI application.
  • Runs natively on Windows, macOS, Kylin OS and UnionTech OS (UOS) – no complex server setup needed for individual users.
  • Users simply drag and drop files into the workspace; the agent executes end‑to‑end tasks.
  • Leverages China Telecom’s Xingchen multimodal foundation model and the “five‑in‑one” intelligent cloud.
  • Implements “Token‑based operations”: an intelligent model router selects the most cost‑effective foundation model and uses context compression to lower latency and inference costs.
  • Persistent long‑term memory adapts to user habits over time.

⚙️ Key details

EditionCore Feature
Enterprise SaaSMulti‑device collaboration and scenario customisation
Private‑deployment (released mid‑August)Models and data remain entirely within the customer’s local, sovereign environment
  • Skill marketplace grew from ~40 K to >50 K skills; 99 % of skills were created by end users solving real‑world productivity challenges.
  • Supports professionals, SMEs and large state‑owned conglomerates.

🔐 Security

  • Four‑layer defence loop covering perception, reasoning, execution and memory.
  • Lightweight security sandbox isolates file access.
  • Certifications from the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT).
  • Cleared 99.7 % of 336 threat‑evaluation scenarios conducted by QiAnXin and other leading security vendors.

📈 Adoption & Impact

  • In‑depth survey of key enterprise pilot and power users:
    • 90 % say the agent is integral to daily routines.
    • Document drafting, industry research and automated data analysis account for >50 % of usage.
    • 79 % report significant productivity gains; 35 % say the tool exceeded expectations.
    • Net Promoter Score (NPS): 62.9.
  • Among the 74 % who previously used rival platforms:
    • 89 % rate TeleAgent on par with market leaders.
    • 16 % rate it superior.
  • Consulting analyst example: a quarterly task that previously required exporting 20 reports from five systems and half a day of manual cleaning now takes five minutes via a simple upload and objective statement.
  • Internal use: a regional sales and channel operations unit reduced cross‑system consolidation from hours to minutes and automated report distribution.

🌐 Ecosystem & Integration

  • Serves as the enterprise flagship for China Telecom’s unified group‑wide agent architecture.
  • Core “Xing Xiaochen” agent hub drives multimodal intent recognition, task planning and tool orchestration under a “build once, reuse across the network” framework.
  • Integrated capabilities across navigation (AutoNavi/AMap), corporate travel (Umetrip), lifestyle and weather.
  • 60 agent applications from 44 external partners are currently undergoing technical integration.
  • Professionals in marketing, engineering, legal and consulting delegate routine paperwork, ledger consolidation and contract risk reviews to TeleAgent.

🚀 Availability

  • Enterprise SaaS edition is already available; private‑deployment edition launched in mid‑August.
  • Native operation on Windows, macOS, Kylin OS and UnionTech OS (UOS) with drag‑and‑drop workflow; no server infrastructure required for individual users.

💡 Why it matters

  • IDC technical evaluation of Chinese enterprise‑grade general‑purpose agents gave TeleAgent a score of 6.85, placing it in the top three nationwide with cost efficiency, security and task performance well above industry benchmarks.
  • The combination of sovereign‑ready private deployment, strong security posture and token‑based operation offers enterprises a cost‑effective, secure AI assistant for a wide range of productivity scenarios.
