Anthropic IPO Closes at $97, Largest Raise in History

Anthropic listed on Nasdaq under the ticker $ANT, pricing its Initial Public Offering at $83 per share. The stock closed at $97.17, marking a 17% increase from the offering price before drifting to $92.60 after the close. The opening trade hit $111.50, which was 34% above the IPO price, placing the company near a $2 trillion fully diluted valuation.

💰 The Record Raise

Anthropic’s $81.3 billion offering overtook SpaceX’s June IPO to become the largest in history, beating SpaceX’s $75 billion raise. The company expected net proceeds of $104.8 billion, a figure that includes a concurrent private placement. During the IPO process, AMD agreed to invest an additional $2.5 billion in Anthropic.

📉 Market Outlook

Analysts expect lockup expiries to weigh on the stock price as approximately one trillion dollars’ worth of shares free up over the next year. This timing coincides with the company’s challenge of maintaining its position as the world’s top model provider.

👥 Stakeholders and Governance

Class F shares, held by co-founders Dario and Daniela Amodei, carry 50.1% of the voting power on most shareholder matters. Google owns a 13.7% stake in the company. SEC filings suggest the average employee could now be worth over $40 million, meaning the 5,300 founders and employees collectively hold more wealth than the bottom third of US households (approximately 45 million families). After-tax reality checks suggest the median employee likely takes home closer to $5 million than the headline figures.

🛡️ Safety Commitments

In their founders’ letter, Dario and Daniela Amodei committed to pacing the training and deployment of AI systems based on whether they could keep them safe and under human control. General Catalyst, an early backer, framed the company’s origin as a contrarian idea by seven people: that the safest way to build AI is also the best way. Notably, Dustin Moskovitz, an early funder, has reportedly not spoken to the co-founders in over a year and no longer speaks to Zuckerberg, Altman, or Holden Karnofsky.

🏆 Product Standing

On the day of the IPO, Anthropic was the #1 lab on Code Arena WebDev, with its model Claude Fable 5.2 scoring 1824, which is 27 points higher than the next lab.
