Microsoft agrees to enforceable AI privacy and safety principles for school districts

Microsoft has reached a new agreement regarding AI privacy and safety rules for schools, establishing legally enforceable principles for districts that adopt them. The commitment follows a week after major school systems in New York City and Los Angeles implemented one-year bans on student-facing AI tools to allow time to establish proper guardrails.

🔍 Overview

Microsoft entered into this agreement with the American Federation of Teachers (AFT) and its New York City affiliate, the United Federation of Teachers (UFT). The terms include ten principles that school districts can choose to add to their contracts. AFT President Randi Weingarten described the terms as “iron-clad” and necessary because “no one else, including the federal government, has stepped up to do the real work.”

⚙️ Key details

The agreement establishes specific data protections and operational requirements for Microsoft’s AI tools in educational settings:

PrincipleRequirement
Model TrainingNo training of AI models on student or educator data
Data CollectionLimits the initial amount of data Microsoft collects
TransparencyDisclosing how tools work to families in plain language
AI CompanionsProhibits the use of AI companions
Risk ManagementRequires human review for “high-risk” decisions

💡 Why it matters

As some parents and educators resist the use of screens and AI in schools, Microsoft is responding to a policy crackdown. The AFT has been critical of tech in schools, with President Weingarten previously calling for a ban on screens before third grade and student-facing AI before middle school. However, the organization is also working with tech companies, including a $23 million AI training hub for educators funded by Anthropic, Microsoft, and OpenAI.

🚀 Availability

Starting in November, school districts can opt to add these new terms to existing or new agreements. The process is designed so districts do not have to renegotiate their entire contracts to include these protections.
