The Binding of Isaac: Repentance Plus faces backlash over undiscovered AI music

The Binding of Isaac: Repentance Plus has spent the last few days getting review-bombed on Steam after it was discovered that a particular segment of the game used AI music. The controversy centers on a Grand Theft Auto-themed section where audio files contained Suno metadata pointing back to tracks on Suno’s site.

🔍 Discovery

Reddit user ShweetsStuff found several audio files embedded in the game with Suno metadata, directly linking them to Suno’s platform and the tracks heard during the GTA parody segment.

⚙️ Key Details

  • Review-bombing occurred on Steam following the discovery
  • Valve requires developers to disclose how and when they use generative AI in the development process
  • Nicalis, Inc., the studio behind Repentance Plus, did not disclose the AI music usage anywhere
  • Creator Edmund McMillen claimed he “had no idea” AI music was used in the latest version
  • McMillen took to X to apologize for the inclusion
  • Within hours of the discovery, Nicalis shared on X that Repentance Plus had been updated and the generated music files had been removed
  • Reviews have yet to rebound; the game remains rated as “overwhelmingly negative” on Steam
  • Users on X appear to be giving McMillen the benefit of the doubt, likely because he has been outspoken on AI in game development, calling it “uninspired and bland” in a TikTok from 2024
