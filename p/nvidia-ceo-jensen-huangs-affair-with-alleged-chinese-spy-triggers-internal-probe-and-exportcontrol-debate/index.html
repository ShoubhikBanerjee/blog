Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s affair with alleged Chinese spy triggers internal probe and export‑control debate

Jensen Huang, co‑founder and chief executive of Nvidia, has acknowledged an affair with Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore‑based cloud provider Megaspeed who U.S. officials allege is a Chinese intelligence asset. Nvidia has opened an internal investigation, and the relationship has intensified scrutiny of recent lobbying for looser U.S. export controls on advanced AI chips.

🔍 Overview

  • Jensen Huang admitted he was “unfaithful” to his wife; the company announced an investigation into his conduct.
  • U.S. officials view Alice Huang as a foreign intelligence asset feeding information to the Chinese government.
  • The affair coincides with Nvidia’s aggressive push to sell advanced chips to China, including lobbying President Donald Trump for looser export restrictions.

📅 Timeline of Events

  • June 2024: Photos show Jensen Huang and Alice Huang with other AI executives at a bar in Taipei, just before Megaspeed became a Nvidia customer.
  • May 2025: Huang was spotted at a Taiwan dinner with Alice Huang, shortly after Nvidia shared the Trump administration’s new restrictions on its H20 chips (April).
  • July 2025: Huang met Trump in the Oval Office to plead for loosening export restrictions on Nvidia’s advanced semiconductors.
  • Days later (July 2025): Nvidia announced the administration had blessed sales of its H20 chips to China.
  • October 2025: Huang lobbied Trump to allow Nvidia to sell more advanced chips to China ahead of Trump’s meeting with Chinese President Xi; Trump ultimately did not move forward.
  • December 2025: Trump allowed Nvidia to sell H20 chips to a “approved customers” list in China, with the U.S. government taking a 25 % cut.
  • Early 2026: Nvidia shares are down almost five percent; the company opened an internal investigation and Huang sent a memo to employees acknowledging the affair.

🛠️ Nvidia’s Position in U.S. AI

  • “It’s difficult to overstate Nvidia’s power in today’s AI landscape.”
  • “The company’s chips are the bedrock upon which advanced AI technology in the US is being built, and demand has consistently outpaced supply for years.”
  • “Nvidia has also been an influential investor in a wide range of up‑and‑coming AI companies … with about $99 billion worth of equity investments in frontier AI labs, neoclouds, infrastructure companies, and more.”
  • “Its chief executive has even become close with President Donald Trump.”

⚖️ Security and Legal Investigations

  • U.S. officials believe the Megaspeed executive is a foreign intelligence asset.
  • Alice Huang is described as an “alleged honeypot who was recruited by the Chinese Ministry of State Security.”
  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio helped draft a memo about the relationship before Trump’s May China visit.
  • Trump was briefed roughly a year earlier on intelligence regarding the relationship but did not act at the time.
  • Nvidia spokespeople initially said the relationship was “strictly business” and “not unusual or unexpected,” but later acknowledged it became personal.
  • The FBI is reportedly looking into the matter.

📉 Market Reaction

  • “Nvidia shares are, as of this writing, down almost five percent.”
  • The investigation and media coverage have kept the stock under pressure despite Nvidia’s $4 trillion‑plus market cap.
