Snap Launches Specs Intelligence Anticipatory AI Service

Snap is introducing Specs Intelligence, an anticipatory AI service designed to help users manage daily tasks and long-term goals by connecting to various digital accounts.

🔍 Overview

Specs Intelligence builds an understanding of a user’s routines, relationships, priorities, and goals based on connected apps and tools. It is designed to:

  • Carry personal context across different devices.
  • Deepen its understanding over time.
  • Anticipate when help is useful and surface information and actions without a prompt.

🧩 Capabilities

The service can integrate with apps such as Gmail and Slack to provide proactive assistance:

ScenarioAI Action
Before a meetingHighlight questions to ask, decisions to make, and things to remember
Upcoming tripAggregate flight, hotel, and dinner plans while flagging work deadlines during the visit
Daily briefingPull personal content and place it “directly into view” when using Specs AR glasses

⚙️ Key details

According to spokesperson Cassie Bumgarner, Specs Intelligence uses “a proprietary combination of open-source models hosted in the US alongside local LLMs.” Snap stated that the mixture of models is regularly updated to iterate toward the best version of the service.

Regarding privacy, Snap states it cannot view or access personal content used with the service. “Personal content from connected accounts will not be used to train or fine-tune Snap’s AI models, or to serve personalized ads.”

🚀 Availability

Specs Intelligence is launched alongside Specs, Snap’s first consumer augmented reality glasses. It is currently available in the following capacities:

  • iOS: Available in preview.
  • Mac: A waitlist is available for the “full early-access experience.”
