California Governor Gavin Newsom Issues Executive Order on AI Safety and Oversight

California Governor Gavin Newsom has issued an executive order to convene experts to address AI safety issues, positioning the state to lead AI oversight amid a lack of federal legislative action.

⚙️ Key details

Governor Newsom’s order directs a group of experts to deliver recommendations within two months on strengthening AI safety measures in state law. The governor has called on Congress and Donald Trump to “review and adopt the state’s framework” or use it as a “floor, not a ceiling.”

Recommendations for the expert group to consider include:

  • Requiring AI companies to embed independent verification groups onsite for regular audits.
  • Subjecting risk assessments and transparency reports to standards of independent auditors.
  • Creating a “kill switch” for frontier models that is routinely verified as effective.
  • Requiring companies to report “loss-of-control incidents,” such as the OpenAI attack on Hugging Face, as critical safety incidents.

Additionally, the order directs a state agency to accelerate the implementation of two previously signed laws:

  • A state registry of AI auditors.
  • A framework for independent verifiers to assess AI safety.

💡 Why it matters

Governor Newsom stated, “The federal government’s abject failure to create any form of meaningful AI oversight or accountability should alarm every American, especially when AI CEOs themselves are begging for regulation.”

This movement toward state-level action follows warnings from former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon, who posted that there is a more than 10 percent chance AI “could kill all humans” within a decade. While there have been bill discussions in Congress, substantive legislation is unlikely before the midterms due to the House being on recess until November and impending elections affecting the Senate. President Donald Trump has stated, “The only control or ‘guardrails’ that AI needs is a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT, and the U.S.A. has that, in spades!”

Newsom has also floated the possibility of calling California lawmakers in for a special legislative session on AI.
