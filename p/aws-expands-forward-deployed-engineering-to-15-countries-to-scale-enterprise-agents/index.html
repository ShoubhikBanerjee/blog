AWS Expands Forward Deployed Engineering to 15 Countries to Scale Enterprise Agents

AWS has expanded its AWS Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) service to 15 countries, providing commercial and public sector customers with built-in, production-ready accelerators to deploy secure, governed agentic AI. Launched in June, FDE helps organizations establish the necessary foundations—including identity, security, context, governance, and operational controls—required to scale agents across high-volume, rule-heavy processes.

🚀 Global Expansion

AWS FDE is now available for commercial and public sector customers in 15 countries:

  • Mexico

🔍 Overcoming Foundational AI Gaps

Before an AI agent can perform useful work at scale, it requires a secure foundation. Many organizations struggle because their data is spread across different systems without a common ontology—the shared model of how the business works. Without this foundation, the cost of consecutive pilots remains high. AWS FDE addresses these challenges by ensuring each agent receives its own distinct identity, permissions, and audit trail, separate from human identity.

⚙️ Standardizing Work with AWS Quick

AWS Quick provides an AI workspace built for work, allowing teams to share dashboards, agents, and automations. Designed with enterprise-grade security, it connects to everyday systems while offering:

  • Tenant-level data isolation to separate distinct user populations or competing dealers.
  • Role-based access controls to enforce fine-grained permissions by business function.
  • Structured approval workflows for agentic actions and protection of confidential information.
  • Automated audit frameworks to prove compliance to regulators and internal standards.
