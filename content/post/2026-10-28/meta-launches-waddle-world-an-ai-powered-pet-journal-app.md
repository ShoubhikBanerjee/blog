# Meta launches Waddle World, an AI‑powered pet journal app

Meta has quietly released a new app called **Waddle World**, described as a tamagotchi‑style pet that chats with users and doubles as a daily journal.

## 🔍 Overview
- Marketed as a “16‑bit pet care game” that is actually “a daily journal disguised as a pet.”
- Features virtual creatures that **chat** with users and **create doodles** to illustrate conversations.

## 🧩 How it works
- Users are encouraged to **talk, not type**, with their pets.
- The app offers “cozy games” such as **stacking groceries** or **word games**.
- The description emphasizes **no pressure, no streaks, no scores** – a warm little escape.

## ⚙️ Key features
- Conversational virtual pet that generates doodles.
- Daily‑journal functionality hidden behind pet‑care gameplay.
- Free to download.
- 16‑bit pixel‑art aesthetic (a “cozy 16‑bit world”).
- “Cozy games” like grocery‑stacking and word puzzles.

## 🚀 Availability
- Listed as free and **only available to adults in the Philippines**.
- Meta also quietly launched an AI fashion app called **Style Sketch**, available only in **Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand**.
- Meta has not confirmed whether either app will appear in the United States or other markets.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The launch aligns with Meta’s broader push of a **sweet mascot** and small **Charm gadgets** for its Muse AI agent, indicating a focus on light‑hearted, conversational AI experiences.
