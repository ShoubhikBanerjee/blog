# xAI Offers Southaven Home Buyouts Conditional on Release From Noise Lawsuit

An Elon Musk xAI subsidiary, MZX, is offering to purchase homes in a Southaven, Mississippi neighborhood on the condition that residents agree not to join an active lawsuit targeting the company's nearby data center and power plant.

## 🔍 Buyout Terms and Resident Impact

According to recent reports and official letters sent to local households, xAI and its real estate broker are seeking to acquire property around its Southaven operations in exchange for legal releases:

* **Scope of Purchases:** A realtor going door-to-door reported that xAI plans to buy 418 homes, with 18 homeowners already planning to sell.
* **Renter Displacement:** Local renters report being forced out with 60 days' notice after their landlords agreed to sell.
* **Legal Release Clause:** To accept MZX's purchase offer, property owners must sign a release agreement waiving all claims related to the Southaven plant, including the *Haley* lawsuit. Signing prevents owners from bringing independent claims or recovering damages.
* **Company Defense:** MZX denies all allegations made against it, stating it requires the property for business purposes.

## ⚙️ Regional Facilities and Resident Noise Complaints

The buyout offers stem from environmental concerns raised by neighboring residents:

| Facility / Location | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Colossus (Memphis, TN) | Two sprawling data center facilities constructed over the past couple of years. |
| Macrohardrr (Southaven, MS) | Data center and accompanying power plant featuring gas-fired turbines. |

Three residents filed the *Haley* lawsuit accusing the Southaven data center and its gas-fired turbines of emitting near-constant noise and vibrations.

## ⚖️ Regulatory and Legal Pressures

xAI's regional expansion faces several additional legal actions and regulatory hurdles:

* **NAACP Lawsuit:** The NAACP has filed legal action accusing xAI of operating dozens of unpermitted methane gas turbines in Southaven. A federal judge blocked an attempt by the Trump administration to dismiss the lawsuit earlier this month.
* **Fines and Unpaid Bills:** SpaceXAI recently reported facing up to $6.1 million in potential civil penalties for environmental law violations, as well as over $220 million in claims of unpaid bills for work at the Southaven and Memphis sites.
* **Construction Moratorium:** Facing growing opposition to AI data centers ahead of midterm elections, the Memphis City Council voted down a one-year moratorium and instead passed an ordinance pausing construction for 135 days.
