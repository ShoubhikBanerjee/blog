# AWS Amazon Connect Customer Named Leader in 2026 Gartner CCaaS Magic Quadrant

Amazon Web Services (AWS) has been named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) for Amazon Connect for the fourth consecutive year.

## 📊 Recognition
- “I’m proud to share that Amazon Web Services (AWS) has been named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) for Amazon Connect for the fourth consecutive year.”
- “I’m proud of the Connect Customer team for receiving this recognition for the fourth year in a row.”
- “Read the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service: Gartner, Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service, 19 October 2026, Drew Kraus Et Al.”
- “Gartner does not endorse any company, vendor, product or service depicted in its publications….”

## 🧩 How it works
- “When we launched Amazon Connect Customer, we set out to evolve how businesses serve their customers: to make personalized, intelligent experiences possible without giving up efficiency, flexibility, or control.”
- “Connect Customer builds a living picture of every customer: who they are, what they’ve been through, what they need next.”
- “Every touchpoint draws on that intelligence.”
- “Meeting that takes more than traditional contact center technology, it takes a comprehensive solution that brings together customer context, AI, and the flexibility to adapt.”

## ⚙️ Key capabilities
- “Most of the industry is still optimizing for deflection: getting customers away from humans as fast as possible.”
- “We took a different path.”
- “Connect Customer is built for the hard things: the disputed claim, the renegotiated mortgage, the disrupted itinerary that needs real action and can’t go wrong.”
- “We carry those interactions all the way through to resolution, and we make them feel natural and personal.”
- “Generative and agentic AI push this further: understanding what a customer needs and acting on it.”
- “Every action is auditable.”
- “Humans step in when the moment calls for it.”
- “You get enterprise scale and the confidence that nothing slips through the cracks.”

## 💡 Why it matters
- “People expect you to recognize them, understand what they need, and stay consistent across every channel.”
- “They don’t want to repeat themselves.”
- “They don’t want to navigate disconnected systems to solve a simple problem.”
- “They want interactions that feel natural, relevant, and genuinely helpful.”
- “The companies that win the next decade of customer experience won’t be the ones with the most channels or the fastest bot.”
- “Connect Customer exists to make that real for businesses of every size.”
- “Our engineers, product teams, partners, and customers shaped what we’ve built.”
