# AI‑Driven Living Digital Twins Bring Real‑Time Autonomy to Factories

Living digital twins infused with AI are moving from off‑line simulation to real‑time, agentic systems that can actively control industrial plants.

## 🌱 Overview
Living digital twins are helping industrial plants run more like living systems, and setting the stage for industries that work in sync. While most people picture industrial environments as cold steel and clockwork machinery, functionally a plant is more like a living thing: it has a pulse and a metabolism, ages, and shows barely perceptible irregularities as it wears.

## 🧩 How It Works
- Digital twin technology has long aimed to mirror that dynamism and model anomalies that may be symptomatic of larger problems.
- Early implementations lagged a few beats behind reality, focusing on simulation, observation, and what‑if scenario planning rather than closed‑loop execution.
- A *living* digital twin combines a dynamic, high‑fidelity digital representation of complex operations **with the capacity to intervene** in the physical world through operations and control systems.
- AI is at the center of this change.
- The twin links engineering and design information with real‑time operational data through a **digital thread** – a connected record that follows an asset through its lifecycle.
- Affordable sensors now stream telemetry from IoT devices and control equipment; software platforms present the data in user‑friendly interfaces.
- Predictive and physics‑based models provide AI‑enriched insights into how a machine or process might behave.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Adaptive, evergreen models that keep pace with live conditions and connect previously isolated systems, offering a layer of visibility and orchestration that reflects the organic rhythms of the facility.
- Agentic AI enables the system to move beyond diagnostics or suggestions and act of its own accord.
- The technology is developing a kind of industrial homeostasis, where small, constant adjustments keep a plant healthy and humming.
- Human judgment and operator oversight remain essential for defining what “healthy” looks like and flagging AI misalignment.
- Living digital twins give engineers the means to monitor AI actions and ensure execution stays on track.
- "You can look at the digital twin through the lens of sustainability: What are my emissions?" says Rob McGreevy.
- "Or, through the lens of maintenance: Is this asset performing in a healthy way? Or, through a performance metric: Is it producing the number of widgets I need?"
