# Texas AI Data Centers Spark Political Pushback and Energy Concerns

Texas is experiencing a surge of AI‑focused data center construction that has ignited political battles, tax‑exemption controversy, and rising energy‑use concerns.

## 📈 Growth of AI Data Centers
- “Nowhere in the world are data centers growing more explosively than in Texas.”
- “The state passed a sales and use tax exemption for data centers in 2013 under former Gov. Rick Perry.”
- “In 2015, Abbott expanded the exemption to include hyperscale data centers, which can span millions of square feet and house tens of thousands of GPUs.”
- “As a result, qualifying data center developers don’t have to pay state taxes on equipment and electricity for up to 20 years.”

## ⚖️ Political Backlash
- “There’s the fight in Taylor over plans to build a data center at a site that’s supposed to be a park.”
- “Temple, around 40 miles away, is another sore spot, Bledsoe says.”
- “The mayor and City Council members there face recall attempts over data center developments.”
- “And some 30 miles outside of Austin, San Marcos became the first city in all of Texas to ban data centers.”
- “The state’s NAACP recently joined dozens of other groups across the political spectrum to form a coalition to stop — or at least stall — data center construction.”
- “Together, Bledsoe estimates the coalition — named We the People Texas — represents as many as 1 million voters across the state.”
- “A majority of Texans — 57 percent — oppose the construction of data centers, an August poll found.”
- “That included 43 percent of Republicans, 75 percent of Democrats, and 54 percent of independents.”
- “Save Rural Texas came together this year after a local county commissioners’ meeting about plans to build a new data center in the Texoma area that borders Oklahoma.”
- “Residents, including Parker, successfully delayed that project.”
- “That victory led Parker, a corporate coach and speaker, to write up a handbook for other communities trying to block data center projects, and she now spends much of her time traveling around the state to train grassroots groups.”

## 💰 Financial Impact
- “The tax exemption was initially expected to cost taxpayers $14.6 million in 2014 and 2015.”
- “But in 2025, state officials estimated that the tax break would lead to $3.2 billion in lost sales tax revenue over two years.”

## ⚡ Energy and Environmental Concerns
- “Electricity bills have grown by 35.4 percent on average over the past five years as demand rises and utilities build expensive new infrastructure to meet that demand.”
- “Data center electricity demand could erase decades of air quality gains in Texas, one recent study found.”
- “Data center proposals have driven a surge of gas and nuclear power projects, both of which need a lot of water for their own cooling systems.”
- “Data center electricity demand could increase nearly tenfold by 2030, the state’s largest grid operator estimates.”

## 🗳️ Electoral Stakes
- “After initially proclaiming his state the “epicenter of AI development,” Texas Gov. Greg Abbott is now fighting for reelection against underdog Democrat Gina Hinojosa, who is seen as having a rare shot at taking a reliably red state.”
- “The state’s high‑profile Senate race is no less of a nail‑biter, with Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton running neck and neck against Democrat James Talarico.”
