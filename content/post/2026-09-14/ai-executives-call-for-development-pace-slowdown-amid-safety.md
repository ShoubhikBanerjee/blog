# AI Executives Call for Development Pace Slowdown Amid Safety Concerns

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has published an open letter calling for the industry to "pace the frontier" and slow down AI development. This proposal has received public support from OpenAI's Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and tentative support from Alphabet's Demis Hassabis.

## 💡 Why it matters
Internal confidence regarding AI safety is deteriorating while external capability announcements accelerate. Recent evidence includes:

* A former Anthropic researcher characterized internal safety work as a "mini Manhattan project" and warned that labs have "just a few years left to make their systems safe."
* Wired reported that "agentic swarms" are "spooking people inside big labs."
* Anthropic disclosed that its Threat Intelligence team detected eight months of Claude misuse by threat actors, including three incidents where models gained unauthorized access to real computer systems.

## ⚙️ Key details
There is a widening gap between frontier AI capability claims and demonstrated safety. While some executives advocate for a slowdown, US political figures have expressed opposition:

* Donald Trump stated, "Look, we’re leading China in AI ... and, frankly, I want to keep it that way, because whoever wins AI, wins."
* House Speaker Mike Johnson told CNN that rushing to place restrictions on AI development could be a "national security threat," arguing that emergency regulation could lead to losing the race to China.

## 🔍 Overview
OpenAI is reportedly exploring whether an AI industry slowdown would be legal, signaling internal debate regarding the pace of development. This occurs as the US regulatory posture remains anchored in voluntary standards, such as the NIST comment deadlines on September 16 and October 6, 2026.
