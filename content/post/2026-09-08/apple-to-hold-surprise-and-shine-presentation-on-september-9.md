# Apple to hold Surprise and shine presentation on September 9, 2026

Apple has announced its annual autumn presentation, titled "Surprise and shine," scheduled for September 9, 2026. This event marks the first major presentation under the leadership of new CEO John Turnus. The broadcast will begin at 10:00 Pacific Time and will be available via the Apple website, Apple TV, and YouTube.

## 🔍 Overview
The event is expected to prioritize premium devices, with a focus on high-end hardware. Anticipated announcements include:
- The first foldable iPhone
- iPhone 18 Pro
- iPhone 18 Pro Max

## ⚙️ Key details
The foldable iPhone is expected to feature a clamshell design, offering a standard external display that unfolds into a compact tablet. Reports indicate the external OLED display will measure approximately 5.5 inches.

| Device | Feature | Expected Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Foldable iPhone | Design | Clamshell, tablet-sized internal screen |
| iPhone 18 Pro/Max | Camera | Possible variable aperture |
| Software | Siri | New generation powered by Apple Intelligence |

Additional expectations for the new iPhone lineup include updated processors, improved battery life, camera upgrades, and further reduction of the elements surrounding the Dynamic Island.

## 🚀 Availability
Following the focus on premium devices at this autumn event, more affordable models are expected to be carried over to a spring 2027 launch. Analysts suggest the foldable iPhone may be priced between $2,000 and $2,500 depending on the configuration. Long-term projections suggest this category could generate tens of billions of dollars in revenue by the end of 2027.
