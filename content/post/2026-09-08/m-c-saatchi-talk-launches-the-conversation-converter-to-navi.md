# M+C Saatchi Talk Launches The Conversation Converter to Navigate AI Search

To mark its 25th anniversary, communications agency M+C Saatchi Talk has launched The Conversation Converter, a new strategic framework that brings together earned media, search, content, and AI discoverability. Managing director Amaya Alvarez states that this framework addresses a major shift in communications: while traditional search helps people find information, AI increasingly digests that information and provides answers directly.

## 🔍 Overview

AI is increasingly acting as a new gatekeeper, synthesizing information about a brand before a consumer ever reaches its official website, social feeds, or product pages. This shift is highlighted by key search data:

* Around 26% of searches showing an AI summary end without another click, compared with 16% where only traditional results appear, according to figures collated by Semrush from Pew Research.
* 85% of brand mentions in AI search originate from third-party pages, according to analysis by AirOps of more than a billion citations cited by AuthorityTech.

## 🧩 How it works

The Conversation Converter framework is designed to help brands navigate this new landscape through a structured process:

* **Audience Analysis**: It starts by looking at the specific questions people are asking around a brand or category.
* **Authority Mapping**: It identifies the publishers, communities, and voices that carry particular authority around those questions.
* **Story Creation**: M+C Saatchi Talk uses this mapping to inform the stories it creates. Rather than relying on traditional media hierarchies, brands develop their own unique "media DNA" based on relevant sources in their category.
* **Discoverability Measurement**: The agency monitors changes in brand discoverability over time rather than focusing on direct attribution.

## 💡 Why it matters

This framework changes both what brand stories need to say and how communications success is measured. Instead of attempting to claim direct attribution, the framework tracks indicators such as where conversations appear, whether the sources are authoritative, and how a brand turns up in search and AI-generated answers.

According to Alvarez, this shift is also changing agency-client dynamics, leading to direct conversations with CMOs and other senior leaders about using communications for long-term brand building.
