# Adam’s GTM Report 2.0 Launches to Track Shifting AI and Market Trends

Adam’s GTM Report 2.0 has officially launched, delivering a new market tracker that details trending topics, market moves, and an updated GTM Tech Index. Built by measuring a monthly conversation panel of over 20,000 AI x GTM posts, the tracker analyzes 123 topics grouped into 24 curated clusters along with 44 brand topics to capture significant shifts in the go-to-market landscape.

## 🔍 August Market Activity
The market tracker logged 47 GTM-relevant moves during the month of August, including:
* 30 product launches
* 8 acquisitions
* 5 funding deals
* 4 strategic moves

## 📈 Shifting Conversation Dynamics
The tracker highlights notable changes in discussion volumes from earlier in the year through August:
* **SaaSpocalypse:** Conversation peaked in February and fell 68% by August.
* **Headless & Agent Interfaces:** Conversation more than doubled from January to August.
* **Signal-Based Sales & ABM:** Declined from 8.3% of tracked topic conversation in January to 4.1% in August.
* **Signal-Based Sales (narrow topic):** Fell from 6.8% of tracked conversation in January to 3.1% in August.

Since the end of June, six signal-based vendors have been sold:
* Common Room (acquired by Zoom)
* Warmly (acquired by HubSpot)
* Seam AI (acquired by Clarify)
* Ciro
* Scout AI
* Commsor

## 🤝 Corporate Integrations and Financials
Developments in major integrations show shifting user behaviors and corporate performances:
* **Salesforce and Anthropic:** Salesforce reported $11.3B in quarterly revenue and 14% cRPO growth. Together with Anthropic, they launched Claudeforce, making Salesforce data and actions available inside Claude. For one team, this integration resulted in a 10× increase in usage, a 40% higher bill, and almost completely stopped team members from logging into Salesforce.
* **Clay:** The company published an AI Writing Policy. Varun Anand's post about the policy generated 8,511 engagements, which was 12.3× his usual response rate and outperformed the posts for Clay's two main August product launches, Workflows and Topic Intent. Clay appears only in the Orchestrators cluster of the GTM Tech Index, while Profound appears only in AEO.

## 🛡️ AI Slop and Provenance
As conversation patterns evolve, quality controls and trust mechanisms have gained momentum:
* **AI Slop:** The broader AI Slop conversation fell from 2.0% of tracked topic conversation in July to 1.8% in August. Additionally, LinkedIn added a "seems like AI slop" report button at the end of July.
* **Watermarking & Content Provenance:** Conversation regarding watermarking and content provenance jumped nearly 14×, rising from 0.07% in July to 0.95% in August. Anthropic announced text watermarking for future Claude models in August.
* **Proof of Human:** Conversation surrounding Proof of Human rose 176%.
