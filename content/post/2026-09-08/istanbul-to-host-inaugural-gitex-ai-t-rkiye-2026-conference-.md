# Istanbul to Host Inaugural GITEX AI Türkiye 2026 Conference in September

The Investment and Finance Office of the Presidency of Türkiye has announced that Istanbul will host the inaugural GITEX AI Türkiye 2026 conference from September 9-10 at the Istanbul Expo Center. The event aims to establish Istanbul as a regional hub for artificial intelligence innovation by connecting local startups with international investors.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Attendees: Over 300 companies and startups, alongside public representatives and technology executives.
- Investor Participation: More than 100 global investors from 20 countries managing over $100 billion in assets.
- Hosting: Hosted by the Investment and Finance Office with a strategic partnership from the Ministry of Industry and Technology.
- Communication Partner: Anadolu Agency.

## 💡 Why it Matters
- The conference serves as a platform to connect emerging artificial intelligence ventures with established tech giants and international funding sources.
- The program focuses on evaluating innovations in sectors including sustainable agriculture, AI-driven education, enterprise technologies, healthcare, and infrastructure.
