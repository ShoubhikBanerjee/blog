# Bidgely to Demonstrate AI-Powered Smart Meter Analytics at Enlit Asia 2026

Bidgely will showcase its behind-the-meter AI solutions at Enlit Asia 2026, taking place from 22–24 September in Jakarta. The company aims to demonstrate how its technology unlocks the potential of smart meter data to drive measurable impacts across utility operations, including customer experience, grid planning, and revenue protection.

## 🔍 Overview

At the event, Bidgely will focus on how energy leaders can extract value from Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) data. The company's technical presentations and panel sessions will highlight the use of AI and machine learning big-data analytics to transform interval data into concrete outcomes for utilities and their customers.

## ⚙️ Key details

Bidgely’s UtilityAI™ platform offers several technical and operational capabilities:

*   **Scale and Reach**: The platform currently serves over 50 million homes globally and leverages more than 19 foundational patents.
*   **Deployment Flexibility**: Solutions can be deployed as fully managed SaaS or within existing cloud ecosystems such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, Snowflake, and Databricks.
*   **Integrations**: The technology integrates with horizontal AI ecosystems including Microsoft Copilot and AWS.
*   **Asset Visibility**: The platform provides premises-level accuracy and visibility for assets, including batteries.

## 🧩 How it works

Bidgely acts as a pioneer in energy intelligence by converting raw meter data into high-definition insights. The company will deliver three technical presentations at the event covering specific methodologies:

| Focus Area | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Data Science Algorithms | Used for asset visibility, including batteries, at the grid level. |
| Forecasting | Feeder level forecasting to improve grid management. |
| Distributed Intelligence | Enhancing grid flexibility and resilience through localized data processing. |

## 💡 Why it matters

As utilities across Southeast Asia complete rapid AMI rollouts, they face the challenge of extracting value from the resulting stream of operational data. According to Nipun Jain, VP (EMEA & APAC) at Bidgely, using AI/ML based analytics helps energy leaders "remove bill shock, eliminate non-technical losses and build a resilient grid for the future." This approach allows utilities to accelerate AI adoption while leveraging their existing technology investments and meeting specific security or data governance requirements.
