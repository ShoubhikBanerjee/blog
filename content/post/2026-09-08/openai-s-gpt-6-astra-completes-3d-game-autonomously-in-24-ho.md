# OpenAI's GPT‑6 Astra Completes 3D Game Autonomously in 24 Hours

OpenAI’s latest model, GPT‑6 Astra, demonstrated a notable milestone by navigating a full 3D game from start to finish without any human intervention, completing the task in just 24 hours.

## 🔍 Overview
- GPT‑6 Astra achieved autonomous completion of a complex 3D game environment.
- The run was performed without any human‑in‑the‑loop control.

## 🧩 How it works
- The model was evaluated on the ARC‑AGI‑3 benchmark, where it operated more efficiently than an average human.
- Despite the efficiency gains, the model showed an 8.5 % failure rate when faced with hidden prompt injection attacks.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Task duration:** 24 hours for full game navigation.
- **Benchmark performance:** Higher efficiency than the average human on ARC‑AGI‑3.
- **Robustness:** 8.5 % failure against hidden prompt injections, indicating areas for safety improvement.
