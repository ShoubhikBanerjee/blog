# Europol Reports AI Tools Used by Terrorist Networks for Recruitment and Attack Planning

European law enforcement agencies have identified a growing trend of terrorist groups utilizing AI technologies to facilitate propaganda, recruitment, and attack preparation. According to Europol, digital environments now account for 85% of investigations into these criminal activities.

## 🧩 How it works
Criminal networks are increasingly leveraging artificial intelligence to amplify the reach and impact of their operations. Current applications include:

*   AI-powered bots to boost recruitment and promote terrorist ideologies.
*   AI-driven apps used for research to prepare for attacks.
*   Generation and manipulation of content using deepfakes, memes, and GIFs to create and edit propaganda.

## ⚙️ Key details
Authorities have noted that AI functions as a force multiplier for violent extremist content. According to statements regarding the impact of these tools, manuals, tactics, and propaganda are being significantly amplified by AI integration. Efforts to combat these digital threats include campaigns to disrupt online communities, such as "The COM," where thousands of web addresses have been identified by police investigators across nine European countries.
