# Hefring Marine Selected for NATO DIANA Mission Track to Deploy Maritime AI

Icelandic tech company Hefring Marine has been selected as one of only 15 companies to advance to the Mission Track of NATO's Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic (DIANA). Chosen from a 2026 cohort of 150 innovators out of more than 3,600 proposals, Hefring Marine is the first and only Icelandic company to reach this stage of the programme, marking a major step toward deploying its maritime intelligence technology across NATO and Allied forces.

## 🔍 Overview

The Mission Track is the follow-on element of the DIANA programme, designed to support technologies that have demonstrated strong defense interest and alignment with Allied operational needs. Selected participants receive:
* Up to €300,000 in non-dilutive funding
* Continued access to NATO test centers
* Tailored support to help transition technologies toward operational deployment

## 🧩 How it works

Hefring Marine's solutions focus on connecting vessel data and providing real-time intelligence at sea:

| Technology | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ODIN (Observation and Domain Awareness Network)** | A maritime intelligence system designed to connect data from navigation and object-detection systems across existing vessels. It combines information across multiple platforms to provide a persistent, networked view of activity at sea to support intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) operations. |
| **IMAS** | Hefring's Marine Decision Intelligence platform, which uses edge AI to provide real-time decision support aboard crewed and uncrewed vessels. ODIN builds directly upon this technology. |

## ⚙️ Key details

The selection follows a period of increasing collaboration between Hefring and Allied defense organizations:
* **Headquarters Demonstrations:** In August, Hefring hosted NATO Supreme Allied Commander Europe General Alexus G. Grynkewich, U.S. Permanent Representative to NATO Matthew Whitaker, and U.S. Ambassador to Iceland Billy Long at its Reykjavík headquarters for demonstrations of IMAS and ODIN. Hefring also recently welcomed Clara Ganslandt, Ambassador of the European Union to Iceland, to discuss the role of European maritime technology in safety and security.
* **Field Testing:** Members of the Hefring team are currently participating in REPMUS 2026 in Portugal, an operational experimentation exercise for maritime robotics and uncrewed systems, allowing them to test and demonstrate the technology alongside potential end users.

## 💡 Why it matters

"Mission Track is important because with ODIN validated in real-time operations and exercises, the focus now moves decisively towards adoption," said Karl Birgir Björnsson, CEO and co-founder of Hefring Marine. "Our priority now is to get these capabilities into the hands of operators, validate them in demanding environments and work through the practical steps needed to deploy them across existing Allied fleets. For a company that started in Reykjavík solving very practical problems aboard small vessels, the opportunity to contribute to maritime security at Alliance level is a significant milestone."
