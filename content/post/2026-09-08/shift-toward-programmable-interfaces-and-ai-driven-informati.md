# Shift Toward Programmable Interfaces and AI-Driven Information Interpretation

Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly using code to interact with the world, as technical progress in coding models has shown astounding growth over the last year. This shift toward a more programmable environment allows AI systems to manage, manipulate, and interpret information, addressing the bottleneck of limited human attention.

## 🔍 Overview
* Code is becoming the primary way LLMs interact with external systems because it is regular, text-based, and checkable.
* While tokens are the native language of LLMs, code functions as a comfortable medium for these models to create instructions on the fly.
* Corporate and consumer systems are expected to open up to being programmed safely to facilitate these AI interactions.

## 🧩 How it works
* LLMs can interact with human interfaces, but they are more effective when using interfaces designed for code.
* The tech ecosystem is moving toward being code-friendly and AI-friendly to meet the demand for user convenience.
* When tasked with complex data, such as reconciling nearly 200 GitHub repositories, models can consolidate information into organized lists and provide detailed explanations.

## ⚙️ Key details
* Human attention is a scarce resource, making it difficult to integrate vast amounts of information into understanding.
* Understanding remains a bottleneck that cannot be easily outsourced, even if the thinking process is handled by a machine.
* AI systems are transitioning into roles where they decide what information surfaces to the human user, similar to how search engines select websites.

## 💡 Why it matters
* Users naturally choose the easiest path to solve problems, which drives the adoption of AI-managed systems.
* Because humans lack the time to process all available data, they will increasingly rely on computers to curate what they pay attention to and think about.
* The internet is evolving from a system that connects people to information into a system that finds, manages, and interprets information on their behalf.
