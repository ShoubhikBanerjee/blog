# Z.ai Releases GLM-5.3 Weights with New Security Review and License Condition

Z.ai has made the weight files for its GLM-5.3 model publicly available, adding a licensing condition that was not anticipated by the community. The model, a mixture‑of‑experts architecture with roughly 743 billion parameters and a 1 million token context window, has been the top performer on the CyberGym security benchmark since its API launch on August 14.

Two weeks after the initial release, the weights were uploaded to Hugging Face in both BF16 and FP8 formats. They are compatible with vLLM, SGLang, KTransformers, and the Hugging Face Transformers library. Under the custom GLM-5.3 License, any company that records more than $10 billion in aggregate revenue over any twelve‑month period must obtain approval through Z.ai’s security review before using the model or any derivative for commercial purposes.

Z.ai explained that the two‑week delay in publishing the weights resulted from what it described as its most extensive risk review to date. The review uncovered 2,436 findings as part of an ongoing security programme that began during the GLM‑5.2 era and involved multiple security teams and evaluation harnesses. The added licensing requirement ties commercial use to the outcome of this security review.

The release demonstrates Z.ai’s continued emphasis on security assessment while providing broader access to a leading large‑scale language model.
