# Texas freezes spending on Flock AI surveillance cameras

Texas Governor Greg Abbott has suspended state spending on Flock's AI surveillance cameras. The move came ahead of a Texas Tribune investigation that revealed the state had spent over $30 million on the devices. The funding was primarily collected through a $1 fee added to insurance policies, which was supposedly intended to help combat catalytic converter theft.
