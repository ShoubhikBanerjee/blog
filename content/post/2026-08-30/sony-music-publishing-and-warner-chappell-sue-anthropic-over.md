# Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over Copyrighted Works

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell have filed a lawsuit against AI company Anthropic in the US District Court for the Northern District of California, alleging unauthorized use of copyrighted materials. The publishers characterize the incident as one of the largest and most blatant ongoing thefts of intellectual property in history.

## ⚙️ Key details

* The suit seeks damages for tens of thousands of copyrighted works.
* Plaintiffs are asking for up to $150,000 per work, plus up to $25,000 for each instance where copyright data was allegedly stripped.
* Total potential damages could reach several billion dollars if the court awards the maximum amount.
* The complaint names Anthropic as an entity, along with co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann as individual defendants.

## 🧩 Allegations

* The suit claims Benjamin Mann used BitTorrent to download over five million pirated books.
* Anthropic employees are alleged to have downloaded at least two million more books from the Pirate Library Mirror.
* The company is accused of scraping lyrics from licensed platforms like MusixMatch and LyricFind.
* The complaint alleges these actions were taken to develop and operate the "Claude" series of AI models.
