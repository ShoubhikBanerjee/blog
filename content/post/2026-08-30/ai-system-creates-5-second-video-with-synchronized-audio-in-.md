# AI system creates 5‑second video with synchronized audio in under three seconds

A new artificial intelligence tool is able to produce a five‑second video clip with synchronized audio in less than three seconds of processing time. The system achieved the top ranking for image‑to‑video with audio generation on the Artificial Analysis benchmark.

The result highlights rapid content creation potential for applications that require short, audio‑aligned video outputs, such as quick previews, educational snippets, or social media content.

Overall, the development demonstrates that AI can now deliver short video content with synchronized audio at a pace that rivals real‑time generation, marking a notable step in multimedia generation performance.
