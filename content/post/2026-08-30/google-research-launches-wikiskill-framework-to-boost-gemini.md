# Google Research launches WikiSkill framework to boost Gemini 3.5 Flash performance

Google Research introduced WikiSkill, a framework intended to help AI agents retain practical lessons from earlier attempts without retraining the underlying model.

In tests across five benchmarks, Gemini 3.5 Flash’s average score rose from 49.5% to 68.1% when it used the system’s evolving skills.

The results indicate that a skill‑evolution approach can raise benchmark scores while keeping the base model unchanged.
