# Trump Administration Directs EPA to Limit Public Input on AI Data Centers

The Trump administration has issued a directive to the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to cease publicizing and seeking public input regarding the construction of new data center projects. This move is designed to prevent local residents from learning about or raising concerns regarding projects prior to the approval of permits and the commencement of construction.

## ⚙️ Key details

* The directive effectively ends the requirement for the EPA to notify the public about planned data center developments.
* President Donald J. Trump has characterized data centers as "big, strong, bold, and Money Machines for the State in which they are built."
* In July, the President stated that communities must be convinced of the value of data centers, noting, "You can't fight it, you have to go with it."

## 💡 Why it matters

Public opinion regarding the rapid expansion of AI-powered data centers remains largely opposed according to recent data:
