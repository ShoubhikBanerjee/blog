# AI Detects Heart Disease in Women Through Mammograms

A new study suggests that artificial intelligence (AI) can identify heart disease in women by analysing mammogram images.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI can detect heart disease in women using mammograms, study suggests.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The capability is demonstrated in a research study.
- It targets female patients.
- The analysis is performed on existing mammogram scans.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a potential method for early heart disease detection in women.
- Utilises imaging already performed for breast cancer screening, offering additional health insights without extra procedures.
