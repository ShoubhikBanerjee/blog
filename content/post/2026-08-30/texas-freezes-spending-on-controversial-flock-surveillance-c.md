# Texas freezes spending on controversial Flock surveillance cameras

Texas Governor Greg Abbott has frozen state spending on Flock AI surveillance cameras.

## 🔍 Overview
- A Texas Tribune investigation revealed the state spent **over $30 million** on Flock cameras.
- The bulk of that money was raised by adding a **$1 fee to insurance policies**, supposedly to help fight catalytic converter theft.

## ⚖️ Controversy
- Multiple officers have been placed on leave or criminally charged for misusing Flock systems, **at least six incidents in Texas alone**.
- The company faces scrutiny for:
  - Data‑sharing practices.
  - Placement of some cameras near playgrounds.
  - Employees’ access to video feeds.
  - Unsecured video feeds.
  - Attempts to avoid transparency.

## 🏛️ Political response
- **Gina Hinojosa**, the Democratic candidate for governor, criticized Abbott for expanding the use of the cameras in the state.
- **Rep. Keith Self** posted on Instagram: “From Flock cameras to kill switches, we will NOT surrender our Fourth Amendment rights to the growing surveillance state.”

## 📉 Current status
- In recent months, **cities around the country have been canceling their contracts with Flock**, including many in Texas.
- The governor’s freeze on state spending comes just ahead of the Texas Tribune investigation’s publication.
