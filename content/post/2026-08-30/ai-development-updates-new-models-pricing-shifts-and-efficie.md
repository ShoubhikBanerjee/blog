# AI Development Updates: New Models, Pricing Shifts, and Efficiency Gains

Recent months have seen significant developments in AI tools and models. Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.7, released in April, achieved a new record of 87.6% on the SWE-bench Verified benchmark. However, its performance dropped to 64.3% on the more challenging SWE-bench Pro, which includes multilingual and contamination-resistant issues. Cursor's Composer 2.5 model ranked third in Artificial Analysis's Coding Agent Index with a score of 62, operating at approximately one-tenth the per-task cost of leading models like Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5.

Pricing adjustments marked a turbulent period: Devin reduced its subscription from $500 to $20 per month, while Cursor surpassed $2 billion in annual recurring revenue earlier this year. GitHub expanded access to its Agent Mode in VS Code and JetBrains IDEs. Transcosmos implemented an internal methodology called VibeOps, reducing a 15.5 person-day project to 1.5 person-days—an 87% efficiency gain.

Agent-focused updates dominated May 2026, the busiest month for this category. Cursor 3 introduced an agent-first interface, demoting its IDE to a fallback pane. OpenAI's Codex CLI transitioned from TypeScript to Rust and launched persistent-thread Goal Mode. Google replaced Gemini CLI with Antigravity CLI, discontinuing free Pro/Ultra requests on June 18. OpenAI removed DALL-E GPT from ChatGPT’s model picker in late August, shifting image generation to ChatGPT Images and retiring its o3 reasoning model from the chat interface. Claude Sonnet 5’s promotional pricing also expired at month-end.
