# Model, Harness, and Tools Form AI Service Architecture

The model is one part of the service.
The agent harness is the rest—the scaffolding the application builds around the model.
The model supplies the reasoning, and the harness supplies the boundaries the model doesn’t have on its own.
Tools are where the harness meets your production systems, so they come with contracts.
