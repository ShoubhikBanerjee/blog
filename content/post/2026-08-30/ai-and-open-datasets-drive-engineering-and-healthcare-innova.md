# AI and Open Datasets Drive Engineering and Healthcare Innovation

Artificial intelligence is currently transforming the field of biology, moving the discipline from discovery to engineering. Parallel to this shift, open datasets are serving as a primary driver for innovation within healthcare.

Vijay Pande has stated that open datasets can accelerate the medical applications of artificial intelligence. According to Pande, these datasets facilitate progress by fostering collaboration among researchers and by improving the accuracy of models. 

However, the high cost of clinical trials remains a significant challenge for the industry. AI is now being explored as a method to optimize these clinical trial processes.
