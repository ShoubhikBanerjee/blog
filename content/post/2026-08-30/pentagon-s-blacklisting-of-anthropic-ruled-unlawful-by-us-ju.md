# Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic ruled unlawful by US judge

A US judge has ruled that the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic was unlawful. The ruling addresses a legal challenge to the defense department's decision to exclude the AI company from certain business opportunities.

The decision comes amid ongoing debates about AI regulation and government oversight of artificial intelligence companies. Anthropic, founded by former OpenAI researchers, has been a prominent player in the development of large language models and AI safety research.

The court ruling represents a notable development in the relationship between the US government and AI firms, though the specific implications for Pentagon contracting procedures remain to be seen.
