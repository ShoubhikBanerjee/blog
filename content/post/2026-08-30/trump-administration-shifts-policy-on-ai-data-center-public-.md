# Trump Administration Shifts Policy on AI Data Center Public Disclosure

The Trump administration has directed the Environmental Protection Agency to stop publicizing or seeking public input on the construction of AI data centers. This change in policy aims to prevent residents from raising concerns about or learning about proposed facilities before construction begins and permits are approved.

## ⚙️ Key Details

* President Donald J. Trump has characterized data centers as "big, strong, bold, and Money Machines for the State in which they are built."
* The administration has encouraged communities to accept these developments, with the President stating, "You can't fight it, you have to go with it."
* Data centers often require hundreds of acres of land and operate continuously, generating constant ambient noise.

## 💡 Why it matters

Recent polling indicates significant public opposition to the construction of local AI data centers:

| Organization | Poll Scope | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gallup | National (March) | 71% oppose |
| Heatmap Pro / Embold Research | National | 75% oppose |
| Franklin & Marshall College | Pennsylvania voters | 79% oppose |
