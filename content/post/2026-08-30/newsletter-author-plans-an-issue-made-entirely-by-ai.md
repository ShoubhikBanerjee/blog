# Newsletter author plans an issue made entirely by AI

An issue of a newsletter made entirely by AI is planned, according to the author of this week's edition. The author said that 100% of this week's newsletter was made by a human, but that readers will see an issue of a newsletter made entirely with AI. The planned AI-only issue is part of the author's ongoing promotion for a new course, AI for Writers.

The author said the experiment will use a data source everyone has access to, and a unique way of manipulating it. The author also described a limitation of generative AI that shapes the experiment. Because generative AI is trained not only in absolute probabilities but in probabilities with nearness, it has a "huge blind spot," and that blind spot is the same one people have: "tunnel vision."

The AI-made issue is tied to the author's promotion of the AI for Writers course.
