# AI Benchmark Rankings Highlight Top Models and Cost Efficiency

A new live AI and LLM benchmark ranking has been released, comparing models across reasoning, coding, math, vision, agents, and tool use.

## 🔍 Overview
- The benchmark explores live AI and LLM rankings across multiple capabilities.
- Scores are TrueSkill conservative ratings aggregated across benchmarks in each capability.
- Bar lengths show the spread within the top‑ten field, not a zero‑based scale.
- Higher conservative rating is better.

## 📊 Ranking Methodology
- Composite indexes are compared first, then individual evaluations for score provenance, coverage, and methodology.
- Preference leaders are identified across semantically classified task clusters.
- Blind head‑to‑head tests measure helpfulness, response quality, and the cost of completing the same task.
