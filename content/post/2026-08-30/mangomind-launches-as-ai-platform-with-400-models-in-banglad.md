# MangoMind Launches as AI Platform with 400+ Models in Bangladesh

MangoMind has launched as an AI platform in Bangladesh, claiming to be the number one AI platform in the country. The platform provides access to over 400 premium AI models, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, and others, all through a single unified dashboard.

Users can make local payments via bKash and Nagad. To get started, visitors are directed to app.mangomindbd.com.

This development marks the arrival of a multi-model AI platform for users in Bangladesh, offering access to a variety of premium models through a local payment system.
