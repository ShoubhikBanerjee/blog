# Strategies for Differentiating Generative AI Outputs

As AI models are increasingly trained on similar datasets, they often produce outputs with low variance. To create distinct results, organizations must focus on differentiating their ingredients or their recipes to overcome the inherent tunnel vision of generative AI.

## 🧩 How it works

Generative AI models are trained on absolute probabilities and probabilities with nearness, which creates a blind spot. Because these models prioritize high-probability concepts, they struggle to generate creative or diverse outputs unless specifically prompted to think outside of the box. 

## ⚙️ Key details

To move beyond generic AI responses, organizations can implement the following strategies:

* **Ingredient Differentiation:** Blend basic AI training data with unique, proprietary data such as internal customer feedback or qualitative company metrics.
* **Methodological Differentiation:** Apply advanced analytical techniques—such as SARIMAX, neural forecasting, or additive regression—to standard datasets to extract unique insights.
* **Prompt Engineering:** Use techniques like the "Add a banana" principle, which forces an AI to incorporate words outside of the primary domain to disrupt standard probability distributions.

## 💡 Why it matters

Because modern AI platforms often utilize the same models and access the same base data, they are prone to producing identical results. Developing unique processes—the way humans direct the AI, the ingredients provided, and the specific tasks requested—serves as the primary differentiator for businesses. Additionally, transparency regarding the use of AI in content generation is becoming increasingly important and may soon be a regulatory requirement for doing business in the European Union.
