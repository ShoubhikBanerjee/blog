# Instagram launches AI‑powered Effect Builder for custom Stories filters

Instagram has added a new web‑based Effect Builder that lets users generate custom Instagram Stories filters with AI.

## 🔍 Overview
- The Effect Builder creates new filters from AI prompts.
- It is accessed through a web bot where you can design any Instagram filter you imagine.

## 🧩 How it works
- A prompt box lets you describe your filter idea; you can also attach style‑reference images.
- After submitting, you can upload your own photos to preview the AI‑generated effect.
- Follow‑up prompts let you ask for changes to the previewed effect.
- An optional feature lets Instagram’s AI rewrite your prompt; e.g., a one‑line request was expanded into several detailed paragraphs about color palette and lighting, though the final filter looked similar to the original output.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Eligibility:** Available now for U.S. residents who are 18 years or older.
- **Submission:** You can submit your filters directly from Effect Builder.
- **Safety review:** Submitted effects must pass a safety review before they become available to other users.
- **Publication:** Once approved, the effect appears in Instagram Stories for anyone to use, and whenever someone uses your effect Instagram links back to your profile.

## 🚀 Availability
- The tool is currently live on the web for eligible users in the United States.
