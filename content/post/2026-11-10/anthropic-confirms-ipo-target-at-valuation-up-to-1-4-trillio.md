# Anthropic Confirms IPO Target at Valuation Up to $1.4 Trillion

Anthropic confirmed on Tuesday that its upcoming initial public offering could be one of the largest ever, despite falling short of earlier valuation expectations.

## ⚙️ Key details

- **Share Price:** Expected to be between $70.00 and $78.00 per share.
- **Valuation:** According to the New York Times, the pricing would value the company as high as $1.4 trillion. Reuters noted that the IPO was previously expected to reach a valuation of up to $2 trillion.
- **Stock Market Listing:** Anthropic has applied to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol "ANT."
- **Expected Timeline:** Sources told The Wall Street Journal that Anthropic is aiming for shares to begin trading next week on Thursday or Friday.
- **Competitor Comparison:** As of last week, rival AI company OpenAI was valued at $1.25 trillion.

## 💡 Context and influencing factors

The reduced expectations surrounding the IPO follow several recent market and political pressures:

- Months of ongoing debate regarding AI safety.
- Midterm election results showing souring public opinion toward AI companies and the large-scale data center projects they heavily invest in.
- Weekend revelations concerning an alleged Chinese spy's involvement with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang.

Upon going public, Anthropic's offering is expected to make it one of the world's most valuable publicly traded companies as early as next week.
