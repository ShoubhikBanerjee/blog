# Adobe unveils agentic AI integrations for Creative Cloud at Max 2026

Adobe announced at its Max conference in Miami that it is going all in on agentic AI, letting both Adobe‑built and third‑party AI agents act as design assistants, automate repetitive creative tasks, and run continuously in the background.

## 🔍 Overview
- Adobe framed chatbots and AI agents as potential next‑generation design assistants.
- New tools let preferred third‑party agents access and modify Creative Cloud projects, while the Firefly AI Assistant gains expanded capabilities.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Local Model Context Protocol (MCP)** integrations let external agents from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, etc., work locally in Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, and After Effects, directly editing native project files.
- All layers, vectors, bins, and sequences remain editable, so users can take over at any time.
- Agents can pull reference documents from other MCP‑capable tools (e.g., Google Docs) and share results via Slack.
- **Always‑on agents** perform recurring background jobs such as asset organization, quality checks, or content preparation, allowing users to step away from the computer.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Third‑party AI agents** can use Creative Cloud apps on the user’s behalf and run social‑media accounts via Firefly.
- **Firefly AI Assistant upgrades** include:
  - *Plan Mode*: review, edit, and approve the agent’s plan before execution.
  - *Model Selector*: choose a specific AI model or let the system auto‑select.
  - Integration with *Firefly Boards*.
  - Customizable formats, colors, brand guidelines, characters, and products applied automatically across projects.
- **Creative agent extensions** now cover Premiere mobile, enabling conversational video edits on the go, and new video‑editing features such as masked subject tracking and batch color/exposure adjustments.

### Feature Summary
| Integration | Capability |
|---|---|
| MCP local integrations | Third‑party agents edit Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects files locally |
| Firefly AI Assistant | Runs social‑media accounts, Plan Mode, Model Selector, Boards, brand‑wide customization |
| Always‑on agents | Continuous background tasks like asset organization and automated graphic generation |

## 📅 Availability
- Creative Cloud local MCP integrations are not yet released; a private beta is planned for early 2027.
- The always‑on preview will arrive first in the Firefly AI Assistant, though Adobe has not given a launch date.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Designers can offload repetitive or ongoing work to AI agents while retaining full editability of their projects.
- Integration of third‑party bots expands the ecosystem beyond Adobe’s own tools, giving users flexibility to employ familiar AI models.
- Continuous background agents free up creative time, enabling focus on higher‑level decisions.
