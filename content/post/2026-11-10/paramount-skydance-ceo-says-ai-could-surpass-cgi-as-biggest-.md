# Paramount Skydance CEO says AI could surpass CGI as biggest creative unlock

Paramount Skydance’s chief executive David Ellison told investors that “it’s hard to imagine” AI won’t have a bigger impact on the film industry than CGI.

## 🔍 Overview
- Ellison compared AI to the advent of CGI and digital composites in the early 1990s, calling it “the biggest creative unlock in modern history.”
- He cited “Jurassic Park” and “Terminator 2” as examples of how CGI changed storytelling and said AI “is hard to imagine… will have a more significant impact than that.”

## 💡 Why it matters
- “All of this makes the value of premium IP and live events more valuable, not less.”
- In a world of “AI slop,” there will “always be a premium for truly hand‑crafted storytelling.”
- Ellison stressed that “artificial intelligence is a tool for artists and will never be a replacement for them.”

## ⚙️ How AI will be used
- The company “has been developing its own AI tools.”
- Ellison said these tools will help “unlock more original storytelling by being able to make the budgets more digestible to be able to take that amount of risk.”
- He gave the example of *Terminator*: "It cost $4 million to make, but if you wanted to make that picture today, shot for shot, it’s $60 to 70 million." AI could help reduce that gap.

## 📈 Expected impact
- “Content will be easier to produce, projects across the organization will speed up, and how we work will transform.”
- AI will “significantly” reshape Paramount Skydance’s business, according to Ellison.
