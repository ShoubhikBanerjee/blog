# Adobe Expands Creative Cloud with New AI Editing Tools

Adobe announced new AI capabilities for Creative Cloud apps and its Firefly platform at the Max event, aiming to speed up design and editing tasks.

## 🔍 Overview
- Adobe is expanding Creative Cloud apps and Firefly AI platform with new capabilities.
- Announcements include automatic removal of objects, blemishes, and distractions in Premiere, Photoshop, and Lightroom, plus new generative AI tools and collaborative experiences.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Premiere Generative Remove (beta)**
  - Mask an object, click Remove; Premiere fills the area with realistic detail using generative AI while surrounding footage stays untouched.
- **Photoshop updated Remove Tool**
  - Better at detecting and reconstructing surrounding elements such as reflections, shadows, and footprints.
- **Photoshop Quick Compare**
  - Before‑and‑after split‑screen slider to review changes without switching layers.
- **Photoshop Relight**
  - Add and position new light sources to dynamically adjust hue and brightness.
- **Lightroom blemish removal**
  - Single‑tap skin retouch on desktop (previously mobile only).
- **Lightroom Open Eyes (public beta)**
  - Replaces closed eyes with natural‑looking open eyes using a reference photo from the library.
- **Premiere new sound editing tools**
  - Clean messy recordings and separate dialogue, music, ambience, and sound effects into individual tracks.
- **Firefly Quick Actions**
  - Ready‑to‑use workflows for creating video thumbnails and product ads, now generally available.
- **Firefly Design**
  - Combined space for illustration, image editing, animation, 3D, and print (beta).
- **Firefly Graph**
  - Helps creators build and iterate on custom, repeatable workflows.
- **Firefly Builder (private beta)**
  - Tool for prototyping websites and apps.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Generative Media tool introduced earlier this year is now generally available.
- Color Mode color editing experience, launched in beta in April, is now generally available.
- Features are part of Adobe’s broader expansion of Creative Cloud and Firefly AI platform.

## 🚀 Availability
- Premiere Generative Remove – beta.
- Photoshop Remove Tool – updated now.
- Photoshop Quick Compare and Relight – newly added.
- Lightroom blemish removal – coming to desktop.
- Lightroom Open Eyes – public beta.
- Firefly Quick Actions – generally available.
- Firefly Design, Graph – beta; Firefly Builder – private beta.
- Generative Media and Color Mode – generally available.
