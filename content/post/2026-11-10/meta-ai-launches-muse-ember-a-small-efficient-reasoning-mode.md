# Meta AI launches Muse Ember, a small, efficient reasoning model

Meta AI just launched Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient model designed for complex reasoning execution and the work agents repeat all day.

## 🔍 Overview
- Smallest and most efficient model in the Claude Haiku/GPT Luna category.
- Targeted at tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long document parsing.

## 🧩 How it works
- Offers six reasoning levels: minimal, low, medium, high, xhigh, and max.
- Visual examples include six pelicans riding bicycles, with the "max" level shown and others noted as comparable to Muse Wonder (40× the price of Ember).

## ⚙️ Key details
| Model | Input price (per M tokens) | Output price (per M tokens) | Token limit |
|-------|---------------------------|-----------------------------|------------|
| Muse Ember | $0.10 | $0.50 | Up to 1 M (price rises after a threshold) |
| GPT‑6 Luna | $0.10 | $0.50 | Up to 1 M (price rises after a threshold) |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 | $0.10 | $0.50 | Up to 1 M (price rises after a threshold) |
| Claude Fable 5.2 | $8.00 | $40.00 | – |
- For shorter prompts Ember is priced the same as GPT‑6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5.
- Pricing becomes less straightforward as prompts get longer; after the threshold Ember’s price sits between Luna/Haiku and Claude Fable.

## 📊 Efficiency metrics
- Artificial Analysis is highlighted as a useful measure.
- The Intelligence Index vs. Cost per Task chart places Muse Ember on the Pareto line, where improving one dimension requires sacrificing another.
- Meta notes Ember sits on the efficiency frontier of the full Artificial Analysis leaderboard: no model scores higher for less per task.
- The grey dot on the Pareto line (mid‑green area) represents GPT‑6.1 Terra (low), noted as worth attention.

## 🚀 Availability
- Muse Ember is available now with pricing identical to GPT‑6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 for short inputs.
- Longer prompts trigger a price threshold, positioning Ember’s cost between the low‑price models and the higher‑priced Claude Fable 5.2.
