# Google Rolls Out Gemini 4 Carbon Model with Agentic Swarm Feature

Google is rolling out Gemini 4 Carbon to the general public after a two‑week preview for cybersecurity defenders via its Fairwind Program. The launch adds an agentic “swarm” capability on the Antigravity platform and positions Carbon as the company’s strongest model yet.

## 🔍 Overview
- Gemini 4 Carbon is described as Google’s “strongest model” and is intended to compete with top systems from Anthropic and OpenAI.
- It “pushes the frontier even further” than the prior Argon release, which remains the preferred choice for most software‑engineering and knowledge‑work tasks.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Pricing**: introductory $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens (about one‑fifth the cost of OpenAI’s GPT‑6 Astra and one‑quarter the cost of Anthropic’s Fable 5.2). Prices double at the end of November but remain cheaper per token than rivals.
- **Positioning**: built for tasks that test the limits of a model’s intelligence—complex, multi‑step coding, debugging, and open‑ended scientific research—using more tokens than Argon to explore thoroughly.

## 🚀 Availability
- Available today via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for Business for all paying developers and enterprises.
- Also rolled out to all Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, and on Antigravity.
- No clear timeline for lower‑tier or free users.

## 🧩 Capabilities
- **Cybersecurity**: strongest Google cybersecurity capabilities to date; leads benchmarks such as SEC‑bench Pro, ExploitGym, and CWE‑bench v1; 100 % recall on scanning and 95 % precision on triage in Project Fortify.
- **Agentic Workflows**: “swarm” feature turns a single prompt into a coordinated team of agents that can be tracked in real time.
- **Knowledge Work**: high‑precision legal, finance, and enterprise operations; generates polished spreadsheets, presentations, and reports following strict schemas.
- **Software Engineering**: excels at multi‑agent collaboration for migrations, root‑cause investigations, architectural redesigns; runs build‑and‑verify loops for web apps and checks rendered UI.
- **Multimodal Reasoning**: visual understanding, spatial reasoning, and 3D/ UI design; partners with Artificial Analysis for the Multimodal Knowledge Agent (MKA) benchmark.

## 📊 Benchmarks
- Ranks #1 on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity’s WANDR, and Zapier’s AutomationBench with >2× lower cost per task than the next best external model.
- In the MKA benchmark, Carbon ranks #2 while costing >6× less per task than the top model.
- Matches best model on Hebbia’s E2E Financial Agentic evaluation and tops 23 configurations on Balyasny Asset Management’s General Financial Intelligence benchmark.

## 🤖 Agentic “Swarm” Feature
- Previewed on the Antigravity platform.
- Allows a single prompt to be split among multiple agents that coordinate in real time.
- Developers can assign Carbon to deep‑reasoning roles (architectural design, auditing) while pairing it with faster, lighter models for parallel work and adversarial verification.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Marks a decisive return for Google to the forefront of AI research after a period when frontier development was dominated by Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Offers a competitively priced, high‑performance alternative for enterprises needing advanced reasoning, cybersecurity, and multimodal capabilities.
