# Ring Unveils Indoor Drone Camera for Home Patrol

Ring founder Jamie Siminoff announced the launch of the Ring Drone Cam at a New York City event this week. The device is designed to patrol inside homes when owners are away and is set to arrive on January 27, 2027, for $249.99.

## 🔍 Overview
The Drone Cam is an AI-powered drone intended as the spiritual successor to the Always Home Cam. It is designed to fly into homes to monitor conditions and provide livestreaming and recording capabilities through the Ring app.

## 🧩 How it works
The drone reacts to triggers from Ring sensors, such as detecting motion, a door opening, a water leak, or an alarm sounding. When triggered, it flies along preprogrammed routes to a set location for recording. It can also perform scheduled patrols around the home or be sent out on demand.

## ⚙️ Key details
*   **Specifications:** The drone offers 2K resolution, a 148-degree field of view, and covers 360 degrees due to its mobile nature.
*   **Design:** It is designed for indoor use only and weighs 165 grams, making it significantly smaller than a 2023 demo model. The camera is integrated into the body rather than dangling.
*   **Battery Life:** Ring claims the drone can fly for five to eight minutes on a charge. Charging takes eight minutes per minute of flight, meaning a five-minute flight requires approximately 40 minutes of charging.
*   **Privacy:** The camera shutter is closed when the drone is docked. It is noisy while flying to prevent stealth. It records video only while in the air.
*   **Security:** Footage is encrypted using Ring’s TAKE (Throw Away the Key Encryption) system, which rotates encryption keys every five minutes and deletes Ring’s copies after 24 hours.

## 🚀 Availability
*   **Launch:** The Drone Cam launches in the US on January 27, 2027.
*   **Cost:** It is priced at $249.99.
*   **Access:** A Ring Protect plan is required for video storage and advanced features.
*   **Integration:** Both the Drone Cam and the Ring Rover Cam work with Amazon’s Alexa Home Map.
*   **Robotics Line:** The Drone Cam launches alongside the Ring Rover Cam, a $999.99 autonomous security robot for businesses using the same AI Pilot technology.
*   **Certification:** Ring is currently in the process of obtaining FCC certification. The drone is assembled in the US and complies with FCC regulations regarding foreign-made drones.
