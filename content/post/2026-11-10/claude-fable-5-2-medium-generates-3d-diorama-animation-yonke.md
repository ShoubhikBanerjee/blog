# Claude Fable 5.2 Medium Generates 3D Diorama Animation Yonkers Rises

A 3D diorama animation titled Yonkers Rises was created using Claude Fable 5.2 Medium to test the model's 3D modeling and animation capabilities while mapping the location and shape of Yonkers.

## 🔍 Overview

Yonkers Rises is an animated 3D diorama of New York City featuring a giant mech cut directly from the Yonkers boundary. In the animation, the mech rises from the ground and travels through Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx.

## 🧩 How it works

To build the project, Claude Fable 5.2 Medium was given a prompt requesting a Three.js animation based on OpenStreetMap mapping data, depicting Yonkers standing up as a mech and walking around Manhattan, Queens, and Brooklyn. 

* **Typo Deduction**: The model correctly deduced that the word "átomos" in the prompt was a mobile keyboard typo for "stomps".
* **Sequence & Behavior**: The scene plays an initial stand-up sequence followed by a continuous looping stomping tour.

## ⚙️ Key details

The animation features several interactive tracking and environmental details:

* **Tracking Metrics**: A stomp counter and a mech height readout track the mech's progress as it moves.
* **Environmental Impact**: Buildings are flattened beneath each footfall of the mech.
* **Status Line Updates**: The status line dynamically changes depending on whether the mech is moving across land or wading through water.
