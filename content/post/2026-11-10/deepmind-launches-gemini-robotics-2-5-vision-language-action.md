# DeepMind launches Gemini Robotics 2.5 vision-language-action model

Today we introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, the most capable vision‑language‑action (VLA) model to date. It expands the range of skills a robot can learn and speeds up acquisition, and it has already been tested on more than 200 distinct tasks and in a real‑world automotive production line proof‑of‑concept.

## 🔍 Overview
- Gemini Robotics 2.5 is the latest VLA model from DeepMind.
- Increases both the breadth of skills a robot can learn and the speed of acquisition.
- Evaluated on over 200 tasks, including serving food with tongs, tying a bow, and pipetting liquids.
- Achieved 30%‑100% higher success rates and 20%‑30% faster completion compared with previous baselines.
- In a Mercedes‑Benz production line pilot, the Apollo 2 humanoid robot attained a 96% success rate in automotive part kitting.

## 🧩 How it works
- Combines a vision‑language reasoning backbone (Gemini Robotics ER) with an actions model that directly controls robot hands and arms.
- Uses inference‑time reasoning guidance to steer around physical hazards in real time.
- Safety steering boosted safe task completion around obstacles by 33% and around nearby people by 43%.
- Gemini 4 Argon generates thousands of simulated practice runs from camera scenes, building realistic digital twins that accelerate development of Gemini Robotics 2.5.

## ⚙️ Key details
| Robot platform | Hand / Gripper |
|----------------|----------------|

- Speed improvement: 20%‑30% faster task execution.
- Safety improvements: +33% around obstacles, +43% around people.

## 🚀 Availability
- Gemini Robotics 2.5 is rolling out to DeepMind’s robotics partners.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Represents a step toward human‑level dexterity by combining advanced tool use, deformable manipulation, and delicate handling.
- Demonstrates reliable, high‑precision performance in a demanding industrial setting.
- Improved safety and jailbreak resistance (Argon 99.5% vs 82% baseline) support broader deployment in unstructured environments.
