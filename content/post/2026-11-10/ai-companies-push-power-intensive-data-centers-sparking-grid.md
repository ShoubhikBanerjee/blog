# AI Companies Push Power‑Intensive Data Centers, Sparking Grid and Environmental Concerns in Virginia

As AI firms chase ever‑larger training workloads, they are demanding power on a scale that is reshaping Virginia’s data‑center corridor and prompting new regulatory and community responses.

## 🔍 Overview
- The region around Ashburn, Virginia – known as “Data Center Alley” – is the world’s largest data‑center hub.
- Concrete monolithic buildings with names like Skybox and CloudHQ line every major road.
- Two days after the midterms, OpenAI’s chief global affairs officer, Chris Lehane, wrote that data centers “have become a proxy for public unease about affordability, corporate power, and people losing control over their futures.”

## ⚡ Power Scale for AI Training
- The average data center in Loudoun County operates at **54 MW**, more than the power draw of Denver International Airport.
- The smallest facilities considered attractive for AI training are **300 MW**.
- The “ideal” AI‑focused data center would require about **6,000 MW**, comparable to the electricity demand of the city of Chicago.
- Anthropic has reportedly agreed to pay **$3.4 billion** for gas turbines – described as the largest power‑generation purchase by an AI company.

## 🏭 Power Sources
- AI‑focused data centers can be powered **(1) by connecting to the electrical grid** or **(2) by generating electricity onsite**.
- In Loudoun County, most data centers are **connected to the existing grid**, which is supplied largely by **natural‑gas‑fired generation** from Dominion Energy.
- An Equinix facility in the area uses **massive diesel generators for backup** but receives its primary power from Dominion Energy.
- Rural sites with little grid infrastructure rely on “behind the meter” power, typically **natural‑gas‑powered turbines**, with **diesel generators** as backup.

## 📜 Regulatory and Community Pushback
- Recent protests have produced new state rules requiring data centers to **pay fully for the cost of new transmission lines** and to ensure those costs are not shared by the broader public.
- Data‑center developers say they are preparing for rules that could **cut them off from grid power during peak demand**.
- Activist Elena Schlossberg told me she’s “always banging my head against the wall” when the industry frames the fight as a culture war.
- Schlossberg also worries that a strained grid will cause data centers to run **more diesel generators** and push **Dominion Energy** to **expand fossil‑fuel inputs rather than renewables**.

## 🌍 Environmental Impact
- Natural‑gas turbines of any scale release air pollutants; diesel generators are **even worse**.
- The Environmental Protection Agency reported that **Elon Musk violated air‑quality laws** when he set up dozens of turbines to power a data center in Memphis.

## 🚀 Industry Responses
- Companies are exploring **better deals** that can raise tax revenues, hire local workers, and increase community involvement, as the protests are leading them to do.
