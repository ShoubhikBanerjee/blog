# AI‑Generated Virus Blueprints Discussed by MIT Technology Review Innovator Samuel King

In a subscriber‑only roundtable recorded on October 16 2026, MIT Technology Review’s senior AI reporter James O'Donnell speaks with Stanford bioengineering PhD candidate Samuel King about his 2025 work using a generative AI model to propose genetic blueprints for microscopic viruses.

## 🔍 Overview
- The conversation is part of MIT Technology Review’s “Roundtables” series.
- It is available only to MIT Technology Review subscribers.
- Listeners can watch the video or listen to the audio.

## 🧬 AI‑Generated Virus Blueprints
- In 2025 Samuel King used a generative AI model to propose genetic blueprints for microscopic viruses.
- The work addresses the question “Can AI design new life forms?”.
- It is not yet an example of AI‑generated life, but could be next.

## 🎙️ Interview Details
- Speakers: James O'Donnell (AI Reporter) and Samuel King (Bioengineering PhD Candidate, Stanford University/Arc Institute).
- King was named one of MIT Technology Review’s Innovators Under 35.
- The session discusses new ways of seeing biology.

## 📅 Recording Information
- Recorded on Friday, October 16, 2026.

## ⚠️ Context
- “Don’t be fooled—LLMs don’t reason.”
