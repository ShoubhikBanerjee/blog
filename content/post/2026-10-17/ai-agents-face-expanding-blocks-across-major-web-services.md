# AI agents face expanding blocks across major web services

A wave of website restrictions is limiting the ability of autonomous AI agents—promised by OpenAI, Meta, Google, Instinct and others—to act on users' behalf across the internet.

## 🔍 Overview
- "Autonomous agents are the hottest thing in AI."
- Companies market agents that can "scour the internet on your behalf, doing anything from booking a dinner reservation and cleaning out your inbox to buying new shoes."
- In recent weeks, users report blocks preventing their AI agents from performing tasks on top websites.

## 🤖 Agents Mentioned
- Meta’s Muse
- xAI’s Grok
- OpenAI’s Dots
- Instinct’s AI agent
- Perplexity’s shopping agent

## 🛡️ Companies Blocking AI Agents
| Company | Action |
|---|---|
| Yelp | Allows only AI agents that pay for its data licensing program |
| Delta Air Lines | Preventing agents from booking tickets |
| JPMorgan Chase | Blocking agents from accessing accounts due to security concerns |
| Charles Schwab | Blocking agents from accessing accounts due to security concerns |
| Cars.com | No longer accessible to agents |
| Amtrak | No longer accessible to agents |
| Amazon | Banned Perplexity’s agentic shopping feature and is blocking Muse, Grok, Instinct agents, and OpenAI’s Dots |
| Google (reports on Reddit) | Blocking some accounts that use Muse due to "suspicious activity" |
| Walmart (reports) | Issues checking out for agents despite Meta partnership |
| Cloudflare | Introduced a one‑click block for AI crawlers in 2025 and now blocks crawlers that scrape for both search indexing and AI training |

## ⚖️ Legal Tussles with Amazon
- "Amazon was already fighting off AI agents last year when it banned Perplexity’s agentic shopping feature and sued the AI startup."
- Amazon claimed it "repeatedly requested" Perplexity stop allowing its agents to shop on its website.
- Perplexity argued Amazon doesn’t want agents because it "won’t get as many human eyeballs in front of its ads."
- A judge ordered Perplexity to stop its agents on Amazon, but an appeals court overturned the ruling in August.
- Amazon has since filed an amended complaint.

## 🤝 Responses & Workarounds
- Meta said, "Turning away a personal agent means turning away the customer behind it," and prefers to work with businesses rather than lose customers.
- Meta partnered with retailers such as Walmart, Gap, Sephora, Best Buy and others to allow Muse to shop on their sites.
- Shopify opened its network of storefronts to AI agents.
- Meta responded to instances of websites blocking Muse in a post earlier this month.

## 🌐 Infrastructure Measures
- Cloudflare’s one‑click block aims to "save the web from an influx of AI bots, which is putting a strain on internet infrastructure and wrecking email inboxes."
- Canal notes that these blocks may make the web more "annoying" as more sites add human‑verification steps, potentially affecting even simple grocery‑shopping sites.
