# McKinsey pilots AI‑simulated client interviews for entry‑level candidates

McKinsey & Company is adding a new step to its entry‑level interview process: a brief conversation with an AI‑generated client simulation.

## 🔍 Overview
- Candidates will have a **10‑minute conversation** with an AI simulation of a client during their interview.
- The simulation is designed to reflect **about 80 % of the scenarios** McKinsey consultants encounter in day‑to‑day work.
- It is **not intended to mimic an “unhinged” client**.

## 🧩 How it works
- The candidate talks with the AI client in real time for ten minutes.
- **Human interviewers remain in the room** and observe the interaction.
- The AI **does not assign scores**; assessment is conducted by the humans present.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The tool focuses on typical consulting situations rather than extreme or unrealistic client behavior.
- It is being piloted for **undergraduate applicants to business‑analyst roles**.
- The pilot is limited to **some offices in North America** and is scheduled for **this fall**.
- The Wall Street Journal estimates that **“hundreds”** of candidates may use the simulation during the test phase.

## 🚀 Availability
- Testing will run this fall for undergraduates applying for business‑analyst positions.
- Only selected North American offices are included in the initial rollout.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Shows a concrete example of AI avatars being used in the hiring process.
- Highlights that final evaluation still relies on **human judgment**, with AI providing the scenario.
- Mirrors a broader industry trend, as **other companies are also building AI‑avatar interview tools**.
