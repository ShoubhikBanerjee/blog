# OpenAI Accuses Former Employee of Taking Confidential Files to Apple

OpenAI filed a new legal document alleging that a former employee who left to join Apple stole more than 100 confidential files containing its product plans and shared them with Apple leadership.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI’s filing, reported by Reuters, comes amid an ongoing legal battle with Apple over trade secrets.
- The filing alleges the employee sent the stolen information to a personal account and then presented it to Apple executives, including the vice‑president of operations in Asia.
- Apple has not responded to The Verge’s request for comment.

## 🧩 Accusations
- The former employee took **more than 100 confidential files** before departing for Apple.
- OpenAI says the employee “took its product plans and presented them to Apple.”
- OpenAI is “very concerned that its trade secret and confidential information is now circulating inside Apple, where Apple engineers can shape Apple’s upcoming products based on OpenAI’s confidential and innovative work.”
- Apple, in turn, accuses OpenAI of stealing confidential documents and spying on hardware prototypes.

## 📂 Confidential Files Allegedly Stolen
| File Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Images | Pictures of at least one unreleased device |
| Supplier Information | Details about component suppliers |
| Engineering Data | Technical data related to hardware development |
| Project Files | Internal project documentation |
| Cosmetic Finish Standards | Guidelines for product appearance |
| Coating & Sealing Reports | Reports about the “coating and sealing of hardware components” |

## 📱 Apple’s New Devices Linked to the Leak
- OpenAI links the former employee’s presentation to Apple’s **new HomeView smart display** and a range of other devices Apple revealed this week.
- OpenAI notes that the presentation “appears to have happened months ago — and now, armed with that information, Apple is making a new push into AI‑enabled products.”
- Apple’s smart display has been rumored for years, reflecting the long timeline typical of Apple’s product plans.

## 📣 Statements
- OpenAI’s filing states: “OpenAI is very concerned that its trade secret and confidential information is now circulating inside Apple…"
- Apple’s response: No comment provided to The Verge.

*The information above reflects only what OpenAI and Apple have publicly alleged in the filing.*
