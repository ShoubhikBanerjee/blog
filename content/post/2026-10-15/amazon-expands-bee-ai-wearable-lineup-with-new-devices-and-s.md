# Amazon Expands Bee AI Wearable Lineup with New Devices and Subscription

Amazon is expanding its Bee lineup with two new AI-powered devices, the Bee Frontier and the Bee Eclipse, alongside a new subscription service designed to enhance functionality and integration.

## 🔍 Overview
Both devices are designed as tools to assist users in staying present by listening to daily conversations to provide summaries, transcripts, insights, and to-do lists. The main difference between the models is their physical form factor.

| Device | Price | Battery Life | Primary Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bee Frontier | $74.99 | 7 days | Wearable (wrist or pin) |
| Bee Eclipse | $99.99 | 21 days | Professional (non-wearable) |

## 🧩 How it works
- Users can integrate Bee devices with third-party services including Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple HealthKit, and photos to enable AI-driven actions like drafting emails or scheduling invites.
- The devices utilize "Private Compute" security architecture to encrypt transcripts, summaries, and to-dos without storing audio recordings.
- During setup, the AI is trained on the individual user's voice, and it will not automatically identify other speakers.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Bee Frontier: Features dual microphones, a recording indicator light, and IP67 water resistance.
- Bee Eclipse: A credit-card-shaped device designed to snap magnetically onto the back of a smartphone.
- Subscription: Amazon is introducing a Bee Pro subscription for $11.99 per month which unlocks unlimited summaries, personal chat assistants, and deeper integrations.
- Amazon & Alexa Plus: The subscription includes integration with Amazon for shopping suggestions and Alexa Plus for hands-free access to device insights.

## 🚀 Availability
- The Bee Frontier is available now in black, green, and lavender.
- The Bee Eclipse is "coming soon" and will be offered in black, pink, yellow, and silver.
