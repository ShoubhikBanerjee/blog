# Anthropic Reports $16.8 B Q3 Revenue and Cuts Model‑Distillation Income

Anthropic disclosed its third‑quarter 2026 financial results, showing revenue of $16.8 billion and a material reduction in revenue generated from model‑distillation.

## 🔍 Overview
- Anthropic told investors that quarterly revenue was up nearly 50% to $16.8 bn.
- Revenue by quarter:
  - Q1 (Jan‑Mar): about $4.8 bn
  - Q2 (Apr‑Jun): $11.5 bn
  - Q3 (Jul‑Sep): $16.8 bn

## 📊 Financial Highlights
- Adjusted operating margins (excluding stock‑based compensation) were 11%.
- The company generated $1.70 in revenue for every $1 spent on computing power.
- Distillation accounted for 5 % to 7 % of revenue in Q3 and was cut back “materially” in that quarter.
- Napkin maths says that if distillation was 5 % of Q2 revenue, then other labs spent over half a billion dollars distilling Anthropic’s models in that quarter.

## 🛠️ Distillation Practice
- Distillation is a controversial practice whereby rival AI labs train their models on the outputs of more advanced ones.
- When rival companies “distil” their models from Anthropic’s models, it involves heavy usage and spending.
- Anthropic told investors that distillation accounted for 5 % to 7 % of revenue and that they had cut it back materially in Q3.

## 📈 Why It Matters
- Strong revenue growth signals Anthropic’s expanding market presence ahead of a potential IPO.
- Reducing reliance on distillation may limit the amount of external spending on Anthropic’s models.
- The company no longer leans on “annualized run‑rate revenue” in investor communications, indicating a shift toward reporting actual quarterly performance.
