# Twin Health uses AI to build metabolic digital twins for patients

Digital twins are a new concept in health where a virtual model of a person is created using various data points, including lab results, wearable data, images, and genetic profiles. Twin Health is a program designed to help users manage their metabolism, weight, and conditions like diabetes by creating an AI-powered, color-coded food database that predicts how a user's glucose levels will react to specific dietary choices.

## 🧩 How it works
- The system consumes daily metabolic data, including body composition from smart scales and blood pressure readings.
- Users integrate data from continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) and wearable devices to feed the model.
- The AI generates insights based on how the body reacts to food intake, which helps refine the accuracy of the twin over time.
- Clinicians can test how a patient might react to specific medicines or treatments by running simulations on their digital twin.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Building a digital twin requires an extensive collection of personal data over a 90-day period.
- Users participate in check-ins with human coaches and doctors to review lab results and monitor progress.
- The service is currently experimental and not available for widespread download.
- Common feedback from users includes reports of tracking fatigue.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Theoretically, digital twins can help healthcare providers assess injury risks.
- The technology aims to predict disease progression.
- It supports the implementation of preventive care while aiming to minimize adverse effects for patients.
