# State Attorneys General Push for Data Protections as Character.AI Files for Bankruptcy

Character.AI announced on Tuesday that it is filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and is selling some assets to Disney. The filing triggered a multi‑state investigation led by New York Attorney General Letitia James, who, together with 39 other bipartisan attorneys general, is seeking safeguards for the company’s user data.

## 🔍 Overview
- Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 and disclosed that several states were mid‑investigation when the bankruptcy filing occurred.
- The investigation began in 2024 after a lawsuit from the mother of a 14‑year‑old who died by suicide allegedly seconds after talking with the chatbot.
- The AGs describe the findings as “exceptionally serious dangers” and “potentially unlawful conduct.”

## ⚖️ Legal Actions
- Letitia James revealed a years‑long multi‑state probe into Character.AI’s consumer‑data practices.
- James is among 40 bipartisan AGs who filed a notice of interest in the bankruptcy case “to put all parties on notice of the regulatory issues that will need to be addressed in any sale or transfer of the Debtor’s business or assets.”
- The states are urging the bankruptcy judge to require the purchaser of Character.AI’s assets to implement specific data‑protection procedures.

## 📊 Consumer Risks Highlighted by the Investigation
- Highly sensitive conversations users have with chatbot characters.
- Large number of minors using the product.
- Chatbots posing as licensed professionals.
- Exposure to sexual and violent content.
- Weak age‑verification checks.
- Misleading safety claims.
- Potential violations of state privacy laws.

## 🛡️ Proposed Safeguards for Any Buyer
- Effective age‑assurance systems.
- Restrictions on how minors can engage with the chatbots.
- Implementation of “Crisis safeguards.”
- Mechanisms that let users delete their data.

The AGs warn that without such protections, “this company collected some of the most private thoughts children have ever shared, and now those conversations could be sold off to the highest bidder.” James said, “I am fighting to make sure any buyer puts children’s safety first and puts real protections in place.”

Character.AI and Disney have not responded to requests for comment.
