# Google launches Universal AI‑Powered Shopping Cart in the US

Google’s intelligent AI shopping cart is finally here, arriving just in time for Black Friday and seasonal holiday spending.

## 🛒 Overview
- The Universal Cart tool Google previewed at its I/O event in May is now rolling out in the US across the Gemini app and Google Search.
- It is described as a “universal” digital cart that lets you save products from multiple retailers in a single unified space and works across different Google platforms.

## ⚙️ How it works
- You can find and add a bunch of products using the Gemini app and Google Search AI Mode, and they’ll all be saved to the same cart.
- The cart can send you notifications about price drops and restocks for items saved, sparing you from checking each retailer’s website individually, and can display any relevant promotional codes it finds.
- You can choose to pay for items from the cart using Google Wallet, or transfer them to the merchant’s own cart so you can checkout with the retailer directly.
- Together with Google Wallet, Universal Cart can also analyze the perks provided by each of your saved credit cards and help you choose the best payment method when purchasing.

## 📦 Features
- **Cross‑retailer storage** – saves products from multiple merchants in one cart.
- **Multi‑platform access** – available in Google Search, Gemini app, with planned integration for Gmail and YouTube (timeline not announced).
- **Price‑watch notifications** – alerts for drops, restocks, and auto‑found promo codes.
- **Flexible checkout** – pay via Google Wallet or move items to the merchant’s cart.
- **Credit‑card perk analysis** – suggests the optimal card using Google Wallet data.

## 🚀 Availability
- The rollout is currently in the United States and is first reaching Google Search and the Gemini app.

## 📈 Why it matters
- The launch comes as competition in the AI‑assisted shopping space is heating up; rivals such as OpenAI and Meta have launched agentic AI assistants that can shop online on the user’s behalf.
- Shopify recently announced it is opening its platform to allow more AI agents to browse and purchase from Shopify listings.
- Big Tech players are entering this race on the bet that the future of online shopping involves having a robot do most of the work for you.
