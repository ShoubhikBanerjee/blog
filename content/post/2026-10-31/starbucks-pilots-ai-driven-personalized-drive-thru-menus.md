# Starbucks pilots AI‑driven personalized drive‑thru menus

Starbucks announced that it will experiment with artificial‑intelligence tools to recognize customers as they arrive at its drive‑thrus and serve each car a personalized digital menu.

## 🔍 Overview
- The company plans to "recognize participating customers as they arrive, present personalized digital menus, and make payment more effortless" (CEO Brian Niccol, earnings call).
- The effort is described as an early‑stage experiment that will be tested, learned from, and scaled if successful.

## 🛠️ How it works
- **Customer recognition** – AI will identify a car or its occupants when they pull up to the lane.
- **Personalized menu display** – A digital board will show menu items tailored to the recognized customer.
- **Background order‑entry AI (2027)** – Starting in 2027, AI models will assist baristas in entering orders more accurately and efficiently.
- **App integration** – AI will be added to the Starbucks app’s search and discovery features, with future support for "conversational and agentic" ordering.
- **Existing AI tools** – Customers can already place orders through ChatGPT, and employees use the AI‑powered Green Dot Assist for recipe and service‑standard queries.

## 📋 Features
- Recognize customers at the drive‑thru entrance.
- Show a personalized digital menu per car.
- Streamline payment and order entry.
- Test background AI models for barista assistance beginning in 2027.
- Expand AI to app search, discovery, and conversational ordering.
- Existing ChatGPT ordering channel and employee‑focused Green Dot Assist.

## 📊 Comparison with peers
| Company | AI Drive‑Thru Feature | Personalization Detail |
|---------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| Starbucks | Recognize customers, display personalized digital menu, background order‑entry AI (2027) | Car‑by‑car menu customization based on identified customer |
| Taco Bell | Experiments with menu boards that change layout, content, visuals on a car‑by‑car basis | Similar car‑specific visual changes |
| McDonald’s | "ArchIQ" chatbot identifies repeat customers and lets them order their "usual" | Repeat‑customer ordering via chatbot |

## 🚀 Timeline / Availability
- **Now** – Announcement and early testing phase (CEO calls it "early").
- **2027** – Planned testing of background AI models for order entry.
- **Future** – Potential rollout of personalized digital menus after testing; exact launch date not disclosed.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enhances individual customer experience at high‑traffic drive‑thrus.
- Aims to reduce order errors and speed up service.
- Extends AI use beyond internal tools to visible consumer‑facing interfaces.
- Signals broader industry interest in AI‑driven personalization (as seen at Taco Bell and McDonald’s).
