# Apple rolls out Apple Intelligence features to China with iOS 27.3

Apple announced that its Apple Intelligence suite is coming to China with the upcoming iOS 27.3 release.

## 📣 Overview
Apple will launch Apple Intelligence in China with iOS 27.3, as announced on the company’s Chinese newsroom site and reported by MacRumors.

## ⚙️ Key details
- iOS 27.3 beta, containing initial Apple Intelligence features, launches on **November 4**.
- The full iOS 27.3 release is scheduled for **December**.
- Apple Intelligence received regulatory approval in China in **July**.
- Apple trained its own large language model specifically for the Chinese market (Reuters, August).

## 🛠️ Features arriving in China
- Notification summaries
- Real‑time translation
- Rewriting and proofreading tools
- Visual intelligence
- Removing objects from photos

## 🤖 AI chatbot integration
- Apple worked with **Alibaba** to add support for its **Qwen** chatbot in some experiences, a setup similar to iOS’s integration with OpenAI’s ChatGPT launched in 2024.

## 🚫 What’s not included
- The new Siri AI is **not** part of this China launch.

## 📅 Availability
- **iOS 27.3 beta** (with the initial Apple Intelligence features) becomes available on **November 4**.
- The **full iOS 27.3** release follows in **December**.
