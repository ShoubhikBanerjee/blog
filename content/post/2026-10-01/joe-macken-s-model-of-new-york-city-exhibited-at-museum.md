# Joe Macken’s Model of New York City Exhibited at Museum

The Museum of the City of New York is hosting "He Built This City: Joe Macken’s Model," featuring a large-scale model of the city.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Dimensions**: 50 x 27 feet
- **Materials**: Balsa wood and cardboard
- **Construction period**: Built over 21 years

## 🚀 Availability
- The exhibition closes on 12th October.
