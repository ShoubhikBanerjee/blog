# Acre Via Nominated for Detektor International Award in ID and Access Control

Acre Security's AI assistant, Acre Via, has been named as a nominee in the ID & Access Control category of the Detektor International Award.

## 🔍 Overview
Established in 2000 by Detektor International magazine, the Detektor International Award is a prestigious global security industry product award. According to Lennart Alexandrie, CEO of AR Media International and editor-in-chief and founder of Detektor magazine, the goal is to "reward innovative design and product development and highlight solutions that, with their usability, can provide great customer benefit."

## ⚙️ Key details
The award is judged by a multinational jury consisting of seven editorial staff members from Detektor International representing four countries:
* Sweden (3)
* Great Britain (2)
* Denmark (1)
* Taiwan (1)

The competition is divided into four categories, with three nominees per category. The first prize winner receives the "Best Product Award," while the other two receive an "Innovative Achievement Award."

## 🧩 How it works
Acre Via is a generative AI assistant designed to support security teams and enable more natural and efficient interaction with the Acre Access Control (AAC) platform. Its capabilities include:
* Allowing operators to ask questions in natural language via text.
* Providing relevant insights faster.
* Reducing the effort required to create and refine reports.
* Shortening the path to actionable information for the operator.

## 💡 Why it matters
Acre Via simplifies the management of security systems for operators. It is one of three nominees in the ID & Access Control category, alongside other security solutions:

| Product | Company | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Acre Via | Acre Security, USA | AI assistant for interacting with the AAC platform via generative AI |
| Stid Architect II | Stid Security, France | Modular access control readers supporting multiple identification technologies |
| HID M1 Transparent Gateway | HID Global, USA | Gateway enabling encrypted OSDP communication for upgrading existing infrastructure |
