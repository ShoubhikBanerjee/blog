# SpaceXAI Releases Grokipedia v0.3 Update with New Design Tweaks

SpaceXAI has released a v0.3 update for Grokipedia, its AI-powered competitor to Wikipedia. The update introduces a new logo and visual refreshes to the homepage, article pages, and live edits page, following a period where the site recently resumed incorporating edits.

## ⚙️ Key details

Benji Taylor, head of design at SpaceXAI, describes the update as a “newly refreshed Grokipedia.” The v0.3 release follows a development timeline that began with v0.1 in late October 2025, which used AI-cloned pages from Wikipedia, and a v0.2 update a month later. While a Lawfare report in August stated that no entry had changed in more than three months, the site has recently started updating again.

## 🎨 Visual and Functional Changes

* **Homepage**: Previously consisting of a logo and search bar, the new homepage now includes:
    * A list of featured articles featuring digital representations of books that can be spun around.
    * A most-read articles section utilizing a horizontally-scrolling shelf of “book” spines.
    * A tracker for “latest edits.”
* **Live Edits Page**: Updated to allow users to see more recent changes at once.
* **Article Pages**: Now feature smaller spacing between lines of text and a redesigned format for the key facts tables located at the top of articles.
