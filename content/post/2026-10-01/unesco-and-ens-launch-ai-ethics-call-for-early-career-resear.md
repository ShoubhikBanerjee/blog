# UNESCO and ENS Launch AI Ethics Call for Early‑Career Researchers

UNESCO and the AI and Society Institute at École normale supérieure (ENS‑PSL) have opened a call for papers inviting researchers who earned their PhD within the last five years to submit proposals on the ethics of artificial intelligence.

## 🔍 Overview
- The initiative follows UNESCO’s 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence.
- It aims to promote emerging research, foster interdisciplinary exchange, and link scholarship with public policy and practice.

## 📅 Key Details
- Selected proposals will be invited to present at a one‑day academic event in Paris on **10 December 2026**.
- The event will gather emerging scholars, established researchers, and institutional representatives for thematic discussions and expert feedback.
- General participation is open to everyone; registration will be available on UNESCO’s website.

## 🎓 Eligibility
- Researchers who completed a PhD within the past five years.
- Work must address issues related to AI ethics.
- Applications are welcomed from all disciplines and geographic regions.
- Interdisciplinary, cross‑sectoral, theoretical, empirical, comparative, and practice‑oriented contributions are encouraged.
- Co‑authored proposals are allowed if all authors meet the eligibility criteria.

## 📝 Submission Guidelines
- Submit an extended abstract of **800–1,000 words** (references excluded) in English, in Word or PDF format.
- Abstracts must be anonymized (no author names or identifying information).
- Proposals will be reviewed by laureates and jury members of the UNESCO Beruniy Prize for Scientific Research on the Ethics of AI.
- Successful applicants will be notified by **15 November 2026** and asked to deliver a full paper of **5,000–7,000 words** by **1 December 2026**.

## ✈️ Travel Support
- One selected author will receive travel support to attend the Paris event, alongside members of the UNESCO Beruniy Prize International Jury and Laureates.

## 📚 Post‑Event Opportunities
- Proceedings will be compiled by UNESCO and the AI and Society Institute.
- Selected authors may adapt their research for publication in the Tribune of the Conseil de l’intelligence artificielle et du numérique (CIANum), reaching a broader public and policy audience.
