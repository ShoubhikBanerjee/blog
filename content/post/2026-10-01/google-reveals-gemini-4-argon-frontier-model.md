# Google Reveals Gemini 4 Argon Frontier Model

Google has revealed its next AI frontier model, Gemini 4 Argon, designed for complex workflows in professional environments.

## 🔍 Overview
According to chief AI architect and Google DeepMind SVP Koray Kavukcuoglu, the model delivers "frontier performance in complex workflows across real-world software engineering, enterprise knowledge work like legal and finance, and cybersecurity defense."

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Internal Use:** The model is already powering Google's "internal workflows," including tasks such as large-scale codebase migrations.
* **Performance:** Google shared a chart indicating that Gemini 4 fares better than competing models from Anthropic and OpenAI across many benchmarks.
* **Safety Measures:** Google is strengthening "critical frontier safeguards" prior to a broader rollout, which include:
    * Monitoring for misalignment
    * Defending against prompt injection attacks
    * Defending against misuse

## 🚀 Availability
Access to Gemini 4 Argon is currently limited to ensure the model is not misaligned. Current availability includes:
* A "set of trusted cyber defenders."
* Google's internal workflows.

Kavukcuoglu stated that Google is "actively engaged in the U.S. government’s voluntary process for pre-release model access while we gradually expand access."
