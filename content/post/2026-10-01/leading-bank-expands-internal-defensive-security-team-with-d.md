# Leading Bank Expands Internal Defensive Security Team with Detection Engineers

A leading bank is appointing Detection Engineers to join a growing internal defensive security team, transitioning detection engineering from a secondary responsibility within the Cloud function to a specialist, full-time discipline.

## 🔍 Overview
The role is focused on defensive security and threat hunting, prioritizing the creation of high-quality detections over increasing volume. The position is part of a global team operating across the UK, Poland, and Mexico.

## ⚙️ Key Details
**Responsibilities:**
* Develop high-quality detection use cases utilizing internal security data and external threat intelligence.
* Translate intelligence into effective detections for the organization.
* Use Red Team outputs to identify gaps, understand attack techniques, and determine where to detect malicious activity.
* Conduct threat hunting to identify opportunities for improved defensive coverage.
* Optimize detection capability by working with technology and security teams.
* Deliver engineering through Scrum, Agile, and sprint-based ways of working.

**Requirements:**
* 3-5 years of experience in security, development, or technology operations.
* Hands-on experience in writing and developing security detections.
* Proficiency in threat hunting and defensive security.
* Experience translating threat intelligence into actionable detections.
* Background working in a large financial services organization or a similarly complex, highly regulated environment.
* Comfort working within an Agile/Scrum delivery environment.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Role:** Contractor
* **Location:** Sheffield or London (1-2 days per week onsite)
* **Contract Length:** Initial 6-month contract
* **Rate:** £600 per day via umbrella (Inside IR35)
