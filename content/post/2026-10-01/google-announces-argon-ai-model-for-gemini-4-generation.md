# Google Announces Argon AI Model for Gemini 4 Generation

Alphabet's Google has announced "Argon," a new top-tier artificial intelligence model designed to anchor its Gemini 4 generation of models in an effort to catch up to rivals OpenAI and Anthropic.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Size:** A company spokesperson stated the model is larger in size than Google's previous advanced "Pro" models.
* **Purpose:** Built for complex workloads.
* **Performance:** 
    * Google describes it as "comparable to frontier models like (OpenAI's) Astra and (Anthropic's) Opus on key coding and cyber benchmarks."
    * According to a company press release, Argon posted better marks than Astra and Opus in self-reported performance on several industry benchmarks.
    * Argon remained behind on certain other metrics, including two of the four coding-related benchmarks included in the release.

## 🚀 Availability

* **Current Access:** The model is being provided to select cybersecurity partners.
* **Government Coordination:** Google is participating in the Trump administration's voluntary process for pre-release model access.
* **Public Release:** Google has not provided a timeline for public release.

## 💡 Why it matters

* **Product Changes:** Google no longer plans to release Gemini 3.5 Pro, which CEO Sundar Pichai had originally said would be released in June.
* **Organizational Shifts:** Google overhauled its DeepMind AI lab; founder and CEO Demis Hassabis stepped aside and several Gemini model leaders left the company.
* **Competitive Landscape:** Delays to AI models have caused Google to fall behind OpenAI and Anthropic. While Pichai pushed back against the notion that Google was losing ground in July, the company has recently shifted messaging from touting cutting-edge capabilities to highlighting cost advantages compared to competitor models.
