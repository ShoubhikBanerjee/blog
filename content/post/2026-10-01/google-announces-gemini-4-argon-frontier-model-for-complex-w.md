# Google Announces Gemini 4 Argon Frontier Model for Complex Workflows

Google has announced Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier model designed to sustain deep reasoning across complex, long-horizon workflows. The model is currently rolling out to a set of trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program.

## 🔍 Overview
Gemini 4 Argon is designed for frontier performance in real-world software engineering, cybersecurity defense, and enterprise knowledge work, such as finance and legal. Internally, thousands of Googlers are using the model for specialized coding tasks, writing quality, and deeper research.

## ⚙️ Key Details
To support complex use cases, Google has expanded the model's output token limit to 1M tokens, increasing it from the previous 64K tokens. This allows the model to generate hundreds of thousands of tokens in a single trajectory to solve difficult problems.

**Pricing:**
- Input tokens: $2 per million
- Output tokens: $10 per million
- Cached input tokens: 95% off the input token price

## 💡 Why it matters
Argon has demonstrated performance across several specialized benchmarks and real-world applications:

*   **Software Engineering:** Sets a new state of the art on DeepSWE v1.1 (77.9%).
*   **Economic Impact:** Leads the Vals Index (finance, coding, legal, and tax work), as well as Vals Finance Agent v2 and Harvey’s Legal Agent Benchmark.
*   **Business Execution:** Ranks #1 on AutomationBench with a score of 51.3%.
*   **Video Understanding:** Achieves state of the art on LVBench with a score of 91.7%.
*   **Cybersecurity:** Ties for first place on CWE-bench v1 (68%) and can autonomously find, validate, and patch critical software vulnerabilities.

**Internal Google Implementations:**
*   **Quantum Computing:** Optimized spacetime resources for subroutines, beating a published baseline by 40% in minutes.
*   **Data Center Optimization:** Argon agents identified memory optimizations that freed over 300 TiB of memory, with total estimated savings between 500 TiB and 1 PiB.
*   **Code Migration:** Migrating C/C++ codebases to Rust, ranging from core libraries like re2 and libgav1 up to 800K+ lines for the Fuchsia Zircon kernel. For libgav1, Argon produced a memory-safe video decoder that runs 2.7x faster than the previous Rust port.

## 🚀 Availability
Because of the level of frontier capabilities, Google is using a phased release approach:

*   **Cyber Defenders:** Available through the Fairwind Program. Trusted defenders and internal Google teams receive the model without cyber guardrails to leverage full defense capabilities. Wiz is currently using the model for its Scan for Good initiative.
*   **General Release:** The model will be made available to developers, enterprises, and consumers after further iteration on guardrails based on early tester feedback.
