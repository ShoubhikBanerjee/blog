# Datavault AI Addresses Securities Litigation and Outlines Platform Operations

Datavault AI Inc. (Nasdaq: DVLT) has issued a statement responding to recently filed shareholder securities litigation against the company and certain of its officers.

## 🔍 Overview
Datavault AI is a Philadelphia-based Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) company that focuses on transforming real-world assets and data into secure, monetizable digital assets. The company is currently managing several legal matters, including:

* Securities litigation filed by shareholders.
* Litigation filed by Datavault AI and its CEO, Nathaniel Bradley, against Dan David and Wolfpack Research, alleging defamation, false light, tortious interference, and unjust enrichment following an October 31, 2025 short-seller report.

## 🧩 How it works
The company's integrated platform utilizes the following technologies to support the lifecycle of digital assets from identification to monetization:

* AI-driven inference layer
* Data valuation and tokenization
* Cybersecurity
* High-performance computing
* Exchange technologies

## ⚙️ Key details
Datavault AI operates through two synergistic divisions:

| Division | Technologies and Businesses |
| :--- | :--- |
| Data Science | Data Vault®, DataValue®, DataScore®, cybersecurity, tokenization, and exchange capabilities |
| Acoustic Science | WiSA®, ADIO®, spatial audio, data-over-sound technologies, CompuSystems, Inc. (operated under Event Citadel), and API Media Innovations Inc. |

## 🚀 Availability
The company is currently focusing on several commercial and operational objectives:

* Commercialization of its technology platform.
* Launch and expansion of exchange infrastructure.
* Scaling of SanQtum.
* Integration of pending and recently acquired strategic assets.
* Converting contracted opportunities into recognized revenue and commercial activity.
