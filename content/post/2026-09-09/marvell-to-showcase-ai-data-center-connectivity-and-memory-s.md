# Marvell to Showcase AI Data Center Connectivity and Memory Solutions

Marvell Technology, Inc. has announced it will present its portfolio of AI data center connectivity and memory solutions at the AI Infra Summit 2026, taking place September 15 to 17 in Santa Clara, California.

## ⚙️ Key details

Marvell will highlight advances in its end-to-end data center connectivity portfolio for scale-up, scale-out, and scale-across applications at Booth #704. The showcase includes:

| Solution | Function/Capability |
| :--- | :--- |
| Marvell® Photonic Fabric™ Technology | Enables memory sharing across multiple racks, extending scale beyond traditional distance constraints |
| Marvell RELIANT™ Interconnect Telemetry Platform | Provides real-time visibility into the health and performance of AI data center infrastructure connectivity across optical and electrical links |
| Marvell Teralynx® T100 | 102 Tbps Ethernet switch device delivering increased bandwidth and efficiency for large-scale AI networks |
| PCIe 6.0 Switch | 256-lane design to connect more accelerators and devices within scale-up AI systems |
| CXL-based memory solutions | Supports expansion, compression, pooling, and near-memory acceleration to reduce dataset movement between XPUs and accelerate large-scale vector databases |
| DPUs and Ethernet switching | Deliver high-performance access to NVMe storage across the network |
| PCIe® 6.0 NVMe SSD controller | Optimized for next-generation enterprise and AI storage |

## 🔍 Overview

During the summit, Marvell executives will participate in panels and presentations regarding connectivity and networking for next-generation AI data center infrastructure. This includes a keynote presentation titled “AI Inference at Scale” delivered by Dave Lazovsky, Executive Vice President and General Manager of the Data Center Networking Business Group.
