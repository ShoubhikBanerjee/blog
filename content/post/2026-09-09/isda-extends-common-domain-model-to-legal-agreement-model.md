# ISDA Extends Common Domain Model to Legal Agreement Model

The International Swaps and Derivatives Association (ISDA) has extended the Common Domain Model (CDM) to represent new areas of its legal agreement model.

## ⚙️ Key details

The recent extension to the Common Domain Model (CDM) represents two previously undeveloped areas of the legal agreement model:
* Umbrella agreements
* Contract amendments
