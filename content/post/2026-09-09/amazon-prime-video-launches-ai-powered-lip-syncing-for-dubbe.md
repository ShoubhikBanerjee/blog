# Amazon Prime Video Launches AI-Powered Lip-Syncing for Dubbed Content

Amazon’s Prime Video is introducing a new AI-powered feature designed to align an actor’s mouth movements with human-dubbed audio.

## 🧩 How it works
Prime Video states that the technology utilizes a combination of the following to make lip movements match translated speech:
* AI
* Visual effects technologies

## 🚀 Availability
The feature is currently available to subscribers globally for the following content:
* *Maxton Hall* (Seasons 1 and 2): Available with the English dub.

Prime Video plans to expand the feature to "additional titles" in the future and will launch it with *Maxton Hall* season 3 on December 9th.

## ⚙️ Key details
Last year, Prime Video conducted experiments with "AI-aided" dubbing in Spanish and English across 12 movies and TV shows, including:

| Title | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| El Cid: La Leyenda | Subject of AI-aided dubbing experiments |
| Mi Mamá Lora | Subject of AI-aided dubbing experiments |
| Long Lost | Subject of AI-aided dubbing experiments |

## 💡 Why it matters
Other platforms have recently introduced similar capabilities:
* Meta and YouTube launched an AI-powered auto-dubbing feature for creators, which includes an optional "lip sync" setting to make creators appear to speak in a user's preferred language.
