# New Cropping Tool Allows Horizontal or Vertical Cuts to Remove Image Middle Sections

On 25th October 2026 a new cropping tool was released that lets users create a horizontal or vertical cut across an image and slide the two sides relative to each other, effectively cropping out a section in the middle of an image.

## 🔍 Overview
- Date: 25th October 2026
- Purpose: to enable cropping out a middle section of an image, such as redacting an irrelevant prompt from a transcript.
- Context: useful when stitching screenshots together with Tailor on iPhone.

## 🧩 How it works
- The tool creates a horizontal or vertical cut across the image.
- Users can slide the two sides of the cut relative to each other.
- The adjusted sides define the portion to keep, removing the middle section.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Supports both horizontal and vertical cuts.
- Operates on any image where a middle section needs removal.
- Designed for workflow where long images are assembled from multiple screenshots.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a simple way to redact content without needing separate image editors.
- Saves time for users who frequently combine screenshots and need to remove unwanted portions.
