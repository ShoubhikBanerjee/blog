# Kevin Roose launches video‑first AI podcast Machine Gods after leaving NYT

Kevin Roose, former New York Times reporter and host of the Hard Fork podcast, has launched a new tech podcast called Machine Gods with fellow journalist Casey Newton.

## 📚 Book – The AGI Chronicles

- Author: Kevin Roose.
- Described as a meticulously reported book about the main frontier labs and the culture shift they’ve undergone as AI becomes a more commercial product.

## 🎙️ New Podcast – Machine Gods

- Co‑founder and co‑host: Kevin Roose and Casey Newton.
- Launched last week.
- Produced in partnership with NPR.
- Designed to be video‑first; the creators say "you can’t do a podcast now without doing video on top of it" and they want to give "a lot of reasons to want to watch as well as listen."
- The goal is to make a high‑quality journalism video experience on YouTube, even though "high‑quality journalism" is a small part of the platform.

## 🏷️ CEO Title

- In the first episode Kevin and Casey discuss that Kevin technically has the title of CEO.
- The title was decided by a coin flip: "I won the coin flip and we needed someone."
- The CEO title is needed "to know who to haul in front of Congress when our first scandal inevitably breaks."
- Kevin notes he’s been a CEO for about five minutes and often forgets the role.

## 📺 Media Strategy

- Emphasis on video: "We want it to be video‑first."
- Aim to "give a shit about what it looks like" more than before.
- Belief that "the future of media is middle‑aged journalists pivoting to YouTube."
- Acknowledgment that "You’ve been trying to undo the YouTube‑pilling on your end, but … it’s clearly the most important media platform in the world right now."
