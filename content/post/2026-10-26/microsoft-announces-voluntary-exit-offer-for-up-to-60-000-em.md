# Microsoft Announces Voluntary Exit Offer for Up to 60,000 Employees, Including AI Teams

Microsoft is extending a voluntary buyout program to as many as 60,000 staff members across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and India. The offer reaches a broader slice of the company’s roughly 223,000 employees and includes many teams that work on artificial‑intelligence products.

## 🔍 Overview
- Voluntary exit offers are being made to up to 60,000 employees.
- The program is intended to allow staff to leave with “financial support and career planning resources” while the company trims headcount without another round of layoffs.
- The offer is described as “new for us” by Microsoft chief people officer Amy Coleman.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Eligibility covers a wide range of groups, including:
  - AI experiences, Copilot, CoreAI, Windows + Devices, Xbox, finance, and human‑resources teams.
  - Cloud + AI, MicrosoftAI, and corporate, external, and legal‑affairs teams (partial participation).
- Teams **not** participating:
  - Marketing, commercial business, discovery and quantum, and security teams.
- The offer is **not** based on age, length of service, or performance, according to an internal HR note.
- It differs from an earlier voluntary‑retirement program that was limited to about 9,000 U.S. employees meeting a combined age‑and‑service threshold of 70.

| Team Group | Participation Status |
|------------|----------------------|
| AI experiences, Copilot, CoreAI, Windows + Devices, Xbox, finance, HR | Eligible (full) |
| Cloud + AI, MicrosoftAI, corporate, external, legal affairs | Eligible (partial) |
| Marketing, commercial business, discovery & quantum, security | Not eligible |

## 💡 Why It Matters
- The voluntary exit offer follows a layoff of 4,800 employees earlier in the year, primarily affecting Xbox and commercial‑sales staff.
- Microsoft’s leadership frames the program as a way to “help people focus on the work that matters most, build new skills and, where it makes sense, find new opportunities across Microsoft.”
- The company states it may consider future voluntary exit offers “where there is a business and workforce need.”

## 📢 Official Statements
- Amy Coleman wrote, “The Voluntary Exit Offer is new for us. We’ll learn from it. At the same time, we’ll keep helping people focus on the work that matters most, build new skills and, where it makes sense, find new opportunities across Microsoft.”
- An internal HR note confirms the offer “isn’t based on age, length of service, or performance.”

## 📅 Recent Context
- The voluntary exit program arrives months after the 4,800‑person layoff.
- Xbox CEO Asha Sharma is reported to be planning new rounds of layoffs.
- The new offer does not target Microsoft’s commercial‑sales teams, distinguishing it from the earlier retirement program.
