# Anthropic files S‑1 to launch Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT

Anthropic has filed its S‑1 prospectus with the SEC, signaling its intention to go public on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol “ANT”.

## 🔍 Overview
- Anthropic filed its S‑1 prospectus with the SEC, preparing to list on Nasdaq under the ticker “ANT”.
- The filing describes a plan “to ‘revolutionize every area of human endeavor’ with AI”.
- The prospectus is public after a confidential filing in June; it includes future plans and risk disclosures.

## 📈 Financial Snapshot
- First half of the year: $80.7 billion net loss on $16.3 billion revenue.
- The majority of the loss stems from accounting charges related to notes issued for funding.

## 🧑‍💼 Leadership Compensation (2025)
- CEO Dario Amodei: nearly $18 million, primarily stock and option awards.
- President & Director Daniela Amodei: $16.4 million.

## ⚠️ Risk Factors
- Possibility of AI models developing “forms of sentience”.
- Potential “AI‑specific taxes”.
- Unfavorable outcomes in ongoing copyright disputes over model training data.
- Risk of “catastrophic” AI‑related incidents; the filing notes that likely no single insurer—or the insurance industry in the aggregate—could underwrite such risk.

## 🌐 Vision & Why It Matters
- Anthropic states: “We believe that AI is one of the most consequential technologies in human history.”
- If managed well, AI could “rapidly accelerate scientific innovation, greatly increase economic growth rates, bring cures for major diseases within reach, and solve problems that have resisted generations of human effort”.
- The company emphasizes delivering these benefits while “navigating and preventing the risks” of a fast‑moving technological revolution.
