# 🧩 HSRD-100: 100 High-Quality 3D Human Scans for AI, Graphics & Research

*Digital Reality Lab releases groundbreaking dataset for 3D human modeling and AI research*

---

## 🚀 Introduction

We're excited to introduce **HSRD-100**, a free dataset of **100 high-quality 3D human scans**,
released by Digital Reality Lab and hosted on Hugging Face.

It's part of our growing collection on Hugging Face and the larger HumanScanRepository project —
 a professional library of 3D human data for AI, graphics, XR, and research.

---

## 📦 What's inside HSRD-100

This comprehensive dataset offers unprecedented quality and detail for 3D human modeling:

- **100 scans:** 10 individuals × 10 unique poses
- **High-resolution photogrammetry:** captured via a 112-camera rig
- **Multi-LOD meshes:**
  - LOD0 ≈ 1 M polygons + 16 K textures - LOD1 ≈ 100 K polygons + 8 K textures
  - LOD2 ≈ 10 K polygons + 4 K textures
- **Metadata:** `person_meta.json`, `pose_meta.json`, scan parameters, and file manifests
- **Source data:** RealityCapture projects, photographic sets, and preview images
- **License:** CC BY-4.0 — free for academic + commercial use (with attribution)

--- 
## ⚙️ Applications

The versatility of HSRD-100 opens doors to numerous cutting-edge applications:

- **AI & Computer Vision:** pose estimation, reconstruction, segmentation, neural rendering
- **3D Graphics & Avatars:** realistic human base meshes for games, film, XR
- **Virtual Fashion:** simulate garment fitting and cloth behavior on real human geometry
- **Education & Prototyping:** compact dataset for universities and labs

--- 
## ⚠️ Limitations

While HSRD-100 demonstrates quality and balance, it's a **compact dataset**:

- Only 10 individuals → limited demographic diversity
- 10 poses per subject → no extreme or dynamic motions
- Captured in studio lighting → consistent but not "in-the-wild"

For large-scale ML or production research, use the **full HumanScanRepository** (20,000+ scans).

--- 
## 🛡️ Ethical Us

All subjects provided informed consent; no biometric identification or surveillance uses are
permitted. Use responsibly under the **CC BY-4.0 license** with attribution to Digital Reality Lab.
