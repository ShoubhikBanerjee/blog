# Google's Gemini 4 Carbon Now Generally Available with Updated Pricing

Google has expanded access to Gemini 4 Carbon, its advanced AI model, making it available to all users after initially limiting access to cyber defenders two weeks ago.

## 🔍 Overview
Gemini 4 Carbon, positioned as Google's most capable AI model to date, is now accessible beyond its restricted launch group. The model demonstrates proficiency in complex tasks like code generation for 3D animations and multi-agent game development workflows.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Pricing**: Launch pricing set at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, scheduled to increase to $4 and $20 respectively on December 1st
- **Performance Tiers**: Offers three "thinking effort" levels (low/medium/high) with medium as default
- **Capabilities**: Generates production-ready code (e.g., Three.js animations), coordinates subagents for complex projects via Google Antigravity's /swarm feature

## 🧩 How It Works
- Uses Google Antigravity IDE for development workflows
- Supports subagent coordination through /swarm commands to manage tasks like 3D asset creation and procedural audio generation
- Converts natural language prompts into executable code (e.g., standalone HTML animations)

## 🚀 Availability
Now available to all users after a limited cyber defender preview. Pricing remains at launch rates until December 1st.

## 💡 Why It Matters
Gemini 4 Carbon enables sophisticated multi-agent development workflows, as demonstrated by user-generated projects like:
- A Three.js animation of a mech traversing NYC boroughs
- An open-world 3D game exploring Google's campus using Blender assets and WebGL shaders
