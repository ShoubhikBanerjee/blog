# Google launches Tabletop, an AI‑powered collaborative planning tool

Google has introduced Tabletop, an experimental AI tool that lets users create customized collaborative hubs for personal planning.

## 🔍 Overview
- Tabletop is a new experimental AI tool from Google.
- It generates personalized interactive tools such as savings trackers, chore charts, event plans, and more.
- The interface resembles workplace management apps like Asana and Monday, but is aimed at personal use.

## 🧩 How it works
- Users add links, files, and ideas, then prompt @tabletop or choose a starter template.
- “Just add in your links, files and ideas, then prompt @tabletop — or pick a starter template — to generate custom interactive trip itineraries, dinner polls, expense splitters and tournament brackets,” Google Labs design and engineering lead Roman Nurik said in the blog post.
- Once a space is ready, an invite link can be shared in a group chat for simultaneous collaboration, similar to a Google Doc.
- “Once your space is ready, drop the invite link in your group chat to let everyone jump in and use it at the same time like a Google Doc.”

## ⚙️ Key details
- Customizable collaborative spaces for personal planning needs.
- Supports creating tools like savings trackers, chore charts, event plans.
- Visual style similar to Asana/Monday with built‑in AI generation.

## 🚀 Availability
- Tabletop is in beta and accessible via a waitlist.
- Currently limited to users aged 18 + in the United States.
- The age requirement may be temporary while Tabletop is in public testing.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Offers a less formal alternative to workplace software for families and friends.
- Enables real‑time shared planning without needing separate apps.
