# OpenAI Launches Verified Business Agents in ChatGPT Beta

OpenAI has announced verified AI 'Business Agents' for ChatGPT, enabling direct interactions between users and brands for tasks like comparison shopping. This marks an expansion of ChatGPT's capabilities into business-focused applications.

## 🔍 Overview
ChatGPT's new Business Agents are AI representatives of companies, designed to assist users with product inquiries, comparisons, and follow-up questions. These agents operate in clearly labeled, separate chat threads distinct from standard ChatGPT conversations.

## 🧩 How it works
- Businesses create custom agents via **Business Hub**, providing instructions and context.
- Users encounter agents when a participating business appears in a ChatGPT response.
- Verified agents enable direct, brand-specific conversations about offerings.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Verification**: Companies claim their identity through Business Hub (verification methods vary).
- **Business Control**: Agents follow brand-defined instructions and context.
- **User Experience**: Agent chats are visually separated and explicitly labeled.
- **Insights**: Businesses access metrics like ChatGPT mentions and discovery topics via Business Hub.

## 🚀 Availability
The feature launches in beta for eligible US-based e-commerce and online service businesses. OpenAI states this is an 'early step' in integrating businesses into ChatGPT.

## 💡 Why it matters
OpenAI aims to enhance decision-making utility for users while offering businesses new engagement pathways. Future plans include exploring expanded connection methods beyond agents and ChatGPT.
