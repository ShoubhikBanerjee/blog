# Anthropic launches AI office tools for non-technical workers ahead of IPO

Anthropic unveiled new AI-powered productivity tools targeting knowledge workers at a San Francisco event, expanding beyond its enterprise coding focus. The launch aims to address adoption friction for non-developers while building momentum pre-IPO.

## 🔍 Overview
Anthropic introduced three core tools for collaboration and analysis: Claude Meetings, Claude Sheets, and Claude Whiteboards. These join existing products like Claude Docs and Slides, with a focus on automating tasks for professionals in meetings, brainstorming, and metrics analysis. The company positions this suite as a solution for "everybody else" beyond technical users.

## 🧩 Key Features
- **Claude Meetings**: AI joins virtual meetings to take notes and generate summaries.
- **Claude Whiteboards**: Collaborative digital canvas for real-time idea visualization.
- **Claude Sheets**: AI-assisted spreadsheet creation and data analysis.
- **Interactive Connectors**: Embed partnered apps (e.g., Adobe PDF editor, HubSpot campaign tracker) directly within Claude.
- **Artifacts UI**: Graphical interface for tool interaction, now generally available.

## ⚙️ Technical Details
| Tool          | Availability          | Key Integration               |
|---------------|-----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Meetings      | Teams/Enterprise beta | Virtual meeting platforms     |
| Whiteboards   | All paid plans (beta) | N/A                           |
| Sheets        | All paid plans (beta) | N/A                           |
| Data (Dashboards) | All paid plans | Business intelligence metrics |

## 🚀 Availability
- Claude Whiteboards and Sheets: Beta access for all paid subscribers.
- Claude Meetings: Beta limited to Teams and Enterprise tiers.
- Claude Data (formerly Dashboards): Now generally available to all paid users.

## 💡 Why It Matters
Anthropic faces investor pressure to demonstrate revenue growth before its anticipated high-value IPO. This expansion into non-technical workflows mirrors strategies from Google (Gemini) and OpenAI (ChatGPT integrations), intensifying competition in AI-driven productivity tools. The move aims to broaden Anthropic's market reach while deepening platform engagement through embedded third-party services.
