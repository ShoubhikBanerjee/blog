# IGF jurors reject AI‑used games as indie community debates Big Walk

The Independent Games Festival (IGF) jurors released a statement refusing to vote for any game that uses generative AI, sparking a heated debate after House House disclosed minimal AI use in its indie title **Big Walk**.

## 🔍 Overview
- IGF jurors announced they will not vote for games that use generative AI in “any capacity.”
- The indie game community is in disarray over AI and Big Walk.

## ⚖️ IGF Jury Position
- The statement, posted by Austin Walker, notes a “significant increase” in games developed with generative AI tools and “fairly intense discussion and disagreement” with no universal consensus.
- Jurors signed the statement include:
  | Name | Notable Work |
  |------|--------------|
  | Sam Barlow | Creator of *Telling Lies* and *Immortality* |
  | Lena Raine | Composer for *Celeste* |
- The jurors clarified the statement does **not** speak for IGF as an organization or for all jury members, and some feared professional blowback.

## 🎮 Big Walk’s AI Use
- House House, developer of *Big Walk*, said AI was used “sparingly” in the final stretch to help work through difficult technical challenges and bugs.
- They estimate **well under 0.1%** of the game’s code originated from LLM‑generated snippets.
- Primary AI use case: researching issues via chatbots, including fixing a shadow problem by modifying LLM‑suggested code.
- The studio disclosed AI usage in its IGF submission but not on the Steam page because the platform does not request such disclosure.
- House House stated:
  - “This is not an endorsement of, or argument for, the use of AI tools, however minimal.”
  - “We have some concern that voluntary disclosures like this might contribute to the normalisation of this technology, or its treatment as ‘inevitable’.”
  - “We support the work of those who are trying to limit the harm generative AI is doing to videogames and our world, and apologise that Big Walk’s LLM usage may not meet their standards.”
- The studio admitted it had no AI policy during development, called this a “failure,” and pledged to create a comprehensive policy.

## 📋 Statements & Beliefs
- The jurors’ statement lists beliefs that generative AI has **negative effects**, that AI‑generated code is **not an exception to these harms**, and that use of AI tools is **not a forgone conclusion** in the industry.
- Sam Barlow clarified that *Big Walk* was not the “impetus” for the IGF discussion but became a “tricky edge case” about minimal AI use.
- Barlow emphasized the stance is not a call to boycott the game, but a reflection on what an indie award should represent.
- Austin Walker shared a longer blog post outlining his position on AI and the IGF awards (added November 10th).

## 🤝 Community Reaction
- The combination of the jurors’ hardline stance and House House’s admission has led to disagreement and uncertainty within the indie development community regarding acceptable AI usage.
- Some developers worry about “professional blowback” for signing the jurors’ statement.
- The debate highlights a lack of universal consensus on AI’s role in indie game creation.
