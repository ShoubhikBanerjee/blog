# Adobe Unveils New AI Experiments for Creative Cloud

Adobe announced new AI experiments today that can transform images into explorable 3D scenes, design AI characters by talking to them, and turn photo editing into a copy/paste process.

## 🧩 Overview

| Project Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Project Screen Test** | Designed to make AI-generated characters easier to develop and use for video content. |
| **Project Sight Span** | Reframes photographs and images by turning them into a 3D environment that you can navigate through and reposition. |
| **Project Sound Bender** | An AI audio mixing feature that lets you customize sound effects as if clicking a cursor around a color wheel. |
| **Project Rinse Repeat** | A feature that lets you copy edits and automatically apply them to other objects or people in the same photo. |

## 🎬 Project Screen Test

The first tool, Project Screen Test, is designed to make AI-generated characters easier to develop and use for video content. Instead of just describing how a character should sound and behave in text prompts, the tool lets you talk to the character in an audition-like environment.

You start by describing base personality traits, such as "anxious, shy, awkward," and giving the character a voice by selecting a preset, describing how it sounds in a prompt, or uploading sample audio. You can then expand its persona by conversing with the character as if you are talking on FaceTime, outlining backstory, temperament, goals, flaws, fears, and movement quirks. Once happy, the tool generates animated screen tests with the character by describing an environment or scenario. This is currently a standalone creative exercise and is not clear how it will feature in existing Adobe AI video tools.

## 🖼️ Project Sight Span

Project Sight Span reframes photographs and images by turning them into a 3D environment that you can navigate through and reposition. The 3D space generates objects and backgrounds outside the original frame that feel like a natural part of the image. While visuals in the 3D environment are initially pixelated and ill-defined, the resolution quality is restored when you select your chosen angle.

Sight Span works with any flat image, including photos, paintings, and illustrations, and can preserve and replicate artistic styles. Even repositioned tattoos and facial features in photographs remain surprisingly recognizable. The tool allows for aggressive repositioning and changing the shooting point entirely, though the author notes it feels far less deserving of being called a photo afterward.

## 🔊 Project Sound Bender

Project Sound Bender is an AI audio mixing feature that lets users customize sound effects as if clicking a cursor around a color wheel. Users upload a video clip and describe the desired audio in a text prompt, such as "bubbling sci-fi cauldron noises." Sound Bender generates sound effects and automatically syncs them to the video.

The tool provides a square sound canvas where each corner automatically assigns a relevant AI-generated sound, such as dense gurgling with popping bubbles, rumbling boiling rocks, pitched bloops, and crackling electric fire. Users can drag their cursor around the canvas to select which noises stand out most or customize each corner with different sound effects.

## ✂️ Project Rinse Repeat

Adobe showcased Project Rinse Repeat, which lets users copy edits and automatically apply them to other objects or people in the same photo. For example, if a user painstakingly airbrushes out acne or blemishes on one person, Rinse Repeat will adapt those specific changes to anyone else in a group who needs similar touchups. It can also be used to add shadows to products in marketing graphics, even if the objects are different shapes and sizes.

Rinse Repeat is built around Photoshop, and the edits applied to other subjects remain fully editable because the tool recreates them with native Photoshop tools.

## 🚀 Availability

These "sneaks" are tech previews of capabilities that might eventually be developed into tools for Adobe’s Creative Cloud apps or Firefly platform, so there is no guarantee they will graduate into available features. Almost all of these projects are built around Adobe’s generative AI developments, and given AI tools now play a central role in the company’s growth strategy, it is likely that at least some of the experiments will see an official launch.

A notable theme across these sneaks is making complicated photo editing, filmmaking, and audio mixing workflows feel more accessible and intuitive. "Making these interactions more intuitive is also about giving experienced creatives more precise control," senior principle Adobe research scientist Gautham Mysore told The Verge. Examples of this include Sound Bender’s sonic exploration capability and Sight Span’s camera positioning, which allow professionals to apply their judgment and refine a result more directly.
