# Disney Debuts AI-Powered Anzellans Animatronics Using AWS Cloud

Walt Disney Imagineering R&D has unveiled three new Audio-Animatronic prototypes that use Amazon’s AWS cloud and AI to enable real-time responses to guest interactions, marking a significant evolution in animatronic technology.

## 🧩 How it works
The Anzellans—Vilo Vele, Kado Bre’a, and Jibu Bre’a—are based on Star Wars alien characters featured in The Mandalorian and Grogu film. Each animatronic has a distinct personality and uses hidden cameras and microphones to capture guest movements and verbal interactions. These inputs are processed by multiple AI models running on AWS cloud servers.

- One “fast-decision model” generates quick responses like making eye contact to simulate attention.
- Larger models plan out longer interactions.

## ⚙️ Key details
The Anzellans’ movements and verbal responses aren’t generated in real time by AI. Instead:

| Animatronic | Personality Traits |
|-------------|--------------------|
| Vilo Vele   | Based on Star Wars alien characters from The Mandalorian and Grogu film |
| Kado Bre’a  | Based on Star Wars alien characters from The Mandalorian and Grogu film |
| Jibu Bre’a  | Based on Star Wars alien characters from The Mandalorian and Grogu film |

Every motion and line spoken is prewritten by Disney Imagineers to ensure the characters remain true to their fictional origins and individual quirks.

## 🚀 Availability
The Anzellans will debut publicly at the AWS re:Invent conference in Las Vegas later this month. Though initially showcased at an AI tech event, the technology may eventually expand to attractions like Star Wars: Galaxy’s Edge at Disney parks.

Unlike other Disney robots such as the Olaf animatronic—which previously relied on a hidden puppeteer using a Steam Deck—the Anzellans operate autonomously with no need for out-of-sight human operators.

## 💡 Why it matters
While the integration of AI and cloud computing enables more responsive and interactive animatronics, the system still relies on scripted content. This approach allows for dynamic interaction timing without deviating from established narratives, blending innovation with storytelling control.
