# SGD Dynamics Outside Teacher Subspace Degrade Generalization in Two-Layer Networks

Researchers analyze how first-layer parameters orthogonal to the teacher subspace affect generalization in two-layer neural networks trained with stochastic gradient descent (SGD) on a multi-index teacher model. The study decomposes weight dynamics and identifies a mechanism where training induces anisotropy that harms test performance.
