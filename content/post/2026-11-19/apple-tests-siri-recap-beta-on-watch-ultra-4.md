# Apple Tests Siri Recap Beta on Watch Ultra 4

Apple is testing Siri Recap, a beta feature on the Apple Watch Ultra 4 that ambiently listens to conversations and generates AI summaries. The author compared it with Amazon's Bee Frontier, noting Apple's minimalist, privacy-first approach versus Amazon's maximalist one. Both require explicit disclosures to people nearby.

## 🔍 Overview
- Siri Recap is a beta feature on the 2026 Apple Watch Ultra 4
- It ambiently listens to conversations and uses AI to summarize them
- The feature is not turned on by default — users must opt in
- Amazon's Bee Frontier offers a similar capability with a different philosophy

## 🧩 How it works
- Users can program when the feature activates (e.g., only at the office between 1 to 2 PM on weekdays)
- Unless saved, all recaps self-destruct after a user-selected period of one, three, or seven days
- No viewable transcript detailing who said what and when
- No raw audio file is ever saved
- Audio is processed and immediately deleted through a secure enclave within the S11 chip, inaccessible to Apple and third parties
- The vast majority of processing is done locally
- Any cloud communication uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute
- Siri Recap does not train on the user's voice
- Unlike Live Rewind, there is no outward indicator or audio chime to signal recording
- The feature automatically turns off when headphones are connected

## ⚙️ Key details
- Summaries heavily prioritize restating facts: bullet points emphasize statistics, factoids, and dates
- Personal anecdotes and strong opinions may be omitted in favor of concrete details
- Media playback (TikTok, podcasts, audiobooks, TV shows) can be summarized as if the user participated in the events
  - Example: A TikTok about a creator's sepsis ER visit was recapped as if the user or someone they knew had been sent to the ER
  - Example: A TikTok discussing an AITA Reddit thread was summarized as if the user discussed a person named Dana at a concert
- Some interview recaps link to corresponding Voice Memos files
- The feature was tested during briefings for new smart glasses from Google, Samsung, Gentle Monster, and Warby Parker

## 🚀 Availability
- Requires the 2026 Apple Watch's S11 chip
- Impossible to use on older Apple Watch models
