# Google rolls out updated Project Genie with Genie 4 agent and community gallery

Google has rolled out a new Project Genie experience to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, introducing Genie 4, upgrades to Genie 3, a Project Genie agent, and a community gallery for discovering and remixing interactive worlds.

## 🔍 Overview
- Project Genie combines advanced world models with Gemini models to generate, explore, and shape interactive worlds directly from a browser.
- The update makes worlds more responsive, creative, and social based on early tester feedback.

## 🧩 Key Features
### Genie 4
- Most realistic and interactive world model yet.
- Allows prompting mid-session to change the world in real time (e.g., shift weather, add characters, redirect action).
- Includes real-time native audio that adapts to the scene, from ambient soundscapes to character dialogue.
- Delivers highest-fidelity visuals, physics, and native audio.

### Genie 3 Updates
- Exploration extended to up to 3 minutes per generation (previously 60 seconds).
- Ability to link multiple worlds for longer journeys.
- Seed new worlds from 360-degree panorama images.
- Dynamically change the environment with natural-language prompts during exploration.
- Remains available in the model picker for longer runs or panorama-seeded spaces.

### Project Genie Agent
- Enables creation through natural-language conversation.
- Orchestrates Google’s multimodal models to craft initial visuals and custom background scores.
- Allows bringing Gemini models into sessions for context-aware suggestions and automatic in-world event triggers.

### Community Gallery
- Curated worlds and community creations available for exploration and remixing.
- Highlights include "Living Memory" (personal photos transformed into explorable spaces), "Dino Field Guide" (prehistoric habitats from 360-degree panoramas), and "Rock Paper Scissors" (themed objects with Gemini as real-time referee).
- Collaborations with creators: "Villa Giallo" (gothic manor mystery with Will aka BLVCKL!GHT) and "The Life of Ants" (ant colony navigation with filmmaker Anand Gandhi).
- Users can publish their own creations for others to explore and remix.

## ⚙️ Model Comparison
| Model | Key Capabilities |
|-------|------------------|
| Genie 4 | Highest-fidelity visuals, physics, native audio; real-time mid-session prompting; real-time adaptive audio |
| Genie 3 | Up to 3-minute generations; link multiple worlds; panorama seeding; mid-session natural-language environment changes |

## 🚀 Availability
- Eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers can access the new Project Genie experience starting today.
- Google AI Pro members can join the waitlist on the same page.

## 💡 Responsible Development
- All generated video and audio includes SynthID watermarking.
- All model inputs and outputs undergo safety screenings.
- Genie 3 and Genie 4 remain frontier research models; active challenges include camera latency, intricate physical interactions, real-time audio precision, and long-term scene permanence.
- A Genie 4 model research overview provides deeper details on limitations and progress.
