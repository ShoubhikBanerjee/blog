# Google Announces Gemini 4 Neon – Pricing Confusion Deepens

Today Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon.

## ⚙️ Pricing

> I wrote recently about how confusing the pricing for the Gemini 4 series was - Neon has somehow made this even worse.

| Variant | Input price per million | Output price per million |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| Neon | $3 | $15 |
| Carbon | $2 | $10 |
| Hydrogen | $1.50 | $7.50 |

- Carbon is currently half its future price.
- Neon is the most expensive variant right now.

## 🧩 Lineup

> The Neon announcement pretends that Argon doesn't exist.

> So the lineup now, from most to least expensive, is Carbon, Neon, Hydrogen.

> Except Carbon is currently half price, so right now Neon ($3/million input, $15/million output) is the most expensive of the three - then Carbon ($2/$10), then Hydrogen ($1.50/$7.50).

> I guess this will make more sense when Carbon doubles to $4/$20 on December 1st.
