# NOTREKS: Improving Causal Discovery with Marginal Independence

A new method for causal discovery, NOTREKS, has been introduced. It incorporates prior knowledge of marginally independent pairs of variables into score-based structure learning.

## 🔍 Overview
- NOTREKS information is derived from the fact that marginally independent pairs of variables cannot be connected by a trek under faithfulness.
- The method introduces a power-series trek (PSTrek) penalty for gradient-based optimization and a trek constraint for order-based search.

## 🧩 How it works
- NOTREKS information helps in causal discovery by restricting the search space and improving both structural recovery and adjustment-based effect identification.
- The method is tested on synthetic benchmarks with oracle NOTREKS information.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The paper is titled "NOTREKS: Marginal Independence in Score-based Structure Learning" and was submitted on 18 Nov 2026.
- The method is part of the Statistics > Machine Learning category on alphaXiv.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The NOTREKS method can improve causal discovery by incorporating prior knowledge of marginally independent pairs of variables.
- It can enhance both structural recovery and adjustment-based effect identification.
