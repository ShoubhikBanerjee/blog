# Databricks Wins AI in Capital Markets Award

Databricks has won the Best AI Solution for Data Intelligence & Governance award at the 2nd annual AI in Capital Markets Awards 2026. Selected by the industry community, this is Databricks' first A-Team award in this category, a distinction arriving as the industry differentiates between experimental AI and production-grade systems capable of withstanding regulatory scrutiny.

## 🏆 Award Recognition
*   **Award:** Best AI Solution for Data Intelligence & Governance.
*   **Event:** 2nd annual AI in Capital Markets Awards 2026.
*   **Significance:** Recognizes Databricks' excellence and innovation in driving AI capabilities across capital markets.

## 📈 Market Context and Challenges
Capital markets firms face acute pressure from elevated intraday volatility and the compression of operational windows due to T+1 settlement in North America. Simultaneously, regulators are sharpening expectations around AI governance, model transparency, and data lineage, citing sources such as ESMA's supervisory briefing on algorithmic trading and the FSB's 2026 work program on AI sound practices.

Recent survey data of managers overseeing roughly $2 trillion in assets indicates:
*   **94%** of portfolio managers and analysts use AI or machine learning in alternative data research.
*   **71%** cite combining data from different sources as their most frustrating challenge.

While large language models are powerful, they cannot inherently access a firm's proprietary order flow, internal research, or risk positions. Traditional cloud approaches often require stitching together separate services for storage, compute, orchestration, and governance, whereas terminal extensions often lack the ability to reason across internal systems.

## 🧩 Platform Capabilities and Use Cases
The Databricks Data + AI Platform is positioned as the proprietary data layer where the competitive edge lies. Institutions are using the platform to unify data ingestion, AI-driven analysis, and content generation within a single governed environment.

| Use Case / Partner | Implementation Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **RBC Capital Markets** | Built **Aiden QuickTakes**, a proprietary AI agent on Databricks. This system auto-generates draft research reports up to 95% faster while maintaining governance and security standards for regulated markets. |
| **LSEG Partnership** | Brought AI-ready financial data natively into the platform. AI agents can query 350,000+ investments using plain English, reducing manual fund screening from hours to minutes. |
| **Post-Trade Operations** | Institutions have built unified cybersecurity analytics platforms to centralize structured and unstructured security data for threat detection and compliance reporting. |
| **Core Banking** | Firms have migrated core banking systems to a governed lakehouse architecture using OpenSharing and Unity Catalog to replace manual reconciliations. |

## ⚙️ Governance and Access
A key differentiator for Databricks in capital markets is its governance architecture, which supports board-level and regulatory scrutiny. The platform ensures that AI applications are trustworthy by grounding answers in Unity Catalog.

*   **Databricks Genie One:** Acts as a data-smart AI coworker for business users. Portfolio managers, analysts, and risk officers can ask questions in plain language without using SQL or notebooks.
*   **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** Extends the governed layer to Microsoft Teams, Excel, and assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT.
*   **Security Controls:** Every request is authorized against Unity Catalog permissions and logged, with generated SQL and lineage remaining inspectable.

This approach allows firms to point large language models at their own proprietary data with the necessary controls in place, scaling AI across workflows without compromising security.
