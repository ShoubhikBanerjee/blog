# Algebraic Characterization of Two-Block Shallow ReLU Pattern Varieties

A complete algebraic characterization of two-block shallow ReLU pattern varieties has been submitted on 18 Nov 2026.

## 🔍 Overview
- Submitted on 18 Nov 2026 under the title "Defining equations for two-block shallow ReLU pattern varieties".
- Proves a conjecture of Alexandr-Montúfar.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The research gives a complete algebraic characterization of two-block shallow ReLU pattern varieties.
- Researchers show that determinantal equations arising from rank constraints imposed by the activation patterns generate the defining ideals.
- The set-theoretic proof utilizes a characterization of orbit closures of matrix pencils in terms of the Kronecker canonical form.
