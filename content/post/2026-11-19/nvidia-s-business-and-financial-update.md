# Nvidia's Business and Financial Update

Nvidia's CFO, Collette Kress, has provided insights into the company's financial strategies and future outlook, amid ongoing discussions about Jensen Huang's leadership and supplier agreements.

## 🔍 Overview
- Nvidia's CFO, Collette Kress, has given clues about the company's financial strategies and future outlook.
- Kress advised Jensen Huang to stop spending so much money on financing customers, a task typically handled by the CFO.
- Nvidia's agreements with suppliers last through the end of the decade.
- Kress shot down the idea that Jensen Huang's departure would lead to a brain drain within Nvidia.
- Nvidia's employees are committed to their work at the company.
- From a business perspective, it's business as usual at Nvidia.

## 💰 Financial Forecast
- Nvidia raised its forecast, saying that revenue will grow by 75 percent next year.
- Singapore alone was responsible for $104 billion of revenue in 2025, making it the second-biggest geographic area by revenue, dwarfed only by the US.

## 🤝 Supplier Agreements
- Elon Musk has stated that Jensen Huang has been making deals with suppliers that were 'a handshake with Jensen' with suppliers — 'not a written contract.'

## 🌍 Export Restrictions
- Nvidia customers allegedly circumvented a ban on selling chips to China.
- The Department of Commerce informed on November 16th that there were new restrictions on selling chips to countries such as Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil.
- Data centers around the world need licenses from the US to lease compute to companies headquartered in China.

## 📊 Revenue and Market Position
- Nvidia's market cap is about $3 trillion.
- Nvidia's revenue from Singapore in 2025 was $104 billion, making it the second-biggest geographic area by revenue, dwarfed only by the US.

## 📈 Business Continuity
- Nvidia did not buy complete directors' and officers' insurance.
- The scandal is about Nvidia customers allegedly circumventing a ban on selling chips to China, not mainly about the affairs.
- Nvidia addressed this issue obliquely by raising its forecast for revenue growth.
