# ChatGPT Adds Live Dictation, Removes 10‑Minute Voice Limit

OpenAI has upgraded ChatGPT’s voice input, introducing Live Dictation and eliminating the previous 10‑minute recording cap.

## 🔍 Overview
- Live Dictation transcribes audio prompts as you speak.
- The 10‑minute recording limit has been removed.
- The feature is more reliable after a recent overhaul of two‑way voice mode.

## 🧩 How It Works
- Speech is transcribed in real time; the AI generates a live text transcript.
- You can tap or click the audio waveform to view the transcript and edit it directly in the prompt box.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Faster transcription compared to the older mode.
- No longer bound by a 10‑minute cap.
- Users can ask the AI to transcribe voice messages or convert them into bullet points.
- A separate “record mode” for transcribing voice notes and meetings remains in beta, limited to Plus, Enterprise, Edu, Business, and Pro workspaces.

## 🚀 Availability
- Live Dictation is rolling out globally on all ChatGPT plans.
- Accessible via the web, desktop app, and iOS and Android apps.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Enables quicker, more seamless voice‑to‑text interaction.
- Expands use cases for meeting transcription and note‑taking.
