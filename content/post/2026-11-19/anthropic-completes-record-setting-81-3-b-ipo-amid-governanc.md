# Anthropic Completes Record‑Setting $81.3 B IPO Amid Governance Questions

Anthropic, an AI research lab founded by former OpenAI executives, completed a record‑setting IPO that raised $81.3 billion, becoming the most valuable public AI company ever.

## 🔍 Overview
- Anthropic’s IPO set an important precedent for future public AI lab debuts.
- The company beat SpaceX to become the most valuable of all time.
- The IPO raises questions about Anthropic’s “good guy” reputation.

## 📈 Corporate Structure
- Anthropic is registered as a Public Benefit Corporation, obligating its board to balance mission with shareholder wealth.
- The company’s founders retain controlling voting rights, preventing them from being voted out by shareholders.

## 🛠️ Long‑Term Benefit Trust (LTBT)
- Introduced in 2023, the LTBT gives a small group of trustees authority to select and remove a portion of Anthropic’s board.
- Trustees are “financially disinterested” in the company.
- The LTBT chooses four of the seven board members; one of those four must be approved by founders.
- Trustees must give 30‑day notice to the nominating and corporate governance committee (co‑founder Daniela Amodei, Reed Hastings, Yasmin Razavi).

## 🏦 IPO Impact
- Adding an IPO complicates Anthropic’s mission; the company’s leadership can pursue it largely unilaterally.
- The structure insulates the company from shareholder pressure to turn a profit but removes typical checks on its power.
- A former employee noted that "the current pace of AI development is untenable under normal market structures."
