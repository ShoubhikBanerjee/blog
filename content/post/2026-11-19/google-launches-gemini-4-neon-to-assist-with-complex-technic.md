# Google Launches Gemini 4 Neon to Assist with Complex Technical Workflows

Today, Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon, bringing frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion across complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. The model excels at multi-step instruction following and can synthesize across diverse inputs in a single 1 million-token context window to turn inputs into structured deliverables, while recording its lowest fabrication rate yet in internal Google Antigravity testing.

## 🔍 Overview

* **Pricing:** Gemini 4 Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 per million cached input tokens (a 95% discount), and $15 per million output tokens.
* **Context Window:** Features a 1 million-token context window.
* **Cost Efficiency:** At its default high thinking level, Neon pairs a high Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score with a 32% lower cost per task than Carbon. It sits on the Pareto frontier on both the Vals Index and Agent Arena at high, with roughly 50% to 80% lower cost per task than top competing frontier models.

## 💻 Developer and Cybersecurity Workflows

Neon delivers competitive results across developer, researcher, and cybersecurity-focused tests:

* **Coding Performance:** Neon ranks #1 overall on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6). It scores 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 and 46.2% on FrontierCode 2.0 at its default high setting.
* **SWE-sweep:** The model leads on the mini-SWE harness with 12.8%, which more than triples to a state-of-the-art 39.0% when utilized within the Google Antigravity harness.
* **AutoBaxBench:** Neon produces more secure code than Carbon across all easy, medium, and hard challenges, achieving state-of-the-art on the hard tier (76%).
* **WebExploitBench v1.2:** Achieves a state-of-the-art 51.26% at half the cost of competing large models.
* **SEC-bench Pro:** Reaches a state-of-the-art 90.5% while costing roughly half as much per challenge.
* **ExploitGym:** Delivers frontier-level performance using half the tokens of Carbon.
* **CWE-bench v2:** Achieves 70.7% on Collinear's benchmark, representing a 3.4% uplift over Carbon.

## 🚀 Availability

Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out today across the following platforms and services:

* The Gemini API in Google AI Studio
* Gemini for business (via API and model dropdown)
* Paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity
* Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app
