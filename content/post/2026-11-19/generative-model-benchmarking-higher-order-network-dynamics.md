# Generative Model Benchmarking Higher-Order Network Dynamics

A new generative model for stochastic network dynamics introduces user‑specified pairwise and higher‑order interactions, enabling controlled benchmarks for information‑theoretic measures.

## 🔍 Overview

- The model represents higher‑order interactions as directed hyperedges.
- A pairwise‑only reference is obtained by removing the higher‑order term.

## 🧩 How It Works

- Users define pairwise and higher‑order interaction structures.
- The model generates stochastic dynamics consistent with these definitions.

## ⚙️ Experimental Setup

- Experiments compare measures on data with the higher‑order term present versus the pairwise‑only reference.
- A second experiment discriminates between a product coupling and a specified nonlinear pairwise control, matched in root‑mean‑square coupling magnitude under the pairwise reference.

## 📈 Results

- Several measures detected sufficiently strong higher‑order interactions across tested topologies and coupling functions.
- In the discrimination experiment, the measures also separated the product coupling from the specified nonlinear pairwise control.
- Recovery accuracy decreased as the number of candidate interactions increased.

## 💡 Why It Matters

- The framework offers a testbed to validate the link between higher‑order signatures and underlying mechanisms before applying measures to empirical data.
