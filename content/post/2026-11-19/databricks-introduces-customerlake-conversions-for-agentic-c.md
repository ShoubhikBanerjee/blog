# Databricks Introduces CustomerLake Conversions for Agentic CDP

Databricks has introduced CustomerLake Conversions, a new capability within its Agentic CDP that connects first-party data and context directly to advertising activation and measurement. This feature is designed to help brands pursue more valuable conversions and improve return on ad spend (ROAS) while maintaining control over the data they share.

## 🧩 How it works
CustomerLake Conversions brings signal creation and activation to the environment where customer data and models already live. It integrates with CustomerLake, the Agentic CDP built natively in Databricks. The feature includes a dedicated Conversions tab within CustomerLake Campaign Agents, allowing marketers to:

- Select conversion data
- Configure destinations
- Manage delivery within the Campaign Agents workflow

Paid media teams can use existing lifetime value models in Databricks to identify specific events, such as completed first purchases from customers likely to become repeat buyers, and configure the delivery of these purchase events to supported advertising platforms through CAPI (Conversions API).

## 💡 Use cases
The capability supports various business scenarios by leveraging specific data signals:

| Business Context | Data Signal Used | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Retailer | Predicted lifetime value | Rapidly adjust lookalike targeting |
| B2B Company | Qualified leads or closed won revenue | Represent progress beyond a submitted form |
| Paid Media Team | Existing lifetime value model | Identify completed first purchases from customers likely to become repeat buyers |

## 🔒 Data governance and agency collaboration
CustomerLake Conversions is designed so that customers own their data and stay in control. It provides a shared foundation for brands and agencies:

- Agencies receive governed access to customer context in the lakehouse without copying or duplicating data.
- The brand controls what is shared and with whom.
- Agencies can utilize better data for their work.

## 🚀 Availability
CustomerLake Conversions will be accessible to all customers using Campaign Agents in CustomerLake when available. CustomerLake Campaign Agents help marketers build audiences in natural language, activate across channels, and power 1:1 personalized experiences.

## 📈 Strategic impact
The feature supports a shift away from traditional launch cycles, where a single campaign can take 6+ teams and 60+ days to launch. Instead, CustomerLake enables a continuous operating model where each customer interaction helps inform the next decision. As campaign insights flow back into Databricks, agents can help marketers and agencies recognize changes, recommend adjustments, and keep activation aligned with business goals.
