# PRISM: A New Representation for Quantifying Repetitive Human Movement

Researchers have introduced PRISM, a novel AI-derived representation designed to characterize repetitive human movement through tracked masks and dense optical flow, addressing limitations of existing anatomical pose estimation methods.
