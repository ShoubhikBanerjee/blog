# AWS Quick User Group DC Meetup Highlights FIG Analytics Platform

On Wednesday, October 7, 2026, data leaders, developers, and practitioners gathered at the AWS Skills Center in Arlington, Virginia, for the AWS Quick User Group DC meetup. The session brought together founders, software engineers, and enterprise leaders, including a team from Financial Independence Group (FIG), to explore AWS Quick and participate in a hands-on application-building exercise.

## 🔍 Overview
- Event: AWS Quick User Group DC meetup
- Date: October 7, 2026
- Location: AWS Skills Center, Arlington, Virginia
- Attendees: Data leaders, developers, practitioners, founders, software engineers, enterprise leaders, and FIG team
- Speaker: Vanessa Gonzalez, Vice President of AI at Financial Independence Group (FIG)

## 🧩 How it works
AWS Quick capabilities demonstrated at the meetup:
- **Agents and skills**: Turn natural-language questions into multi-step reusable actions that aid in decision making across enterprise data
- **Built-in integrations**: Connect tools like Salesforce, Slack, Google Drive, and Microsoft 365, breaking down data silos
- **Unified workspace**: A cohesive environment that bridges research, automated visual generation, and business artifact creation (presentations, documents, and spreadsheets) directly where users work

## ⚙️ Key details
FIG's implementation of AWS Quick:
- **Role**: AWS Quick serves as FIG's "single front door" for enterprise analytics
- **Data scope**: Brings together structured data (CRM systems, databases, finance data) and unstructured content (internal knowledge bases, policy documents, collateral) through a governed data layer
- **AI analytics loop**: Moves from reporting on what already happened to a continuous cycle: Ask → Analyze → Automate → Act
- **Centralized discovery**: Analytics portal organized by business area (sales, operations, marketing, commissions) with built-in AI chat assistants so teams can talk directly to their documents and datasets
- **Democratized insights**: Self-service exploration tools for non-technical business users, plus a learning hub that builds prompt engineering and AI skills across teams while keeping proper governance in place

## 🚀 Availability
Hands-on results from the meetup:
- Every attendee successfully designed and launched a custom application in AWS Quick within 50 minutes
- A subset of six participants built multi-step, event-triggered flows that handle repetitive data tasks
- These flows replace manual reporting handoffs with automated, repeatable pipelines
- New ideas now take hours or even minutes to build, not weeks or months

## 💡 Why it matters
- FIG moved from existing analytics to AWS Quick to bring AI into its dashboards, reporting, and analytics
- The platform enables a governed transition from static reporting to an continuous AI-driven analytics loop
- Self-service tooling with embedded learning reduces time-to-insight for non-technical users while maintaining governance
- Rapid application development (50 minutes for a custom app) accelerates organizational experimentation and deployment
