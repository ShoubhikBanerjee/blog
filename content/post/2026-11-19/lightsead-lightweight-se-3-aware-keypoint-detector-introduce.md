# LightSEAD: Lightweight SE(3)-Aware Keypoint Detector Introduced

The pursuit of improved performance in deep learning has often been accompanied by increasing architectural complexity and computational demands.
We explore an alternative direction: achieving more with less through structured supervision.

## 🔍 Overview
- We introduce LightSEAD, a lightweight SE(3)-aware keypoint detector initialised with calibrated Harris pseudo-labels and enriched through viewpoint-aware and appearance-aware supervision and selective knowledge distillation.

## 🧩 How it works
- LightSEAD comprises only six convolutional layers and 84,289 learnable parameters, achieving competitive repeatability across HPatches, KITTI 06 and 4Seasons while demonstrating its downstream applicability to relative pose estimation on MegaDepth-1500.

## 🚀 Performance
- Further optimised for mixed-precision inference, LightSEAD Coarse achieves approximately 909 FPS on an NVIDIA RTX 4090 and 139 FPS on a Jetson Orin Nano Super.
