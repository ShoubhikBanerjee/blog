# MarketBeat expands AI‑powered news sentiment scoring for companies

MarketBeat today released an update to its AI‑driven news sentiment platform, which assigns a numeric score to every headline and aggregates the results over the past seven days to show how companies are perceived in the press.

## 🔍 Overview
- The system scores each headline on a scale from **2 (good news) to -2 (bad news)**.
- Sentiment scores are averaged across all articles mentioning a company in the **most recent 7 days**.
- Over **hundreds of news outlets** are tracked and tagged by company.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Headline scoring** – every news headline receives a score in the –2 to 2 range.
- **Aggregation** – the platform calculates the average sentiment for each company based on the last seven days of coverage.
- **Article‑count comparison** – a weekly chart compares the number of articles this week with the typical weekly average for that company.

## 🚀 Availability
- The sentiment engine runs continuously in real‑time, pulling headlines from **more than one hundred trusted media outlets**.
- Users can receive daily updates via MarketBeat’s free newsletter or optional SMS alerts (available in multiple countries).

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides investors with a quick, quantitative view of market perception.
- Highlights companies gaining positive or negative press trends before price movements.
- Helps analysts prioritize research based on real‑time sentiment shifts.
