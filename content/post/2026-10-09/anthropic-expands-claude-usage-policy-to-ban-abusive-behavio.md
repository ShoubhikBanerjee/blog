# Anthropic expands Claude usage policy to ban abusive behavior and high‑risk misuse

Anthropic has revised its Claude usage policy for the first time in over a year, adding new prohibitions on abusive treatment of the model and on several high‑risk misuse scenarios such as election interference, weapons development, and surveillance.

## 🔍 Overview
- The update reflects “new and high‑risk cases of misuse.”
- It consolidates scattered restrictions into clearer bans on deceptive political or commercial campaigns, propaganda, surveillance, and weapon‑related activities.
- Anthropic states the changes apply only in extreme cases of repeated cruelty toward its models.

## ⚙️ New Policy Prohibitions
| Category | Prohibited actions |
|---|---|
| Abusive or cruel behavior toward Claude | Sustained, needless abuse; Claude may end conversations with persistently harmful users. |
| Deceptive political/commercial campaigns | Efforts to obscure who is behind a message or amplify content through fake accounts or posts. |
| Election interference | Voter deception, misinformation about candidates or voting, impersonating candidates or officials, or attempts to suppress turnout. |
| Weapons development | Guidance, software, or components that make weapons work, including arming drones and other autonomous vehicles. |
| Surveillance | Tracking people without consent (real‑time or via past data), using Claude to decide investigations, arrests, or to build surveillance tools. |
| Autonomous hardware operation | When models are connected to hardware that takes autonomous physical actions, a qualified operator must be able to observe the equipment and stop it if needed. |

## 🚦 Model Welfare Enforcement
- Terminating conversations remains the “primary enforcement mechanism.”
- Anthropic did not comment on whether additional mechanisms such as user bans will be added.
- The policy applies only to extreme, repeated cruelty with “no discernible purpose,” not to typical user frustration, dark creative themes, or model testing.

## 📜 Conditional Government Use
- Rules may be modified for contracts with certain governmental customers if Anthropic judges the contractual restrictions and safeguards adequate.
- The company has previously contracted with the U.S. military.

## 🤖 Physical Embodiment Safeguard
- A new rule requires a “qualified operator” to monitor and stop any equipment that Claude controls when the hardware can cause injury.
- It is unclear whether the operator must be human.

## 🧠 Context on Model Welfare & Consciousness
- Anthropic’s model‑welfare research investigates questions of internal experience, consciousness, moral status, and welfare as models become more capable.
- Kyle Fish, who leads the research, told The Verge that these questions are “serious.”
- CEO Dario Amodei said the company “doesn’t know if the models are conscious.”
- Microsoft’s AI code of conduct took a contrary stance, rejecting legal personhood or welfare for AI models.
