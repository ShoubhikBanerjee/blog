# California Athletic Commission Issues Cease-and-Desist to Rek Over Human‑vs‑Robot Fight

A human‑vs‑robot cage match staged by startup Rek drew a cease‑and‑desist letter from the California State Athletic Commission.

## 🔍 Overview
- The California State Athletic Commission sent a cease‑and‑desist letter to Rek, a startup that hosted a match between a human and a robot.
- California is trying to shut down robot vs. human cage matches.
- The league calls itself the “humanoid robot fighting league.”

## ⚖️ Legal Action
- The commission says Rek cannot host fights involving any human in California without permission.
- The CEO of Rek, Cix Liv, posted the letter to X on September 30, calling the September 18 fight “unsanctioned.”
- The letter demanded that Liv and Rek stop “holding, promoting or advertising any boxing or mixed martial arts contest match, or exhibition, involving any human” in California without the commission’s approval.
- Liv responded publicly with the comment, “We made it boys.”

## 🥊 Fight Details
- The match took place on September 18.
- Human fighter: Frankie LaPenna.
- Robot opponent: owned by Rek, piloted by a human using a remote virtual‑reality system (as described by The New York Times).
- The robot appears to be a unit from EngineAI fitted with a Terminator‑like head.
- A replay of the fight is available on YouTube.

## 🚀 Next Event
- Rek’s next planned match, scheduled for Friday, will feature two robots equipped with weapons fighting each other.
