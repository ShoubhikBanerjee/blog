## ⚙️ Key Metrics Explained
- **Last Trade** – most recent transaction price for the option.
- **Bid/Ask** – best buying (bid) and selling (ask) prices offered.
- **Volume** – number of contracts traded during the current session.
- **Open Interest** – total contracts outstanding that have not been exercised or closed.
- **Implied Volatility (IV)** – market‑expected volatility of the underlying stock, expressed as a percentage.
- **ITM (In‑The‑Money)** – indicates whether the option’s strike price is favorable relative to the current underlying price.
