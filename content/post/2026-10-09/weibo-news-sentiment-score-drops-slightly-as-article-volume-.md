# Weibo news sentiment score drops slightly as article volume declines

Weibo’s latest AI‑driven news sentiment metrics show a modest decline in score and a lower count of articles this week compared with its typical weekly average.

## 🔍 Overview
- Each headline receives a score ranging from 2 (good news) to ‑2 (bad news).
- News sentiment scores track the average news sentiment of articles about each company over the most recent 7 days to identify companies receiving positive press.
- Headlines are collected from hundreds of news outlets and tagged by company.

## 📊 Current Metrics
- **WB News Sentiment:** ▼ 0.05
- **Average Communication Services News Sentiment:** ▲ 0.44
- **WB Articles This Week:** ▼ 3
- **WB Articles Average Week:** ▲ 2
- **Page Views:** 1,873 (+11 %)
- **Searches:** 1

## 📈 How it works
- The system scans headlines from a wide network of sources (e.g., MarketBeat, Yahoo Finance, CNN).
- Each headline is assigned a sentiment score on the ‑2 to +2 scale.
- Scores are aggregated to produce a weekly average for each company.
- A comparison chart shows the number of articles about the company in the last seven days versus the average number of articles in a typical week.
