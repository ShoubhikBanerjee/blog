# Deep Sequence Model Reveals Disrupted Emotion‑Action Coupling in Adolescents with NSSI

A new AI study submitted on 8 Oct 2026 combines an experimental pain paradigm, EEG microstate analysis, and an interpretable deep sequence model to uncover neurodynamic mechanisms linking social distress to non‑suicidal self‑injury (NSSI) in depressed adolescents.

## 🔍 Overview
- Study population: 106 adolescents with depression (67 with NSSI, labeled DN+; 39 without NSSI, labeled DN-).
- Conditions examined: social pain, physical pain, and resting‑state.
- Goal: Identify brain‑state dynamics that differentiate DN+ from DN- during social pain.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Data acquisition**: High‑density EEG recorded while participants experienced social and physical pain stimuli.
- **Microstate analysis**: EEG segmented into recurring topographic patterns (microstates) labeled MS3, MS5, etc.
- **Deep sequence model**: Integrated disease‑specific, domain‑adversarial, consistency, and contrastive learning objectives to capture higher‑order dependencies in microstate sequences.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Classification performance**: Social‑pain condition achieved 68.55% accuracy in distinguishing DN+ from DN-, an 8.94‑point improvement over the best baseline.
- **Neurodynamic findings**:
  - DN+ adolescents showed weakened bidirectional transitions between MS3 and MS5 during social pain.
  - Source reconstruction linked MS3 to emotional/interoceptive processing and MS5 to action preparation, indicating disrupted emotion‑action coupling.
  - Time‑resolved analysis revealed:
    * Greater early‑to‑middle recruitment of action‑related states.
    * Later recruitment of emotion‑related states in DN+ participants.
- **Mediation patterns**:
  - In DN- adolescents, MS5‑to‑MS3 dynamics mediated the link between social‑evaluation sensitivity and affective outcomes.
  - This mediation was absent in DN+ adolescents, while MS3‑to‑MS5 transitions correlated with increased negative affect in DN+.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a mechanistically interpretable neural signature for objective identification of NSSI.
- Highlights disrupted emotion‑action coupling as a core neurodynamic mechanism in adolescents experiencing social pain.
- Demonstrates the utility of interpretable deep sequence modeling for uncovering higher‑order brain‑state dependencies in clinical neuroscience.
