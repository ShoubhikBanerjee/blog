# SpaceXAI Joins Omacom Foundation, Donates $1.5M Grok Tokens to Omarchy

SpaceXAI announced it will become a Founding Corporate Patron of the Omacom Foundation – the body that oversees the Omarchy Linux distribution – and will donate $1.5 million worth of Grok tokens to the project.

## 🔍 Overview
- SpaceXAI is backing Omarchy, a controversial Linux distro, by joining its overseeing foundation as a founding patron.
- The partnership was announced today and includes a sizeable token donation.

## 💰 Funding Details
- **$1.5 million worth of Grok tokens** donated to David Heinemeier Hansson’s Linux project.
- **$1.5 million in compute resources** pledged to support Omarchy.

## 🛠️ Token Use
- According to Hansson’s blog post, the Grok tokens will be used to:
  - Accelerate development of the distro.
  - Review code.
  - Patch bugs.

## 👤 Key Figures
- **David Heinemeier Hansson** – Danish entrepreneur, creator of Ruby on Rails and co‑founder of the company behind Basecamp; leads the Omarchy Linux project.
- **Elon Musk** – Mentioned in speculation that he and SpaceXAI would back a Linux distro, suggesting alignment with Omarchy.

## ⚠️ Controversy
- Hansson has attracted criticism for anti‑immigration blog posts, including a July comparison of immigrants to wolves with the statement, "When wolves get out of control, you shoot them."
- Other companies such as 1Password and Cloudflare have faced scrutiny for contributing to Omarchy.
- Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince, when asked about the contributions on the Decoder podcast, said he did not see it as his job to "police different people’s political beliefs."
