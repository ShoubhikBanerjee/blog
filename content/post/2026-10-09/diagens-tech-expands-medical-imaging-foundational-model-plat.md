# Diagens Tech expands medical imaging foundational model platform across specialties

Diagens Tech is extending its medical‑imaging foundational model platform beyond a single regulated use case, allowing clinicians to build, deploy and run thousands of custom models across multiple specialties and hospital sites.

## 🌍 Global Landscape
- Today clinicians view chest CT scans, compare them with prior tests, and interpret new findings.
- Tomorrow’s trillion‑dollar medical‑AI companies are expected to arise from three pillars: day‑to‑day clinician workflow systems, data that span the whole patient‑care journey, and foundational models that continuously add diagnostic capability.
- Three global hubs illustrate this trajectory:
  - **Pro Medicus** (Melbourne, Australia) – imaging‑workflow software.
  - **Tempus AI** (Chicago, United States) – molecular diagnostics and patient‑data services.
  - **Diagens Tech** (Hangzhou, China) – large foundational models for medical imaging.

|---|---|---|---|

| Diagens Tech | Hangzhou, China | Medical‑imaging large foundational models | RMB 22 billion |

## 🧩 Diagens Tech’s Platform Strategy
- Starts from a single, regulated use case and is now expanding to additional specialties and hospital sites.
- Provides a unified foundation on which clinical specialists can **build, deploy and operate thousands of custom models**.
- Positions the company to occupy the emerging third asset class in medical AI – models that generate new clinical capabilities continuously.

## ⚙️ How the Platform Evolves
- As AI amplifies specialist expertise, medical‑imaging foundational models shift from standalone products to **scalable platform assets**.
- The platform is designed to integrate with existing workflow systems, enabling hospitals to organise image reading, reporting and cross‑institutional collaboration around a single foundation.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Imaging accounts for roughly 80 % of global clinical data and supports about 14,000 indication‑specific tasks across modalities such as CT, MR, ultrasound, pathology and more.
- With over 25 billion imaging scans performed worldwide each year, a platform that lets specialists rapidly create and run custom models promises faster, more specialized insights.
- If workflows and data already command high valuations (as seen with Pro Medicus), foundational‑model platforms like Diagens Tech could become the next high‑value asset class in medical AI.
