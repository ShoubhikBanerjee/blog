# Instinct AI Launches Text-Based Assistant With 10 Billion Dollar Valuation

Instinct AI, a startup founded by former Sierra employee Noah Shinn, has launched a new AI agent that operates through a straightforward text message interface. After a quiet release in August that relied on an invite-only model with no marketing, the company was valued at $10 billion in late September, despite mounting pressure from competitors like OpenAI and Meta.

## 🔍 Overview
Instinct is designed as a consumer-friendly AI agent that handles personal chores and administrative tasks. Unlike many contemporary AI products that use traditional app interfaces or anthropomorphized mascots, Instinct operates entirely through existing messaging channels. The startup focuses on a specific niche that founder Noah Shinn believes sets it apart from larger players building products for a broader range of customers.

## 🧩 How it works
Instinct functions by providing the AI assistant with access to a virtual machine and personal integrations. 

*   **Interface:** Conversations happen exclusively over text through iMessage, WhatsApp, or email.
*   **Integrations:** Users can connect the agent to Google Workspace, Slack, and Notion.
*   **Security:** To log into specific websites, the agent provides a link to a "secure vault" where users enter their credentials.
*   **System Access:** The agent can plug directly into a user's personal email and calendar to manage schedules.

## ⚙️ Key details
The agent is a consumer-oriented version of developer tools like Codex and OpenClaw. It is capable of handling vague instructions and managing various digital tasks:

| Task Type | Examples of Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| Appointments | Booking DMV appointments and emailing doctor's offices to set up visits. |
| Communications | Sending follow-up emails and handling digital paperwork for Ikea returns. |
| Research | Finding age-appropriate lessons and verifying times and dates for community center classes. |

## 🚀 Availability
Instinct currently lacks a traditional app or a monthly subscription fee. Access is limited through the following methods:
*   An invitation from a current user.
*   Approval from a waitlist.

Because it relies on text messaging, the service is accessible on any device that supports iMessage or WhatsApp, including CarPlay and smartwatches.

## 💡 Why it matters
The launch of Instinct represents a shift toward autonomous agents for the general public. While the agent has demonstrated some limitations—such as missing specific prerequisite information for class enrollments—its ability to operate across various messaging platforms without a dedicated app separates it from the "cuddly bear" or "blob" mascots used by competitors like Muse and Dots. Shinn remains unfazed by the competition, citing Instinct's specialized focus as its primary advantage.
