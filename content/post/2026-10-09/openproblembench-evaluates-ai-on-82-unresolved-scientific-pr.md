# OpenProblemBench evaluates AI on 82 unresolved scientific problems

OpenProblemBench was released as a new benchmark that gathers 82 open problems from mathematics and theoretical physics to test whether AI systems can contribute to unresolved scientific questions.

## 🔭 Overview
- The next frontier for artificial general intelligence is tackling unresolved scientific problems, calling for benchmarks that assess progress beyond established knowledge.
- OpenProblemBench contains 82 unresolved problems drawn from the mathematics and theoretical physics literature.

## 🧩 How it works
- Each problem supplies the research context, assumptions, and prior progress needed to investigate the question.
- Problems are selected whose proposed solutions admit comparatively clear checks of their decisive mathematical or computational claims.
- Four evaluator models independently assess the correctness, completeness, and degree of progress of each submission without reference solutions.

## 📊 Results
| Model Type | Mean Judged Solve Rate |
|------------|------------------------|
| GPT-6‑Astra | 14.0% |
| Full‑size Open Models | 5.5‑6.7% |
| Flash Models | 2.4‑3.7% |

- Case comparisons connect stronger outcomes to changes in problem representation, general arguments that extend beyond finite evidence, and proofs of the steps needed to complete a solution.

## 💡 Why it matters
- By grounding evaluation in questions arising from the research literature, OpenProblemBench provides a setting for investigating the capabilities and limitations of AI as a contributor to foundational theoretical science.
