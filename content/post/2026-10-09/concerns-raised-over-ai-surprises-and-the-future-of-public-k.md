# Concerns Raised Over AI Surprises and the Future of Public-Key Encryption

A speaker has raised worst-case concerns regarding the future of public-key encryption in the face of rapid AI-driven surprises. The warnings highlight a massive gap between the speed of AI progress and the timeline required for humans to update security standards.

## 🔍 Overview

The core issue is a mismatch in operational speeds between technology and human standardization. 

* The speed of AI producing surprises and the speed of human beings replacing security standards are orders of magnitude different.
* This discrepancy remains true even when humans utilize the very best AI assistance.
* Recovering from a sudden cryptographic surprise is only possible if the preparation is done in advance.

## ⚙️ Key details

The speaker outlined specific probabilities regarding the breakdown of current cryptographic assumptions:

* **1% chance** we live in "Minicrypt"—a hypothetical world formulated by Russell Impagliazzo in which public-key encryption is impossible.
* **15% chance** we functionally lose confidence in our existing public-key encryption algorithms.
