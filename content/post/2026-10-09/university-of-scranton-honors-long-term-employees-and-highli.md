# University of Scranton Honors Long‑Term Employees and Highlights Disability Advocacy

The University of Scranton hosted several notable events in September 2026, recognizing staff longevity, presenting a high‑profile Ignatian Values lecture, and awarding leaders in disability inclusion.

## 📅 Overview
- **Friday, Sept. 18** – Order of Pro Deo induction ceremony in the Pearn Auditorium, Brennan Hall.
- **Thursday, Sept. 17** – Ignatian Values in Action Lecture at the Byron Recreation Complex.
- **2025 Conference on Disability** – Awards presentation recognizing lifetime advocacy.

## 🎖️ Order of Pro Deo Induction
- The Order of Pro Deo, founded in 1961 by Rev. John J. Long, S.J., honors individuals for service to the University.
- Sixteen employees were formally recognized for **20 years of service**.
- Rev. Joseph G. Marina, S.J., presented the awards, offered a blessing, and gave remarks.
- Since its inception, **797 individuals** have been inducted.

## 🗣️ Ignatian Values in Action Lecture
- Featured presenter: Rev. James Martin, S.J., H'17, a globally recognized Jesuit priest, author, and speaker.
- Martin discussed themes from his book *“Searching for God at Ground Zero,”* focusing on encountering God amid suffering and uncertainty.
- The session included a discussion and Q&A hosted by Teresa Grettano, Ph.D., associate professor of English and Theatre and president of the Faculty Senate.
- Additional campus activities for Martin included a community breakfast, a Schemel Forum World Affairs Luncheon, and a book‑signing.
- The lecture series has featured prominent Catholic voices; last year’s speaker was Rev. Gregory Boyle, S.J., founder of Homeboy Industries.

## ♿ Disability Advocacy and Awards
- Keith Williams, G’09, highlighted 25 years of progress in disability inclusion, noting curb cuts, accessible facilities, and visual/audio street signals.
- Williams, manager at the Office of Vocational Rehabilitation Grant and Advocacy at MyCIL, emphasized that **equal access to employment** remains a major hurdle.
- At the 2025 U.S. Conference on Disability, Williams received the **Edward R. Leahy, Jr., Award for Excellence in Leadership and Advocacy** for his lifelong public‑service work.
- He praised the conference founders, Edward ’68, H’01 and Patricia Leahy, for applying personal experience to create a world‑class event focused on education and employment access.

*The combined events underscore the university’s commitment to Jesuit values of service, inclusion, and community engagement.*
