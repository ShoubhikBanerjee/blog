# Version 1 Expands AI Development with New Machine Learning and Generative AI Roles

Version 1 is expanding its technology and transformation services by recruiting hands-on AI/ML Engineers to develop production-grade machine learning and Generative AI solutions. This development follows 30 years of business as a premier partner for global technology leaders including AWS, Microsoft, and Oracle.

## 🔍 Overview
Version 1 is a €350/£300m revenue business with over 3,300 employees and a 30-year history in the UK and Ireland. The company is currently seeking to integrate AI models into business systems and support the full AI development lifecycle for global brand customers.

## ⚙️ Key details
The development focuses on several core technical responsibilities and systems:

*   **Generative AI**: Developing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and AI agents.
*   **Application Development**: Designing and maintaining AI-powered applications and services.
*   **Model Integration**: Integrating foundation models and APIs into enterprise applications.
*   **Infrastructure**: Utilizing AWS, Docker, and Kubernetes for model deployment.
