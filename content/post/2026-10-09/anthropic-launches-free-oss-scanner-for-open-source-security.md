# Anthropic launches free OSS Scanner for open‑source security

Anthropic announced a new free service, OSS Scanner, that lets open‑source projects opt‑in to AI‑powered security vulnerability scans.

## 🔍 Overview
- OSS Scanner is an AI‑driven vulnerability scanner for open‑source projects.
- Projects that opt‑in receive “thorough, periodic security scans by our strongest models at no cost.”
- The service is positioned as a way to alert projects to possible security issues sooner.

## 🧩 How it works
- Scans are performed by Anthropic’s strongest models, including Claude Mythos.
- All vulnerability reports are generated entirely by the model; there is **no human review or triage**.
- The model‑only approach enables faster and more frequent scanning.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Free of charge** for any open‑source project that opts in.
- **Periodic scans** provide ongoing security monitoring.
- Reports are **fully model‑generated**, which can lead to **incorrect or invalid findings**.
- Uses the same “strongest models” that power Anthropic’s other AI services.

## 🚀 Availability
- Open‑source projects can **opt‑in** to receive the scans.
- Once opted in, projects receive vulnerability reports automatically at no cost.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Faster detection of vulnerabilities gives projects a larger defensive advantage.
- OSS Scanner joins a growing ecosystem of AI bug‑hunting tools that have already uncovered major flaws, such as the “Copy Fail” bug that impacted nearly every Linux distribution.
- The influx of AI‑generated bug reports is already challenging some projects, including efforts by Linus Torvalds and Google, highlighting the need for tools that can keep pace.
