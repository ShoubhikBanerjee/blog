# Wiz Hires Support Engineer with AI-Driven Automation Focus

Wiz, a cloud security company, published a job advertisement for a Remote Solutions Support Engineer based in Ireland, indicating a new role that combines deep technical troubleshooting with a growing emphasis on AI-driven automation to scale support operations.

## 🔍 Role Overview
The Remote Solutions Support Engineer will be part of a global team delivering expert-level technical support across the Wiz cloud-native security platform. The role involves investigating complex, multi-domain issues spanning cloud infrastructure, workload protection, vulnerability management, and runtime defense across AWS, Azure, and GCP environments.

## ⚙️ Key Requirements
- 5+ years of relevant customer-facing technical experience.
- 2+ years working with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) including IAM, networking, and core compute/storage services.
- 1+ years of hands-on experience deploying, managing, and troubleshooting production Kubernetes clusters and containerized applications.
- Experience reading and debugging code in Python, Shell, JavaScript/JSON, Java, Go, or Terraform/HCL.
- Proficiency with command-line tools and Linux operating system environments.
- Familiarity with the current AI landscape, including LLM architectures and the integration of AI-driven tooling into technical workflows.

## 💡 Why It Matters
Wiz states it is redefining security for the AI era and is "powered by Google," offering an AI-powered platform that harnesses Google’s Threat Intelligence and Security Operations. The job description emphasizes a growing emphasis on AI-driven automation to scale support operations and improve resolution quality, with preferred qualifications including experience building or supporting agentic workflows, RAG architectures, or AI-powered automation.

## 🚀 Availability
Visa sponsorship is not offered for this role. The position includes an after-hours, holiday, and weekend on-call rotation.
