# EU Parliament Pushes for Global AI Governance Treaty and New Safeguards

European lawmakers on the Parliament’s Constitutional Affairs Committee (AFCO) have approved a report that asks Brussels to spearhead a global AI agreement, tighten safeguards against election manipulation, and create a budget‑neutral AI Observatory inside the European Parliament.

## 🔍 Overview
- The AFCO report calls for an international AI‑governance treaty modelled on nuclear non‑proliferation arrangements.
- It was backed by 21 votes in favour, three against and no abstentions.
- The proposal argues that AI’s scale, speed and cross‑border impact justify a dedicated global convention or treaty.

## 📜 Report Highlights
- **Human oversight:** AI used by EU institutions must remain under human control, even though the technology can help identify legal inconsistencies.
- **Election safeguards:** Stronger action is proposed against deepfakes, micro‑targeting and voter manipulation, addressing the risk of AI influencing elections.
- **Fundamental‑rights updates:** The report urges amendments to the EU Charter of Fundamental Rights to protect personal identity and likeness, ensure algorithmic transparency, guarantee digital self‑determination and prevent algorithmic discrimination.
- **AI Observatory:** A new European Parliament AI Observatory would provide technical and analytical support to parliamentary committees, be budget‑neutral and built from existing expertise, without regulatory or enforcement powers.

## 🌐 Global AI Governance Proposal
- The report likens AI to transformative technologies such as nuclear technology, suggesting a similar international regime.
- It emphasizes that fragmented European electoral frameworks are ill‑suited to counter AI‑driven misinformation, reinforcing the need for coordinated global rules.

## 🛡️ Safeguards & Human Oversight
- **Deepfake & micro‑targeting controls:** Targeted measures against synthetic media and manipulative political advertising.
- **Human‑in‑the‑loop requirement:** AI can assist law‑making but must not replace human responsibility.

## 🏛️ AI Observatory Details
- **Purpose:** Deliver technical and analytical assistance to parliamentary committees.
- **Funding:** Designed to be budget‑neutral, leveraging existing parliamentary expertise.
- **Authority:** No regulatory or enforcement powers are granted.

## 📅 Next Steps
- The report is slated for debate before the full Parliament during the 19 – 22 October plenary session.
- It builds on the March‑approved AI Convention that placed fundamental rights, democracy and the rule of law at the centre of AI governance, moving the agenda a step further.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- By positioning the EU as a leader in global AI governance, the proposal seeks to shape international standards that match AI’s rapid, cross‑border influence.
- Strengthening election safeguards and human oversight aims to protect democratic accountability and fundamental values in the AI era.
- The AI Observatory could enhance parliamentary scrutiny without creating new regulatory burdens.
