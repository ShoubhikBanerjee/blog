# InterPore2027 Opens Call for Conference Courses and Grants

The InterPore2027 Programming Committee has announced the opening of proposal submissions for Conference Courses, along with new minisymposia and conference grants, as part of the upcoming Annual Meeting in Rio de Janeiro.

## 📋 Course Submission Details

- Submission of proposals for Conference Courses at InterPore2027 is now open.
- Courses will be organized as satellite events of the InterPore Annual Meeting in Rio de Janeiro.
- Standard duration of a given course will be half a day (3-hour lecture).
- Proposals for non-standard duration, if well justified, will be considered.
- Main criteria for selection of proposals include track record of the proposers as well as the opportunity to ensure diversity to a full extent across all courses.
- To submit your proposal, please complete the submission form.

## 🎓 Conference Grants

- InterPore Foundation would once again like to offer a number of conference grants to researchers from academic institutions from countries with low- or middle-income economies, as defined by the World Bank, and to graduate students, to support participation in the InterPore2027 Conference.
- In addition to covering the conference registration fee, a select number of grants will also provide up to €500 toward travel costs.
- Applications are reviewed by a dedicated committee based on academic achievement and the quality of the research abstract.
- Deadline: 15 January 2027 (no applications will be accepted after this date, no exceptions).
- The winners will be announced by mid February.

## 🔬 New Minisymposia

The InterPore2027 Programming Committee announces the introduction of seven new minisymposia, highlighting the continued growth and interdisciplinary reach of the InterPore community. These new sessions cover topics including:
- Phase-change processes in porous media (such as evaporation, freeze-thaw phenomena, and reactive transport that alters pore structures)
- Induced seismicity due to e.g. geothermal energy production
- Biomedical and biological applications
- Colloid and particle transport
- Development of open-source software tools
