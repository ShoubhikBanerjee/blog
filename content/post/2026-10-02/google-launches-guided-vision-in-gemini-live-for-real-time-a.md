# Google launches Guided Vision in Gemini Live for real‑time audio descriptions on Android

Google’s Guided Vision feature debuted today in the Gemini Live app on compatible Android devices, providing real‑time audio descriptions of anything pointed at with the phone’s camera.

## 🔍 Overview
- Guided Vision works when you share your camera in Gemini Live.
- It delivers spoken information about small text, surroundings, objects, and details on specific items.
- Users can ask follow‑up questions, such as reading an expiration date.

## 🧩 How it works
- The phone’s camera feed is processed by Google’s Gemini AI, which generates audio cues to help line up the shot if the target is off‑frame.
- The system is designed for people who are blind, have low vision, or need situational assistance, similar to Apple’s VoiceOver Live Recognition.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Reading fine print** – the AI can read small text and fine print.
- **Object identification** – it can find or identify objects in the scene.
- **Follow‑up queries** – users can ask additional questions about what they see.
- **Audio alignment cues** – Gemini provides cues to adjust the camera view.
- **Safety disclaimer** – Google advises not to rely on Guided Vision for navigation, safe‑travel guidance, obstacle detection, or as a substitute for a cane or mobility aid.

## 🚀 Availability
- Integrated in the Gemini Live app.
- Also available through Google TalkBack.
- Can be enabled via an accessibility shortcut in the Settings app on Android 9 and above, following recent Pixel updates.

## 💡 Why it matters
Guided Vision extends AI‑powered visual assistance to Android users, offering a new way for low‑vision individuals to access information about their environment without additional hardware.
