# Foley Law Firm Advances Generative AI with Blueprint Methodology and Award-Winning FoleyChat

Foley, a major law firm, has rolled out a structured AI Blueprint and its proprietary chatbot, FoleyChat, which won the Best Use of Artificial Intelligence Award at Legalweek 2026.

## 🔍 Overview
- Generative artificial intelligence (AI) is no longer a novelty in legal services.
- By early 2026, 74% of lawyers, tax advisors, and other professionals reported using AI several times a week.
- In corporate law departments, generative AI use more than doubled in twelve months to 52%.

## 📊 Client Expectations
- Nearly 80% of corporate clients say AI‑enabled quality is “very important or essential” when selecting advisors, while only 6% believe they receive it.
- 32% of disappointed clients have reconsidered or plan to reconsider law‑firm relationships based on the use of AI, and among those clients, one‑third estimate that more than $1 million in annual work is at risk.

## 🛠️ Foley’s AI Approach
| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| Design | Workflows are superior to lists of tools. |
| Authorization | Insist on an owner for agentic work; an attorney must authorize, review, and be accountable for the AI output. |
| Proof | Use of AI (e.g., eDiscovery) must be defensible; requires validation, sampling protocols, escalation procedures, and audit trails tracking attorney decisions. |
| Accountability | A defensible workflow documents source materials, AI outputs, attorney revisions, and the authority supporting the final work product. |

## 🤖 FoleyChat and Other Tools
| Tool | Use Case |
|---|---|
| FoleyChat | Enables attorneys to build proprietary agents for recurring legal tasks; IP team uses it to stress‑test claim amendments and arguments against patent materials, prior art, and examiner data. |
| Relativity aiR | Compresses discovery review from weeks or months to days. |
| Harvey & CoCounsel | Identify and organize key authorities so attorney time focuses on strategy rather than only research. |

## 🚀 Impact on Practice
- Foley’s attorneys have integrated AI into pre‑existing workstreams, such as using AI throughout a deal’s lifecycle to surface diligence findings early and benchmark drafted language against filing databases.
- Litigators employ AI‑assisted review to shift attorney effort from pure research to strategic advice.
- The objective is faster, more consistent work that gives clients earlier decisions on a more complete record.
- Through its Practice Innovation program, Foley leverages AI across diligence, legal research, and contract drafting by partnering legal‑technology teams with practice groups to deliver vetted AI solutions.
- Foley’s AI Blueprint methodology starts with a legal task, maps where AI adds value, and specifies inputs, source documents, and quality‑control checkpoints before a workflow earns “validated” status.
