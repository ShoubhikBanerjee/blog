# MarketBeat launches weekly company news sentiment scoring system

MarketBeat has introduced a news sentiment scoring system that rates each headline on a scale from 2 (good news) to -2 (bad news) and tracks average sentiment for companies over the most recent 7 days.

## 🔍 Overview
- Sentiment scores are calculated from headlines collected from hundreds of news outlets.
- Scores help identify companies receiving positive or negative press.
- The system also compares the number of articles published in the last week to a typical week.

## ⚙️ How it works
- News headlines are tagged by company.
- Each headline receives a score ranging from 2 (good) to -2 (bad).
- Company news sentiment scores track the average sentiment of all articles about the company over the most recent 7 days.
- The platform shows a sector‑average sentiment for context.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Investors can gauge short‑term market perception of a company.
- The sentiment metric provides a quick visual cue of news tone.
- Comparing weekly article volume to the average helps spot spikes in media attention.
- Sector‑level sentiment offers a benchmark for individual company scores.
