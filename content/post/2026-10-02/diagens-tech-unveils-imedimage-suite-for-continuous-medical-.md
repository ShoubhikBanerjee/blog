# Diagens Tech Unveils iMedImage Suite for Continuous Medical Imaging AI

## 🔍 Overview
On Sept. 25, during the fifth Global Digital Trade Expo, Diagens Tech demonstrated how medical imaging data can drive AI‑enabled productivity. The company presented a four‑layer product suite that builds on its universal foundation model, iMedImage®, and showed more than 20 use cases across specialties such as obstetrics, cardiology, emergency care, and oncology.

## 🧩 Architecture
| Layer | Description |
|------|-------------|
| iMedImage® | Large‑scale foundation model for medical imaging covering 43 organs/anatomical sites and 61 disease areas |
| iMedStudio™ | Workspace for multimodal intelligent annotation, AI‑assisted image processing, expert review and quality control |
| iMedMaaS® | Platform for training and deploying models for specific specialties |
| iMedLoop™ (includes DoctorBench®) | Closed‑loop system linking data collaboration, evaluation, release and feedback |

## ⚙️ How it works
- Clinicians can participate in data annotation and correction through iMedStudio™, feeding professional expertise into ongoing model training.
- iMedMaaS® trains specialty‑specific models on the foundation model’s knowledge.
- iMedLoop™ continuously updates the production system, addressing the reality that clinical scenarios are neither standardized nor static.
- AI AutoVision®, a karyotype‑assisted diagnostic software built on iMedImage®, has received Class III medical device registration from China’s NMPA.

## 📊 Key details
- More than **3,000 professional annotators** support the platform.
- The underlying dataset contains **28.95 million medical imaging records**.
- The suite supports imaging modalities including CT, MRI, X‑ray, ultrasound, CTA and endoscopy.
- Partnerships with **99 hospitals**, including **65 Grade A tertiary hospitals**.
- Demonstrated **>20 representative use cases** spanning obstetrics & gynecology, growth & development, cardiovascular & cerebrovascular conditions, emergency & critical care, and oncology.

## 💡 Why it matters
Medical AI lacks a fixed set of standardized answers; clinicians may interpret the same scan differently, and new disease entities and diagnostic modalities constantly emerge. Diagens Tech’s closed‑loop workflow and customizable foundation model aim to keep AI tools up‑to‑date, allowing continuous, compliant model rebuilding as clinical knowledge evolves. This approach aligns with the view that future competition will focus on the ability to create tailored models efficiently and continuously.
