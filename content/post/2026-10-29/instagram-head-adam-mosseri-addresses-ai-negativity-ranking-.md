# Instagram Head Adam Mosseri Addresses AI Negativity, Ranking, and Account Labeling

In an interview on the Colin and Samir podcast, Instagram head Adam Mosseri addressed growing anxiety surrounding artificial intelligence, discussing creator concerns, platform ranking, and Instagram's policies on AI-generated content.

## 🔍 Overview

Mosseri acknowledged that negativity surrounding AI is "going to get worse" and will continue to intensify over the next couple of years, particularly across creative industries. He noted that while some frustration and anger is founded and some unfounded, the underlying sentiment across various fields comes down to "a fear of being left behind in some way."

## ⚙️ Content Ranking and Policies

Mosseri detailed how Instagram approaches AI-generated material and profiles on the platform:

- **AI Ranking Quality:** Instagram does not rank AI creators differently, but Mosseri indicated the platform likely does not rank AI content as well as non-AI content due to quality variations. He noted there is both "great AI content" and "AI slop," though he believes the term "slop" is used "too liberally."
- **No Banning Strategy:** Instagram is actively working on solutions for misleading AI content like celebrity deepfakes, but Mosseri stated, "I don't think it's practical for us to try and ban AI content from the platform."
- **Account Labeling:** Following an initial label launch earlier this year and a crackdown on AI-generated profiles in August, Instagram is starting to require AI accounts to label themselves so users can distinguish between real and un-real content.

## 💡 Key Concerns Identified

During the interview, Mosseri outlined specific fears expressed by creators and professionals:

- **Content Rights:** Concerns over creators owning their content and models being trained on that content.
- **Safety and Displacement:** Fears regarding platform safety and job displacement affecting content creators, engineers, and mathematicians.
