# Microsoft Ends Copilot Support on LG and Samsung Smart TVs

Microsoft announced that it will discontinue the Copilot AI assistant on all smart TVs, including the LG and Samsung models that launched with the feature at CES 2025.

## 🔍 Overview
- Microsoft is ending Copilot support on smart TVs.
- The decision affects LG and Samsung TVs that received Copilot access in 2025.
- The information originated from an on‑screen notification on a webOS‑powered LG TV, not from an official press release.

## 🗓️ Timeline
- **CES 2025:** LG and Samsung announced new smart TVs with built‑in Microsoft Copilot.
- **December 2025:** LG added an unremovable Copilot shortcut to its webOS, prompting user backlash.
- **Early 2026:** LG allowed the shortcut to be deleted after user complaints.
- **2026 (scheduled):** Copilot functionality is slated for discontinuation on all TVs; the exact date is “subject to Microsoft’s timeline.”

## 🤖 What Copilot Did on TVs
- Acted as an AI‑powered companion that users could chat with.
- Answered questions about the current screen content.
- Provided recommendations for TV shows and movies.

## ⚙️ Why the Change
- Earlier in 2026, Microsoft launched a new Copilot “super app” that bundles chat, agents, and coding tools.
- The super app shifts Copilot’s focus toward business and productivity uses rather than a digital companion on consumer devices.

## 📣 Current Status
- A notification on LG TVs states: “the Copilot‑enabled feature on your TV is no longer available and may stop functioning shortly.”
- No exact shutdown date has been provided; the feature is scheduled for discontinuation during 2026.

*The discontinuation marks the end of the first generation of AI assistants embedded in mainstream smart TV platforms.*
