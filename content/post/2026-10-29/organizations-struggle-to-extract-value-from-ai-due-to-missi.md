# Organizations Struggle to Extract Value From AI Due to Missing Context Layer

While organizations are widely adopting sophisticated AI platforms and conversational assistants, many are failing to see transformative returns on their investments. An MIT Technology Review Insights poll reveals that despite universal adoption, only a small fraction of enterprises have moved beyond pilots to achieve significant business value, often because the AI lacks a proper understanding of the business context.

## 🔍 Overview

Recent data highlights a significant gap between the implementation of AI and the extraction of value. While every organization surveyed is using AI to some degree, the level of success varies significantly:

| Return on AI Investment | Percentage of Respondents |
| :--- | :--- |
| Moderate gains in specific tasks or teams | 40% |
| Minimal returns or none at all | 23% |
| Meaningful gains | 21% |
| Transformative value | 16% |

## ⚙️ Key details

Most enterprises are still in the early stages of integrating AI into their workflows. Implementation levels remain concentrated in testing and assistive roles:

*   **Piloting use cases:** 38% of respondents.
*   **Employee assistance (co-pilots/chat assistants):** 35% of respondents.
*   **AI agents in production:** 12% of respondents.
*   **Automating most work in key processes:** 3% of respondents.

## 🧩 How it works

Prukalpa Sankar, co-founder of Atlan, identifies "context" as the missing layer between a business and its AI models. This context layer acts as architectural infrastructure with specific functions:

*   **Positioning:** It sits between an organization’s systems of record and data and its AI platforms.
*   **Translation:** It converts enterprise intelligence into governed, machine-usable context for models and agents.
*   **Knowledge Compounding:** It enables organizational knowledge to compound over time.

## 💡 Why it matters

Without a context layer, AI outcomes can be wrong or confused. Sankar explains that artificial general intelligence is already here, but it remains underutilized because it lacks business understanding. "Without a context layer for AI, it’s almost like you’re hiring the smartest intern in the world, blindfolding them, and throwing them into your organization," Sankar says. This lack of situational awareness is cited as a primary reason why companies struggle to move beyond moderate gains.
