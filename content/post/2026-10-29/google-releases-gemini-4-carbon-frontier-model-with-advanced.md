# Google Releases Gemini 4 Carbon Frontier Model with Advanced Cybersecurity Capabilities

Google has announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its latest frontier model featuring advanced cybersecurity defense, coding, and long-horizon engineering capabilities. Building on the Gemini 4 Argon model released two weeks ago, Carbon delivers improved execution reliability, higher output quality, and state-of-the-art performance across multiple AI evaluation benchmarks.

## 📊 Benchmark Performance

| Benchmark | Score / Rank | Area of Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5.0) | #1 spot (Score: 59) | Overall capability, trailing Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra |
| SEC-bench Pro | 88.2% (#1 spot) | Autonomously discovering flaws in real-world software |
| ExploitGym | 54.8% (#1 spot) | Turning vulnerabilities into exploits |
| CWE-bench | 71.0% | Turning findings into working code fixes (patching) |
| WebDev Arena | #4 spot (1768 Elo) | Web development quality and artifacts (+89 Elo jump) |

## ⚙️ Key Capabilities and Security Stack

- **Cybersecurity & Coding:** Achieves more than a 2x uplift over Argon in secure code generation. In internal testing, the Chrome team reported Carbon performed more than 10X better at generating high-quality patches than another lab's large frontier model. Cloud security platform Wiz also observed Carbon leading benchmarks in web penetration testing for its Scan for Good initiative.
- **Long-Horizon Engineering Tasks:** Internal teams have observed Carbon running for days to persist through dead ends and handle multi-step engineering tasks, such as root-causing and patching subtle bugs across low-level systems and large codebases.
- **Reduced Misalignment and Fabrication:** Cuts unsupported claims by approximately 2x compared to Argon. High-severity misalignment alerts dropped by almost 80% in live internal coding environments, and successful cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0 was reduced to 0.0%.
- **Task Execution:** Reduced overreach by 26% and under-service by 29%, enabling reliable end-to-end multi-step execution within project constraints.
- **User Collaboration:** Retrives relevant sources and surfaces discrepancies to users before taking action. It provides methodological caveats or explicitly lists remaining requirements when actions are blocked.
- **Multi-Layered Safety:** Built on Argon's safety stack, combining model training, prompt-injection resilience, real-time misuse defenses, and chain-of-thought monitoring.

## 🚀 Pricing and Rollout

- **Current Availability:** Access is currently limited to a subset of cyber defenders and cybersecurity defense companies, with phased expansion planned for trusted partners in the Fairwind Program.
- **Pre-Release Testing:** Google is actively engaged in the U.S. government's voluntary process for pre-release model testing.
- **Future Release:** Access will expand to developers, businesses, and consumers across Google product surfaces after gathering feedback and iterating on guardrails.
- **Pricing:** Launches at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens discounted by 95%.
