# Chrome adds Gemini‑powered autofill that pulls data from Gmail

Chrome’s enhanced autofill is expanding. Starting today it will use Gemini with Personal Intelligence to suggest information sourced from your Gmail, helping you fill forms without hunting for emails.

## 🔍 Overview
- The feature builds on last year’s improvements that added name, passport number, email address, and physical addresses from Google Accounts and services like Google Wallet.
- It now taps Gmail to surface details such as order numbers, tracking numbers, and travel bookings.

## 🧩 How it works
- Gemini with Personal Intelligence scans your Gmail for relevant data when you are completing a form.
- It can retrieve:
  - Shipment tracking numbers
  - Flight booking numbers or hotel reservations
  - Order numbers of recent purchases
- The suggestions appear directly in the form fields.

## ⚙️ Key details
- All suggested data remains encrypted while displayed.
- Users can manage what enhanced autofill can access in Chrome’s settings or disable the feature entirely.

## 🚀 Availability
- Google is rolling out the improvements today in the United States.
- The feature is available through Gemini in Chrome for users with Personal Intelligence turned on in the desktop, Android, and iOS versions of the browser.

## 💡 Why it matters
- By pulling information from Gmail, the feature can save you the time of searching for confirmation emails, receipts, or shipping updates yourself.
