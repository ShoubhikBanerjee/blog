# GitHub Octoverse 2026 Shows Tripling of Commits and Pull Requests Driven by Agentic Engineering

GitHub’s 2026 Octoverse report, announced by EVP Jay Parikh, reveals a sharp rise in development activity that the report links to the rise of “agentic engineering.”

## 📈 Overview
- The report describes a “pretty stark representation of the impact of agentic engineering on GitHub.”
- Growth is most pronounced in the second half of the year.

## 📊 Key Metrics
| Metric | Monthly Value (2026) |
|---|---|
| Merged pull requests | 220 million |
| Commits | 7.38 billion |
| New repositories | 30.3 million |

## 🤖 Agentic Engineering Impact
- Agentic engineering is cited as the primary driver behind the observed surge in activity.

## 🛠️ AI‑Powered Methodology
- **Location normalization:** LLM classification extracts ISO 3166‑1 alpha‑2 country codes from user profiles to adjust for VPN‑related bias.
- **Industry classification:** A fine‑tuned multilingual embedding model maps public repositories (2022‑2026) to NAICS sectors using name, description, topics, and README.
- **Interoperability detection:** Repositories are counted when their metadata mentions terms such as “interoperability,” “reverse engineering,” “clean room implementation,” “drop‑in replacement,” or “protocol re‑implementation.”

## 🌐 Industry & Interoperability Insights
- The report breaks out activity by industry, based on the NAICS‑sector classification.
- Separate counts are provided for repositories involving interoperability or reverse engineering.
