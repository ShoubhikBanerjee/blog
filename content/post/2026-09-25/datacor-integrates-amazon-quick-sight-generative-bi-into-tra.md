# Datacor Integrates Amazon Quick Sight Generative BI into TrackAbout Solution

Datacor has collaborated with Amazon Web Services (AWS) to integrate Amazon Quick Sight into its TrackAbout solution, providing industrial gas and welding distributors with a multi-tenant rental analytics tool featuring interactive dashboards and a natural language search bar.

## 💡 Why it matters
Previously, rental data was stored within TrackAbout’s operational systems, but extracting insights required going through Datacor’s support team. Customers had to request raw data exports or custom SQL Server Reporting Services (SSRS) reports, with delivery times ranging from several days to weeks.

## 🧩 How it works
The solution relies on an automated cross-cloud data pipeline that ingests and transforms operational data from TrackAbout’s third-party cloud environment into AWS. This pipeline regularly refreshes datasets in Amazon Quick Sight SPICE (Super-fast, Parallel In-memory Calculation Engine) on a scheduled basis to ensure dashboards reflect recent data.

## ⚙️ Key details
At the center of the experience is Amazon Q in Quick Sight, a generative AI-powered assistant for business intelligence. This capability allows business users to ask plain-language questions and receive contextually relevant answers.

**Example Natural Language Queries:**
* "Show me rental revenue by product type last quarter"
* "Which customers have the most unreturned cylinders this month?"
* "What is the average rental rate by asset class across my top 20 accounts?"
* "How many cylinders have been out for more than 90 days?"

**Pre-built Dashboard Metrics:**
* Fleet utilization rates
* Billing exception summaries
* Revenue recovery trends
* Customer-level asset aging

## 🔍 Overview
TrackAbout helps distributors manage cylinder and container assets across the supply chain. Collectively, TrackAbout customers manage over 725,000 bills per month and 27.5 million assets.
