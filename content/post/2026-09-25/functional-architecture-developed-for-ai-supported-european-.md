# Functional Architecture Developed for AI-Supported European Electricity Trading

A new paper proposes a functional architecture for AI-supported electricity trading designed to operate within the constrained multi-layer system of the EU, where legal design, exchange microstructure, and network physics are executed across forward, day-ahead, intraday, and balancing horizons.

## 🧩 How it works
The framework proposes deploying AI as a bounded decision component inside regulated market operation with explicit governance, rather than as an unconstrained prediction layer. The formal system specification consists of:

* A decision-state vector
* Residual-exposure accounting
* A constrained optimization objective
* An executable-action permission gate
* Fail-closed AI control logic with auditable records

## ⚙️ Key details
The architecture is aligned with market-coupling mechanics, cross-zonal transfer constraints, and compliance obligations under the following regulations:

| Regulation | Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| REMIT | Compliance obligations |
| MiFID II | Compliance obligations |
| MiFIR | Compliance obligations |
| EMIR | Compliance obligations |

## 🔍 Overview
Through the mapping of Nominated Electricity Market Operator (NEMO) venues and related exchange operators into an operational venue topology, the analysis identified specific areas where cross-border coordination fails in practice:

* Interface-level timing
* Permission heterogeneity
* Balancing-layer coupling
