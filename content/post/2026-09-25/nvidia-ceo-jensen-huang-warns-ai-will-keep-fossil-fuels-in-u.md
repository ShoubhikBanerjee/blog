# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang warns AI will keep fossil fuels in use

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang discussed the future of energy and AI’s impact on our planet on the latest episode of The Ezra Klein Show.

## 🔍 Overview
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang discussed the future of energy and AI’s impact on our planet on the latest episode of The Ezra Klein Show.

## 🌍 Climate Context
- Earth is expected to lose 99 % of its coral reefs.
- One in 10 people worldwide (770 million) already face “acute risk” from sea level rise.
- Since 2001, peak daily wildfire growth rates have more than doubled in the western US.

## ⚡ Energy & AI
- Huang said, “Over the next several years we have to unfortunately use fossil fuels, because we just don’t have enough sustainable energy to make a difference.”
- He attributes record renewable energy purchases to AI.
- Regarding data‑center siting, he stated, “If they don’t want data centers to be built in their town or whatever it is, then so be it. But if you’re going to build in their town, be sure to go there and let them know what’s coming.”

## 📜 Policy & Commitments
- The accord sets a goal of cutting greenhouse gas emissions in half by 2030 and reaching net zero around 2050.

## 🏠 Personal Details
- Huang reportedly bought a $7.5 million home on Maui in 2004 — before wildfires devastated the island in 2023.
- An Nvidia spokesperson confirmed the Huangs “have owned the same home in Maui for many years.”
- The spokesperson also said the Huangs “donated significantly to local relief efforts.”

## 🤝 Response & Criticism
- Huang blames climate action for stalling construction of energy infrastructure, presumably for fossil fuels.
