# Apple Launches Apple Intelligence for Home with HomeKit Secure Video

With the release of iOS 27, Apple has introduced Apple Intelligence for Home, bringing AI-powered text descriptions and video analysis to its HomeKit Secure Video (HKSV) service.

## 🔍 Overview
Apple Intelligence for Home enables compatible cameras to provide natural-language descriptions of activity, such as "someone delivered a package," "a cat played in the room," or "A dog sat on the floor." This capability joins other major security camera providers, including Ring, Google Nest, Blink, Wyze, and Arlo, who offer similar text descriptions combining motion triggers with Vision Language Models.

## ⚙️ Key details
Apple Intelligence adds several new capabilities to the existing smart alerts, facial recognition, and 10 days of video storage provided by HomeKit Secure Video:

* **Natural-language video search:** Users can search through video content using natural language.
* **Event management:** The system surfaces "noteworthy" events and can stitch relevant clips together.
* **Notification control:** A "reduce notifications" feature combines related events into a single updating alert (enabled by default).
* **Video summaries:** A "summarize videos" feature is available (disabled by default).

## 🚀 Availability
To use these features, users require the following:

* An AI-capable iPhone running iOS 27
* A HomeKit Secure Video-compatible camera
* An Apple Home Hub running HomePod/tvOS 27
* An iCloud Plus plan

## 💰 Pricing and Tiers
Apple Intelligence for Home is bundled into specific iCloud Plus plans. All tiers supporting Apple Intelligence also include basic HKSV features for unlimited cameras.

| iCloud Plus Plan | Monthly Cost | Camera Limit for Apple Intelligence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2TB Plan | $9.99 (or Apple One $39.95) | One camera |
| 6TB Plan | $29.99 | Two cameras |
| 12TB Plan | $59.99 | Up to five cameras |

No tier supports more than five cameras. For comparison, standard HKSV recording starts at $0.99 for one camera (50GB plan), $2.99 for five (200GB), and $9.99 for unlimited (2TB). Other platforms, such as Amazon's Ring and Google Home's Nest, charge $20 a month for their respective AI features and recording options for unlimited cameras.
