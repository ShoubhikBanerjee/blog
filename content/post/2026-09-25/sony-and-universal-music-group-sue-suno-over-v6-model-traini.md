# Sony and Universal Music Group Sue Suno Over v6 Model Training

Sony and Universal Music Group (UMG) have filed a lawsuit against Suno, alleging that the company's new v6 model infringes on their copyrights.

## ⚖️ Legal Allegations
Sony and UMG, who did not sign licensing agreements with Suno, accuse the company of "model laundering." The labels argue that:
* Training a new model on the outputs of an infringing model does not eliminate infringement.
* Value from copied recordings is passed from infringing models into their outputs, and then into v6.
* v6 is described in the complaint as "the fruit of the same poisoned tree."
* Even models not directly trained on the plaintiffs' recordings benefit from Suno's "retained unauthorized copies."

## ⚙️ Technical Claims
Sony further alleges that Suno utilized distillation to train v6, claiming it was trained to replicate results from previous "teacher" models created using infringing data. The labels contend that Suno's models will continue to be built on stolen data unless the company starts from scratch.

## 🔍 Suno's Response
Regarding the development of v6, Suno representatives have stated the following:
* Jack Brody told The Verge it was "trained from the ground up, with a new set of data," which included "user data."
* Suno later confirmed to Engadget that this data includes "creations" from users.
