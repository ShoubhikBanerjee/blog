# EAGER Reinforcement Learning Framework Boosts Structured Event Extraction

A new reinforcement‑learning framework called EAGER has been introduced to improve generative event extraction.

## 🔍 Overview
- EAGER combines fine‑grained verifiable rewards with Schema‑Contrastive Advantage Estimation.
- Designed to alleviate advantage collapse under sparse binary rewards.
- Evaluated on seven benchmark datasets.

## 🧩 How it works
- Reward design explicitly targets:
  - Structural validity
  - Extraction accuracy
  - Groundedness
  - Coverage
  - Over‑generation
  - Span precision
- Uses Schema‑Contrastive Advantage Estimation to compute advantages in a contrastive manner, addressing advantage collapse.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Experiments show EAGER consistently outperforms prompting, supervised fine‑tuning, and prior RL baselines.
- Achieves a substantial improvement over the strongest prior method.
- Results demonstrate that task‑aligned verifiable rewards and contrastive advantage estimation substantially improve structured extraction.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Structured extraction benefits from more reliable reward signals, reducing errors in generated events.
- The framework leverages large language models to enhance information extraction from complex texts.
