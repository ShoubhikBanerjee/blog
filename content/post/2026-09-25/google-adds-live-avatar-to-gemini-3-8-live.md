# Google adds Live Avatar to Gemini 3.8 Live

Google has updated Gemini 3.8 Live with a new feature called "Live Avatar," which provides an animated AI persona that responds to users in real time.

## 🔍 Overview
Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar gives Google’s AI a face. This update follows the reveal of the Gemini 3.8 Live model, which Google says "processes visual inputs in near real-time."

## ⚙️ Key details
Live Avatar includes the following capabilities:

* **Visual responses:** The avatar can lip-sync and show different facial expressions during conversations.
* **Multilingual support:** It can transition between 97 supported languages "without degrading video fidelity or introducing visual drift." Google shared a video showing the avatar's mouth animation lining up while talking in English and Japanese.
* **Information display:** It can pull up information on-screen while speaking.
* **Customization:** Google will offer a library of preset avatars, but organizations can also create their own.
* **Safety and verification:** Output includes an invisible SynthID watermark and safeguards to "respect identity."

## 🚀 Availability
Live Avatar is currently only available to Gemini Enterprise customers.
