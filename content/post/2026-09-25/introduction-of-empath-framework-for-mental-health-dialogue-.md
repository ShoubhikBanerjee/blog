# Introduction of EMPATH Framework for Mental Health Dialogue Analysis

Researchers have introduced EMPATH, a framework designed to understand affective dynamics within mental health dialogues.

## 🧩 How it works
EMPATH analyzes affective dynamics across three granularities:

| Granularity | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Turn-level | Labels |
| Transitions | Probabilities |
| Global | Conversation archetypes |

## ⚙️ Key details
When applied to text-based crisis conversations with self-identified Black texters discussing grief, the framework identified:
* Persistent negative affect
* Gradual hope-ward transitions
* Distinct emotional roles between texters and volunteers
* Heterogeneous recovery trajectories
