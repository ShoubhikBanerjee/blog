# Wonderful Thailand CEO Discusses Scaling AI Agents at Techsauce 2026

Ariya Banomyong, President of Wonderful Thailand, addressed the challenge of moving AI from pilots to production at the Techsauce Global Summit 2026.

## 📈 Scaling from Pilots to Production
Most organizations are already running AI pilots, but few reach production. Banomyong stated that the constraint is rarely the technology itself, but how organizations are set up to design, deploy, and measure it. He noted that in previous technology cycles—internet adoption, e-commerce, and online video—the technology arrived as expected, and what differed was when organizations chose to act.

## 🧩 Architecture and Design Strategy
To scale effectively, organizations should avoid building separate agents within each function. Banomyong argued that the value of AI tends to appear across functions rather than within any one of them. When each department funds its own initiative and selects its own provider, the result can be several implementations that were never designed to work together. Solutions built for a sandbox are rarely ready for enterprise volumes or the wider set of use cases a business will want to run on the same foundation. Designing for production scale and multiple use cases from the outset, then piloting within that architecture, reduces later rebuilds.

## 📊 Use Case Examples
Banomyong illustrated successful deployments with two client examples:

| Scenario | Agents Involved | Key Mechanism | Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SME Lending | Relationship assistant, lending agent, credit reviewer | Context sharing across agents; legacy system integration | Chat message moves to booking in core system |
| Home Broadband | Single assistant | Camera reads router status lights | Customer handled from start to finish without queue jumping |

## 🛡️ Risk and Governance
On risk, Banomyong reframed the discussion around designing safeguards. In Wonderful's architecture, an observer agent runs in the background to check that other agents operate within policy, governance, and regulatory requirements. Bank Hapoalim in Israel went live within three weeks at 88% containment, meaning roughly 880 of every 1,000 calls or chats were resolved without human involvement. Case studies on Wonderful's website cite comparable results at 80% or above.

## 🚀 Company Updates
Wonderful operates in more than 35 markets and recently opened in Thailand with a local team. Founded in 2025 and headquartered in Amsterdam, the company positions its product as an enterprise AI operating system. In September 2026, the company announced a $550 million Series C led by Insight Partners at a $5 billion valuation. The company was also named Official Enterprise AI Partner of FC Bayern in the same month.

## ⏱️ Delivery Timeline
Deployments generally take four to eight weeks, depending on workflow and integration complexity. Governance, guardrails, and evaluation tooling are in place from the start. Wonderful's teams work directly with clients to redesign cross-functional workflows, then transfer capability to internal teams who build and manage agents on the same platform.
