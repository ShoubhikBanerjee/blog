# Meta Launches Meta One Subscription Bundles with Enhanced AI Usage

Meta is launching Meta One subscription bundles that pair its standalone app subscriptions with additional AI usage. While some bundles were tested earlier this year, they are now available globally for individual users, creators, and businesses.

## 🧩 How it works

Meta One bundles integrate subscriptions for Instagram Plus, WhatsApp Plus, and Facebook Plus. These bundles provide "more media generation with Meta AI," including the use of Instagram’s Restyle and generating images with Muse. Meta states that the "core experience" of Meta AI and its apps will remain free, and standalone subscriptions for Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp will remain available without a bundle.

## ⚙️ Key details

### Individual Plans
Individual bundles start at $7.99 per month and include perks such as a bold follow button and 48-hour Instagram stories. These bundles are a few dollars cheaper than paying $11 per month for all three standalone subscriptions.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Core | $7.99 | Instagram Plus, WhatsApp Plus, Facebook Plus, and increased Meta AI media generation |
| Premium | $19.99 | Instagram Plus, WhatsApp Plus, Facebook Plus, and increased Meta AI media generation |

### Creator and Business Plans
Bundles for creators and businesses range from $14.99 to $499 per month and include a verified badge, higher search result rankings, and monitoring for fake impersonation accounts.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Essential | $14.99 | Verified badge, verified WhatsApp Business channel, impersonation protection, and Meta Business Agent on WhatsApp for 24/7 customer response |
| Advanced | $49.99 | Scheduling stories up to 30 days in advance, links in reels and organic posts, exportable analytics, audience insights, team account access, more linked devices, WhatsApp business broadcast credits, and increased Meta Business Agent responses |
| Expert | $149 | Highest levels of feature access and Meta Business Agent capacity for scale |
| Max | $499 | Highest levels of feature access and Meta Business Agent capacity for scale |

## 🚀 Availability

Meta One bundles are available globally starting today. Meta plans to expand these bundles over time to include AI glasses and "Edits."
