# Anchor Ventures to Host Panel on AI Agent Workflows for Founders

Anchor Ventures is hosting a two-part session featuring a panel of founders who have spent the past year building with specialized AI agents to increase shipping pace with lean teams.

## ⚙️ Key details

**Panel Participants**

| Name | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Kat Erickson | Moderator, Author and Strategist, Tech Confidential: The Insider's Playbook for Daring Entrepreneurs |
| Greg Cangialosi | Founder and CEO, Twilight Technologies Inc. |
| Chris White | Founder, NexSys Labs |
| Rob Wray | Founder and CEO, TrayVerify |

**Session Topics**

* Comparison of what a three-person team can ship versus a 15-person team
* The advantages of a team of agents over a single large agent
* Current implementations used by founders and their associated costs
* Available free cloud and startup credits via hyperscaler programs
* Tasks that cannot be handed off and require human sign-off
* Managing burnout and the shortening lifespan of technical skills

## 🚀 Availability

This is the first of two sessions. The second part, "AI Agents in Action: A Live Deep Dive," will take place on Thursday, October 29, and will feature the live build of an agent workflow.
