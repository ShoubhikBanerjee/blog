# New Educational Programs for Agentic AI and AI Engineering

Several new educational programs have been introduced to teach agentic AI, focusing on systems that can perform tasks, use tools, make decisions, and complete multi-step workflows with limited human intervention.

## 🔍 Overview

Agentic AI differs from basic chatbots through its ability to utilize tools and manage complex workflows. Key technical concepts include:

* **Agentic RAG**: Combines retrieval capabilities with agent-based decision-making.
* **Multi-agent systems**: Allows multiple specialized agents to work together.

## ⚙️ Key details

Comprehensive curricula for agentic AI are designed to go beyond basic prompt engineering, covering the following technical areas:

* **Architecture and Frameworks**: AI agent architecture, LangGraph, and CrewAI for multi-agent workflows.
* **Development**: LLM APIs, function calling, structured outputs, tool use, and API/system integrations.
* **Data and Memory**: Vector databases, memory, and retrieval.
* **Operations**: Agent evaluation, monitoring, observability, error handling, fallback strategies, and production deployment.

Practical workflows taught in these courses include automating repetitive tasks, connecting AI tools, creating business workflows, using AI for research and analysis, and designing practical AI use cases.
