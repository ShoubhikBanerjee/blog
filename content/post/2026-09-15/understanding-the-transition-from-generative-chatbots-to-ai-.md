# Understanding the Transition from Generative Chatbots to AI Agents

AI agents represent a step forward from generative chatbots, marking a transition from reactive AI to AI that takes action by independently pursuing goals and developing solutions.

## 🔍 Overview
Chatbots and AI agents both serve meaningful use cases and can improve everyday work life, particularly in knowledge management for customer service and ITSM. While chatbots provide easy access to knowledge bases and answer user questions, AI agents are intended to simplify, accelerate, and make processes more consistent.

## 🧩 How it works

### Chatbots
Chatbots are based on generative AI and rely on large language models (LLMs). They use machine learning (ML) and statistical patterns to generate natural language. They operate on a question-and-answer principle where the human user must provide precise, purposeful instructions—a practice known as prompt engineering—to obtain compelling results.

### AI Agents
AI agents are specific applications of the overarching concept of agentic AI. An AI agent can be a piece of a larger agentic AI system that completes a sequence of tasks to pursue a goal. Unlike chatbots, agents:
* Independently plan, reason, and complete multi-step tasks.
* Act proactively rather than responding to questions.
* Break tasks down into individual steps and make decisions on how to achieve a goal.
* Adapt as needed and operate independently.

## ⚙️ Key details

| Feature | Generative Chatbots | AI Agents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Function** | Describe possible paths to a solution | Independently produce the solution |n| **Input Dependency** | Dependent on human input/prompts | Not dependent on human input; proactive |
| **Approach** | Reactive (Question-and-Answer) | Action-oriented (Autonomous goal pursuit) |
| **Primary Focus** | Generating useful content | Workflows and repetitive, routine processes |

## 💡 Why it matters
AI agents can handle complex and dynamic processes. In ITSM, for example, they can independently analyze, prioritize, route, and resolve tickets. By focusing on routine processes, they prevent errors and free up human time for value-creating activities.
