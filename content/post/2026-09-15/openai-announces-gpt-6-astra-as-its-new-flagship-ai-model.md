# OpenAI Announces GPT-6 Astra as its New Flagship AI Model

OpenAI has announced GPT-6 Astra as its new flagship model. Positioned officially as the company's "most intelligent and most aligned model," the launch presents Astra as a highly capable assistant that can perform any computer-based task quickly. The model represents the culmination of years of pre-training, reinforcement learning (RL), and post-training.

## 🔍 Overview

While OpenAI marketed the model under the premise that Astra can replace humans for any computer-based task, the rollout itself experienced several complications. The launch was marked by delayed access, broken or delayed blog posts, and confusion regarding user eligibility. Furthermore, early access granted to influencers and select testers prior to general paid users sparked significant dissatisfaction. In response, OpenAI compensated ChatGPT paid users with "banked resets" (補填的なリセット枠) based on the number of days they were unable to access Astra.

The model is being rolled out gradually. It was initially made available to select organizations, with plans to expand to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, API, and AWS within a few days.

### Pricing Structure

OpenAI has established two tier options for Astra:

| Tier | Input Cost (per 1M tokens) | Output Cost (per 1M tokens) | Performance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| standard | $10 | $50 | Standard speed |
| fast | $20 | $100 | Up to 2.5x speed |

## ⚙️ Key Features and Runtimes

Alongside the core model, OpenAI introduced several runtime and product features:

*   **Codex Updates**: Allows users to ask questions while Codex continues working.
*   **Experimental Context Feature**: Keeps notes during long-running tasks and enables past context searches.
*   **Responses API Additions**:
    *   Asynchronous function calling
    *   Mid-turn steering (inserting messages or tool outputs mid-reasoning)
    *   Changing reasoning effort without breaking the cache

## 📊 Performance and Benchmarks

Astra demonstrated strong results across various benchmarks, though some metrics vary based on the testing harness used:

### General Benchmarks

*   **FrontierMath Tier 4**: 98% accuracy.
*   **ExploitBench**: 100% accuracy.
*   **Mind2Web**: 1.9x faster than GPT-5.6 Sol (incorporating Codex harness improvements).
*   **ECI (Epoch AI)**: Achieved a record score of 169 (previously 163).
*   **SRE-Bench (Vals)**: Virtually saturated with a 99.2% pass@4 score (compared to GPT-5.6 Sol's 68.7%), requiring about one-fourth of the output tokens (noting that OpenAI utilized pass@4, no step limits, and a custom harness).
*   **Mathematical Achievements**: OpenAI stated that Astra has already assisted in solving long-unresolved open math problems, highlighting prime gap-related postings as a concrete example.

### ARC-AGI Evaluations

*   **ARC-AGI-3**: Claims of 99.9% on the provider adapter harness, though standard harness scores were reported at 62.7% (or 63% direct score). François Chollet reported standard harness at 66%, rising to nearly 100% with a continuous conversation harness and custom compression. The evaluation noted Astra built precise symbolic models for unknown environments, outperforming humans at a 96% level. A single game run cost approximately $360.
*   **ARC-AGI-2**: 95.0% score.
*   **ARC-AGI-1**: 98.5% score (tied with Fable 5). The standard run max cost reached $26,000, but due to Astra's low action count, it was cheaper than low or medium settings, completing 96% of levels with fewer actions than the human median.
*   *Note*: François Chollet noted that ARC-AGI-3 saturated about twice as fast as expected, climbing from under 1% to nearly 100% in six months.

### Comparative Indices (Artificial Analysis & Others)

*   **Coding Agent Index**: Astra scored 67, placing it on par with Claude Opus 5 and Fable 5 (Fable 5.1 leads at 70). Astra exhibits 70% higher token efficiency than GPT-5.6 Sol. On the Codex harness, it uses one-third of the tokens of GPT-5.6 Sol and one-fifth of Claude Opus 5 xhigh. At equivalent scores, Astra costs less than half of Claude Fable 5.
*   **Intelligence Index**: Astra scored 61, tying with GPT-5.6 Sol but falling 5 points behind Claude Fable 5.1 and trailing Meta Muse Spark 1.3 max. Its output tokens are roughly 10% fewer than GPT-5.6 Sol max effort, but its token unit price is 2.5x higher (resulting in a 75% higher cost per task at max effort).
*   **Perplexity / WANDR**: Achieved a score of 0.682 at $11.98 per task. This was the highest score among all tested models, representing a 13.5% higher score and 6.1% lower cost than Fable 5.1, and a 27.0% higher score at a 3.3% higher cost than Opus 5.
*   **FrontierCode 1.1 (Cognition)**: Astra scored 0.4 points behind Fable 5 but at a 64% lower cost, achieving a new SOTA on internal benchmarks.

## 💡 Analysis and Debate

Astra achieved significant safety improvements, reducing its hallucination rate from 92% to 51% while raising overall accuracy by 4 percentage points. 

However, a major point of contention emerged regarding model transparency. The documentation indicated that while alignment improved, "Chain-of-Thought observability (visibility of thinking) decreased." This has sparked a significant industry debate regarding the auditability and transparency of the model's reasoning processes.
