# Former Anthropic Employee Reports Researcher Fears of AI Existential Risk

Jacob Coxon, a former Anthropic employee, recently confirmed that many researchers at the company believe artificial intelligence could lead to an extinction-level event by the end of the decade. Bryan Cantrill has since responded to these claims, specifically addressing concerns regarding biological weapons.

## 🔍 Overview
According to Jacob Coxon, Anthropic researchers have expressed fears that AI development could result in the death of the human population by 2030. The specific mechanisms for this risk were identified as:
* Hacking critical infrastructure
* The creation of extinction-level bioweapons

## ⚙️ Key details
Bryan Cantrill discussed these claims during an appearance on the Oxide and Friends podcast. While the researchers cite catastrophic outcomes, Cantrill expressed doubts regarding the feasibility of AI-generated bioweapons.

| Individual | Role/Action | Stated Position |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Jacob Coxon | Former Anthropic employee | Confirmed researchers believe AI could kill all humans by the end of the decade via hacking and bioweapons. |
| Bryan Cantrill | Industry commentator | Expressed doubt regarding bioweapon fears on the Oxide and Friends podcast. |

## 💡 Why it matters
The discussion centers on whether fear-based narratives are influencing the perception of AI capabilities. Cantrill warned against these speculative scenarios, stating, "I really think we need to be careful because it's so easy to be overcome with fear when we kind of make up these... it can give you biological weapons."
