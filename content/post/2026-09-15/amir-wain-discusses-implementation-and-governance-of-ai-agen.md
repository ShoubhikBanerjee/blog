# Amir Wain Discusses Implementation and Governance of AI Agents at i2c

Amir Wain is piloting the use of AI agents at i2c, shifting from adding tools to individual stages of a process toward redesigning the software development life cycle.

## 🧩 How it works
While a Large Language Model (LLM) interprets prompts and generates responses, an agent utilizes those capabilities within a broader system. This system is defined by the following components:

* **Perception:** The ability to perceive information.
* **Decision-making:** The ability to decide what to do.
* **Action:** The ability to act on those decisions.
* **Learning loop:** A fourth component added by Wain that allows the system to improve based on the outcomes of its actions.

## 💡 Why it matters
Agents enable work to move across functions without an employee initiating every step, performing tasks that previously passed between software applications, departments, and employees. This shift has impacted development speed:

* The cost and time to move from an idea to a prototype have fallen sharply.
* Wain stated that an idea can potentially become a live product on the same day.

## ⚙️ Key details
Wain argues that many operational activities currently requiring human intervention may eventually be performed autonomously. To manage this, he suggests evaluating autonomy based on two dimensions:

| Dimension | Consideration |
| :--- | :--- |
| Reversibility | Whether an action can be readily undone permits more latitude than irreversible actions. |
| Potential Impact | High-risk actions involving regulatory obligations, customers, or money require more control. |

Regarding governance and risk, Wain noted:
* Limits on authority are essential because agents authorized for larger-scale activity create larger exposure.
* Controls are necessary to prevent "rogue agents."
* Enterprises must defend against AI deployed by others and govern their own agents to combat new forms of fraud.
* Human responsibility remains mandatory; Wain stated, “You can’t blame it on the agent,” and “You have to take the responsibility.”
