# ChatGPT Updates with GPT-5 and GPT-5.5 Models

ChatGPT has updated its capabilities with the introduction of GPT-5 and GPT-5.5 models, expanding its utility as a general reasoning and generation assistant.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Model Family:** GPT-5 and GPT-5.5 provide substantially improved reasoning over earlier versions.
* **Deep Research Mode:** Autonomously browses dozens of web sources to synthesise a structured report with citations.
* **Projects:** Maintains persistent context and custom instructions across conversations to accumulate memory for ongoing research or writing projects.
* **Custom GPTs Marketplace:** Offers pre-configured specialist assistants for specific tasks, industries, and knowledge domains.
* **Multi-modal Input:** Handles text, images, files, PDFs, and voice.
* **Additional Integration:** Includes web browsing for real-time information and DALL-E image generation within the interface.

## 🚀 Availability

* **Free Tier:** Provides meaningful access to GPT-5.5 Instant.
* **Plus Tier:** Costs $20 per month and unlocks the full model range, Advanced Voice, file analysis, and higher usage limits.

## 💡 Why it matters

By mid-2026, ChatGPT had adopted 500 million-plus weekly active users, becoming the most widely used AI assistant in the world. The Deep Research mode specifically moves the tool from answering individual questions to conducting multi-source research on behalf of the user.
