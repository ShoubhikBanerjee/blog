# JustVugg releases colibri a minimalist pure C inference engine for MoE models

The new local inference engine JustVugg/colibri has emerged as a high-trending repository, gaining +2,233 stars. The project pushes local inference to its minimalist extreme by running frontier Mixture of Experts (MoE) models on existing hardware.

## 🔍 Overview

With a daily gain of +2,233 stars, JustVugg/colibri is today's second-highest gainer in the tracked repositories. This surge signals a real demand for minimalist local runtimes optimized for MoE-era models, joining a broader local-AI wave that includes new audio and voice tools.

## 🧩 How it works

The engine is designed to optimize local deployment through several core technical implementations:

* **Pure-C codebase:** Built with zero external dependencies to keep the engine as lightweight as possible.
* **Disk streaming:** Streams expert weights directly from disk rather than requiring them to be fully loaded into system memory.
* **Standard hardware compatibility:** Runs complex frontier MoE models on hardware that users already own.

## 💡 Why it matters

The default gateway for local open-weight large language models has expanded to cover models like Kimi-K2.6, GLM-5.2, DeepSeek, gpt-oss, Qwen, and Gemma. Highly optimized local runtimes like colibri and VoiceStudio extend this ecosystem by lowering the resource barriers, making complex multimodal and MoE architectures accessible on consumer-owned hardware.
