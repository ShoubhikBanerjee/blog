# Organizations define new specialized roles to secure AI platforms and manage cyber risks

Organizations are establishing new technical roles to manage the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, focusing on secure platform design and threat mitigation. These positions address emerging risks in generative AI, cloud infrastructure, and critical industrial systems.

## 🔍 Overview
The scope of these responsibilities spans from executive leadership to specialized engineering, covering cloud-based Data and AI/ML services, on-premises environments, and operational technology (OT).

## 🧩 How it works
| Role | Primary Functions |
| :--- | :--- |
| AI & Security Architect | Design secure AI agent platforms, including tools, memory, and models; define controls for sandboxing and human approvals. |
| GenAI CBRNE Cyber Security Expert | Red-team AI models for industrial systems and test safeguards against jailbreaks and adversarial prompts. |
| Senior Lead Security Engineer – Google Cloud | Secure cloud-based Data and AI/ML services and guide engineering teams on security requirements. |
| Manager Cyber Cloud Security and AI | Lead client projects on AI security strategy, governance, and regulatory requirements. |
| Cyber Engineering Manager | Oversee AI/ML-based threat detection and lead security research for Navy cyber-physical systems. |

## ⚙️ Key details
Professional responsibilities within these new frameworks include:
- Defining security controls for sensitive data, credentials, and agent actions.
- Managing emerging threats, including post-quantum risks.
- Assessing vulnerabilities in Kubernetes, CI/CD, and serverless workloads.
- Monitoring endpoint threats across Windows, macOS, Linux, and VDI environments.
- Investigating incidents and strengthening detection across Microsoft 365, Azure, and AWS.
- Designing cyber assessments for DoW programs to identify mission impacts.

## 💡 Why it matters
These specialized roles are designed to prevent information disclosure and misuse of AI models while ensuring that critical infrastructure and data protection standards are maintained as AI technologies are integrated into enterprise backend services.
