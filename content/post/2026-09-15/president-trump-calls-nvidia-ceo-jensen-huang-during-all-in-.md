# President Trump Calls Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang During All-In Summit

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang received a phone call from President Trump while onstage at the All-In Podcast’s All-In Summit on Monday. Huang placed the president on speakerphone to address the live audience.

## 🔍 Overview
During the live call, President Trump addressed concerns regarding the future of artificial intelligence. He described recent fears about AI development as a “hoax” and stated that “the robots will not be taking over.”

## ⚙️ Key details
* President Trump’s comments followed an essay by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, which advocated for slowing AI development and increasing government coordination.
* In response to the discourse, President Trump stated that he intends to push back against the current narrative surrounding AI risks.
* The president also remarked on the economic impact of data centers, claiming they have turned struggling communities into wealthy ones.

## 💡 Why it matters
Following the president's statements, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang commented on the future of the industry:

| Participant | Stance on AI Future |
| :--- | :--- |
| President Trump | The robots will not be taking over |
| Jensen Huang | We’re going to make sure that everybody wins in the AI race in America |

Huang further stated that he intends for every industry, company, state, and individual to benefit from the AI race in America.
