# SimpliSafe Launches Video Doorbell Series 2 with Active Guard AI

SimpliSafe has released the Video Doorbell Series 2, a wired doorbell that integrates AI analysis with live monitoring agents to detect and deter potential threats.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Resolution:** 2K
* **Field of View:** 150-degree head-to-toe view
* **Zoom:** 10x
* **Audio:** Two-way audio
* **Additional Hardware:** Built-in siren
* **Connectivity:** Dual-band Wi-Fi
* **Video Features:** Up to five seconds of pre-roll video and smart alerts for people and packages

## 🧩 How it works

The doorbell utilizes a combination of on-device AI, cloud-based computer vision, and facial recognition to detect suspicious activity. When triggered, the system can utilize Active Guard, which allows a SimpliSafe monitoring agent to drop in on the camera to “see, speak to, and attempt to deter potential intruders and package thieves.”

| Feature | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| LED Status Lights | Amber indicates a monitoring agent is speaking; blue indicates the homeowner is speaking |
| Familiar Faces | Users can create a library of familiar faces to prevent agents from deterring known individuals |
| Privacy | Footage is encrypted at rest and in transit (not end-to-end) |
| Data Sharing | The company "won’t share camera footage with law enforcement unless required by law" |

## 🚀 Availability

* **Price:** $199.99
* **Requirements:** Requires a SimpliSafe base station and keypad
* **Plan Compatibility:** Works with any monitored SimpliSafe plan; also compatible with self-monitored plans with video recording
* **Active Guard Pricing:** Requires a Pro or Plus SimpliSafe monitoring plan starting at $49.99 a month

Active Guard is an opt-in feature; agents can only view live footage when the system is armed and has been triggered.
