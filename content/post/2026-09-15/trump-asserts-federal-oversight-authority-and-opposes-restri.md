# Trump Asserts Federal Oversight Authority and Opposes Restrictive AI Guardrails

US President Donald Trump stated Monday that his administration holds extensive oversight and regulatory power over artificial intelligence companies, rejecting calls for restrictive guardrails as a conspiracy benefiting Beijing. The president vowed to maintain American dominance in the sector, arguing that domestic debates over slowing AI expansion only satisfy Chinese interests.

## ⚙️ Key details

*   President Trump asserted on his Truth Social platform that the federal government maintains "sweeping oversight authority" and "tremendous criminal and regulatory power" over tech enterprises.
*   The president specifically singled out Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for "pretending to be a 'perfect little angel'" regarding industry safety debates.
*   US Vice President JD Vance characterized corporate lobbying for state restrictions as a potential "Trojan horse," suggesting that frontier AI tech companies are begging for regulation for disingenuous reasons.
*   Trump warned against domestic policies that would "kill the Golden Goose," claiming the U.S. currently leads all other countries by a significant margin.

## 💡 Why it matters

*   **Global Competition:** President Trump affirmed that the US leads global competitors and must maintain dominance because "whoever wins AI, wins!"
*   **Industry Safety Debate:** The administration's stance coincides with a push from industry leaders like Dario Amodei and Sam Altman to moderate the speed of model deployment to ensure safety.
*   **International Friction:** The US administration views restrictive domestic guardrails as a benefit to China, while China has warned that such framing creates "malicious competition" damaging to global governance.
*   **Regulatory Approach:** While the president focuses on executive strength as a guardrail, Vice President Vance stressed that Washington must be thoughtful about risks while maintaining its advantage against China.
