# UN Security Council Briefing Highlights Militants' Use of Artificial Intelligence

During a briefing on September 11, 2026, the Security Council discussed the evolving nature of counter-terrorism threats, specifically noting how militants are leveraging new technologies.

## 💡 Why it matters
Briefers highlighted that militants are increasingly utilizing specific technologies for their operations:

* Artificial intelligence
* Drones
* Encrypted platforms for attack planning and recruitment
