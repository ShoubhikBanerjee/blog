# TU Delft Invites Applications for Fully Funded Sustainable Engineering PhD Positions

Delft University of Technology (TU Delft) in the Netherlands has opened online applications for multiple fully funded PhD positions across various departments. These roles focus on advancing sustainable infrastructure, decarbonization in the steel sector, and climate-resilient building design.

## 🔍 Overview

These research opportunities allow candidates to contribute to projects aimed at reducing environmental impacts and accelerating circularity in the built environment. Eligible candidates are encouraged to apply as soon as possible.

## ⚙️ Key Details

| PhD Position Title | Department / Program | Application Deadline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PhD Position in the Fatigue Performance and Weldability of Next-Generation Structural Steels | Steel & Composites section / Grow with Green Steel | 11 Oct 2026 |
| PhD Position in Multi-Scale Assessment of Circular and Climate-Resilient Building Construction | Faculty of Architecture and the Built Environment / AURORA project | 4 Oct 2026 |

## 🧩 Research Projects and Focus Areas

### Fatigue Performance and Weldability of Next-Generation Structural Steels
This project addresses the steel industry's transition to lower its environmental impact through new production routes, such as hydrogen direct reduced iron (DRI), electric arc furnaces (EAF), and increased scrap use.

* **Research Focus:** Investigate how green production methods and varying scrap levels affect the fatigue behavior and weldability of sustainable structural steels to ensure safe application in infrastructure.
* **Methodology:** Combine experimental testing with advanced numerical modelling to analyze both the base material and welded joints.
* **Design Evaluation:** Determine whether existing fatigue design methods developed for conventional steels remain applicable to next-generation sustainable steels.
* **Collaboration:** Work within the "Grow with Green Steel" (Groeien met Groen Staal) programme alongside a multidisciplinary consortium of universities, research institutes, and industrial partners.

### Multi-Scale Assessment of Circular and Climate-Resilient Building Construction
This position is hosted by the Faculty of Architecture and the Built Environment under the supervision of Assistant Professors Simona Bianchi and Olga Ioannou, and Associate Professor Maria Pregnolato.

* **Project Context:** The role contributes to the Horizon Europe project AURORA (Shaping safe, sustainable, and human-centered places), which aims to accelerate climate resilience and circularity in the built environment.
* **Research Focus:** Map and catalogue innovative building technologies to study how individual products and systems interact in circular, resilient configurations.
* **Digital Workflow:** Assist in developing a digital database of structured data for construction and renovation pathways tailored to different typologies and hazard scenarios.
* **Case Study:** Apply the developed multi-scale assessment framework to a Dutch urban neighborhood case-study.
