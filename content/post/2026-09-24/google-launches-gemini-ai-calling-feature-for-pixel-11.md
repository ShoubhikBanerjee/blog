# Google Launches Gemini AI Calling Feature for Pixel 11

Google is introducing an "early experiment" feature on the Pixel 11 that allows users to delegate phone calls to local businesses to Gemini.

## 🧩 How it works
Users can tell Gemini to call on their behalf using the Gemini app on a Pixel device. The AI handles the following process:

* Introduces itself to the business
* Navigates automated phone menus
* Waits on hold
* Manages the conversation with the person on the other end

Users maintain control through a live transcript view and have the ability to take over the conversation at any point.

## ⚙️ Key details
Gemini can handle various tasks, including:

| Task | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Reservations | Making a reservation with a business |
| Product Inquiries | Checking if a product is in stock |
| Appointments | Rescheduling an existing appointment |

This feature differs from a "Call for me" feature announced last year, which could ask businesses about product availability but did not allow users to take over the calls.

## 🚀 Availability
The feature is rolling out as an "early preview" for users who meet the following criteria:
* Gemini paid subscribers
* Enrolled in the Phone by Google Public Beta
* Using a Pixel 11 in the US
