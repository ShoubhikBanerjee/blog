# Google Releases Gemini 3.8 Flash Text-to-Speech Models

Google has released two new text-to-speech models: gemini-3.8-flash-tts and gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Voice Library**: Over 2,000 voices are available.
* **Customization**: Users can create a custom voice using a 30-second audio sample of their own voice or a voice they have the rights to use.
* **Multi-character Support**: The API allows for the definition of full conversations between multiple characters, each with distinct voices and voice style instructions.

## 🧩 Performance

Using Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, 1m 18s of audio was generated in approximately 20 seconds at a cost of 2.74 cents.
