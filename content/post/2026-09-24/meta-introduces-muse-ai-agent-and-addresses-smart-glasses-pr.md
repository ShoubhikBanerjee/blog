# Meta Introduces Muse AI Agent and Addresses Smart Glasses Privacy

Meta has kicked off its Connect conference, featuring the introduction of the new Muse AI agent and discussions regarding the privacy design of its smart glasses.

## 🔍 Overview
* **Muse AI agent**: A new development that appears to be an early hit at the Connect conference.
* **Meta glasses**: Wearable devices equipped with a camera in the temple and a recording light.

## 💡 Why it matters
CEO Mark Zuckerberg noted that recording with glasses is "meaningfully different" from using a phone, as phones require a user to take the device out of a pocket, open an app, and hold it in a person's face. With glasses, the "big physical announcement" of beginning to record is not present, although a light exists.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Privacy Design**: The glasses were designed from the first version to include a light that turns on during recording.
* **Communication Challenges**: Over time, it became unclear to some what the recording light meant or that it existed. Zuckerberg stated the company now needs to communicate more specifically about the light.
* **Mitigation Efforts**: Meta has started down-ranking videos on Instagram that involve the glasses being used to harass women and retail workers to reduce the incentive to create such content.
