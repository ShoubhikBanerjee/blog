# Google to Launch AI Processor Satellite via Project Suncatcher

Google is launching a satellite equipped with its AI processors on October 1st to test their performance in space. This mission is part of Project Suncatcher, an experimental initiative that could eventually put AI data centers into orbit.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Launch Date:** October 1st
* **Launch Vehicle:** SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket
* **Hardware:** Google's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)
* **Orbit:** Low Earth orbit, where the satellite will harness the power of the sun

## 🔍 Overview

The goal of the mission is to measure how well TPUs "handle the physical stress of spaceflight and the radiation and thermal extremes of space." While Google found during testing that its TPUs can withstand radiation and high levels of g-force, the company notes that "some things can only be tested in space."

## 🧩 How it works

Google is testing a cooling system using radiators and heat pipes while the chips are in orbit. According to Travis Beals, Project Suncatcher’s senior director of product management, the chips can only run for approximately 15 minutes before they must be turned off to cool down.

## 🚀 Availability

Following this initial launch, Google plans to put two satellites into orbit next year.
