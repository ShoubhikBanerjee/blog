# LEGO Framework Combines Legal Expert GraphRAG and Expert Chain-of-Thought

Researchers have introduced LEGO, a dual-module framework designed to improve complex legal reasoning by synergizing Legal Expert GraphRAG and expert Chain-of-thought.

## 🧩 How it works
LEGO utilizes two primary modules to handle legal data:

* **ExpertGraphRAG**: Employs a greedy normative-coverage retrieval algorithm and an expert-annotated civil code graph of normative relations to dynamically extract instance-specific provision subgraphs.
* **ExpertCoT**: Organizes case facts and retrieved provisions into a structured Provision-Fact-Conclusion reasoning format.

## ⚙️ Key details
Using a Qwen3-8B backbone, the framework demonstrated the following performance metrics:

* **Accuracy**: Achieved 40.53% exact-match accuracy on LawExamQA_Civil.
* **Comparative Performance**: Outperformed evaluated RAG and CoT baselines and performed comparably to larger evaluated models.
* **Robustness**: Maintained robustness on multi-hop questions and achieved the best results among evaluated baselines on open-ended benchmarks.
