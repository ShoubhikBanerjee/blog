# Meta unveils Muse Charm standalone AI device

Meta unveiled a standalone hardware device for its Muse AI agent called Muse Charm at Meta Connect.

## 🔍 Overview
- Muse Charm is a dedicated hardware device for Meta’s Muse AI agent.
- It resembles a chunky smartwatch without a strap, featuring a big screen and a lanyard.
- The device does not require a phone to operate.

## 🧩 How it works
- Activates via a fingerprint sensor on the top right corner.
- Includes a small camera and at least three microphone holes on the front.
- Users can speak to the Muse agent without unlocking a phone or opening an app.

## ⚙️ Key details
| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Form factor | Chunky smartwatch‑style screen with lanyard |
| Activation | Fingerprint sensor (top right) |
| Sensors | Small camera; ≥3 microphone holes |
| Connectivity | Standalone, no phone needed |
| Exterior (planned) | Translucent to show internals |
| Aesthetic | Described as an “elevated hacker” look |
| Production status | Only a few units built so far |
| Finishing | Material still being finalized |

## 🚀 Availability
- Meta plans to ship Muse Charm “in time for the holidays in December.”
- The device is expected to ship this year.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enables hands‑free access to Muse AI without a smartphone.
- Provides a dedicated form factor for interacting with the agent’s capabilities (e.g., computer use on Macs, email control, upcoming smart‑glass integration).
- Supports Muse’s growing feature set, such as taking actions on a user’s behalf and forthcoming video‑call avatar interactions.
