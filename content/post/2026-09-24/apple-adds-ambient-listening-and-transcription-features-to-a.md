# Apple Adds Ambient Listening and Transcription Features to Apple Watch

Apple has announced that its new Apple Watches will be capable of continuously listening to every spoken word they detect to create summaries of the surrounding environment. This move signals Apple's entry into a growing trend of ambient consumer surveillance devices.

## 🧩 How it works

Rather than storing audio, the devices attempt to transcribe audible speech and sounds in real time. Apple has introduced two specific features for this purpose:

| Feature | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| Live Rewind | Uses a rolling buffer that only transcribes the last 15 seconds of speech |
| Siri Recap | Provides “high-level summaries” of what was captured throughout the day |

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Speaker Identification:** The features operate without identifying individual speakers.
* **Privacy Safeguards:** An audible chime warns people nearby when the device is transcribing to prevent surreptitious eavesdropping.

## 💡 Why it matters

In 2026, devices that constantly listen to and record surroundings—with or without consent—have become a primary consumer technology trend. Other companies are following similar paths, such as Meta, which provides a tiny, blinking red light on its smart glasses as a privacy safeguard.
