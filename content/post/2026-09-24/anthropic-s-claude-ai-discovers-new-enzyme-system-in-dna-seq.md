# Anthropic's Claude AI Discovers New Enzyme System in DNA Sequences

Anthropic has announced that its AI, Claude, has autonomously discovered a new enzyme system similar to the machinery used in the gene-editing tool Crispr.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Discovery:** A previously uncharacterized enzyme system found in bacteriophages (viruses that infect bacteria).
* **Resources used:** Nearly 1,000 Claude agents processed 210 million tokens over a period of 21 hours.
* **Method:** Claude searched through a massive database of DNA sequences until one agent identified and flagged an unusual repeating pattern for human review.
* **Human involvement:** Anthropic scientists were limited to the initial prompt and the subsequent lab work.

## 💡 Why it matters

This discovery is the first result from Anthropic's newly-launched wet lab and serves as an early test of Claude's usefulness for science as the company prepares to go public. While Anthropic is still working to understand the function of the enzyme system, the company stated it was important to share the findings early to demonstrate Claude's capabilities and provide the community with insight into their work.
