# LeakScale framework measures privacy‑controlled accuracy improvements

LeakScale, a new interventional framework, estimates a previously missing quantity by measuring how private, family‑specific information affects task accuracy.

## 🔍 Overview
- LeakScale is an interventional framework for estimating a missing quantity.
- It operates across 2,048 unique families, two model families, two executable domains, and 262,144 generations.

## 🧩 How it works
- Creates fresh executable tasks that require private, family‑specific information absent from and non‑derivable from the public task.
- Controls access to that private information.
- Estimates the resulting control‑adjusted change in executable accuracy.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Exposure improves accuracy in every model‑by‑domain combination.
- Reported accuracy gains range from +7.17 to +27.31 percentage points.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a systematic way to quantify the impact of private information on model performance.
