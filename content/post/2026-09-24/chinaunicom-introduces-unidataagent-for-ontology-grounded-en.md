# ChinaUnicom Introduces UniDataAgent for Ontology-Grounded Enterprise Report Automation

ChinaUnicom has developed UniDataAgent, an ontology-grounded system designed for reusable question-to-report analysis that separates semantic acquisition from online execution.

## 🧩 How it works

UniDataAgent operates through two primary stages:

| Stage | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ontology Acquisition and Validation (OAV) | Builds versioned enterprise ontologies from metadata, business knowledge, and supporting materials using expert authored business skills, constrained generation, question verification, and selected expert review. |
| Question-to-Report Execution (QRE) | Retrieves semantic contracts for each question, coordinates skills and data tools, validates results, and produces evidence linked reports. |

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Purpose**: Preserves organization specific semantics rather than just translating questions into queries.
* **Performance Metrics**:
    * **Ontology Construction**: Took a few hours for 27 enterprise tables and roughly thousands of metric types, compared to approximately one week manually.
    * **Report Generation**: Took a few minutes, compared to several working days.
    * **Accuracy**: Achieved 95.0% strict accuracy on real business questions, compared to 72.5% for document RAG, particularly on compositional and structured tasks.

## 🚀 Availability

The system has been deployed to generate cost savings and has the potential for replication in other enterprises.
