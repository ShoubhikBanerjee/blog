# VHD-Play Improves Qwen3.6 Agentic Performance

VHD-Play is an update to an AI development that improves agentic capabilities by generating environments and training a model to solve them.

## 🔍 Overview
VHD-Play reverses a dependency by sampling and solving a mathematical model before a corpus-grounded setter renders its decision process as stateful tools. The executable dynamics and trajectory-scoring reference are inherited from the same solved model.

## ⚙️ Key details
*   The pipeline produces 3,300 diverse agentic environments at a cost of a few cents each.
*   Training Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on three families raises its mean agentic score from 0.204 to 0.815 in a five-family diagnostic.
*   Gains appear on held-out instances from all three training families and eight unseen mechanism families.
*   Gains extend beyond the generated substrate to external benchmarks for general function calling, travel planning, and 365-day e-commerce.

## 🧩 How it works
*   A frozen 35B setter realizes larger environments.
*   Scale-matched training retains gains as mechanism size and horizon grow, indicating the potential for an evolving training substrate.
*   The comparison shows that most of the learnable gap lies in stateful interaction rather than underlying problem solving.

## 📊 Benchmarks
*   On E-Commerce Bench, the trained checkpoint completes every run without bankruptcy and exceeds Qwen3.7-Max.
