# NADI 2026 Shared Task Advances Multidialectal Arabic Speech Processing

The seventh edition of the Nuanced Arabic Dialect Identification (NADI) shared task series, NADI 2026, has concluded. This iteration is the second dedicated to multidialectal Arabic speech processing.

## 🔍 Overview
NADI 2026 comprised five tasks and eight subtasks. This edition introduced three new areas to the series: Text-to-Speech (TTS), Spoken Language Translation (SLT), and Spoken Language Understanding (SLU).

| Task | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ASR | Automatic Speech Recognition |
| SDID | Spoken Dialect Identification |
| TTS | Text-to-Speech |
| SLT | Spoken Language Translation |
| SLU | Spoken Language Understanding |

## ⚙️ Key details
To ensure realistic evaluation, the task focused on the following settings:
* Low-bandwidth
* Mixed-dialect
* Code-switched
* Out-of-domain
* Zero-shot

Participation included 21 teams from at least 13 countries, resulting in 14 submitted system-description papers and 48 test-phase submissions.

## 💡 Why it matters
Results from the shared task highlighted the effectiveness of ensemble methods, multimodal dialect identification approaches, and recent Arabic-specialized speech models. However, the findings also indicated that out-of-domain generalization continues to be a major bottleneck.
