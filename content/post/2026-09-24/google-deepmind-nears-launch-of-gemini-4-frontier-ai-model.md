# Google DeepMind Nears Launch of Gemini 4 Frontier AI Model

Google is preparing to launch Gemini 4, a new frontier AI model, following a period of leadership changes and a shift in release strategy.

## ⚙️ Key details

Koray Kavukcuoglu, the new leader of Google’s DeepMind division, has provided the following updates regarding the model:
* **Current Status:** The model is in its refinement stage.
* **Release Goal:** Google aims to launch the model "much earlier" than the end of the year and "long before the end of 2026."
* **Development Approach:** The company intends to release an "early post-training output" as soon as possible and intends to "continue the fast-paced iterations" moving forward.

## 💡 Why it matters

This development follows a period where Google trailed rival developers on flagship releases. Key context includes:

* **Competitive Landscape:** OpenAI's GPT-6 and Anthropic's Mythos series have launched since November 2025, and both outperform Gemini 3.
* **Previous Releases:** Google's last flagship AI model was the Gemini 3 series in November 2025. A planned Gemini 3.5 Pro update for June was never rolled out because the company "took a little bit of a step back" to prioritize faster, less powerful Flash models.
* **Leadership Changes:** Former AI head Demis Hassabis stepped down in August after stating that "great progress" was being made on Gemini 4.

When asked about falling behind rivals, Kavukcuoglu stated, "In my mind, it’s a certainty that we are always gonna be at the frontier."
