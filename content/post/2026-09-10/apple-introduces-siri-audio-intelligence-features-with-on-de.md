# Apple Introduces Siri Audio Intelligence Features with On‑Device Privacy Protections

Apple announced a handful of new Siri AI Audio Intelligence features — Siri Recap, Live Rewind, Sound Recognition, and Music Recognition — at the iPhone Duo launch event, accompanied by a privacy‑focused technical document.

## 🔍 Overview
- **Siri Recap** – summarizes recent interactions.
- **Live Rewind** – lets users double‑tap the Digital Crown to replay recent audio.
- **Sound Recognition** – identifies ambient sounds.
- **Music Recognition** – detects music playing nearby.

## 🧩 How it works
- Audio is captured by the microphone and immediately routed into the **Secure Enclave** of the S11 chip found in Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4.
- The Secure Enclave is a hardware‑isolated compartment that processes sensor data separately from the rest of the system.
- Within the Enclave, audio is analyzed for speech, sounds, or music **without transcribing or storing the raw audio**.
- Processed data lives in a continuously overwritten buffer that never creates an audio recording and **is not saved as a file**.
- If results need to move to an iPhone, they are encrypted and transferred through the Secure Enclaves of both devices.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The raw audio **is not accessible** to the operating system, apps, or Apple.
- Users control when Audio Intelligence features are active; for example, **Live Rewind requires a double‑tap of the Digital Crown each time**.
- Users decide what text to keep from Siri Recap and Live Rewind.
- Kept text syncs with **end‑to‑end encryption** when a device passcode and iCloud two‑factor authentication are enabled.
- Apple states it **cannot access users’ raw audio** from these features.
