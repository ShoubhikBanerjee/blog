# Suno Releases v6 AI Music Model with Record Industry Support

Suno has launched v6, its first AI music model developed with support from the record industry. The model is being rolled out starting today, and Suno intends to eventually retire its previous models.

## 🧩 How it works

According to Suno’s Jack Brody, v6 was “trained from the ground up, with a new set of data that does not include the same data that our previous models were trained on.”

Training data includes:
* Content licensed from partners Believe, BMG, and Warner Music Group
* “User data”

It remains unclear if the training data is completely free of dubiously obtained content.

## ⚙️ Key details

Suno v6 consists of three different models:

| Model | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| v6-mini | Available for free to all; focused on fast, resource-light creation. Results are simpler and often contain more artifacts. |
| v6-wild | Designed for “happy accidents and natural imperfections.” |
| v6 | The standard model. |

### Capabilities and Performance
* **Genre Understanding:** All three models show dramatic improvement in understanding genres, accurately capturing the superficial hallmarks of styles such as "krautrock" or "hyperpop."
* **Multimodal Prompts:** Users are no longer limited to text; v6 can create tracks based on video, images, or other audio.
* **Editing and Mashups:** Users can now use plain language within a chat to edit specific parts of a song, such as a single lyric or guitar line, without regenerating the entire track. The model can also combine multiple elements from a user's Suno library into new creations.

### Limitations
* **Human Imperfections:** The model cannot capture "natural imperfections." Attempts to produce dissonant, off-key, or out-of-tune results—including "out-of-tune piano played haphazardly"—have failed. 
* **Prompt Adherence:** v6 may ignore specific prompts, such as requests for "no drums" or "monotone vocals."
* **AI Artifacts:** Vocals in v6 may produce more harsh-edged artifacts common to AI models than those found in v5.

## 🚀 Availability

Suno v6 starts rolling out today.
