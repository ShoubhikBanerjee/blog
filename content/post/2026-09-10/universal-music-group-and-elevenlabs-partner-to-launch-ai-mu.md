# Universal Music Group and ElevenLabs Partner to Launch AI Music Platform

Universal Music Group (UMG) announced on Thursday the launch of a new AI-powered platform developed through a multiyear licensing agreement with ElevenLabs, a company specializing in AI voice and music generation.

## 🔍 Overview
The platform will allow users to utilize UMG's catalog of licensed music to create:
* Song remixes
* Mashups
* New takes on tracks

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Artist Participation:** Artists can choose whether or not to participate in the platform.
* **Independence:** The platform will remain separate from ElevenLabs’ Music API and ElevenMusic generator.
* **Future Development:** The partnership includes the development of additional products and fan experiences in coming months and years, though specific details were not provided.

## 💡 Why it matters
ElevenLabs CEO Mati Staniszewski stated in the press release, "By combining UMG’s global community and rights management expertise, with our AI models and products, we’ll enable artists and songwriters to create powerful new experiences for their fans, and ensure they are fairly compensated."
