# U.S. Census Bureau Proposes AI Updates to Business Trends and Outlook Survey

The U.S. Census Bureau is seeking comment by October 9, 2026, on revisions to the Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS), which include updates to how the agency collects data on artificial intelligence.

## 🔍 Overview
BTOS is the only biweekly business tendency survey produced by the federal statistical system. It uses ongoing data collection to provide granular, timely, and high-frequency information regarding current trends and economic conditions. The survey targets all nonfarm employer businesses in the United States, Puerto Rico, and the District of Columbia with receipts of $1,000 or more.

## ⚙️ Key details
The Bureau is requesting approval for several changes to the survey structure:

* **AI Supplement:** The Bureau seeks to repeat the AI supplement with minor content changes that have undergone cognitive testing.
* **Core Question Additions:** The Bureau is requesting to add three core questions regarding AI and one new core question regarding business uncertainty (which has also been cognitively tested).
* **Data Frequency:** The Bureau is exploring methods to publish and collect data from the AI supplement more frequently than annually, but less frequently than bi-weekly.

## 🧩 How it works
To reduce respondent burden, the Bureau will implement skip logic, ensuring AI-related questions are only asked of businesses that report using AI.

| Question Focus | Source/Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| Generative AI usage | Moved from AI supplement to core questions |
| Impact of AI on total number of employees | Moved from AI supplement to core questions |
| Impact of AI on new employees | New question (cognitively tested) |
| Business uncertainty | New core question (cognitively tested) |
