# Meta Launches Muse AI Assistant for Productivity and Personal Tasks

Meta has launched Muse, its first foray into AI-powered productivity tools. The company describes the AI agent as a tool designed to "take the busywork off your plate" by assisting with tasks such as emails, trip-planning, and online shopping.

## 🧩 How it works

Muse operates using a cloud-based virtual computer to carry out actions. To function, the agent requires permissions to access third-party accounts; for example, connecting to a Google account allows Muse to access, read, and delete emails.

## ⚙️ Key details

Muse includes a variety of generative and organizational capabilities:

* **Content Generation:** Creates images, videos, AI podcasts, and interactive documents or webpages called “artifacts”.
* **Information Retrieval:** A “Feed” tab that generates news summaries based on text prompts.
* **Organization:** Maintains a running list of “goals” to complete and a list of personalized “ideas” for tool usage.
* **Personalization:** Can provide side-by-side product comparisons and track Pokémon card releases.

| Feature | Capability |
| :--- | :--- |
| Online Shopping | Can pick out and purchase items based on size, style, and color |
| Email Management | Can delete promotional emails and updates |
| Subscriptions | Can find and cancel forgotten subscriptions |

## 💡 Why it matters

Tests of the tool reveal varying results regarding safety and data access. Muse refused to generate images of an “Apple CEO” or specific cartoon characters, yet it successfully generated images and video of a product launch event featuring Apple's logo and device hallucinations. 

Regarding privacy, Meta states it does not share information with advertisers and only exchanges data with third-party apps that is “needed for them to work for you.” However, Muse demonstrated the ability to access API data from connected Instagram and Facebook accounts to surface specific user interests not visible in the app's UI, and it identified a user's current location using a shipping address from an Amazon order.
