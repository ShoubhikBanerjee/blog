# SeaPRwire Launches AI‑Powered Newsroom Platform for Real‑Time PR Targeting

SeaPRwire, Asia’s leading AI‑driven earned media management platform, announced that its smart “AI Newsroom” platform is now online.

## 🔍 Overview
- Smart "AI Newsroom" platform is officially online.
- Provides an intelligent analysis engine for reader interest points.
- Transforms PR from a one‑way distribution channel to an intelligent hub with two‑way perception capabilities.
- Connects clients to over 80,000 journalists and an influencer matrix reaching 300 million followers across APAC markets.

## 🧩 How it works
- When an enterprise distributes news, the AI engine performs deep mining of reader interaction data from massive media websites and social platforms in real time.
- Analyzes click‑through rates, dwell time, forwarding preferences, and comment sentiments.
- Generates dynamic “interest profiles” of audiences across regions and circles.
- Provides real‑time strategic feedback based on these profiles.
- Example: Detecting strong reader response in Southeast Asia to a “green environmental protection” element triggers the system to suggest increased exposure of that angle and automatically adjust news summaries for regional journalists.

## 💡 Why it matters
- "In the past, PR felt more like metaphysics; it was hard to know what readers genuinely wanted to see," said the technical director of SeaPRwire.
- "Now, the AI Newsroom gives us data‑driven X‑ray vision."
- The platform ensures drafts are not only sent out but also seen, understood, and resonated with by the right people, helping enterprises achieve penetrating communication and precise delivery of PR content.

## 🚀 Availability
- The AI Newsroom platform is live and available for enterprises to use immediately.
