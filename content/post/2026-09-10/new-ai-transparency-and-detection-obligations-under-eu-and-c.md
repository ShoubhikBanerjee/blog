# New AI Transparency and Detection Obligations Under EU and California Law

Since 2 August, AI providers and deployers have been subject to new obligations under California’s Artificial Intelligence Transparency Act (CAITA) and Article 50 of the EU AI Act.

## ⚙️ Key details

AI companies are now required to fulfill the following:

* Provide publicly available detection tools that allow people to verify if content was created or edited using their systems.
* Adhere to transparency obligations regarding AI-generated content.

## 🧩 How it works

Different providers are implementing these rules through various methods:

| Provider | Implementation Method |
| :--- | :--- |
| Anthropic | Applying the rule to Claude-generated text via secret-key patterns |

## 💡 Why it matters

These regulations come amid findings regarding AI and disinformation:

* RSF found that most mainstream chatbots will retrieve content from sanctioned Russian state media if asked.
* Investigator Henk van Ess found that chatbots echo users’ false premises back as facts.
* Factiverse audits found LLMs often validate the ideology behind violence after condemning it just long enough to pass safety filters.
