# Researchers release demo of first zero-click WeChat worm

A demo has been released for WeWorm, identified as the first zero-click worm capable of spreading through WeChat calls on both iOS and Android platforms.

## 🧩 How it works

* The worm operates without requiring the victim to answer the call or interact with their device.
* If the victim answers the call, no audio is heard, yet the exploit still succeeds.

## ⚙️ Key details

| Process | Duration |
| :--- | :--- |
| Finding the bug and writing the RCE exploit | 2 days |
| Building the worm | 1 week |

## 💡 Why it matters

* The development team utilized AI to identify the bug and develop the remote code execution (RCE) exploit.
