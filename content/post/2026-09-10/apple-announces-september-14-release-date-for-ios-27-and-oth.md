# Apple Announces September 14 Release Date for iOS 27 and Other Updates

Apple has announced that its next generation of software updates, first unveiled at WWDC26, will be released on Monday, September 14. These updates will be available as free downloads for compatible devices across the Apple ecosystem.

## ⚙️ Key details

The updates introduce major upgrades including design refinements, performance improvements, and updates to various apps. A primary focus of the release is the introduction of new Apple Intelligence and Siri AI features.

| Operating System | Key Features and Improvements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **iOS 27** | Siri AI, Apple Intelligence, Visual Intelligence, expanded customization, and upgrades to Photos, Safari, Messages, Mail, Passwords, Shortcuts, and Home |
| **iPadOS 27** | Apple Intelligence features and iPad-specific productivity improvements |
| **macOS 27 Golden Gate** | Siri AI, Visual Intelligence, Apple Intelligence features, and performance improvements |
| **watchOS 27** | New Siri, fitness, health, and Smart Stack capabilities |

## 🚀 Availability

In addition to the major operating systems, Apple is releasing tvOS 27, visionOS 27, HomePod software 27, and new AirPods firmware on September 14. Following developer betas in June and public betas in July, users can install the updates via the following methods:

* **iPhone and iPad**: Settings > General > Software Update
* **Mac**: System Settings > General > Software Update
* **Apple Watch**: Through the Watch app on a paired iPhone
* **Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro**: Through Settings
