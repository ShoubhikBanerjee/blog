# Analysis of AI Verifiability in Software Engineering and Mathematics

Nilay Patel has discussed the high levels of excitement and hype surrounding AI, specifically focusing on the ability of AI systems to write software and manipulate databases.

## 💡 Why it matters
Patel notes that the current AI hype cycle is closely tied to the capability of AI to write software, a domain where results are verifiable. Key points include:

* **Verifiability in Software**: Software engineering is verifiable because code can be run through a compiler to see if it works.
* **AGI Connections**: Claims from labs regarding the achievement or imminent arrival of AGI are very much connected to AI's ability to write software.
* **Challenges Outside Software**: Once moving outside the domain of software, verifiability becomes significantly harder.
* **Math as a Verifiable Domain**: There is a level of verifiability in math, where burning a large number of tokens can solve some unsolved math problems.
