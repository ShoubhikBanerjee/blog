# OpenAI Announces Solution to Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem Amid Academic Controversy

OpenAI announced Tuesday that an unreleased model solved the Navier-Stokes problem, a legendary Millennium Prize problem concerning the movement of fluids that has stumped human researchers for nearly 90 years. While OpenAI hailed the achievement as a "milestone," the breakthrough has been complicated by allegations of scooping, spying, and violations of academic norms.

## 🧩 How it works
OpenAI reported that its model reached the solution through a concentrated, high-resource effort:
* Utilized a swarm of approximately 10,000 AI agents powered by an internal model.
* Completed the solution in 88 hours.
* Cost the company millions of dollars in a hurried development cycle.

## ⚙️ Key details
The project reportedly began after OpenAI researcher Sébastien Bubeck heard rumors on Twitter that other researchers were making progress on Millennium Prize problems. This led the company to deploy its resources in a last-minute effort to reach a solution first.

| Abhishek Saha | Queen Mary University of London | Stated OpenAI engaged in things mathematicians generally will not do. |

## 💡 Why it matters
The controversy centers on the ethics of AI research and academic collaboration. Tristan Buckmaster claimed that when he contacted OpenAI to inquire about their training data and progress, the conversation turned hostile. He alleged an OpenAI researcher asked, “Why would you ruin your career?” and told him, “If you don’t want me to be nice, then I don’t have to be nice.” 

OpenAI has flatly denied using specific user data to solve the problem, specifically addressing concerns regarding Buckmaster's usage of Codex. While the company stated it cannot rule out that de-identified data helped improve its models, it stressed that its proof differs significantly from Buckmaster’s work. OpenAI has stated it has no intention of claiming the $1 million bounty associated with the problem, which is administered by the Clay Mathematics Institute.
