# Research Solutions Reports Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results and AI Growth

Research Solutions, Inc. (NASDAQ: RSSS) has reported its financial results for the fourth quarter and full fiscal year ended June 30, 2026.

## ⚙️ Key details

**Fiscal Year 2026 Performance**
* Total revenue: $48.3 million
* Total gross profit: $25.1 million (up 3.6%)
* Total gross margin: 51.9%
* Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $22.5 million
* Net income: $2.8 million
* Adjusted EBITDA: $5.8 million (a company record)
* Cash flow from operations: $5.3 million
* Ending cash and cash equivalents: $12.6 million

**Fourth Quarter Performance**
* Total revenue: $12.1 million
* Total gross profit: $6.4 million
* Gross margin percentage: 53%
* Net income: $666,000
* Adjusted EBITDA: $1.4 million
* Cash flow from operations: $1.8 million

## 💡 Why it matters

The company reported a continued Platform mix shift, with Platforms revenue accounting for 43% of total revenue compared to 39% in the prior year. Platform subscription revenue for the fiscal year was $20.8 million, a 9.8% year-over-year increase driven primarily by organic growth in core B2B platforms, including 105 net new B2B platform deployments.

Regarding AI-specific growth:
* B2B ARR reached $16.2 million, including AI-related ARR of $0.8 million.
* AI-related ARR grew 125% sequentially from the third quarter of fiscal 2026.
