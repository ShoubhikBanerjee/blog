# Grokipedia Resumes Article Updates After Months-Long Pause

Grokipedia, the AI‑powered online encyclopedia from SpaceXAI, has started updating articles again after a months‑long pause.

## 🔍 Overview
- Grokipedia is an AI‑powered online encyclopedia built by SpaceXAI.
- After a months‑long pause, the platform is showing new activity.

## ⚙️ Recent Activity
- The live‑updates site now displays recent changes, many labeled “recheck all references and sources.”
- The Barack Obama page shows a note that it was “Fact‑checked by Grok” two days ago.
- The Elon Musk page was fact‑checked within the last hour.
- The Honolulu page was last fact‑checked 7 months ago, indicating uneven coverage.

## 🛠️ User Contributions
- A suggested edit to the Gears of War: E‑Day page adding the October 6 release date was accepted and now appears on the article.
- A request for a new article about Meta’s Muse chatbot is listed as “In Review.”

## 📢 Statements from Leadership
- About a year ago, Elon Musk claimed Grokipedia would be “a massive improvement over Wikipedia.”
- Benji Taylor, head of design at X and SpaceXAI, posted last week: “We haven’t forgotten about Grokipedia” and “v0.2 will be better than ever” (v0.2 launched in November).

## 🚧 Current Limitations
- SpaceXAI did not respond to a request for comment.
- Elon Musk has not posted about Grokipedia on X since February.
