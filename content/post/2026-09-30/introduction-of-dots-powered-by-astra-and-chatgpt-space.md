# Introduction of Dots powered by Astra and ChatGPT Space

A new AI development called "Dots" has been introduced, functioning alongside a new environment called ChatGPT Space.

## 🔍 Overview
Dots are powered by Astra, which Sam described as "our most aligned model," intended to allow users to trust their Dot with "as much responsibility as you are comfortable with.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **ChatGPT Space**: A new environment featuring a Notion-style slash menu.
* **Integration**: Dots can have access to tools including ChatGPT, Slack, and Teams.
* **Capabilities**: Dots can use Codex on a laptop to build apps and launch them in the iPhone Simulator.
* **Specialization**: "Specialist dots" are being developed for sectors such as finance and legal.

## 🚀 Availability
ChatGPT Pro and Enterprise customers have access to Dots today.
