# Google Launches Pilot Program to Pay Publishers for AI Contributions

Google has introduced a pilot program that compensates publishers for their contributions to its AI-powered search features and chatbot, according to reports from Digiday and The Information.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The program began less than a year ago.
- Approximately 100 publishers have joined the pilot.
- Payments are based on how much content contributed to:
    - AI Overviews
    - AI Mode in Search
    - Gemini chatbot

## 💰 Compensation Examples
| Publisher Status | Reported Earnings |
| :--- | :--- |
| Joined at program start | Over $1 million in a year |
| Joined a few months ago | Around $50,000 to $60,000 |

## 💡 Why it matters
Google is facing scrutiny regarding the impact of its AI features on web traffic. This comes alongside regulatory actions:
- In June, the UK ruled that Google must allow publishers to opt out of appearing in AI search features.
- The European Union has opened an investigation into the impact on web traffic and ordered Google to make changes to its search engine.
