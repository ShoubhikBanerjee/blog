# Trump and Tech Executives Sign Joint Commitment On Frontier Responsibilities

President Trump and leaders from several major AI companies have signed a "morally binding" AI safety deal titled the Joint Commitment On Frontier Responsibilities. The accord, shared online by presidential advisor and tech founder David Sacks, establishes a framework for companies to self-regulate their artificial intelligence technology.

## ⚙️ Key Details

The agreement states that companies will meet regularly to establish best practices and standards to improve system safety. It outlines four specific rules to ensure frontier AI models behave as intended:

* **Internal Controls:** Implement robust controls to monitor model alignment and capabilities during training and deployment, specifically regarding chemical threats, biosecurity, cybersecurity, and preventing unintended access to technical systems.
* **Internal Monitoring:** Empower an internal team to ensure detection and monitoring controls are operating correctly and remediate any issues.
* **External Auditing:** Partner with an independent evaluator or external auditor to assess if detection and monitoring controls are functioning as intended.
* **Board Oversight:** Designate an independent committee of the board of directors to oversee reports from auditors and internal teams and ensure remediation of identified issues.

## 💡 Why it matters

The agreement aims to provide the public and users "confidence that the technology is operating as intended" amid mounting safety concerns. While the document notes it "may make sense to codify these steps into laws or regulations" over time, the current rules do not mention penalties for violations. Additionally, the deal follows reports that Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google violated the first rule in recent months when their AIs hacked government websites and other companies.

Alongside the announcement, President Trump ordered the US government to refer to AI as "Super Intelligence," stating that "super is the best word of all" and that "artificial" sounds bad.
