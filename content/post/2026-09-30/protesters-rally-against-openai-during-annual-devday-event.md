# Protesters Rally Against OpenAI During Annual DevDay Event

OpenAI's annual DevDay event in San Francisco's Fort Mason began on Tuesday amid protests, flyers, and chants from a bipartisan movement opposing government regulation gaps and power concentration in the AI industry.

## 🔍 Overview
More than a dozen organizations sponsored a rally outside the venue entrance, including:
* Bay Resistance
* Tech Workers Coalition
* Service Employees International Union
* San Francisco Rising
* San Francisco Labor Council

## ⚙️ Key details
Protesters targeted several specific areas of OpenAI's operations and the broader industry:

* **Government Contracts:** Demonstrators specifically targeted military and ICE contracts, with signs reading "No killer robots for ICE" and "Drop the ICE contract."
* **Environmental Impact:** Concerns were raised regarding data centers, including a flyer suggesting a safe distance from such centers is more than 100 miles away.
* **Industry Power:** Protesters expressed opposition to the concentration of power in the AI industry.

During the event, individuals dressed in cardboard robot costumes waved cardboard scythes, and some protesters addressed OpenAI employees via loudspeaker, encouraging them to leave the company. The mention of Sam Altman was met with booing and one person yelling, "Fascist!"

## 💡 Why it matters
The protest is part of a broader trend where AI labs have faced criticism for "rogue" systems hacking competitors' networks and accessing personal data. Additionally, organizations like QuitGPT have led boycotts, and some individuals have attempted to attack AI CEOs at their homes.
