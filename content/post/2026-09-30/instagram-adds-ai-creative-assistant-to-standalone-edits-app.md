# Instagram adds AI creative assistant to standalone Edits app

Instagram has announced a new AI-powered "creative assistant" within its standalone Edits app designed to provide creators with feedback and suggestions for their posts.

## 🧩 How it works
The AI creative assistant analyzes data and metrics from a user's Instagram account, including:
* Likes
* Views
* Shares
* Retention on videos

Instagram states that the tool can identify patterns over time to provide suggestions that exceed what metrics alone can offer.

## ⚙️ Key details
Creators can use the assistant to ask open-ended questions and receive guidance on several areas:

| Category | AI Assistance Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| Content Analysis | Why one video worked and another didn't, and which content resonated most |
| Trend Tracking | What is trending on Instagram and which audio tracks are trending |
| Creative Guidance | Ideas for content, how to hook viewers, caption suggestions, and video scripting |

## 🚀 Availability
The new feature is free for creators to use up to a certain point. Meta says "power users" have the option to purchase a Meta One subscription for increased usage.

## 💡 Why it matters
This development follows similar moves by YouTube, which recently updated its AI-powered dashboard (initially announced in 2025) to include A/B testing and AI editing to help creators maximize engagement. While both platforms now offer personalized advice on how to navigate the algorithm, tools like CrowdTangle—previously used by journalists and researchers to monitor popular content on Meta platforms—have been restricted or shut down.
