# Fountain 0 Releases AI Generated Film Odysseus: The Fall

AI firm Fountain 0 has released a new film titled *Odysseus: The Fall*, which is described as "the first completely AI generated film produced at the level of a Hollywood blockbuster."

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Production:** Written and directed by company cofounder Ash Koosha, who created all the music and lent his likeness to the title character.
* **Motivation:** Koosha stated he adapted the story because he "always felt passionately about Homer’s epic tale since [he] originally read it years ago."
* **Studio History:** The studio's only previous work was an AI film called *Dreams of Violets* that was featured in Tribeca earlier this year.

## 🚀 Availability

* **Price:** Available to rent for $9.99.
* **Platform:** Viewable via a web browser.

## 🔍 Observations

Because current AI models are only capable of generating scenes lasting a few seconds, the film is described as a disjointed collection of moments stitched together. Reported technical and narrative issues include:

* **Visual Inconsistencies:** The height of the cyclops changes between shots, oars bend and twist, a sword transforms into a dagger, and waves move in the opposite direction of the wind.
* **Animation Errors:** Mouths do not match spoken words (sometimes moving without sound), and one boat in a fleet is seen moving sideways.
* **Audio Issues:** AI-produced voice-overs can be incomprehensible, and the names of Zeus and Odysseus are pronounced incorrectly.
* **Character and Plot:** Characters exhibit baffling acting with unexplained pauses and bursts of laughter. The plot structure is disjointed, with Penelope’s suitors not introduced until the movie is nearly over.
* **Visual Style:** Hyperreal human characters are contrasted with monsters that look like "shabby stop-motion creatures from the 1930s."
* **Anomalies:** One scene features an alien carcass roasting over a spit.
