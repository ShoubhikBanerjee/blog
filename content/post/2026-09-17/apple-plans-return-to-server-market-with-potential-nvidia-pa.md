# Apple Plans Return to Server Market With Potential Nvidia Partnership

Apple is planning to re-enter the server market, potentially partnering with Nvidia to develop the new hardware.

## 🔍 Overview
Apple retired its Xserve line in 2011 and has since largely left enterprise machines to other manufacturers. This new development follows the popularity of the Mac Mini and Mac Studio among AI developers, which has led to shortages.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Hardware:** The servers are rumored to run on either two or four M8 Ultra chips, depending on the model.
* **Integration:** The servers could incorporate Nvidia's NVLink Fusion technology, which allows multiple chips to connect and operate as one.
* **Context:** Apple recently utilized Nvidia chips to power its revamped Siri servers.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Expected Debut:** Likely not until 2029.
* **Chip Timeline:** The M8 line is still a ways out, as Apple only announced the M6 in August.
