# Development of the AI Leadership Battery Behavioral Framework

Researchers have developed the AI Leadership Battery, a measure designed to provide a behavioral framework for examining leadership within AI-enabled work.

## 🧩 How it works
The AI Leadership Battery is structured as follows:
* **Content Families**: 11 theory-specified families
* **Subdimensions**: 36 behaviorally specific subdimensions

## ⚙️ Key details
The development of the Battery followed established scale-development procedures, including:
* Deductive item generation
* Content validation of definitional correspondence and definitional distinctiveness
* Exploratory factor analysis and item reduction
* Confirmatory factor analysis in independent samples
* Tests of internal consistency reliability, convergent validity, discriminant validity, and criterion-related and incremental validity

## 💡 Why it matters
Analyses provided evidence for the Battery's reliability, multidimensional structure, and content, as well as its distinction from selected orbiting constructs. The framework allows researchers to examine how leaders in AI-enabled work regulate:
* Judgment
* Learning
* Adaptation
* Transparency
* Accountability

Additionally, the Battery provided information beyond orbiting constructs regarding the following areas:

| Area | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Organizational Growth | Additional information beyond orbiting constructs |
| Decision Speed | Additional information beyond orbiting constructs |
| Customer/Stakeholder Response | Capability |
| Team Performance | AI-enabled |
| Work Experience | AI-enabled |
| Security and Risk Management | AI |
| Adoption and Integration | AI |
