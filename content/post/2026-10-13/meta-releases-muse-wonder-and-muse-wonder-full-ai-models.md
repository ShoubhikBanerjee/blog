# Meta Releases Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full AI Models

Meta has introduced two new AI models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, as part of its effort to reestablish itself as a major force in the AI industry. Developed by the revamped Superintelligence Labs division, these models follow the previous releases of the Muse Spark model and the Muse AI agent.

## 🔍 Overview
Meta claims these new models can go toe-to-toe with the industry's best. According to the company, internal testing on several industry benchmarks indicates that the models can rival leading offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic. This launch is part of an aggressive push that has seen Meta invest billions into talent and infrastructure.

## ⚙️ Key details
Meta has compared its new models to several specific competitors:

| Model | Developer |
| :--- | :--- |
| GPT-6 Astra | OpenAI |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | Anthropic |
| Claude Fable 5.1 | Anthropic |

It is currently unclear how these models stack up against Google’s Gemini 4 Argon, as those capabilities are still coming into focus.

## 🧩 How it works
* **Muse Agent Integration**: Meta eventually plans to power its Muse AI agent with the Wonder model.
* **Specialized Models**: The company intends to develop specialized models to bring to everyone using Muse.
* **Infrastructure**: The models are supported by Meta's significant investment in AI infrastructure and the expansion of its Muse lineup, which includes the image model Muse Image.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Muse Code subscribers**: Both Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full are available starting today.
* **Meta Model API**: Muse Wonder is also accessible through this API.
* **Future Timeline**: Meta has not yet shared specifics or a timeline for when the Muse agent will be fully powered by the new models for all users.

## 💡 Why it matters
Meta is making up for lost time in the AI race by steadily expanding its lineup. The company is heavily focused on bringing useful AI agents to the masses through Muse and continues to release models intended to compete with the industry's highest-performing systems.
