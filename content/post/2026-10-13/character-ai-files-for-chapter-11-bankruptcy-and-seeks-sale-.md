# Character.AI files for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and seeks sale of assets to Disney

Character.AI, an early generative‑AI chatbot startup, announced on Monday that it has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in the United States and is pursuing a sale of its assets to Disney.

## 🔍 Overview
- The company filed for bankruptcy to facilitate the sale of the business and its assets.
- Disney is positioned as the lead bidder, offering $20 million for certain undisclosed assets and a non‑exclusive license to unspecified intellectual property.
- The sale includes transition support using former Character.AI employees hired by Disney.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Estimated liabilities are listed between **$50 million and $100 million**.
- Disney would also receive a non‑exclusive license to the company’s intellectual property.
- The company expects the sale process to close **late November or early December**.
- Character.AI will keep its services, including paid subscriptions, running while the sale proceeds.
- The platform will be **wound down** if a suitable bid does not emerge.
- The company emphasized that “maintaining a safe and engaging experience for our community remains a top priority.”

## 👥 Leadership Change
- Disney hired former Character.AI CEO **Karandeep Anand** as its chief technology officer.
- Anand’s tenure as CEO lasted just over a year, during which he oversaw the move into microdramas.
- Disney also expects “a number of Character.AI’s technical team” to join the company.

## 📚 Legal & Safety Challenges
- Character.AI has faced mounting scrutiny and lawsuits over the safety of its chatbots.
- Disney previously sent cease‑and‑desist letters, claiming Character.AI was damaging its brand and exploiting Disney intellectual property by allowing unauthorized chatbots of Disney characters.

## 🔄 Acquisition Strategy
- The deal resembles an **“acquihire”** approach, where the buyer primarily seeks talent.
- Character.AI previously survived a similar talent‑acquisition when **Google spent over $2 billion** in 2024 to hire much of the company’s team, including co‑founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas.

## 📅 Timeline & Next Steps
- Bankruptcy filing and press release were published on Monday (date not specified).
- Sale process expected to complete **late November or early December**.
- Services will continue operating, with paid subscriptions remaining active, unless no suitable bid is received.
- Disney’s involvement includes payment, licensing, and transition support using former staff.
