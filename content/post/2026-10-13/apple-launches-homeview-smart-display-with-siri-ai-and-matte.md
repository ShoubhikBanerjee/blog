# Apple launches HomeView smart display with Siri AI and Matter hub

Apple’s long‑rumored smart display, the Apple HomeView, is now available.

## 📦 Overview
- Small, vibrant 7‑inch square screen atop a speaker base.
- Functions as a video‑call device (FaceTime with Center Stage), photo frame, and quick‑info hub with Siri AI.

## 🖌️ Design & Display
- Square shape makes it feel less imposing than larger smart displays.
- Liquid Retina display with True Tone, ambient‑light adjustment, and brightness ranging from 0.3 nits to 500 nits.
- Color options: silver, starlight, rose pink, and space gray.
- No physical volume or power buttons; 12‑megapixel FaceTime camera used only for calls and facial recognition.
- Adjustable stainless‑steel arm attaches the screen to a surprisingly small aluminum speaker base.
- Audio hardware: two speaker drivers in the display, plus a woofer and two force‑canceling passive radiators in the base.

## 🎤 Siri AI & Interaction
- Built‑in Siri AI (same version that arrived on iPhone with iOS 27) enables natural‑language voice queries, smart‑home control, and personal‑context answers from messages, calendar, and emails.
- Personal context requires facial recognition and an Apple Intelligence‑capable iPhone on the same network, preventing others from reading your texts.

## 🏠 Smart Home Hub
- Acts as a Matter controller and Thread Border Router.
- Can orchestrate Apple Home and Matter devices, including Apple’s new cameras, thermostats, and door lock.
- Home widget face offers controls for lights, climate, cameras, and other devices.
- Displays live feeds from HomeKit Secure Video cameras.

## 🎧 Audio
- Sound tuned a few notches above the HomePod Mini but does not compete with the HomePod.
- Two drivers in the display plus a woofer and two passive radiators deliver richer bass.

## 📱 Ecosystem & Apps
- Supports Apple apps: Calendar, Reminders, Messages, and Music.
- Works with third‑party audio/video apps that use the CarPlay architecture (e.g., Spotify, Audible are available; no video apps yet).

## 📦 Availability & Pricing
- Price: $299.
- Launch includes a new collection of cameras, thermostats, and a door lock designed for Apple Home.
- Pre‑orders are open; devices ship on November 13.
- Available at launch in eight countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, and India.
- A wall‑mountable version (no speaker base) also costs $299 and offers three mounting options: plug‑in, in‑wall power, and in‑wall PoE.
