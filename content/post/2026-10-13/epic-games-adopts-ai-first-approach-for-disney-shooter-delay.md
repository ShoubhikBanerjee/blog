# Epic Games adopts AI‑first approach for Disney shooter, delaying release

Epic Games announced that its unannounced Disney shooter will be built under an “AI‑first” mandate, pushing developers to use AI tools such as Anthropic’s Claude for both code and art. The project has been moved out of its original March release window.

## 🔍 Overview
- **Project**: an unannounced shooter featuring characters from Marvel and Star Wars.
- **Collaboration**: part of a multiyear partnership between Epic Games and Disney, which invested $1.5 billion in Epic in 2024.
- **Shift**: developers have been given an “AI‑first” mandate to use tools like Anthropic’s Claude.

## ⚙️ AI‑first mandate
- Developers were told to use AI tools such as Anthropic’s Claude.
- One of the game’s directors said to staff, “No more hand‑written code.”
- Staff were also told that all of the game’s art will be “generated with full AI workflows.”
- Epic spokesperson Jake Jones said the company is “embracing AI‑accelerated development.”

## 📅 Timeline & status
- Original target: March release window.
- Current status: delayed beyond the March window (no new date provided).

## 💡 Why it matters
- Shows large‑scale use of generative AI for both programming and visual content in a AAA title.
- Epic already uses AI in Fortnite, which has previously featured Disney characters like an AI Darth Vader.
- AI game‑development tools are also emerging from other large companies, such as Meta and Google.
- Epic CEO Tim Sweeney has pushed for Steam to drop its “made with AI” tag, arguing that “AI will be involved in nearly all future production.”
- Earlier this year, Epic laid off over 1,000 employees following a downturn in Fortnite engagement since 2025.
