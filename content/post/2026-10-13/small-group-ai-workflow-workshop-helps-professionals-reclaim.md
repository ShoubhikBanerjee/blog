# Small-Group AI Workflow Workshop Helps Professionals Reclaim Up to 5 Hours Weekly

A new hands‑on co‑working session lets participants turn one repetitive task from their real work into an AI‑powered workflow, promising measurable time savings each week.

## 📌 Overview
- "Get 5 Hours Back!"
- The session focuses on a single repetitive task such as emails, research, reports, content, data, meetings, documents, customer questions, or admin.
- It is designed for people who "probably already use AI" but are still doing most of the work manually.

## 🛠️ How the Session Works
- **Small group, hands‑on, your tools ✌️** – max 4–5 people per session.
- No lecture, no 50‑slide deck, no generic prompt‑engineering course.
- Steps:
  1. Identify the parts AI can handle.
  2. Design a better workflow.
  3. Build the workflow using modern AI tools (Claude Code, OpenClaw, and whatever fits your workflow).
  4. Test the workflow to verify it actually saves you time.
- Outcome: participants leave with one working system and a clear idea of what to automate next.

## 🎁 What Participants Receive
- ✅ Small co‑working session where you automate one real task from YOUR actual job.
- ✅ Personalized guidance adapting to your tools, workflow, and goals.
- ✅ Hands‑on experience with Claude Code, OpenClaw, and other suitable tools.
- ✅ Measurable before/after results: "hours back, every week" (e.g., 5 hours 30 minutes).
- ✅ Free 1:1 follow‑up hour after the workshop to check progress or debug issues.
- ✅ Open to anyone with repetitive work, technical or not.

## 👥 Who Facilitates
- The facilitator has founded 20+ businesses, including:
  - Explorer Elite – adventure marketplace for solo guides and small outdoor businesses.
  - 52 Waypoint – free incubator for serious founders who want to build without funding.
  - 111 Network – open source, decentralized, offline‑first communication.
  - UnitEd – NGO, free global teacher‑training platform.
- Quote from the facilitator: "I believe the more we let AI take care of the robotic aspects of our lives, the more time we have for the human parts of it."

## 📍 Location & Logistics
- Location may change due to capacity; participants will be informed in advance.

## 💭 Why It Matters
- Automating even one repetitive task can return up to five hours each week, freeing time for higher‑value, human‑focused work.
