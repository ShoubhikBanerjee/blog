# Google Releases SynthID Detector; Microsoft Teams to Add Deepfake Detection

Google has made its SynthID Detector available to the public, and Microsoft announced that Teams will support third‑party deep‑fake detection beginning in November.

## 🔍 Overview
- Google opened SynthID Detector to the public.
- Microsoft Teams will support third‑party deep‑fake detection, expected in November.

## ⚙️ Key details
- SynthID Detector is a tool that identifies synthetic media; it is now publicly accessible.
- Teams integration will allow users to leverage external deep‑fake detection services.

## 📅 Availability
- Google’s SynthID Detector: currently public.
- Microsoft Teams deep‑fake detection: slated for November.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides organizations and individuals with means to verify authenticity of audio/video content.
- Addresses growing concerns about AI‑generated deepfakes used in misinformation and legal contexts.
