# Intel Begins Customer Sampling of GPU d-Series for Agentic AI

Intel accelerates open infrastructure for the agentic AI era; begins customer sampling of GPU d-series.

## 🚀 Overview
Intel is accelerating open infrastructure for the agentic AI era and has begun customer sampling of the GPU d-series.

## ⚙️ Key Details
*   **Sampling Phase:** Sampling is the early stage of a qualification cycle that in this industry typically runs many quarters.
*   **Strategy:** Open-infrastructure positioning is a deliberate contrast with the incumbent's proprietary stack, a strategy challengers in this market have pursued before with mixed results.

## 📊 Market Context
The peer-set read-through matters here, since the dominant AI accelerator vendor's share position and the custom-silicon programs at the large cloud buyers define the bar Intel's d-series must clear, and design-win commentary from hyperscalers has historically carried more signal than the vendor's own framing.

Product and sampling announcements from Intel have historically drawn a muted tape response relative to peers in the accelerator space, reflecting a track record in which announced data-centre silicon roadmaps have repeatedly slipped between sampling and volume revenue.

## 🔍 Future Outlook
The follow-ons are management commentary on the next earnings call, any named-customer confirmations, and foundry versus product segmentation in the reporting.
