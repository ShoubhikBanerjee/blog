# Alibaba Announces Qwen 4 27B Tier but No Public Release Yet

Alibaba announced the Qwen 4 27B tier at the Apsara Conference on 22 September 2026, but the model remains in training and no download, API, or benchmark information is available.

## 🔍 Overview
- Qwen 4 27B is a named member of Alibaba's next‑generation Qwen 4 family.
- The name was shown on stage at the Apsara Conference in Hangzhou on 22 September 2026.
- Alibaba’s written release on that day states only that “Qwen 4 is currently in training”.
- No model card, repository, API identifier, licence, context window, parameter count, or release date exists for Qwen 4 27B.
- The only Qwen models that can be run today are the Qwen 3.8 series.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Four tiers were presented on stage by the Qwen LLM lead: Qwen 4 Max, Qwen 4 Flash, Qwen 4 Plus, and Qwen 4 27B.
- None of these tiers appear in Alibaba’s written release, and no specifications were attached to any of them on stage.
- The 27B tier is notable because previous 27B‑parameter models (Qwen 3.5‑27B, Qwen 3.6‑27B, Qwen 3.8‑27B) have been released with open weights, so the announcement creates expectations of downloadable weights.
- Alibaba’s press release also mentions future Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 series projected to scale to 5–10 trillion parameters, but no parameter count is given for Qwen 4 itself or the 27B tier.
- The most concrete external information about the upcoming architecture is the Qwen 3.8‑Flash‑Next preview, released on 24 August 2026, which is described as “an experimental preview of the architecture that will underpin Qwen 4”. It lists its own components (Hybrid attention with QSA, Gated residual, N‑gram embedding, a tailored training recipe) and its sizes (125 billion main parameters, 6 billion activated per step, 51 billion‑parameter n‑gram embedding, 4 billion‑parameter head, 262,144‑token native context extensible to 1,000,000), but does not confirm that Qwen 4 27B inherits any of these details.

## 🚀 Availability
- As of 13 October 2026, no Qwen 4 models, including the 27B tier, have shipped.
- The only downloadable Qwen models are the Qwen 3.8 series and earlier 27B variants (Qwen 3.5‑27B, Qwen 3.6‑27B, Qwen 3.8‑27B).
- The Qwen 4 27B tier cannot be downloaded, called, or benchmarked at this time.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The 27B size has a history of being released with open weights, so the announcement of a Qwen 4 27B tier creates expectations that may not be met until the model is actually released.
- The gap between the announced name and the lack of concrete artifacts (weights, model card, specifications) explains the purpose of the reference page that tracks what is known and unknown about the tier.
- Understanding that the Qwen 3.8‑Flash‑Next preview is merely a preview of the architecture—not a specification of Qwen 4 27B—helps set realistic expectations for developers and researchers watching Alibaba’s roadmap.
