# AWS Hands‑On Workshop Showcases Agentic AI‑Driven Development with Kiro

AWS hosted an in‑person **Hands‑On Workshop: Agentic AI‑Driven Development with Kiro**.

## 🔍 Overview
- The workshop focused on the **AI‑Driven Development Life Cycle (AIDLC)** using **Kiro**, described as *AWS's revolutionary agentic IDE*.
- Target audience included **developers** working on **web development**.
- The session was conducted in **English** and featured **Aditya Srivastava | Solution Architect** as the presenter.
- Two skill levels were referenced: **100 – Foundational** and **200 – Intermediate**.

## 📅 Agenda
- **Introduction to AIDLC** – Overview of AI‑Driven Development methodology and Kiro capabilities
- **Setup** – Install Kiro locally and link with an AWS account
- **Live Demo** – Hands‑on demonstration of the AIDLC workflow
- **Practical Exercise** – Build a prototype using Kiro with guided practice
- **Q&A / Networking** – Open discussion and networking

## 🎓 What You’ll Learn
- Spec‑driven development
- Automated code generation
- AWS service integration
- Techniques to reduce development time significantly

## 🛠️ Prerequisites & Registration
- **Bring your laptop** for interactive exercises.
- Complete registration through the event link.
- After registration you will receive an email with next‑step instructions.
- Upon confirmation, a **one‑time password (OTP)** for building access will be sent one day prior to the event.
- Personal data will only be shared with event organizers and not disclosed to third parties.
- AWS reserves the right to modify or cancel the event if necessary.
- By registering, you agree to the **AWS Event Terms & Conditions** and **AWS Code of Conduct**.

## 👥 Who Should Attend
- Developers interested in moving from AI‑assisted to truly AI‑driven development.
- Individuals at the **Foundational (100)** or **Intermediate (200)** skill levels.

## 📍 Logistics
- The workshop was an **in‑person** event.
- Participants were asked to bring laptops for the hands‑on components.
