# ABU Hosts Hands-On AI Agents Training for Staffing Professionals

The Association of Temporary Employment Agencies (ABU) is organizing a hands-on training on October 12, 2026, focusing on AI Agents. This workshop aims to move beyond basic text generation and summarization, teaching participants how to automate complete processes and connect various systems and tools.

## 🔍 Overview

AI agents allow for the automation of complete processes and the delegation of tasks. The training goes deeper than using tools like ChatGPT or Copilot, focusing on converting recurring work into smart, automated workflows. Examples include agents that process incoming emails, structure information, and prepare draft responses, as well as processes where different systems collaborate.

| Attribute | Details |

## 🧩 What You Will Learn

*   What AI agents are and what is currently possible
*   Which processes within your organization are suitable for automation
*   How to work with no-code automation tools
*   Step-by-step construction of your own AI workflow or agent
*   Connecting diverse tools and data sources
*   Safety, reliability, and human control
*   Practical examples and use cases relevant to the staffing industry

## 🛠️ Requirements

*   **Experience:** The training is intended for people with some experience in AI who wish to deepen their knowledge.
*   **Technical:** Programming knowledge is not necessary, but basic digital skills and enthusiasm to experiment are required.
*   **Tools:** A paid subscription to ChatGPT or Claude is required. Participants should bring their own laptop to apply what they learn directly.

## 📝 Registration

*   **Action:** Register easily via the button.
*   **Data:** By registering, you agree to share your name, job title, email address, and employer with the trainers.
*   **Cancellation:** If you are unable to attend, please cancel at least two weeks (28 September) in advance or arrange for a colleague to attend via ledenservice@abu.nl.
*   **Access:** A paid subscription to ChatGPT or Claude is required to participate.
