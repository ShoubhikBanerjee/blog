# Meta launches Wonder and Wonder Full models to challenge ChatGPT and Claude

Meta unveiled two new artificial intelligence models, called **Wonder** and **Wonder Full**, on Monday, which the company claims perform at a level comparable to OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude.

## 🔍 Overview
- The models are described by Meta as the "largest and most capable" in its portfolio.  
- CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced on X: "Muse Spark was our first family of models, and now Muse Wonder is a much larger and more advanced class of models that brings us to the frontier."
- Chief AI officer Alexandr Wang added on X: "Wonder is our largest and most capable model yet and rivals the big (boys)."

## ⚙️ Model Details
| Model | Notable Claim |
|-------|---------------|
| **Wonder** | Performs at a level comparable to ChatGPT and Claude; touted as "our largest and most capable model yet" |
| **Wonder Full** | Part of the same family, released alongside Wonder |

## 📊 Benchmarking & Cost
- Meta cited benchmarking data from the independent firm **Artificial Analysis**, which *appears to show* that Wonder outperforms Meta’s earlier Spark models and also outperforms models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google.
- Artificial Analysis’s website lists Wonder as **among the most expensive** models.

## 🚀 Availability
- The new models will soon be integrated into Meta’s personal‑agent app **Muse**, which will be offered **only to paid subscribers**.
- Muse is part of a broader suite that includes:
  - **Muse Code** (launched August) – a coding assistant competing with similar tools from OpenAI and Anthropic.
  - **Muse** (agentic app launched September) – performs tasks such as coding, research, shopping, and reservations.
  - **Muse Charm** – a handheld AI hardware product announced at Connect, letting users interact with their Muse agent without a smartphone.

## 🛡️ Safety & Investment
- Meta delayed shipping Muse for several months to focus on safety and security, as Zuckerberg wrote on X: "We didn't call for everyone else to do this before we would."
- The company has pledged **billions** to compete in the global AI race, with a projection in July that AI‑related spending could reach **$145 billion** this year.
- In June 2025, Meta invested **$14 billion** in Scale AI and appointed co‑founder Alexandr Wang as chief AI officer of the newly created **Superintelligence Labs**.
- Zuckerberg joined six other tech executives in signing a commitment, described by former President Donald Trump as a "morally binding" pledge to build safeguards around advanced AI models.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Meta’s push signals a direct challenge to established AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) by targeting the same high‑performance segment.
- The integration of Wonder into a paid‑subscriber agent app illustrates Meta’s strategy to diversify revenue beyond advertising.
- Safety‑first messaging and large financial commitments highlight the tension between rapid AI advancement and responsible development within the industry.
