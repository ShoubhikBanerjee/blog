# Toned-Down DLSS 5 To Debut In Games Soon

Nvidia’s toned-down DLSS 5 is poised for upcoming game debuts, alongside key performance and model updates.

## 🔍 Key DLSS 5 Revisions
- Toned down from its earlier-this-year showcase, with face and environmental changes as subtle as those in NBA 2K27
- Teenagers no longer look like middle-aged men
- October update shrinks the model footprint by 24 percent to roughly 118 million parameters (per Nvidia’s Andrew Burnes)
- October update reduces 4K VRAM usage by 20 percent (per Nvidia’s Andrew Burnes)
- October update makes DLSS 5 twice as fast on RTX 50-series GPUs and upcoming RTX Spark laptops

## 🚀 Availability Details
- Toned-down DLSS 5 debuts in Hogwarts Legacy on November 5th
- Toned-down DLSS 5 comes to Where Winds Meet late next month
- The new DLSS 5 model from the October update debuts first in NBA 2K27 on October 16th
- The new DLSS 5 model ships by default with future DLSS 5 games
- Nvidia maintains developers have “detailed artistic control” over DLSS 5 implementation

## ⚙️ Supplementary Nvidia App Update
- New Nvidia app beta update includes a faster performance mode for Frame Generation on RTX 40- and 50-series GPUs
- Updated AI model uses a “faster and lighter algorithm to boost frame rates” for Frame Gen, Multi Frame Gen, and Dynamic Multi Frame Gen
- Nvidia states the performance mode has “minor trade‑offs in image quality”
- Nvidia states the performance mode for Frame Generation is up to 35 percent faster
