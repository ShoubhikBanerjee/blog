# Nothing launches Nothing OS 5.0 with AI‑enhanced customization

British company Nothing has officially unveiled Nothing OS 5.0, the latest mobile customization layer, bringing more than 80 new features, design updates, performance tweaks, and several AI‑powered functions.

## 🔍 Overview
- Built on Android 17.
- Keeps a recognizable interface that differs from the more cluttered skins of other manufacturers.
- Offers a new home‑screen alternative and an adaptive color system that extends wallpaper tones across the screen.
- Widgets and the search bar use transparency for added depth, a treatment that also applies to apps such as Gallery.

## ⚙️ Key Features
- **AI‑driven personalization** – Built‑in intelligence learns which apps you use most, keeping them ready for quick access.
- **Essential Voice** – Faster, better‑structured transcriptions; can distinguish lists from paragraphs, shows real‑time spoken words, and can capture audio from the Ear (3) charging case via SuperMic.
- **Smart Collections** – Artificial intelligence recommends content and helps organize memories into collections.
- **Essential Apps Builder** – Standalone app that lets you describe an app, generates it with AI, and adds it to the home screen without any coding.
- **Glyph Interface app** – Dedicated app to manage Glyph functions, sounds, ringtones, and schedules; includes a Glyph Timer widget for countdowns displayed on the back‑panel.
- **Collector** – Create visual collections from camera captures or Gallery selections.
- **Away Time** – Tracks screen‑free time and shows weekly evolution.
- **Multi‑item home‑screen editing** – Select and move multiple apps and widgets simultaneously.
- **System font change** – Switches to Geist with new text sizes and adjustments to corners, blurs, and icons.
- **Gooey style** – More striking and fluid visual language.
- **Micrographics style** – Shows date, time, weather, and upcoming meetings; allows clock color to match wallpaper.
- **Redesigned status bar, Settings, Camera, and Gallery apps** – Gallery now includes new sorting options and a revamped album view.
- **Improved touch response, background app management, and system resource usage** – Prioritises the foreground app and reduces interruptions when memory is under heavy load; nightly maintenance optimises memory.
- **Bluetooth controls in Quick Settings** – Redesigned for easier device discovery and connection.
- **Camera QR‑code handling** – Keeps detected QR codes on screen for one‑tap access.

## 🚀 Availability
- Open Beta (called Open Beta) began on 26 August 2026 for the Nothing Phone (3).
- The update will roll out in phases to the rest of the product line; specific release dates for each model are listed in the rollout schedule included in the update media kit.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Nothing OS 5.0 expands on‑device AI capabilities, adding personalization, voice transcription, and app‑creation tools that let users shape their phone experience without external development resources. The combination of AI‑driven features with a deep visual overhaul aims to deliver a more fluid, responsive, and customizable user experience.
