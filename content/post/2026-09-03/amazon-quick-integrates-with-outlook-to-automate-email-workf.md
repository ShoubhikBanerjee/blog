# Amazon Quick integrates with Outlook to automate email workflows

AI is reshaping the workday by quietly removing work and delivering meaningful time savings. Email is often where that shift shows up first, and as organizations grow, inboxes can consume valuable hours parsing threads and rebuilding context. Amazon Quick, a generative AI assistant that connects to business data, now integrates with Microsoft Outlook to automate repetitive email tasks and surface relevant information.

## 🔍 Overview
- Effective AI implementations can remove work, leading to meaningful time savings.
- Email is a primary area where productivity gains appear.
- Amazon Quick integrates with Outlook to summarize long threads, draft replies with context, schedule meetings, and trigger downstream workflows.

## 🛠️ How it works
1. In the Amazon Quick navigation menu, choose **Connect apps and data**, then **Connectors**.
2. Search for **Outlook** and select **Connect**.
3. A sign‑in page appears; sign in with your Outlook email.
4. After signing in, choose your Amazon Quick account to authorize access.
5. Once authorized, the connector displays a **Signed in, Ready** status.

> The integration uses the Microsoft Graph API and OAuth 2.0 to securely access Outlook on your behalf without storing your password.

## ⚙️ Features
| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Quick chat agents** | Draft contextual email responses and summarize lengthy threads without leaving the inbox; access organization knowledge bases, product documentation, and internal wikis. |
| **Quick Flows** | Build AI‑powered workflows that automate routine email tasks such as automatically generated meeting briefings, action‑item extraction from threads, and routing customer inquiries to the right teams. |
| **Quick Automate** | Orchestrate complex, multi‑step processes spanning email, calendars, and other enterprise systems (e.g., customer onboarding, procurement approvals, cross‑department coordination). |

## 📈 Why it matters
- A Gartner study reports AI saves sellers nearly **five hours a week**.
- Teams can reclaim productive time by automating email handling and surfacing relevant information when needed.
- Executives and assistants benefit from quick summarization of long threads and identification of critical action items.
- Sales representatives can query chat agents directly from Outlook for pricing, competitive positioning, and customer history.
- Meeting preparation time can be reduced with automated briefings.

## 🔐 Technical requirements
- Administrative access to the Microsoft Entra admin center (formerly Azure AD).
- Application Developer permissions (or higher) in your Microsoft tenant.
- Access to Amazon Quick in your AWS account.
- OAuth 2.0 authentication to grant specific permissions such as reading email or managing calendars.
- Microsoft Graph API connectivity between Outlook and Amazon Quick.
