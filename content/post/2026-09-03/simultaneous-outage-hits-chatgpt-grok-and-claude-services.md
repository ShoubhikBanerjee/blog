# Simultaneous outage hits ChatGPT, Grok, and Claude services

At around 11 AM ET, users of three of the biggest AI chatbots began seeing errors, marking a coordinated‑time disruption across OpenAI’s ChatGPT, xAI’s Grok, and Anthropic’s Claude.

## 🔧 Overview
- OpenAI’s ChatGPT started returning error messages and its status page displayed “elevated errors across ChatGPT and Codex.”
- xAI’s Grok experienced an outage on Android, iOS, and the web at 9:30 AM ET, showing the message “This model is overloaded right now. Please try again shortly or pick a different model.”
- Anthropic’s Claude services (Claude, Claude Code, and the Claude API) showed problems beginning around 10:30 AM ET.
- The three chatbots went down at the same time, affecting many users.

## ⏰ Timeline
- **9:30 AM ET** – Grok outage begins across all platforms.
- **10:30 AM ET** – Anthropic reports partial outage affecting several model versions.
- **11:00 AM ET** – ChatGPT error messages appear for users; status page notes elevated errors.

## 🛠️ Affected Services
- **ChatGPT** – login failures, file uploads, voice mode, search, deep research, image generation, and other functionalities are degraded.
- **Grok** – both mobile apps and web interface are unavailable; users see an overload notice.
- **Claude** – Claude, Claude Code, and the Claude API experience partial outages; specific model versions are impacted (see table).

## 📣 Company Responses
- **OpenAI** – says it has “applied a mitigation” and is “monitoring recovery,” while acknowledging “degraded performance.”
- **Anthropic** – technical staff member CJ Avilla stated an “infrastructure issue” caused a partial outage and added, “We’re working on it but don’t have an ETA yet.”
- **xAI** – a notice on the Grok website says the team is “working on restoring service as quickly as possible.”

## 🔍 Open Questions
- It’s not clear what has gone wrong, or if the issues are somehow related.
- The Verge’s requests for comment to OpenAI, xAI, and Anthropic have not been answered yet.
