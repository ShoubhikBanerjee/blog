# Google launches WeatherNext 3 AI weather model

Google has introduced WeatherNext 3, an updated AI weather model designed to improve forecast accuracy, particularly for rain and snowfall, by leveraging real-time satellite observations.

## 🔍 Overview
- WeatherNext 3 is Google’s latest AI weather model, succeeding WeatherNext 2.
- It produces forecasts with higher spatial and temporal resolution, described as five times sharper than its predecessor.
- The model is now integrated into Google Search, Maps, and Gemini.

## 🧩 How it works
- WeatherNext 3 makes predictions based on real-time satellite observations, updating forecasts hourly.
- It ingests live 1-hour geostationary satellite mosaics alongside historical data.
- The model uses a Functional Generative Network (FGN) mesh transformer to output dense gridded fields, cyclone tracks, and station-level predictions.
- Unlike traditional physics-based models, it avoids the time-lag associated with solving complex atmospheric equations.
- It trains directly on sparse weather station observation data and high-quality precipitation sources, including NASA’s IMERG and Google’s own satellite radar-based reanalysis.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Resolution**: Forecasts temperature and moisture at up to 5-kilometer resolution, other surface variables at 10 kilometers, and atmospheric variables (e.g., wind speed) at 25 kilometers.
- **Update frequency**: Produces a new forecast every hour, compared to WeatherNext 2’s 6-hour intervals.
- **Accuracy**: Precipitation forecasts are up to 50% more accurate for predictions at least one day in advance.
- **Coverage**: Improves forecasts in regions with fewer ground-based rain gauges, such as parts of Latin America, Africa, and Asia-Pacific.
- **Energy applications**: Forecasts wind speed at 100 meters (turbine height) and cloud cover/sun radiation levels to support renewable energy planning.

## 🚀 Availability
- WeatherNext 3 is now incorporated into Google Search, Maps, and Gemini.
- Google has collaborated with the US National Hurricane Center and agencies in Asia to enhance forecasting using its AI models.

## 💡 Why it matters
- AI weather models like WeatherNext 3 provide faster and more localized predictions than traditional methods, which rely on supercomputers and have inherent time-lags.
- The model’s hourly updates and high resolution help predict fast-moving weather systems, such as rain and snow, more effectively.
- It addresses gaps in forecasting for underserved regions, where traditional models are cost-prohibitive.
- The model supports renewable energy planning by providing precise wind and solar generation forecasts.
