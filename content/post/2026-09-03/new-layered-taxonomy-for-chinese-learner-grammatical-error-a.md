# New Layered Taxonomy for Chinese Learner Grammatical Error Annotation

A new research paper introduces a layered taxonomy for annotating grammatical errors in Chinese learner writing, aiming to bridge computational Chinese grammatical error correction (CGEC) with pedagogical error analysis.

## 🔍 Overview
The paper proposes a structured scheme to label grammatical errors in Chinese learner writing with consistency and linguistic meaning.

## 🧩 How it works
- The scheme first identifies character- and punctuation-level orthographic errors, labeling them by edit operation and subtype.
- Other errors receive a three-layer core label combining:
  - Edit operation
  - Linguistic domain
  - Part of speech
- Optional Chinese-specific extensions include aspect, modality, comparison, argument structure, and complements.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The taxonomy draws on CGEC resources, learner-error taxonomies, and Mandarin grammar.
- It was evaluated through:
  - A coverage analysis of automatically extracted MuCGEC edits
  - A preliminary consistency study involving five large language models applying the taxonomy to a sample.
- Results support the layered approach but highlight category boundaries requiring further refinement.

## 🚀 Availability
