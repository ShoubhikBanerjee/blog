# Google launches AI‑powered voice assistant modes for Gmail, Docs, and Keep

Google is rolling out AI‑powered voice assistant modes for Gmail, Docs, and Keep that let users manage the apps by talking to them.

## 🔍 Overview
Google’s new real‑time conversational features—Gmail Live, Docs Live, and Keep Live—extend the Gemini Live experience to its core productivity apps. The tools are designed to make it easier to note down or find specific information when you’re on the move or unable to interact manually.

## 🧩 How it works
- The assistant listens to natural‑language voice commands and responds verbally while showing an on‑screen transcript.
- Users can interrupt the conversation mid‑sentence or ask follow‑up questions without starting a new session.
- If permission is granted, the assistant can pull data from Gmail, Drive, Chat, and the web to personalize its output.

## ⚙️ Key details
| Feature | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Gmail Live** | Surfaces details from your inbox without digging through threads; fetches information, summarizes it verbally, and links the source emails in the transcript. |
| **Docs Live** | Formats ideas into structured documents based on conversational descriptions; can summarize longer documents or turn them into business proposals; can pull information from Gmail, Drive, Chat, and the web when allowed. |
| **Keep Live** | Transcribes natural speech into notes; contextualizes entries without needing specific keywords; can add items like “ingredients for pesto” to a shopping list. |

## 🚀 Availability
- Previewed at Google I/O in May, the features are now rolling out globally in English on mobile platforms.
- **Gmail Live**: Available on iOS and Android for users on Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra plans.
- **Docs Live**: Coming to AI Pro and Ultra plans; available on both iOS and Android.
- **Keep Live**: Coming to AI Pro and Ultra plans; Android only.
- All three are "coming soon" for Workspace business customers.

## 💡 Why it matters
The voice‑first interface provides a hands‑free way to fetch information, summarize content, and transcribe notes, helping users stay productive when manual interaction isn’t practical.
