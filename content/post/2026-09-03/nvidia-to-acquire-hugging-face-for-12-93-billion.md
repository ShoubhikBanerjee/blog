# Nvidia to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93 billion

Nvidia has agreed to purchase Hugging Face, the popular open-source AI platform, for $12.93 billion.

## 🔍 Overview
- Hugging Face is an online platform founded in 2016 that hosts open-source AI models, datasets, and tools.
- It is often described as the "GitHub" for AI due to its browsable library and community collaboration features.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The acquisition price is $12.93 billion, nearly triple Hugging Face’s last valuation of $4.5 billion in 2023.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated: "Together, we will scale Hugging Face’s platform, strengthen its infrastructure and expand access to AI for developers and institutions worldwide."
- Hugging Face will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem.
- Developers will retain the choice of models, frameworks, clouds, inference service providers, and computing platforms.
- Nvidia compute will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face.

## 📅 Timeline
- Speculation began on August 23rd after reports that Hugging Face was exploring a $13 billion sale.
- By August 27th, reports indicated Nvidia was in acquisition talks with Hugging Face, with earlier claims of a $12.9 billion deal already agreed upon.
- Nvidia has now publicly declared its intent to purchase Hugging Face.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Nvidia gains a strategic foothold to help preserve its AI hardware dominance as closed-source AI providers develop their own chips.
- Hugging Face previously rejected a $500 million investment offer from Nvidia in 2023, which would have valued it at $7 billion.
- Hugging Face generates around $150 million in annualized revenue, a fraction of its purchase price.
