# HKEX launches new carbon marketplace for sustainable finance

HKEX has introduced a new carbon marketplace, adding another pillar to its Sustainable & Green Exchange offering.

## 📈 Overview
- The new carbon marketplace is announced by HKEX as part of its sustainable‑finance initiatives.
- It joins other recent HKEX product launches such as 39 MSCI equity index futures and options contracts.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Purpose**: Provide a dedicated platform for carbon‑related trading and clearing.
- **Collaboration**: Developed under the HKEX Sustainable & Green Exchange framework, which works with a broad set of stakeholders on sustainable finance.
- **Integration**: Available alongside HKEX’s existing product suite, including equity, ETFs, warrants, SPACs and debt securities.

## 🚀 Availability
- The marketplace is now live for eligible market participants.
- Details on subscription, trading rules and data services can be accessed through HKEX’s market data services and official announcements.
- Market participants can also consult HKEX’s centralised education platform and e‑Learning resources for guidance on using the new carbon marketplace.
