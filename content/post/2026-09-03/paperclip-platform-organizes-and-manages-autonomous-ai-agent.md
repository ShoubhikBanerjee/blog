# Paperclip platform organizes and manages autonomous AI agent workflows

Paperclip is a new platform designed to manage AI agents for business operations, providing a centralized system to organize tasks, goals, budgets, and agent templates instead of managing separate automations.

## 🧩 How it works
Paperclip allows users to manage business goals by organizing various AI agents—such as those for development, content, marketing, QA, and research—under a single organizational structure. Users can define a company mission, set budgets, and monitor progress via a central dashboard. Agents are hired if they can receive a heartbeat, and they function within an organizational chart that supports automatic delegation of tasks.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Agent Management:** Supports diverse roles including CEO, CTO, engineers, designers, and marketers across any LLM provider.
- **Operational Workflow:** Every task is recorded as a ticket with a clear owner, status, and thread. Instructions, responses, tool calls, and decisions are captured with full tracing.
- **Transparency:** The system maintains an append-only history of all activity with no edits or deletions permitted.
- **Context and Integration:** Agents use SKILL.md to discover necessary context. The platform is designed for extensibility through drop-in extensions.
- **Financial Tracking:** Budgets can be set per agent, task, project, or goal to track costs.

## 💡 Why it matters
Instead of wiring individual automations from scratch, Paperclip provides a structured environment where agents wake up on a schedule, check their work, and respond to ticket assignments. This approach allows users to manage high-level business goals rather than individual pull requests.
