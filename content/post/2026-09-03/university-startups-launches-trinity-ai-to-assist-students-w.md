# University Startups Launches Trinity AI to Assist Students with Disability Transition Planning

Trinity is a conversational AI solution developed by University Startups to assist students with disabilities in taking ownership of their postsecondary and Individualized Education Program (IEP) planning.

## 🧩 How it works
University Startups utilized g/d/n/a to build a fully serverless architecture on AWS. The system utilizes a multi-agent solution powered by Amazon Bedrock and Claude 3.5 Sonnet to provide targeted guidance. 

The architecture employs a hierarchical six-agent system:

| Agent | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| Orchestrator Agent | Reads student conversation, selects the right path, sequences agents, and consolidates final output |
| College Agent | Recommends 4-year universities and community colleges from an over 30,000 record Knowledge Base |
| Employment Agent | Matches jobs and career pathways from an over 1,000 occupation Knowledge Base |
| Training Agent | Recommends vocational programs and certifications |

## ⚙️ Key details
The platform includes accessibility features such as Amazon Polly for text-to-speech and Amazon Transcribe for speech-to-text. Data security is maintained through compliance with HIPAA and FERPA standards, while Amazon Bedrock Guardrails are used for content filtering and hallucination reduction.

## 🚀 Availability
Trinity reached educators and students across more than a dozen U.S. states within its first year. International expansion into Saudi Arabia and Kuwait is now underway.
