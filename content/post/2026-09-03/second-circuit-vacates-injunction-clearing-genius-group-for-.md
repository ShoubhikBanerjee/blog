# Second Circuit Vacates Injunction, Clearing Genius Group for $1.2 B Capital Plan

The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit has vacated the preliminary injunction that blocked Genius Group Limited from issuing shares, raising capital, and purchasing Bitcoin, removing the legal barrier to the company’s board‑approved $1.2 billion capital plan and dual treasury strategy.

## 🔍 Overview
- Genius Group (NYSE American: GNS) is a global education group delivering AI‑powered education and acceleration solutions for the future of work.
- The company serves over 6 million users in more than 100 countries through its Genius City model and online digital marketplace.
- It operates a dual treasury strategy that includes a Bitcoin Treasury and an AI (AGI Infinity) Treasury alongside its core education business.

## ⚖️ Legal Victory
- The Second Circuit vacated the preliminary injunction issued by the Southern District of New York on March 13, 2025.
- The injunction, which had restricted the company from issuing shares, raising capital, and purchasing Bitcoin, is now terminated and the matter remanded for status‑quo proceedings.
- The decision ends an 18‑month legal battle in which Genius Group argued the injunction was based on inaccurate, untrue statements intended to obstruct its corporate strategy.
- The vacatur follows a favorable ICC arbitration award issued on April 16, 2026.

## 💰 Financial Impact
| Item                               | Amount (USD) |
|------------------------------------|--------------|
| Shares returned to Genius Group    | 7,387,374 shares |
| Monetary damages awarded           | $6,595,180.00 |
| Legal fees and expenses awarded   | $1,375,988.53 |

- The total monetary award (damages plus fees) equals **$7,971,168.53**.
- The company plans to retire 30.1 million shares, of which the 7.4 million returned are part of that retirement.
- The board‑approved $1.2 billion capital plan will fund the Bitcoin Treasury and the AGI Infinity Portfolio, targeting $2 billion in total assets by FY2031.
- Net proceeds from any legal wins will be allocated **50 % to shareholder distributions** and **50 % to purchase Bitcoin** for the Bitcoin Treasury.

## 🛠️ Treasury Strategy
- The dual treasury strategy is intended to complement Genius Group’s education business.
- Bitcoin purchases are a core component of the treasury, subject to forward‑looking statements about price and volatility risks.
- The AI Treasury (AGI Infinity Portfolio) is positioned to support the company’s AI‑focused initiatives.

## 📈 Next Steps
- Genius Group is petitioning the district court to confirm the ICC arbitration award under Section 9 of the Federal Arbitration Act, seeking immediate return of the 7,387,374 shares and collection of the $7,971,168.53 monetary award.
- A RICO case against LZG International officers and insiders continues in the Southern District of Florida, seeking treble damages of over $750 million.
- The company remains subject to forward‑looking statements and associated risks, including Bitcoin price volatility, capital availability, and regulatory developments.
