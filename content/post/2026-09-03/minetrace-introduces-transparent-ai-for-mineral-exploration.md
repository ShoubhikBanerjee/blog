# MineTRACE Introduces Transparent AI for Mineral Exploration

Researchers have introduced MineTRACE, a web-based system that uses AI to provide evidence-grounded mineral prospectivity analysis for eight commodities: Cu, Au, Ni, W, Sn, Co, Ta, and Mn.

## 🔍 Overview
MineTRACE addresses the challenge of integrating heterogeneous geochemical, geophysical, and geological evidence in mineral exploration, where existing prospectivity systems often provide only opaque scores or heatmaps.

## 🧩 How it works
- Users can explore prospectivity maps.
- Users can query locations or regions.
- Users can inspect supporting evidence.
- Users can interact through natural language.
- A transparent expert tree, informed by geological knowledge and known deposits, combines multi-source evidence into interpretable prospectivity scores.
- For a new location, the conversational assistant retrieves the score and supporting evidence from the analysis pipeline and presents them in natural language.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The scorer achieves spatial AUC values of up to 0.917 across different test scenarios.
- End-to-end evaluation assesses query accuracy and response grounding.

## 💡 Why it matters
MineTRACE makes public geoscience data easier to access, interpret, and verify, supporting more efficient and transparent mineral exploration.
