# Google Releases Gemini 3.8 Flash with Enhanced Reasoning and Specialized Cyber Version

On September 2, 2026, Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash, arriving just a few weeks after the release of its predecessor. The new model is designed to "work harder" by performing more reasoning steps on complex tasks and utilizing iterative tool calling. While it maintains the same introductory per-token pricing as Gemini 3.7 Flash, the model may incur higher total costs due to increased token usage at higher effort levels.

## 🔍 Overview

Gemini 3.8 Flash introduces significant improvements for software engineering and autonomous AI agents. The model is available with low, medium, and high thinking levels, allowing users to adjust performance based on task complexity. Early impressions from Artificial Analysis describe the model as the cheapest measured at its level of intelligence, despite an approximate 40% increase in total task cost driven by a 30% increase in output tokens and more turns on agentic evaluations.

## ⚙️ Key Details

*   **Pricing:** $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens.
*   **Coding Performance:** Aigora.ai CEO John Ennis compared its performance to Anthropic’s "Opus 5 coding quality" at a fraction of the cost, noting its speed for tasks like making remotion videos.
*   **Capabilities:** The model demonstrates proficiency in HTML and JavaScript, such as adding sandboxed iframe support for HTML blocks in markdown rendering tools.
*   **Safeguards:** Includes protections against misuse in cyber offense and Chemical, Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear (CBRN) domains.

## 🛡️ Fairwind Program and Cyber Variant

Google also released Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a specialized version available exclusively to governments and "trusted partners" through the new Fairwind Program. This program includes 650 members, such as CrowdStrike and the Center for Internet Security. Participants gain access to:

*   **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber:** A model restricted to "trusted defenders."
*   **CodeMender Agent:** An autonomous tool designed to find and fix vulnerabilities to protect critical infrastructure and national security.

## 🚀 Availability

Gemini 3.8 Flash is available now for consumers with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription, as well as developers and enterprise users. Developers who wish to minimize token usage may continue to use Gemini 3.7 Flash.
