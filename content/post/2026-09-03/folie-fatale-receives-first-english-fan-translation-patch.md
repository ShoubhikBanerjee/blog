# Folie Fatale receives first English fan translation patch

## 🔍 Overview
Folie Fatale, a visual novel originally released on Steam in May 2024, has received its first English fan‑translation patch on July 7 2026.

## 🗓️ Release timeline
- **May 30 2024** – Original Steam launch (paid version by STUDIO SPUTNIK).
- **July 7 2026** – English fan‑translation patch released (EN patch).

## ⚙️ How to apply the English patch
1. Purchase Folie Fatale on Steam.
2. Download the fan‑translation patch file.
3. Apply the patch via the local files folder of the Steam installation.

## 📋 Key details
- **Developer:** Kanmidoko (神海処)
- **Publisher:** 346lab Localization
- **Content warnings:** Self‑harm, suicide, violence, murder, drug abuse, sexual violence, animal cruelty (no graphics), incest, yandere character, non‑con, dub‑con, human trafficking, body horror.
- **Steam version:** Paid version, brought by STUDIO SPUTNIK, the original creator of *Guardian of the Sacred Seal*, featuring a more extensive storyline.

## 💡 Why it matters
The fan‑translation makes Folie Fatale accessible to English‑speaking players, allowing a broader audience to experience its complex narrative and extensive content warnings. This community‑driven effort expands the reach of a niche visual novel without altering the original paid Steam release.
