# Prompt-engineering framework enables personalized responses for AI teaching assistants

A new study presents a prompt-engineering-based framework designed to personalize general-purpose LLM/RAG-based AI teaching assistants, such as Jill Watson, across various academic courses and disciplines.

## 🧩 How it works
The framework adapts AI responses by utilizing six specific dimensions related to the learner:

* Self-assessment
* Abstraction preference
* Verbosity preference
* Perceptual orientation
* Information processing style
* Level of understanding

These dimensions allow for the creation of 96 distinct learner profiles. Additionally, student queries are analyzed using Bloom's Taxonomy to estimate the cognitive complexity of each interaction. This data is encoded into structured prompts that condition the LLM, enabling adaptive behavior without the need for model retraining.

## ⚙️ Key details
The framework was evaluated through both NLP metrics and a human study involving five participants. Results indicate:

* Perceived differences in the structure and style of responses across personalization conditions.
* Statistical evidence identifying specific learner attributes that correlate with measurable changes in AI output.

## 💡 Why it matters
These findings provide preliminary evidence that prompt-based personalization can effectively support adaptive behavior in LLM-powered educational agents, allowing for tailored interactions within an academic context.
