# Solidcore Resources Upgraded to B Rating and Retains Prime ESG Status

Solidcore Resources plc announced that its ISS STOXX ESG Corporate Rating has been upgraded from **B‑** to **B**, while the company continues to hold “Prime” status, positioning it among the industry leaders worldwide.

## 🔍 Overview
- ISS STOXX ESG Corporate Rating upgraded to **B** (from B‑).
- “Prime” status retained, which exceeds the sector‑specific ESG threshold for mining.
- Ranking in the top decile of the Mining & Integrated Production industry.
- Performance Score of **59** with a “Very High” transparency level (as of 28 August 2026).

## 📈 ESG Rating Details
- ISS STOXX ESG Corporate Rating uses a twelve‑point scale from **A+** (excellent) to **D‑** (poor).
- Assessment is based on roughly **100 industry‑specific indicators** selected for materiality from a pool of more than **700 indicators**.
- “Prime” status is granted to companies whose ESG performance meets or exceeds a sector‑specific threshold; for mining, the highest Prime threshold applies.

## 🏆 Rankings & Scores
- Top decile of the Mining & Integrated Production industry.
- Overall Performance Score: **59**.
- Transparency level: **“Very High.”**
- S&P Corporate Sustainability Assessment score: **63**, placing Solidcore in the top 10 % of mining companies worldwide.
- CDP disclosure grades: **“B”** for Water Security, **“B”** for Supplier Engagement, **“C”** for Climate Change.

## 📣 Company Statement
> “This upgrade reflects the sustained efforts of our senior management and teams across all our operations to embed responsible business practices throughout the Company. Retaining ‘Prime’ status well above the sector threshold is an important independent validation of our sustainability management systems and our commitment to transparency towards investors and other stakeholders.” – **Michael Vasilev, Head of Sustainability Reporting, Solidcore Resources**

## 🏢 About Solidcore Resources
- Leading gold producer registered in the **AIFC, Kazakhstan**.
- Listed on the **Astana International Exchange**.
- Operates two producing gold mines and a major growth project (**Ertis POX**) in Kazakhstan.

## 🏢 About ISS STOXX
- Leading global provider of research, analytics, and technology solutions for institutional investors and companies.
- Covers corporate governance, sustainability, cyber risk, and fund intelligence, as well as market indices under the STOXX and DAX brands.
- Majority‑owned by **Deutsche Börse Group** and serves clients worldwide.
