# Aqara launches AI‑driven smart lighting series and Builder platform at IFA 2026

Aqara, a global leader in IoT, announced a new lineup of smart lighting products and a community platform for smart‑space creators at IFA 2026 in Berlin. The launch underscores the company's vision of AI‑driven spatial intelligence – environments that understand users and adapt automatically.

## 🔎 Overview
- New indoor and outdoor lighting series integrates with Aqara’s existing sensors, switches, and hubs.
- Introduces **Aqara Builder**, a platform for creators to share ideas and discuss smart‑space concepts.
- Demonstrated at IFA 2026, Booth #157, Hall 1.2, Berlin.

## 🛠️ Product lineup
| Product | Primary use | Notable specs |
|--------|------------|---------------|
| Floor Lamp T1 | Indoor | 1,420 mm tall, side‑emitting design, 18 controllable zones, 16‑bit 5‑channel chip, 2,000 K–9,000 K, up to 1,000 lm |
| LED Downlight T2 | Indoor (low‑ceiling) | Ultra‑thin profile, RGB CCT version offers 2,000 K–9,000 K and 16 M colors, CCT version up to 1,200 lm, power‑off memory |
| LED Strip T2 | Indoor (flexible) | 108 LEDs/m, 16‑bit 5‑channel chip, 18 zones every 3 m, supports smooth color gradients and music‑synced effects |
| Outdoor String Lights H1 | Outdoor patio/deck | Individually controllable zones, 2,000 K–9,000 K range, weather‑proof, AI‑powered Gen‑Scene for natural‑language scene creation |
| Permanent Outdoor Lights H1 | Outdoor (permanent) | One‑time installation, one‑tap presets, independently controlled LEDs |

## ⚙️ Technical details
- **Connectivity**: All products support Thread, Zigbee, and are Matter‑enabled.
- **Platform compatibility**: Seamlessly work with Apple Home, Home Assistant, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, SmartThings, and Homey.
- **AI integration**: Lights respond to presence, routines, and automations via Aqara sensors and the Aqara Home app. Users can describe a desired mood in natural language to generate coordinated scenes.
- **Gen‑Scene**: AI‑powered feature that builds full scenes across zones from a simple description (highlighted for Outdoor String Lights H1).

## 🏡 Real‑world demo at IFA
- Booth showcased a fully constructed living space where lighting interacts synchronously with motion sensors, smart switches, and central hubs.
- Visitors could walk through scenarios demonstrating lights automatically adjusting as someone enters a room, setting mood based on daily routines, or reacting instantly to environmental triggers without manual input.

## 📅 Availability
- The new lighting series and Aqara Builder platform were presented at IFA 2026 (Booth #157, Hall 1.2, Berlin). Further rollout details were not provided in the announcement.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Extends smart, connected illumination to more parts of the home, both indoors and outdoors.
- Shows true spatial intelligence: lighting adapts automatically rather than relying on manual control.
- Provides a unified ecosystem where developers and hobbyists can collaborate through the Aqara Builder community platform.
