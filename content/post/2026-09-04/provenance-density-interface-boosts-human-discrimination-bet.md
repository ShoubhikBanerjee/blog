# Provenance Density Interface Boosts Human Discrimination Between Truth and Fabrication

Researchers introduced Provenance Density, an evidence‑visualization interface that displays how densely verified claims appear within a text.

## 🔍 Overview
- Provenance Density is an evidence‑visualization interface that shows the density of verified claims in a text.

## 🧩 How it works
- The interface visualizes where verified claims are concentrated throughout a document, giving readers a sense of claim density.

## 📊 Key details
- **User study**: In a study with 81 participants, an idealized Provenance Density interface produced a large discernment gap between truth and fabrication (+4.15 points, d=1.82). Participants who received no signal showed no detectable discrimination.
- **Technical audit**: An audit of 200 samples revealed that retrieval density alone is insufficient; the Consistency Veto carries most of the discriminative signal on dynamic queries.

| Signal | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| Retrieval density | Insufficient alone |
| Consistency Veto | Carries most discriminative signal on dynamic queries |
