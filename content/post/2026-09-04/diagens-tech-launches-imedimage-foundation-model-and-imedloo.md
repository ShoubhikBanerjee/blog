# Diagens Tech launches iMedImage foundation model and iMedLoop platform

Diagens Technology Co., Ltd. announced the launch of the world’s first foundational medical imaging model iMedImage® and the end‑to‑end platform iMedLoop™, creating a commercial closed‑loop that links data, model development, product, clinical practice and data again.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI for Science (AI4S) is reshaping the paradigm of life‑science exploration.
- In medical imaging, AI4S has evolved beyond image‑reading assistance to build generalized underlying intelligence infrastructure for clinical and scientific research.
- Diagens Tech does not engage in pharmaceutical R&D, but builds fundamental AI research infrastructure to empower life‑science imaging analysis.
- The iMedImage® model and iMedLoop™ platform enable hospitals and research institutions to share AI production capabilities through projects and co‑development.

## 🛠️ How it works
- A commercial closed‑loop featuring **data → model development → product → clinical practice → data** underpins Diagens Tech’s one‑of‑a‑kind medical imaging AI Model Factory.
- Standardization is the first step to convert raw imaging data into high‑quality data assets.
- iMedStudio™ uses intelligent annotation and expert quality‑control mechanisms to deliver multi‑layered data refinement through AI precise segmentation, intelligent arbitration and manual expert review, converting raw images into standardized training samples.
- The end‑to‑end data governance pipeline, built over nearly a decade of hospital collaboration, eliminates format and annotation inconsistencies while preserving cohort and device diversities.

## 📊 Key details
- **Data assets**: ~28.95 million annotated samples as of end‑June 2026, generated by a professional team of >3,000 specialized annotators.
- **Model scale**: iMedImage® has 104 billion parameters, trained on >80 million medical images covering 19 mainstream imaging modalities.
- **Specialty‑specific models**: deployment time compressed to 2–3 months; 158 specialty‑specific model projects delivered through cooperation with nearly 100 hospitals nationwide.
- **Financials (first half 2026)**:
  - Model‑service revenue: RMB 94.541 million (year‑on‑year increase 101.1 %, 86.9 % of total revenue).
  - R&D expenditure: RMB 64.12 million (up 67.4 % YoY).
- **Economic advantage**: The foundational investment is a one‑time massive cost; iterative development of unlimited specialty‑specific models requires no repeated high‑cost input, and parallel deployment lets all online models benefit from each foundational model iteration.

## 🚀 Deployment & Impact
- Specialty‑specific models no longer require full‑cycle training from scratch; they are rapidly deployed via targeted fine‑tuning with specialty‑specific data.
- The high‑diversity data assets refined from complex real‑world clinical scenarios form the cornerstone of robust cross‑scenario generalization capabilities.
- Value accumulates across the entire pipeline, transforming medical AI model development from craftsmanship to industrial manufacturing.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Medical imaging data is inherently heterogeneous, a challenge amplified by China’s diversified clinical settings, varied equipment brands, vast territory and large population, which create unique patient cohorts and disease spectrums.
- Diagens Tech’s proprietary foundational infrastructure, built on self‑accumulated clinical data assets, cannot be purchased or rapidly replicated, granting a multi‑year technological lead in foundational model development.
- The closed‑loop and standardized framework enable sustainable commercial resilience and in‑depth scientific research.
