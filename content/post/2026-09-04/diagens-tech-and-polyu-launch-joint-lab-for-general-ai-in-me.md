# Diagens Tech and PolyU Launch Joint Lab for General AI in Medical Applications

On 2 September, Diagens Technology Co., Ltd. (02526.HK, Diagens‑B, “Diagens Tech”) and Hong Kong Polytechnic University (“PolyU”) jointly unveiled the PolyU – DIAGENS Joint Laboratory for Artificial General Intelligence and Medical Applications on PolyU’s campus.

## 🔬 Overview
- The plaque of the Joint Lab for General AI and Medical Applications was unveiled on PolyU’s campus, marking an accelerated boost for AI‑healthcare integration.
- The unveiling ceremony was officiated by Professor CHAO Yu Hang, PolyU’s Senior Vice President (Research and Innovation), and Dr. SONG Ning, Founder and Chairman of the Board of Diagens Tech.
- Attendees included Professor CHEN Changwen, Dean of PolyU’s Faculty of Computer and Mathematical Sciences; Dr. LI Yongqi, Project Lead of the Joint Lab; and representatives from PolyU’s Research and Innovation Office, PolyU – Hangzhou Technology and Innovation Research Institute, and Diagens Tech.

## 🎯 Priorities
- Medical image analysis
- Medical foundational models
- Automation and AI empowerment of R&D workflows

## 🌐 Strategic Context
- Globally, AI technologies are evolving at a rapid pace, penetrating sectors at an accelerating rate.
- The integration of AI and healthcare has attracted widespread attention from all stakeholders.
- In the 2025 ShanghaiRanking’s Global Ranking of Academic Subjects, PolyU’s AI discipline secured the No.1 spot in Hong Kong and 16th globally. AI was included in this global ranking for the first time, and PolyU claimed the top position locally.

## 🤝 Partnership Benefits
- Combining PolyU’s research strengths and Diagens Tech’s industrial capabilities is expected to galvanize advances in medical AI and further improve the quality and efficiency of healthcare services in Hong Kong, across China and worldwide.
- The Joint Lab bridges academia and industry to explore new productivity paradigms for medical AI and usher in the next era of medical AI for Science.
- It delivers win‑win empowerment by integrating PolyU’s capacity for original innovation and Diagens Tech’s industrial‑scale delivery capabilities.

## 🛠️ Diagens Tech Capabilities
| Product | Function |
|---|---|
| iMedImage® | World’s first and only foundational medical imaging model |
| iMedStudio™ | Intelligent image annotation platform |
| iMedMaaS® | Dedicated model training and delivery platform creating an end‑to‑end value chain covering data generation, model development and deployment optimization |

## 📈 Current Achievements
- As of H1 2026, Diagens Tech has collaborated with 99 hospitals to train 158 vertical models spanning 43 human organs and 61 disease areas, validating the technical pathway for batch model training enabled by reuse of foundational capabilities.
- Diagens Tech has achieved a major technological leap in medical AI, moving from one model per disease to industrialized mass production.

## 📚 Why It Matters
- The establishment of the Joint Lab represents concrete actions by PolyU and Diagens Tech to respond to national strategic needs, seize technological opportunities and fulfil the social responsibilities of universities and enterprises.
- Professor Chao stated that China is pressing ahead with the Healthy China initiative.
- Dr. Song commented that AI for Science (AI4S) is reshaping the global medical AI landscape, presenting challenges and opportunities comparable to the Apollo Program in helping humans decode life and health, and advance diagnosis, prevention and prediction of complex diseases.
- The partnership marks a key milestone in Diagens Tech’s long‑term AI4S strategy and will accelerate the development of the medical AI industry beyond large model development into the next phase of AI4S, enabling systematic research and scientific validation for more critical research topics sourced directly from clinical practice.
