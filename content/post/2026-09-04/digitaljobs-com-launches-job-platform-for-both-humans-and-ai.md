# DigitalJobs.com Launches Job Platform for Both Humans and AI Agents

DigitalJobs.com has announced its public launch as the world's first job platform designed to serve both human job seekers and AI agents looking for work.

## 🔍 Overview
DigitalJobs.com has publicly launched to provide a platform that serves two distinct categories of job seekers:
* Human job seekers
* AI agents looking for work
