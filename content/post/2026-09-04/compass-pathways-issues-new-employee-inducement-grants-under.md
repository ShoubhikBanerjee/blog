# Compass Pathways Issues New Employee Inducement Grants Under Nasdaq Rule 5635(c)(4)

Compass Pathways plc announced that it granted equity awards to thirty‑one newly hired non‑executive employees as a material inducement under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(c)(4).  The awards were granted on September 1, 2026.

## 🔍 Overview
- **Company**: Compass Pathways plc (Nasdaq: CMPS), a biotechnology company focused on mental‑health treatments.
- **Grant date**: September 1, 2026.
- **Purpose**: Material inducement to employment, approved under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(c)(4).

## 📄 Grant Details
- **Recipients**: 31 newly hired non‑executive employees.
- **Award types**:
  - Options to purchase an aggregate of **280,600 shares**.
  - Restricted share units or nominal‑cost options covering an aggregate of **133,500 shares** for employees in the United Kingdom.
- **Exercise price**: $13.24 per share, equal to the closing price of the Company’s American Depositary Shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on the grant date.

| Award Type                              | Shares Covered |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------|
| Options                                 | 280,600        |
| Restricted Share Units / Nominal‑cost options (UK) | 133,500        |

## ⏳ Vesting Schedule
- **Options**: 25 % vest on the first anniversary of the grant date; the remaining 75 % vest in equal monthly installments over the following three years, subject to continued employment.
- **Restricted Share Units / Nominal‑cost options**: Vest in four equal annual installments, subject to continued employment.

## ✅ Nasdaq Compliance
- The equity awards were approved by the **Compensation and Leadership Development Committee** of Compass’s Board of Directors.
- The grants were made **as a material inducement** to each employee’s employment, satisfying Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(c)(4).

## 🧬 About Compass Pathways
- **Therapeutic focus**: Development of **COMP360**, a proprietary synthetic psilocybin treatment under evaluation for treatment‑resistant depression (TRD) and post‑traumatic stress disorder (PTSD).
- **Regulatory designations**:
  - **Breakthrough Therapy** designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for TRD.
  - **Innovative Licensing and Access Pathway (ILAP)** designation in the United Kingdom for TRD.
- **Clinical programs**: Leading the world’s largest classic psychedelic clinical program in TRD and conducting a late‑stage trial for PTSD.
