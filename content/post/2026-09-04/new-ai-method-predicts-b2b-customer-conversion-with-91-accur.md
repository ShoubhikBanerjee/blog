# New AI Method Predicts B2B Customer Conversion with 91% Accuracy

A paper submitted on 3 Sep 2026 introduces a methodology for predicting whether a B2B customer will convert, achieving 91 % accuracy.

## 🔍 Overview
- In one‑time selling B2B contexts, buying cycles can last months or years.
- Effective marketing requires targeting customers with high purchase potential and recommending personalized campaigns.
- The study tackles three problems: B2B customer data aggregation, customer feature generation, and conversion prediction.

## 🧩 How it works
- An algorithm aggregates individual contacts to the B2B customer level using multiple keys.
- For non‑standardized keys such as company names, a novel architecture clusters them despite spelling mistakes and variants.
- A set of features is defined and generated from the aggregated data.
- The CatBoost model is applied to predict conversion into the sales funnel.

## 📊 Results
- The framework attains 91 % prediction accuracy.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Prediction results and model analysis are used to discuss personalized campaign recommendations that aim to foster conversion.
