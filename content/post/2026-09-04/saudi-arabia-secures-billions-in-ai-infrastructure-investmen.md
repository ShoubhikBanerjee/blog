# Saudi Arabia Secures Billions in AI Infrastructure Investments and Global Partnerships

Saudi Arabia has experienced a massive surge in technological investments, with more than $15 billion in launches, investments, and partnerships announced on the opening day of a major industry event, followed by approximately $2.5 billion on the second day and $857 million in technology and manufacturing investments on the third day. These developments mark a significant expansion of cloud and artificial intelligence infrastructure across the country.

## 🔍 Overview

Amazon Web Services (AWS) confirmed that its first cloud infrastructure region in Saudi Arabia is on track to launch in December 2026. Backed by a planned investment of more than $5.3 billion, this region will enable developers, startups, government entities, and businesses to run workloads from data centres located directly within the kingdom. 

Alongside AWS, several global tech firms and local entities—led by HUMAIN—have established collaborative projects to scale up local computing power, deploy enterprise AI software, and expand sovereign AI services.

## ⚙️ Infrastructure and Capacity Targets

A series of multi-megawatt data centre developments and hardware deployments have been initiated to support the growing demand for AI training and inference:

* **AMD, Cisco, and HUMAIN:** Production AI computing infrastructure powered by AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs, AMD EPYC CPUs, and Cisco networking is already live in Saudi Arabia. The partners plan to deploy up to an additional 250 megawatts beginning in 2027, with their joint venture targeting up to 1 gigawatt of AI infrastructure by 2030.
* **Together AI and HUMAIN:** This partnership will provide 55 megawatts of dedicated AI capacity and build a 250-megawatt data centre. An initial deployment of 25 megawatts is planned with AMD, targeting an infrastructure capacity of 250 megawatts over the next two to three years.
* **HUMAIN AI Cloud:** HUMAIN has launched a locally hosted AI cloud powered by NVIDIA HGX B300 Blackwell Ultra systems. It currently features 1.1 megawatts of operational infrastructure with a utilization rate of over 90 percent, and capacity is planned to expand to 35.1 megawatts by 2027.
* **Oxagon Data Centre:** HUMAIN and DataVolt have commenced construction on the 100-megawatt initial phase of an AI-ready data centre in NEOM’s Oxagon. The facility is part of a planned 360-megawatt development.
* **Al Moammar Information Systems:** This entity announced a $1.2 billion investment to expand its data centres, increasing capacity to 192 megawatts.
* **NHC Innovation:** Announced an $800 million investment to develop Khuzam Digital Valley, with plans to expand capacity up to 65 megawatts by 2033.

## 🧩 Partnerships and Integrations

To deploy these hardware resources, a network of software, translation, and productivity integrations has been established:

| Partner Entity | Collaboration Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AWS & HUMAIN** | Expanding partnership to provide up to 50 megawatts of capacity within Saudi Arabia's first AI zone by 2028 using AWS Trainium chips and NVIDIA infrastructure. HUMAIN's Arabic large language model ALLAM will be available on Amazon Bedrock, and HUMAIN Fabric will be offered via AWS Marketplace. |
| **Adobe** | Committing more than $4 billion through an expanded partnership with the Saudi Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and HUMAIN. The agreement provides free access to Adobe Firefly Standard and Adobe Express Premium to over 27 million eligible residents aged 13 and above for 12 months by the end of 2026. It also includes co-developing an image-generation model using Adobe Firefly Foundry and HUMAIN tailored to Saudi culture and Arabic prompts. |
| **Microsoft** | Partnering with HUMAIN on a proposed AI productivity bundle combining HUMAIN ONE and Microsoft 365 Copilot, aiming for 1 million enterprise users across the Middle East and Africa. HUMAIN will also launch an AI PC running Microsoft Windows. |
| **Arabic.AI & Tarjama** | Receiving a strategic investment from HUMAIN to expand Arabic-language translation, document intelligence, and enterprise AI (focusing on translation management, legacy archive digitisation, and document-processing AI agents). |
| **Reflection AI** | Collaborating with HUMAIN to host advanced foundational models directly on Saudi infrastructure. The companies will explore model customisation, sovereign AI, and use HUMAIN capacity for future training and inference. |
| **KORA Systems** | Co-developing "HUMAIN OS" with HUMAIN, an enterprise-focused, AI-native Linux operating system designed to interpret user intent and allocate models or compute resources. |
| **stc group** | Expanding strategic collaboration with AWS to accelerate cloud and AI adoption across government, healthcare, finance, sports, education, energy, industry, and logistics, while investing in cloud and AI skills. |
| **ZainTECH** | Signing a multi-year collaboration with AWS to help organisations modernise systems, adopt AI, and meet compliance, security, and data residency needs. |

## 🚀 Availability

* **September:** Enterprise availability begins for the HUMAIN AI PC running Microsoft Windows.
* **Later this year:** Expected release and hosting of Reflection AI's advanced foundational models on Saudi infrastructure.
* **December 2026:** Scheduled launch of the AWS cloud infrastructure region in Saudi Arabia.
* **End of 2026:** Deadline for eligible citizens and residents to receive 12 months of free access to Adobe Firefly Standard and Adobe Express Premium.
* **2027:** Targeted expansion of HUMAIN's AI cloud capacity to 35.1 megawatts, and commencement of the AMD-Cisco-HUMAIN joint venture's next 250-megawatt deployment.
* **2028:** Projected availability of the first 100-megawatt phase of the Oxagon data centre; deadline for the AWS-HUMAIN 50-megawatt AI zone capacity.
* **2030:** Long-term target of up to 1 gigawatt of AI infrastructure by the AMD, Cisco, and HUMAIN joint venture.
* **2033:** Target for expanding Khuzam Digital Valley capacity to 65 megawatts.
