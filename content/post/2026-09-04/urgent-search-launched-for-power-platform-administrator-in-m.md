# Urgent Search Launched for Power Platform Administrator in Manchester IT Hub

A blue-chip client has launched an urgent recruitment drive for a Power Platform Administrator to support its operations in Manchester or Inverness. Managed through a preferred supplier, this contract role represents a key technical opening within Manchester's technology sector, which encompasses fintech, software, data engineering, and enterprise IT.

## 🔍 Overview
The newly opened contract position is an onsite role requiring five days per week of office-based work. The position operates under PAYE via an umbrella company, with immediate interviews available for qualified candidates.

## ⚙️ Key details
The contract features specific operational parameters and structural requirements:
* **Locations:** Onsite in Manchester or Inverness.
* **Attendance:** Mandatory five days per week onsite (100% office-based).
* **Service Window:** A 12-hour service window from 07:00 to 19:00 UK time (08:00 to 20:00 CET).
* **Payment Status:** PAYE via an umbrella company only.

## 🧩 How it works
The Power Platform Administrator will be responsible for maintaining the health, security, and compliance of the organization's data tools. Key responsibilities include:
* Administering and maintaining the Power BI Service, Workspaces, Apps, and Dataflows.
* Managing tenant settings, user access, workspace permissions, and security controls.
* Enforcing Power BI governance standards, compliance requirements, and reporting certification processes.
* Monitoring platform health, capacity utilization, refresh schedules, usage metrics, and audit logs.
* Managing gateways and data source connectivity while troubleshooting performance and refresh issues.
* Maintaining technical documentation and collaborating with business and technical stakeholders to ensure platform adoption and best practices.

To qualify for the position, candidates must demonstrate the following skills:
* Strong knowledge of Power BI Service administration and governance for both Cloud and Reporting Server.
* Experience with Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), access management, and security configuration.
* Knowledge of enterprise governance, compliance, and audit requirements.
* Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills.

## 🚀 Availability
This position is currently open for applications, with immediate interviews being scheduled. Candidates can apply by submitting their CV directly to the preferred supplier.
