# New Book: Mathematical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning

## 📚 Introduction

A new book entitled *Mathematical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning* has been released, offering a mathematical but friendly introduction to fundamental concepts, basic problems, and classic algorithms in reinforcement learning.

## 📖 Overview

- The book provides an adequate depth of mathematics while ensuring readability
- Readers can selectively engage with materials presented in gray boxes
- All examples are based on a grid world task for clarity
- The ten chapters are coherently organized and highly correlated
- Content is built progressively, with each chapter forming a necessary foundation for the next

## 📂 Content Structure

The book is divided into two parts:

| Part | Focus |
|------|-------|
| First | Basic tools |
| Second | Algorithms |

## 👥 Target Audience

- Senior undergraduate students
- Graduate students
- Researchers
- Practitioners interested in reinforcement learning

## 📚 Prerequisites

- Requires knowledge of probability theory and linear algebra
- Some mathematics basics are included in the appendix
- No prior reinforcement learning background needed

## 🏫 Teaching Background

- The material originates from a graduate-level course on reinforcement learning taught since 2019
- The lecture videos have received 2,100,000+ views with positive feedback

## 🚀 Availability

- Professors preparing courses can contact the author via email for slide content and source code
- Slides were created using Latex/Beamer
- Note: Responses to feedback may experience significant delays due to the author's commitments
