# Jensen Huang Reinstated as Nvidia CEO Following Ouster

Jensen Huang has returned to Nvidia following an ouster driven by alleged indiscretions, including an alleged affair with a Chinese spy, relationships with two subordinates, and allegedly terrorizing employees.

## 🚀 Market Reaction

* Nvidia's stock price recently took a tumble for obvious reasons.
* Following the reinstatement, NASDAQ-100 futures jumped so quickly that trading was halted.

## 💡 Key Details

* The Nvidia board's decision to reinstate Huang was made with input from senior levels of the United States government.
* The US government determined that Huang's reinstatement poses no security risk.
* President Donald Trump posted lengthy congratulations on Truth Social, commending the board for making the correct and proper decision and describing Huang as a Great Patriot and National Treasure.
* Tech leaders, including Elon Musk, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Atreides founder and CIO Gavin Baker, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeny, and Bill Ackman, offered congratulations or commented on the return.
