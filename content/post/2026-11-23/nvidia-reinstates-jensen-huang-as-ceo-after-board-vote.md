# Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO After Board Vote

Nvidia announced on Monday morning that the board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO following discussions and meetings with Huang. The board stated it was satisfied with Huang's candor and ability to lead NVIDIA. The board also confirmed that it received input from senior levels of the United States government and determined that Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk. Donald Trump responded on Truth Social, stating that Nvidia made the correct decision and that the U.S. government has no reason to believe Huang's return poses any risk to national security, citing the Attorney General's confirmation that no evidence of unlawful conduct exists.

## 📋 Key facts
- The board voted unanimously to bring back Huang as CEO, President, and Board director
- The board received input from senior levels of the United States government
- The investigation into reports of a Chinese spy plot will continue with Huang's support
- Interim CEO Colette Kress will remain as CFO
- Harvey Jones will remain as board chair
- Huang resigned as CEO earlier this month following reports of extramarital affairs
- More than 200,000 people, including Elon Musk, Tim Sweeney, Garry Tan, and Travis Kalanick, signed a letter calling for his reinstatement
- Nvidia shares tumbled in Huang’s absence
- Industry analysts expressed concerns about an outsider replacing him
- The board maintained as recently as last Tuesday that Huang wasn’t coming back

## 🗣️ Statements
- Huang said: "I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution."
- Huang added: "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process."

## 🇺🇸 Government perspective
- The U.S. government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security
- The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct

## 🔁 Leadership structure
- Colette Kress – Interim CEO (will remain as CFO)
- Harvey Jones – Board chair (will remain in role)
