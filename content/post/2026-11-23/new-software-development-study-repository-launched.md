# New Software Development Study Repository Launched

I have recently created a new study repository called 'estudy' that contains a collection of my records from studying and researching software development. Starting as a simple theory log, it has grown to include deep dives and research, personal thoughts and reflections, as well as the values and experiences I consider important.

## 📚 What's Inside

The repository includes:

- Theory documents from my software studies
- Deep dive analyses and research findings
- Personal reflections and viewpoints
- Experiences and values I consider significant
- Various formats and structures of content

## 🌳 From Trees to Forest

Initially, the records were made to showcase achievements and satisfy intellectual vanity, but as the collection grew, I realized it also reflected my hidden desires. Some content may not be helpful to others, but some might become turning points.

## 💾 Timeless Records

Once committed to this repository, the data becomes eternal. No matter the content, structure, or format, all records are valuable and meaningful data in this space.

## 📖 Welcome to My Knowledge Forest

I cordially welcome you to visit my knowledge forest 'estudy' and express my sincere gratitude for your visit.
