# TeleMem Launches as High-Performance Drop-In Replacement for Mem0 with Video Reasoning Support

TeleMem is a new open-source memory management layer that serves as a drop-in replacement for Mem0, offering semantic deduplication, long-term dialogue memory, and multimodal video reasoning capabilities. Designed for AI agents, it enables seamless storage, retrieval, and reasoning over video content while maintaining full local operation by default.
