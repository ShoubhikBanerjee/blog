# ArisGlobal’s NavaX Faces Rising Inference Costs as Prompts Grow

ArisGlobal developed LifeSphere NavaX on Amazon Bedrock to automate adverse-event case processing. The company reports that its shared prompt grew by 150% over six months, while inference costs per case increased by more than 200%.
