# Perplexity hits $1B revenue; Qwen4-Max and OpenAI evals released

Perplexity has crossed a $1B annual revenue run rate, according to CEO Aravind Srinivas, while Alibaba's Qwen4-Max preview moved to a shipping model and OpenAI granted external partners access to its internal systems for evaluation.

## 🔍 Overview

- Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas told Semafor that the company crossed a $1B annual revenue run rate.
- The Information previously reported Perplexity was under $250M at the start of the year and over $750M by August.
- The growth was partly driven by its product, Computer.
- At typical agent lab multiples, this revenue level suggests a valuation of roughly $30-50B for Perplexity.
- This performance builds on a $20B funding round in September 2025.

## 🧩 How it works

- **Perplexity Features**: 
  - September’s Automations allow the system to pick up recurring tasks.
  - November’s Teammates handle ongoing jobs.
  - Computer runs fully locally on supported Macs.
  - The Monitor API watches a topic or page and triggers a webhook when relevant changes occur.
- **Qwen4-Max Specifications**:
  - Total parameter count is 2.4T, with 71B active per token (a 25% reduction).
  - Accepts images and text with a 1M-token context window.
  - Smaller open-weight Qwen4 models are planned for release.
- **OpenAI Evaluation Program**:
  - Apollo, METR, Transluce, and Goodfire have internal access to OpenAI models and systems.
  - Apollo places six evaluators onsite for four months.
  - METR reviews OpenAI's largest RL run with access to transcripts, monitor records, and checkpoint snapshots.
  - Transluce conducts a six-week audit of post-training alignment evals, with a fuller follow-up in early 2027.
  - Goodfire embeds interpretability researchers to monitor training runs.

## ⚙️ Key details

- **Qwen4-Max Performance**:
  - Scored 43 on the Intelligence Index at the highest reasoning setting, up from 34.
  - Average cost fell from $10.02 to $3.27 per task.
  - Alibaba cut output pricing from $6 to $4.50 per million tokens; the model generated about 39% fewer tokens.
  - On Arena, it ranks #14 overall and is the top Chinese model, with +7.5% net over 14.7k sessions.
  - Placed #6 in Code Arena WebDev at 1750, up 74 points from its predecessor.
  - Placed #6 in Image-to-WebDev, just above Claude Opus 5 and GPT-6 Sol.
  - Ranked #11 overall on Vals, with gains in coding and finance.
  - On HLAB, it gets 95.7% of checklist items right but passes only 21.3% of tasks, with output tokens rising 3.6x.
  - Roboflow notes it is underwhelming on its VLM benchmark compared to Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic.
  - At high reasoning effort, it is ~4.5x faster and ~68% cheaper per image than Qwen3.8-Max.

- **Competitor Benchmark**:
  - Kimi K3.1 Pro cost $3.68 per index task and scored 67% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, compared to Qwen’s 48%.

## 💡 Why it matters

- Perplexity’s revenue milestone qualifies it for the “>$10B fundraise” rule, marking a significant valuation increase.
- The new Perplexity research lab has access to 10,000+ GPUs and plans to publish papers, data, models, and code.
- OpenAI’s decision to grant internal access to independent evaluators represents a structured approach to external safety and alignment auditing.
