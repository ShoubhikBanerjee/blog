# Novo Nordisk deploys agentic AI to manage pharmaceutical documentation

Novo Nordisk has developed an intelligent support system powered by agentic AI and deployed on AWS to manage quality-relevant documentation within its manufacturing technology operations. The system is designed to reduce lead times, improve documentation visibility, and make critical manufacturing knowledge more accessible.
