# Jensen Huang Says He Is Willing to Return as Nvidia CEO

Jensen Huang, who resigned as Nvidia’s CEO last week, has stated he is willing to return to the role if the company’s board of directors requests it. This statement comes after his resignation following reports of two affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy and a direct report.

## 🔍 Background on Resignation
Huang resigned on Thursday. In a statement, he acknowledged that he "did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at NVIDIA, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life." He noted that he created problems for the Board that he deeply regrets and owns his mistakes completely. He is currently committed to cooperating with the Board’s independent investigation.

## ⚙️ Interim Leadership and Support
Following Huang’s departure, CFO Colette Kress was appointed as interim CEO. However, significant public support for Huang’s return has emerged. More than 193,000 people, including CEOs such as Elon Musk and Tim Sweeney, have signed a letter to "bring Jensen back." President Trump has also expressed support for Huang’s return, stating that it "certainly isn’t a bad idea."

## 💡 Huang's Statement on Return
Huang said he has seen an "outpouring of calls" from Nvidia’s employees, investors, business partners, and customers asking him to return. He noted that many continue to reach out to him directly as the company navigates complex technology, market, competitive, and geopolitical dynamics. He stated, "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO."

In the statement, Huang also reflected on his time in the U.S., noting he came to the United States 53 years ago and described becoming an American citizen as one of the greatest gifts and proudest moments of his life. He emphasized that nowhere but in America could a company like NVIDIA be possible.

## 🚀 Board Stance
Despite the public support, Nvidia’s board reportedly is "not considering" having Huang return. Nvidia did not immediately reply to a request for comment regarding this development.
