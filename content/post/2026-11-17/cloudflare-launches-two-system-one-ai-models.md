# Cloudflare Launches Two System One AI Models

Cloudflare announced it is turning its AI platform into a neolab and unveiled two new System One models, Clef and Clairic.

## 🔍 Overview
- Cloudflare has been essential web infrastructure for the past decade and a half.
- It is now positioning itself as a neolab, per a presentation at the AI Engineer Code Summit by Michelle Chen, who runs Cloudflare’s AI Platform on the product side.
- The company claims to be a neolab because it has begun launching models.
- Two System One models were introduced: Clef and Clairic.
- The term neolab was coined in November by Stephanie Palazzolo and defined as a new generation of research‑focused AI startups.
- Cloudflare does not meet at least half of the neolab criteria because it is a public company with significant revenue.

## 🧩 How it works
- Clef is a decision model directly inspired by TypeSafe AI’s Jev model.
- Clairic is an extract model that can take unstructured input and extract, compute, estimate, and forecast values in one forward pass.
- System One models enable work to be done by smaller, faster models with output measured in milliseconds rather than seconds.
- Post‑training is the primary focus of Cloudflare’s model development team.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Record earnings: annualized revenue crossed $3 billion and quarterly revenue of $782 million, up 39% year over year.
- CEO Matthew Prince called the third quarter Cloudflare's best ever.
- Jev's September arrival was described as "the east wind" that reshaped Cloudflare's AI model perspective.
- Clef was built in two weeks, a copy of Jev; TypeSafe AI spent two years developing Jev.
- TypeSafe is credited with creating the Jev model and providing the API schema Cloudflare uses.
- Cloudflare’s AI Platform team concentrates on post‑training.

## 💡 Why it matters
- System One models let smaller, faster models perform work measured in milliseconds, improving efficiency.
- Clairic can extract typed values such as order numbers from unstructured data like invoices, reducing LLM error risk.
- The approach allows Cloudflare to handle some requests without sending them to a large language model, potentially lowering latency and cost.
- Post‑training is the main development focus, suggesting efficiency gains from refining existing models rather than building new ones.
- Cloudflare’s public status and revenue indicate it is not a typical neolab, but its AI platform expansion signals a strategic shift toward research‑focused capabilities.
