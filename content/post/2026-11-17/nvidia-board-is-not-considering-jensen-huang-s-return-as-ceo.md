# Nvidia Board Is Not Considering Jensen Huang’s Return as CEO

CNBC reports that Nvidia’s board is not considering bringing Jensen Huang back as CEO and remains focused on an investigation into his conduct.

## 🔍 Board’s position

A source told CNBC the board “is focused on an investigation into Huang, and is not in discussions to bring him back.” Nvidia has hired Wachtell Lipton to investigate Huang’s conduct.

## 🧩 Calls for Huang’s return

Since Huang’s resignation last week, tech executives and current and former Nvidia employees have called for his reinstatement. An online petition seeking his return has gathered more than 180,000 signatures, including signatures from Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, and Replit CEO Amjad Masad.

President Donald Trump said on Monday that it “certainly isn’t a bad idea” for Huang to return to Nvidia. A Financial Times report also suggests Huang floated the idea of returning as CEO with some of his colleagues.

## ⚙️ Interim leadership

Nvidia appointed its chief financial officer, Colette Kress, as interim CEO.
