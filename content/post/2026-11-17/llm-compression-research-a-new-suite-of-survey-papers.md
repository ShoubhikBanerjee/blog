# LLM Compression Research: A New Suite of Survey Papers

A newly curated set of AI research papers focuses on techniques to compress large language models and accelerate their training and inference.

## 🔍 Overview
- The collection aggregates several survey papers covering a wide range of efficiency‑oriented topics in large language models.
- Papers are organized by year in collapsible blocks, newest first; the current year is expanded by default.

## 🧩 How it Works
- Each paper provides a comprehensive review of existing methods for a particular sub‑field such as model routing, distillation, memory management, and low‑precision training.
