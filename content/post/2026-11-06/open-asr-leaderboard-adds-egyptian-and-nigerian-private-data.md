# Open ASR Leaderboard Adds Egyptian and Nigerian Private Data Splits

A new collaboration with Liva AI expands the Open ASR Leaderboard with two private, conversational English splits for Egyptian 🇪🇬 and Nigerian 🇳🇬 accents, continuing the effort to improve representation and trust in automatic‑speech‑recognition evaluation.

## 🔎 Overview
- Earlier this year the leaderboard added private data: 11 splits from Appen and DataOceanAI covering scripted and conversational speech from five accents.  
- The community praised the held‑out sets for enabling trustworthy evaluations.
- A subsequent addition introduced a private split for Indian‑accented English supplied by Voice Arena.
- The latest update introduces two new accents via Liva AI.

## 📊 New Private Splits
| Dataset | Accent | Duration (hrs) | Speech Type | Transcription |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liva AI Conversational EG | Egyptian | 1.34 | Conversational, spontaneous | Punctuated, cased, with disfluencies |
| Liva AI Conversational NG | Nigerian | 1.55 | Conversational, spontaneous | Punctuated, cased, with disfluencies |

## 🌍 Representation & Trust
- The leaderboard now reports a richer breakdown of accents using Kachru’s Three Circles framework (credit to Hume AI for the idea).
- As more accents join, the Outer and Expanding Circles will be split to reflect finer granularity.
- Adding new private splits helps minimise “bench‑maxxing” and provides more reliable, held‑out evaluation data.

## 🛠️ How Liva AI Contributes
- Liva AI collects multimodal interaction data through consumer products used by real people.
- Owning both the collection environment and distribution lets Liva design products that target specific model gaps across domains, industries, languages, modalities, and interaction types.
- This approach yields natural, high‑context behavior that emerges from real product use, rather than from artificially elicited or task‑based interactions.

## 📈 Why it matters
- More accents and languages improve the global relevance of ASR benchmarks.
- Private, high‑quality data supports the community in pushing ASR performance for the world 🤗
