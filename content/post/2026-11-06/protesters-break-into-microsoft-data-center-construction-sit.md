# Protesters Break Into Microsoft Data Center Construction Site in Amsterdam

Protesters broke into the construction site of a new Microsoft data center in Amsterdam, marking the latest incident in a wave of data center protests across Europe.

## 🔍 Amsterdam site protest

Members of the group Geef Tegengas helped organize the protest against the construction of three 85-meter data center towers in the outskirts of Amsterdam. According to a report from Reuters, around 100 people entered the construction site.

Key actions during the demonstration included:
- Scaling a crane to hang a "fuck AI" banner.
- Unfurling a "Microsoft powers genocide" banner on the site.
- Chanting "Microsoft has got to go" and "free Palestine."

## ⚙️ Project background and site details

The facility's development has navigated local municipal bans and planning history:
- **Tenant:** Microsoft will serve as the sole tenant of the new data center.
- **Developer:** British developer Pure Data Centres is building the site, having navigated loopholes to proceed with construction.
- **Timeline:** The project was planned as far back as 2016, before Amsterdam announced a temporary stop to data center development at the end of 2023 and subsequently banned new data centers until 2030.

## 🌐 Regional and global context

The incident in Amsterdam coincides with broader resistance to data center expansion:
- **Dublin:** Activists staged a mock Israeli military checkpoint outside a Microsoft data center in Dublin.
- **United Kingdom:** Microsoft abandoned the UK's largest data center project after electricity grid officials stated power would not be supplied until the 2030s.
- **United States:** Voters rejected unrestrained data center development across the country, where local opposition frequently targets the impact of popping up AI data centers.

## 🏢 Internal employee protests

Microsoft has also faced internal pushback from former and current employees over the past year:
- **Build Keynote:** An employee interrupted Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella's keynote at the company's Build developer conference.
- **50th Anniversary:** Employees interrupted Microsoft's 50th anniversary party to protest tech usage in Israel's war against Hamas, with one calling Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman a "war profiteer."
- **Headquarters Occupation:** Microsoft fired two employee protesters who occupied the office of the company's president.
