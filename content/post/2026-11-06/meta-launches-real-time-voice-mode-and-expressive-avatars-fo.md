# Meta launches real‑time voice mode and expressive avatars for Muse AI agent

Meta announced that its Muse AI agent now supports real‑time voice conversations with expressive animated avatars.

## 🔍 Overview
- Muse AI agent now has a voice mode.
- Voice mode includes selectable voices and expressive avatars that animate while chatting.

## 🧩 How it works
- Users can talk to Muse in real time; the avatar at the top of the conversation animates in time with the words.
- The voice tone can be changed by asking the agent what you want or by browsing options in the avatar settings.
- In brief tests, the agent responds quickly over voice, with occasional pauses while it looks up information, similar to text‑chat behavior.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The feature has been rolled out to users in the United States and is available on the iOS app (tested while writing this article).
- Avatars are customizable; one user set the avatar to look like Mark Zuckerberg, noting the experience feels "quite weird."
- The voice cannot be made to sound exactly like Mark Zuckerberg.

## 🚀 Availability
- Voice mode and expressive avatars are rolling out now in the US for the Muse app.
- Meta says they will arrive “soon” for its AI glasses.
- The features will also be included in the forthcoming Muse Charm gadget, which is expected to ship later this year.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enables spoken interaction with a personal AI agent in real time.
- Adds visual expressiveness through animated avatars that move in sync with speech.
