# Amazon adds SMS ordering to Alexa for Shopping

Amazon is expanding its Alexa for Shopping AI assistant with a new texting capability that lets users order items, get recommendations, and track deals via SMS.

## 📱 Overview
- Users can text a new contact that appears in their phone as **"Amazon Alexa"** to shop.
- The feature lets customers **describe products or send a photo**, and Alexa searches Amazon listings, texts back recommendations, and can place the order on the user’s behalf.
- It builds on the Alexa for Shopping experience that launched in May, targeting moments when texting is more convenient than the app or website.

## 🧩 How it works
| Request type | Alexa’s response |
|---|---|
| Weekly groceries | Orders groceries via text messages |
| Gift recommendations | Provides product suggestions |
| Find similar product from a photo | Searches Amazon listings and texts matching results |
| Track deals and prices | Texts when a watched item goes on sale |
| Remember personal preferences | Uses preferences (e.g., vegan options) for future recommendations |

## ⚙️ Key details
- The contact is saved as **"Amazon Alexa"** and can handle a wide range of shopping requests, including groceries, gifts, and photo‑based product searches.
- Alexa says it **remembers personal preferences over time** and can **track deals and prices**.
- The texting capability is **rolling out to a small group of Amazon customers in the US starting today**, who will receive a text invitation.
- Amazon plans to expand the service to **“millions more customers” in the coming weeks**.
- Quote from Rajiv Mehta, vice president of Conversational Shopping at Amazon: “Customers often jot down what they need the moment they think of it in their notes, in their messages, in their group chats.”

## 🚀 Availability
- Initial rollout: small US group receiving invitation texts **today**.
- Next phase: rollout to **millions more customers** in the coming weeks, according to Amazon’s announcement.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Amazon joins other companies that let users **text chatbots** for commerce, such as OpenAI’s **Dot** and DoorDash’s **Ask DoorDash** AI chatbot.
- The feature offers a **text‑first** shopping experience, letting users “jot down what they need” and have a personal shopping agent work “just a text away.”
- By handling **searching, comparing, and buying** via SMS, Alexa aims to let users get on with their day without opening an app or website.
