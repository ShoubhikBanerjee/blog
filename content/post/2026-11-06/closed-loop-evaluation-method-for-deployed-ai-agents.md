# Closed‑Loop Evaluation Method for Deployed AI Agents

Researchers have introduced a closed‑loop evaluation framework that continuously monitors the performance of AI agents after they are deployed.

## 🔍 Overview
- "Once an agent is live, teams want to know whether it is doing its job, not just whether it throws errors."
- "Yet most evaluation is one‑off: a benchmark run, a look at traces, a single score."
- "Its conclusions neither check themselves nor reach the agent."

## 🛠️ How it works
- "We propose closed‑loop evaluation, an evaluation method for deployed agents."
- "It models evaluation as performance estimation under biased raters and censored outcomes, and it requires every level's conclusion to trace back to atomic data and to be computed by versioned rules."
- "The method covers four levels of analysis (span, trace, conversation and engagement), measures along a spine of Execution, Substance, Trajectory and Results, treats Conduct as a constraint throughout, and uses Provenance and Adjudication components to certify the evidence and the evaluators."

## 📊 Key details
- "Evaluator agents generated by an Agent Factory carry these components, each with a domain knowledge pack and an adapter harness."
- "Two loops close the evaluation: an inner loop audits the evidence, rules and metrics themselves, and an outer loop uses per‑component regression checks to drive agent improvement."
- "We also give a pipeline that runs this process automatically."
- "Using the business‑analysis agent SharpInsight as a case and porting the method to public conversation data, we show how problems differ across levels and how evidence‑and‑rule judgment is more reliable than holistic scoring (ICC 0.86 vs. 0.81; kappa 0.81 vs. 0.63)."

## 💡 Why it matters
Continuous, self‑auditing evaluation addresses the gap where deployed agents are only checked once, providing a systematic way to ensure ongoing reliability and alignment with intended tasks.
