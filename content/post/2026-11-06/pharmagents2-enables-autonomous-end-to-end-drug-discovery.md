# PharmAgents2 Enables Autonomous End-to-End Drug Discovery

PharmAgents2, an autonomous system for end‑to‑end drug discovery, has been introduced, showing that a virtual pharmaceutical company can operate without human intervention throughout the research pipeline.

## 🔍 Overview
- Drug discovery requires continuous scientific decision‑making across a vast research space.
- PharmAgents2 combines knowledge‑enhanced decision‑making with organized large‑scale exploration to address this challenge.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Paper2Skill** extracts transferable scientific decision knowledge from public sources; together with expert‑developed skills, it supports research decisions.
- **Self‑Scaling Discovery** expands exploration through parallel agents that inherit research context and decision‑making capabilities across targets, scientific hypotheses, and discovery strategies.

## 📊 Benchmarks
- **PharmAgents‑Bench v.0.1** contains 121 questions spanning nine decision scenarios and three end‑to‑end tasks, assessed with expert‑defined process rubrics.
- PharmAgents2 outperforms evaluated baselines in most decision categories and achieves the highest process scores on all three end‑to‑end tasks.

## 🧪 Real‑world validation
- **LpxH study**: PharmAgents2 hypothesized a previously unreported allosteric site, screened compounds against it, and identified a novel inhibitor scaffold with experimentally confirmed inhibitory activity and binding.
- **JAK1 study**: The system prioritized a covalent inhibition strategy and nominated compounds with experimentally demonstrated activity.
- **Proteome exploration**: Starting from a known bioactive molecule, Self‑Scaling Discovery explored candidate targets across the proteome, identified a new binding target, and the interaction was subsequently confirmed experimentally.

## 💡 Significance
- PharmAgents2 represents a prototype of a virtual pharmaceutical company, marking a step toward autonomous end‑to‑end drug discovery.
- The work was submitted on 5 Nov 2026.
