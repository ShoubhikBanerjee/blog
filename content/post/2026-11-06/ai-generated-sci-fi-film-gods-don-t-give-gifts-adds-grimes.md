# AI‑Generated Sci‑Fi Film *Gods Don’t Give Gifts* Adds Grimes

AI‑generated sci‑fi anthology *Gods Don’t Give Gifts* is set to open in theatres on Dec 4, and it has added pop‑star Grimes, who will provide both her likeness and vocal performance.

## 🔍 Overview
- Billed as “the first AI film to come to theatres.”
- Produced by AI studio **Fable**.
- Release date: **December 4** (the film releases in December).
- A trailer for the film has been released.

## 🎬 Cast & Creativity
- **Grimes** will appear, lending both her likeness and vocal performance.
- Grimes’s involvement is described as giving the project some legitimacy.
- Grimes is also working on an AI‑themed album titled **Psy Opera**.

## 🎥 Production Details
- Directed by **Jack London**, better known online as **Gossip Goblin**.
- The work is an anthology of **four interconnected stories** set in a future where bodies are disposable and consciousness is commoditized.
- Fable’s press release states: “casting the likeness of an actor with their spoken vocal performance will become more common with animated films using AI: audiences will see actors, athletes, musicians giving vocal performances with their likeness in films.”

## 📚 Themes
- The setting depicts a world where **bodies are disposable** and **consciousness is commoditized**.
- It explores the **horrifying consequences of humanity’s attempt to transcend the flesh**.
- These themes align with Grimes’s past interest in **transhumanism and artificial intelligence**.

## 📈 Industry Context
- The film is presented as a notable test case for the **commercial potential of AI films**.
- While there has been hype about AI in Hollywood, no project has yet shown the technology as a vital storytelling tool.
- Previous AI‑generated attempts, such as two versions of *The Odyssey* (including one made with Elon Musk’s Grok), “left plenty to be desired.”
