# Perplexity launches always‑on “Teammates” AI agents for workplace collaboration

Perplexity is taking on OpenAI and Anthropic with its own work‑focused AI agents.

## 🔍 Overview
Perplexity introduced an always‑on feature called **Teammates** that can assume multiple roles inside a workspace. The agents pull existing projects, skills, and memory from the Perplexity tools you already use.

## 🧩 How it works
- Teammates integrate with **Perplexity Computer**, an AI system that can deploy agents on your device to complete tasks.
- They “build on top” of Computer, offering the ability to collaborate and complete “ongoing, large‑scale work.”
- Each Teammate can be assigned a different job that it performs simultaneously, much like OpenAI’s Dots and Claude Duet.
- Like OpenAI’s Dots and Claude Duet, Teammates can perform an array of tasks while running 24/7.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Always‑on**: Teammates remain active throughout the day.
- **Multi‑role**: One agent can handle several responsibilities at once.
- **Progress tracking**: You can keep tabs on Teammates’ progress through individual threads in the Perplexity app and interact with them in Slack.
- **Pricing**: The feature is available on the **$200 / month Max plan**.
- **Vision**: “Over time, we envision every org having hundreds of teammates working for each person,” Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas says on X.

## 🚀 Availability
Teammates are delivered as part of Perplexity’s Max subscription tier. They work in conjunction with Perplexity Computer and are accessible via the Perplexity app and Slack integrations.

## 💡 Why it matters
By offering always‑on, multi‑role agents that integrate with existing Perplexity tools, the company positions itself as a direct competitor to OpenAI and Anthropic’s work‑focused AI offerings.
