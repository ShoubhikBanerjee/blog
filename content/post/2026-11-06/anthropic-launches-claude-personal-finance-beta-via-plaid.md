# Anthropic launches Claude Personal Finance beta via Plaid

Anthropic is adding a Personal Finance feature to Claude that connects to users’ bank and card accounts through the Plaid platform.

## 🔍 Overview
Claude can now read a user’s banking data (read‑only) and answer questions about spending, upcoming bills, affordability of purchases, and potential savings.

## 🧩 How it works
- The connection is facilitated by Plaid, so Claude never sees banking passwords and banks do not have to deal with unvetted AI bots.
- Access is read‑only; Claude cannot move, manage, or invest money and is not designed to give investment advice.
- Claude can see the bank’s name, account names, account type, the last four digits of the number, balances, credit limits, and up to two years of transaction history.

## ⚙️ Key details
| Capability | What Claude can do |
|------------|-------------------|
| Spending overview | Explain where money went this month and break down spending by categories such as rent, utilities, shopping, and food |
| Bill tracking | Answer broader questions about what bills are left before the next payday |
| Purchase affordability | Indicate if a big purchase like a holiday trip is affordable |
| Savings insight | Estimate how much money could be saved each month |
| Terminology help | Explain financial terms the user doesn’t understand |
| Balance queries | Answer questions about balances and transactions |

## 🚀 Availability
- Personal Finance is rolling out in beta this week to Claude Pro, Max, and Ultra plan subscribers in the US.
- Anthropic’s announcement follows OpenAI introducing a similar feature for ChatGPT in May, which also plugs into bank accounts through Plaid.

## 💡 Why it matters
> “Personal Finance is a first step. For life’s bigger money decisions, we think people are best served by Claude working alongside the licensed professionals and regulated firms they trust. We’re exploring how to make that possible in Claude, and we’d like to hear from banks, wealth managers, and financial services companies who want to help shape it.”

Allowing AI oversight into finances may ring some alarm bells, but Anthropic frames this as an early step toward collaborative financial assistance.
