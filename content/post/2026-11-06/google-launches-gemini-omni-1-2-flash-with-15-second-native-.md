# Google launches Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash with 15‑second native video and lower price

Today, Google is rolling out Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash and making it generally available across the Gemini API and Gemini for business.

## 🔍 Overview
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash adds native 15‑second video creation, multi‑turn conversational editing, and stronger reference following.

## 🛠️ Features
- Native video generation up to **15 seconds** (up from 10 seconds) with one‑second granularity from **3 – 15 seconds**.
- Multi‑turn conversational editing works across full clips, allowing background swaps, lighting changes, aspect‑ratio adjustments, or product swaps turn by turn without re‑generating the entire clip.
- Enhanced reference following keeps faces, garments, packaging typography, brand colors, and lighting consistent across camera angles and motion.
- Supports input of character, product, or style images, short motion clips (up to 3 seconds), and audio cues; the model retains those details throughout generation.
- Generates more fluid continuous camera movements—such as sweeping FPV fly‑throughs and dramatic zoom‑outs—with significantly fewer jump cuts.
- Produces clearer lip‑sync, natural‑sounding dialogue, and distinct multi‑speaker conditioning.

## 📏 Duration, Resolution & Pricing
- Max video length: **15 seconds** (previously 10 seconds).
- Duration can be set in **one‑second increments** between **3 and 15 seconds**.
- Drafts can be created at **360p or 720p**; final clips can be upscaled to **1080p or 4K**.
- Price reduced **30 %** from Omni 1.1, now **$0.07 per second for 720p video**.

## 🎞️ Consistency & Editing
- Faces, garments, and branding stay consistent across camera angles; e.g., a single model photo plus front‑and‑back shots of three outfits generates a 15‑second virtual try‑on sequence with consistent identity and garment details.
- Less visual drift or morphing when interpolating between keyframes or extending clips.
- Multi‑turn edits can change background turn by turn (e.g., striped pavilion tent → oak forest → stable with a white horse → market fair → stone castle) while preserving character performance, armor reflections, and framing.
- Reference matching improved: Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash builds layered posters sequentially, locking material, shadow, and typographic details through the final frame.

## 🚀 Availability & Infrastructure
- First time Gemini Omni is **generally available**, backed by Google Cloud’s enterprise‑grade infrastructure and security.
- **Provisioned Throughput** is offered; outputs include an imperceptible **SynthID watermark**.
- Ongoing development targets fast sports action, rapid script changes, and complex physics, but the current version is ready for businesses, developers, and creators producing videos at scale.

## 💡 Use Cases & Adoption
- Businesses, startups, and creatives are already integrating Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash into workflows.
- Applicable for multi‑character dialogue in Flow, video explainers in Gemini for business, and polished avatar‑driven videos with music and narration in Google Vids.
- Enables efficient production of product explainers, social ad spots, virtual try‑on clips, catalog spins, and branded video ads.
