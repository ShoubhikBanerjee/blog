# Graham Dumpleton Announces Muru, a New AI-Assisted Rust Server for Python Apps

Graham Dumpleton, who started mod_wsgi 20 years ago, has introduced Muru, a new AI-assisted Rust server designed to serve Python web applications.

## 🔍 Overview

- Muru aims to provide tools for running ASGI and WSGI Python applications in both development and production.
- The project has been in development with published accounts over the last three weeks.
- Graham Dumpleton draws on twenty years of experience with mod_wsgi to guide the AI development process.

## 💡 Why it matters

- The AI handles much of the surface work, while twenty years of experience with mod_wsgi helps determine what is not yet good enough.
- The creator is committed to polishing Muru until it reaches a level of trust for production use.
