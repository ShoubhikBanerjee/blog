# AWS Quick adds Meta ads AI connector for marketing workflows

AWS Quick has added a Meta ads AI connector, enabling marketers to create, manage, and analyze campaigns using natural language while staying connected to the Meta ad management network.

## 🔍 Overview
The connector addresses the friction of manual data handling in marketing teams. Previously, data such as campaign results, creative assets, and inventory lived in separate systems like Meta Ads Manager, shared storage, and data lakes. Analysts often had to export reports and manually update campaigns, leading to slow insights. With the new connector, marketing teams can use one place to go from campaign planning to execution, acting on business insights directly in their ads without tool switching.

## 🧩 How it works
The Meta ads AI connector gives AWS Quick access to ads Model Context Protocol (MCP) server operations. Installation involves navigating to **Customize > Connectors** in AWS Quick, signing in with Facebook, and selecting which Pages, Businesses, and Instagram accounts AWS Quick can access. Once connected, users can interact with the system via natural language prompts. The system can combine Meta ad management data with first- and third-party data sources to generate dashboards, documents, slide decks, and automated workflows. It also integrates with fal.ai’s image, audio, and video models within the same workflow to test creative ideas.

## ⚙️ Key details
The connector supports specific capabilities for managing ad lifecycle tasks:

* **Plan and launch campaigns**: Drafts campaign, ad set, and ad settings from an approved brief; flags missing budget and audience decisions; runs pre-launch checks for naming, UTM tags, budget, and special ad categories.
* **Analyze and share performance**: Tracks spend, CTR, and ROAS at various levels; compares results to the media plan; builds weekly decks of top ads, weak ads, and next tests; allows leaders to review decks and ask follow-up questions.
* **Refresh creative and audiences**: Flags creative fatigue when frequency rises and CTR falls.
* **Pulling insights and creation**: Supports creating product catalogs and editing or creating new campaigns.

The system also executes specific prompt-based outputs, such as turning past campaign results into an eight-slide launch-plan deck or building a dashboard comparing spend, leads, and cost per lead for two ad sets.

## 💡 Why it matters
By automating the path from insight to action, the connector reduces manual handoffs and tool switching. Marketing teams can spend more time reaching customers and testing more creative ideas, as supported by the integration of fal.ai media models.
