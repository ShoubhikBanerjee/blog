# New Free Comprehensive Reverse Engineering Tutorial Covers Multiple CPU Architectures

A new free comprehensive reverse engineering tutorial has been released, covering a wide range of architectures including x86, ARM, and RISC-V. Assembled by @0xInfection on Twitter, the instructional resource is designed to guide users from basic malware reverse engineering concepts to entry-level assembly language examination.

## 🔍 Overview
The tutorial provides a structured curriculum that addresses several key hardware architectures:
* x86 and x64
* 32-bit and 64-bit ARM
* 8-bit AVR
* 32-bit RISC-V

## ⚙️ Key details
The series includes specific lessons that focus on different aspects of reverse engineering and assembly:

| Lesson | Course | Topic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lesson 1 | x86 Course (Part 1: Goals) | Basic concepts of malware reverse engineering and entry-level assembly. |
| Lesson 2 | x86 Course (Part 2: Techniques) | Specific reverse engineering techniques. |
| Lesson 359 | AVR Assembler Course | Infrared (IR) Receiver (Chapter 013). |

## 🧩 How it works
The tutorial begins by teaching the fundamental concepts of malware reverse engineering. It then graduates to a basic examination of assembly language. Practical applications include Lesson 359, which teaches students how to write a high-speed assembly loop to visualize digital data packets transmitted through the air by a TV remote control.

## 🚀 Availability
This comprehensive learning resource is available for free.
