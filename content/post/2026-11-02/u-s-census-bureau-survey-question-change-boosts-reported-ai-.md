# U.S. Census Bureau Survey Question Change Boosts Reported AI Adoption

The U.S. Census Bureau’s Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS) changed its AI question in late 2025, and the latest October 2026 release shows a marked rise in reported AI use among large firms.

## 🔍 Overview
- BTOS runs every two weeks and surveys 200,000 businesses out of a total of 1.2 million.
- Each business is on a rotating panel and completes the survey once every twelve weeks.
- Results are published on alternating Thursdays at 10 am; the most recent release was October 22 2026.

## 🛠️ Survey Question Change
- **Before 17 Nov 2025**: “In the last two weeks, did this business use Artificial Intelligence (AI) in producing goods or services? (Examples of AI: machine learning, natural language processing, virtual agents, voice recognition, etc.)”
- **3 Dec 2025 announcement**: “In the last two weeks, did this business use Artificial Intelligence (AI) in any of its business functions? (Examples of AI: machine learning, natural language processing, virtual agents, voice recognition, etc.)” – the “producing goods or services” phrasing was removed.
- **19 Oct 2026**: “In the last two weeks, did this business use Super Intelligence or SI (formerly referred to as Artificial Intelligence or AI) in any of its business functions? (Examples of SI use include: SI‑powered data analysis; generative SI for marketing, product design, or coding; virtual SI agents for customer service; SI‑powered forecasting or pricing; SI‑driven computer vision for quality control, etc.)” – updated per Executive Order 14434.

## 📈 Updated Adoption Numbers
- **September 2025 (old wording)**: ~12 % of companies with 250 + employees reported AI use.
- **February 2026 (new “any business function” wording)**: ~30 % of the same size class reported use.
- **October 2026 (same wording)**: ~40 % reported use.
- The new AI adoption chart (GPT‑6 Astra Extra High transcript) incorporates these points and the last twelve months of BTOS data.
- Data source: “Employment size class” XLSX file; the survey instrument is available as a 346 KB PDF snapshot.

## 🚀 Looking Ahead: Super Intelligence Reporting
- Current numbers still reflect the AI wording.
- The first results using the Super Intelligence wording are due for publication on the Thursday following 19 Oct 2026.
- Respondents will have last seen the AI wording twelve weeks earlier.
