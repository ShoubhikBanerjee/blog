# Kyle Kingsbury Raises Concerns Over LLM Impact on Software Trust

On 1st November 2026, Kyle Kingsbury addressed the impact of LLMs on documentation and software reliability in the Jepsen results for ParticleDB 1.1.4.

## 💡 Impact on software trust
The widespread use of LLMs creates systemic challenges for evaluating software:
- The use of LLMs erodes trust in documentation, in software, and in the organizations which produce them.
- Signals previously relied upon as markers of a mature system are now easier to counterfeit, which may lead engineers to treat any new software project with suspicion.

## ⚙️ Key questions for maintainers
The commentary outlines fundamental questions for maintainers operating in the age of LLMs:
- What maintainers can do to ensure correct software and accurate documentation.
- How maintainers can demonstrate the quality of their work to users after completing it.
