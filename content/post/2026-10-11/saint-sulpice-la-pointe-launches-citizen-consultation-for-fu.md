# Saint‑Sulpice‑la‑Pointe launches citizen consultation for future neighborhoods around the station

The municipality of Saint‑Sulpice‑la‑Pointe has opened a citizen consultation called **« Imaginez les futurs quartiers autour de la gare »** to shape the evolution of the area surrounding its train station.

## 📍 Overview
- Location: Saint‑Sulpice‑la‑Pointe, around the railway station.
- Project name: *Imaginez les futurs quartiers autour de la gare*.
- Goal: Co‑design the future services, public spaces, mobility options and living places that will meet residents’ needs.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Build a **shared vision** for this strategic sector that will undergo major changes in the coming years.
- Ensure future developments **respond to the expectations and needs of users**.
- Involve a wide range of stakeholders: residents, station users, merchants, associations, economic actors, institutional partners and elected officials.

## 🤝 Participation Process
- An **assemblée citoyenne** will be created so participants can exchange experiences, share uses of the neighbourhood and propose concrete solutions.
- Throughout the consultation (October 2026 – June 2027), multiple participation sessions will gather contributions.
- Anyone can submit ideas and proposals now via the questionnaire on the municipal website **saintsulpicelapointe.fr**.

## 📅 Timeline
| Phase | Dates |
|-------|-------|
| Consultation launch | October 2026 |
| Ongoing participation sessions | October 2026 – June 2027 |
| Final synthesis of proposals | June 2027 |

## 🗺️ Themes to Shape the Future
- **Halle aux grains** – multimodal exchange hub.
- **Habitat** – housing options.
- **Commerce** – local businesses.
- **Economic activities** – jobs and enterprises.
- **Services and public spaces** – daily life amenities.

## 📣 How to Get Involved
- Visit the municipal website **saintsulpicelapointe.fr**.
- Fill out the online questionnaire with your ideas.
- Attend the scheduled citizen assembly meetings and workshop sessions.
- Stay informed through municipal communications about upcoming participation dates.

The municipality hopes that this inclusive approach will produce a **collectively imagined** and **well‑aligned** development plan for the railway‑zone neighbourhood.
