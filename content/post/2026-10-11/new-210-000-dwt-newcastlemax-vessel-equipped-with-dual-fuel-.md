# New 210,000‑DWT Newcastlemax Vessel Equipped with Dual‑Fuel Ammonia Engine

On 10 October 2026 a 210,000‑DWT Newcastlemax vessel built by Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding Heavy Industry in China was launched with a dual‑fuel engine that can run on ammonia or conventional marine fuel.

## 🔍 Overview
- Built by Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding Heavy Industry in China.
- Size: 210,000 DWT Newcastlemax vessel.
- Powerplant: dual‑fuel engine capable of operating on ammonia or conventional marine fuel.
- Construction status: the vessel has completed.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Dual‑fuel capability offers flexibility between low‑carbon ammonia and traditional marine fuels.
- The vessel joins a broader industry focus on alternative marine fuels, as ports seek partnerships for green hydrogen, green ammonia and other sustainable fuels.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The ship adds capacity for large container volumes, echoing recent record operations at ports such as Lamu that highlight growing hub potential.
- Growing logistics demand in India – driven by manufacturing, exports and economic expansion – underscores the need for cleaner, efficient maritime solutions.
- Port initiatives to build ecosystems for production, storage and distribution of green fuels align with the introduction of ammonia‑capable ships.
