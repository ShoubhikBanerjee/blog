# Packt Publishing Launches Workshop to Build Private, Production‑Ready AI Assistants

Packt Publishing announced an intensive 4‑hour online workshop that teaches participants how to build and deploy a privacy‑focused AI assistant that runs entirely on their own infrastructure.

## 🔍 Overview
- Build a production‑ready, privacy‑focused AI assistant that runs entirely on your own infrastructure.
- The workshop is delivered online and lasts four hours.
- No cloud APIs or external dependencies are required, giving you full control over your data.

## 🛠️ How it Works
- Start with OpenClaw configuration to manage local large language models.
- Run and manage local LLMs using Docker Model Runner.
- Follow a complete deployment pipeline that includes security hardening and integration with messaging platforms such as WhatsApp or Telegram.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Duration: 4‑hour intensive online session.
- Requirements: Docker installed on your laptop; minimum 16 GB RAM recommended for smooth local inference.
- Outcome: A fully functional local AI assistant running on your machine.

## 🚀 What You’ll Get
- A working assistant running locally, configured with OpenClaw and Docker.
- Integration with your choice of messaging platform.
- Understanding of security and privacy implications.
- A reusable architecture you can adapt for future AI projects.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Most AI assistants today rely on external APIs, creating concerns around privacy, cost, and data control.
- This workshop teaches a different approach: building a private AI assistant that runs entirely on your local machine, giving you full ownership of infrastructure and data.
