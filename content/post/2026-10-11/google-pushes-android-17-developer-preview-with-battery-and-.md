# Google pushes Android 17 developer preview with battery and privacy enhancements

The Android ecosystem is busy this weekend as Google released a fresh update to the Android 17 developer preview, focusing on better battery life, background‑process control, and new privacy tweaks.

## 🔍 Overview
- Android 17 developer preview arrives with a revamped battery settings menu.
- Users can now see which background services consume the most power.
- A new Private Space folder lets you hide specific apps from the app drawer without uninstalling them.
- Notification snooze lets you silence alerts for one or two hours.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Battery Management**
  - Revamped battery settings menu.
  - Visibility of power‑hungry background services.
- **App Privacy**
  - Hide apps in the Private Space folder.
  - Google aims to help users find apps that behave badly.
- **Notification Control**
  - Swipe a notification to the side for a snooze option.

## 📱 Additional Android Updates
- **Google Maps** – Minor update adds the ability to save multiple custom routes as home‑screen shortcuts.
- **Google Photos** – Refresh includes a faster search index for quicker object and person searches.
- **Security Patch** – October security patch released for all supported Pixel devices, fixing three major flaws that could allow unauthorized media‑file access. Other manufacturers (OnePlus, Motorola) expected to roll it out by mid‑next week.

## 🚀 Availability
- The Android 17 preview is now available for developers.
- Google plans to open the beta to more devices soon.
- **Reminder:** Back up your data before installing major system updates; keeping copies of photos and documents is always wise.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Improved battery visibility helps users extend device runtime.
- Enhanced privacy controls give users more granular app management.
- Faster search in Google Photos and custom route shortcuts in Maps streamline everyday tasks.
- The October security patch addresses critical vulnerabilities, reinforcing device security across the ecosystem.
