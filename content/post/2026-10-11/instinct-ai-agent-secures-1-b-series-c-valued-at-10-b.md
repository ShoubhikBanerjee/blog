# Instinct AI Agent Secures $1 B Series C, Valued at $10 B

Instinct, a viral AI agent, announced a massive Series C financing round that values the company at $10 billion.

## 🔍 Overview
- Series C round size: $1 billion
- Company valuation post‑funding: $10 billion
- The deal is expected to close by the end of the year and is valued at $8.2 billion.

## 💰 Funding Details
- The round is anticipated to be Instinct’s last before its delayed public debut scheduled for 2027.
- A $300 million employee tender was co‑led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price.
- Flow Engineering, which is bringing the AI agent to hardware design, secured Roelof Botha as an angel investor and board member.

## 📅 Timeline
- Expected closing: by year’s end.
- Public debut: delayed to 2027, after this final private round.

## 📊 Why It Matters
- The size of the financing underscores strong investor confidence in agentic AI technology.
- The involvement of prominent investors such as Wellington, T. Rowe Price, and Roelof Botha signals broad institutional interest.
- Positioning the round as the final pre‑IPO raise suggests Instinct is gearing up for a public market entry after a period of rapid growth.
