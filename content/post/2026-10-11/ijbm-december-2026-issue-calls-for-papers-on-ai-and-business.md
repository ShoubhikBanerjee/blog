# IJBM December 2026 Issue Calls for Papers on AI and Business Topics

The International Journal of Business Management (IJBM) has announced a Call for Papers for its December 2026 issue, inviting original, unpublished research on a range of business and AI topics.

## 📢 Announcement
- Call for Papers: IJBM December 2026 Issue (Vol. 21, No. 6)

## 🗓️ Important Dates
- Posted on Oct 10, 2026
- Full Paper Submission Deadline: November 10, 2026
- Peer Review Notification: Within 4–10 weeks after submission
- Expected Publication: December 2026 issue (Vol. 19, No. 6), subject to peer review and final acceptance

## 🎯 Topics of Interest
- Business Strategy, Innovation, and Entrepreneurship
- Organizational Behavior, Leadership, and Human Resource Management
- Marketing Management and Consumer Insights
- Operations Management, Supply Chain, and Risk Management
- Corporate Governance, Business Ethics, and Corporate Social Responsibility
- Digital Transformation, AI in Business, and Data-Driven Decision Making
- Sustainable Business Models, ESG, and Green Management
- International Business, Globalization, and Cross-Cultural Management

## 📄 Submission Requirements
- Manuscripts must be original, unpublished, and not under review elsewhere.
- Submit via the Online Submission System or email to ijbm@ccsenet.org.
- Include a cover letter confirming manuscript originality and authorship, and any conflict-of-interest statement.
- All submissions are screened for plagiarism using iThenticate.
- All submissions undergo a double‑anonymized peer review by at least two independent external reviewers.

## 💰 Publication Fees
- Upon acceptance, authors are required to pay an Article Processing Charge (APC) of USD 300.
- The fee supports open‑access publishing, including copyediting, typesetting, DOI registration, and long‑term digital archiving.
