# Bryden Wood Expands Spanish Architecture Teams for Complex Data Centre Projects

Bryden Wood announced the expansion of its Barcelona and Madrid studios, hiring architects to support the delivery of large‑scale technical facilities across Europe and beyond.

## 🌍 Overview
- Expanding Spanish teams to support delivery of some of the world’s most complex and ambitious projects for leading technology companies, industrial organisations, and global investors.
- The role offers a unique opportunity to work on high‑profile, technically demanding projects that are shaping the future of the built environment.

## 📍 Locations & Integration
- Positions are based in the Barcelona or Madrid studios.
- Architects will be fully integrated into the international Architectural team and will work closely with colleagues across the UK headquarters and global project teams.

## 🛠️ Role & Responsibilities
- Collaborate with experts in Architecture, Structures, Civils, MEP, Sustainability, Digital Design, and Project Management to develop innovative, coordinated, and efficient design solutions from concept through to delivery.
- Focus on technical design development, multidisciplinary coordination, BIM‑led delivery, permitting, and the production of high‑quality construction documentation.
- Produce comprehensive architectural documentation, including floor plans, elevations, sections, schedules, and detailed construction packages that integrate with structural and building services systems.
- Support and drive architectural design development from concept through detailed design (RIBA Stages 2–6 or equivalent), transforming design intent into coordinated technical solutions and tender documentation.
- Ensure designs comply with local building regulations, codes, and international standards, adapting technical approaches to suit project‑specific jurisdictions.
- Assist in the preparation and coordination of permit and statutory approval documentation for submission to local authorities and professional bodies across multiple international locations.
- Participate actively in design reviews, coordination workshops, and clash detection/resolution processes.
- Contribute to the delivery of complex Data Centres and large‑scale manufacturing, industrial and technology projects, maintaining high standards of design quality, technical excellence, and project performance.

## 🎓 Requirements
- Master’s Degree in Architecture (or equivalent 5‑year professional qualification).
- Professional registration with a recognised architectural body (e.g., ARB, Colegio de Arquitectos, or international equivalent) required or actively being pursued.
- Minimum 2–5 years post‑qualification experience within architecture, with a strong track record of producing and coordinating detailed technical design and construction documentation.
- Strong experience in architectural technical design development and coordination, particularly across RIBA Stages 3 and 4 (or equivalent).
- Proven ability to interpret, research, and apply a broad range of local building regulations, planning requirements, and international design standards.
- Demonstrable experience supporting the preparation, coordination, and submission of statutory approvals, permit applications, and technical documentation to local authorities across multiple territories.
- Experience delivering large‑scale industrial, infrastructure, data centre, pharmaceutical, manufacturing, logistics, or other technically complex commercial projects.
- Previous data‑centre experience is advantageous but not essential; sector‑specific training and support will be provided.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Joining Bryden Wood means becoming part of a practice that is redefining how buildings are designed and delivered through design excellence, digital innovation, modern methods of construction, and industrialised design principles.
- The teams work at the forefront of the industry, helping clients solve major challenges while delivering projects that are smarter, more sustainable, and more efficient.
