# Memorial Coliseum Ticketing, Parking, and Accessibility Guidelines Updated

The Memorial Coliseum has released a set of detailed guidelines covering show hours, ticket verification, parking rates, accessibility accommodations, photography rules, and child admission policies.

## 📅 Show Hours & Admission
- Shows run from **10 AM to 4 PM**.
- Children **3 years and under are free** for most events.
- Children **under 2 years are free** unless a separate seat is requested.
- Rules for children can vary by event; verify the individual event for specifics.

## 🎟️ Ticketing Details
- Ticket office phone: **(260) 483‑1111**.
- Doors open **2 hours prior to the event**.
- To pick up tickets, present **one** of the following:
  - Photo ID
  - Confirmation number
  - Credit card used for the purchase

## 🚗 Parking Rates
| Parking Area | Rate per Vehicle |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Main Lot | $8.00 |
| Preferred Lot | $12.00 |
| Buses, RVs, Limos | $16.00 |

## ♿ Accessibility
- **Accessible seating** is available at all purchase points.
- Seats are **reserved for sight‑ or hearing‑impaired patrons**.
- Special amenities include **hearing devices** and **sign‑language interpreters**.
- For assistance, contact the MidWest America Ticket Office at **260‑483‑1111**.
- TTY assistance is reachable at **260‑480‑2108**.

## 📸 Camera & Recording Policy
- **Still‑photo cameras** are permitted for certain events.
- **Video cameras and recording devices** are prohibited for most events.
- For event‑specific policies, visit **www.memorialcoliseum.com**.

## 📞 Contact Information
- General ticketing inquiries: **(260) 483‑1111**.
- Special‑amenities requests: **(260) 483‑1111**.
- TTY machine: **(260) 480‑2108**.

*All information reflects the latest guidelines as of October 10, 2026.*
