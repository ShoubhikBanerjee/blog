# A new unified romanization scheme for Thai and Lao languages

A new research paper introduces a phonemically comprehensive, ASCII-only romanization scheme for Thai and Lao, treating the two languages as a unified cross-lingual design problem.

## 🧩 How it works

The scheme establishes a systematic correspondence between Thai and Lao while maintaining one-symbol-one-phoneme transparency. It achieves this by representing segmental contrasts, vowel length, and lexical tone.

## ⚙️ Key details

*   **Phonetic Alignment:** Prioritizes synchronic phonetic correspondence, including alignment with Pinyin and Jyutping where applicable, while preserving historical-phonological correspondence.
*   **Tone Representation:** Utilizes a compact single-digit default notation, with optional support for tone-value and historical tone-category representations.
*   **Design Constraints:** Provides an ASCII-only format that is both readable and keyboard-friendly.

## 💡 Why it matters

The scheme creates a machine-processable phonemic representation suitable for language learning and cross-lingual speech processing applications.
