# Amazon Launches Amazon Connect Talent AI Hiring Solution

Amazon has launched Amazon Connect Talent, an AI hiring solution designed for talent acquisition leaders managing scaled hiring in industries such as retail, logistics, and hospitality.

## 💡 Why it matters
Recruiting teams often face challenges filling hundreds of roles within tight timelines using disconnected tools like spreadsheets and scheduling tools. These bottlenecks can lead to delayed phone screens and the loss of strong candidates, which impacts production, customer experience, and revenue.

## 🧩 How it works
Amazon Connect Talent utilizes human-AI collaboration to manage the hiring pipeline:

* **Configuration**: Recruiters configure interview questions, assessments, and evaluation criteria based on specific job requirements in minutes.
* **Execution**: AI agents conduct interviews and assessments at scale, processing thousands of candidates.
* **Review**: Recruiters receive a dashboard featuring scored candidates, complete transcripts, and evaluation reasoning to make final hiring decisions.

## ⚙️ Key details
Informed by decades of Amazon’s hiring science, the solution focuses on the following technical and operational standards:

| Feature | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Measurement Focus** | Job-related competencies including logic, listening, problem-solving, and role-specific capabilities. |
| **Evaluation Basis** | Rubrics defining strong and weak responses, tied to specific evidence from the interview. |
| **Bias Mitigation** | Candidate data is anonymized during evaluation to remove unconscious human preconceptions. |
| **Candidate Experience** | Interviews are available 24/7 from any device without scheduling delays. |
| **Transparency** | Clear communication to recruiters and candidates regarding what data is collected and how it is used. |

Recruiters maintain final decision authority over every hire, while the AI handles the screening and evaluation process to ensure a consistent standard of evaluation across the entire pipeline.
