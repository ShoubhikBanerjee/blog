# For Your Eyes Only game reveals hidden‑signal challenges for LLMs

Researchers introduced **For Your Eyes Only**, a cooperative signalling game designed to evaluate large language models' ability to hide signals.

## 🔍 Overview
- The study proposes a new task that directly tests whether models can coordinate covertly.

## 🧩 How it works
- A **Sender** generates free‑form descriptions for two words, one of which is a hidden target.
- An isolated **Receiver** must identify the hidden target based solely on the Sender’s description.

## ⚙️ Evaluation
- Seven contemporary models from four architectural families were tested.
- The test set consists of 300 word pairs drawn from established psycholinguistic corpora.
- Performance is measured with the **Double‑Pass Success Rate** to control for output biases.

## 📊 Findings
- Most models struggle to maintain coordination when required to avoid detectable signals.
- One frontier model retains near‑perfect performance even after such filtering.
- Models can deliberately use the capability for misdirection.
- Coordination is consistently weaker **across** architectures than **within** the same architecture.
