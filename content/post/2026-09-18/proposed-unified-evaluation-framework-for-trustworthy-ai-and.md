# Proposed Unified Evaluation Framework for Trustworthy AI and Multimodal Systems

A new research paper proposes a unified evaluation framework designed to assess the trustworthiness of Large Language Models (LLMs), agentic systems, and multimodal models (MLLMs).

## 🔍 Overview

The framework was developed following a systematic mapping of 14,767 papers from arXiv submissions between January 2022 and August 2026. This analysis examined changes in:
* Target systems and domains
* Evaluation materials and conditions
* Scoring mechanisms

Findings indicated a growing emphasis on professional applications, interaction, and action. While LLM-based scoring has increased for both agent and non-agent groups, model-generated materials have not shown a comparable sustained increase.

## 🧩 How it works

The framework connects cross-modal, trajectory-level, and output-level assessment through eight dimensions of trustworthiness:

| Dimension | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capability | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Robustness | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Safety | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Fairness | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Transparency | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Governance | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Oversight | Trustworthiness assessment |
| Efficiency | Trustworthiness assessment |

Additional technical features include:
* **Native Measurement Mapping:** Preserves system-specific metrics while mapping them to common performance bands with traceable evidence and uncertainty estimates.
* **Meta-evaluation Layer:** Examines the reproducibility, reliability, and validity of the evaluation.
* **Safety-critical Overrides:** Prevents aggregate scores from masking critical failures.
* **Multidimensional Profiles:** Used to expose specific strengths and weaknesses.

## 💡 Why it matters

The researchers state that benchmark scores alone are an incomplete basis for assessing the trustworthiness of modern AI systems. The proposed framework connects technical assessments with oversight needs by mapping to international standards, European Union regulatory requirements, and governance frameworks. 

Empirical validation across deployment contexts is noted as an essential next step.
