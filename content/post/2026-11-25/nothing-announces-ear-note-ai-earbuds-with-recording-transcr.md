# Nothing announces Ear Note AI earbuds with recording, transcribing, and summarizing features

Nothing has provided a detailed look at its upcoming AI earbuds, the Ear Note, ahead of their December 8th release. These earbuds can record calls, while their charging case can record, transcribe, and summarize conversations. The microphone-equipped case includes a "Talk" button for recording your own voice, and a new "Record" button for recording and transcribing conversations. According to a post on X, Nothing CEO Carl Pei states the button will allow users to record calls through the earbuds and meetings through the case as a "first step toward a hardware context layer for AI."

The buds will connect to an app for managing recordings, transcriptions, and AI summaries, and will support interactions with various AI agents, including a more basic voice-control system developed by Nothing, enabling back-and-forth conversations. The Ear Note buds resemble the Ear 3 and Ear 3A but offer Nothing's "best in-ear audio to date."

This new device joins other AI-powered note-taking products like the Amazon Bee and the Plaud Note Pro. Apple has also integrated AI capabilities into its Watch Series 12, allowing Siri to listen to conversations and generate recaps.
