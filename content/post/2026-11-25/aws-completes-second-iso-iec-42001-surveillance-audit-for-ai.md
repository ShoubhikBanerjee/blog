# AWS Completes Second ISO/IEC 42001 Surveillance Audit for AI Services

AWS successfully completed its second surveillance audit for ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the standard for Artificial Intelligence Management Systems (AIMS). This audit resulted in no findings and marks the addition of a new service to the certification program.

## ⚙️ Key Details

*   **Audit Timeline:** In November 2024, AWS was the first major cloud service provider to announce the ISO/IEC 42001 accredited certification for AI services. The second surveillance audit was completed in November 2026.
*   **Audit Result:** The audit concluded with no findings.
*   **New Coverage:** AWS DevOps Agent has been added as a new service covered under the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certification program.

## 📄 Services Under ISO/IEC 42001

The following services are covered under the AWS ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certification program:

| Service | Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| Amazon Bedrock | Originally certified in November 2024 |
| Amazon Q Business | Originally certified in November 2024 |
| Amazon Textract | Originally certified in November 2024 |
| Amazon Transcribe | Originally certified in November 2024 |
| AWS DevOps Agent | Added during the second surveillance audit in November 2026 |

## 💡 Why It Matters

This completion demonstrates the continuous commitment by AWS to responsible AI practices. With this independent validation, customers gain assurance regarding the commitment to responsible AI, which enables them to build and operate AI applications responsibly using AWS services.

## 🚀 Accessing Certifications

*   A full list of AWS services certified under ISO and CSA STAR is available on the AWS ISO and CSA STAR Certified page.
*   Customers can also access these certifications through the AWS Management Console for AWS Artifact.
