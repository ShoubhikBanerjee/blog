# CIA and FinCEN Report Cyberattacks Using ARTEX Framework

Unknown attackers breached an open-source intelligence (OSINT) analysis system at the US Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) and also affected the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. Additionally, the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) disclosed an investigation into unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems.

## 🔍 Overview
* The cyberattack was reportedly carried out using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework.
* The incident affected the CIA, the ODNI, and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency.
* FinCEN is a US Department of the Treasury bureau that acts as the country's official Financial Intelligence Unit and protects the nation's financial system from illicit activities.

## ⚙️ Key details
* The CIA and ODNI have not yet replied to a request for more details regarding the incident.
* No classified data or systems were exposed in the breach.
* A source stated, "The hack did not involve our classified networks or classified information, and no other systems are currently at risk."
* After detecting the attack, FinCEN took some systems offline as a precaution and told banks to use the Financial Institutions Hotline to file reports, since some services may be unavailable.
* It is unclear how the attackers gained access, when the breach occurred, or whether they stole any information from the compromised systems.

## 💡 Why it matters
* These incidents follow a series of other cyberattacks that have led to security breaches at other US federal agencies in recent years.
* One week ago, the US National Security Agency's classified network suffered a days-long outage, which the agency attributed to scheduled routine maintenance.
* Two years ago, the Silk Typhoon Chinese state-backed hacking group breached the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), the Treasury's Office of Financial Research, and the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States.
* Hackers reportedly breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC).
* The ShinyHunters extortion group claimed it breached the FBI's recruitment website, FBIJobs.gov, prompting an FBI investigation that led to the arrests of multiple suspected members.
