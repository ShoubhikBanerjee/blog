# Jensen Huang denies Trump's role in Nvidia reinstatement

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang addressed staff during his first all-hands meeting since returning to the company, explicitly denying that President Donald Trump or other political figures helped him regain his position. This update clarifies the circumstances surrounding Huang's reinstatement following his November resignation due to personal conduct allegations.

## 🗣️ Huang's Denial of Political Involvement
Huang reportedly told Nvidia staff that it is “not true” that Trump got him rehired. He dismissed claims that the President was directly involved in his reinstatement—narratives publicly promoted by Trump himself—as “completely untrue.”

*   Huang stated, “Don’t think for a second politics played a role in the company. We are the most apolitical company.”
*   He also rejected the idea that his return was due to “tech bro” culture or that he was “above the law” or “above the code of conduct.”
*   Huang described the surrounding narrative as the “most tortured” story in human history.

## ⚖️ Official Statements and Context
While Huang denied political interference, official statements from the White House suggest otherwise. The White House stated that Trump was “totally involved” in the decision to reinstate Huang.

*   **Trump’s Stance**: Trump confirmed working with Vice President JD Vance and other officials to bring Huang back. In a Truth Social post, he noted the government has “no reason to believe” Huang’s return poses a national security risk and that the US attorney general “does not possess evidence” of “unlawful conduct.”
*   **Tech Leader Support**: A swath of tech leaders, including Tesla CEO Elon Musk, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, and former White House AI czar David Sacks, favored Huang’s return.
*   **Board Decision**: According to the New York Times, the Nvidia board “evaluated the entire situation” while receiving assurance from the government that Huang posed no national security threat.

## 🔍 Ongoing Investigations and Consequences
Despite his reinstatement, legal and internal scrutiny continues. Nvidia is facing an investigation regarding its accounting practices, and the company is conducting its own internal investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a Chinese spy plot.

*   **Congressional Testimony**: Huang is scheduled to testify before Congress on December 1st regarding national security concerns. Democrats are planning to press him on his return and Trump’s involvement.
*   **Personal Accountability**: Huang acknowledged violating Nvidia’s code of conduct and admitted to making “terrible mistakes.” He stated, “I was unfaithful to Lori, and I hurt my kids and of course, I hurt all of you.”
*   **Current Status**: Huang is attending therapy daily. He also remarked that he “doesn’t believe in succession planning.”
