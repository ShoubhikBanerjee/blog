# GPT‑6.2 Astra Identifies 1808 Sketchbook Creator in 22 Minutes

In a recent demonstration, GPT‑6.2 Astra was able to autonomously identify the creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale’s Beinecke Library.

## 🔍 Overview
- With only minimal prompting, GPT‑6.2 Astra identified the sketchbook’s creator.
- The task was completed in just 22 minutes.
- The model used publicly available primary and secondary sources.

## 🧩 How It Works
- Astra discovered a little‑known source in a paper archive.
- It predicted that examining this source would prove its hypothesis.
- The source was confirmed to support the hypothesis.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The new source is a major find for Canadian and fur trade historians.
- Astra found a diary reference to a visit by the vicomte de Chabot dated five days before a date mentioned in the sketchbook at a nearby river.
- Astra suggested that Chabot's journal of his travels (available by order from Cleveland State University) might confirm that the sketchbook was his work.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- The achievement demonstrates how AI can perform tasks that human historians might not complete as quickly.
- It highlights the potential for AI to uncover new historical sources that have implications for specialized fields.
