# Amazon talks to end Meta Muse AI shopping block

Amazon has held talks regarding the potential unblocking of Meta’s Muse AI agent on its online marketplace. These discussions could remove Amazon’s current restriction on Muse, a move that contrasts with its previous stance on third-party AI shopping assistants.

## 🔍 Overview

According to a report from Fortune, Amazon and Meta have been in talks about allowing Muse to shop on Amazon’s platform. Previously, Amazon had been hesitant to open its digital doors to AI agents, unlike other online retailers such as Walmart, Best Buy, Gap, and Sephora, which have allowed similar tools. Amazon also blocked Perplexity’s AI agent, focusing instead on promoting its own Alexa for Shopping assistant.

## ⚙️ Key details

The core of the negotiations centers on several specific concerns raised by Amazon:

*   **Advertising Revenue:** Amazon depends on humans seeing ads across its marketplace. There is concern that an AI agent like Muse, which may not engage with ads in the same way, could negatively impact this revenue stream.
*   **Product Recommendation Quality:** Amazon is reportedly worried about the quality of the product search results that Muse presents to users.
*   **Customer Relationships:** There are concerns about how the AI agent might affect Amazon’s direct relationship with its customers.

## 🚀 Availability

Currently, Amazon blocks Muse AI from shopping on its site. The company has not officially confirmed if this policy will change following the reported talks. Neither Amazon nor Meta immediately responded to The Verge’s request for comment regarding the status of these discussions.
