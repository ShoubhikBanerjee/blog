# Tidal to Ban AI-Generated Music and Delete 29 Million Tracks

Tidal has announced it will officially ban AI-generated music starting December 9th. As part of this policy, the platform will delete approximately 29 million existing AI-generated songs and reject an estimated 80,000 to 85,000 new tracks per day, according to Tidal music lead Robert Andersen.

## 🔍 Overview
This move follows a shift from earlier efforts where Tidal began labeling and demonetizing tracks identified as 100 percent AI-generated. Andersen noted on X that the company needed to take a more “drastic” measure because AI music constituted 43 percent of the tracks distributed to the platform in the last 90 days. He added that he believes the ban will resonate with artists and listeners, as 96% of all AI-generated songs receive zero plays.

## 🧩 How it works
Tidal is implementing the ban by prohibiting music distributors from submitting AI songs. To enforce this, the platform uses the AI detection tool Ircam Amplify alongside metadata checks to reject submissions generated solely with AI. The policy explicitly distinguishes between wholly AI-generated output and music created with AI assistance, allowing the latter as long as AI does not constitute the “sole output.”

## ⚙️ Key details
The platform stated in a separate post on X that wholly AI-generated music intended to mislead listeners and impersonate artists is a serious problem that diverts streams and royalties away from genuine artists and confuses fans.

## 💡 Industry Context
Other music services are addressing the volume of AI songs with varying strategies, including applying new labels, removing tracks from user algorithms, or banning them entirely:

* **Bandcamp:** Banned AI music earlier this year.
* **Spotify:** Removed 50 million AI slop and spam tracks from recommendations over the past year and launched new “AI persona” badges for artists using an AI identity.
* **YouTube:** Rolled out AI music labels.
* **Apple Music:** Planning to launch AI labels this year.
* **Deezer:** Provides users with the option to filter out AI music.
