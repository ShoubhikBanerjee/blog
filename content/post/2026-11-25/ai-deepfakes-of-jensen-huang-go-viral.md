# AI Deepfakes of Jensen Huang Go Viral

AI-generated deepfakes of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang dancing in Ibiza have gone viral on Instagram, sparking memes about his cheating scandal.

## 🔍 Overview
- Jensen Huang, former CEO of Nvidia, is featured in AI-generated deepfakes dancing in Ibiza.
- The videos, with nearly 6 million views, are remakes of an existing meme called “Ibiza Final Boss.”
- Huang is swapped into the original video featuring a British construction worker.
- Other viral AI hits include Huang in scenes from The Office, George Michael’s “Careless Whisper,” and as the former Astronomer CEO.
- Unlike deepfake pornography or election disinformation, these AI videos are memes making fun of Huang’s cheating scandal.

## 🧩 How it works
- The AI videos rely heavily on Nvidia’s tech stack, including video diffusion models running on Nvidia GPUs.
- The creator used models like TikTok developer ByteDance’s Seedance 2.5 and Alibaba’s Wan 3.
- The Instagram account behind the Ibiza video has made three Huang deepfakes so far.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The creator is a 40-year-old man from Melbourne, Australia, who has spent “thousands” on AI video generation.
- The Huang deepfakes are among the most popular, alongside versions featuring Elizabeth Holmes and Amanda Seyfried.
- The creator says his Instagram page has been going “super viral” in the past two weeks, thanks to the Ibiza meme format.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The deepfakes highlight the irony of Huang’s scandal benefiting Nvidia by generating millions of AI memes.
- Jensen Huang was reinstated as Nvidia’s president, CEO, and board director following discussions with the U.S. government.
- Nvidia’s stock has surged since Huang’s reinstatement, and new AI Huang memes cast him in a victorious light.

## 📈 Impact
- The deepfakes have been viewed nearly 100 million times on various iterations featuring different celebrities.
- The creator aims to elevate content by putting news events into memes, which he believes will get traction.
