# Google launches AI Edge Foresight, an offline‑only note‑taking app for macOS

Google has released an experimental note‑taking app called **Google AI Edge Foresight** that can transcribe meetings and audio files entirely offline.

## 🔍 Overview
- Experimental app, free to use
- Runs on macOS using Google’s on‑device EmbeddingGemma 2 model
- Transcribes meetings completely offline

## 🧩 How it works
- Audio is processed by an on‑device model; no cloud connection is required
- The model runs locally on Macs with Apple Silicon

## ⚙️ Features
- Offline transcription of meetings and audio files
- Summarizes meetings while providing a space for personal notes, similar to Granola and Wispr Flow
- Turns shorthand bullet points entered during a meeting into polished notes based on the transcript
- Built‑in assistant lets you ask questions about the content you heard
- Ability to connect local files for the assistant to reference when answering questions
- Files, meeting audio, and notes never leave your computer

## 🚀 Availability
- Free to use
- Optimized for macOS on Apple Silicon Macs only

## 💡 Why it matters
- Operates entirely on‑device, keeping data private
- Provides a privacy‑focused alternative in the growing AI note‑taking market
