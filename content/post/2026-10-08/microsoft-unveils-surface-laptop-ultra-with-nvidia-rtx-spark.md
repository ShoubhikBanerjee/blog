# Microsoft unveils Surface Laptop Ultra with Nvidia RTX Spark and Hybrid Intelligence

Microsoft just wrapped up a big Windows and Surface‑focused keynote in San Francisco. The headline announcement was the Surface Laptop Ultra, a new laptop powered by Nvidia’s RTX Spark Arm‑based chip.

## 🔍 Overview
- Surface Laptop Ultra runs on Nvidia’s RTX Spark, an Arm‑based processor that can support up to 128 GB of unified memory.
- It ships with a 15‑inch HDR touchscreen that can reach up to 2,000 nits of peak brightness.
- The device replaces the old proprietary Surface Connect port with a magnetic USB‑C charging connector that Microsoft calls **Magnetic Connect**.
- Microsoft also announced the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, a developer‑focused mini PC that runs the same RTX Spark platform.

## 🧩 How it works
- **RTX Spark chip** – an Arm‑based processor designed for high‑performance creative software, gaming, and local AI tasks.
- **Hybrid Intelligence** – a Windows update that lets Copilot access files stored on the PC and perform actions. In a live demo, Copilot searched folders, renamed files, zipped them, and drafted an email with the attachment.
- **Dev Box** – equipped with 128 GB of unified memory, a 100‑watt thermal envelope and Nvidia Tensor cores, enabling local AI models exceeding 120 B parameters and a strong gaming capability.

## ⚙️ Key specifications
- **Display:** 15‑inch HDR touchscreen, up to 2,000 nits peak brightness
- **Memory (base config):** 24 GB RAM
- **Storage (base config):** 512 GB SSD
- **Charging:** Magnetic USB‑C (Magnetic Connect)
- **Trackpad:** Larger surface with haptic feedback
- **Processor:** Nvidia RTX Spark Arm‑based chip (up to 128 GB unified memory)

## 🚀 Availability
- **Surface Laptop Ultra** starts at **$2,599** for the base configuration and will be available on **October 16**.
- **Surface RTX Spark Dev Box** is available for preorder at **$5,999** and is slated to ship in **November**.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Both devices target the premium end of the laptop and PC market, offering hardware capable of running demanding creative apps, games, and on‑device AI workloads.
- The Hybrid Intelligence integration gives Copilot the ability to act on local files, expanding the usefulness of AI within Windows.
- The Dev Box’s developer‑optimized Windows 11 Pro setup and preinstalled tools (VS Code, Git, GitHub Copilot, WSL, Python, Node) make it a ready‑to‑use platform for AI model testing, prototyping agents, and on‑device coding assistance.
