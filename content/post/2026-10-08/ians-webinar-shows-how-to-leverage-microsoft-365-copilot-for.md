# IANS Webinar Shows How to Leverage Microsoft 365 Copilot for Cybersecurity

IANS hosted a live session where faculty member Shannon Lietz walked through practical ways to use Microsoft 365 Copilot in a security context.

## 🔍 Overview
- The webinar demonstrated how to **supercharge your cybersecurity practice** with M365 Copilot, drawing on the IANS MCP and insights from 170+ faculty experts.
- Attendees learned how to **use their own models and context** to extend Copilot so it is specifically useful in their environment.

## 🛠️ How it works
- **Most security teams already have Microsoft 365 Copilot.**
- **Far fewer have connected it to the expert guidance** that will help them optimize program initiatives.
- Copilot **is only as useful as the context you give it**, and generic answers don’t help when you’re building a security strategy, working through intricate security challenges, or developing a checklist for your environment.
- The session covered practical steps to provide relevant context, integrate internal models, and tailor Copilot responses to the organization’s security needs.

## 📚 Credentials
- **Attendees may earn up to 1 credit** through the partnership with ISC2.
- **Attendees will receive a Certificate of Completion within two weeks of the Webinar via email** for any other certification needs.
- If a CISSP number was provided during registration, **the credit will be automatically submitted to ISC2**.

## 🚀 Why it matters
- **Increased AI capabilities and adoption are beginning to shape how CISOs and other security executives think about structuring their teams.**
- Leveraging Copilot with proper context enables security teams to move beyond generic AI output and apply precise, actionable guidance to real‑world security programs.
