# AI Tools Challenge Trade Secret Protection, Says Legaltech Article

An article by intellectual property attorney Terry Wikberg in *Legaltech News* examines how artificial intelligence is reshaping trade secret protection and creating new risks for companies that rely on proprietary information.

## 🔍 Overview
- The piece discusses the ways AI, especially large language models (LLMs), can threaten trade secrets.
- It highlights the need for companies to reassess their secrecy measures in the AI era.

## 🧩 How AI Interacts with Trade Secrets
- LLMs and other AI tools can **aggregate, analyze, and infer information from public sources**.
- They can quickly **reconstruct valuable trade secrets** from seemingly unrelated disclosures such as marketing materials or conference presentations.

## ⚖️ Legal Implications
- Trade secret owners may face **heightened scrutiny of secrecy measures**.
- There is a **growing role of AI‑assisted reverse engineering** in uncovering protected information.
- AI capabilities affect the **"readily ascertainable" metric** used in trade secret defenses.

## 💡 Recommendations for Businesses
- **Audit public disclosures** to identify unintended exposure of proprietary data.
- **Strengthen internal controls** around how confidential information is shared and stored.
- **Proactively evaluate trade secret protection strategies** to address AI‑related risks.
