# Camtek Schedules Investor & Analyst Breakfast During SEMICON West

Camtek Ltd. announced that it will host an Investor & Analyst Breakfast Presentation on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, as part of SEMICON West in San Francisco.

## 📅 Event Details
- **Date & Time:** October 14, 2026, live webcast starting at approximately 7:20 am Pacific Time / 10:20 am Eastern Time.
- **Location:** SEMICON West, San Francisco (event hosted in conjunction with the conference).
- **Attendance:** Invitation‑only, intended for institutional investors and sell‑side analysts.
- **Registration:** Interested investors can register via the webcast link: https://event.summitcast.com/view/5d7e4doR58GH862Ss7d5Tu/guest_book?session_id=NNvKTQbv9qmyZSQhkTFLJc
- **Replay:** A replay will be posted on Camtek’s investor‑relations website after the event.
- **Contact:** Request an invitation by emailing [email protected].

## 👥 Speakers
- **Lior Aviram** – Executive Chairman of the Board
- **Rafi Amit** – Chief Executive Officer
- **Ramy Langer** – President & Chief Operating Officer
- **Moshe Eisenberg** – Chief Financial Officer
- **Eyal Segev** – Marketing Director

Each executive will present the company’s growth strategy, technology roadmap, and financial outlook, followed by a Q&A session.

## 🏭 About Camtek
- Camtek develops and manufactures high‑end inspection and metrology equipment for the semiconductor industry.
- Its systems inspect integrated circuits (IC) and measure IC features on wafers throughout production, from front‑end to post‑dicing stages.
- Target market segments include Advanced Interconnect Packaging, Heterogeneous Integration, Memory and HBM, CMOS Image Sensors, Compound Semiconductors, MEMS, and RF.
- Customers span leading global IDMs, OSATs, and foundries.
- Manufacturing facilities are located in Israel and Germany, complemented by eight offices worldwide.
