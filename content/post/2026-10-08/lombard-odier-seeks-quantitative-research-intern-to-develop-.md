# Lombard Odier Seeks Quantitative Research Intern to Develop AI Investment Tools

Lombard Odier is expanding its investment research capabilities through a new initiative to develop artificial intelligence (AI) and Large Language Model (LLM) tools. Based in Geneva, the firm is seeking a Quantitative Research Intern to help build AI-driven research tools and investment insights across both long-only and long-short equity and fixed income investment strategies.

## 🔍 Overview

Lombard Odier is an independently owned global wealth and asset manager with a history spanning over 230 years. The firm manages close to CHF 300 billion and operates from over 25 offices across four continents. Its asset management business, Lombard Odier Investment Managers (LOIM), is targeting the integration of modern machine learning and foundation models to enhance its investment research and decision-making processes.

## 🧩 How it works

The development focus centers on building AI systems that work closely with quantitative researchers, portfolio managers, and investment analysts. Key technical areas of development include:

* **Information Extraction and Synthesis:** Building systems to extract, summarize, and analyze unstructured datasets, including corporate filings, earnings calls, credit research, central bank communications, and news.
* **RAG and Agentic Workflows:** Designing and evaluating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and multi-agent workflows to automate parts of the investment research process.
* **Signal Creation:** Applying statistical and machine learning techniques to structured financial, macroeconomic, and market datasets to generate novel textual and alternative-data signals.
* **System Integration:** Prototyping tools that integrate internal databases, research repositories, and external data providers.

## 💡 Why it matters

By developing an AI-powered research assistant, Lombard Odier aims to identify material changes in corporate fundamentals, credit quality, and macroeconomic conditions. This initiative will evaluate the robustness, reliability, and investment relevance of these AI-generated insights compared to traditional quantitative signals and research methods, aiding the team's ongoing investment decision-making.
