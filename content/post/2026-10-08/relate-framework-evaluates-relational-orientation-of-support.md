# RELATE framework evaluates relational orientation of supportive LLMs

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used for emotional support, prompting researchers to examine how these systems orient users toward continued assistance. On 7 Oct 2026, the paper *RELATE: An Evaluation Framework for measuring Relational Orientation of Large Language Models* was submitted to the Computer Science > Computation and Language archive, introducing a new way to audit relational dynamics in supportive AI.

## 🔍 Overview
- Existing evaluations focus on safety, empathy, or helpfulness.
- RELATE targets the under‑examined *relational orientation* of LLMs, asking whether the model nudges users to rely on the AI (inward‑facing) or to seek real‑world human connections (outward‑scaffolding).

## 🧩 How it works
- The framework defines two non‑exclusive dimensions:
  | Dimension | Description |
  |---|---|
  | Inward‑Facing (IF) | Positions the AI as the user's ongoing source of support |
  | Outward‑Scaffolding (OS) | Encourages real‑world human connection |
- RELATE pairs 76 help‑seeking situations (adapted from natural questions) with three simulated user styles, yielding **228 evaluation stimuli**.
- Seven LLMs are run through multi‑turn dialogues (six assistant turns each), producing **1,596 dialogues** and **69,194 assistant sentences**.
- Sentence‑level labels are generated by a primary rubric‑based LLM judge; a secondary judge validates a subset.

## 📊 Key findings
- The proportion of IF‑labeled sentences **increases** from the first to the sixth assistant turn.
- OS‑labeled sentences are **substantially lower** for hesitant, indirect simulated users compared with explicit, reassurance‑seeking users.
- RELATE offers a reproducible, sentence‑level signal for auditing and steering the relational orientation of supportive LLMs.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a systematic way to monitor whether supportive AI encourages users to stay socially isolated or to reconnect with human contacts.
- Enables developers to adjust model behavior toward healthier relational outcomes.
- Supplies the research community with a shared benchmark for future studies on AI‑mediated emotional support.
