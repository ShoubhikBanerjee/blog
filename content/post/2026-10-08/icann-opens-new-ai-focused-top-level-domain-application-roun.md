# ICANN Opens New AI‑Focused Top‑Level Domain Application Round

For the first time in years, the Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers — better known as ICANN — is accepting applications for new top‑level domains (TLDs).

## 🔍 Overview
- ICANN announced the 1,615 applications that have been submitted so far, from 481 different applicants.
- The latest batch is dominated by AI‑related domain ideas.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Application volume**: 1,615 TLD applications submitted by 481 applicants.
- **Domain types**: Two broad categories – generic domains (hundreds) and branded domains (345 brand applications).
- **Generic examples**: .geek, .haha, .anime, .jackpot, .itiswhatitis.
- **Branded examples**: .applebees, .bankofamerica, .zillow, .zoom.
- **Brand‑specific requests**:
  - Meta applied for .facebook, .instagram, .messenger, .meta, .frommeta, and .threads.
  - Bluesky applied for .bsky.
  - OpenAI applied for .chatgpt, .codex, .openai, .gpt, and others (total of 15 TLDs).
  - Anthropic wants .anthropic and .claude.
- **Industry interest**: OpenAI applied for a total of 15 TLDs; Meta wants a whopping 21.

## 📊 AI‑Focused TLDs
| Domain | Applicants (count) | Notable applicants |
|--------|--------------------|---------------------|
| .agent | 10 | Meta, OpenAI |
| .agi | 7 | OpenAI |
| .asi | 6 | OpenAI |
| .intelligence | 4 | Meta |
| .superintelligence | 2 | Meta (one of two interested) |

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Even as AI pervades the digital landscape, memorable domain names continue to be valuable.
- The AI companies themselves consider domain branding important.
- Past alternate TLDs such as .ai or .app have seen moderate uptake, while many niche TLDs (e.g., .restaurant, .babysitting) remain rarely used.
- Historically, only Google and a few other firms have secured branded TLDs; the current surge shows broader brand interest.

## 🚀 Next Steps
- The application process involves several more steps; no TLD will be official before sometime next year.
- Continued submissions are expected as more companies seek AI‑related or brand‑specific domains.
