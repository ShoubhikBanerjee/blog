# Free Vibe Coding Workshop Series Teaches AI-Driven Software Creation

WIT // HSV and ARC are hosting a free, three‑part Vibe Coding Workshop Series that shows participants how to turn natural‑language prompts into working software using AI‑powered code generators.

## 🔍 Overview
- Hands‑on, immersive workshops (not lectures) that guide AI tools to generate, iterate, and improve code.
- Series consists of three 2‑hour modules for a total of 6 hours of instruction.
- Open to professionals, students, educators, creators, and curious minds.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Vibe coding** lets you build functional software simply by describing intent in natural language.
- Participants will explore cloud‑native AI app builders to turn ideas into interactive applications without writing code manually.

| Platform | Role |
|----------|------|
| Lovable | Cloud‑native AI app builder |
| Bolt.new | Cloud‑native AI app builder |
| Google AI Studio | Cloud‑native AI app builder |

- The curriculum advances from browser‑based low‑code platforms and AI agents, to cloud development environments with controlled code customization, and finally to fully local setups with open‑source tools and LLM architectures.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Free to attend** – no cost for participants.
- **Open to all** – no prerequisites or prior coding experience required.
- **Bring your own laptop** – required for the fully immersive experience.
- **Certificate of Completion** – awarded to attendees who complete all three workshops (a valuable addition to a resume or LinkedIn profile).
- **Workshop schedule**:
  - Prompt to Prototype – 10/8
  - Collaborative Cloud Coding – 10/22
  - Owning Your Code – 11/12
- **Spots are limited and fill fast**.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The barrier between having an idea and building something that works is disappearing.
- Natural language is becoming a development interface thanks to AI‑powered platforms and coding agents.
- The next generation of builders will be defined by the ability to clearly describe what should be built, not by years of programming experience.
- Vibe coding is changing who can create software.
