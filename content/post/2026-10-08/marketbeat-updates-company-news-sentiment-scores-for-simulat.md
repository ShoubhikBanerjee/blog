# MarketBeat Updates Company News Sentiment Scores for Simulations Plus, AMZE, and UNF

MarketBeat has refreshed its weekly company news sentiment metrics, adding the latest sentiment scores, article counts, and engagement data for three listed firms.

## 🔍 Overview
- MarketBeat assigns each news headline a sentiment score from **-2 (bad news) to +2 (good news)**.
- The platform aggregates these scores across all headlines mentioning a company to produce a **7‑day average news sentiment**.
- Tracking is performed across **hundreds of trusted media outlets in real time**, with headlines tagged by company.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Score assignment** – Every headline is evaluated and given a numeric score within the –2 to +2 range.
- **Sentiment aggregation** – Scores are averaged over the most recent seven days to create a company‑level sentiment metric.
- **Article tagging** – News articles are automatically tagged to the relevant company, enabling per‑company tracking.
- **Coverage comparison** – The system compares the number of articles published in the last seven days to a typical week for each company.
