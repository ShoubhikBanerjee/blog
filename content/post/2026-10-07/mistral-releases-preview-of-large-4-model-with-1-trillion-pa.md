# Mistral Releases Preview of Large 4 Model with 1 Trillion Parameters

Mistral released a preview of its new Mistral Large 4 model.

## 🔍 Overview
Mistral Large 4 is described as a "1 trillion parameter, 49 billion active parameter" model. It represents a significant step up from the previous Mistral Large 3, which scored 9 on Artificial Analysis.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Parameters**: 1 trillion total, 49 billion active
- **Training hardware**: Cluster of 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs
- **Reasoning levels (API)**: "none" and "high"
- **Scoring**: 38 on Artificial Analysis, just behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash (552 B model)
- **Comparison**: Improves from a score of 9 on the prior model

## 📊 Performance
| Reasoning Level | Output Tokens |
|-----------------|---------------|
| none            | 3,275 |
| high            | 2,717 |

The "high" reasoning output looked better despite using fewer tokens.

## 🚀 Availability
- The preview is accessible via the Mistral API.
- Open weights are promised to be released at the end of this month.

## 💡 Why it matters
Mistral Large 4 brings the company back to a position only about six months behind the current frontier, offering a higher‑scoring model that is not classified as a Fable‑class model.
