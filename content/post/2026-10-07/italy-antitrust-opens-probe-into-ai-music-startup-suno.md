# Italy Antitrust Opens Probe into AI Music Startup Suno

Italy's antitrust authority announced on Tuesday that it has opened an investigation into Suno, an AI‑powered music‑generation startup, over concerns that some clauses in its terms of service may be unfair to consumers.

## 🔍 Overview
- Investigation launched on Tuesday.
- Targeted company: Suno, an AI music startup.
- The focus is on potentially unfair clauses in Suno’s terms of service.

## 📣 Response
- Suno was not immediately available for comment.
