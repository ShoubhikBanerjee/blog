# AI Builders Community Announces Upcoming Technical Meetup in Hamburg

The AI Builders community has shared updates for its upcoming meetup in Hamburg on Wednesday, October 7. The event will focus on hands-on demos of AI agents, developer tools, and agent frameworks, hosting participants at the House of AI in Germany.

## 🔍 Overview
AI Builders is a self-organizing, nonprofit community of over 8,000 AI enthusiasts and more than 6,000 developers, founders, and engineers. The community organizes over 100 meetups and hackathons per year across Europe and the United States. Currently, the organization is building a democratic AI platform designed to be completely co-owned and co-governed by its attendees.

## ⚙️ Key details
The upcoming Hamburg event is organized with local hosts Jan Schnedler and Martin Zielinski. Key logistics and the evening schedule include:

* **Location**: House of AI, Hongkongstraße 2, 20457 Hamburg, Germany.
* **Date and Time**: Wednesday, October 7, starting at 18:00 and ending at 21:00.
* **Amenities**: Pizza and drinks will be available for attendees.

| Time | Session / Activity | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 18:00 | Walk-in | Event arrival |
| 19:00 | Something Special to Copy Maybe | Demo presentation |
| 19:25 | I Cloned My Boss | Demo presentation |
| 19:45 | The Voice Surface: Voice AI That Acts, Not Just Talks | Demo presentation |
| 20:10 | Beyond the Website: Professional Reinvention with Notion AI | Behind the scenes of building a new professional identity and business system with an interactive live experiment by Sascha Adam @ A-Volution |
| 20:20 | Drinks & Networking | Networking session with refreshments |
| 21:00 | End | Event conclusion |

## 🧩 Technical requirements
To fully participate in the meetup, organizers recommend that attendees have a basic understanding of LLM concepts. Key recommended topics include:

* AI Agents and Agent frameworks
* Embeddings
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* Model Context Protocols (MCPs)
* Inference
* Structured Output
* Finetuning
* APIs
* Reinforcement Learning

## 🚀 Participation and access
The event organizers have outlined several options for community involvement and attendance:

* **Presenting**: Attendees can message Jan to secure a stage slot. Community demos are allocated 10 minutes, and builder demos run for 15 to 20 minutes.
* **Support and Sponsored Tickets**: Those who need a sponsored ticket or want to volunteer by arriving 1.5 hours early to help set up can message Jan.
* **Media and Recording**: Some sessions will be recorded and streamed live. By attending the event, participants consent to being photographed and filmed.
