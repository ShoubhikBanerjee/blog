# Tokyo Fun Language Exchange – Multilingual Meetup in Tokyo

Welcome to Tokyo Fun Language Exchange – a regular meetup where participants practice a variety of languages in small groups.

## 🗓️ Overview
- **Official start time:** 7.35 pm (arrive **before 7.30 pm** for name registration and payment).
- **Event fee:** ¥1000.
- **Frequency:** Events are held regularly; participants are encouraged to attend at least twice a month.
- **Group size:** Four sessions of 25‑30 minutes each, with groups of two or three people. No large‑group sessions.

## 📋 Participation Guidelines
- **All abilities are welcome.** Beginner? No problem.
- **No direct contact requests** (Line, Facebook, Instagram, etc.) unless you have met several times.
- If you wish to share information (e.g., webpage details), **write it on paper**.
- The organiser will **announce the language** for you to practice; change language only to help explain something quickly.
- You may need to act like a teacher if other participants are weaker.

## 📢 Organizer Role & Community Conduct
- The organiser **coordinates the event**; the event succeeds based upon the attendees.
- If you have an issue with another attendee, **inform the organiser directly**.
- After the event, you may **submit a review/evaluation**; low evaluations impact the group.
- **Cancellation policy:** Late cancellation (within 3 hours of start) or no‑show with non‑payment via PayPay within 24 hours may result in removal from the group.
