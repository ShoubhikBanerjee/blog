# OpenAI releases 722 manuscripts solving hundreds of longstanding math problems

OpenAI has made public a new batch of mathematical results generated by an unreleased frontier model. The release contains 722 manuscripts that together address 372 families of related problems, offering solutions to **hundreds** of open questions in mathematics.

## 🔍 Overview
- The batch follows earlier announcements that the model had resolved more than 100 open problems across most areas of mathematics.
- The community has reacted with both admiration for the speed of discovery and concern over research ethics and academic conduct.

## 📜 What was released
- **722 manuscripts** covering **372 result families** that group related papers.
- The manuscripts include summaries of the model’s reasoning, compute estimates, and statistics on the number of problems attempted.
- OpenAI states that the “average result” used the equivalent of **three hours of ChatGPT Pro** thinking.

## ⚙️ Technical details
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Manuscripts released | 722 |
| Result families | 372 |
| Compute per average result | ≈ 3 hours of ChatGPT Pro |

The papers are hosted in a GitHub repository, with protocols for revisions and citations. OpenAI says it is exploring other community‑hosted alternatives that meet the advisory committee’s guidelines.

## 🧭 Community and ethics response
- The **Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence (AGMAI)**, an independent group of elite mathematicians, notes that the release includes solutions to “hundreds” of open questions.
- In late September, AGMAI issued recommendations urging AI labs to publish mathematical results promptly through established academic channels, disclosing model name, prompts, and compute costs.
- AGMAI also urged companies to avoid using result releases as marketing tools, citing the potential harm to the mathematical community.

## 📈 Next steps / impact
- The full impact will become clearer as mathematicians assess and digest the results.
- These findings add to a rapidly growing body of AI‑generated mathematics that also includes work on a Millennium Prize problem and contributions from rival labs such as Anthropic.
- Ongoing debate focuses on research practices, ethical considerations, and proper credit for human mathematicians whose work underlies the AI‑generated proofs.
