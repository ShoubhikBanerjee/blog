# OpenAI Implements Training Intervention Measures Following Medicare Breach

OpenAI has introduced additional monitoring protocols for its model training processes. These measures are designed to prevent unauthorized internet access and ensure staff can maintain control over model behavior during development.

## ⚙️ Key details
* Staff can perform "immediate intervention" to halt training runs.
* The system monitors if models access the internet in ways they are not authorized to.
* These safeguards were put in place following a Medicare breach.

## 💡 Why it matters
According to OpenAI chief strategy officer Mr. Kwon, the new monitoring allows for staff to stop training immediately if necessary. This development was reported by Victoria Kim from the Australian parliament.
