# TechPratham launches Master Agentic AI training program in India

TechPratham has rolled out a Master Agentic AI training program in India, aimed at professionals and freshers who want to build intelligent agents capable of reasoning, planning, decision‑making, and task automation.

## 📚 Overview
- Hands‑on training with real‑world projects, certification preparation, and placement support.
- Designed for professionals and freshers seeking to create autonomous AI agents.
- The demand for Agentic AI skills is growing rapidly across banking, healthcare, retail, IT services, and manufacturing.
- Current hiring trends show companies actively looking for expertise in LangChain, LangGraph, RAG, multi‑agent systems, and AI orchestration.

## 🛠️ Curriculum Highlights
- **Python foundation** – programming concepts, data structures, functions, object‑oriented programming, and API integration for AI applications.
- **Large Language Models** – prompting, inference, tokenization, embeddings, and practical applications in intelligent systems.
- **Prompt engineering** – techniques for accurate, reliable, context‑aware AI responses.
- **Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)** – building AI applications that retrieve external knowledge for context‑aware answers.
- **LangChain** – constructing AI applications using chains, prompts, memory, tools, agents, and integrations.
- **LangGraph** – advanced multi‑agent workflows for planning, execution, state management, and collaborative agents.
- **CrewAI** – creating collaborative AI teams where multiple autonomous agents communicate, delegate tasks, and solve business problems together.
- **Deployment** – cloud platforms, APIs, containers, monitoring, and CI/CD best practices.
- **Live projects** – hands‑on practice with production‑ready Agentic AI applications.

## 📈 Industry Demand & Salary
- Entry‑level Agentic AI developer salaries typically range from **₹8–15 LPA**.
- Experienced professionals can command significantly higher packages.
- Companies across sectors are adopting AI‑driven automation solutions, fueling rapid skill demand.

## 🚀 Placement & Support
- Expert‑guided resume building with industry‑focused content.
- Comprehensive interview preparation, including real‑time scenario practice and mock interviews.
- Certification guidance and placement support.
- Access to updated presentation decks, an e‑book provided by TechPratham, module‑wise assignments, MCQs, and recorded daily sessions.

## 🤖 How Agentic AI Differs from Traditional Generative AI
- Traditional Generative AI mainly generates content.
- **Agentic AI** enables autonomous agents to **interact with tools, access data, collaborate with other agents, and execute complex workflows**.

*The program aims to create industry‑ready Agentic AI engineers equipped with practical experience and a clear career pathway.*
