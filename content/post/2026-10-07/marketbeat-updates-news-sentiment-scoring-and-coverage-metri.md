# MarketBeat updates news sentiment scoring and coverage metrics

MarketBeat has refreshed its real‑time news sentiment platform, providing clearer scoring for headlines and new comparative coverage data for individual companies.

## 🔍 Overview
- Extended trading is trading that happens on electronic markets outside of regular trading hours.
- This is a fair market value extended hours price provided by Massive.

## ⚙️ How it works
- Each headline receives a score ranging from **2** (good news) to **-2** (bad news).
- Company news sentiment scores track the **average news sentiment of articles** about each company over the most recent **7 days** to identify firms receiving positive press.
- The system **tracks news headlines from hundreds of news outlets** and tags them by company.

- **MKTX** sentiment is below the finance sector average, with fewer articles this week than its typical weekly count.
- **CCS** sentiment is also below its consumer‑discretionary sector average, and article volume this week is lower than the usual weekly volume.

## 📈 Media Coverage Comparison
- A chart compares the **number of articles** about each company in the last seven days with the **average number of articles** for a typical week.
- The platform highlights when coverage deviates up or down, helping investors spot changes in media attention.
