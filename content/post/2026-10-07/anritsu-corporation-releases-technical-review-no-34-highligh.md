# Anritsu Corporation Releases Technical Review No. 34 Highlighting AI and Engineering Research

ANRITSU CORPORATION announced the publication of Anritsu Technical Review No. 34 on October 7, 2026. This publication is the English edition of Japanese Issue No. 101 and compiles research and development achievements from Anritsu researchers and engineers.

## 🔍 Overview
The technical review is designed to share expertise and knowledge both inside and outside the company. By presenting research across diverse fields, the publication aims to promote technical exchange and the creation of new value.

## ⚙️ Key details
This issue, dated September 2026, covers development platforms and research in several key areas:
* Communications test and measurement
* Food and pharmaceutical inspection
* Advanced materials and devices
* AI and security
* EV powertrain evaluation

| Research Title | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- |
| Trends and Overview of Evaluation Techniques in Image Recognition AI | AI and security |
| Development of X-ray Inspection System for Saving Labor and Effort | Food and pharmaceutical inspection |
| Development of Test Solution for Next-Generation eCall | Communications test and measurement |
| Development of 200G/Lane 60 GHz Optical Sampling Oscilloscope | Communications test and measurement |
| Technical Demonstration of EV Powertrain Evaluation Combining Simulation and Real Power Supply | EV powertrain evaluation |

## 🚀 Availability
The complete issue of Anritsu Technical Review No. 34 is available for download. Additionally, the Anritsu Technical Review Archive provides access to previous publications.
