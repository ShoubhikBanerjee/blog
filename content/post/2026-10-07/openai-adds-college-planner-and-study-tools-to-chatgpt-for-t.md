# OpenAI adds College Planner and study tools to ChatGPT for Teens

OpenAI is expanding its ChatGPT for Teens mode—first launched in August with safeguards and break reminders—by adding a College Planner tool and new studying features.

## 🔍 Overview
ChatGPT’s new College Planner will help users keep track of application and financial‑aid deadlines, while additional study‑related updates let teens upload multiple photos on iOS and generate flashcards from notes.

## 🧩 How it works
- **College Planner** – "College Planner brings together application requirements, deadlines, tasks, and financial‑aid steps for schools on a student’s list in one plan," OpenAI says. Students can return to the plan, update progress, use the timeline to plan ahead, and ask ChatGPT questions such as "what a requirement means" or "how to get started."
- **iOS multi‑photo capture** – Users can now use a continuous, multi‑photo capture to upload a bunch of photos at once and then turn them into one PDF.
- **Flashcard generator** – ChatGPT will also be able to make flashcards that can be generated from a user’s notes.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Target audience: students in grades **10 through 12** planning to attend a four‑year college in the United States.
- The mode was introduced in **August** with built‑in safeguards and break reminders.
- OpenAI claims that, on average, teens spend **under 15 minutes a day** using ChatGPT.

## 🚀 Availability
- The College Planner feature will arrive **"soon"** according to OpenAI.
- The new studying tools are already available to iOS users.

## 💡 Why it matters
The updates come shortly after **Common Sense Media** labeled ChatGPT for Teens an “unacceptable risk,” noting that the AI tool "still does kids’ homework" and does not send alerts to parents when it should. OpenAI’s addition of structured planning and study aids aims to address concerns while keeping teen usage brief.

## 📊 Feature Summary
| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| College Planner | Brings together application requirements, deadlines, tasks, and financial‑aid steps for schools on a student’s list; lets students track progress, plan ahead, and ask ChatGPT questions |
| Multi‑photo capture (iOS) | Allows continuous capture of multiple photos and converts them into a single PDF |
| Flashcard generator | Generates flashcards from a user’s notes |
