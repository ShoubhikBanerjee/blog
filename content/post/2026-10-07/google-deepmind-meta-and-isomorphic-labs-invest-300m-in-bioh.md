# Google DeepMind, Meta, and Isomorphic Labs invest $300M in Biohub AI Initiative

Google DeepMind, Meta, and AI drug‑discovery startup Isomorphic Labs are jointly putting $300 million into Biohub, the nonprofit biomedical research organization founded by Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan. The money is part of a larger $1.8 billion effort to build AI datasets that let researchers ask, predict, and answer biological questions digitally, advancing a long‑term goal of a “virtual cell.”

## 🔍 Overview
- Biohub was founded in 2016 to combat disease through large‑scale data‑driven biology.
- The new investment will expand Biohub’s AI dataset creation to support a digital “virtual cell” that can be used for simulations.

## 💰 Funding & Partners
- **$300 million** joint investment from Google DeepMind, Meta, and Isomorphic Labs.
- **$1.8 billion** overall initiative to build AI datasets for biological research.
- **> $500 million** from the U.S. Department of Energy over the next five years.
- **> $500 million** in NIH‑contributed datasets, repositories, and knowledge bases from prior federal investment.
- Additional “millions” from Google toward the virtual‑cell effort.

## 🧬 Goal: Virtual Cell & AI Datasets
- Create AI‑driven datasets that enable scientists to **ask, predict, and answer** biological questions digitally.
- Build a predictive model of biology that lets researchers perform experiments in silico.
- Support disease prevention and management by allowing large‑scale computational simulations.

## 🗣️ Voices
> “An accurate predictive model of biology could dramatically accelerate scientific discovery by enabling scientists to perform experiments digitally.” – Alex Rives, Biohub head of science

> “The creation of a virtual cell is one of the most important challenges for the next era of science. It will require coordinated data generation efforts at a national and international scale, which is why these partners are coming together.”
