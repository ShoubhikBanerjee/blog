# AI-Generated Software Blog Posts Growing, Readers Crave Personality

Developers are increasingly delegating their software‑blog writing to AI, and the result is becoming bland and homogenous. At the same time, readers are explicitly asking for more personality in technical posts.

## 📈 Observation
- "With so many developers delegating their writing to AI, software blogging is becoming bland and homogenous."
- "Readers are hungry for writing with personality."

## 🧭 Guidance from Michael Lynch
- **Avoid meandering intros** – Michael warns against "meandering intros".
- **Know your audience** – Do not misjudge the reader's existing knowledge.
- **Don’t assume prior reading** – Avoid assuming readers have read your previous posts.
- **Skip excessive formality** – Beginner bloggers often believe they must write in a stiff, overly formal way, but Michael says, "Just write the way you talk."
- **Limit reliance on links** – Overreliance on links can become an excuse not to explain terminology. Michael notes, "My rule of thumb is that my article should still make sense to the reader even if they don't click any links."
- **Link click‑through reality** – "I do this all the time, but I have a nagging suspicion that almost nobody ever clicks on them."

## ✍️ Practical Tips for Adding Personality
- Use your own voice; write as you speak.
- Keep introductions focused and on‑point.
- Explain terms directly instead of sending readers away.
- Assume the reader may be encountering the topic for the first time.
- Maintain a conversational tone rather than a formal lecture.

These points together highlight a growing tension: AI‑assisted content creation is streamlining production but also stripping away the human flavor that technical readers value.
