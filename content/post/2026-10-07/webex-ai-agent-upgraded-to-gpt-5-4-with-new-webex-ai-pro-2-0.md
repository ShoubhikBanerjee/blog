# Webex AI Agent upgraded to GPT-5.4 with new Webex AI Pro 2.0 engines

Webex Contact Center announced a major upgrade for its AI Agent, moving the underlying model from GPT‑4.1 to GPT‑5.4.

## 🔍 Overview
The upgrade aims to provide more accurate, natural, and goal‑aligned conversations for end‑customers.

## 🆕 What’s New
- Underlying model now GPT‑5.4 (previously GPT‑4.1).
- Enhanced accuracy, more natural dialogue, and better prompt adherence.

## ⚙️ Engine Options
| Engine Variant | Region |
|---------------|--------|
| Webex AI Pro 2.0 | Global |
| Webex AI Pro US 2.0 | United States |
| Webex AI Pro EU 2.0 | Europe |

## 🚀 How to Upgrade
- Existing agents should be upgraded to version **2.0** to access the new capabilities.
- Support for version **1.0** engines will end on **February 15, 2027**.

## 💡 Why it Matters
- **Greater accuracy** helps resolve customer issues faster.
- **More natural conversations** improve the overall user experience.
- **Improved prompt adherence** keeps the AI aligned with specific business goals.

Upgrade now to leverage the latest AI performance in your contact‑center workflows.
