# Crimson Harvest Event Launches Blood Raider Assault on New Eden

On June 9, 2026, CCP Games launched the Crimson Harvest event, marking a new assault by the Blood Raiders across New Eden and turning Pochven into a contested battleground.

## 🔍 Overview
- The Blood Raiders have once again launched an assault on New Eden.
- This year, in addition to their normal harvest, Pochven has become a battleground.
- The event’s initial release date was 2026‑06‑09, with the latest update on 2026‑10‑06.

## ⚔️ Factions & Alignments
- Capsuleers can choose to align with either the **Blood Raiders** or the **Order of St. Tetrimon**.
- **Blood Raiders** seek to learn the secrets of Triglavian biotech and establish a permanent foothold in Pochven.
- **Order of St. Tetrimon** seeks to thwart the Blood Raiders; if they prove their fitness, Triglavians may retreat from some Minor Victory Systems in Amarr space.
- Alignment also determines which SKINs capsuleers receive as they progress the reward track.

## 🎁 Rewards & Progression
- The reward track includes a blueprint copy for the new **Akoman Blood Raider Attack Battlecruiser**.
- Capsuleers earn tokens that can be exchanged at item traders for additional rewards.
- Rewards include:
  - SKINs
  - New and returning Officer modules
  - Remote armor repairer mutaplasmids
  - More items
- Traders are located in **Raravoss, Vale, Otela, Ahtila, Sakenta, Gammel, Sosala, Kamela, Anila, Ibani, Sakenta, and Shera**.
- Daily login rewards include Skill Points, boosters, SKINs, and more.

## 🌌 New Gameplay Elements
- **Crimson Gauntlet** and **Tetrimon Base** combat sites can become flashpoints; rifts may open, spilling Triglavians, Rogue Drones, and Drifters.
- Destroying these invasions grants additional rewards, though it does not affect standings.
- In YC127, capsuleers suppressed Hollow Clones in Biocybernetic Incident sites, but new outbreaks have appeared in Hollow Outbreak and Origin sites across New Eden.
- Recover **Criterion Ciphers** to access new **Criterion of Fitness** sites, featuring three waves of challenges that increase in difficulty based on efficiency.
- New Pochven wormholes have begun appearing as staging points for both factions, located in Blood Raider‑controlled systems in the Delve region and in the Eugidi, Angils, and Essin constellations. They function like normal Extra‑Pochven wormholes with escalating phases and roaming enemies.

## 📅 Timeline & Updates
- Outside high‑sec, capsuleer ships drop 90% of their loot, and pods have a 50% implant drop chance.
- The Crimson Harvest reward track is extended and escalates in the final week.
- Localization for Crimson Harvest will arrive on Wednesday **10.07.2026**.
