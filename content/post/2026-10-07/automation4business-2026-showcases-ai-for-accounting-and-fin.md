# Automation4Business 2026 Showcases AI for Accounting and Finance

Automation4Business 2026 took place on 9 November 2026 at Cubex Centre Prague, bringing together professionals and solution providers focused on automation, digitalisation, and modern technologies for accounting, finance, and HR.

## 🔍 Overview
- Visitors will gain valuable insights from experts, students, and representatives of international schools and universities.
- The programme offers practical insights into implementing AI, automating routine processes, digitalising accounting and improving business management efficiency.
- Short, dynamic presentations focus on technology, automation, AI and the digitalisation of business processes.
- The EXPO STAGE features experts from technology companies, accounting firms and the financial sector.
- Participants can look forward to inspiring presentations, case studies, expert discussions, and practical workshops.

## 🛠️ Programme Highlights
- A specialised programme focuses on digitalisation, automation and the modern operation of accounting firms and finance teams.
- The programme provides a detailed look at the practical use of technology, real‑world business experience and opportunities for expert discussion.
- Demonstrations include API calls.

## 🤖 AI‑Focused Sessions
| Session | Leader(s) | Format |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Robotics Zone | Michal Kříž (HandyBot) | Three sessions |
| Reporting AI Lab | Denisa Žďárská (Digipartner.ai & AI ve financích) and Martin Plachý (REKAP) | Two sessions |

- In the Robotics Zone participants receive hands‑on experience with the Microsoft Power Automate Desktop automation platform, working with desktop and web applications, email, Excel and SharePoint data.
- The Reporting AI Lab combines working with business data, reporting and the practical use of artificial intelligence.

## 🏢 Venue & Sustainability
- Cubex Centre Prague is Prague’s only venue in a LEED Platinum‑certified building, using 100 % sustainable electricity, rain‑water flushing and eco‑friendly catering.
- Recognised by leading publications and industry insiders for innovation, eco‑conscious operations and premium event execution.
- The venue can feature up to 360° panoramic projections, LED walls and cutting‑edge AV technology through AV MEDIA.
- Located in central Prague with excellent transport connections; Václav Havel Airport is 30 minutes away.
- As part of the Prague Event HUB, the venue provides access to 441 hotel rooms in Panorama by Verdi Hotels, a 2‑minute walk away.

## 📅 How to Attend
- The event provides an opportunity to gain new insights, share experiences, and explore technologies that help organisations automate processes, improve efficiency, and support sustainable growth.
- To secure preferred dates for large or seasonal events, booking 6–12 months in advance is recommended.
- Interested parties can schedule a guided tour of the flexible event spaces and advanced technologies by contacting rezervace@cubexcentrum.cz.
- The dedicated coordination team assists with planning, logistics and technical setup for hybrid or fully virtual experiences.
