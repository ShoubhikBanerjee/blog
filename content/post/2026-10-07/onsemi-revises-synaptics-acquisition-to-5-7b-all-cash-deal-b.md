# onsemi revises Synaptics acquisition to $5.7B all‑cash deal, boosting AI‑related power strategy

onsemi (NASDAQ: ON) announced a revised acquisition of Synaptics that is expected to accelerate growth in AI‑related power solutions.

## 🔍 Overview
- onsemi changed its proposed acquisition of Synaptics to an approximately $5.7 billion all‑cash transaction after a competing bid emerged.
- The revised structure is viewed as more shareholder‑friendly and supports the company’s AI and power‑management strategy.

## 💰 Deal Structure
- Lowers the deal cost by about $1.3 billion.
- Avoids equity dilution.
- Expected to provide more immediate earnings accretion.

## 🤖 AI & Power Strategy
- Management is positioning Synaptics’ connectivity and edge‑computing capabilities to complement onsemi’s power semiconductors.
- The combined business targets physical AI, automotive and industrial applications.
- Investors see the transaction as a way to broaden exposure beyond cyclical semiconductor demand.

## 📈 Market Reaction
- Shares increased as investors focused on the revised deal.
- The stock recently traded near $86.31, below its 200‑day moving average of $88.79 but well above its 52‑week low.
- **Positive sentiment**: Deal viewed as shareholder‑friendly and supportive of AI strategy.
- **Neutral sentiment**: Technical momentum improved; market commentary points to a potential higher low near $68, rising volume and a possible breakout confirmation above roughly $86.50.
- **Negative sentiment**: Analyst caution remains. Stifel raised its price target to $80 from $75 but kept a Hold rating, noting that inventory correction is largely complete while demand recovery is uncertain. The new target is below the recent trading level.
- **Negative sentiment**: Insider selling is a secondary concern; recent disclosures show executives sold shares, including CFO Trent Thad’s reported sales of about 90,000 shares.
