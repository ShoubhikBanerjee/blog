# Meta adds iPad support to Muse AI agent app

Meta’s Muse, the agentic AI tool that debuted on iPhone nearly a month ago, received an update that adds native iPad support.

## 🔍 Overview
- Launched nearly a month ago and spent several weeks as the top free app in Apple’s App Store.
- The latest update introduces native support for the iPad.
- The iPad version should function similar to Muse on iPhones but better take advantage of the extra screen real estate and iPadOS multitasking capabilities.
- iPad support is limited to a one‑line mention at the bottom of the release notes for the latest version, alongside several iPhone updates.

## ⚙️ New Features
- Ability to connect Muse to additional software tools: Canva, Dropbox, Figma, QuickBooks, GitHub, Zoom, Asana, and Klaviyo.
- New connectors for apps such as Granola and Notion.
- Targeted at small‑business owners: users can give Muse a business goal, and the agent will autonomously plan marketing, client pitches, and develop “new product pages.”

## 💻 Desktop Extension
- A Mac version of Meta’s agentic AI tool was released about a week after the original mobile version debuted, expanding usefulness to desktop tasks like organizing files.

## 🚀 Availability
- iPad support is included in the latest iOS update for Muse.
- The Mac version is already available.
- Meta also announced a standalone Tamagotchi‑like device called the Muse Charm that runs the AI agent without a smartphone, and is working to bring Muse to smart glasses.

## 💡 Why it matters
- While it took Meta 15 years to release a dedicated iPad version of Instagram, the faster turnaround for Muse’s iPad support signals strong backing for its AI initiatives.
- Extending Muse from phone to tablet and desktop broadens its utility for small businesses and developers seeking AI‑driven productivity tools.
