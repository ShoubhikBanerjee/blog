# AI‑Powered Rapid Landing Page Creation Showcased at Startplatz AI Hub Event

An AI‑focused meetup took place in Cologne, bringing together over 100 vibe coders and marketers each month (more than 2,000 since June 2025).  Speakers demonstrated how AI can turn a vague brief into a functional landing page in minutes, and shared workflows for building web‑to‑app experiences without traditional development skills.

  - *Transit:* Media Park S‑Bahn station – 3 minute walk.
  - *Parking:* Synodome parking house.

## ⚙️ Key Takeaways
- **Cost & speed:** Avoid €15k MVP projects that take months; create landing pages in 15 minutes.
- **Analytics:** Set up tracking yourself without additional services.
- **Automation:** Use AI to handle repetitive tasks such as bug fixes, testing, and documentation.
- **Compliance:** Follow EU AI Act guidance by cataloguing tools, assessing risk, and maintaining documentation.
