# Meta Launches Custom Spaces Feature and AI Model Updates in Muse

Meta has introduced a new feature called "spaces" within its Muse app, allowing users to build customized, app-like experiences. In addition, Meta announced that it is bringing new capabilities from its Muse Wonder AI model into both the Muse app and the Meta AI chatbot.

## 🧩 How spaces work

- **Creation by description:** Meta says users can create a new space simply by describing it.
- **App-like experiences:** The AI assistant can generate customized experiences such as a shopping hub, school dashboard, music tracker, trivia game, and more.
- **Direct interaction:** Users can interact with elements within their spaces by making edits, adding notes, and checking items off.
- **App integrations:** When connected to supported outside apps—such as Gmail, Google Calendar, OpenTable, and Notion—Muse continuously pulls background information to automatically update dates, pricing, and other details.

## ⚙️ Layout and scheduling options

Users can customize how spaces are displayed within the Muse app:

- **Placement:** Spaces can be placed in the sidebar or moved into the main tab.
- **Scheduling:** Users can schedule when specific spaces appear. Meta says, "For example, keep your weekly planner up front during the workweek, then swap it for your weekend plans on Saturday."

## 🧠 Model capabilities

Meta is adding "Active Memory" and "Active Focus" features from its Muse Wonder AI model to Muse and Meta AI:

- **Contextual awareness:** Allows Muse to recognize when past information becomes relevant during conversations.
- **Goal tracking:** Enhances the AI's ability to work toward specific goals.

## 🚀 Availability

- **Rollout:** Meta is rolling out spaces to all Muse users.
- **Collaboration:** The company is currently exploring ways to enable users to share spaces and collaborate with others.
