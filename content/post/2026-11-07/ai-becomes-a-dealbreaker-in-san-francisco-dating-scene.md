# AI Becomes a Dealbreaker in San Francisco Dating Scene

When Irene moved to San Francisco last year and started using dating apps, she quickly discovered that AI has become a dealbreaker for many users.

## 📱 AI Signals in Profiles
- "AI is the new dating dealbreaker"
- "Swipe left if you’re not aligned"
- Hinge includes a prompt, "We’ll get along if…" and users sometimes answer with "Fuck AI."
- Example profile line: "You should *not* go out with me if… you use AI."

## 🌆 San Francisco Context
- San Francisco is described as "the epicenter of tech."
- The "metaphorical AI bomb went off in 2022 with the release of OpenAI’s ChatGPT."
- Since then, "conversations surrounding artificial intelligence now permeate the dating scene in one shape or another."

## 🗣️ Voices from the Field
- **Irene** noticed the trend while swiping on Hinge.
- **Kyle**, a researcher at an AI‑safety nonprofit, says the AI safety community sometimes sees a 10 % chance of human extinction and admits, "It’s hard to have that planning conversation because I don’t really expect a long‑term future."
- Kyle prefers to discuss these views in person rather than over text.
- **Bryan** recently entered a relationship and says AI is "the least [relevant] thing," but notes that "a bunch of people who are rich from AI, and, you know, being wealthy is always a plus."
- Matchmakers report clients now listing “AI engineer” as a preferred qualification, especially with the upcoming IPOs of Anthropic and OpenAI.

## 📊 Market Data Supporting the Trend
- A CBRE report (August) notes **more than 20,000 AI jobs** were added to the San Francisco Bay Area’s technology workforce, even as overall tech talent contracted by **6 % since 2022**.
- "Member of technical staff" roles are lucrative; the average salary of an AI researcher can be around **$300,000**.
- According to the New York Post, a matchmaker’s client booked a six‑month stay in San Francisco "because of the possibility of meeting a significant other who might be connected to an AI company going through an IPO."

## 🤖 AI in Personal Interactions
| Metric | Percentage |
|---|---|
| Americans turning to AI chatbots for personal, emotional, or social queries | 27 % |
| People who occasionally tell AI things they would not tell other people, including romantic partners | 38 % |

## 🧭 Why It Matters
- AI preferences are showing up next to traditional signifiers like political affiliation or solidarity emojis.
- Some daters are wrestling with the ethics of dating someone who builds AI, weighing financial benefits against uncertainty about the future.
- The rise of high‑paying AI jobs is directly influencing dating criteria in a tech hub where AI talent is expanding rapidly.
