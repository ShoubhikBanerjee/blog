# China’s MSS recruited Megaspeed exec to spy on Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang

China’s Ministry of State Security (MSS) recruited Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, to gather intelligence on Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang and U.S. AI chip technology.

## 🔍 Overview
- The New York Times reports that the MSS enlisted Alice Huang to spy on Jensen Huang.
- The recruitment aims to glean information about American AI chips and potentially influence Nvidia to make its most advanced chips available to China.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Megaspeed** is a customer of Nvidia.
- Alice Huang has “relationships with executives at the highest levels of the A.I. chip industry,” including leaders at **Gigabyte**.
- The NYT describes the relationship between Alice Huang and Jensen Huang, who are not related, as **“close.”**

## 🏛️ Political Response
- President Trump was briefed last year about the close relationship but took no action.
- Yesterday, three House committees asked Jensen Huang to appear for a joint, closed‑door briefing on his interactions with Alice Huang.

## 📰 Reporting Note
- The detail that China’s MSS recruited Alice Huang is new, according to the latest NYT reporting.
