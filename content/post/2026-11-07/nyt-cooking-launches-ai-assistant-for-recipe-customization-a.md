# NYT Cooking Launches AI Assistant for Recipe Customization and Ingredient Substitutions

The New York Times has introduced an AI-powered assistant for its NYT Cooking app to help subscribers modify recipes and find ingredient substitutions. The tool is designed to provide practical cooking guidance based on reputable sources and user feedback rather than generating purely AI-generated recipes from scratch.

## 🔍 Overview
The assistant was trained on the New York Times archives, restaurant data, cookbooks, food columnists, and reader comments. Unlike purely AI-generated recipes, which the author found to be of poor quality, this tool is designed to work with existing, human-tested recipes. It is programmed not to comply with requests that the editors believe will result in a dish that tastes bad.

## 🧩 How it works
The feature appears as a small text box on the recipe page with the prompt “Customize this recipe…” Once a user enters a request, the assistant generates a modified version of the recipe:

*   **New Title:** It creates a unique title for the modified version (e.g., “One-Pot Chicken and Rice With Ginger and Green Beans”).
*   **Non-destructive Editing:** It leaves the original recipe unchanged and creates a separate entry.
*   **Highlighting:** All changes are highlighted under a “Your Version” tab.
*   **Persistence:** Users can flip back to the original recipe at any point.

## ⚙️ Key details
The assistant can handle a variety of tasks ranging from simple measurement conversions to complex dietary modifications.

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ingredient Swaps | Suggests alternatives like Thai basil for cilantro or lime zest for makrut lime leaf. |
| Recipe Scaling | Adjusts quantities to feed specific numbers of people, such as seven diners. |
| Dietary Changes | Modifies existing recipes to be vegan or gluten-free. |
| Equipment Advice | Provides suggestions for missing tools, such as a missing Bundt pan. |
| Measurement Conversion | Converts metric weights to US measurements seamlessly. |

While the tool is useful, it is not perfect; in one test, it incorrectly suggested halving the required butter for a cake. It also highlights how substitutions might change the flavor profile of a dish, such as noting that lime zest lacks the deep floral aroma of makrut lime leaf.

## 🚀 Availability
The tool is currently available to NYT Cooking subscribers. At launch, the assistant is accessible exclusively on the iOS version of the app.
