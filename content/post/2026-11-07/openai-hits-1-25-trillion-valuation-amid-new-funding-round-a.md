# OpenAI hits $1.25 trillion valuation amid new funding round and IPO delay

OpenAI announced a new funding round that pushes its valuation to $1.25 trillion, while CEO Sam Altman reiterated that the company is postponing an IPO to focus on safety.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI’s latest round raised **$11 billion** and lifted its valuation to **$1.25 trillion** (Bloomberg).
- The company remains one of the world’s most valuable firms, shortly after a March raise of **$122 billion** at a **$852 billion** valuation.
- CEO Sam Altman says OpenAI is “prioritizing safety instead of going ‘guns blazing’ toward an IPO.”

## 📈 Funding Details
- **Current round:** $11 billion raised, valuation $1.25 trillion.
- **Potential follow‑on:** Bloomberg reports OpenAI is still pursuing investments that could bring the total to **$20 billion**.
- **Original target:** The firm had hoped to raise as much as **$30 billion** at a higher valuation of **$1.4 trillion**.
- Market context: tech stocks fell and midterm elections shifted public opinion against AI companies and data‑center projects.

## 📊 Valuation Timeline
| Funding Event | Amount Raised | Valuation |
|---------------|--------------|----------|
| March 2024     | $122 billion | $852 billion |
| Latest round   | $11 billion  | $1.25 trillion |
| Planned follow‑on (potential) | up to $30 billion | up to $1.4 trillion |

## 🚀 IPO Outlook
- Altman has said OpenAI is **holding off on its own IPO** until it can make stronger safety claims.
- No specific timeline has been given.
- He added, “I think it’s also kind of bad for the world if OpenAI waits too long to go public.”

## 🤝 Competitive Landscape
- Rival Anthropic filed its **S‑1** to go public last week.
- Anthropic’s IPO filing highlights the growing pressure on OpenAI to consider a public listing.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The valuation jump underscores investors’ confidence in OpenAI’s technology despite broader market headwinds.
- The safety‑first stance may shape how AI firms balance rapid growth with responsible development.
- Anthropic’s impending IPO adds competitive urgency for OpenAI’s own public‑market plans.
