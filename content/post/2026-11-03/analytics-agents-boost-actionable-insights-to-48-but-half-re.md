# Analytics agents boost actionable insights to 48% but half remain ineffective

Generative AI has broadened enterprise analytics, and the next wave—agentic AI—aims to automate whole workflows and act on the insights they generate.

## 🔍 Overview
- Analytics agents are software assistants that surface descriptive and prescriptive insights for business users.
- While most respondents praise the quality of descriptive insights, reviews of prescriptive insights are mixed.
- Failure to produce business‑improving insights harms ROI, and flawed insights can cause reputational or financial damage.

## 📈 Performance Update
- **48%** of insights generated by analytics agents now lead to actions that improve the business. This is up from **37%** a year ago.
- More than half of the insights still do **not** help the business.
- A small cohort called *insight leaders* achieves **>70%** actionable‑improvement rate and rates the quality of their agents’ insights higher than the overall respondents, especially for prescriptive insights.

## ⚠️ Challenges
- The top reason agents produce substandard insights is a **lack of business context** (cited by **55%** of executives).
- **Fragmented or inaccessible data** is the second major factor (**45%**).
- **52%** of surveyed organizations say agents generate too many insights to be properly evaluated and acted upon.
- Only **24%** can evaluate all agent insights; most evaluations are done case‑by‑case and are human‑intensive.

## 🛠️ Improvement Initiatives
- **AI and data governance** initiatives are a priority for **60%** of companies to improve insight quality.
- Adding a **semantic layer** to the data architecture is being pursued by **45%** of respondents; experts interviewed stress that a semantic layer is crucial for high‑performing agents.
- Insight leaders report fewer struggles with accessing business context.

## 🚀 Outlook
- Agentic AI promises to move beyond delivering insights to **automating entire workflows** and taking actions based on those insights.
- Closing the gap in context and data accessibility, and managing insight volume, are critical steps for more organizations to join the insight‑leader group.
