# Apple Highlights AI Growth on Record‑Breaking Earnings Call

Apple’s fiscal‑year earnings call featured AI more prominently than any prior quarter, signaling how the company plans to weave intelligence into its products and revenue streams.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI and Apple Intelligence were mentioned a record **45 times** on the call, surpassing the count after the June 2024 Siri revamp.
- CFO Kevan Parekh and CEO John Ternus both emphasized a “tremendous opportunity in AI” for future growth.

## 📊 Financial Context
- Apple posted its **best‑ever fiscal year** for revenue and earnings per share.
- **iPhone revenue** rose **17.5 %** YoY to a September‑quarter record **$57.6 B**.
- **Mac revenue** climbed **24 %** to **$10.8 B**, the highest in four years.
- Shares fell in pre‑market trading after Apple warned that **rising memory costs** could squeeze profit margins in the holiday quarter.
- Operating expenses increased **21 %** to **$19.2 B**, reflecting higher R&D spending, including on AI.
- Capital expenditures were **$2.2 B**, a fraction of peers such as Amazon (**$77.3 B**) and Alphabet (**$51.4 B**).

## 🤖 AI Strategy
- Apple’s approach blends **on‑device processing**, its own servers, and **outside AI technology**.
- **Siri AI** uses models developed with **Google’s Gemini** technology.
- Apple plans to let users choose among **outside AI providers** for features like writing tools and image generation.
- Parekh said it is “too early to predict how many requests will require servers rather than on‑device processing.”
- Siri AI’s cloud costs will appear in **services cost of sales**.

## 🚀 Product Rollout
- The revamped assistant launched in **beta for US English** users in **September**, then expanded to **French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish** in **October**.
- Ternus noted that server‑side features, including Siri AI and intelligent photo editing, will have **usage limits**.
- Customers needing more capacity “**will be able to purchase expanded access in the future**.”

## 🖥️ Local AI on Macs
| Device | Local AI Use | Partner / Note |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Mac Mini | Loss‑prevention across hundreds of Gap stores, avoiding cloud token costs | Gap |
| Mac Studio | Pricing‑estimate workflows for Christie’s, improving efficiency | Christie’s |
| iPhone | AI‑driven features provide a compelling reason to upgrade and switch devices | – |
| Siri AI | Server‑side model with usage limits; cloud costs counted in services cost of sales | – |

## 💰 Revenue Opportunities
- Parekh cited a “wide range of ways that AI can contribute to revenue growth over time.”
- He added that AI offers “a compelling reason for customers to upgrade and switch in order to get the latest capabilities.”
- He also highlighted “opportunities across third‑party apps and partnerships as well as first‑party opportunities.”
- The Wall Street Journal reported Apple is on pace to earn **more than $1 B in App Store fees** from generative‑AI apps by **2026** (AppMagic).

## ⚠️ Challenges & Cost Pressures
- Memory‑cost inflation may compress margins in the upcoming holiday quarter.
- Predicting the split between on‑device and server processing remains uncertain.
- Apple’s capital spending on AI infrastructure remains modest compared to its big‑tech peers.
