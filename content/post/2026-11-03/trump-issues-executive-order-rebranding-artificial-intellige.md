# Trump Issues Executive Order Rebranding Artificial Intelligence to Super Intelligence

President Trump has signed an executive order directing government agencies to rebrand "artificial intelligence" as "Super Intelligence." This update, signed at the end of September, establishes "Super Intelligence"—or "SI"—as the official government terminology, replacing contenders such as "Extreme Intelligence" and "Supreme Intelligence."

## 🔍 Overview

The administration stated that the name change reflects how modern systems "amplify human ingenuity" rather than merely "imitate" intelligence. This rebrand follows a White House tradition of renaming entities to signal new priorities or project dominance, similar to previous changes for the "Gulf of America," "Lake America," and the "Department of War."

## ⚙️ Key details

*   **Scope:** The order does not apply to previously issued regulations or documents.
*   **Enforcement:** Failure to adopt the new terminology may carry political consequences; the administration previously barred the Associated Press from certain events for not using the term "Gulf of America."
*   **Implementation:** The National Institutes of Health (NIH) now displays banner warnings about the terminology transition, while Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has begun requesting "SI-powered" surveillance.
*   **Future Actions:** The president has suggested a new rule may be introduced to outright ban the term "artificial intelligence" from government documents.

## 🧩 Adoption and Resistance

While the executive order mandates the change for agencies, adoption remains inconsistent across the public and private sectors.

| Entity | Reported Terminology Usage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Meta | Consistently uses "superintelligence" (a practice started last year). |
| Musk / SpaceXSI | Musk pledged to rename SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI; still mixes terms on X. |
| Microsoft | Referred to the "Super Intelligence era" in a recent shareholder letter. |
| Pentagon | Used "AI" for the Project Meridian initiative and the Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office. |
| Federal Trade Commission | Used "superintelligence" (lowercase) instead of the mandated capitalized version. |
| Salesforce | CEO Marc Benioff directed a name change from AIForce to SIForce. |
| Newsmax / HHS | Have shown no sign of adopting the new terminology in recent releases. |

## 💡 Why it matters

The rebrand has turned terminology into a political loyalty test within the national conversation. On the same day the order was signed, the White House Accord on Super Intelligence was released, featuring signatures from leaders at Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI, and Nvidia. Despite this, high-ranking officials like Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and news outlets like the Wall Street Journal have continued to use the term "AI," leading to public criticism from the president.
