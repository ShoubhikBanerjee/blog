# Google Rolls Out Personalized Gemini AI Features for Android Auto and Cars

Google is introducing Personal Intelligence to Gemini AI in vehicles, enabling the assistant to access and use information from a user's Google apps while they are driving. This update allows Gemini to tap into data from Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, YouTube, and Search to provide more tailored assistance.

## 🔍 Overview
With the Personal Intelligence update, Gemini in Android Auto and cars with Google built-in can now draw from across your personal Google information. This integration allows the assistant to provide contextually relevant answers based on your digital activity.

## 🧩 How it works
Gemini uses cross-app data to streamline tasks while on the road. Users can perform several personalized actions through voice commands:

*   **Flight Status:** Ask Gemini about your flight status, and it will pull details from a confirmation message in Gmail to provide the current status.
*   **Navigation by Appointment:** Request to be taken to a "doctor’s appointment," and Gemini will find the location in your calendar and start navigation.
*   **Visual Retrieval:** Ask Gemini to find a specific location, such as a car wash from a photo taken the previous week, and it will navigate there.

## ⚙️ Key details
To use these features, users must specifically enable the Personal Intelligence setting. Personalized responses are only shared when a phone is connected to the vehicle. If this setting is disabled, core functions such as navigation, music, and general questions remain available, but the responses will not be tailored to the individual.

## 🚀 Availability
Personal Intelligence is rolling out to Android Auto starting today. Support for vehicles featuring Google built-in is expected to arrive in the coming months. Google originally brought Gemini to Android Auto last year before expanding it to cars with Google built-in in April.
