# Google Gemini Conversational AI Now Available on Apple CarPlay

Google has expanded its Gemini conversational AI to work with Apple CarPlay, letting drivers chat directly with Google Maps while on the road.

## 🔍 Overview
- Google is now bringing Gemini to Apple CarPlay users.
- The conversational Gemini in navigation is available today for US drivers, with “rollout happening globally too,” according to Google.

## 🧩 How it works
- Users activate the feature by tapping the Gemini icon in Google Maps on their car’s infotainment display.
- They can talk to the voice assistant in Google Maps to get hands‑free help while driving.
- The assistant handles multi‑step questions, such as asking for recommendations on places to visit and then prompting it to direct you there while avoiding toll roads.

## ⚙️ Key details
- CarPlay users in the US can now use Gemini to chat with Google Maps.
- Google describes this CarPlay support update as a conversational way to report traffic disruptions and find information about stops and places on‑the‑go.

## 🚀 Availability
- The feature is currently available to US CarPlay users.
- Google says the rollout is happening globally as well.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a conversational, hands‑free method for drivers to interact with navigation, reducing the need to look at the screen while driving.
