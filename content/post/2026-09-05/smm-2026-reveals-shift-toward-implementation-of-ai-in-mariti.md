# SMM 2026 Reveals Shift Toward Implementation of AI in Maritime Operations

At the SMM 2026 maritime trade event, industry focus regarding artificial intelligence has moved from theoretical potential toward practical, ready-to-use implementations for ship operations.

## ⚙️ Key Developments

Industry participants at SMM 2026 emphasized that the current priority is the integration of AI-supported applications into maritime workflows. Conversations have shifted away from discussing potential to addressing specific operational requirements:

* Deployment of solutions for onboard collision avoidance.
* Integration of data from multiple sources to assist with navigation and decision-making.
* Implementation of predictive maintenance strategies.
* Development of AI-based situational awareness tools.

## 💡 Implementation Challenges

During the Maritime Future Summit, stakeholders examined both the value and the operational limitations of these technologies. Key areas of discussion included:

* The use of AI in safety-critical applications.
* Established standards for certification and liability.
* Ensuring the traceability of AI-based decision-making processes.
