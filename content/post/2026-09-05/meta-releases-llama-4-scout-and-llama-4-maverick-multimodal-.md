# Meta Releases Llama 4 Scout and Llama 4 Maverick Multimodal Models

Meta Platforms has released the latest versions of its large language model (LLM), introducing two new models: Llama 4 Scout and Llama 4 Maverick.

## 🧩 How it works
Meta describes these models as multimodal AI systems. This architecture allows for the processing and integration of various data types, including:
* Text
* Video
* Images
* Audio

These systems are capable of converting content across these diverse formats.

## ⚙️ Key details
Meta stated that Llama 4 Scout and Llama 4 Maverick are its most advanced models to date. The company identified them as the best in their class for multimodality.
