# Google Gemini model access shifted to paid tiers starting Oct 9

Starting on October 9th, Google Gemini’s free tier will be limited to the Flash Lite model.

## 🔍 Overview
- Free users previously could choose Gemini Flash Lite, Flash, and Pro.
- Access to the standard Flash model now requires a $4.99/month Google AI Plus subscription.
- The AI Plus tier will soon lose Gemini Pro access.
- AI Plus subscribers will be notified by email when they lose access.
- Users can still select low, medium, or high effort levels for each model, though higher effort levels may consume usage limits faster.

## ⚙️ Model Access Changes
| Model | Required subscription | Monthly price |
|-------|------------------------|--------------|
| Gemini Flash Lite | Free plan | — |
| Gemini Flash | Google AI Plus | $4.99 |
| Gemini Pro / Deep Think | Google AI Pro or Ultra | $19.99 or $99.99 |

## 📅 Availability
- The restriction to Flash Lite for free users begins on October 9.
- After the change, only paid tiers will retain access to Gemini Flash and Pro models.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Free users lose the ability to run higher‑performance Gemini models without paying.
- The top‑tier Gemini Pro model and the “Deep Think” advanced reasoning option become exclusive to higher‑cost subscriptions, potentially reshaping how individuals and developers budget for AI usage.
