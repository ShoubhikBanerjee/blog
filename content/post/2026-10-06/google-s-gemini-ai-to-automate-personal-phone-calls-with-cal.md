# Google's Gemini AI to Automate Personal Phone Calls with 'Call for Me' Expansion

Google may be expanding its “Call for Me” AI feature beyond business calls so you can use it to send messages to friends and family.

## 🔍 Overview
Android Authority reports a “Gemini Calling” introductory screen in an APK teardown. The screen shows example prompts such as:

| Example Prompt | Intended Action |
|---|---|
| Call Mom and tell her I will be 15 minutes late | Deliver a quick voice message to a family member |
| Call John and ask if he is coming for dinner | Deliver a quick voice message to a friend |

These examples suggest the feature would allow users to dictate short, text‑message‑style calls.

## 🧩 How it Works
- Users issue a spoken prompt to Gemini (e.g., "Call Mom and tell her I will be 15 minutes late").
- Gemini generates a brief spoken message based on the prompt.
- The AI places a phone call and plays the generated message, reaching the recipient without the user having to speak.
- Android Authority also found “granular permissions” for Gemini actions in individual apps, including phone‑call permissions, letting users control which apps can invoke the feature.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The rumored Android update would automate personal phone calls with Gemini AI.
- Gemini Call for Me might tell your mom you’re running late, or deliver other short updates.
- Even with this expansion, Gemini would likely be limited to basic, text‑message‑like calls; it is not expected to conduct full, back‑and‑forth conversations.
- Users could opt out of receiving incoming automated calls from Gemini via the granular permission settings.
- Android Authority notes Google might choose not to publicly release the expansion or the permission controls.

## 🚀 Availability
- The feature is currently rumored and has been observed in an APK teardown; no official release date or public announcement has been confirmed.

## 💡 Why it Matters
- It offers a quick way to reach people who prefer phone calls, miss text messages, or still use landline phones.
- The granular permission model could give users fine‑grained control over AI‑driven calling capabilities on Android devices.
- If released, the feature would extend Google’s AI services from business contexts into everyday personal communication.
