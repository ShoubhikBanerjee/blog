# Workshop Teaches Building a Competitive Intelligence AI Agent in 3 Hours

A new online workshop, **“3 Hours to Research Intelligence: Build Your First AI Agent**,” lets participants create a custom AI research agent that can save 10+ hours a week on job‑search or competitive‑analysis tasks.

## 🔍 Overview
- Current research is fragmented: scattered reviews, Google searches, LinkedIn checks, and missed details.
- The workshop promises to replace that manual workflow with an automated agent built in just **3 hours**.

## 🛠️ What You’ll Build
- **Fork** a ready‑made competitive‑intelligence agent from GitHub.
- **Wire in** your own data sources – RSS feeds, open APIs, or any available feed.
- **Feed** the agent a company or industry keyword.
- Receive **structured intelligence** including recent news, hiring patterns, market positioning, gaps, and red‑flags.
- Walk away with a deployed agent you can run **100 + times**.

## ⏱️ Workshop Format
- **Duration:** 3 hours, online.
- **Pre‑work (optional, 10 min):** Clone the repo, set up your API key, verify it runs.
- **Live steps:**
  1. Clone the GitHub repository.
  2. Add custom data sources.
  3. Test on a real company or competitor.
  4. Export a post‑work recap doc covering what you built, iteration tips, advanced techniques, and troubleshooting.
- If you skip the pre‑work, the instructor will set everything up in the first 20 minutes.

## 🎯 Who It’s For
- **Job seekers** who want to research a target employer before applying, interviewing, or accepting an offer.
- **Competitors analysts** needing market gaps, hiring trends, and strategic moves.
- Employees and side‑hustlers looking for a repeatable research workflow.

## ⚙️ Technical Requirements
- A laptop with a web browser capable of accessing **Claude.ai**.
- Basic comfort with Claude (already asking questions and iterating).
- Access to RSS feeds or APIs you wish to incorporate.

## 📊 Modes of Use
| Mode | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Job Seeker Mode** | Research a company before you apply, interview, or accept an offer; understand growth, hiring, leadership, red‑flags. |
| **Competitive Mode** | Understand what competitors are doing, market gaps, hiring trends, strategic moves. |

## 💰 Pricing
- One‑time fee of **$17–27**, covering all materials and the post‑work recap document.
- Optional upsell: an **Advanced Agent Techniques** course (separate paid offering).

## ✅ Outcomes
- Participants finish the session ready to run their own intelligence agent immediately.
- The agent can be reused across dozens of companies or industries, delivering consistent, structured insights.
