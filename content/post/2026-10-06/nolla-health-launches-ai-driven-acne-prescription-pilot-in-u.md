# Nolla Health launches AI‑driven acne prescription pilot in Utah

People in Utah can now use AI to get a prescription for acne treatment.

## 🔍 Overview
On Monday, healthcare startup **Nolla Health** announced that Utah residents can scan their faces with the company’s app. The AI system analyzes acne severity and autonomously writes a prescription, as reported by Bloomberg.

## 🧩 How it works
- Users open the Nolla Health app and capture a facial scan.
- The AI evaluates the scan for mild‑to‑moderate acne.
- If the AI is confident, it selects one of eight skin‑treatment options and generates a prescription.
- If the AI cannot “confidently select a treatment,” the user is directed to a physician.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Physician oversight** – The pilot starts with two physicians approving each AI‑generated prescription for the first 100 patients. Oversight relaxes after up to 500 prescriptions, then at least 10 % of prescriptions are reviewed each month plus any escalations or side‑effects.
- **First‑time prescriptions** – Utah has allowed AI systems to renew certain medications earlier this year, but Nolla Health is the first in the country to issue **initial** prescriptions via AI.
- **Cost** – The service is priced at **$4.99 per month**.
- **Eligibility** – Available to Utah residents who are **18 years or older** and have **mild‑to‑moderate acne**.
- **Treatment options** – The AI can currently prescribe **eight different skin treatments**.
- **Purpose** – The pilot is described as complementary to physicians, handling routine, low‑acuity cases so dermatologists can focus on complex, high‑risk patients.

## 🚀 Availability
The program launches as a pilot in Utah with gradual loosening of physician oversight as outlined above.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Demonstrates a regulatory approval pathway for AI‑generated **initial** prescriptions.
- Shows a model where AI handles routine dermatology cases, potentially reshaping dermatologist workload.
- Provides a low‑cost, subscription‑based option for patients with mild acne.
