# OpenAI launches textGrain watermark for ChatGPT and Codex in EU

OpenAI is adding an invisible, machine‑readable watermark called **textGrain** to text generated by ChatGPT and Codex. The rollout begins with eligible users in the European Union.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI is adding text watermarking in ChatGPT and Codex.
- The feature is called **textGrain** and is rolling out to the EU first.
- OpenAI says the rollout is **not a global default** at launch.

## 🧩 How it works
- textGrain embeds an invisible, machine‑readable pattern in the model’s text output.
- OpenAI claims textGrain **matched or exceeded** other approaches such as Google DeepMind’s **SynthID** and the watermark Anthropic announced in August.
- Benchmarks show **similar performance** from watermarked and unwatermarked text.
- OpenAI notes that textGrain **does not guarantee reliable detection** and does **not verify accuracy, ownership, human contribution, or prove human authorship**.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The rollout is limited to **eligible ChatGPT and Codex users across all plans in the EU**.
- OpenAI is using a **regional approach** to learn from real‑world use and feedback.
- **API customers worldwide** can **opt‑in** to watermarked outputs for select models.
- OpenAI is working with **cloud partners** to make watermarking available through their services in the coming weeks.
- **Researchers and expert organisations** can apply for **detector access** on a case‑by‑case basis, in line with the Code of Practice.
- The detector reports **whether an OpenAI watermark is present** without identifying the user or revealing prompts or conversations.
- Because of the risk of missed watermarks and false positives, the detector is **not publicly available at launch**.

## 🚀 Availability
- **EU rollout:** Over the coming weeks, eligible ChatGPT and Codex users in the EU will receive watermarked text.
- **API opt‑in:** Starting today, API customers worldwide can enable watermarking for select models.
- **Cloud integration:** Planned for the near future with OpenAI’s cloud partners.
- **Research access:** Approved researchers and expert organisations can apply for detector access starting today.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The EU rollout aligns with **requirements of the EU’s AI Act**, mirroring similar moves by Anthropic.
- Providing a watermark helps **track provenance** of AI‑generated text while acknowledging its limitations.
- Opt‑in capabilities give **customers flexibility** to meet their own transparency obligations.
- Controlled detector access supports **independent evaluation** and improvement of text provenance tools.
