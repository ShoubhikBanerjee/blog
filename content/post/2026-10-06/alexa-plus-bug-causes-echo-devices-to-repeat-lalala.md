# Alexa Plus bug causes Echo devices to repeat “lalala”

An Alexa Plus bug has caused some Amazon Echo smart speakers to repeat – and sometimes sing – “lalala” for minutes, often in the middle of conversations.

## 🔍 Overview
- Certain Echo devices running Alexa Plus get stuck on a loop of the word “lalala”.
- The behavior can last for several minutes.

## 🧩 Symptoms
- The speaker says only “lalala” repeatedly.
- Occasionally the device sings the same syllable.
- Alexa does not recognize or explain the behavior when asked.

## ⚙️ Reported incidents
- Users have posted about the issue on Reddit over the last few weeks, with reports as recent as three days ago.
- The problem appears across multiple models of Echo speakers.
- Several users have uploaded videos showing the looping audio.
- Android Authority posted one of these videos on YouTube.

## 🚧 Amazon response
- Amazon confirmed the bug in a statement to Android Authority.
- The company said it affects “a small number of Alexa Plus” users.
- A fix is reportedly in development.

## 📹 Media coverage
- Android Authority’s YouTube upload demonstrates the looping behavior.
- The story has been discussed on Reddit and cited by tech outlets.
