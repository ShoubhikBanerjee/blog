# Salesforce launches AIforce to enable AI‑driven CRM experiences

Salesforce announced **AIforce** at Dreamforce on September 15, 2026. AIforce is a live interface layer that brings Salesforce data, workflows, business logic, semantics, permissions, security, and governance to any AI interface, letting CRM follow users wherever they work.

## 🔍 Overview
- More than a chatbot‑style add‑on, AIforce exposes CRM context and governed actions through connected AI experiences.
- Access remains tied to existing permissions and governance rather than becoming an unrestricted data bridge.

## 🧩 How It Works
- **Live interface layer**: Bridges Salesforce’s core capabilities to any AI workspace.
- **Initial AI experiences**:
  | Experience | Purpose |
  |------------|---------|
  | Claudeforce | AI interaction using Claude models |
  | Slackforce | AI‑enhanced collaboration inside Slack |
  | Agentforce Coworker | AI‑assisted agent console |
- **Headless Toolkit**: Provides MCPs, APIs, plug‑ins, skills, and developer tools so developers can connect Salesforce with agents, applications, and custom interfaces.
- **Practical use**: A sales rep can pull customer information from an approved AI workspace; a support agent can invoke Salesforce workflows while preparing or updating records.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Flexibility**: Enables building CRM experiences around a team’s workflow instead of forcing every user onto a single UI.
- **Security & Governance**: Permissions, audit trails, data‑handling policies, and escalation paths stay consistent across all AI interfaces.
- **Developer responsibilities**:
  - Define which objects an agent can access.
  - Specify allowed actions and error handling.
  - Determine when human approval is required.
- **Modular architecture**: Positions CRM as a set of capabilities reachable through multiple interfaces, not a fixed screen.

## 🚀 Availability & Licensing
- Salesforce has not published a universal price or full availability matrix.
- Licensing requirements, regional access, and rollout status must be confirmed for each organization.
- Adoption will require administration and integration work.

## 🛠️ Implementation Guidance
- **Start narrow**: Choose a measurable workflow such as retrieving account summaries, preparing follow‑up tasks, routing service requests, or drafting updates for review.
- **Test security and governance** alongside convenience; verify that permissions apply consistently across custom apps and third‑party workspaces.
- **Map permissions, test business logic, monitor agent behavior, and validate actions** before moving to production.
- **Consultation services** can help define integration boundaries and set implementation priorities.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- AIforce lets companies assemble tailored employee portals, customer‑support tools, mobile apps, or internal automation that leverage Salesforce data without requiring users to learn the standard UI.
- The approach signals a shift toward modular, interface‑independent CRM systems, but the benefit depends on accurate reflection of underlying records and rules.
