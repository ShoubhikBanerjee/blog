# TechWolf acquired in largest Belgian VC-backed software deal

TechWolf, a Belgian AI company that provides workforce skills-mapping software, has been acquired in what its co-founder describes as the largest acquisition of a venture-capital-backed software company in Belgian history. No financial details have been disclosed.

## 🔍 Overview

Founded in 2018 by Ghent University students Andreas De Neve, Jeroen van Hautte and Mikaël Wornoo, TechWolf develops AI software that gives companies an overview of their employees' skills by drawing on data from business systems. The company is headquartered in Ghent, with additional offices in London and New York, and is opening a San Francisco office this year.

## 💰 Funding History

- In 2020, the Flemish government's investment company PMV invested in TechWolf.
- Further funding rounds followed in 2022 and 2024, raising €10 million and €40 million respectively.
- SAP was an investor in the 2024 round.
- PMV also participated in both the 2022 and 2024 funding rounds. It is unclear how large a stake PMV holds in TechWolf.

## 📊 Recent Financials

In its most recent financial year, ending in January 2026, TechWolf reported revenue of €8.68 million.
