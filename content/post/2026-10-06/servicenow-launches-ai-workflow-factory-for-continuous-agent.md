# ServiceNow Launches AI Workflow Factory for Continuous Agentic Workflow Improvement

ServiceNow announced the AI Workflow Factory, a platform that turns workflow improvement into a continuous, agentic AI‑powered loop.  The announcement also introduced Flow by ServiceNow, an AI service desk that can be deployed instantly, and highlighted new agent‑to‑agent capabilities emerging from collaborations with Work4Flow and LogicMonitor.

## 🔍 Overview
- **AI Workflow Factory** launches to enable a continuous loop of workflow improvement powered by AI agents.
- **Flow by ServiceNow** is a new AI service desk that deploys instantly.
- **Work4Flow and LogicMonitor** extend Edwin AI investigations in ServiceNow with new agent‑to‑agent capabilities.

## 🧩 How it works
- Workflows are continuously analyzed and refined by AI agents, creating an ongoing improvement cycle.
- The system leverages **agentic AI**, meaning AI entities act autonomously to identify and apply enhancements.
- **Agent‑to‑agent capabilities** allow separate AI components (e.g., Edwin AI, Work4Flow, LogicMonitor) to cooperate on investigations and optimizations.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Continuous, self‑optimizing workflow loop.
- Agentic AI that can act without direct human commands.
- Instant deployment of the Flow AI service desk.
- Integration of multiple AI agents for deeper investigations (Edwin AI, Work4Flow, LogicMonitor).

## 🚀 Availability
- Flow by ServiceNow can be deployed immediately after announcement.
- AI Workflow Factory is positioned as a new service offering from ServiceNow.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enables enterprises to keep their processes up‑to‑date automatically.
- Reduces the manual effort required to monitor and improve workflows.
- Combines multiple AI agents to broaden investigative reach and accelerate problem resolution.
