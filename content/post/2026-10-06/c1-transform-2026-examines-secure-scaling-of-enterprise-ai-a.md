# C1 Transform 2026 Examines Secure Scaling of Enterprise AI Agents

C1 Transform 2026 gathered senior technology and security leaders in San Francisco to address the emerging challenge of securely scaling artificial intelligence adoption while maintaining robust governance over autonomous AI systems.

## 🔍 Overview
- Executive‑level conference focused on AI governance, identity security, and enterprise AI enablement.
- Held in San Francisco at The Clancy hotel.
- Designed for CISOs, security leaders, senior engineers, and executives responsible for AI strategy.

## 🎤 Speakers
- Okta
- Cursor
- Together AI
- Brex
- Vanta
- Daytona.io
- DraftKings

## 🛠️ Core Themes
- As organisations increasingly deploy AI agents capable of independent action, the intersection of identity security, access management, and AI governance has become a critical operational concern.
- The shift to production deployments of agentic systems introduces complex questions about how traditional identity and access management frameworks apply to non‑human actors.
- Central to the programme is the concept of the *agentic enterprise* – AI agents operating as autonomous workers alongside human employees.
- **AI‑native identity governance**: security architectures built from the ground up to accommodate agentic workloads, including identity platforms that manage both human and machine identities, AI access gateways, and credential management systems for autonomous systems.
- Operational and governance dimensions include policy enforcement across human and machine identities, audit trails for autonomous actions, credential proliferation, and accountability frameworks for AI decisions.

## 📅 Event Format
- Structured as an executive‑level gathering combining keynote presentations, breakout sessions, and networking opportunities.
- Emphasises peer‑to‑peer dialogue between practitioners actively implementing AI governance strategies.
- Focuses on operational frameworks and implementation approaches that attendees can apply directly to their AI transformation initiatives.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Enterprises are moving beyond experimental AI to production‑grade agentic workloads, raising security and compliance challenges that intersect with existing regulatory obligations.
- The conference provides a forum for sharing approaches to integrating AI governance into established GRC programmes while adapting to the unique characteristics of autonomous systems.
- Senior leaders responsible for AI strategy, security architecture, or governance can gain actionable insights for protecting their organisations as AI agents scale.
