# Jennifer Neville Discusses AI Evaluation on Microsoft Research Podcast

Jennifer Neville, a Partner Research Manager at Microsoft Research, discussed the role of evaluation in pushing AI performance and the surprising failures that emerge when models are tested beyond traditional benchmarks in a conversation with Principal Applied Scientist Chad Atalla on the Microsoft Research Podcast.

## 🔍 Overview

In this episode of the Microsoft Research Podcast, Chad Atalla interviews Jennifer Neville, a partner research manager at Microsoft Research and the Samuel D. Conte chair professor of computer science and statistics at Purdue. Neville's research examines machine learning and AI for interactive domains and structured data, focusing on how training data affects AI system behaviors and alignment with user needs. The conversation explores evaluation's role in pushing AI performance boundaries and the "surprising failures" that emerge when models are tested beyond traditional benchmarks.

## 🧩 Key Details

- **Neville's career path** was unconventional: she majored in math, then physics, dropped out of college, returned to study cognitive science, and finally chose computer science despite initially avoiding it because her father was in the field.
- She discovered AI by happenstance and got into research through an honors program requirement, publishing a paper on statistical relational learning that hooked her on the field.
- Neville has published over 130 papers with more than 10,000 citations and received honors including a National Science Foundation CAREER Award, a spot on IEEE’s "10 to Watch" in AI list, and best paper awards from the International Conference on Data Mining and the International Conference on Learning Representations.
- The episode also covers practical guidance for working with current AI systems and why closely examining data matters when results defy expectations.
