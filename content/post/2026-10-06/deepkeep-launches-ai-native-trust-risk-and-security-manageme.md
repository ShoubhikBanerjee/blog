# DeepKeep Launches AI‑Native Trust, Risk and Security Management Platform

In May 2024 DeepKeep, a Tel Aviv‑based startup founded in 2021, emerged from stealth with a $10 million seed round led by Awz Ventures to launch an AI‑native trust, risk and security management (TRiSM) platform.

## 🔍 Overview
DeepKeep is an AI security and trust platform that protects large language models, computer‑vision, and agentic applications across their lifecycle. It targets CISOs and AI platform teams that need to secure both the AI their organization builds and the third‑party AI tools its employees and developers use.

## 🧩 How it works
The product consists of several modules that share a single policy engine:

| Module | Primary function |
|--------|-----------------|
| **AI Red Teaming** | Automated and human‑steered adversarial testing for prompt injection, jailbreaks and data leakage (includes the ‘Vibe AI Red Teaming’ mode driven by the agent Reddy). |
| **AI Firewall** | Real‑time evaluation of prompts and responses with >60 contextual, multilingual guardrails; works across text, images and video. |
| **Model Scanning** | Supply‑chain risk checks via MLBOM generation and CVE detection; also assesses bias, hallucination and leakage before deployment. |
| **AI Agent Scanner** | Maps tools, intents, data sources and vulnerabilities for agentic workflows built on Microsoft, Salesforce Agentforce, OpenAI, CrewAI, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, n8n and Make; references the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications and produces visual risk maps. |
| **AI Lens** | Discovery and shadow‑AI visibility plus role‑based usage control. On Oct 1 2026 added *AI Lens for Developers*, a lightweight plug‑in for Cursor and Claude Code that inspects prompts, file reads, shell commands and MCP calls, flags leaked credentials or insecure code, routes destructive commands for human approval, and logs every session. |

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Founded:** 2021 in Tel Aviv (CEO Rony Ohayon, CTO Yossi Altevet)  
- **Funding:** $10 M seed round, led by Awz Ventures (May 2024)  
- **Target audience:** CISOs and AI security leads at regulated enterprises that need a single platform for model/agent testing, runtime guardrails and usage control.  
- **Deployment options:** SaaS, private VPC, on‑premises or air‑gapped.  
- **Compliance certifications:** SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR.  
- **Customers:** ST Engineering, Macnica, EY.  
- **Recognition:** The Hacker News 2026 Cybersecurity Stars award for best AI security platform.

## 🚀 Availability
- The core platform launched in May 2024.  
- **AI Agent Scanner** became generally available in March 2026.  
- **AI Lens for Developers** plug‑in launched on Oct 1 2026.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides *red teaming*, *runtime guardrails*, *agent attack‑surface mapping* and *coding‑agent controls* under a single policy engine.  
- Supports highly regulated and defense environments through on‑prem and air‑gapped deployments.  
- Gives security teams audit logs and policy control over coding agents such as Cursor and Claude Code, preventing credential leaks and enforcing human approval for destructive commands.  
- Enables organizations to discover shadow‑AI tools and apply role‑based usage policies, reducing the risk of data exfiltration via unsanctioned assistants.
