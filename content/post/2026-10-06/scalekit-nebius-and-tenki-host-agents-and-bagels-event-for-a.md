# Scalekit, Nebius, and Tenki Host Agents and Bagels Event for AI Founders

Scalekit, Nebius, and Tenki are hosting a special SF Tech Week edition of Agents & Bagels, a curated breakfast for founders and AI builders. The gathering is designed for individuals building production-grade agents and infrastructure to engage in high-signal networking over coffee and bagels.

## 🔍 Overview
The event focuses on bringing together people working across several specific domains in the AI ecosystem:
- Agents
- Developer tools
- Infrastructure
- Applied AI

## ⚙️ Key details
The breakfast gathering is structured to prioritize conversation over formal presentations.
- **Format:** No panels, pitches, or long presentations.
- **Catering:** Coffee and bagels provided for participants.
- **Access:** Attendance is by approval only and invitations are non-transferable.
- **Media:** Organizers may capture and distribute photos, videos, and audio recordings for promotional and marketing purposes.

## 🧩 How it works
The event is held in collaboration with several co-hosts and the Ugly Talk community.

| Entity | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Scalekit | Co-host |
| Nebius | Co-host |
| Tenki | Co-host |
| Ugly Talk | Event organizer and community platform |

## 🚀 Availability
Participation is managed through a registration and approval process. Approved guests receive a confirmation email and a calendar invitation. Registration requires agreement to a Code of Conduct and a liability waiver. Attendees also agree to receive marketing communications from Ugly Talk and its partners, with the option to unsubscribe at any time.

## 💡 Why it matters
This gathering provides a venue for builders to share raw lessons and insights, following a model established by Ugly Talk in 2019 to transform business setbacks into breakthrough moments. It targets those scaling agent workflows and exploring next-generation AI infrastructure, offering a focused environment for peers in the ecosystem to start the day with meaningful conversations.
