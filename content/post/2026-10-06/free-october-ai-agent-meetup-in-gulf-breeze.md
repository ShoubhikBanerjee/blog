# Free October AI Agent Meetup in Gulf Breeze

AutoEDU is hosting a free, in‑person AI Agent meetup this October for anyone who wants to see practical AI‑agent workflows and connect with local builders.

## 📅 Overview
- Free, monthly meetup (October edition)\
- In‑person at Gulf Breeze Cowork Annex, 3000 Gulf Breeze Pkwy, Gulf Breeze, FL 32563\
- Duration: 2 hours\
- No programming experience required; open to beginners\
- Bring your questions, ideas, and, if you like, a laptop and charger

## 🛠️ What You’ll Experience
- Live demonstrations of useful AI‑agent workflows\
- Plain‑English explanations for beginners\
- Practical agents for small businesses, educators, creators, and everyday work\
- Q&A with local builders and operators\
- A look at upcoming AutoEDU hands‑on workshops

## 👥 Who Should Attend
- Small‑business owners\
- Educators and teachers\
- Content creators\
- Anyone interested in practical AI applications\
- People who want to meet others exploring AI on the Emerald Coast

## 📍 Location & Logistics
- Venue: Gulf Breeze Cowork Annex\
- Address: 3000 Gulf Breeze Pkwy, Gulf Breeze, FL 32563\
- Time: 2‑hour session (exact time not specified in the announcement)\
- Bring: Laptop and charger if you want to follow along\
- Questions? Email **hello@autoedu.ai** or call **850‑990‑3780**

## 📚 Next Steps
