# Lupa Records Milestone of 3,600 Active Gemini Capsules

Lupa has identified a significant milestone for Gemini capsules, with the count of active and accessible entries reaching 3,600. This data highlights a growing collection of capsules currently accessible to the network.

## ⚙️ Key details
*   There are currently 3,600 Gemini capsules known to Lupa.
*   The capsules tracked are confirmed to be both active and presently accessible.

## 🚀 Availability
*   The number of active capsules is projected to reach and exceed the 3,600 mark by the end of this month.
