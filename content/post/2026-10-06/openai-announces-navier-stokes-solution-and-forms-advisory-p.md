# OpenAI Announces Navier‑Stokes Solution and Forms Advisory Panel Amid Backlash

OpenAI announced that its internal AI model has produced a solution to the Navier‑Stokes Millennium Prize problem, and the company responded to the ensuing controversy by creating an independent advisory panel of elite mathematicians.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI claimed one of its biggest prizes yet: a solution to a legendary Millennium Prize problem – the Navier‑Stokes problem, a challenge unsolved for around 90 years.  
- The breakthrough was presented in a blog post on a Tuesday and described as an achievement that demonstrates how quickly AI is transforming mathematics.  
- The announcement sparked immediate backlash from the mathematical community, raising concerns about academic norms, credit, and the motivations behind the work.

## ⚙️ What OpenAI announced
- The solution was generated by an internal AI model that the company said is “more powerful than the newly re…”.  
- OpenAI framed the result as a historic achievement, noting that such a discovery would normally be celebrated.

## 📣 Community reaction
- Mathematicians voiced unease, describing OpenAI as an “impossibly well‑resourced interloper” that appears to be driven by winning rather than advancing the field.  
- Accusations surfaced of scooping, spying, and violating long‑standing academic norms, which some fear could have a chilling effect on research.  
- Andreas Thom raised concerns that prior interactions with the ChatGPT chatbot may have contributed to the success, and OpenAI acknowledged that the result built heavily on his and Gábor Kun’s earlier work.  
- A second mathematician accused OpenAI of “unethical and dishonest” behavior and a lack of transparency about the origins of its training data.  
- Abhishek Saha, a mathematics professor at Queen Mary University of London, explained that OpenAI has engaged in “the kind of things that mathematicians will generally not do.”

## 🤝 OpenAI’s response
- OpenAI announced a new independent panel of elite mathematicians tasked with advising the company and other AI firms on how to interact with mathematical research and the wider community.  
- The panel’s formation “caught many mathematicians by surprise,” and researchers questioned what the group will actually do, how much influence it will have, and whether OpenAI will heed its advice.  
- The first assignment for the panel is to help coordinate the release of scores of additional results that OpenAI says its unreleased model has produced, a prospect that has already stirred dread among researchers.

## 📈 Why it matters
- The episode illustrates the tension between rapid AI‑driven discoveries and established scholarly practices.  
- How OpenAI manages the release of AI‑generated mathematics could shape future collaborations—or conflicts—between industry and the academic community.
