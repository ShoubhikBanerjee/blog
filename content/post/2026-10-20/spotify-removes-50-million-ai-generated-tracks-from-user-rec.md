# Spotify Removes 50 Million AI-Generated Tracks From User Recommendations

Spotify has announced that it removed more than 50 million songs categorized as AI-generated slop or spam from its recommendation system over the past year.

## 🔍 Overview
Spotify has implemented changes to its music recommendation system to prioritize songs from human artists. The company reports a "marked decrease in listener concerns" regarding the appearance of slop or spam in recommendations since these changes were made. This follows previous efforts last year where the company took down more than 75 million songs identified as spam.

## 🧩 How it works
To determine if a track is AI-generated slop, Spotify utilizes a combination of the following:
* Listener engagement and feedback
* Upload patterns
* Metadata
* Third-party AI detection tools

Additionally, Spotify editors maintain a standard where they do not program content identified as slop or spam.

## ⚙️ Key details
Spotify has introduced specific policies for AI identities:
* **AI Persona Badge**: A label for artists using an AI identity who do not represent a real person.
* **Recommendation Policy**: Music from AI personas will not be recommended.

## 💡 Why it matters
Spotify's approach differs from other streaming platforms handling AI music:

| Platform | Approach to AI Music |
| :--- | :--- |
| YouTube Music | Labels AI songs |
| Deezer | Provides users an option to filter out AI slop |
| Bandcamp | Bans AI music entirely |

Despite these restrictions on slop, Spotify is partnering with Believe (an independent music publisher) and Universal Music Group to launch a tool for creating AI music remixes.
