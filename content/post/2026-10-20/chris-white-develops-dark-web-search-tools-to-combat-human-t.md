# Chris White Develops Dark‑Web Search Tools to Combat Human Trafficking

In summer 2010, postdoc Chris White worked with a multidisciplinary team at DARPA to test a new data‑analysis approach aimed at preventing violence and protecting troops in Afghanistan. After the program, DARPA asked him to demonstrate the value of his work on the ground. Later, as director of Catalyst Lab at Microsoft Research, White helped create dark‑web search tools used to combat human trafficking.

## 🔍 Overview
- In summer 2010, postdoc Chris White worked with a multidisciplinary team at DARPA to test a new data‑analysis approach aimed at preventing violence and protecting troops in Afghanistan.
- At the conclusion of the program, the agency asked White to prove the value of his work on the ground.
- As director of Catalyst Lab at Microsoft Research, White helped create dark‑web search tools used to combat human trafficking.

## 🛠️ Development and Role
- Served as on‑the‑ground advanced tech lead during the war in Afghanistan.
- Became a DARPA program manager.
- Leads the newly formed Cat Lab (Catalyst Lab) in Microsoft Research.
- Focuses on technology with immediate, real‑world implications.

## 🏆 Recognition
- Work featured in Popular Science.
- Received the 2016 Presidential Award for Extraordinary Efforts to Combat Trafficking in Persons.

## 👤 Personal background
- Harvard postdoc.
- Nerdy Eagle Scout who built his own computers and hosted LAN parties.
- Enjoys nature, camping, rock climbing, and debate.
