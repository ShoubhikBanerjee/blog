# Amazon SageMaker Studio Simplifies Sign-up and Access for New Customers

Amazon has introduced a streamlined sign-up process for new Amazon SageMaker AI customers, allowing them to land in a fully configured SageMaker Studio environment in just a few steps. This update removes the need for traditional, complex AWS account setup steps like credit card entry and phone number verification for most users.

## 🧩 How it works

New users can now create an account using existing credentials from Amazon, Apple, GitHub, Google, or a standard email address. Once registered, the SageMaker environment is created automatically in approximately 20 seconds. Permissions for tasks including model customization, training, evaluation, and deployment are pre-configured, eliminating the requirement for manual IAM role creation.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Free Tier Credits:** New customers receive $100 in AWS Free Tier credits with the opportunity to earn an additional $100. Credits apply to model customization, training jobs, Studio notebooks, and endpoint deployments.
* **Usage Security:** The Free Tier period lasts for 6 months or until credits are exhausted. At that point, resource usage stops automatically to prevent unexpected charges.
* **Instance Access:** Eligible accounts have default access to G5, G6, and G4dn GPU instances. The Studio interface now displays quota availability within the instance selection menu.

## 🚀 Availability

This updated sign-up experience is available for new AWS accounts. Existing customers retain their current sign-in methods.

## 💡 Why it matters

This update removes the final major hurdle for developers to begin building with SageMaker AI, enabling startup teams to move from discovering a model to customization or deployment in minutes.
