# Adobe Lightroom Mobile Adds Generative AI Tool to Open Closed Eyes

Adobe has announced that the mobile version of Lightroom, available on iOS, iPadOS, and Android, can now open closed eyes in photos using generative AI with a single tap.

## 🧩 How it works
When the app detects a person or group of people with closed eyes in a photo, it automatically presents an "Open Eyes Quick Action" icon over the image.

Unlike previous methods, this feature utilizes generative AI to ensure replacement eyes match the lighting and pose. To accurately match the color, size, and shape of the subject's own eyes, the app uses other images of the same person found within the user's Lightroom library as a reference.

## 💡 Why it matters
Adobe's existing tools differ in approach and accessibility:

| App | Method | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Photoshop Elements | Reference Photo | Sources replacement from another photo; results vary based on image variations. |
| Photoshop | Generative AI | Not a one-tap fix and is not free. |
| Lightroom Mobile | Generative AI | One-tap fix using library references for accuracy. |

## 🚀 Availability
* **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, and Android.
* **Pricing:** Users of the free version of Lightroom mobile can use the Open Eyes feature twice. After two uses, users are prompted to subscribe to an Adobe Creative Cloud plan to receive more credits for AI-powered tools.
