# Impact of Coding Agents on the Programming Profession

The emergence of coding agents is altering the nature of professional programming, specifically regarding the role of solving logic puzzles in software development.

## 💡 Why it matters

The shift toward LLMs impacts programmers differently based on their professional motivations:

* **For programmers who thrive on building working systems** and do not care deeply about the problems solved along the way, LLMs are described as "the gift that keeps on giving."
* **For programmers who enjoyed the puzzle of developing code**, the loss is described as "very real."

Historically, programming was characterized by the following:

* Professionals were paid to work on "tricky little logic puzzles," with higher pay for trickier puzzles.
* Programmers set the strategy, defined the timeline, and determined what was and was not possible.
* Regular people were unable to perform these tasks, making even simple puzzles valuable.
