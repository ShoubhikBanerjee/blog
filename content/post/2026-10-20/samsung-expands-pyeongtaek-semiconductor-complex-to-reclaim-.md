# Samsung Expands Pyeongtaek Semiconductor Complex to Reclaim Lead in AI Memory Chips

Samsung is building a massive new semiconductor manufacturing complex in Pyeongtaek, South Korea, as it attempts to reclaim its lead in the global memory chip market. The build-out in the farming city south of Seoul comes as the company predicts demand for memory chips will continue to surge through 2028 due to the AI boom.

## 🔍 Overview

The Pyeongtaek campus covers an area approximately the size of New York City’s Central Park and operates as a sprawling chipmaking complex where rice paddies abut the industrial facilities.

*   **Operational Status:** Three fabs already operate around the clock.
*   **Current Expansion:** A fourth fab is currently coming online.
*   **Restarted Construction:** Construction of a fifth fab was halted in 2024 during a drop in memory demand but restarted when AI-driven demand increased.

## ⚙️ Key details

The construction site employs hundreds of women, many of whom serve as spotters to ensure safety around heavy equipment. 

| Role | Responsibilities | Daily Pay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Safety Officer | Overseeing crews responsible for building cable ducts | 140,000 won ($97) |
| Spotter | Walking ahead of heavy equipment | 140,000 won ($97) |

## 💡 Why it matters

Samsung is using the Pyeongtaek expansion to address shifts in the global semiconductor landscape:

*   **Market Competition:** Domestic rival SK Hynix has pulled ahead in the global market for high-bandwidth memory models used in Nvidia’s AI accelerators.
*   **Foundry Dominance:** Taiwanese chipmaker TSMC currently dominates the foundry business, manufacturing chips designed by other companies.
*   **Future Demand:** Samsung forecasts that the surge in demand for memory chips will persist through 2028.
