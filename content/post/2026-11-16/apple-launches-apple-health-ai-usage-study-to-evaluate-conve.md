# Apple Launches Apple Health AI Usage Study to Evaluate Conversational AI Tools

Apple has announced the Apple Health AI Usage Study to better understand how individuals interact with conversational AI health tools and to assess the usefulness and effectiveness of their responses.

## 🔍 Overview
The study is being conducted in collaboration with OpenEvidence, a clinical AI platform used by healthcare professionals. The platform generates responses based on medical information sourced from publications such as *The New England Journal of Medicine*.

## 🧩 How it works
Participants interact with the study through a new iOS app designed for health inquiries:

- **Prompting:** Participants submit health-related questions using text-based prompts, with dictation supported via the iOS keyboard.
- **Responses:** The app provides accessible responses containing links to peer-reviewed clinical sources.
- **Evaluation:** Users rate responses based on criteria including accuracy, detail, and relevance.
- **Health Data Integration:** During enrollment, participants can optionally share Apple HealthKit data (such as activity, sleep, or clinical lab results) to give responses personal health context.
- **System Integration:** The app is not integrated with Siri or Apple Intelligence.

## ⚙️ Requirements and Availability

| Parameter | Requirement / Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Target Enrollment | Up to 200,000 participants |
| Age & Location | Age 21 and older, based in the US |
| Hardware | iPhone 15 Pro |
| Operating System | iOS 26 or later |
| App Status | Available for download; enrollment open |

## 🔒 Privacy Measures
Privacy is treated as a priority for the study. Apple states that measures are in place to keep user information confidential, and collected data will not be used to train AI models.

## 💡 Study Goals
The objective of the study is to enhance the overall design and functionality of AI-powered health tools, enabling them to deliver relevant health information that is easier for users to understand and act upon.
