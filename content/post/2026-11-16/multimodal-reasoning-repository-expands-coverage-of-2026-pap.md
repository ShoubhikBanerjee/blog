# Multimodal Reasoning Repository Expands Coverage of 2026 Papers

A GitHub repository dedicated to organizing papers on Multimodal Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) has been updated with a new volume of research. This development reflects the growing intersection of visual (and audio) capabilities with reinforcement learning (RL) powered reasoning, an area where researchers hold high hopes for the future of MLLMs, LVLMs, and LSLMs.

## 📚 Repository Scope

The repository is designed to support research surveys by aiming for comprehensive coverage rather than limited curation. The maintainers state that covering all possibly relevant papers is more valuable to researchers than providing a curated list of only the best papers. Recent additions demonstrate this focus on breadth across multiple modalities and reasoning techniques.

*   **Multimodal Generation:** Includes papers on visual generation (image and video) using RL and Chain-of-Thought (CoT).
*   **Reasoning Techniques:** Covers advancements in multimodal reasoning, including RL-based reasoning and alignment with human preferences.
*   **Modalities:** Spans image, video, and audio reasoning in foundation models.

## 🧩 Featured Research Topics

The recent additions highlight several specific technical approaches and applications within the multimodal reasoning landscape:

*   **Image Generation and Reasoning:** New work such as "Reasoning with Image Generation" explores the link between generative tasks and logical processes.
*   **Model Adaptation:** "On-Policy Distillation for Vision-Language Model Adaptation" addresses adaptation on low-quality multimodal data.
*   **In-Context Learning:** "Beyond Surface Imitation" introduces contrastive modeling for reasoning path alignment in multimodal in-context learning.
*   **Change Detection:** "From Pixels to Hierarchical Sequences" proposes quadtree mask encoding for vision-language binary change detection.
*   **Autonomous Driving:** "Drive by Hindsight and Foresight" utilizes tool-grounded synergistic reasoning over hierarchical memory.
*   **Image Quality Assessment:** "From Glance to Scrutiny" presents progressive distortion reasoning for fine-grained assessment.
*   **Segmentation:** "SeGDeP" offers semantic- and geometric-aware decoupled prompts for reasoning segmentation.
*   **3D Reasoning:** "CoVeR" implements coverage-based token pruning for multi-view 3D reasoning in VLMs.
*   **Stability in Reasoning:** "Stable-MM-R1" anchors multimodal reasoning dynamics via entropy-guided stratification.
*   **Text Spotting:** "SupGRPO" enhances GRPO with matching-based online SFT.
*   **Spatial Reasoning:** "MV-STRIDE" enables MLLMs to master multi-view spatial reasoning via hierarchical capability modeling.
*   **Evaluation and Calibration:** Recent papers include "Counterfactual Tests for Measuring Chain-of-Thought Faithfulness" and "Separating Capability from Confidence" for grounded dual-state calibration in medical vision-language models.
