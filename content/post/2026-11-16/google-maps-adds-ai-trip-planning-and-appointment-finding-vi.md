# Google Maps adds AI trip planning and appointment finding via Ask Maps

Google is launching a new AI trip-planning feature for Maps that allows the app to create custom itineraries and provide real-time details about each stop. The update is rolling out to the Gemini-powered Ask Maps chatbot inside the app.

## 🧩 How it works
The AI-powered Ask Maps feature can suggest stops for your next trip while providing real-time details about each location. Users can plan a one-day outing or a trip spanning several days. For example, Google states that you can use Ask Maps to plan a day trip for holiday shopping or create a schedule for a three-day road trip with stops for coastal hikes and oceanfront dining. From there, Ask Maps generates a day-by-day schedule with a list of stops, showing your route and numbered pins for each location on the map.

## ⚙️ Key details
- You can share the itinerary with friends or family members.
- You can export the itinerary to Google Docs on desktop.
- You can ask Maps to show your trip schedule before you leave or select the “itinerary” badge in your chat history.
- Google Maps displays real-time information about traffic and a location’s hours alongside directions.
- The “know before you go” details include parking, car charging, weather conditions, potential delays, and tolls during your trip.
- Ask Maps can find specific beauty, pet care, auto, and home repair appointments and call the locations for you.
- You can find saved places on your list by asking queries like, “Which spots on saved in my lists have availability for five people in the next 30 minutes?”

## 🚀 Availability
Google is expanding Ask Maps to over 30 countries in Europe, including Spain, Germany, and the UK. Trip-planning and saved list search are rolling out to the more than 200 countries where Ask Maps is available. The “know before you go” tips and appointment-finding features are launching in the US to start.
