# Congress Seeks Tighter AI Chip Export Limits After Nvidia Spy Scandal

Following a scandal involving now-former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and a suspected Chinese spy, Congress is seeking to further restrict the export of AI chips. Members of both political parties have voiced support for measures to tighten export rules and have demanded that Huang and other Nvidia leaders testify before Congress.

## 🔍 Overview
The push for stricter oversight follows revelations regarding Jensen Huang’s relationship with a suspected Chinese spy. Both Democrats and Republicans have expressed a desire to crack down on the distribution of powerful AI hardware to China.

## ⚙️ Key Details
* **Congressional Testimony:** Senate Banking Committee Chairman Tim Scott has sent letters to Jensen Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick calling for public testimony. Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer stated that Huang and the Trump administration need to answer to Congress.
* **Renewed Legislative Interest:** The Remote Access Security Act, which was first introduced in the House in April 2025 but stalled in the Senate, is receiving renewed attention.
* **Consensus on Limits:** House Speaker Mike Johnson noted a "growing consensus in Congress on limiting chip exports to China" and personally supports efforts to restrict China’s access to powerful chips from companies like Nvidia.
