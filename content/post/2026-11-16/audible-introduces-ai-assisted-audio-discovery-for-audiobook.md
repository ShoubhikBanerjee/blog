# Audible introduces AI-assisted audio discovery for audiobooks

Audible is introducing a new AI-assisted audio discovery feature designed to help users determine whether they will enjoy an audiobook before starting to listen. The update aims to prevent users from needing to research titles on other platforms by providing a soundbite synopsis.

## 🔍 Overview
The new feature combines audiobook samples, AI overviews, and author commentary to give users more information before choosing their next story. It also includes recommended audiobook collections personalized to individual listening tastes.

"Picking your next listen should feel like getting a tip from a friend. With audio discovery, you’ll get a quick audio take on why the title might be right for you, from our editors, fellow listeners, or even the author," Audible chief product officer Andy Tsao said in the announcement. "Some of the best listening experiences are those rare finds or hidden gems, and we want to make those great discoveries happen more often and more easily."

## 🧩 How it works
Audio discovery plays users a snippet of the audiobook along with a brief AI-generated overview. This overview includes the book's themes and listener reactions, drawing from reviews and other publicly available data. The feature can also include commentary from an author or Audible editor, which is pulled directly from past Audible interviews.

In addition to audio discovery, Audible is using AI to personalize audiobook collections around users’ listening tastes. These collections are intended to make titles that do not appear on best-selling lists easier to discover.

## ⚙️ Key details
Audible is also expanding two existing beta features as part of this update:
* **Ask a Question**: Allows users to ask AI about characters, plot points, references, and more.
* **Recaps**: Provides spoiler-free audio summaries of what the user has already listened to.

These expanded features are available for a "select set" of additional English-language titles in the US, with a broader rollout planned to start in December.

## 🚀 Availability
Audio discovery and the personalized collections are rolling out on select English-language titles in the US, with plans to eventually expand to other regions. Like audio discovery, the personalized collections are rolling out to customers in the US and also include commentary from Audible’s editors.

## 💡 Why it matters
These are the latest AI-powered features that Audible is introducing in an effort to attract new users and fend off growing competition from rival platforms like Spotify.
