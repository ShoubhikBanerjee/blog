# FrontierML Benchmark Shows AI Agents Can Spend Money to Improve Performance

AI agents are gaining the ability to autonomously allocate resources for self-improvement. Researchers have developed FrontierML, a benchmark that measures how well agents can turn capital into model performance on challenging machine learning tasks. The benchmark spans six category areas, including LLM training, efficiency, and computer use, and shows that today's frontier systems are already able to, when given some money, turn this into improving their own performance on a basket of 21 distinct tasks.
