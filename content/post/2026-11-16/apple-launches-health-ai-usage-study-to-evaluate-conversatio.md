# Apple Launches Health AI Usage Study to Evaluate Conversational Medical Tools

Apple has announced a new study in collaboration with OpenEvidence to investigate how users interact with conversational AI health tools. The Apple Health AI Usage Study aims to evaluate the accuracy and usefulness of AI-generated medical responses using clinical grounding and scientific rigor.

## 🔍 Overview
Apple is partnering with OpenEvidence, a clinical AI platform used by healthcare professionals that draws information from peer-reviewed sources like The New England Journal of Medicine. The goal of the research is to improve the design and functionality of AI-powered health tools so they can deliver relevant information that is easier for users to understand and act on.

## 🧩 How it works
Participants in the study will use a new iOS app to ask medical questions and receive responses that include links to clinical sources. Users then rate these responses based on specific criteria:

*   Accuracy
*   Detail
*   Relevance

During enrollment, participants have the option to share personal HealthKit data, such as sleep patterns, activity levels, or clinical lab results. Sharing this data allows the app to provide more context within its responses.

## ⚙️ Key details

| Feature | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| Input Method | Text-based prompts (dictation via iOS keyboard available) |
| Data Privacy | Data is confidential and not used to train AI models |
| System Integration | Not integrated with Siri or Apple Intelligence |
| Device Requirement | iPhone 15 Pro |
| Software Requirement | iOS 26 or later |

## 🚀 Availability
Enrollment for the study begins today, and the dedicated app is now available for download. Apple is seeking up to 200,000 participants for the research. To qualify, individuals must be at least 21 years old and based in the United States.

## 💡 Why it matters
According to Sumbul Desai, MD, Apple’s vice president of Health and Fitness, "responsible innovation starts with scientific rigor and clinical grounding." The company believes a dedicated research study is the most thoughtful way to understand how people evaluate health AI in everyday life. Insights from this study are expected to advance the scientific conversation and help Apple build trusted tools that support people throughout their health journeys.
