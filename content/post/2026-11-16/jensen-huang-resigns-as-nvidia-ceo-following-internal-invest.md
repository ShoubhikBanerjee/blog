# Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO Following Internal Investigation and Spy Allegations

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang has resigned from his role as chief executive officer following reports of extramarital affairs, an investigation into potential national security risks, and changes to the company's executive structure.

## 🔍 Timeline of Events

* **October 29**: *The New York Times* reported that Jensen Huang appeared to be targeted in a Chinese spy plot involving Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed.
* **November 6**: *The New York Times* reported that Alice Huang was specifically recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to spy on Nvidia's CEO.
* **November 8**: Jensen Huang admitted to an extramarital affair that raised national security concerns, leading Nvidia's board to launch an investigation. Nvidia leadership, including General Counsel Tim Teter and Chief Marketing Officer Alison Wagonfeld, had previously been misled about the nature of the relationship.
* **November 11**: *The New York Times* reported a second affair between Jensen Huang and direct report Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's vice president of health care.
* **November 12**: Nvidia officially announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as CEO.
* **November 13**: Nvidia's stock closed at $162.85, down from its all-time high of $239.24 recorded on October 6.

## 💡 Reinstatement Campaign and Industry Reaction

Following the resignation, reports surfaced that Jensen Huang discussed the possibility of returning as CEO with close allies. A public letter demanding Nvidia "bring Jensen back" gathered over 150,000 signatures, backed publicly by figures such as Elon Musk and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney. However, *Business Insider* reported that the authenticity of the signatures is difficult to verify, with entries including names like "Tim Cruise, Hollywood actor" and "Xi Jingping."

Opinions remain divided among Silicon Valley insiders. According to the *Financial Times*, one ally noted that having an affair with a subordinate is a fireable offense. Additionally, 23andMe founder Anne Wojcicki commented on executive power dynamics to Bloomberg, noting, "Power corrupts, and people start to think they are invincible … We are all complicit in making these people think they are gods."

## 📊 Business Impact and Infrastructure Investments

Nvidia faces these leadership shifts while managing extensive capital commitments and ongoing legal challenges:

* Nvidia has invested $99 billion in various AI labs, startups, and neo clouds.
* The company announced plans to mobilize over $500 billion in financing to build out AI data centers and infrastructure.
* The US Commerce Department previously investigated Megaspeed over suspicions of illegally smuggling Nvidia AI chips into China, where sales of advanced chips have been restricted since the Biden administration.
* Nvidia is currently subject to a pre-existing shareholder lawsuit alleging the company deceived investors.
