# Google Introduces Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash-Lite Text-to-Speech Models

Google has introduced two new text-to-speech (TTS) models to the Gemini family, expanding voice generation from static presets into a dynamic creative studio for creators, developers, and enterprises.

## ⚙️ Model Overview

| Model | Primary Purpose | Key Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS | Deep creative direction and character design | Natural language prompts for new voices; granular control over acting, pacing, and dialect |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS | High-volume, cost-efficient scale | Optimized for dubbing, audio content creation, and expressive voice agents |

## 🧩 Key Capabilities

*   **Voice Creation and Customization**: Users can create bespoke voices from scratch using natural language prompting to customize role, accent, and characteristics across more than 100 languages and dialects. This includes access to over 2,000 production-ready voices with regional varieties such as Scots English, Quebec French, and Mexican Spanish.
*   **Performance Direction**: The models allow for line-by-line direction using stage directions or natural script cues. Users can add realistic textures via scripted vocal bursts and backchanneling, such as `<laughs>`, `<sigh>`, `<gasp>`, and interjections like `|mhm|` or `|yeah|`.
*   **Advanced Audio Production**: 
    *   **Long-form generation**: Maintains quality and timbre across hours of audio with minimal speaker drift.
    *   **Native two-speaker scene staging**: Directs multi-turn conversations from a single script while keeping voices distinctly separated.
*   **Voice Replication**: Consistent vocal profiles can be recreated from a 30-second audio sample of a voice the user has the rights to use.

## 💡 Performance and Benchmarks

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS holds the #1 overall spot on Hume AI’s Voice Design Benchmark (71.4) and leads in accent modeling (60.8). Both the Flash and Flash-Lite models secured the #1 and #2 spots, respectively, on Hume AI’s Overall Quality Index. Additionally, blind human preference evaluations on Voice Arena placed these models in top positions for several global languages, including Hindi, Vietnamese, Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, and Mexican Spanish.

## 🛡️ Safety and Transparency

To protect voice talent and ensure transparency, Google has implemented several safeguards:

*   **Consent Verification**: Voice replication requires a verbal consent recording from the voice owner that matches the reference speaker.
*   **Watermarking**: Every audio clip generated by Gemini Audio models is watermarked with SynthID, an imperceptible watermark woven into the audio output.
*   **Credentials**: The system utilizes C2PA credentials to protect developers and vocal talent.
