# OpenAI hires former Patreon executives to lead creator product strategy

OpenAI announced it is bringing three former Patreon executives onto its creator‑product team. The move gives OpenAI the talent behind the modern creator‑subscription economy.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI hired three former Patreon execs to anchor its product strategy for creators.
- Sam Yam, co‑founder and technology chief of Patreon, will lead Creator Product at OpenAI.
- Former Patreon product head Drew Rowny and engineering head Shannon Ma have also joined the new venture.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Yam announced his move on X, noting: "We’re going to build together with Creators at OpenAI and share early access to a new set of tools that I think will be critically valuable to Creators and their communities."
- He also urged: "Pay attention to OpenAI DevDay next week! I’m becoming active today (and experimenting with some innovative early tools here!)"
- OpenAI DevDay is scheduled for Tuesday, September 29th, and an Altman post teases another big announcement later in the week.
- The hiring suggests OpenAI is exploring entry into the creator‑monetization market, building on Patreon’s subscription‑economy blueprint.

## 📋 Team Table
| Person | Former Patreon Role |
|---|---|
| Sam Yam | Co‑founder & Technology Chief |
| Drew Rowny | Product Head |
| Shannon Ma | Engineering Head |

## 💡 Why it matters
- By adding the team that built Patreon’s subscription platform, OpenAI positions itself to offer tools that help creators monetize and grow their communities.
- Patreon faces a talent loss as Yam departs, shortly after announcing a major feature roadmap and after a January layoff of 20 % of staff.
