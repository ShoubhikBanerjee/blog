# San Francisco to host Birds of a Feather session on agentic engineering

A community event focused on agentic engineering is scheduled to take place in San Francisco on Wednesday, October 14th. Hosted by Jesse Vincent, the gathering aims to bring together builders and experimenters working with coding agents.

## ⚙️ Key details

*   Date: Wednesday, 14th October 2026.
*   Location: San Francisco.
*   Format: An informal show-and-tell and a single flowing conversation.
*   Focus: Early-stage explorations, unfinished projects, and experimental work that has not been discussed publicly.

## 💡 Why it matters

The event is intended for those building unique or unconventional projects with coding agents. It serves as a space for participants to share experiences, compare notes, and discuss both successes and unresolved challenges, specifically excluding product pitches.
