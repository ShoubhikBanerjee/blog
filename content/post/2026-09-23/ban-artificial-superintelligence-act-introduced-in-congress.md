# Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act Introduced in Congress

Senator Bernie Sanders (I‑VT) and Representative Greg Casar (D‑TX) have introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act, which would prohibit the development of artificial superintelligence and pause advanced AI systems.

## 🔍 Overview
- The bill defines artificial superintelligence as technology capable of the “destruction or disempowerment of humanity,” including overthrowing the government.
- It also pauses development of “frontier models,” which lawmakers classify as advanced AI systems trained on a specific data threshold.

## ⚙️ How the Bill Works
- A scientist‑led Department of Artificial Intelligence would be created to oversee the technology.
- AI companies must obtain permission from this department before developing advanced AI models.
- The department would monitor frontier AI models, remove dangerous features, and supervise the destruction of artificial superintelligence.
- Development of advanced AI could resume only after the department is established.

## ⚖️ Penalties
- AI leaders who violate the law could face up to 20 years in prison.
- The same penalty applies to “rogue actors” who break the rules but are not employed by an AI company.
- The penalty is described as “similar to existing penalties related to unlawfully developing nuclear weapons.”

## 📊 Context
- A former Anthropic researcher warned there is more than a 10 percent chance AI could “kill all humans” by the end of the decade.
- Last week, California Governor Gavin Newsom proposed a “kill switch” for frontier models.
