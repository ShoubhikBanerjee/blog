# YouTube Announces AI Agent and New Creator Optimization Tools

At the annual Made on YouTube event, the company announced updates to the suite of AI-powered creator tools it first unveiled in 2025.

## ⚙️ Key details
YouTube is introducing an AI "agent" designed to optimize a creator's channel in the background. Its capabilities include:

* **Catalog Monitoring**: The tool monitors back catalogs for content that is trending, relevant to the news, or has taken on a new life, offering suggestions for title and thumbnail tweaks.
* **Content Generation**: The agent can generate entire titles and thumbnails based on an uploaded video's content.
* **Creator Feedback**: Creators can request feedback from the tool regarding their own thumbnails.
* **Brand Pitching**: According to Amjad Hanif, vice president of creator products at YouTube, the tool can create brand pitches by pulling demographic and audience data from a creator's channel.

## 🧩 How it works
YouTube is introducing several A/B testing and optimization features to boost watch time:

| Feature | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| Dynamic Thumbnails | Assigns one of up to three uploaded images to different audience segments. |
| Video Testing | Tests three versions of a video (e.g., different hooks or structures) with small audience segments. |

Regarding video testing, Hanif says the system ensures the three versions are not "dramatically different." Creators can manually select a "winner" version or allow YouTube to do so automatically after seven days.
