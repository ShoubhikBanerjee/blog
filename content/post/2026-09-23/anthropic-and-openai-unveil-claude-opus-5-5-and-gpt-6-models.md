# Anthropic and OpenAI Unveil Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT‑6 Models with Aggressive Pricing

Today Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, and within an hour OpenAI released GPT‑6 Sol and GPT‑6 Luna.

## 🔍 Overview
- Claude Opus 5.5 arrives with a 20 % price reduction to $4 / $20 per million input/output tokens.
- OpenAI introduced two GPT‑6 variants: Sol and Luna, both priced significantly lower than their GPT‑5.6 predecessors.
- GPT‑6 Luna is priced at one tenth of the current Haiku 4.5 pricing ($1 / $5).
- Claude Opus 5.5 supports a maximum output of 128,000 tokens.

## ⚙️ Pricing Details
| Model | Input ($/M tokens) | Output ($/M tokens) |
|-------|--------------------|---------------------|
| GPT‑6 Luna | 0.10 | 0.50 |
| GPT‑5.6 Luna | 0.20 | 1.20 |
| Grok 4.7 | 2 | 6 |
| GPT‑6 Sol | 2 | 10 |
| GPT‑5.6 Terra | 2 | 12 |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 4 | 20 |
| GPT‑5.6 Sol | 4 | 20 |
| Claude Fable 5.1 | 10 | 50 |
| GPT‑6 Astra | 10 | 50 |
| GPT‑4.1 Nano | 0.10 | 0.40 |
| GPT‑5 Nano | 0.05 | 0.40 |

## 📊 Comparison
- At $0.10 / $0.50, GPT‑6 Luna is among the cheapest OpenAI models, beaten only by the weaker GPT‑4.1 Nano and GPT‑5 Nano.
- GPT‑6 Sol’s $2 / $10 pricing is half the promotional price of GPT‑5.6 Sol ($4 / $20).
- Grok 4.7’s $2 / $6 pricing is less than half of GPT‑5.6 Sol and now matches GPT‑6 Sol on input cost.
- GPT‑6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 share the same $10 / $50 pricing tier.

## 🚀 Availability
- Both Anthropic and OpenAI announced their new models on the same day, with Claude Opus 5.5 released first and GPT‑6 Sol and Luna following within an hour.
- Anthropic indicated that Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are slated for future release.
