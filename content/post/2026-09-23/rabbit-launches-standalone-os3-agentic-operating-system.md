# Rabbit Launches Standalone OS3 Agentic Operating System

Rabbit is releasing a standalone AI agent called OS3, an "agentic operating system" that can be used without the company's R1 hardware.

## 🧩 How it works
OS3 runs in the cloud but operates locally across Windows, Mac, and Linux devices. The system is designed to automatically determine the following components required to complete a task:
* Devices
* Files
* Apps
* AI models

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Account Capacity:** Users can add up to five devices and their preferred AI models to one account.
* **Privacy:** Rabbit claims OS3 will not store, copy, use, or sell user data, though it will keep chats and associated memories on its servers. Data handled by other linked AI providers is subject to those providers' own privacy rules.

## 🚀 Availability
OS3 can be accessed through several methods:
* A dedicated desktop site
* Paired messaging apps such as iMessage or Telegram
* The Rabbit R1 device

## 💡 Why it matters
Founder Jesse Lyu has informed Wired that Rabbit has stopped manufacturing R1 devices. However, the company is focusing on a new vibe-coding "cyberdeck" designed to run OS3, which is expected to launch within "months."
