# GPT-6 Sol and Luna Generally Available on Amazon Bedrock

GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna are now generally available on Amazon Bedrock, giving developers two new model options tuned for different workload patterns.

## 🔍 Overview
- Both models are offered on Amazon Bedrock with a high‑performance inference engine.
- Pricing is significantly lower than the GPT‑5.6 predecessors.
- The GPT‑6 family also includes GPT‑6 Astra for the highest‑quality, cost‑less‑concern projects.

## 🛠️ Model Variants
| Model | Primary focus |
|------|----------------|
| GPT‑6 Sol | Strong reasoning and coding for regular, complex tasks |
| GPT‑6 Luna | High‑volume, repeatable tasks with low latency and cost |
| GPT‑6 Astra | Upper‑end quality for ambitious projects where cost is secondary |

## ⚙️ Capabilities
- **GPT‑6 Sol**
  - Implements features, debugs, refactors, and reviews code.
  - Analyzes data and runs multistep processes across tools and applications.
  - Improves coding and computer‑use ability over GPT‑5.6 Sol.
  - Makes roughly half as many factual mistakes as GPT‑5.6 Sol in internal tests.
  - Communicates results more clearly, helping teams spot gaps early.
- **GPT‑6 Luna**
  - Extracts information from large document collections.
  - Summarizes incoming material and classifies inputs.
  - Answers focused questions at scale for many users or applications.
  - Shows better factual reliability and clearer result communication than GPT‑5.6 Luna.
  - Allows per‑request reasoning effort adjustment to balance quality, responsiveness, and cost.
- Both models support explicit prompt caching on Bedrock, letting repeated calls reuse instructions, tool definitions, policies, and reference material.

## 💰 Pricing & Reliability
- API pricing is notably lower than that of GPT‑5.6 models.
- Internal evaluations report halved factual errors for Sol and improved factual reliability for Luna.

## 🔐 Security & Governance
- Access can be controlled with AWS IAM policies and audited via AWS CloudTrail.
- VPC endpoints powered by AWS PrivateLink keep traffic within network boundaries.
- Inference runs on hardware‑isolated infrastructure with zero‑operator access; AWS staff cannot view prompts or completions.
- Inference data is not used for model training, and no opt‑in to share data with OpenAI is required.
- Classifier‑flagged traffic is retained up to 30 days for abuse detection; zero‑retention can be requested.

## 🚀 Availability
- GPT‑6 Sol and GPT‑6 Luna are generally available on Amazon Bedrock today.
- They run on an inference engine built for high performance, security, and reliability at scale.
