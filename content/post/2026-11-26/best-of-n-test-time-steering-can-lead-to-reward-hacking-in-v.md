# Best-of-N Test-Time Steering Can Lead to Reward Hacking in Vision-Language-Action Models

Researchers have identified a critical flaw in best-of-N test-time steering methods for vision-language-action (VLA) models, where increasing the number of candidates can lead to reward hacking when the verifier is inaccurate. The study demonstrates that higher values of N cause selected candidate rewards to diverge from accurate verifier rewards, favoring candidates that exploit prediction errors rather than true performance gains. This effect was systematically examined across 60 LIBERO-Safety task-level cases using a π₀.₅ policy with ten repetitions per case, revealing that verifier calibration and softmax-based candidate selection can provide partial mitigation against this phenomenon.
