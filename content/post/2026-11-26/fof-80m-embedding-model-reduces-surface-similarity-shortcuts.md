# FOF-80M embedding model reduces surface similarity shortcuts in factual text retrieval

## 💡 Why it matters

Text embedding models that conflate surface-form similarity with factual identity can lead to misleading results in information retrieval and semantic search applications. FOF-80M demonstrates that targeted contrastive training can reduce this shortcut, producing embeddings that better preserve factual content under stylistic variation. This approach provides a foundation for building more factually robust embedding systems.
