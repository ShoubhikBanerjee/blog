# New Meta-Learning Approach Enables Statistical Inference Without Parameter Updates

Researchers have introduced a novel approach to statistical inference using meta-learning, where neural networks learn inference rules from simulated tasks and apply them to new datasets without parameter updates, enabling a form of in-context learning. The method leverages statistical decision theory to design training objectives whose population minimizers are Bayes rules, and connects these to classical inference procedures to establish frequentist guarantees for the learned rules.
