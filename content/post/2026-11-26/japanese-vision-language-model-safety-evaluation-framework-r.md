# Japanese Vision-Language Model Safety Evaluation Framework Released with New Dataset

Researchers have extended the Multimodal Safety Test Suite (MSTS) to Japanese, creating the 12th language version with machine-translated and post-edited test cases to address the lack of multimodal safety evaluation in non-English settings. The new evaluation framework introduces a 5-point safety scale that rewards useful responses and separates image recognition from safety judgment to isolate recognition errors. Testing five Japanese VLMs — Gemini-3-Flash, GPT-5, InternVL3, LLM-JP-4-VL, and Qwen3-VL — the study found substantial variance in safety readiness across models. The research also evaluated two LLM-as-a-Judge approaches, discovering that reference answers improve agreement with human judgments, though recall for detecting unsafe responses remains limited. The complete dataset will be released upon publication.
