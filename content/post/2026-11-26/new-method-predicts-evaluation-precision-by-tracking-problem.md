# New method predicts evaluation precision by tracking problem sources in AI benchmarks

Researchers have introduced a method to forecast how much the standard deviation of AI model evaluation scores will grow when an evaluation budget is concentrated on fewer, shared problem sources rather than diverse ones. The approach uses pilot data to predict precision before running full evaluations, helping planners avoid misleading results from overly concentrated sampling. The technique was validated on GSM-Symbolic and SWE-bench Verified benchmarks, showing accurate predictions when finite-population terms are included in the design effect calculation.
