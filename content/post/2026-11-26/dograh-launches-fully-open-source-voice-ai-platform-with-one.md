# Dograh Launches Fully Open‑Source Voice AI Platform with One‑Click Self‑Hosting

Dograh, an open‑source voice AI platform, is now available as a self‑hosted alternative to Vapi and Retell.

## 🔍 Overview
- 100% open source and self‑hostable, eliminating vendor lock‑in.
- Licensed under the BSD 2‑Clause license.
- Maintained by YC alumni and exit founders who are committed to keeping voice AI open.

## 🧩 How It Works
- **Visual workflow builder** enables building and testing production voice agents in minutes.
- **MCP native support** lets AI coding assistants help design and edit agents.
- **One‑click self‑hosting**: run a single Docker command to get the platform up in about 60 seconds.
  ```bash
  curl -o docker-compose.yaml https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dograh-hq/dograh/main/docker-compose.yaml
  curl -o start_docker.sh https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dograh-hq/dograh/main/scripts/start_docker.sh
  ```
- Remote deployment is supported via documentation.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)** across Speech‑to‑Speech, LLM, STT, and TTS.
- Flexible integration with any LLM, STT, or TTS provider, or use Dograh’s own stack.
- Full source‑level customization – every line of code is open and modifiable.
- Data residency is under your control: your infrastructure, your rules.
- Pricing: free for self‑hosted use; cloud usage is billed on a usage‑based model.
- Telemetry is optional; it can be disabled by setting `ENABLE_TELEMETRY=false` before startup.

## 🚀 Availability
- **Cloud trial** available at https://app.dograh.com.
- Join the community on Slack (link labeled “Community”).
- Featured by Better Stack and highlighted on Product Hunt as #1 Product of the Day, Week, and Month in Developer Tools.
- The project has garnered 31,007 stars on GitHub (Trendshift).

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Provides a self‑hosted, open‑source alternative to proprietary voice AI services like Vapi and Retell.
- Eliminates vendor lock‑in and offers transparency through an open codebase.
- Enables organizations to keep voice data on‑premises, meeting strict data residency requirements.
- Offers developers a visual, low‑code way to create voice agents while still allowing deep customization.
