# Google Patents My Channel: AI-Generated Personalized Videos

Google filed a patent for a system called My Channel that aims to create personalized, AI-generated videos and advertisements using user data. The patent has been rejected, but the company may appeal or resubmit an amended version.

## 🔍 Overview
My Channel is designed to generate feeds filled with personalized content based on information Google knows about its users. First spotted by Patentlyze, the patent — filed last May and published last week — describes a system that uses demographic data and viewing history to create prompts fed into an LLM for generating AI videos tailored to individual interests.

## 🧩 How it works
The system would capture contextual data from users' devices, including IP addresses, GPS locations, and age details. It could also base prompts on current news like sports scores and social media activity, with all video generation occurring on Google's servers.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Create entire movies of various genres
- Connect to other streaming services for more user data and training content
- Generate digital avatars of users to serve as protagonists in AI-generated content

According to the patent, if a user watches many videos about Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, My Channel might produce content similar to Ash Koosha's Odysseus: The Fall.
