# OpenRLHF 0.10 Adds Multi-Turn VLM RLHF with FlashREINFORCE Support

OpenRLHF 0.10 introduces Multi-Turn Vision-Language Model (VLM) Reinforcement Learning and VLM RLHF support, enabling end-to-end training of models like Qwen3.5 with image inputs. The framework also adds FlashREINFORCE, a critic-free, single-rollout asynchronous RL approach for agentic language models. Additional improvements include ProRL V2 integration for prolonged training and the MARTI fork for multi-agent system training.
