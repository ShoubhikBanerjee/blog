# Google Updates Privacy Policy to Allow Targeted Ads from AI‑Linked Data

Google has updated its privacy policy, adding language that could let the company serve personalized ads based on data you share with its AI features such as Personal Intelligence.

## 🔍 Overview
- The change was first spotted by Android Authority.
- An exception was added to the promise that Google would not use Drive, Gmail, or Google Photos for personalized ads.
- The new wording says Google may use these services *"unless you choose to make data from these services available to other Google services that support personalized ads"* (effective October 16).

## 📜 Policy Details
- Users who allow services like Search or Gemini Apps to access data from Google Photos, Gmail, or Drive enable a more personalized experience, **subject to the policies and settings of the accessing service**.
- The policy explicitly states that Search may process the data you permit it to access to personalize content, **including ads**.
- Depending on your settings, Google may show you personalized ads based on your interests, activity, or personalized content from Google services.
- Example given: searching for “mountain bikes” could lead to ads for sports equipment on YouTube.

## 🎯 How Ads May Be Served
- Sharing your data for better AI recommendations now also opens that data up for more personalized ads.
- Anyone who has connected Drive, Gmail, or Google Photos to AI Mode or other Search tools via Personal Intelligence may see ads targeted on the basis of that content.
- Advertisers **don’t get access** to your Drive, Gmail, and Photos information or personally identifying details such as your name or email address.
- Google says it **won’t show ads based on sensitive categories** like race, sexual orientation, religion, or health.

## ⚙️ How to Opt Out
- You can prevent your personal data from being used for targeted ads by disconnecting apps from Search’s Personal Intelligence features.
- Alternatively, disable **Personalized Recommendations** via the Search Services Personalization settings page.
- Adjusting these settings controls whether your data is used to personalize ads.
