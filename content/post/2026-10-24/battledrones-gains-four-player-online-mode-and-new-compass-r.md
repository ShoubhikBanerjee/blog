# Battledrones Gains Four-Player Online Mode and New Compass Rose Map

On 24 October 2026, the developer added a four‑player online mode to Battledrones, expanding the previous two‑player limit.

## 🔍 Overview
- Four‑player online mode added.
- New map "Compass Rose" with four‑way rotational symmetry.
- Supports up to four human players via WebSocket non‑audio multiplayer; spare slots filled by AI.
- New team colors: purple and green, alongside existing blue and red.

## 🧩 How it works
- The WebSockets network code was modified to handle up to four connections for the "Compass Rose" map.
- Players can join using share links or QR codes.
- If fewer than four human players join, AI opponents fill the remaining slots.

## ⚙️ Key details
| Map | Features |
|---|---|
| Compass Rose | Four‑way rotational symmetry, up to four AI players, WebSocket non‑audio multiplayer, join via share link or QR code |

## 🚀 Availability
- Release date: 24 October 2026.
- Colors can be previewed in the updated model browser.
- A replay of a four‑way AI battle with the "Goliath Armada" mutator (each side starts with four giant Goliath mech units) is available.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Increases maximum concurrent human players from two to four.
- Introduces two additional team color schemes, expanding visual variety.
- Enables flexible multiplayer sessions using share links or QR codes.
