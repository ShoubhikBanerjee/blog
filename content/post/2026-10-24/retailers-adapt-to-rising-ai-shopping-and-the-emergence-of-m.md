# Retailers adapt to rising AI shopping and the emergence of Muse agents

The Vergecast highlighted a recent surge in AI‑driven shopping and how it is pushing retailers to rethink customer interactions, now referred to as *Muse agents*.

## 🔍 Overview
- An “ongoing increase in AI shopping” is shaping how retailers operate online.
- The hosts framed this shift as a core issue for the future of the web, AI, and everything.

## 🛒 Impact on Retailers
- Retailers are “forced to figure out how they want to interact with customers — and by customers, we mean Muse agents.”
- This implies a need to redesign engagement strategies, though specific solutions were not detailed.

## 🤖 What Are Muse Agents?
- The term *Muse agents* is used by the hosts to describe the AI‑powered entities that act as customers in this new shopping landscape.
- No further definition or technical description was provided beyond the naming.

## 💭 Why It Matters
- The discussion connects the AI shopping trend directly to broader questions about the future of the web and AI applications.
- Understanding how Muse agents influence retail could become a focal point for future web and AI development conversations.
