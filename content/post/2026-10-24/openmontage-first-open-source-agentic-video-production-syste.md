# OpenMontage: First Open‑Source Agentic Video Production System

The open‑source community released OpenMontage, described as the world’s first open‑source, agentic video production system.

## 🔍 Overview
- World's first open‑source, agentic video production system.
- Provides 12 production pipelines, over 100 tools, and more than 700 agent skill and production‑knowledge files.
- Turns an AI coding assistant into a full video production studio.

## 🧩 How it works
- The agent builds a corpus from free stock footage and open archives.
- It retrieves actual motion clips, edits them into a timeline, and renders a finished piece.
- OpenMontage can make image‑based videos and also make a real **video video** for free/open‑source workflows.

## ⚙️ Key details
- License: AGPLv3.
- Website: https://openmontage.video.
- Follow The Build.

## 🚀 Availability
OpenMontage is released under the AGPLv3 license and can be accessed at https://openmontage.video.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enables free/open‑source video creation pipelines that combine stock‑footage retrieval, AI‑generated motion clips, and open‑source rendering tools.
- Demonstrates end‑to‑end production—from concept to final render—at costs as low as $1.33 for a 60‑second short.
