# AI Tool Developed to Identify Reply Bots on Bluesky

A new tool has been developed using Opus 5.5 to examine Bluesky profiles for evidence of likely reply bots.

## 🧩 How it works
The tool analyzes profiles for specific signals to identify bot behavior:

* Replies posted within seconds of other posts from the same account
* Accounts that consistently reply to messages from higher-follower users without posting their own content, images, or links
* The presence of question marks

## ⚙️ Key details
The development of this tool was made possible because Bluesky has a freely available and useful API, unlike Twitter, where automated reply bots are described as a scourge and have begun manifesting on Bluesky as well.
