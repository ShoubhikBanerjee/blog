# Thoughtful Things Launches Engram AI Sampler and Groovebox on Kickstarter

Music startup Thoughtful Things has launched a Kickstarter campaign for Engram, a sampler and groovebox that utilizes AI to process audio and generate new sounds.

## 🔍 Overview
Engram is designed for experimental sound design and creating uncanny sounds rather than producing radio-ready music. Founder Evan King describes the device as a “field recorder for latent space,” focusing on exploring AI hallucinations and pushing audio models beyond their limits.

## 🧩 How it works
Unlike cloud-based AI tools, Engram operates without an internet connection. Its technical specifications include:

* **Local Processing:** Runs a “tiny AI” locally on the device.
* **Custom Model:** Uses an in-house, custom-trained model.
* **Training Data:** Models are trained on open datasets containing audio licensed for commercial use (CC-BY or similar). The company states it has not and will not train models on non-commercial, pirated, or stolen data.
* **Circuit Bending:** The device allows users to tweak or break its AI models, drawing inspiration from circuit bending.
* **Open Firmware:** Thoughtful Things plans to open the firmware to allow users to tweak the system or load custom models.

## ⚙️ Key details
In a campaign video, founder Evan King demonstrated the device by using his voice to ask for “piano,” resulting in a glitchy, vaguely piano-like sound.
