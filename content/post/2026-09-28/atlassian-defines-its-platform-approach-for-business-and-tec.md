# Atlassian Defines Its Platform Approach for Business and Technology Collaboration

Atlassian, led by cofounder and CEO Mike Cannon-Brookes, provides a platform that enables businesses to collaborate and manage work across their teams.

## 🔍 Overview
Atlassian connects business and technology teams to handle challenging work problems within a singular platform. For organizations where software and technology are core competitive advantages, the platform allows them to:
* Collaborate on content
* Manage projects
* Unleash knowledge across business, strategy, operations, and service teams

## ⚙️ Key details
All Atlassian products are different expressions of a single core platform. This platform allows people to organize and manage big teams and create shared databases of company information.

| Product | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Trello | Understanding work at an individual level |
| Jira | Understanding work at a team level; organizing software engineers, filing tickets, and prioritizing tasks |
| Strategy Collection | Understanding work at an organizational level for large-scale strategy and operations |

Regarding Jira, Mike Cannon-Brookes states it is a "human reference to work," noting that developers do not live in Jira and the work is not done within it.
