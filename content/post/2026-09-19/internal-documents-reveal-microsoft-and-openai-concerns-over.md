# Internal Documents Reveal Microsoft and OpenAI Concerns Over AI Content Strategy

Internal documents and employee statements from Microsoft and OpenAI indicate that the companies' AI content strategy may be damaging the web and the economic foundations of the data sources used to train their models.

## 💡 Why it matters

Internal Microsoft documentation describes a "doom loop" where the company's AI content strategy threatens the "content supply chain" by creating an end-product that threatens its essential suppliers. Key concerns include:

* **Supply Chain Impact**: Microsoft admitted that "LLMs are a product that destroys its own supply chain" because they can act as a substitute for the training data they rely on.
* **Web Traffic Decline**: OpenAI experts attributed a drop in referral traffic for sites like the Times directly to AI summaries; they speculated search referrals may be down as much as 60 percent.
* **User Behavior**: Satya Nadella admitted chatbots have basically replaced search and removed the need to go straight to the source. OpenAI's Nick Turley stated that once a user gets an answer from a chatbot, there is "no good reason to click" on a source link.

## ⚙️ Key details

Documents and employees have highlighted several issues regarding data acquisition and model output:

* **Data Scraping**: Hecht characterized the harvesting of data for ChatGPT and Copilot as the "largest theft of labor in human history" and claimed Microsoft's defense makes a "complete mockery of the idea of 'fair use.'"
* **Content Regurgitation**: Employees admitted GPT-4 "memorized a ton of data and therefore will be insanely good at regurgitation." Examples show ChatGPT outputting long strings of copy from the Times, Mercury News, The Denver Post, LifeHacker, and Eurogamer.
* **Paywalls**: An OpenAI representative admitted to being "unaware" of any effort to remove paywalled content from training data.
* **Labor Impact**: OpenAI Policy Director Jack Clark stated the company was "creating systems that substitute for the labor of the people that define the ‘culture’ of society."
