# Hollywood unions lock in AI guardrails as tech firms urge industry slowdown

Hollywood unions and writers have secured enforceable guardrails for generative AI in entertainment, while heads of major AI companies are calling for an industry‑wide slowdown.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI models that generate digital facsimiles of actors and replicate traditional filmmaking tools "pose a threat to some human jobs." 
- The AI tools used in entertainment production differ from the AI systems that have sparked recent whistleblowing and apocalyptic declarations.
- Anthropic alignment lead Evan Hubinger warned the technology could “kill all humans” within the next decade.

## 🛡️ Union Negotiations
- SAG‑AFTRA chief negotiator Duncan Crabtree‑Ireland said the union recognized early on how AI could impact its members’ livelihoods.
- "We bargained for and achieved enforceable, meaningful guardrails around how this technology can be implemented by the major employers in our industry," Crabtree‑Ireland explained.
- The guardrails **do not** include companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google directly, but do cover all U.S. entertainment content creators.
- WGAE executive director Sam Wheeler condemned the “tech oligarchs pushing AI on our society.”
- The WGAE has long warned that unregulated artificial intelligence poses an existential threat to workers, to our democracy, and to the world.
- Guild members have bargained meaningful AI protections into their contracts; in 2023 WGA members spent 148 days on strike before studios agreed to real safeguards for film and television writers.

## ⚙️ Industry Response
- Hollywood unions’ fight for strict rules around generative AI did not stop the entertainment industry from embracing AI.
- Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers (AMPTP) has been reluctant to adopt demands such as requiring studios to gain permission and compensate actors for AI‑generated replicas.
- Crabtree‑Ireland noted that without the principle of compensation, “the economic incentive to use replication and to replace human performances would have been much stronger” and would have resulted in “a much greater and faster adoption of digital replication.”

## 🚨 Risks & Concerns
- AI‑generated digital replicas threaten certain acting and filmmaking jobs.
- Prominent AI leaders (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, X) have called for an industry‑wide slowdown on the technology’s development.

## 🏛️ Political Context
- President Donald Trump dismissed AI concerns as overreactions.
- Some other U.S. politicians have felt pressured to take legislative action.
