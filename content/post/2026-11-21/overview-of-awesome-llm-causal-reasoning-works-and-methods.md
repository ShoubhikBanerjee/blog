# Overview of Awesome LLM Causal Reasoning Works and Methods

Awesome LLM Causal Reasoning is a collection of LLM-based causal reasoning works, including papers, codes, and datasets, providing a comprehensive review of research aimed at enhancing Large Language Models for causal reasoning (CR).

## 🧩 How it works

- Existing methods are categorized based on the role of LLMs: either as *<u>reasoning engines</u>* or as *<u>helpers</u>* providing knowledge or data to traditional CR methods.
- End tasks are categorized into three groups: causal discovery, causal inference, and additional causal tasks.
- Performance of LLMs is evaluated on various causal reasoning tasks using pass@1 accuracy with strategies such as zero-shot.
