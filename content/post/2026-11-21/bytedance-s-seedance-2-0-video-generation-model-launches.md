# ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 Video Generation Model Launches

ByteDance's Seedance 2.0, the industry's first model supporting simultaneous quad-modal input — image, video, audio, and text, has launched. This innovative tool offers a range of features and capabilities for video generation.

## 🔍 Overview

- Seedance 2.0 is a video generation model developed by ByteDance.
- It supports simultaneous quad-modal input — image, video, audio, and text.
- The model can generate videos up to 1080p resolution and 4–15 seconds duration.

## 🧩 How it works

- Text-to-Video — Generate videos from text descriptions
- Image-to-Video — Animate static images into dynamic videos
- Video-to-Video — Transform and extend existing videos
- Audio-Driven — Generate videos driven by audio input

## ⚙️ Key details

- Auto dubbing & scoring — Automatic voiceover and background music
- A curated collection of high-quality video generation prompts is available, including Seedance API guides, character consistency tips, and advanced video workflows.

## 🚀 Availability

- The curated collection of video generation prompts is available for educational purposes.

## 💡 Why it matters

- Seedance 2.0 offers a unique and innovative approach to video generation, with a wide range of features and capabilities.
- The model's support for simultaneous quad-modal input sets it apart from other video generation tools.
- The curated collection of video generation prompts provides users with a valuable resource for creating high-quality videos.
