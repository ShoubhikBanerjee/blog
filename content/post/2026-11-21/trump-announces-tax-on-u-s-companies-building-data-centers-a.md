# Trump Announces Tax on U.S. Companies Building Data Centers Abroad

President Donald Trump recently announced a plan to impose a significant tax on companies that build data centers outside the United States.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Trump promised to put a “tremendous tax” on companies that build data centers outside the U.S.
- He stated: “We’re not going to allow our companies to build data centers outside of our country.”
- Trump warned that “China will take them all” if U.S. companies build abroad.
- The proposal has no detailed plan and is being floated publicly for the first time.
- States and cities are installing moratoriums and new restrictions on data‑center proposals.
- A new Democratic majority Congress could make enforcement difficult.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The policy could affect where U.S. AI and data‑center companies decide to locate infrastructure.
- It reflects growing concerns about data security and national competition.
