# Anthropic Becomes First AI Lab to Go Public on the Nasdaq

Anthropic, the AI lab behind Claude, has gone public on the Nasdaq as the first AI lab to make the move, pricing its IPO at $83 a share and reaching up to $111.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Anthropic is the first AI lab to go public.
- The IPO was priced at $83 a share and rose up to $111.
- The company has become one of the most valuable companies on the planet.
- Thousands of new millionaires and hundreds of new centimillionaires, along with billionaires, are expected to be born from this.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The company is continuously growing its revenue to pay its ever-increasing compute bills, which total roughly $900 billion over the coming years.
- Anthropic's safety branding sits in conflict with the profit-oriented part of its business, and its corporate governance structure is a point of discussion.
- Tech workers inside various companies report that the AI bubble is swelling as they are tasked with doing more AI, even if it reduces productivity and work quality.
- Meanwhile, competitors like OpenAI have faced setbacks, public hatred, and scandals throughout the year.
