# Bell Canada Investigates Data Breach Linked to Agentic Threat Actor

Bell Canada has confirmed that an "agentic" threat actor breached its systems on November 17, 2026, and stole enterprise customer data. The unauthorized access targeted multiple systems, including a pre-production environment of the company's Maximo system, which is used to order and provision telecommunications network connections and circuits.
