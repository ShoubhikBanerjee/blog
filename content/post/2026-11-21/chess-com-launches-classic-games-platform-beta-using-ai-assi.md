# Chess.com Launches Classic Games Platform Beta Using AI Assisted Coding

Chess.com is expanding beyond chess with the launch of its new "Classic Games" platform. Built with the help of artificial intelligence for coding, the new platform brings 10 additional classic games to the company's ecosystem. The launch follows the release of Gambit, a gambling-free poker site, earlier this year, bringing the total number of new classic games added this year to 11.

## 🔍 Overview

The Classic Games platform is launching its public beta on Saturday on the web and through a new app for iOS and Android. The expansion introduces 10 new games, including strategy games, card games, and puzzles:

*   Backgammon
*   Checkers
*   Go
*   Hearts
*   Mahjong
*   Shogi
*   Sudoku
*   Solitaire
*   Xiangqi
*   Word (a Scrabble alternative)

## 🧩 How it works

Each game on the Classic Games platform incorporates the features that drove the growth of Chess.com's community:

*   **Lessons and Progress:** Every game features lessons, leaderboards, and a rating system.
*   **Bot Integration:** Multiplayer games like checkers or mahjong allow matches against other players or Chess.com's existing bot characters, including Martin, who remains the lowest-rated bot across Chess, Gambit, and Classic.
*   **Tailored Play Modes:** Play modes vary across games. Word and mahjong feature puzzles, sudoku has daily grids, and solitaire offers daily deals.

## ⚙️ Key details

AI played a key role in allowing Chess.com to launch 11 new games in a matter of months with a development team comprising less than 1 percent of the company.

*   **Infrastructure Reuse:** Because the new games are built on top of the existing Chess.com code base, design system, bot characters, and rating systems, developers can bring games to market quickly.
*   **AI for Coding:** The development team utilized AI almost entirely for coding tasks, accelerating the build process as they did with Gambit earlier this year.
*   **Human-Crafted Design:** AI was not used for the art and design of Classic Games, which remains carefully crafted by human designers.

## 💡 Why it matters

By building on an established infrastructure, Chess.com can spin up adjacent games much faster than starting from scratch. Lessons learned from this accelerated development process have also allowed Chess.com to ship more major features on its main site than in previous years. Chief growth officer Albert Cheng notes that while AI and chess have been intermixed for roughly 30 years, leveraging AI alongside a small team with strong product and design judgment allows the company to serve diverse classic gaming communities that lack a dedicated digital home.
