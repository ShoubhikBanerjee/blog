# Nothing announces first AI‑native device launching in December

Carl Pei, the CEO of Nothing, used his X post to confirm that the company’s first AI‑native device will ship next month, in December.  The announcement follows earlier statements about “AI‑native” devices and an AI operating system.

## 🔍 Overview
- Pei posted that the first AI‑native product ships next month.
- He emphasized that the device **has no new screen** and **couldn’t have been an app**.
- The product is described as **"a tool, but one built with the personality and care that have come to define a Nothing product."**

## ⚙️ Key details
- No screen – the device is entirely screen‑free.
- Not an app – Pei said it "couldn’t have been an app."
- Positioned as a **tool** rather than a traditional consumer gadget.
- The announcement comes a little over a year after Pei announced plans for “AI‑native” devices and an AI operating system.
- Earlier rumors (from the previous month) suggested Nothing might launch two pairs of AI smart glasses, including a version without cameras, and that the company is "repositioning itself as an AI‑first company" with a shift to AI accessories.

## 🚀 Availability
- The device is slated to ship **next month** (December).
- No further specifications or pricing were revealed in the post.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Nothing’s screen‑free AI device joins a small line of similar concepts such as the Humane AI Pin, the Friend, and AI‑powered stuffed animals.
- Pei remarked that "the whole industry is hunting for its iPhone moment: the pendant, the pin, the glasses, the companion, some magical consumer device that makes AI part of everyone’s life," but also added, "Looking for the iPhone of AI today is a dead end."
- The launch signals Nothing’s move toward AI‑first hardware, aligning with broader industry interest in dedicated AI accessories.
