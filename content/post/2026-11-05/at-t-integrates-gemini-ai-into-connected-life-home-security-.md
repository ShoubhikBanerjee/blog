# AT&T Integrates Gemini AI Into Connected Life Home Security System

AT&T has integrated its Connected Life smart home security system with Gemini for Home, making it the first home security product to support Google Home Premium. This update enables a suite of Gemini-powered features that allow users to summarize home events and search through video history using natural language.

## 🔍 Overview
The integration leverages Google’s Gemini-powered Home APIs, which allow smart home brands and developers to build AI-driven features into their devices. This development follows the initial launch of the Connected Life system last December, which AT&T created in partnership with Google and Abode.

## ⚙️ Key details
The system now supports several AI-enhanced capabilities for monitoring home activity:
* **Natural Language Search:** Users can query recorded footage with specific questions such as "What dug up the yard last night?" or "Who let the dogs out?"
* **Detailed Event Descriptions:** Gemini can provide specific details about captured events, such as identifying who delivered a package and how many items they dropped off.
* **Activity Summaries:** Users can review daily summaries of activity occurring both inside and outside the home.
* **Automated Arming:** AT&T is adding the ability to automatically arm or disarm the security system based on the user's proximity to their home.

## 🚀 Availability
These Gemini-powered features are not available on all tiers of service. Access to these tools requires:
* **Plan:** AT&T’s professional Connected Life monitoring plan.
* **Cost:** $29.99 per month.

## 💡 Why it matters
While AT&T is the first to integrate these Google Home Premium features, other companies are expected to follow. ADT has also hinted at plans to bring Gemini for Home features into its own security systems.
