# Qualcomm pivots to AI data‑center chips as smartphone sales slump

Qualcomm announced a shift toward AI data‑center chips to offset a roughly 20 % decline in smartphone sales compared with 2025.

## 📉 Market backdrop
- Smartphone sales are tanking by around 20 % compared to 2025, prompting Qualcomm to look to data centers to boost its bottom line.

## 🤝 Partnerships
- Qualcomm is pursuing AI data‑center opportunities through partnerships with **Amazon** and **Meta**, announced earlier this year.

## 📊 Revenue forecasts
- The company forecasts **around $650 million** in data‑center revenue for the first quarter of 2027.
- It expects that revenue to grow to **$5 billion by the end of 2027**.
- The long‑term target is **$15 billion by 2029**.

## ⚙️ Business ambition
- Qualcomm expects data‑center chips will eventually account for **about 30 % of its business**.
- With total 2026 revenue of **$10.4 billion**, that share would represent a significant portion of earnings.
- The company confirmed during its Q4 2026 earnings call that data‑center chips are a core growth area.

## 📈 Why it matters
- The move shows there is “plenty of demand that needs to be filled and enough AI money to go around.”
- Although Qualcomm’s 30 % target is less aggressive than AMD’s goal of over 80 % of revenue from data centers, it signals a major strategic shift for the chipmaker.
