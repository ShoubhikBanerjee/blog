# SpaceX renames Superintelligence division to Artificial Intelligence amid midterm spending reports

SpaceX has transitioned the name of its "Superintelligence" division back to "Artificial Intelligence." This administrative change occurs as reports detail that Elon Musk spent nearly $500 million on midterm elections, including significant financial involvement in Texas state politics.

## ⚙️ Key details
* The internal division previously known as "Superintelligence" at SpaceX has been renamed to "Artificial Intelligence."
* This change in nomenclature was described as being free to implement.
* Simultaneously, Elon Musk was observed deleting social media posts following the conclusion of election cycles.

## 🔍 Overview
Elon Musk directed nearly $500 million toward midterm elections. A specific portion of this funding, totaling $47 million from America PAC, was used in an effort to defeat Texas Democrat James Talarico. Talarico won the election with 55 percent of the vote against Musk’s preferred candidate, Ken Paxton. In his campaign, Talarico utilized the slogan "Texas is not for sale" to highlight Musk's financial influence.
