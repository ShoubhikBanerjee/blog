# SoundCloud adds ElevenLabs AI music integration

SoundCloud announced a new partnership with ElevenLabs, expanding its suite of generative AI music tools.

## 🔍 Overview
- SoundCloud is partnering with another generative AI music service, ElevenLabs.
- This follows an earlier partnership with Moises earlier this year.

## ⚙️ Integration Details
- The integration allows songs generated by ElevenLabs to be published directly to SoundCloud.
- Songs published from ElevenLabs will be labeled to make their AI origins clear.
- The ElevenLabs integration isn’t as deep as the Moises integration.
- Moises lets users pull tracks from SoundCloud for stem separation and remixing, then republish them directly to SoundCloud.

## 🎁 Artist Benefits
- SoundCloud Artist Pro subscribers receive three months of ElevenMusic Pro for free, enabling experimentation with generative AI.

## 📍 Labeling & Compliance
- SoundCloud encourages users subject to EU rules to label tracks as “AI‑generated or manipulated.”
- The platform maintains rules against impersonation.

## 📊 Why it matters
- SoundCloud appears more willing to embrace AI‑generated music than other streaming services.
- It has avoided loud‑and‑proud press releases about removing spammy AI songs or demonetizing them.
