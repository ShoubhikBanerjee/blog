# AutoClip MCP – Auto‑based Video Re‑editing and AI Agents Platform

## 🧩 Overview
AutoClip is an open‑source, local‑first AI video clipping and Machine‑Learning‑control (MCP) server that runs entirely on your computer without any subscription or upload queues. It adds smart reframing, automatic captions, custom covers, titles, subtitle generation, and live‑stream replay in a single workflow.

## 🔍 How it works
- Start with a video, links, or live‑stream replay.
- AutoClip identifies the best moments and speaker tracks, applies OCR for subtitles.
- Generates relevant custom covers, thumbnails and ready‑to‑post titles.
- Exports the final clip for TikTok, Shorts, Reels, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube Shorts, etc.
- Supports Ollama/LM Studio off‑line mode, any API key, and 6 live‑replay sources (OBS, Twitch, YouTube, Discords, Kick, local recordings).
- Provides live replication options via OBS WebSocket (4455) with loss‑less trim and auto‑queues.
- Additional capabilities include game‑highlight detection, unified one‑click clip cards, multilingual UI, local real‑time processing, and collaborations with external AI APIs.
