# Adaptive Security launches AI agents for email threat detection

Adaptive Security has introduced AI agents that analyze email intent, context, and behavior to uncover phishing, business email compromise, and invoice fraud.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI agents analyze email intent, context, and behavior.
- They aim to uncover phishing, business email compromise, and invoice fraud.

## ⚙️ How it works
- Uses AI to evaluate intent, context, and behavior of email messages.
- Provides a 14‑day read‑only scan of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace with no MX changes.

## 🚀 Features
- Detects threats that existing defenses may miss.
- Offers a free email security scan.

## 📦 Availability
- The 14‑day read‑only scan is available without requiring MX changes.
