# AI firms ramp up lobbying spend in Q3 2024 ahead of midterm elections

It was a busy summer for AI lobbyists. Anthropic, OpenAI and Nvidia each recorded their highest‑spending lobbying quarter between July and September, collectively spending nearly $6 million on issues such as cybersecurity and child safety.

## 📊 Spending Overview
- Total lobbying spend by the three AI firms in Q3 2024: **~$6 million**.
- **Nvidia** led the group with **$2.2 million**.
- **Anthropic** followed with **$2.1 million**.
- **OpenAI** spent **$1.5 million**.

| Company   | Q3 2024 Lobbying Spend | Notable Topics |
|-----------|-----------------------|----------------|
| Nvidia    | $2.2 million           | 6G networks, responsible AI design, competition policy, quantum computing, export control policy |
| Anthropic | $2.1 million           | Kids Online Safety Act (KOSA), AI Overwatch Act export provisions, Trump’s “Super Intelligence” executive order, Ratepayer Protection Act |
| OpenAI    | $1.5 million           | Copyright, cybersecurity evaluations for frontier models, AI safety oversight frameworks, antitrust protections for information sharing |

## 🏛️ Policy Areas Targeted
- Cybersecurity evaluations for frontier models
- Child‑online safety (KOSA)
- Export‑control provisions (AI Overwatch Act)
- AI safety oversight and responsible AI design
- Competition and antitrust issues
- 6G network development and quantum computing
- Energy cost allocation for data centers (Ratepayer Protection Act)

## 🤝 Political Context
- Tech CEOs attended a White House lunch with President Donald Trump in late September, resulting in a "morally binding" AI safety accord.
- Lobbying disclosures show additional behind‑the‑scenes discussions just before Trump named an AI czar and task force.
- Democrats are expected to win back at least some control in Congress after the 2024 midterm elections, making engagement with lawmakers a strategic priority for AI companies.

## 📈 Comparison with Established Big Tech
- Microsoft has spent **at least $2 million** per quarter since 2021.
- Google regularly spends **over $3 million** per quarter, including this quarter.
- The AI firms’ quarterly sums are therefore "not out of the ordinary" compared with long‑established Big Tech lobbying levels.

## 🔎 Company‑Specific Highlights
- **Anthropic** released a detailed list of the bills, executive orders and topics it lobbied on in Q3, including KOSA, the AI Overwatch Act, the "Super Intelligence" executive order, and the Ratepayer Protection Act.
- **OpenAI**’s lobbying appears to respond to fallout from the Hugging Face attack, covering copyright, cybersecurity, AI safety oversight, and antitrust protections for information sharing.
- **Nvidia** lobbied on a range of issues across Congress and multiple federal agencies, including 6G networks, responsible AI design, competition policy, quantum computing, and export control policy.

## 🔮 Outlook
- With data‑center development and AI safety on many voters’ minds, AI lobbyists’ work is likely to increase regardless of which party controls Congress next year.
