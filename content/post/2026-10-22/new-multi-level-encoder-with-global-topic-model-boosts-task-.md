# New Multi‑Level Encoder with Global Topic Model Boosts Task‑Oriented Dialogue Summarization

A new model called MLEGT (Multi‑Level Encoder with Global Topic) has been introduced to improve task‑oriented dialogue summarization.

## 🔍 Overview
- Task‑oriented dialogue summarization automatically extracts key information to generate domain summaries.
- The goal is to improve service efficiency and quality.

## 🧩 How it Works
- **Multi‑level encoder** discovers temporal and spatial dependencies in dialogues.
- During graph construction, the encoder simultaneously obtains a simple yet effective **global topic**.
- A **global topic‑guided pointer mechanism** is added to the summarizer to capture ignored but important information, enhancing summary accuracy.

## 📊 Evaluation
- A comprehensive study was conducted on two public datasets:
  - A real‑world large‑scale Chinese police interrogation dataset.
  - A medical reporting service dataset.
- Results prove the superiority of the MLEGT method over several strong baselines.
