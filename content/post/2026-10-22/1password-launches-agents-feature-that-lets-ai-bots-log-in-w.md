# 1Password launches “Agents” feature that lets AI bots log in without seeing passwords

1Password announced a new capability called **1Password for Agents** that enables AI agents to sign into your accounts without ever exposing the actual passwords.

## 🔍 Overview
- The feature allows AI agents to sign in without handling your passwords.
- It does **not** give bots unrestricted access – you must grant approval for each sign‑in request.
- 1Password says the workflow should be less cumbersome for tasks that require agents to log into multiple services.

## 🛠️ How it works
1. An AI agent presents a unique digital key to verify its identity to 1Password when it reaches a sign‑in form.
2. 1Password sends a notification to your device describing which account credentials are needed and why.
3. You approve the request on your own device – a process described as similar to two‑factor authentication.
4. Upon approval, 1Password fills in the required login information; the credentials are never handed to the agent.
5. After signing in, the agent continues its task without ever having seen your password.

## 💡 Why it matters
> “Agents are starting to do real work on our behalf. The old model where you hand over a credential and hope for the best doesn’t work,” said 1Password chief product officer Aashish Tripathi in a press statement.

> “An agent should be able to do the job it’s been asked to do without getting permanent access to everything behind the account, and 1Password provides the trust layer that makes this possible across personal agent experiences.”

These statements underscore the shift toward a trust‑layer approach where users retain control over each credential use case.
