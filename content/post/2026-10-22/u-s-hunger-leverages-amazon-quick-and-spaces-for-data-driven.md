# U.S. Hunger Leverages Amazon Quick and Spaces for Data‑Driven Food Assistance

U.S. Hunger has adopted Amazon Quick and its Spaces capability to bring real‑time data insight to its food‑assistance operations.

## 🔍 Overview
- Delivered more than **250 million meals**.
- Historically reached **59 countries**, now focused primarily on the United States.
- Reported **~500 % growth** over four years while headcount grew only **15–20 %**.
- President and CEO **Rick Whitted** spent **30 years in banking** before leading the organization.

## 🛠️ How Quick and Spaces Work
- **Quick** extracts data from a **SQL Server** into its in‑memory engine **SPICE** via nightly refresh jobs at **2 AM ET**.
- Data originates from **Impact Manager**, the organization’s proprietary cloud‑based platform, and from the **Full Cart** program that delivers donor‑funded food to households.
- **Spaces** provide a shared workspace where colleagues can store documents, definitions, and analyses for a specific business area.
- Shared definitions of **KPIs** ensure everyone agrees on metric meanings.
- A leadership‑wide KPI document was built over more than a year across **NetSuite, Salesforce, Impact Manager,** and the payroll system.
- The technology team and a research data colleague prepare datasets and supply definitions to help the CEO interpret information.

## 📊 Impact on Operations
- **17 colleagues** across executive leadership, technology, Full Cart, finance, compliance, and support have access to Quick.
- In Quick, the CEO can **comment directly on drafts**, regenerate reports with incorporated changes, and refine instructions without moving between Word and chat.
- Chat serves as the CEO’s primary day‑to‑day AI workspace, while **Spaces** is the most used capability.
- Using a Space with Salesforce call reports, the CEO drafts instructions on measures, data sources, and definitions for the technology team.
- Quick provides a **live connection** to how leadership thinks through problems, allowing others to pick up where the CEO left off in a financial model or KPI framing without scheduling another meeting.

## 👥 Leadership & Collaboration
- The organization first learned about Quick at an **AWS Social Entrepreneur Accelerator** event led by **Kat Esser**.
- **CTO Ezekiel Brooks** enabled access to core Full Cart datasets for the CEO.
- **Rick Whitted** remarked, “*The families we serve today, their stories are my story.*”
- He also said, “*With all the knowledge and data we have, Space reduces as much noise as possible and narrows my discussion.*”
- The **financial Space** is shared with the CTO, COO, and finance leadership, granting access to tools and business context behind analyses.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Shared definitions and live data reduce reliance on meetings and manual document handling.
- The combined Quick + Spaces workflow lets the organization scale its impact efficiently while maintaining data‑driven decision‑making.
- By grounding financial and operational insight in the real stories of families, U.S. Hunger aligns its rapid growth with its mission.
