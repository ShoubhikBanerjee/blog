# Meta Announces New Non‑Voting Share Class to Finance AI Initiatives

Meta disclosed that it will issue a new class of non‑voting shares to fund its artificial‑intelligence strategy, potential acquisitions, and employee compensation.

## 🔍 Overview
- Meta says it’s issuing a new class of shares to fund its AI initiatives.
- The new class will also fund acquisitions and employee compensation.
- Trading of the non‑voting stock is slated to begin in 2027.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The announcement was made in Meta’s third‑quarter earnings report released on Wednesday.
- The press release states: “The scale of investment required to compete, sustain growth, expand infrastructure, and pursue new strategic initiatives amidst the AI transformation taking place in the industry is significant.”
- CFO Susan Li said the shares will “expand our funding tools and give us flexibility to invest behind the unprecedented momentum we’re seeing across every part of our business driven by AI.”
- Meta expects capital expenditures between $135 billion and $145 billion this year, up from the previous $130 billion to $145 billion estimate.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- The new share class provides Meta with an additional financing mechanism without diluting voting control.
- It aligns capital allocation with the company’s aggressive AI roadmap, including recent launches such as the Muse AI agent.

## 🚀 Related AI Activity
- Meta launched its AI agent, Muse, in September; the app quickly rose to the top of app stores and reached 10 million U.S. downloads.
- Muse’s capabilities include cleaning email inboxes, booking restaurant reservations, and planning trips.

*All statements are drawn directly from Meta’s press release and earnings call.*
