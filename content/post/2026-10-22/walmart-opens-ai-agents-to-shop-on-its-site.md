# Walmart Opens AI Agents to Shop on Its Site

Walmart will soon let AI agents shop on its website for you.

## 🔍 Overview
- Walmart is inviting AI agents to shop for you.
- The move contrasts with Amazon, which has blocked AI shopping agents such as Perplexity’s shopping agent and Meta’s Muse AI assistant.

## 🧩 How it works
- Walmart has built an **Agent Gateway**, currently in beta, that connects AI agents through the **Model Context Protocol**, an open standard for plugging agents into web services.
- Agents operate through new **Agent Profiles** inside Walmart.
- By default, purchases made by an AI agent require shopper approval, but shoppers can set a spending limit (e.g., up to $100) to allow autonomous purchases.
- Shoppers can review the agent’s purchases separately from their own and can turn the agent off at any time.
- The system is also being extended to Sam’s Club.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Agent terms** (as outlined by Walmart):
  | Requirement | Description |
  |---|---|
  | Intent limitation | Agents must not take actions beyond what the shopper intends |
  | Payment method protection | Agents cannot change a customer’s payment method |
  | Data access restriction | Agents can only access information needed to complete their task |
- Walmart will block agents that do not follow these terms.
- The gateway prevents agents from accessing a shopper’s broader purchase history while still allowing them to locate frequently bought items (e.g., sourdough bread or a preferred brand of milk).

## 🚀 Availability
- The AI‑shopping feature launches **next month**.
- It is initially available in beta via the Agent Gateway.
- The same capability will be offered on Sam’s Club.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Walmart’s openness to AI shoppers stands in stark contrast to Amazon’s decision to shut out AI agents.
- By setting clear rules and privacy safeguards, Walmart aims to let shoppers benefit from AI assistance without compromising intent or personal data.
