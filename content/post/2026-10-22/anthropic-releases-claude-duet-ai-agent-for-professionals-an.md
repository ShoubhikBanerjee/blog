# Anthropic releases Claude Duet AI agent for professionals and small businesses

Anthropic announced Claude Duet, a new AI agent aimed at professionals and small business owners.

## 🔍 Overview
- Claude Duet is Anthropic’s new AI agent for professionals and small business owners, launching as a research preview.
- Competes with Meta’s Muse and OpenAI’s Dots.
- Built around Claude Opus 5.5.

## 🧩 How it works
- Pulls in memory, skills, and connected apps from Claude.
- Includes a virtual machine with a browser for completing work.
- Sends a short brief each morning with the most important items across connected apps.
- Monitors apps and messages throughout the day, notifying only when something needs attention.
- Can handle tasks ranging from simple scheduling to building dashboards, creating financial or sales forecasts, prototyping, and building web apps.
- Before any big step like sending a payment or deleting something, Claude Duet messages you for approval.
- Uses a new dedicated classifier and safety system prompt designed to reject requests to share the strictest tier of information, such as credit card numbers.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Anthropic says Claude Duet is an early version and may misread a thread or miscalibrate what’s urgent.
- It is not as strong at personal errands like booking tickets.
- Available as a standalone app on iOS and Android and through Claude on the web.
- Access requires joining a waitlist and being a Pro or Max subscriber in the US.

## 🚀 Availability
- Launched as a research preview.
- Waitlist required; limited to Pro or Max subscribers in the United States.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides an AI‑driven assistant tailored for professional workflows.
- Combines memory, app integration, and a browser VM into a single agent.
- Introduces safety mechanisms for handling sensitive information.
