# Tech Giants Invest 15 Billion Dollars in AI Infrastructure at LEAP 2026

The opening days of LEAP 2026 in Riyadh have resulted in approximately $15 billion in investments, partnerships, and computing infrastructure agreements. Held under the theme "Into New Worlds," the fifth edition of the conference highlights Saudi Arabia's ambition to build and export technology. Major global tech companies have deepened their commitments to the region, focusing on sovereign cloud, AI infrastructure, and physical logistics.

## 🔍 Overview

Much of the early dealmaking involves HUMAIN, a Public Investment Fund-owned AI company. The organization is expanding partnerships across cloud infrastructure and personal technology to support training and inference workloads. The Kingdom is working toward a goal of having up to 1 gigawatt of AI infrastructure in place by 2030.

## ⚙️ Key details

| Partner | Project/Infrastructure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Amazon Web Services (AWS) | $5.3 billion investment for a cloud infrastructure region and a 50MW AI Zone. |
| AMD & Cisco | Deployment of MI355X and next-generation MI400 Series GPUs for GPU-as-a-service. |
| Microsoft | Expansion of HUMAIN ONE AI productivity service and a new cloud data center region. |
| stc group | Development of 250MW AI-ready data center capacity and global connectivity networks. |
| Qualcomm | Launch of the Horizon Ultra AI PC powered by the Snapdragon X2 Elite platform. |
| HP | Launch of HP Garage 2.0, a startup incubator for Vision 2030 solutions. |

## 🧩 How it works

*   **AI Computing:** AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs and Cisco networking technology are currently operational, allowing HUMAIN to provide GPU-as-a-service for model training.
*   **Cloud Regions:** Microsoft's Saudi Arabia East region will include three availability zones to improve latency, security, and business continuity for government and private-sector organizations.
*   **AI Productivity:** HUMAIN ONE is hosted on Azure and provides a single interface for enterprise applications. It integrates Microsoft 365 Copilot and HUMAIN's ALLAM models to support Arabic-language agents.
*   **Hardware Integration:** The Horizon Ultra AI PC uses an 18-core Qualcomm Oryon CPU, Adreno GPU, and Hexagon NPU to distribute workloads between local processing and cloud infrastructure.

## 🚀 Availability

*   **Current:** AMD Instinct MI355X infrastructure is now operational and in use by HUMAIN customers.
*   **September 2026:** HUMAIN's AI PC is scheduled for release to enterprise customers.
*   **November 2026:** Microsoft's cloud data center region in Saudi Arabia East is expected to open.
*   **December 2026:** AWS expects to open its first cloud infrastructure region in the Kingdom.
*   **2027:** Deployment of AMD's MI400 Series GPUs and the introduction of HUMAIN OS for the Horizon Ultra device.
*   **2028:** Up to 50MW of capacity is expected to be available in Saudi Arabia's first AI Zone.

## 💡 Why it matters

stc Group CEO Olayan Alwetaid stated that the Kingdom is undergoing "a rapid transformation in the field of artificial intelligence." The various infrastructure projects, such as the AWS investment and the 1GW goal for 2030, are designed to support local data storage, lower latency, and the expansion of digital services across the Middle East and Africa.
