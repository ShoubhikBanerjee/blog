# Chinese sodium-ion battery rivals Tesla’s performance

A Chinese sodium-ion battery has demonstrated performance comparable to Tesla’s batteries, with production quality and design features matching industry standards.

## 🔍 Overview
Researchers found that the battery performs far better than expected, suggesting potential as a cheaper and more abundant alternative to lithium.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Production quality and design features are comparable to Tesla’s batteries.
- Sodium-ion batteries could become a viable option for electric vehicles (EVs) and large-scale energy storage.

## 🚀 Availability
If engineers can improve cold-weather charging and energy density, sodium-ion batteries may see broader adoption.

## 💡 Why it matters
Sodium is more abundant and potentially cheaper than lithium, offering a sustainable alternative for battery production.
