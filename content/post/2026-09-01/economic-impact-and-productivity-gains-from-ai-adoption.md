# Economic Impact and Productivity Gains from AI Adoption

Recent analysis indicates that AI is driving significant shifts in labour productivity, with projections suggesting growth between 0.4-1.3 percentage points in high-exposure G7 countries and an estimated 0.2 percentage point boost in trend growth.

## ⚙️ Key details

* Productivity improvements for many tasks are currently measured at approximately 50%.
* Efficiency gains are observed in coding, translation, writing, and data analysis.
* As agentic AI adoption grows, further increases in productivity are expected as entire functions become automated.
* Historically, the benefits of such productivity improvements, like automation and machinery, have favored capital owners over labour.

## 💡 Why it matters

AI is acting as a tool for the democratisation of expertise and information, helping to offset skill and knowledge deficits. 

* Accessibility: The technology makes content more intuitive and easier to digest, bridging knowledge gaps.
* Skill augmentation: Individuals with limited technical or mathematical skills can now produce sophisticated analytical models or perform complex tasks using natural language prompts.
* Entrepreneurship: Barriers to starting a business are shifting from being capital-constrained to creativity-constrained, making one-man or resource-light companies more viable.
* Competitive landscape: Smaller, leaner teams are increasingly able to compete with larger organizations.
