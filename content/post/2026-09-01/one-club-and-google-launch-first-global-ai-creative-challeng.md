# One Club and Google launch first global AI Creative Challenge

The One Club for Creativity, in partnership with Google, has launched the first global One Club AI Creative Challenge, bringing its live-action competition to a worldwide stage.

## 🔍 Overview
The AI Creative Challenge is a bracketed global esports-style tournament where human creativity and AI tools are tested under pressure. It culminates in a single-elimination face-off between the world’s top AI creators, judged on speed, prompt mastery, and creative execution.

## 🧩 How it works
Participants compete in timed 10-minute sprints to fulfill a secret, randomly selected creative brief using generative AI. A jury of top creatives scores each round on:
- Speed of execution
- Prompt mastery
- Creative execution

The bracket format advances one winner per city through quarterfinals and semifinals to a grand finale hosted by Google in New York on February 18, 2027.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Prize**: $50,000 and the title of AI Creative Champion
- **Format**: Single-elimination tournament with 128 AI experts across 16 cities
- **Judging criteria**: Speed, prompt mastery, creative execution
- **Public access**: All local events are free and open to the public as spectator experiences

The challenge frames AI as a spectator sport, highlighting the intersection of human creativity and generative AI. It offers a public platform to observe how top creators leverage AI tools to meet tight deadlines and creative briefs, emphasizing technical skill and prompt engineering under extreme pressure.
