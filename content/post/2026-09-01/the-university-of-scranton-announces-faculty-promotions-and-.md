# The University of Scranton Announces Faculty Promotions and Appointments for 2026-27

## 🏛️ New Academic Leadership

WeiDong Zhu, Ph.D., joined The University of Scranton in July. Dr. Zhu previously spent 19 years at Saint Peter’s University, where he held roles including dean of the College of Arts and Sciences and interim vice president for academic affairs.

## 🌐 Professional Recognition

Gretchen J. Van Dyke, Ph.D., associate professor of political science, has been invited to join the Bretton Woods Committee. This international network focuses on the stability of the international economic and financial system, with membership granted by invitation based on professional achievement in economic and policy matters.
