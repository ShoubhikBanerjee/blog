# John Deere tests new 'JD' AI assistant for farmers

John Deere is testing a new AI assistant called "JD" designed to help farmers make more money by answering questions based on their own farm data.

## 🔍 Overview

- John Deere launched an AI chatbot for farmers
- The assistant is called "JD"
- It is currently in testing through an Early Access Program
- The company says it can help farmers make more money

## 🧩 How it works

The JD AI assistant uses farmers' own data to answer questions about their operations and equipment. According to the evidence, it draws on:

- Field data
- Machine data
- Operational data

It can answer questions on topics including:

- Equipment settings
- Fuel usage
- Harvest timing
- Best practices
- Historical trends

## ⚙️ Key details

The launch comes with John Deere's ten-point Farmer Data Commitment. Key points include:

| Commitment | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Data ownership | You control your farm data |
| No data sales | John Deere does not sell your farm data |
| Usage limits | John Deere uses your farm data only as described in its agreements and policies |
| Transparency | John Deere will require dealers and connected partners to maintain transparent data practices |
| Value delivery | John Deere uses your farm data and aggregated, anonymized data to deliver measurable value through improved machine performance and decision-making insights |
| Third-party control | You can choose which third parties to share, and not share, your farm data with |
| Opt-out freedom | You have the freedom to turn off the flow of your farm data to third parties at any time |
| No commodity trading | John Deere does not use your farm data for agriculture commodity trading or speculation |
| Clear value | You should receive clear value from your farm data |
| Continuous improvement | John Deere will continue to listen to farmers, strengthen its data practices, and develop new solutions |

Notable technical detail: The press release didn't mention which AI technology is behind this platform.

## 🚀 Availability

- Currently available to "select" US customers in the John Deere Operations Center
- Plans to roll out more broadly on the web
- Plans to roll out on mobile
- Eventually planned for in-cab displays on tractors and other farm equipment

## 💡 Future expansion

John Deere says it plans to expand the AI assistant with capabilities tailored to customers across:

- Turf
- Construction
- Roadbuilding
- Forestry
