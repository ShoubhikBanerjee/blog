# Phil Schiller Steps Back from App Store and Apple Events Role

Apple Fellow Phil Schiller is officially reducing his operational responsibilities, stepping down from overseeing the App Store and Apple product launches as the company prepares for a leadership transition to incoming CEO John Ternus.

## 🔍 Overview

- Schiller is stepping down from overseeing the App Store and Apple product launches
- He will retain only his Apple Fellow role
- The move coincides with John Ternus set to take over as Apple CEO
- The App Store is returning to Eddy Cue, who oversaw it until 2015

## ⏰ Background and Workload

- In 2024, The Wall Street Journal reported Schiller was working close to 80 hours a week
- As Apple Fellow, Schiller continued to oversee the App Store and Apple events, but the role may not have left him with sufficient time for his own endeavors
- Schiller was present with Steve Jobs and Tim Cook at the start of Apple’s comeback in 1997–1998
- He has a combined 35 years of service to Apple: 1987–1993 and 1997 to today

## 🔄 Organizational Changes

| Role | Person | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| App Store oversight | Eddy Cue | Previously oversaw it until 2015; will take more influential role under Ternus |
| Day-to-day App Store operations | Carson Oliver | Longtime App Store team member |
| Apple CEO (incoming) | John Ternus | Will lead as Schiller steps back |

- Cue is Apple’s services chief and will reportedly be taking a more influential role under Ternus’ leadership
- Carson Oliver, who has long been on the App Store team, will oversee its day-to-day operations

## 📋 What This Means

- Schiller is officially stepping back to only his Apple Fellow role
- The App Store returns to Eddy Cue after nearly a decade under Schiller
- Leadership restructuring appears timed to coincide with the CEO transition to Ternus
