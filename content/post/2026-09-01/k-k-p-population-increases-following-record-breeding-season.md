# Kākāpō Population Increases Following Record Breeding Season

## 📊 Population Update

The total population of the kākāpō has reached 325 individuals as of 31st August 2026. This increase follows a record breeding season, with this year's chicks having now matured into juveniles.

## 💡 Historical Context

* 1995: 51 kākāpō remaining
* 31st August 2026: 325 kākāpō recorded
