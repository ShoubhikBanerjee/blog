# Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode Reduce Publisher Traffic

Publishers spent years figuring out how to get clicks on Google, building a reciprocal relationship where they supplied content and Google supplied exposure. In 2024 and 2025 Google introduced AI‑driven search features that have reshaped that relationship and sharply lowered traffic to many news sites.

## 🔍 Overview
- **AI Overviews (2024)** – An AI‑generated summary appears at the very top of Google Search, followed by links to sources the AI deems most useful. [Evidence]
- **AI Mode (May 2025)** – Replaces standard search with a chatbot‑like interface that provides detailed summaries from web results. [Evidence]
- The features change how results are ranked and displayed, making it harder for publishers to rely on Google traffic alone. [Evidence]

## 🗓️ Timeline
| Year | Feature | Key Change |
|------|---------|------------|
| 2024 | AI Overviews | AI‑generated summary placed at top of results with source links below. |
| 2025 | AI Mode | Standard search replaced by a chatbot‑style interface that returns detailed summaries. |

## 📉 Impact on Publishers
- News outlets that spent time and money optimizing pages for clicks saw traffic **tank** after the AI features launched. [Evidence]
- The Wall Street Journal (June 2025) reported declines for Business Insider, The Washington Post, HuffPost, and the WSJ itself since 2022. [Evidence]
- Similarweb data showed The New York Times’ search traffic fell from **44 % to 35.5 %** over three years. [Evidence]
- DCN reported 19 members lost **1–25 %** of Google traffic between May and June 2025. [Evidence]
- DMG Media (Daily Mail, Metro) told the UK CMA click‑through rates dropped **up to 89 %** between June and July 2025, citing AI Overviews. [Evidence]
- Pew Research found users are **less likely** to click on external links when an AI Overview is shown. [Evidence]
- The News/Media Alliance warned AI Overviews would be “catastrophic” for web traffic. [Evidence]

## 🛠️ Google’s Response
- Offered a **toggle** that lets sites opt out of appearing in AI Overviews and AI Mode without losing standard search visibility (rolled out after a UK order). [Evidence]
- Made source links more prominent: hovering over a link shows a pop‑up with article titles and images. [Evidence]
- Added another AI change that puts **in‑line links higher up** in responses more frequently. [Evidence]
- Google’s leadership statements: Liz Reid said click volume remained “relatively stable,” while Nick Fox said “from our point of view, the web is thriving.” [Evidence]

## ⚖️ Legal and Regulatory Context
- US District Judge Amit Mehta dismissed an antitrust suit from Chegg and Penske Media and rejected a class‑action claim from Arkansas newspapers over AI Overviews. [Evidence]
- The UK Competition and Markets Authority received complaints from DMG Media about click‑through declines linked to AI Overviews. [Evidence]

The rollout of AI Overviews and AI Mode marks a significant shift in Google Search, turning a long‑standing traffic source into a more opaque AI‑mediated layer and prompting measurable traffic losses across the digital publishing ecosystem.
