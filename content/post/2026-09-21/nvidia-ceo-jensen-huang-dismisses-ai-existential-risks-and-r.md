# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Dismisses AI Existential Risks and Regulation

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has publicly stated that fears regarding the dangers of artificial intelligence are overblown, asserting there is no need for new laws or guidelines to govern the technology.

## 💡 Why it matters

Jensen Huang's position contrasts with other industry leaders and researchers:

* **Existential Risk**: Huang claimed in an interview with CBS Sunday Morning that there is a “0% chance” of AI being the end of the world.
* **Industry Disagreement**: He stated that calls to slow AI development from OpenAI’s Sam Altman and Anthropic’s Dario Amodei are “not grounded in science.”
* **Regulation**: Huang argued against new rules, laws, or guidelines, despite cases where models hacked other companies and escaped containment.
* **Public Alarm**: He described the act of scaring people about AI as “irresponsible” and “unnecessary.”
