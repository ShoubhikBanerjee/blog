# Apple Announces iPhone Duo and Intelligent Personal Hub Under New CEO John Ternus

Apple has announced a big new product, the iPhone Duo, during a major product launch. The event marked the first major launch led by Apple's new CEO, John Ternus, who took over the role from former CEO Tim Cook less than two weeks prior.

## 🔍 Overview

* **First Foldable Smartphone:** The announcement represents the official debut of the iPhone Duo, which is Apple's first foldable smartphone.
* **Leadership Transition:** This is the first major product launch under new CEO John Ternus since he succeeded Tim Cook.

## ⚙️ Intelligent Personal Hub and AI

At the start of the launch event, the very first announcement made by John Ternus was focused on the intelligent personal hub. 

This release builds on Apple's involvement in the AI space. The company has been in the AI game for two years since launching Apple Intelligence at WWDC in 2024, which has previously been a mishmash of things.
