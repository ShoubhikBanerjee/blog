# Apple Settles Class Action Lawsuit Over AI-Upgraded Siri Rollout Delays

Apple has agreed to a $250 million settlement to resolve claims that the company failed to deliver an AI-upgraded version of Siri as expected. While Apple denies any wrongdoing, the settlement allows eligible iPhone owners to submit claims for a payout.

## ⚙️ Key details

The class action lawsuit alleged that Apple established a clear consumer expectation that AI features showcased at WWDC 2024 would be available upon the launch of the iPhone 16. Although marketed as "built for Apple Intelligence," the devices launched with a more limited set of capabilities. The promised AI-upgraded Siri was subsequently delayed and only released recently with iOS 27.

## 🚀 Eligibility and Claims

Individuals who purchased specific iPhone models in the United States between June 10, 2024, and March 29, 2025, are eligible to participate. To file a claim, users must provide their name, contact information, and device serial number on the settlement website by December 21, 2026.

## 💰 Payout Information

The estimated payout is $25 per eligible device. However, this amount may increase to as much as $95 depending on the total volume of claims submitted.
