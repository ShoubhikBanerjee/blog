# Amazon Blocks Meta's Muse AI Agent From Shopping on Its Platform

Amazon has blocked Meta's Muse AI agent from shopping on its e-commerce platform on behalf of users. Users began seeing a popup message on Sunday stating that "continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon’s Conditions of Use, to which our customers have agreed."

## ⚙️ Key details

Amazon has cited several reasons for the block, including:
* **Lack of Notification:** Meta did not notify Amazon that Muse would access its store.
* **Security and Privacy:** Amazon expressed concerns over Muse failing to identify itself while browsing and seemingly capturing customer credentials.
* **Operational Transparency:** An unnamed Amazon spokesperson told GeekWire, “We think it’s fairly straightforward that third-party applications that offer to make purchases on behalf of customers from other businesses should operate openly and respect service provider decisions about whether or not to participate."

## 🔍 Overview

While Meta stated at Muse's launch earlier this month that the agent cannot see card payment information or secure login details, the agent was previously used during testing to successfully purchase tank tops from Amazon. Additionally, some privacy concerns have emerged since launch, including reports that Muse can see the contents of user messages without the necessary access permission enabled.

## 💡 Why it matters

This move is part of a broader effort by Amazon to keep customers shopping directly with the retailer and fend off competition from rival agentic AI services. Other related actions include:
* **Legal Action:** In November of last year, Amazon sued Perplexity to keep its platform out of the Comet shopping experience, though a judge sided with Perplexity in August.
* **Data Limitation:** Since July, Amazon confirmation emails have become sparse, omitting product images and specific item names to limit information being mined by external AI services.
