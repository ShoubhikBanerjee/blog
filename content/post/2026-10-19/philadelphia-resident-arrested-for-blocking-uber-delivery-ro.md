# Philadelphia resident arrested for blocking Uber delivery robot protest

A Philadelphia resident was arrested after deliberately blocking an Uber delivery robot, citing concerns over job loss.

## 🔍 Overview
- Carolyn Marvin blocked the path of an Uber delivery robot in downtown Philadelphia.
- Police asked her to move; she refused and was placed in cuffs.
- The incident was captured on video and gained media attention.

## ⚖️ Legal outcome
- Marvin was charged with disorderly conduct.
- She was arrested after refusing to move out of the robot’s way.

## 🤖 Delivery robots in Philadelphia
- Delivery robots have become a common sight on the streets of downtown Philadelphia.
- This is not Marvin’s first encounter; earlier this year a robot was kicked over in Center City.

## 📈 Broader impact
- Marvin said, "I know they’re taking the jobs of human delivery workers and so, I have been protesting them whenever I saw them by standing in front of them and preventing them from going forward."
- The incident reflects growing concern about how autonomous systems, including robotaxis, delivery robots, and AI agents, affect lower‑skill workers.
