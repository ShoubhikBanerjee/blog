# Illinois Gov. JB Pritzker warns AI could extend lifespan or pose nuclear‑like threats

Today I’m talking with Illinois Gov. JB Pritzker about his view of AI, tech and privacy.

## 🔍 Overview
Gov. Pritzker says AI is a technology that could **help us live to 100** or **threaten us like nukes**. He is willing to discuss the “existential weeds” of what government is for when it comes to these sweeping possibilities.

## 🧩 AI & Society
- **Potential upside:** Pritzker believes AI could extend human life to a century.
- **Potential danger:** He warns AI could become a threat comparable to nuclear weapons.

## ⚙️ Government Role
- Pritzker argues that many AI‑related challenges are **collective‑action problems** that require government intervention.
- He stresses that **we can’t solve these problems one at a time, or even one state at a time**.

## 📊 Political Realignment
Pritzker notes a “big and maybe kind of unexpected political realignment” driven by three areas:
- **Data centers** – a bipartisan backlash around their expansion.
- **Privacy** – growing concerns across the political spectrum.
- **Antitrust** – broader competition issues, especially in the AI industry.

## 🏛️ Antitrust & Competition
- David Sacks retweeted Lina Khan (Biden administration) on whether the AI industry needs an antitrust exemption for safety.
- Both agree **AI companies do not need antitrust exemptions** and should actually be **more competitive**.

## 📅 Why It Matters Now
- The conversation comes as **midterm elections approach**, with early voting already underway.
- Pritzker, a former tech‑industry businessman and self‑described progressive, says the stakes are “pretty high right now.”

*The interview reflects a rare willingness from a governor to explore AI’s existential implications and the need for coordinated policy responses.*
