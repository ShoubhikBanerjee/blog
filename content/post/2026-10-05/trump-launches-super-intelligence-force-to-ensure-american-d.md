# Trump Launches Super Intelligence Force to Ensure American Dominance in Technology

Donald Trump is launching a "Super Intelligence Force" aimed at ensuring American dominance in the global technology landscape. This initiative follows a directive that has already impacted major private sector players, specifically within Elon Musk's SpaceX.

## 🔍 Overview
The Super Intelligence Force is a new government-led effort to secure the United States' position in a national security "tech arms race." The move aligns with stated goals to maintain technological superiority through federal orders and strategic restructuring.

## ⚙️ Key details
* A SpaceX arm is receiving a new name following an order from Trump.
* Elon Musk has stated there will be "no more AI" for the renamed SpaceX division.
* The Super Intelligence Force is specifically designed to ensure American dominance.

## 💡 Why it matters
This national security push faces potential internal challenges. A congressman has warned that local backlash could "steamroll" and potentially derail these efforts, highlighting a collision between federal strategic plans and local community reactions.
