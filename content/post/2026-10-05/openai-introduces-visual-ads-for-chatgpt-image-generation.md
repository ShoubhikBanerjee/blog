# OpenAI introduces visual ads for ChatGPT image generation

OpenAI is testing a new ad format that places images of sponsored products and services alongside generated images in ChatGPT.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI’s latest ad format will put images of sponsored products and services on your screen.
- The visual ads will initially appear when you generate images using ChatGPT.
- This format is separate from the generated image and will not influence the answers ChatGPT provides.

## 🧩 How it works
- Ads appear only when you generate an image, not in text‑only chats.
- They are shown in a distinct “sponsored” section, separate from the AI output.
- Guardrails keep ads out of conversations that are emotionally vulnerable, sensitive, or otherwise unsuitable.
- Ads are omitted for users on ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Enterprise plans.

## ⚙️ Key details
- OpenAI will begin testing the visual ads in the US later this month.
- The test involves an initial group of advertisers, with plans to try new formats later.
- OpenAI first introduced ads to ChatGPT in February, originally showing only a company’s name, logo, and a link beneath the chat.

## 🚀 Availability
- Ads will initially appear for free‑tier users in the United States.
- Subscribers to Plus, Pro, or Enterprise will not see these visual ads.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Keeps advertising content separate from AI‑generated answers.
- Protects users by preventing ads in sensitive or emotionally charged contexts.
