# Springboards launches LLM aimed at broader response diversity

Springboards, a startup, is developing a large‑language model that aims to generate a wider variety of responses than mainstream rivals.

## 🔍 Overview
- The model is intended to “come up with a wider variety of responses than its mainstream rivals do.”
- The Springboards CEO said there is “no walking back from LLMs—but you could still make them do something different.”
- The company describes itself as “a self‑loathing AI company” and admits uncertainty about whether it “really likes what we’re doing.”

## 📈 Context – public sentiment and adoption
- Pew Research Center reports more US adults think AI will have a negative personal and societal impact than a positive one; pessimism is strongest among the young.
- Stanford University finds more than half of people worldwide say AI products and services make them nervous.
- Gallup (May) shows 71% of US adults would oppose construction of a new AI data center, compared with 53% who would oppose a new nuclear power plant.
- An NBC poll in March found AI was less popular than ICE.
- ChatGPT reached a billion monthly users in May (Sensor Tower).
- Google DeepMind’s Gemini logged 950 million users in July.
- Pew reports half of US adults now say they use a chatbot, more than twice the 2023 figure; one in four say they do so every day.
- Over a third of adults across 38 OECD countries report using generative AI tools in the last three months.
- All 50 US states have passed or proposed AI laws, creating a patchwork of more than 2,100 bills—a ten‑fold increase in three years.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The launch occurs amid strong public anxiety and a rapidly expanding regulatory landscape.
- Open‑source alternatives to major providers already exist, increasing competition for new models.
- Wider response diversity could address concerns about AI predictability and perceived homogeneity of mainstream LLM outputs.

## ⚙️ Key details (facts from the evidence)
- **User reach:** ChatGPT = 1 billion monthly users; Gemini = 950 million users.
- **Adoption rates:** 50% of US adults use chatbots; 25% use them daily; >33% of OECD adults used generative AI in the past three months.
- **Regulation:** >2,100 AI‑related bills across all states.
- **Public opinion:** Majority view AI negatively; high opposition to AI data centers; AI less popular than ICE.
