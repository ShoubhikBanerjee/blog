# JetGEO 1.0.0 Launches AI‑Ready Control Center for WordPress

This month the Crocoblock ecosystem introduced JetGEO 1.0.0, a control‑center plugin that makes WordPress sites AI‑ready.

## 🔍 Overview
- The idea behind it is simple: content for AI visibility.
- JetGEO is built as a complete control center that makes your WordPress site AI-ready and gives you full command over how AI tools read, parse, and represent your brand.

## ⚙️ Key Features
- Decide exactly which post types or pages AI can access.
- Private and hidden content stays safe automatically.
- Auto‑generate, preview, and update your site’s AI index file using custom templates and dynamic tags, with no server edits needed.
- Serve pure, text‑only Markdown directly to AI agents.
- It strips out HTML noise, menus, and scripts for fast and flawless parsing.
- Manage your robots.txt visually.
- Identify, allow, or block specific AI bots in a couple of clicks.
- Track real‑time crawler activity: see which bots visited, which pages they analyzed, and what was blocked.

## 🚀 Availability
- JetGEO 1.0.0 is officially here.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Gives site owners precise control over AI access.
- Protects private content without extra configuration.
- Provides a clean, Markdown‑only AI index for faster parsing.
- Offers visual robots.txt management and bot blocking.
- Enables real‑time monitoring of AI crawler activity.
