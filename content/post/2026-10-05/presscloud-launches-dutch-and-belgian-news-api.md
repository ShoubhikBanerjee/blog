# Presscloud launches Dutch and Belgian News API

Presscloud has made its long‑standing Dutch and Belgian media index publicly queryable through the new Presscloud News API.

## 🔍 Overview
- For years, Presscloud has been tracking where news in the Netherlands and Belgium appears.
- The index now powers the live Presscloud News API, allowing anyone to query the data.

## 🧩 How it works
- The API works like a mailman that delivers Dutch and Belgian news articles all day, neatly sorted for use in tools or dashboards.
- Every article is returned within minutes to a maximum of twelve hours after publication as clean, structured data containing title, summary, publication date, medium, and, when available, author name.
- Data can be retrieved as JSON or RSS.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Coverage: over 2,300 sources, including more than 1,900 Dutch and 400 Belgian media, ranging from national dailies to regional papers, specialist outlets, and niche sites.
- Most international news APIs stop at a few hundred major titles per country; Presscloud’s index goes much deeper.
- The underlying index already existed as part of Presscloud’s Media Monitoring service and continues to grow weekly.

## 🚀 Availability
- Access is obtained by signing up at presscloud.ai/nl/news-api.
- New users receive a number of free credits to test the API.
- Ongoing usage is billed based on the number of calls, with packages suited to every company size—from small test integrations to large‑scale deployments that include historical data.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enables PR and media‑monitoring tools to look beyond the national press.
- Supports teams tracking funding rounds, product launches, and board changes across the Benelux region.
- Provides researchers with a large, structured Dutch/Belgian news dataset.
- Allows newsletters, aggregators, and compliance teams to build sector‑specific feeds without crawling hundreds of sources themselves.
