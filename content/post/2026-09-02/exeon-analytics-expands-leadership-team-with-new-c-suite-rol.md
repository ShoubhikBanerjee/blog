# Exeon Analytics Expands Leadership Team with New C-Suite Roles

Exeon Analytics has appointed Wayne Jennings as Chief Solutions Officer and Johan Roman as Chief Revenue Officer, expanding its management team to support its next growth phase.

## 👥 New Leadership Roles

- Wayne Jennings takes on the newly created position of Chief Solutions Officer.
- Johan Roman becomes Chief Revenue Officer.

## 📌 Responsibilities

| Name | Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Wayne Jennings | Chief Solutions Officer | Technical customer lifecycle (pre-sales, proof of concept, implementation, long-term technical account management), technical enablement of partners |
| Johan Roman | Chief Revenue Officer | Sales, marketing, channel partnerships, expanding partner network, driving growth in EMEA |

## 📅 Background

- Wayne Jennings joined Exeon Analytics in July 2024 and initially led Support and Engineering.
- Before Exeon, he was a Senior Manager in Cybersecurity & Privacy at PwC Switzerland, focusing on threat intelligence and incident response.
- Johan Roman brings over 15 years of experience in commercial growth, go-to-market strategy, strategic partnerships, and marketing in cybersecurity and technology.
- He spent over 12 years at Kudelski Security, most recently as Senior Director of Global Alliances, Technology Partnerships & Marketing and a member of the Executive Leadership Team.

## 🎯 Company Priorities

The expanded management team supports three priorities:
- Scaling Exeon.UEBA, introduced in January 2026, which complements Exeon.NDR with user, entity, and application behavior analysis to address identity-based attacks.
- Developing a module for detecting and securing AI agents, available in Q4 2026, to cover autonomous agents with their own access rights.
- Geographic expansion in further EMEA markets through a growing network of distributors, integrators, and managed security service providers.

## 💬 Leadership Perspective

"Wayne and Johan bring exactly the combination we need for the years ahead: technical depth close to the customer and experience in scaling go-to-market internationally," says Gregor Erismann, CEO of Exeon Analytics.

## 🏢 Company Context

- The appointments coincide with Exeon Analytics' 10th anniversary as an ETH Zurich spin-off.
- The company delivers holistic security analytics for organizations facing growing blind spots, encrypted traffic, and regulatory pressure.
