# Jabil Leverages SAP Integration Suite to Simplify Global Supply Chain and Enable AI-Driven Planning

Jabil, a global manufacturing company with more than 100 sites in over 30 countries, has begun using SAP Integration Suite to unify its technology landscape and lay the groundwork for AI‑driven supply‑chain insights.

## 🔍 Overview
- Global footprint: >100 sites, 30‑plus countries, 140,000+ employees
- Headquarters: St. Petersburg, Florida, USA
- 60 years of engineering, supply‑chain, and manufacturing experience
- Serves more than 400 of the world’s top brands
- Existing environment: sites operate with varying process maturity, legacy systems, and localized workflows, creating data silos and limited early‑issue visibility

## 🛠️ How it works
- Adopted a "simplify‑first, then‑innovate" mindset to avoid adding complexity
- Uses SAP Integration Suite as a foundation to:
  - Connect disparate systems across all sites
  - Retire fragmented tools
  - Enable more consistent, end‑to‑end processes globally
- Integrated workflows provide shared data visibility and reduce manual reconciliation
- Real‑time supply‑chain event visibility supports faster disruption response and operational‑risk reduction
- Trusted, unified data enables exploration of:
  - Predictive supply‑chain insights
  - Intelligent exception handling
  - AI‑driven planning and forecasting

## 💡 Why it matters
- Simplicity at scale is identified as a competitive advantage
- Standardizing processes and governance is pursued without disrupting existing operations
- Modernization efforts are tied to measurable business value and operational resilience
- Integrated, data‑driven operations shift employees from "chasing information" to acting on insights
- Faster, data‑backed decisions improve supply‑chain agility and reduce risk
