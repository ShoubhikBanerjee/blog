# Google partners with MrBeast for multi-year Gemini and Fitbit promotion

Google has signed a multi-year partnership with Jimmy "MrBeast" Donaldson, one of YouTube's biggest creators, to promote Gemini, Google Health, and the Fitbit Air across upcoming videos.

## 🔍 Overview

The deal marks a significant collaboration between Google and Donaldson, whose YouTube channel became the first to hit 500 million subscribers a couple of months ago. The partnership comes amid reported efforts by YouTube to keep prominent creators exclusive to its platform.

## ⚙️ Key details

- **Partnership scope**: Multi-year agreement featuring Gemini, Google Health, and Fitbit Air
- **First video**: Scheduled for September 5
- **Format**: Wilderness survival challenge with Jimmy and his crew
- **Additional promotion**: Donaldson will appear in a Gemini campaign from Google

## 🧩 How it works

The inaugural video will follow Donaldson and his crew as they attempt to survive extreme and treacherous climates. Teams will race to navigate three unforgiving landscapes:

- The jungle
- The desert
- The Arctic

According to Google, participants will use Gemini to help them identify dangers, survive brutal weather changes, and stay one step ahead. Donaldson will also use a Fitbit Air in one of his upcoming challenges.

## 🚀 Availability

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| First video release | September 5 |
| Featured products | Gemini, Google Health, Fitbit Air |
| Campaign type | Multi-year partnership with dedicated videos and campaign appearance |

## 💡 Why it matters

The partnership reflects broader platform dynamics in the creator economy. A recent Bloomberg report noted that YouTube has been offering prominent creators deals to keep their content exclusive to its site as more creators strike deals with Netflix.
