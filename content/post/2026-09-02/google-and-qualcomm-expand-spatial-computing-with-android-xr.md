# Google and Qualcomm Expand Spatial Computing with Android XR and Gemini AI Integration

In 2026, the extended reality (XR) landscape has reached an inflection point with more than 60 million devices in the market. Google has announced a strategic re-engagement with immersive technology through Android XR, a dedicated operating system for spatial computing. This platform integrates the Gemini AI model and is supported by hardware developments from Qualcomm, including the Snapdragon Reality Elite chipset.

## 🔍 Overview
Extended reality serves as an umbrella term for virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), and mixed reality (MR). These technologies create immersive experiences by transporting users to digital environments or overlaying digital information onto the physical world. The XR gadget platform comprises an interconnected ecosystem including:
- Hardware devices
- Operating systems specifically designed for spatial computing
- Software applications
- Developer tools such as OpenXR, WebXR, and Unity

Modern XR devices feature lightweight designs, high-resolution displays, and advanced tracking capabilities, overcoming limitations found in early pioneers like Google Glass and Daydream.

## 🧩 How it works
Google's XR strategy combines the Android XR operating system with the Gemini AI model. Gemini provides natural language processing, intelligent assistance, and multimodal AI capabilities. The platform is built on OpenXR standards, allowing developers to leverage existing tools and frameworks to create applications.

Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Reality Elite chipset powers these devices, supporting on-device processing for large vision models and large language models. This hardware integration allows users to transition between different device form factors while maintaining a consistent experience.

## ⚙️ Key details
The Snapdragon Reality Elite platform provides specific performance enhancements to support Android XR devices:

| Feature | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| AI Processing | Up to 48 TOPS |
| GPU Performance | 60% higher than previous generations |
| CPU Performance | 30% higher than previous generations |
| Visual Resolution | Up to 4.4K per eye at 90 frames per second |
| Battery Life | Up to 20% longer |
| Thermal Operation | Significantly cooler performance |

## 🚀 Availability
Google showcased progress on Android XR at the 2026 Google I/O Connect China conference. During the event, Google demonstrated the XREAL AURA, which is one of the first devices to leverage the Snapdragon Reality Elite technology.

## 💡 Why it matters
The integration of Android’s established developer community and a familiar interface makes XR devices more approachable for mainstream users. By providing the Android XR operating system and the Gemini AI model, Google is establishing the foundation for a comprehensive spatial computing ecosystem.
