# Kier Group integrates AI tools and develops custom agents

Kier Group plc, a construction and infrastructure services company based in Salford, England, has adopted AI tools and is developing custom AI agents to enhance operations.

## 🔍 Overview
Kier Group plc, with roughly 11,000 employees, builds schools, prisons, and large-scale water and nuclear energy projects. The company began embracing AI two years ago.

## ⚙️ Key details
- About 40 percent of colleagues use Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- The remaining employees use Teams Premium, which includes advanced AI features for chat and meetings.
- Copilot is used within Outlook, Excel, Teams, and PowerPoint.
- Data is securely stored on Microsoft’s Azure cloud platform.

## 🤖 AI agents in development
Kier is in the early stages of developing AI agents for specific use cases.

| AI Agent | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Safety data agent | Collects and organizes safety data for analysis to address risk areas and forward-looking actions. |
| Safety standards agent | Trained on Kier safety standards and linked to job site photographs, equipment images, and drone video. |
| Response time agent | Designed to speed up response times while allowing direct human interaction when needed. |

## 🏗️ Additional applications
- Drones reduce the need for people to work at height or interact with certain environments.
- AI is being considered for facilities management to predict servicing needs for building parts like pumps or elevators.
