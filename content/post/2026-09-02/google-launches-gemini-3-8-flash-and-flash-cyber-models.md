# Google launches Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash Cyber models

## 🔍 Overview
Today Google announced Gemini 3.8, the latest Flash‑speed reasoning and coding model, and a specialized Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber variant focused on cybersecurity.

## ⚙️ Model variants
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash** – the most intelligent work‑horse model, improving on 3.7 Flash for software engineering, agentic tasks, and multi‑step reasoning in specialized domains.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber** – a cybersecurity‑focused model with frontier‑level performance in vulnerability detection and automated patching, available to trusted defenders through the Fairwind Program.

## 📈 Performance highlights
- Approaches the performance of higher‑cost frontier models while keeping the speed and cost of 3.7 Flash.
- On the DeepSWE v1.1 benchmark, 3.8 Flash outperforms most larger frontier models in end‑to‑end software‑engineering problem solving.
- Achieves 54.9 % on HLE‑Verified, showing strong multi‑step reasoning across STEM, humanities, and professional fields.
- Beats 3.7 Flash and other frontier models on Vals Finance Agent V2 and Harvey’s Legal Agent Benchmark.
- Demonstrates greater diligence on complex tasks, executing extra reasoning steps and calling tools iteratively.
- **Flash Cyber** reaches a success rate > 70 % on the CyberGym vulnerability‑finding benchmark, surpassing 3.5 Flash Cyber and larger frontier models.
- Pass@1 of 47.2 % on an industry benchmark places Flash Cyber on the Pareto frontier (leading frontier model at 47.8 %).
- Google’s Chrome Security team observed 2.6 × more correct patches than the best commercial models.
- Wiz reported a 7.5‑9.7 % higher recall on internal penetration‑testing benchmarks at 2.3‑5.2 × lower cost.
- Google Cloud Vulnerability Research used Flash Cyber to discover a critical foundational vulnerability in under 2 hours, a task that usually takes months.

## 💡 Why it matters
- **Cost efficiency** – Introductory price $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens, identical to 3.7 Flash.
- **Coding gains** – Significant improvements in autonomous software‑engineering tasks at a fraction of the cost of larger models.
- **Security advantage** – Flash Cyber gives trusted defenders expert capabilities, enabling faster vulnerability discovery and patch generation.
- **Safety** – 3.8 Flash includes safeguards against misuse in CBRN and cyber offense; Flash Cyber has a more permissive mitigation set but is limited to trusted users.
- **Robustness** – Notable leap in prompt‑injection robustness measured by Gray Swan.

## 🚀 Availability
- Gemini 3.8 Flash is available at the same introductory price as 3.7 Flash.
- Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is offered through the Fairwind Program to a set of trusted defenders.
- Developers can start building with 3.8 Flash via the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Android Studio, or generate UIs in Stitch.
- For compute‑efficient workloads, lower effort levels can be used to minimize token overhead, or developers can continue with 3.7 Flash, which remains fully supported.
