# Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.1 with Improved Performance and Reduced Pricing

Anthropic has released its latest AI models, Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, aimed at addressing customer feedback regarding pricing, data retention, and safety constraints.

## 🔍 Overview
Claude Fable 5.1 provides enhanced performance over its predecessor, particularly in complex agentic tasks. Anthropic has categorized Fable 5.1 as a "Covered Model," subjecting it to specific data retention, safety review, and access policies.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Pricing:** Fable 5.1 costs approximately 25% less than Fable 5, with savings reaching up to 45% for complex agentic tasks due to reduced pricing on cached, previously processed data.
* **Capability Improvements:** The model shows significant gains in reasoning, including a 52.6% score on the Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 benchmark.
* **Safety and Safeguards:** Fable 5.1 features more precise safeguards, reducing the likelihood of blocking basic biology questions. It is now permitted for use in identifying software vulnerabilities, though specific cybersecurity tasks remain redirected to Opus models.
* **Reasoning Settings:** The model includes five levels of reasoning: low, medium, high, xhigh, and max, with no option to disable reasoning entirely.

## 🧩 How it works
Anthropic and AWS have introduced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS), which allows eligible customers to keep data within their own controlled cloud infrastructure. Key data management features include:
* **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** Available for eligible customers on Amazon Bedrock and Claude Platform on AWS.
* **aws_review mode:** Required for Fable 5.1 usage, where prompts and outputs are retained by AWS within their boundary for safety review.
* **Future Safeguards:** Later this fall, customers will be able to manage prompts and outputs using their own encryption keys, access policies, and audit logging.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Claude Fable 5.1:** Available on all platforms, including Amazon Bedrock (US and Global inference profiles) and Claude Platform on AWS in North America.
* **Mythos 5.1:** Currently restricted to Project Glasswing participants only.
