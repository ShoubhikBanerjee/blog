# OpenAI Faces 30 New Lawsuits Over Tumbler Ridge School Shooting

OpenAI and its CEO Sam Altman are facing 30 new lawsuits accusing the company of providing "substantial assistance and encouragement" to the suspect in Canada's Tumbler Ridge school shooting. The lawsuits were filed in a California federal court on Wednesday by students, teachers, and the principal who were at the school at the time of the shooting.

## ⚖️ Allegations of Inaction

These new lawsuits, similar to those filed by the victims' families in April, accuse OpenAI of aiding and abetting the mass shooting. The core allegations include:

* **Failure to Alert Authorities:** OpenAI's automated review system flagged conversations that the alleged shooter, Jesse Van Rootselaar, had with ChatGPT regarding gun violence. Although OpenAI's safety team recommended contacting Canadian authorities, the lawsuits allege that Chief Global Affairs Officer Chris Lehane was involved in a decision to remain silent to protect the company's "reputational and financial standing."
* **Inadequate Ban Procedures:** The lawsuits allege that OpenAI allowed the suspect to maintain access to ChatGPT by "deactivating" his account instead of implementing a system-wide ban. This allowed him to regain access to the chatbot simply by signing up with a different email address.
* **Facilitating the Attack:** The filings argue that "Providing the Shooter with continued access to this tool constituted substantial assistance in itself," claiming that OpenAI's "substantial assistance was a key factor in causing the Shooter to plan and carry out the attack."

## 🛡️ Response from OpenAI

Jason Kwon, OpenAI's chief strategy officer, responded to the allegations in a post on X, stating that the lawsuits contain "false claims" regarding the company's safety teams. Kwon stated that it is "completely untrue to say that the people at the center of these challenging decisions do not prioritize safety, or that there are 'political' or 'public relations' factors at play."

## 🔍 Wider Legal Challenges

These filings are not the only legal challenges of this nature that OpenAI currently faces. The company is also facing a lawsuit from the state of Florida, which accuses OpenAI of aiding and abetting mass shooters, including a suspect who carried out an attack at Florida State University last year.
