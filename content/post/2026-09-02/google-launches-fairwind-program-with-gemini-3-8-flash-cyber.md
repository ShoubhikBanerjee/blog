# Google launches Fairwind Program with Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber for autonomous vulnerability remediation

Google has opened the Fairwind Program to a trusted set of Google Cloud customers, government agencies, and cybersecurity partners. The initiative delivers the Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber model together with the CodeMender harness, letting defenders *autonomously* locate and fix software vulnerabilities at scale.

## 🔍 Overview
- Defenders have been forced to choose between costly, hard‑to‑control frontier models and smaller open‑weight models that lack deep vulnerability‑remediation capabilities.
- The Fairwind Program provides a middle path: powerful, specialized AI that can be run inside a customer’s secure cloud environment.
- Early access gives organizations a window to harden systems before adversaries exploit the same AI capabilities.

## 🛠️ How it works
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber** supplies the core reasoning needed to understand code flaws.
- **CodeMender** acts as the harness that turns the model’s output into verified, deployment‑ready patches.
- The combined system can generate verified fixes **in minutes** instead of weeks, reducing the dwell time of vulnerabilities.
- All operations run inside the customer’s Google Cloud tenant and require multi‑factor authentication and strict role‑based access.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Cost efficiency**: CodeMender with Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber operates at a fraction of the cost of traditional frontier models.
- **Scale**: Designed for “agentic scale” – the ability to find, verify, and patch vulnerabilities across large codebases automatically.
- **Partner ecosystem**: More than 650 partners worldwide are already participating, and trusted defenders are already putting the model into practice.
- **Broader access**: While the Fairwind Program prioritizes selected partners, any Google Cloud customer can use CodeMender with publicly available models on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, complemented by AI Threat Defense solutions.

## 📋 Target sectors
| Sector                              | Purpose                                                                        |
|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Governments & national cyber authorities | Harden public‑sector networks and citizen services against targeted intrusions |
| Critical infrastructure operators  | Protect healthcare, telecom, energy, and financial services from disruption     |
| Core technology platforms           | Secure foundational software used by millions of downstream users             |

## 🚀 Availability
- Initial access is being staged to the most critical government and enterprise partners.
- Participating organizations must limit AI access to internal cybersecurity, incident‑response, or penetration‑testing teams and enforce MFA.
- The program will evolve with partner feedback, expanding offerings and collaborating with open‑weight community leaders to balance openness and security.

## 💡 Why it matters
- **Speed**: Shrinking the gap between flaw detection and patch deployment gives defenders the edge against “agentic‑speed” threats.
- **Resilience**: The initiative builds on Google’s zero‑trust architecture and billions‑daily‑account protection to bolster global cyber resilience.
- **Investment**: Through Google.org, the company has pledged > $100 million to cybersecurity, including $36 million for 35 cyber clinics serving hospitals, schools, and utilities.
- **Automation**: By automating both discovery and remediation, the Fairwind Program helps keep critical infrastructure and public services one step ahead of AI‑enabled attackers.
