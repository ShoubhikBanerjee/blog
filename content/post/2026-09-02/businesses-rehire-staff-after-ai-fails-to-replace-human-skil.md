# Businesses Rehire Staff After AI Fails to Replace Human Skills

A growing number of companies are reversing AI-driven redundancies after discovering that human skills remain irreplaceable in key areas.

## 🔍 Overview
Business leaders are backtracking on job cuts attributed to AI, with many rehiring staff after realizing the technology could not fully replicate human capabilities.

## ⚙️ Key details
- 39% of business leaders made employees redundant because of AI.
- Of those, 55% admit they made the wrong decisions about those redundancies.

## 🏢 Company examples
| Company | Action | Reason |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Ford | Rehired over 300 engineers | AI failed to match their skills and experience |
| Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) | Reversed 45 job cuts | Described the cuts as an "error" |
| IBM | Rehired staff after AI handled routine HR requests | AI struggled with complex judgment, empathy, and ethical reasoning |

## 📊 Industry outlook
Research from Gartner estimates that by 2027, 50% of companies that attributed headcount reductions to AI will rehire staff to perform similar functions under different job titles.

## 💡 Why it matters
Unique human skills like critical thinking, creativity, and ethical reasoning cannot yet be replicated by AI.
