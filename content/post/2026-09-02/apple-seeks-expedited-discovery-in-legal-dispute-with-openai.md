# Apple Seeks Expedited Discovery in Legal Dispute with OpenAI

Apple is requesting "expedited discovery" in its ongoing legal battle against OpenAI, citing concerns regarding the destruction of forensic evidence. The dispute centers on three former Apple employees who transitioned to OpenAI, including Chang Liu.

## ⚙️ Key details

*   Apple alleges that OpenAI failed to inspect a company-owned MacBook used by Liu, despite having it in their possession since July.
*   OpenAI provided the laptop to Apple on August 21st. An inspection of the device allegedly revealed that Liu downloaded a confidential Apple circuit schematic and used it in his work at OpenAI.
*   Apple claims messages on the device show Liu discussing the need to "restore" and "start using" Apple-owned equipment after learning of an internal investigation in June.
*   Apple maintains there is a "real risk of evidence destruction" regarding transient data such as logs, metadata, and usage records.
*   OpenAI argues that the dispute stems from Apple's own "mess" of an employee exit process.
*   OpenAI states there is no evidence that Liu accessed Apple information for improper purposes, noting he has "no use for Apple confidential information."
