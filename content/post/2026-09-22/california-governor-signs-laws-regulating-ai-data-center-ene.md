# California Governor Signs Laws Regulating AI Data Center Energy and Water Use

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed a package of seven bills designed to regulate the utility consumption of AI data centers and prevent them from passing costs onto residents.

## ⚙️ Key details

The new legislation mandates several operational requirements for data centers:

| Requirement | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Rate Classification | The California Public Utilities Commission must introduce a new rate classification for data centers. |
| Infrastructure Costs | Data centers are required to pay for upgrades to local power grids and water systems. |
| Transparency | Proposed data centers must disclose estimated water use, energy efficiency, and drought planning to local governments. |
| Approval Process | Facilities must meet specific energy, water, and fuel consumption requirements to qualify for a streamlined approval process. |

## 💡 Why it matters

Governor Newsom stated that these laws aim to ensure that those profiting from data centers do not do so at the expense of residents. He noted that the regulations address community concerns regarding electricity demand, grid constraints, water use, and pollution. Additionally, the Governor signed a separate executive order last week to accelerate the creation of an AI “kill switch.”
