# Jun Kim Joins Hugging Face to Support oMLX and MLX Community

Jun Kim, the creator and maintainer of oMLX, has joined Hugging Face to support the MLX community. MLX is Apple's framework for local AI, specifically optimized for Apple Silicon.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **oMLX Status**: The project remains under the Apache 2.0 license, and Jun Kim continues to lead it.
* **Project Evolution**: oMLX is transitioning from a side job to a fully maintained and funded project.
* **Objectives**: The goal is to provide the tools and building blocks necessary to unblock the community from running local AI in any shape or form.

## 💡 Why it matters

* **Development Speed**: Funding and maintenance are expected to provide stability and faster development, allowing Jun Kim to better guide contributors for the long-term.
* **Innovation**: oMLX is expected to serve as a testbed for new ideas while leveraging dependencies such as mlx-lm and mlx-vlm.
* **Streamlining Models**: A primary focus is the quick transition from a transformers model definition to a reference MLX implementation. Since the transformers library is the reference for ML model definitions, this aims to streamline making new transformers models run on MLX.
* **Collaboration**: Hugging Face intends to strengthen relationships with teams including those of Cheng, Prince, and Yagil, as well as projects like LMStudio, mlx-lm, and mlx-vlm.
