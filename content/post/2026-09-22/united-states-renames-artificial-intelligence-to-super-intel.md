# United States Renames Artificial Intelligence to Super Intelligence

During a speech at the UN General Assembly on Tuesday morning, Donald Trump announced that the United States is officially renaming artificial intelligence (AI) to "super intelligence" (SI).

## ⚙️ Key details

*   **Reason for change:** Trump claims the word "artificial" makes intelligence fake and that "super" is a more accurate term.
*   **Implementation:** All United States documents will be changed to use the term "super intelligence," with the hope that the rest of the world will follow.
*   **Global Stance:** The United States rejects any "globallist scheme" to control the technology.

## 💡 Why it matters

Trump described the technology as potentially bigger than the internet or the Industrial Revolution, stating that "whoever wins super intelligence wins." He asserted that the U.S. currently leads China and other nations in this field.

## 🔍 Oversight and Growth

*   **Approach:** The administration intends to encourage rather than rein in the growth of super intelligence.
*   **Monitoring:** The Department of Justice and other law enforcement bodies will be used to watch the technology closely and rein things in if necessary.
