# MIT Technology Review Investigation Finds Failures in Border Surveillance Towers

An investigation by MIT Technology Review has documented that over a thousand people died in areas monitored by U.S. southern border surveillance towers without being reached or apprehended.

## 🔍 Overview
Over the past 25 years, the US has invested billions of dollars into a “virtual wall” of surveillance towers along the southern border. These towers were intended to:
* Detect border crossers
* Apprehend border crossers
* Save lives

## ⚙️ Key details
Recent updates to the surveillance infrastructure include the installation of AI-powered towers. These specific towers are designed to spot people automatically; however, the investigation found that some individuals who died were under the watch of these AI-powered systems.
