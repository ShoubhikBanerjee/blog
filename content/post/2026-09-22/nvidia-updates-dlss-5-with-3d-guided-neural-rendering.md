# NVIDIA Updates DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering

NVIDIA has updated DLSS 5 to introduce DLSS 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, providing game developers with granular controls to add material detail and lifelike lighting while preserving artistic intent.

## 🧩 How it works
DLSS 5 extends the graphics pipeline by adding a final neural-rendering stage. This process uses the following as an unyielding foundation:
* The game engine’s rendered frame
* Artist-authored geometry
* Textures
* Lighting buffers

## ⚙️ Key details
Developers have access to several tools to tune the output:
* **Model Selection:** Developers can choose from multiple models and mix them across scenes, gameplay, or cutscenes.
* **Tuning Controls:** Structure Intensity and Tone Intensity can be adjusted to tune broader lighting, color response, and high-frequency detail.
* **Masking:** Semantic AI masking can apply or hold back effects across recognized scene elements, while engine-level masks can isolate asset groups such as foliage, water droplets, or glassware.

## 🚀 Availability
DLSS 5 runs locally on a single GeForce RTX 50 Series GPU at up to 4K. It is currently available via:
* **Hardware:** All GeForce RTX 50 Series desktop and laptop GPUs.
* **Games:** NBA 2K27, developed by Visual Concepts and published by 2K.
* **Cloud:** GeForce NOW Ultimate members streaming from NVIDIA-operated GeForce RTX 5080-powered gaming rigs.

## 💡 Why it matters
In the case of NBA 2K27, DLSS 5 preserves scanned facial geometry while enhancing the following:
* Skin subsurface scattering
* Contact shadows
* Light transmission through ears and hair
