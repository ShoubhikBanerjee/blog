# President announces plans to form an AI force and appoint AI czar

President Trump has announced his intention to establish an "AI force" led by an "AI czar." This initiative comes amid increasing calls from the industry and political spectrum to slow down AI development.

## ⚙️ Key details

* The administration aims to foster the growth of the AI industry without hindering it.
* The President compared the proposed AI force to the creation of the Space Force.
* No specific timeline or leadership candidates have been announced for the position.
* The President stated that "Only High I.Q. individuals need apply" for the czar role.

## 💡 Why it matters

* Data centers: The President asserted that data centers are "wealthy and prestigious" and claimed they raise salaries, lower taxes, and improve community safety, though no evidence was provided to support these claims.
