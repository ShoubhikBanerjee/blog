# Protegrity-PII model sets new benchmark for personal data detection

On 21 Oct 2026 a technical report titled **Protegrity-PII** presented a new decision model for detecting personally identifiable information (PII) in both structured and unstructured text.

## 🔍 Overview
- Introduces **Protegrity-PII**, a decision model that works on structured and unstructured text.
- Accompanied by a newly proposed benchmark called **PII‑SCOPE**.

## 🧩 How it works
- Trained on an expansive entity‑type taxonomy, which contributes to strong out‑of‑distribution generalization.
- Evaluated against a selection of state‑of‑the‑art models and datasets, showing superior performance.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Performance on PII‑SCOPE**:  typed recall = 0.915, precision = 0.907.
- **Efficiency**: processes text at 0.49 seconds per million characters.
- **Cost**: $0.0005 per million characters on a single RTX 6000 using batched inference.

## 💡 Why it matters
- High recall and precision indicate reliable identification of PII, which is critical for privacy‑preserving applications.
- Low processing time and cost make the model practical for large‑scale deployments.
- Strong out‑of‑distribution generalization suggests robustness when applied to new domains or data sources.

## 🚀 Availability
- The model and benchmark were reported in the **Protegrity-PII Technical Report** submitted on 21 Oct 2026.
