# Vanguard’s Jing Wang Discusses Building Scalable AI Foundations at EmTech Future

Jing Wang, Principal of Vanguard’s Center for AI & Insights, presented at MIT Technology Review’s EmTech Future conference, where the session explored how to build a strong foundation for scalable AI in a highly regulated environment.

## 🔍 Overview
- A scalable AI system requires a strong foundation.
- Data, engineering, platforms, operating models, and governance must converge to work as one.

## 🏗️ Foundations for Scalable AI
- **Data**
- **Engineering**
- **Platforms**
- **Operating models**
- **Governance**

## ✅ Responsible Implementation
This session from MIT Technology Review's EmTech Future conference explores what it takes to build that foundation in a highly regulated environment and how a responsible, trustworthy implementation can turn emerging capabilities, including agentic systems, into lasting value for investors.

## 👩‍💼 Speaker Profile
- Jing Wang leads Vanguard's Center for AI & Insights (CAI), advancing the firm's AI, machine learning, generative AI, and behavioral science capabilities.
- Her team drives enterprise-wide innovation, research, and delivery, enabling Vanguard to translate emerging technologies into trusted solutions that enhance client experiences, improve investor outcomes, and create measurable business value.
- Before her current position, Jing served in a range of leadership roles across Vanguard's Financial Services, Workplace Solutions, International, and Corporate Services divisions.
- She joined Vanguard in 2009 after several years as a Principal at the Boston Consulting Group.
- Jing earned a B.S. in Physics from the University of Science and Technology of China, a Ph.D. from the University of Pennsylvania, and an M.B.A. in Finance and Marketing from the Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania.
- Jing holds Series 7, 24, and 65 licenses.
