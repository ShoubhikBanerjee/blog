# Canva launches Canva Craft AI image generation model

Canva announced the launch of Canva Craft, an AI image‑generation model that directly competes with offerings from OpenAI, Microsoft, and Google.

## 🔍 Overview
- Canva Craft uses a two‑stage architecture that plans a design before generating an image, providing greater precision, consistency and control.
- The model is positioned as Canva’s first AI image model that plans designs before generation.

## 🧩 How it works
- The two‑stage system first creates a layout plan, then renders the final image, which helps it generate complex images that require legible text and precise object placement.
- Canva trained Craft on “millions of human‑made templates” from its library, including social media posts, presentations, infographics, and menus.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Generates images up to 2K resolution.
- Supports more than 30 aspect ratios.
- Capable of creating social media posts, marketing content, realistic scenes, digital art, environmental imagery, and more.
- Includes layers for AI‑generated images, allowing edits to parts of an image while leaving the rest untouched.
- Built on prompt‑based editing capabilities launched in April, which let users describe changes to a design.
- Ranked fourth on Design Arena’s leaderboard for the best AI image generation models, behind OpenAI’s GPT‑Image‑2.5 Sunburst, GPT‑Image‑2.5 Flare, and GPT‑Image‑2.
- Provides robust input and output moderation, privacy and safety controls, and reporting tools.
- Future roadmap includes AI agents plugging into Canva Craft via the open Model Context Protocol and the rollout of faster variants (Craft‑fast and Craft‑ultrafast).

| Rank | Model |
|------|-------|
| 1 | OpenAI GPT‑Image‑2.5 Sunburst |
| 2 | OpenAI GPT‑Image‑2.5 Flare |
| 3 | OpenAI GPT‑Image‑2 |
| 4 | Canva Craft |

## 🚀 Availability
- Canva Craft is available now to all users globally.
- To try it, open Canva, click the three‑dot option on the left‑hand menu, and select **Dream Lab**.

## 💡 Why it matters
- By planning designs before rendering, Canva Craft aims to give designers more control over AI‑generated visuals, addressing common challenges such as text legibility and object placement.
- The model expands Canva’s AI portfolio, complementing earlier features like prompt‑based editing and layered image manipulation.
- Ranking fourth on a crowdsourced benchmark shows Canva’s growing competitiveness in the AI image‑generation space.
