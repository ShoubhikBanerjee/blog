# EU AI Act Introduces Agent Disclosure Rules

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) has introduced transparency obligations under Article 50(1), known as Agent Disclosure Rules.

## 🔍 Overview
Agent Disclosure Rules are the transparency obligations mandated by the EU AI Act for AI agents.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Agent Disclosure Rules fall under Article 50(1) of the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689).
