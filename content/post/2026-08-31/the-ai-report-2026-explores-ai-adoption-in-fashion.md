# The AI Report 2026 explores AI adoption in fashion

The AI Report 2026 has been released, offering insights into AI's role in the fashion industry through interviews, surveys, and industry perspectives.

## 📖 Overview
The AI Report 2026 is essential reading for anyone interested in AI for fashion. It includes profiles and exclusive interviews with 17 AI companies, stories and opinions from 12 different industry perspectives, and real survey feedback from around 100 voices from every level of fashion.

## 🧩 Key details
- The report addresses the tension between adopting AI and questioning its planetary, human, and ethical costs.
- It highlights the industry's reliance on fast-paced, automated systems, overproduction, and overconsumption, while exploring the shift toward agentic decision-making.
- The report features survey data and industry-wide perspectives on AI adoption.

## 💡 Why it matters
- The report provides a platform for discussing AI's relationship with people, planet, and production in fashion.
- It encourages reflection on what work can be done by AI and what should remain human-centric.
- The report underscores the need to balance AI's opportunities with its potential trade-offs.
