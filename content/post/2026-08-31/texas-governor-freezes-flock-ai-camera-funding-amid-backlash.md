# Texas Governor Freezes Flock AI Camera Funding Amid Backlash

Texas Governor Greg Abbott has frozen state spending on Flock AI surveillance cameras, a move that came just ahead of a Texas Tribune investigation revealing the state spent over $30 million on the systems. The funding was primarily raised by adding a $1 fee to insurance policies, allegedly to combat catalytic converter theft.

## 🔍 Overview
- Flock cameras have faced growing bipartisan backlash due to privacy concerns.
- Multiple incidents of officers misusing Flock systems have been reported, including at least six in Texas alone.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The company has faced scrutiny over:
  - Data-sharing practices
  - Placement of cameras near playgrounds
  - Employees' access to video feeds
  - Unsecured video feeds
  - Attempts to avoid transparency

## 🏛️ Political response
- Cities across the U.S. have canceled contracts with Flock, including many in Texas.
- Abbott’s Democratic opponent, Gina Hinojosa, has criticized the expansion of Flock cameras in the state.
- Republican representative Keith Self stated on Instagram: *“From Flock cameras to kill switches, we will NOT surrender our Fourth Amendment rights to the growing surveillance state.”*
- Online, cheering on the destruction of Flock cameras has become a unifying pastime.
