# OpenAI's Advertising Business Reaches $1 B Annualized Revenue Run Rate

OpenAI announced on Monday that its advertising business has hit a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate and is expanding globally as the company emphasizes a diversified business model ahead of an IPO.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI — AI research and deployment company.
- New milestone: $1 billion in annualized advertising revenue.
- Announcement made on a Monday.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The ad business is **expanding globally**.
- OpenAI describes the achievement as part of a **“diversified business model.”**
- The disclosure comes **ahead of an IPO**, indicating a focus on revenue diversification.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Reaching a $1 billion run rate signals that OpenAI’s non‑core revenue streams are scaling.
- Global expansion suggests the advertising platform is being rolled out across multiple regions.
- Positioning the ad business within a diversified model may strengthen investor confidence ahead of the upcoming public offering.
