# AgentGuide launches AI Agent development and job-seeking knowledge base

A new open-source knowledge base, AgentGuide, has been introduced to provide a systematic, practical, and job-oriented solution for AI Agent development and career preparation.

## 🔍 Overview
AgentGuide positions itself as a one-stop solution for AI Agent development and job-seeking, offering a structured learning path, practical guidance, and career-focused insights. It integrates existing high-quality resources without reinventing the wheel, focusing on delivering actionable content.

## 🧩 How it works
- Aggregates and organizes fragmented resources into a complete learning roadmap, clarifying the sequence of topics to study.
- Provides job-seeking orientation by annotating each knowledge point with interview questions and resume-writing tips.
- Covers the full AI Agent development stack, from model fine-tuning to deployment, without binding to a single framework.

## ⚙️ Key details
AgentGuide addresses common challenges in AI Agent development, including:
- Long-task execution issues (early stopping, loops, state loss) and debugging difficulties.
- Lack of a unified system architecture for concepts like LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, MCP, Skills, and Multi-Agent.
- Integration of Context, Memory, Tools, Permissions, Sandbox, and Trace in reliable systems.
- Gaps in RAG implementations for complex documents, multimodal data, citation tracing, evaluation sets, and online monitoring.
- Missing baselines, metrics, failure analyses, and engineering trade-offs in project documentation.
- Unclear skill and portfolio requirements for development, algorithm, Agentic RL, and research roles.
- Rapid updates in papers, frameworks, and open-source projects, with guidance on prioritization and validation.

The project organizes content around four pillars:
- **做得出 (Buildable)**: Capability to develop AI Agents.
- **跑得稳 (Stable)**: Reliability in execution.
- **测得准 (Measurable)**: Accurate evaluation and testing.
- **讲得清 (Explainable)**: Clear communication of methods and results.
