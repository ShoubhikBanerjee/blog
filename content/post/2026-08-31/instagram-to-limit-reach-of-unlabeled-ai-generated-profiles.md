# Instagram to Limit Reach of Unlabeled AI-Generated Profiles

Instagram is implementing new measures to address the rise of fake AI-influencer accounts that have become increasingly difficult to detect.

## ⚙️ Key details

Instagram is introducing the following changes to identify AI-generated personas:

* **Label Update**: The “AI creator” label is being renamed to “AI-generated profile” to clarify when a profile features an AI-generated person rather than a real human being.
* **Enforcement**: The platform will identify accounts that fail to self-label their AI-generated profiles.
* **Penalties**: Accounts that do not self-label will have their reach limited, meaning their posts will not be recommended to non-followers in Explore and Reels.
* **Appeals**: Profile owners have the option to appeal if they are wrongly flagged.

## 💡 Why it matters

Instagram stated in a blog post, “We’ve heard that people don’t like seeing a profile that seems human, only to find out later that the person featured is AI-generated,” adding that users “want to know when a profile features an AI-generated person.”

## 🧩 How it works

| User Type | Label Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| AI-generated person | Must self-label as an "AI-generated profile" |
| People using AI tools for content | No label required |
