# Qwen3.8 Max Leads August 2026 AI Model Rankings

Alibaba's Qwen3.8 Max has been ranked as the best new model released in August 2026, according to the BenchLM LLM leaderboard tracking 228+ models across 406+ benchmarks.

## 🔍 Overview
- The BenchLM LLM leaderboard ranks models across coding, reasoning, knowledge, and multimodal tasks using normalized weighted averages.
- Qwen3.8 Max is the top-ranked model released in August 2026.
- The leaderboard includes frontier and open-source models like GPT-5, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama, and others.

## 📊 Rankings and Metrics
- Models are scored based on benchmarks, pricing, runtime signals, and context window.
- Supported and Estimated labels indicate evidence strength for each model's position.
- Benchmarks include SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, GPQA Diamond, MMLU-Pro, and others.
- Confidence is tracked separately from scores to reflect public benchmark evidence.

## 🧩 How it works
- Each model's overall score is a normalized weighted average of category averages.
- Benchmark results are normalized to a common scale and combined using weights favoring harder, less-saturated evaluations.
- Display-only benchmarks remain visible for context but do not receive ranking weight.
- Data sources include OpenBench, official model papers, and public leaderboards.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The leaderboard tracks 406+ large language models across 408 benchmarks.
- Categories include agentic workflows, math, multilingual tasks, and instruction following.
- Operator tradeoffs like price and context window are included in comparisons.
- The top open-source models in 2026 include Meta's Llama 4, DeepSeek R1 and V3, Mistral's Large and Medium, Alibaba's Qwen 2.5 series, and Google's Gemma.

## 🚀 Availability
- The leaderboard is updated hourly with the latest rankings and evidence.
- Users can filter models by capability, workload, or other criteria.
- Embeddable live leaderboards and shareable filtered views are available.
- Open-source models can be self-hosted, fine-tuned, and deployed without vendor lock-in.
