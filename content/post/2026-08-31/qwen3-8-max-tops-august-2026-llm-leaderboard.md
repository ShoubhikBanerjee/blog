# Qwen3.8 Max Tops August 2026 LLM Leaderboard

Alibaba's Qwen3.8 Max has been ranked as the best-performing model released in August 2026 on the BenchLM LLM leaderboard, which tracks 406+ large language models across 408 benchmarks.

## 🔍 Overview
The BenchLM LLM leaderboard 2026 ranks 228+ models and tracks 406+ large language models side by side across 408 benchmarks, including coding, knowledge, reasoning, and multimodal tasks. Each model's overall score is a normalized weighted average of category averages, with data sourced from OpenBench, official model papers, and public leaderboards.

## 📊 Leaderboard Highlights
- **Best ranked model released in August 2026**: Qwen3.8 Max (Alibaba).
- **Supported and Estimated models**: 105 Supported and 123 Estimated among 406 tracked LLMs.
- **Benchmarks covered**: 408, including SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, GPQA Diamond, MMLU-Pro, and Terminal-Bench 2.0.
- **Confidence and evidence**: Scores are separated from confidence, which reflects public benchmark evidence. Display-only benchmarks remain visible without ranking weight.

## 🧩 How it works
- Benchmarks are grouped into categories (e.g., coding, knowledge, reasoning) and normalized to a common scale.
- Weights favor harder, less-saturated evaluations within each category.
- External consensus signals are used as bounded calibration inputs but are not exposed in exported data.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Model comparison**: Users can compare any two ranked models, with exact units and evidence coverage displayed.
- **Filtering**: Benchmarks, pricing, runtime signals, and context window data are available in one table. Filter state syncs to the URL for shareable views.
- **Evidence strength**: Supported and Estimated labels indicate the level of independent evidence backing each model's position.

## 🚀 Availability
- The leaderboard is updated continuously and is free to access.
- Rankings are based on real pricing, runtime data, and benchmark performance.
