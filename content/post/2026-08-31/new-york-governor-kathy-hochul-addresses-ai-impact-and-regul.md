# New York Governor Kathy Hochul Addresses AI Impact and Regulation

New York Governor Kathy Hochul has discussed her perspectives on artificial intelligence, focusing on the technology's ethical direction and its impact on the workforce.

## 💡 Why it matters
Governor Hochul has expressed the view that AI should be “less evil”. To address the societal effects of the technology, she has established the Future Works Commission. This commission brings in experts from across the country to analyze:

* The displacement of jobs caused by AI
* Identifying who is most vulnerable to these changes
* Methods for training people to overcome obstacles or embrace AI as a possible career

## ⚙️ Key details
In addition to workforce concerns, the Governor has taken specific regulatory actions regarding AI-related infrastructure:

* In July, Governor Hochul signed a one-year moratorium on data center construction.
