# Replica Cyber launches governed AI agents for regulated enterprises

Replica Cyber has announced governed AI agents running inside isolated environments designed for regulated enterprises, enabling automation of high-risk digital work such as AI experimentation, investigations, fraud analysis, threat intelligence, and dark web monitoring.

## 🔍 Overview
Replica Cyber’s new architecture allows financial institutions and other regulated enterprises to deploy AI agents for high-risk tasks while maintaining strict security and governance controls.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Every agent operates inside its own isolated environment, eliminating direct paths to the corporate network.
- Security teams can observe, pause, terminate, and fully replay every agent action.
- Governance controls meet regulatory requirements for auditability and oversight.
- Traditional AI governance tools monitor activity after the fact, whereas Replica governs agent behavior at runtime via isolation.

## 🧩 How it works
- Agents are contained within zero-trust isolated environments proven over 8 years in production.
- The platform supports faster research, automated intelligence gathering, accelerated fraud analysis, and audit-ready evidence without policy exceptions.

## 🚀 Availability
Replica Cyber’s governed AI agents and environments are available today to financial services and regulated enterprise customers.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Gartner projects more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027 due to inadequate risk controls.
- Replica’s Exception Economy data shows 32% of security leaders delayed or canceled an AI deployment in the past year because no secure environment existed.

## 🏢 Company background
- Founded by counterintelligence experts from the U.S. Intelligence Community.
- Backed by 24+ AI-based patents, SOC 2 Type II, DoD Authority to Operate, and Certified B Corp status.
- Has isolated production workflows across U.S. defense and global financial services since 2018.
