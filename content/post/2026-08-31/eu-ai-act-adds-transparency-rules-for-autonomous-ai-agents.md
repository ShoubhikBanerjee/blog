# EU AI Act adds transparency rules for autonomous AI agents

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) has introduced new transparency obligations for autonomous AI agents under Article 50(1). These rules require agents to disclose their operational status, capabilities, and limitations to users and regulators.

## 🔍 Overview
Agent disclosure rules are the transparency obligations under Article 50(1) of the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) that mandate clear communication about an AI agent's identity, purpose, and constraints.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Agent identity** is the set of credentials, characteristics, and records that allow an autonomous AI agent to be uniquely identified and authenticated.
- **Agentic AI** refers to autonomous, goal-driven AI systems that perceive their environment, plan multi-step actions, invoke external tools, and interact with users or other systems.
- **Agent disclosure rules** require agents to reveal their identity, operational scope, and any limitations to ensure transparency and compliance.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Regulatory basis**: Article 50(1) of the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689).
- **Scope**: Autonomous AI agents operating within the EU or interacting with EU users.
- **Requirements**: Agents must provide clear, accessible disclosures about their capabilities, limitations, and any data they collect or process.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enhances user trust by ensuring agents are transparent about their operations.
- Supports regulatory compliance for organizations deploying autonomous AI systems.
- Reduces risks of misuse by making agent behavior and limitations explicit.
