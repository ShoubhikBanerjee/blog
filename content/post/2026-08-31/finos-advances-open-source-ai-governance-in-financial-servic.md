# FINOS Advances Open Source AI Governance in Financial Services

FINOS has announced new initiatives and events to support financial institutions in adopting AI through open source frameworks and governance. The organization is hosting technical deep-dives, working group meetings, and a dedicated conference to address AI governance, compliance, and enterprise deployment.

## 🔍 Overview
FINOS is focusing on AI governance, open source collaboration, and enterprise AI deployment in financial services. Key efforts include OSERA, the FINOS AI Fund, and governance-as-code frameworks.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Events**: A two-day conference in NYC on November 4–5, featuring tracks on AI governance, OSERA, FDC3, HPC, and open compliance.
  - Day 1 includes Fluxnova Con (financial orchestration and multi-agent systems) and parallel Enterprise AI tracks on SDLC controls, FINOS CALM architectural compliance, and security frameworks for Agentic AI.
  - Day 2 covers AI Implementation, Zero Trust AI & Security, OSPO Culture, Platform Engineering (FDC3/CDM), and Hot Topics (Quantum & HPC).
- **Working Groups**: Scheduled meetings include:
  - FDC3 Sail Project
  - Labs - CyberThirdEye
  - Fluxnova - AI Working Group
  - Open Financial LLM Leaderboard (OFLL)
  - CCC - Compliant Financial Infrastructure (EU/APAC)
  - Zenith - Emerging Technologies SIG
  - SDLC Governance Framework
  - CDM Contribution Review
  - AI Readiness SIG
  - Open Source Talent Acceleration SIG
  - FDC3 Use Cases and Workflows Discussion Group
  - FINOS Morphir Project
- **Community Engagement**: A keynote and presentations on the Common Domain Model (CDM) benefits across the securities processing lifecycle, including a networking reception.
- **Executive Participation**: Gabriele Columbro, FINOS Executive Director, will participate in a panel at Boston Fintech Week on September 22, titled "Owning the Stack: Open Source Fintech AI Innovation."
- **Podcast**: An episode of the Open Source in Finance Podcast features Dr. Aditya Vikram Kashyap (FINOS Ambassador, IEEE Senior Member, Forbes Technology Council member) discussing AI and open source in finance, hosted by Peter Smulovics (Morgan Stanley, FINOS Zenith Lead).

## 🚀 Availability
- Conference: November 4–5 in NYC.
- Boston Fintech Week panel: September 22.
- Working group meetings and podcast episodes are available for sign-up or streaming as listed.
