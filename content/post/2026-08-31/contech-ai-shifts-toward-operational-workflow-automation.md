# ConTech AI Shifts Toward Operational Workflow Automation

AI adoption in construction technology is progressing beyond conversational interfaces to the automation of operational workflows. This shift is part of a broader new phase in the US AEC software market characterized by increased venture funding and strategic M&A activity.

## ⚙️ Key details
AI is shifting from chat interfaces into executing specific construction tasks. Capabilities now include the automation of:
* Estimating
* Preconstruction
* Document review
* RFIs
* Field reporting
* Scheduling

## 💡 Why it matters
This transition represents a move from tools that simply assist users to platforms focused on executing work. Procore's positioning of AI-powered "digital coworkers" illustrates this focus on executing operational workflows rather than relying on conversational interfaces.
