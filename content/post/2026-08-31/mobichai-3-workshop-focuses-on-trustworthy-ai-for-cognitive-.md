# MobiCHAI’3 Workshop Focuses on Trustworthy AI for Cognitive Support

The ACM International Conference on Mobile Human-Computer Interaction 2026 will host the 3rd International Workshop on Mobile Cognition-Altering and Cognitive-Augmenting Technologies using Human-Centered AI (mobiCHAI’3).

## 🔍 Overview
- MobiCHAI’3 is the third edition of the International Workshop on Mobile Cognition-Altering/Augmenting Technologies (CAT) Using Human-Centered AI.
- The workshop follows two successful events in 2024 and 2025.
- Organizers include Agnes Gruenerbl, Jan Spilski, Paul Lukowicz, and Thomas Lachmann, all affiliated with institutions in Kaiserslautern, Germany.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The focus has shifted from exploring the design space to trustworthy, mission-oriented, and domain-specific mobile and wearable AI systems for cognitive support in real-world contexts.
- Key domains include healthcare and other high-stakes areas, such as:
  - Support for surgical preparation and perioperative procedures
  - Monitoring of stress and workload
  - Cognitive assistance for clinicians and care teams
- Special attention is given to trust calibration in AI-supported decision-making.
- Contributions are welcome from education, work, accessibility, well-being, and everyday life.

## 🧩 Topics
- Multimodal sensing of cognitive and affective states
- Human-centered modeling and simulation of cognition
- Methods of cognitive augmentation
- Ethical, social, and trust-related implications

## 🎓 Audience
- The conference addresses an interdisciplinary community spanning:
  - Mobile HCI
  - Ubiquitous Computing
  - Cognitive Science
  - Healthcare
  - AI
  - Design Research
  - Social Sciences
