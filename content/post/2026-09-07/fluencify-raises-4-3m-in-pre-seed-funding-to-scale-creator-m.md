# Fluencify Raises $4.3M in Pre-Seed Funding to Scale Creator Marketing Platform

Fluencify, a Stockholm-based provider of an AI-powered creator marketing platform, has raised $4.3 million in Pre-Seed funding. The round was led by byFounders, with participation from Wave Ventures.

## 🔍 Overview
Led by CEO Erik Romdhane, the company provides an end-to-end service designed to help businesses create and distribute short-form user-generated content at scale. Fluencify currently connects businesses with over 13,000 active creators worldwide and has reached $2 million in ARR within six months of launch.

## 🧩 How it works
The platform utilizes AI agents to automate the following processes across more than 85 countries:

* Creator discovery and matching
* Outreach and reply handling
* Briefing and post scheduling
* Paid boosting
* Cross-border payouts

## ⚙️ Key details
The company intends to utilize the newly raised capital for the following strategic initiatives:

| Initiative | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Hiring | Expand engineering team in Stockholm |
| Geographic Expansion | Open a New York office and expand US sales |
| Network Growth | Grow the active creator network |
| Product Development | Strengthen the platform's product offering |
