# Cyara Appoints Ben Corser to Lead APAC Expansion and Announces Agentic CX Summit

Cyara, an AI trust company for customer experience, has appointed Ben Corser as General Manager and SVP, Go-to-Market for Asia-Pacific. The move signals an investment in the Australian market and a focus on accelerating growth throughout the region.

## 👤 Leadership Appointment
Ben Corser joins Cyara following senior leadership roles at several global technology firms:

| EMC | Previous leadership |
| Nokia | Previous leadership |
| Microsoft | Previous leadership |

## ⚙️ Cyara Agentic Platform
Chief Product Officer Amitha Pulijala will present innovations regarding the Cyara Agentic Platform. Key capabilities include:

* Proving AI agent behavior and end-to-end customer journeys prior to deployment.
* Maintaining visibility into quality and compliance for live agents.
* Moving from monitoring agent availability to verifying that agents behave as intended via AI Trust Observability.
* Scoring every live conversation for quality and compliance.

## 🚀 Upcoming Regional Events
Cyara plans to engage with the regional market through two upcoming events:

* Cyara Agentic CX Summit: September 8 in Melbourne.
* Genesys Xperience APAC: November 11–12 in Sydney.

## 💡 Why it matters
Cyara currently supports more than 450 enterprises, including significant Australian organizations such as two of the Big Four banks, two national mobile network operators, and the largest supermarket retailer. The platform operates across all major CCaaS providers and supports over 55 AI agent vendors.
