# Penn researchers develop hybrid light-matter particle for energy-efficient AI computing

Researchers at Penn have created a hybrid light-matter particle that could dramatically speed up AI computing while using far less energy.

## ⚙️ Key details
* Created by researchers at Penn.
* Consists of a hybrid particle combining light and matter.
* Designed to increase the speed of AI calculations.
* Engineered to operate with significantly lower energy requirements.
