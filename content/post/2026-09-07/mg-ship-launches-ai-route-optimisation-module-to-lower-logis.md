# MG Ship Launches AI Route Optimisation Module to Lower Logistics Costs

Logistics technology provider MG Ship has introduced a new AI-powered module focused on route optimisation and carrier recommendations for global retailers and shippers. In tandem with the product launch, MG Ship CEO Suki Cheung will join industry executives from Pos Malaysia, Omniva, and OnyX Space at the WMX Asia conference on September 16-17, 2026, in Hong Kong to discuss how real-world AI applications are delivering measurable operational and financial results.

## ⚙️ Key details

The newly introduced module is designed to help shippers transform logistics into a competitive advantage by recommending the best route, the right carrier, and the lowest-risk option based on real-time conditions. According to early deployments, the solution is designed to help shippers achieve the following results:
* Reduce lead-time variability.
* Lower premium freight and expedite spending.
* Improve on-time-in-full (OTIF) performance.
* Strengthen inventory planning accuracy.
* Increase product availability, improve sell-through rates, and enhance working-capital efficiency.

The module integrates with MG Ship's core visibility and intelligence platform, which combines real-time shipment visibility with predictive analytics, trade intelligence, and risk monitoring capabilities.

## 💡 Why it matters

While many industry conversations remain focused on future possibilities, AI is already delivering measurable business outcomes. Across early adopters, AI-enabled supply chain programs are delivering average logistics and operational cost reductions of 10% to 25%, lowering forecast errors by 20% to 40%, and increasing warehouse productivity by 25% to 35% within the fifth year of deployment.

Research and industry case studies show that some of the fastest returns on investment are generated in three key areas:

| AI Application | Operational Improvements | Financial & Inventory Benefits | Payback Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dynamic route optimisation** | Improves delivery speed by 15% to 25% | Reduces fuel consumption by 15% to 20%, lowers transportation costs by 12% to 22%, and reduces operating costs by 12% to 20% | 3 to 6 months |
| **AI-driven demand forecasting** | Reduces forecast errors by 20% to 40%, and improves forecasting accuracy by up to 35% | Lowers inventory levels by 20% to 30% | 6 to 12 months |
| **Freight documentation automation** | Reduces manual processing time by up to 85% | Significantly improves productivity | 3 to 6 months |

## 🚀 Availability

Global shippers, retailers, and e-commerce leaders attending WMX Asia can experience live demonstrations of MG Ship's AI route optimisation and carrier recommendation platform. Attendees are also invited to explore pilot programs designed to quantify operational and financial ROI within their own logistics networks.

The 2026 WMX Asia event, themed "Delivering the Future: eCommerce, Innovation & Opportunity in Asia’s Logistics Landscape," will take place on 16-17 September 2026 at the Kerry Hotel, Hong Kong.
