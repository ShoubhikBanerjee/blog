# Gain.Energy launches Upstrima AI platform for oil and gas engineering

Gain.Energy has launched Upstrima, an AI platform designed to help oil and gas companies automate engineering tasks and improve the speed of technical work.

## 🔍 Overview
- Upstrima combines AI agents, automated workflows, data processing and engineering expertise in a single platform.
- Designed specifically for the oil and gas industry to help engineers use AI in day-to-day technical operations.
- Offered as a subscription‑based web application for oil and gas engineers and organisations.

## 🧩 How it works
- Companies can use ready‑to‑use AI agents from a marketplace or develop customised tools based on their own data, procedures and engineering requirements.
- The marketplace is open to third‑party developers and includes agents for a range of engineering applications.
- Users can also build and train their own AI agents or request customised agents from Gain.Energy’s engineering and technology team.
- Multiple AI agents can be linked into automated AI workflows, allowing several technical tasks to be connected rather than handled separately.
- The platform can process large amounts of unstructured oil and gas data, identifying and classifying information across more than 50 commonly used file formats, including scanned, typewritten and handwritten documents, and can ingest data from sources such as WITSML.

## ⚙️ Key capabilities
- Reduces need for manual data preparation before engineering teams can use the information.
- Automates processes of varying complexity; specialised agents and workflows can cut execution times by 50 %–70 %.
- Enables engineers to spend less time on repetitive technical work and more time on analysis and important engineering decisions.

## 📂 Marketplace applications
- Offset drilling analysis  
- Stuck‑pipe prevention  
- Drilling dysfunction root‑cause analysis  
- Mechanical equipment failure analysis  
- End‑of‑well reporting  
- Well integrity  
- Procurement spend analysis  
- Plug‑and‑abandonment activities  

## 🚀 Availability
- Upstrima is available as a subscription‑based web application.
- Gain.Energy positions the platform as a way for businesses to introduce AI into existing engineering processes without building every application from scratch.

## 🏢 Company background
- Gain.Energy was established in the United States in January 2024 by Dr Vlad Karen Payrazyan.
- The company focuses on developing AI technologies for industrial applications, with particular expertise in petroleum engineering.
- The team combines oil and gas engineering knowledge with software and AI development capabilities.
