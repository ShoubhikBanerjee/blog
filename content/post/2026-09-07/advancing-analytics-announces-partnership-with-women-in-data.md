# Advancing Analytics Announces Partnership with Women in Data

Advancing Analytics has announced a new partnership with Women in Data, an organization focused on advocacy, mentorship, and networking for women in the data and AI industries. This collaboration aims to support the mission of driving gender parity within the field.

## 🤝 Partnership Goals

The collaboration aims to provide support and create opportunities through:

* Advocacy and networking events
* Professional mentorship opportunities
* Access to specialized resources
* Industry jobs board access
* Workshops to further professional development

## 💡 Why it Matters

Advancing Analytics identifies inclusive teams that bring together different perspectives as a key driver for the strongest thinking and best project outcomes. By joining the Women in Data community, the firm seeks to support a mission aligned with its internal values regarding diversity and professional growth in data and AI.
