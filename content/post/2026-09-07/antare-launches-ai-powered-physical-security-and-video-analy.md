# Antare Launches AI-Powered Physical Security and Video Analysis Platform

UK-based company Antare has introduced an AI-based physical security platform designed to automatically process footage from fixed and body-worn cameras. The system converts recorded video into searchable data by transcribing and indexing events as they are recorded.

## 🔍 Overview
The Antare Console performs real-time analysis of video and audio streams. By utilizing AI agents, the platform can monitor for specific activities, including physical and verbal assaults, medical events, safety risks, and suspicious behavior. Security teams receive alerts and live video feeds through the Antare App.

## 🧩 How it works
Users can perform free-text searches on recorded footage to locate specific incidents. The platform also identifies patterns across recorded data, such as identifying theft hotspots, repeat offenders, and recurring incidents across different locations.

## ⚙️ Key details
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Encryption | Data is encrypted on devices and in transit |
| Access Control | Role-based access for cloud-stored data |
| Certifications | SOC 2 Type I certified; SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 in progress |

## 🚀 Availability
* Current compatibility: Antare’s two body-worn camera models.
* Upcoming hardware: Antare Cloud Gateway will connect the platform with existing fixed cameras.
* Future development: Antare plans to launch a proprietary fixed camera range in early 2027.
