# UWA Launches Researcher Development Academy to Support University Research

The University of Western Australia (UWA) has launched the Researcher Development Academy (RDA), a university-wide initiative designed to integrate researcher training, leadership development, and industry engagement under a single framework.

## 🔍 Overview
Launched by Parliamentary Secretary to the Minister for Science and Innovation, Yaz Muburakai MLA, the academy aims to strengthen research excellence and societal impact. It serves over 4,000 researchers, including higher degree by research (HDR) students, early-to-mid-career researchers, senior researchers, and supervisors.

## ⚙️ Key Details
The academy provides an integrated framework focused on research capability, leadership, communication, and career development. Key focus areas include:
* Training in entrepreneurial mindsets and commercialization pipelines.
* Project management and collaborative teamwork skills.
* Communicating research to diverse audiences.

## 🧩 Programs and Activities
| Program/Activity | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Research Ready | Integrated training and support framework |
| Future Forward | Professional and leadership development |
| HDR Supervisor Training | Training for research supervisors |
| Bold Ideas, Better Futures | Flagship series featuring industry and academic speakers |

## 💡 Why it matters
According to Pro Vice-Chancellor (Research Training), Professor Hélène de Burgh-Woodman, the academy is a significant investment in researchers and aligns with State priorities in innovation, workforce development, and economic diversification. The academy recently hosted its first theme, "Research Futures in the Age of AI," which explored artificial intelligence in higher education, including an international keynote by Professor James Zou from Stanford University on AI agents and scientific discovery.
