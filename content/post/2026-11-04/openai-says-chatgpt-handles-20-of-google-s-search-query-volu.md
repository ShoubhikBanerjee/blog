# OpenAI says ChatGPT handles 20% of Google’s search query volume

In many circles, “Ask Chat” has replaced “Google it,” and that’s reflected in a new announcement from OpenAI: the volume of search queries run through ChatGPT now equals 20 percent of the volume run through Google.

## 🔍 Overview
- "OpenAI says ChatGPT is catching up to Google in search"
- "ChatGPT processes one-fifth the volume of search queries as Google, per OpenAI."
- The announcement was made by Adam Fry, OpenAI’s consumer product lead for ChatGPT, at a press event on Monday.

## 🧩 Definition of Search
- Fry said the company defines a search as any time a user inputs a query that results in a web search, as opposed to tasks like defining a term or a simple how‑to explanation.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The reported volume is *one‑fifth* of Google’s search traffic.
- "Having one-fifth as many searches as Google is still a long way from overtaking it, but for comparison, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said during Google’s 2023 antitrust trial that Bing had about 3 percent of the search engine market share after trying for more than a decade."
- OpenAI likely uses Microsoft Bing for a significant part of its searches, along with its in‑house search index, although there is still speculation that some queries are routed through Google.
- "During the trial, Google described AI as a potential disruptor, arguing ChatGPT was poised to seriously compete with it — now, OpenAI seems to agree."

## 💰 Monetization
- "Much like Google, OpenAI sees a potential path to profitability through ads, particularly as it approaches an IPO potentially slated for early next year."
- "OpenAI announced last month that the company would begin introducing images of sponsored products and services in ChatGPT rather than just a company’s name, logo, and a link to its product like before."
- "Ads have not negatively impacted the product or the usage of the product," Fry said, adding that “most people do not seem to mind ads in the product.”
- "Ads have maybe shown us that we could be doing this more, actually," Fry said.

## 📊 Competitive Landscape
- Google continues to reduce conventional referrals to third‑party sites and invests heavily in AI tools, including personalized search results with visualizations and charts.
- Other companies are investing in AI‑focused search experiences; for OpenAI, it’s called “Intelligent UI,” a visual and interactive interface within a chatbot response.

## 🚀 Future Direction
- Fry described the vision: "That’s where the future of this technology goes. You’re talking to a canvas," he said.
- OpenAI’s focus on an “Intelligent UI” suggests a shift toward a more visual, interactive search experience embedded in chat.
