# Nexus Mods Requires AI Disclosure for All New Mod Uploads

Nexus Mods, the largest PC‑gaming mod repository, announced today that every newly uploaded mod must include a clear statement about whether artificial‑intelligence tools were used in its creation.

## 🔍 Overview
- Authors will have to declare **whether AI has been used** in their work.
- Existing tags for **AI‑generated** and **AI‑assisted** mods will become **more prominent** on mod pages.
- The change follows community concerns that AI use was “not clearly disclosed.”

## 🏷️ Disclosure Requirements
| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Declaration | Authors must indicate if AI was used in the mod. |
| Tag visibility | AI‑generated and AI‑assisted tags will appear more prominently. |
| Explanation for AI‑assisted mods | A public explanation of **how and where AI was used** must be included. |

## ⚖️ Policy Stance
- Nexus Mods says it is “pro‑human creativity” but **will not ban** AI‑generated mods.
- Reason: AI is already embedded in many creation steps (script autocompletion, texture, voice, and full‑asset generation), making a clear cut‑off difficult.
- An outright ban would only hide AI usage rather than eliminate it; the platform prefers openness and regulation.
- The site pledges **not to use mods to train AI** and will **not sell or license** them for that purpose.

## 🛠️ Platform Changes
- **Attribution controls** are being strengthened for mods that incorporate another author’s work with permission.
- The **Recently Added** section will limit how many mods from a single author are displayed at once and tighten restrictions on uploads from new accounts.
- New controls will allow users to **hide AI‑generated content** on game pages, the homepage, and eventually in search results.

## 📅 Timeline
- Mod authors have a **60‑day grace period** to update existing mods with the required disclosures.
- The grace period ends on **January 2**, after which the new rules will be enforced.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- Provides transparency for players who want to know how a mod was created.
- Helps maintain trust in the modding ecosystem by preventing undisclosed AI use.
- Sets a precedent for other user‑generated‑content platforms grappling with AI attribution.
