# Data Lineage Becomes Core Security Perimeter for Agentic AI

A recent workflow failure showed that when an AI agent pulls data from multiple internal sources and hands it off to another agent without human oversight, sensitive and confidential information can leak undetected. The incident highlights why AI governance must evolve to focus on data visibility rather than just tools.

## 🔍 Overview
- A sales representative asked an AI agent for customer pricing.
- The agent aggregated data from restricted pricing systems, internal communications, and archived quotes.
- A second AI agent used that aggregated data to draft a proposal.
- No human checks were applied at either handoff.
- The final proposal contained unapproved, confidential, and sensitive information, and the source of the problematic data could not be traced.
- This scenario illustrates why AI governance needs to evolve, rapidly and dynamically.

## 🛡️ Data Lineage as the New Security Perimeter
- "Data lineage is the new security perimeter, providing the visibility needed for governance, accountability, and trust, without impeding AI adoption at scale."
- It offers the ability to trace how data moves, is copied, summarized, transformed, reused, and redistributed.
- Establishing data lineage enables teams to manage governance, accountability, and trust across human, agentic, and hybrid workflows.

## ⚙️ How Agentic AI Exposes Data Visibility Gaps
- Enterprises using agentic AI are dealing with moving and fragmented data, not just static files.
- Traditional security approaches built solely around applications, systems, networks, or endpoints are no longer sufficient.
- Securing agentic AI at scale requires understanding how data moves.

## 📈 Implications for Governance
- AI security is fundamentally a data visibility problem, not a tool problem.
- Without clear data lineage, organizations cannot trace where sensitive data entered a workflow or how it spread.
- Rapid, dynamic governance mechanisms are needed to keep pace with continuous data transformation in AI‑driven processes.

## 🚀 Path Forward
- Teams should prioritize establishing comprehensive data lineage.
- Governance frameworks must account for the ingestion, copying, summarizing, transformation, reuse, and redistribution of data across agents.
- By focusing on data visibility, organizations can maintain trust and accountability while scaling AI adoption.
