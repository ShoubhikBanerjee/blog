# Apple Releases watchOS 27.3 Beta Featuring Siri Audio Intelligence

Apple has introduced the watchOS 27.3 developer beta, which enables Siri to utilize the Apple Watch’s microphone for new Audio Intelligence features. This update includes Siri Recap and Live Rewind, which utilize "ambient listening" to summarize or transcribe audio detected by the device.

## 🧩 How it works
The new Audio Intelligence tools allow Siri to process conversation audio for immediate recall or later review:

| Feature | Functionality |
| :--- | :--- |
| Siri Recap | Uses ambient listening to generate high-level summaries, titles, and key points of conversations in the Siri app. |
| Live Rewind | Generates a text snippet of the last 15 seconds of detected audio for users to catch missed information. |

Users can activate Live Rewind by double-pressing the Digital Crown to display a text snippet of a conversation. Users can then ask Siri about the content of that text or save it to the Siri app. When Siri Recap is enabled, Apple Intelligence automatically generates a title and key points after a conversation concludes.

## ⚙️ Key details
Apple released an Audio Intelligence Privacy Overview detailing how user data is protected during around-the-clock listening. According to the documentation:
* Raw audio is handled within dedicated hardware and is not saved as a file.
* Audio data is not accessible to the operating system, apps, or Apple.
* Saved summaries and snippets are end-to-end encrypted when synced across devices for users with iCloud two-factor authentication and a device passcode.

Additionally, a redesigned Health app is available for testing in the iOS 27.3 developer beta. It includes an Insights tab for heart, sleep, and fitness summaries, a For You section with personalized recommendations, and educational health content.

## 🚀 Availability
The watchOS 27.3 and iOS 27.3 developer betas are rolling out now. The update includes expanded language support for global English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), French, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish, and Vietnamese.
