# Mike Walker Leads AI‑Powered Cyber Defense and Autonomous Hacking Tournament

Mike Walker, a research manager at Microsoft Research, is advancing the use of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity. In a Microsoft Research Podcast he discusses his work on autonomous AI systems that discover and patch software vulnerabilities, the first all‑machine hacking tournament, and his perspective on the social role of security rules.

## 🔍 Overview
- Walker’s career combines AI, cybersecurity, and large distributed systems.
- He helped create the first all‑machine hacking tournament, showing how autonomous AI can compete in vulnerability discovery.
- His work aims to improve how computers are trained to defend against cyberattacks.

## 🧩 Background
- **DARPA Cyber Grand Challenge (CGC):** Walker led the program that proved autonomous AI could find and patch software flaws. CGC marked its 10th anniversary in 2026.
- **Microsoft Research:** As a research manager, he leads projects at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity, and large distributed systems.
- **Prior experience:** Participation in hacker culture guided his move into next‑generation cyber defense.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Technical focus areas:**
  - Cloud‑native memory analysis
  - Automated malware analysis
  - Next‑generation cyber defense technologies
- **Philosophy on security rules:**
  > "Well, you have to understand the rules if you want to be able to do what you want."
  > "Rules are a social construct… when a security system asks something of users, you’re asking for their trust. Trust is a precious commodity. When you squander user trust, the security regime falls down."
- **Core question raised:** Are today’s networked systems any safer than they were a decade ago?

## 💭 Perspectives
- Walker emphasizes the need to view security rules within their social context rather than applying instant adjudication.
- He believes understanding the social dynamics of trust is essential for robust cyber defense.

*This story was shared in a Microsoft Research Podcast hosted by Principal Technical Program Manager Weishung Liu.*
