# Netflix adds AI Discover tab to TV app for themed content suggestions

Netflix’s TV app is getting a new “AI Discover” tab where the company will show experimental, AI‑powered features to help you find new shows and movies to watch.

## 🔍 Overview
- Netflix introduces an **AI Discover** tab in its TV app to surface experimental, AI‑powered recommendations.

## 🧩 How it works
- The feature is **powered by a new intelligence layer** that “responds to what members tell us in real time and keeps learning.”
- When you pick a theme, Netflix shows recommendations that match that theme.
- If you don’t like a recommendation, you can tell Netflix, and the app will remember your feedback.

## ⚙️ Features
- **Themed recommendations** such as “Something Light,” “Family Movie Night,” and “Quick Watches.”
- Real‑time learning from user feedback.
- Dedicated **Discover** tab in the top menu of the TV app.

## 🚀 Availability
- The Discover tab appears along the top menu of the TV app.
- Rolling out gradually in the United States.
- Available to members who use Netflix in English on supported TVs.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a new way for members to discover content based on themes and personal feedback.
- The announcement follows Netflix’s recent layoff of around **1,000 employees** as growth has slowed and viewership has declined.
