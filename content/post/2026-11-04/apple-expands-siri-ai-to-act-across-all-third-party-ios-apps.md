# Apple expands Siri AI to act across all third‑party iOS apps

Apple announced that Siri AI can now perform actions across third‑party apps as part of the iOS 27.3 update.

## 🔍 Overview
- Apple will now let Siri AI perform actions across third‑party apps.
- The change is part of the iOS 27.3 update rolling out on Tuesday.
- Developers can integrate Siri AI’s new features in “all apps,” allowing the voice assistant to draw on a user’s personal context, process natural language prompts, and use on‑screen awareness to complete requests.

## 🧩 How it works
- Siri AI can use personal context, natural language prompts, and on‑screen awareness.
- Users won’t have to mention an app’s name in their request while it’s in the foreground, during a Live Activity, or in a conversation with Siri AI about the app.

## ⚙️ Key details
- A new “app skills” feature lets third‑party developers combine several tasks into a single action.
- “For example, a person might request a ride with a ride share app, follow updates to the app’s Live Activity while they wait for their ride to arrive, then request to move the pickup location to a different address by saying ‘Hey Siri, change my ride pickup to a new address,’” Apple says.
- Apple previously made a smaller set of features available to third‑party developers (as noted by 9to5Mac).

## 🚀 Availability
- The update is rolling out as part of a Developer Preview and will be available with iOS 27.3.

## 📱 Expanded built‑in app support
Apple is bringing Siri AI’s expanded capabilities to its own apps as well, including:

## 💡 Why it matters
- Users can ask Siri AI to find a coffee shop they visited last week.
- Users can ask Siri AI to archive files that haven’t been opened in over a year.
- Users can ask Siri AI what it can do in each app.
