# OpenAI CEO Sam Altman pushes for more RAM amid AI‑driven shortages

OpenAI chief Sam Altman has taken to X to argue that everyone should be able to install as much memory as they want in their computers, even as AI data centers are depleting the global DRAM supply.

## 🔍 Overview
Altman’s comments came after an interview on the Cheeky Pint podcast where he described the situation as “quite terrible to have everybody have a bunch of the world’s limited supply of DRAM sitting in their own computer and being used five percent of the time.”

## 🗣️ Altman’s public statements
- Changed his X handle to **“Ram Altman”** in response to a user comment.
- Stated, “i actually do want the world to have tons of memory and people to put as much as they want in their computers… i have been arguing for the world to ramp up memory production for a long time.”
- Repeated on X, “i want you to have so much ram” and “let’s build more.”
- Emphasised that people should be free to “put as much as they want in their computers.”

## 📉 Drivers of the RAM shortage
- AI data centers are aggressively buying the world’s RAM, creating price hikes and limiting availability for ordinary consumers.
- The shortage is reinforced by the fact that much of the installed DRAM is under‑utilized, according to Altman’s podcast remarks.

## 📅 Outlook
- **Intel** expects the RAM shortage to persist **through at least next year**.
- **SK Hynix** predicts the shortage could **drag on until 2030**.

| Company | Forecast |
|---|---|
| Intel | Shortage expected through at least next year |
| SK Hynix | Shortage could drag on until 2030 |

Altman’s stance highlights a tension between the growing memory needs of AI workloads and the limited global DRAM supply.
