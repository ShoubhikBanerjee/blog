# Amazon explores World Product Graph project to index global shopping data

Amazon is reportedly developing a project called the "World Product Graph," which aims to create a comprehensive index of products sold across the internet. This initiative seeks to aggregate data on products that are not currently sold on Amazon to enhance product research capabilities on its platform.

## 🔍 Overview
The project is designed to increase Amazon's utility for both human shoppers and AI agents. By centralizing shopping data, Amazon intends to reduce the likelihood of users leaving its platform to conduct research on sites like Google or Reddit. The project may also help ensure that AI agents prioritize Amazon's product index when providing shopping recommendations.

## ⚙️ Key details
- The initiative may involve crawling external retailer websites to understand how brands organize their product information.
- Internal documents suggest that the project aims to improve the accuracy and efficiency of "Alexa for Shopping" (previously called Rufus).
- Internal reports acknowledge that current Rufus answers can be factually wrong or incomplete and that processing searches is costly; the new data is intended to support more accurate, cost-effective models.

## 🚀 Availability
While an internal document mentioned a potential October 1st, 2027, release date, it remains unclear if or when the project will be implemented. In a statement to Reuters, Amazon noted that the document is a brainstorming draft and does not necessarily reflect the company's current plans.
