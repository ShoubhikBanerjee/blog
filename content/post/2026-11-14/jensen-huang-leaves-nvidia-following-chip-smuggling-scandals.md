# Jensen Huang Leaves Nvidia Following Chip Smuggling Scandals and Personal Allegations

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has left the company he cofounded 33 years ago, ending his tenure as the visionary leader of the world’s first $5 trillion company. The departure comes amid reports of the executive enabling two chip smuggling scandals and allegations of three extramarital affairs. The news has raised concerns regarding the stability of the US AI industry and the S&P 500 index, of which Nvidia represents approximately 6 percent.

## ⚙️ Key details

* Huang's exit follows allegations of enabling two chip smuggling scandals and three extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy.
* The former CEO was known for a distinct personal style, including a signature black leather jacket and an intense leadership model where approximately 60 employees reported directly to him.
* Huang had no designated deputies and had not established a succession plan for the company.
* Analysts have described Huang as both the visionary and tactical leader of Nvidia, drawing comparisons to the role Steve Jobs held at Apple.

## 🔍 Overview

* Nvidia was cofounded by Huang 33 years ago at a Denny’s diner.
* In 1996, the company nearly failed due to a graphics chip for the Sega Dreamcast, but Huang later convinced Sega to invest $5 million.
* Huang focused the company on visual computing, gaming graphics, image recognition, and AI simulations.
* Notable public demonstrations included a 2008 project using an 1,100-nozzle paintball gun to demonstrate parallel processing by painting the Mona Lisa in one shot.

## 💡 Why it matters

* Nvidia GPUs are the essential hardware powering the worldwide AI boom.
* Huang's centralized leadership style means that his absence creates a vacuum in a company built entirely around his vision and tactics.
* While major tech companies like Microsoft, Apple, and Google have successfully transitioned leadership, Nvidia faces a unique challenge due to the lack of internal deputies.
* Industry analysts note the loss of a primary AI industry spokesperson and culture setter.
