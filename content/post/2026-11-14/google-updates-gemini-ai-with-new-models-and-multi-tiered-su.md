# Google Updates Gemini AI with New Models and Multi-Tiered Subscription Plans

Google has updated its AI ecosystem with the introduction of the Gemini 4 model family and a restructured subscription framework. These developments include the launch of the Gemini 4 Argon, Hydrogen, and Carbon models, alongside a new functional design language called Neural Expressive.

## ⚙️ Subscription Tiers and Model Access
The update introduces three distinct Google AI plans with varying levels of access and usage limits. Users can manage their consumption through limits that refresh every 5 hours until a weekly cap is reached, or by purchasing additional AI credits.

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Usage Limits | Model Access |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Google AI Plus | Not specified | 2x higher than Free | Gemini 4 Argon, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Pro model, Flash-Lite |
| Google AI Pro | Not specified | 4x higher than Free | Access to Pro model features |
| Google AI Ultra | $199.99 | 20x higher than AI Pro | Highest access to Gemini 4 Carbon, Argon, and Hydrogen |

## 🧩 Functional Features
New capabilities focus on both complex task management and creative content generation:
*   **Gemini 4 Argon:** Built to manage and complete complex tasks from start to finish.
*   **Gemini Live:** Now integrated into chat sessions, allowing users to switch between talking and typing.
*   **Gemini Omni:** Functions as a video-focused version of Nano Banana.
*   **Neural Expressive:** A new functional design language created for the AI era.
*   **Creative Reimaging:** Users can show Gemini their surroundings and use the phrase “reimagine this” to create new visuals with Nano Banana.
*   **Personalized Digest:** The platform now offers a Daily Brief to help users start their day.

## 🚀 Agentic Development and Code
The update includes tools for building and governing autonomous AI entities through Google Antigravity, an agentic development platform. Users can utilize these autonomous agents to write, test, and deploy code. The Google AI Ultra plan provides the highest rate limits to agent models within the Antigravity platform and includes first access to features like Tasks in Gemini.

## 🌎 Availability and Restrictions
Google has expanded the geographic reach of its AI services, though access is limited to users aged 18 and older for Google AI plans and Gemini for Gmail and Docs.
*   **Google AI Plus:** Available in more than 160 countries and territories.
*   **Google AI Pro and Ultra:** Available in more than 150 countries.
*   **Project Genie:** Available in more than 140 countries.
*   **YouTube Premium:** The individual plan is available in over 40 countries.
*   **Regional Specifics:** Access to the Gemini 4 Carbon model and Nano Banana Pro in AI Mode is restricted to select countries.
