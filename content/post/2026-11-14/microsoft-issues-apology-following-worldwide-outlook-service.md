Microsoft has issued an apology following a worldwide outage affecting Outlook services. The company has officially identified the cause of the disruption as a technical issue.

## 💡 Why it matters

* The service interruption impacted Microsoft Outlook users on a global scale.
* Microsoft has acknowledged the incident and provided an official apology to its user base.
* The disruption was attributed by the company to a specific technical issue.
