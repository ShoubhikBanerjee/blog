# Meta Changes AI Chatbot Prompt Suggestions After Digging for Personal Information

Meta is making changes to the suggested prompts of its AI chatbot following a viral video that showed the assistant seeking personal information about a user's young daughters. 

## 🔍 Overview

The changes come after Instagram user Kalie Robins posted a video detailing an invasive prompt presented by Meta AI. After cross-posting a video of her and her child singing in a car to Facebook, the prompt "Who is the child passenger?" appeared beneath the clip. 

When Robins selected the prompt, Meta AI gathered information about her daughters from her previous posts, as well as posts shared by her relatives. The assistant then:
* Suggested additional prompts asking about her children's ages.
* Suggested prompts asking where Robins lives with her family.
* Showed photos of her daughters posted to the platform, including one Robins claims she deleted years ago.

## ⚙️ Key details

In a statement, Meta spokesperson Dina El-Kassaby stated that the company "missed the mark," adding that "the feature never should have prompted the individual with questions like that." 

El-Kassaby provided several clarifications regarding the incident and the feature's mechanics:
* **Fix implemented:** Meta has resolved the issue causing Meta AI to suggest prompts related to personal topics.
* **Feature intent:** The feature was designed to help users obtain more information about topics or posts that interest them.
* **Information access:** When a user clicks a prompt, the generated response is based entirely on information the user already has access to. In this specific case, El-Kassaby noted that Meta AI may have pulled from videos where Robins named her children.

## 🌐 Context and Integration

Meta has integrated its AI assistant across its primary platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger, where it can answer questions, write posts, and generate images. This is not the first time Meta has adjusted its AI features due to user response; in July, the company pulled a feature that allowed users to create AI deepfakes of other Instagram users after facing fierce backlash.
