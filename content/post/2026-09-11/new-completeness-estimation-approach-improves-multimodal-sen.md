# New Completeness Estimation Approach Improves Multimodal Sentiment Analysis

Researchers have introduced a new approach to improve multimodal sentiment analysis by addressing performance degradation caused by missing or noisy data in real-world scenarios.

## 🧩 How it works

The development introduces two primary components to handle incomplete data:

* **Completeness Estimation**: An approach that quantifies the degree of sentiment-relevant information preserved in incomplete data to guide the reconstruction of missing semantics.
* **Training Strategy**: A method designed to stabilize multi-task learning while jointly optimizing completeness estimation and sentiment prediction.

## 💡 Why it matters

Recent multimodal sentiment analysis studies often use text-centric fusion to exploit sentiment information in textual modalities. However, these approaches can suffer from performance degradation during inference when sentiment-related cues are missing or data is noisy.

## ⚙️ Key details

Experiments conducted on three benchmark datasets demonstrate that this approach results in:

* More accurate semantic reconstruction.
* More precise sentiment prediction.
