# Qwen3.8 Max Leads September 2026 Open‑Weight LLM Ranking

The latest open‑weight benchmark shows Qwen3.8 Max as the top‑scoring open‑source large language model.

## 🔎 Overview
- Qwen3.8 Max is the best open‑source LLM by current open‑weight benchmark score.
- It leads the September 2026 open‑weight ranking with a BenchAlign v5 score of 71.6.
- The ranking includes 104 models and uses the public BenchAlign v5 overall lane filtered to downloadable weights.

## 📈 Ranking Details
| Model | Score | Evidence |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Qwen3.8 Max (Alibaba) | 71.6 | Supported |
| GLM-5.3 | 68.4 | Estimated |
| GLM-5.2 | 68.1 | Supported |
- The open‑weight slice starts with Qwen3.8 Max, followed by GLM‑5.3 and GLM‑5.2.
- Scores compare measured capability and do not decide licensing permissiveness, hardware fit, or cost of self‑hosting versus API use.
- Supported and Estimated describe the evidence behind each position; they are not separate leaderboards.

## ⚙️ Technical Context
- Open‑weight models are ranked by the same public BenchAlign v5 overall score as proprietary models.
- The live table recomputes from the public BenchAlign v5 artifact.
- MiniMax M3 led a July 14 snapshot, showing that rankings can change over time.

## 📚 Licensing & Deployment
- Every ranked model publishes downloadable weights, but not every license is OSI‑approved.
- The license directory separates OSI‑approved licenses from community terms.
- A smaller deployment directory lists models with a self‑host catalog record, keeping license and hardware claims auditable.
- A missing row in that directory means the deployment catalog is incomplete, not that the model cannot be self‑hosted.

## 🌐 Why it matters
- The current top tier is dominated by Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Zhipu/GLM, Moonshot/Kimi, Alibaba/Qwen, MiniMax) with Meta and Mistral behind on the live ranking.
- Open‑weight ranking provides a transparent view of model capability independent of proprietary offerings.
