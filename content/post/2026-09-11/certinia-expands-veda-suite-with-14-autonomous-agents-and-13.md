# Certinia Expands Veda Suite With 14 Autonomous Agents and 135 Tools

Certinia has expanded its Veda suite by adding 14 autonomous agents and expanding its Intelligent Actions library to 135 tools.

## 🧩 How it works

The platform connects and coordinates key processes across the enterprise lifecycle by performing the following actions:
* **Workflow Coordination**: It coordinates professional services, customer success, and accounting processes.
* **General Ledger Actions**: It executes general ledger actions through Model Context Protocol connections.

## ⚙️ Key details

The expansion introduces several key updates to the platform's automation capabilities:
* **Autonomous Agents**: The suite now includes 14 new autonomous agents.
* **Intelligent Actions**: The system's tool library has been expanded to a total of 135 tools.
* **Operational Reach**: The tools span professional services, customer success, and accounting workflows.
