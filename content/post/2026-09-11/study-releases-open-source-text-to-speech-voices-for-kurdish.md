# Study Releases Open-Source Text-to-Speech Voices for Kurdish

A recent study has released three Kurdish voices, 35 hours of recorded speech, and a descriptive paper, all available for free download to address the lack of technology capable of reading Kurdish aloud.

## 🚀 Availability
* Three Kurdish voices
* 35 hours of recorded speech
* A paper describing the work

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Data Source:** The voices were built from three people reading prepared texts.
* **Performance:** While the voices sound fluent, they represent the reading styles of the speakers rather than Kurdish as a whole.
* **Technical Issues:** 
    * A coding fault mishandles long numbers.
    * Test recordings are left unlabeled among training data.
    * A settings file lists equipment that was never used.

## 💡 Why it matters
* Kurdish is spoken by millions, but has limited technology for reading text aloud.
* The use of prepared texts removes much everyday and regional speech, and does not account for major regional and written variations in Kurdish.
* Compared to English and German, Kurdish has far less linguistic description and dictionary support to help identify wrong pronunciations, meaning software choices can go unchecked.
* There is a discrepancy between reports, as the download page claims a stronger result than the paper reports and recommends one voice for general use.
