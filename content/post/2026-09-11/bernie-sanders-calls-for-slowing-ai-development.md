# Bernie Sanders Calls for Slowing AI Development

Bernie Sanders has expressed concerns regarding the pace of artificial intelligence development, stating that the existence of all humanity is at stake.

## 💡 Why it matters
* Bernie Sanders told Newsnight that AI means "all of humanity's existence is at stake."
* Sanders stated, "You gotta be a moron not to say slow it down."
