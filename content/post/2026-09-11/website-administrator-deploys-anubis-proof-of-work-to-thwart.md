# Website Administrator Deploys Anubis Proof‑of‑Work to Thwart AI Scrapers

The administrator of a website has set up Anubis to protect the server against the scourge of AI companies aggressively scraping websites.

## 🔍 Overview
- Anubis is a protective measure installed by the site admin.
- It targets AI companies that scrape websites aggressively.

## 🧩 How it works
- Employs a Proof‑of‑Work scheme inspired by Hashcash.
- The work imposes negligible cost on individual users but becomes costly for large‑scale scrapers.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Designed as a “good enough” placeholder to buy time for fingerprinting and headless‑browser detection (e.g., via font rendering).
- Requires modern JavaScript features that are disabled by privacy plugins such as JShelter.
- Classified as a hack rather than a full‑featured solution.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Increases the economic cost of mass scraping, deterring abusive AI scrapers while keeping impact on normal visitors low.
- Allows site owners to focus resources on more sophisticated bot‑detection techniques.
