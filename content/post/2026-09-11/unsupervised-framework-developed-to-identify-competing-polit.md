# Unsupervised Framework Developed to Identify Competing Political Narratives on Social Media

A new unsupervised framework has been presented for identifying and characterizing competing narratives in political discourse on social media, specifically focusing on tweets from German politicians.

## 🧩 How it works
The system utilizes a multi-stage pipeline that integrates the following natural language processing techniques:

* Topic modeling
* Event detection
* Event linking

## 💡 Why it matters
By organizing data into coherent stories and uncovering the perspectives of user communities, the framework can:

* Detect key competing narratives
* Highlight divergent framings and conflicts regarding trending political topics

## ⚙️ Key details
The efficacy of the methodology was demonstrated through two case studies on polarizing political issues, which showcased the system's ability to analyze and uncover divergent viewpoints.
