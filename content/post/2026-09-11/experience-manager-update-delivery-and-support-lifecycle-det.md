# Experience Manager Update Delivery and Support Lifecycle Details

Adobe provides different update mechanisms and support timelines for Experience Manager depending on the deployment model.

## ⚙️ Key details

| Deployment Model | Update Delivery Method |
| :--- | :--- |
| Experience Manager as a Cloud Service | Monthly feature activations, with maintenance releases (enhancements and bug fixes) twice a month |
| Experience Manager on-premise and Managed Services | Service Packs and Cumulative Fix Packs (CFPs) |
| Experience Manager 6.5 | Service Packs |
| Experience Manager 6.5 LTS | Service Packs |

## 🚀 Availability

Support timelines for specific customers are as follows:
* Adobe Managed Services customers: ends by August 31, 2026.
* On Prem customers: core support currently planned to end by February 2027.

Additional dates listed include:
* Monday, November 23 2026 until Tuesday, December 1 2026.
* Monday, December 14 2026 until Sunday, January 3 2027.
