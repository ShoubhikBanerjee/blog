# Amazon Reports Strong Financials and Highlights AI Services

Amazon.com, Inc. released its latest financial metrics and reiterated the breadth of its business, which spans retail, devices, media, and a robust cloud platform that includes artificial‑intelligence and machine‑learning services.

## 🔍 Overview
- Amazon operates through three segments: North America, International, and Amazon Web Services (AWS).
- The company sells consumer products, advertising, and subscription services both online and in physical stores.
- It manufactures electronic devices such as Kindle, Fire tablets, Fire TV, Echo, Ring, Blink, and eero, and it creates media content.
- Programs enable third‑party sellers, authors, musicians, filmmakers, Twitch streamers, skill and app developers, and other creators to sell through Amazon’s platforms.
- Advertising services include sponsored ads, display, and video advertising.
- Amazon Prime is the company’s membership program.

- **North America** – Retail and services focused on U.S. and Canadian customers.
- **International** – Retail operations outside North America.
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** – Cloud computing, storage, AI/ML, database, analytics, and related services.
