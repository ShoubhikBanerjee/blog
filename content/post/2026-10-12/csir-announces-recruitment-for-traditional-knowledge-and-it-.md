# CSIR Announces Recruitment for Traditional Knowledge and IT Project (TKDL)

On 6 Oct 2026, a result‑of‑interview session was held referencing Advertisement TKDL/06/2026, which focuses on Sanskrit and Metallurgy. The interview outcome and several related recruitment notices were released in mid‑October 2026.

## 🔍 Overview
- Advertisement TKDL/06/2026 covers positions related to **Sanskrit and Metallurgy**.
- A walk‑in interview for project personnel was scheduled on **14 Oct 2026** (Reference R&C/591/2026).
- Document verification for the post of Assistant Section Officer (ASO) was announced on **16 Oct 2026** under Advt. E‑I/RC/2026/2.
- An additional advertisement sought a Director for CSIR‑National Chemical Laboratory, Pune, with a deadline of **30 Nov 2026**.
- A broader engagement call for IT, Ayurveda, Unani, Sowa Rigpa, Siddha, Patent, Sanskrit and Yoga under **Project No. MLP002639** closed on **12 Oct 2026** (Reference TKDL/08/2026).

## 📅 Key Dates
- **06 Oct 2026** – Interview result released (TKDL/06/2026).
- **14 Oct 2026** – Walk‑in interview for project personnel (R&C/591/2026).
- **16 Oct 2026** – Document verification notice for ASO (E‑I/RC/2026/2).
- **12 Oct 2026** – Last date to apply for Project MLP002639 personnel.
- **30 Nov 2026** – Application deadline for Director, CSIR‑NCL.

## 🗂 How to Apply
- For the IT‑focused Project MLP002639, applicants were instructed to “click here” in the advertisement dated **12 Oct 2026**.
- Relevant PDFs listed above contain detailed application procedures and eligibility criteria.

## ⚙️ What the Recruitment Covers
- **Traditional Knowledge Domains:** Sanskrit, Ayurveda, Unani, Sowa Rigpa, Siddha, Yoga.
- **Technical Domains:** IT, Metallurgy, Patent.
- **Administrative Roles:** Assistant Section Officer, Director (CSIR‑NCL).

## 📌 Why It Matters
These coordinated recruitment drives aim to staff a multi‑disciplinary project that brings together traditional knowledge systems and modern technical expertise, ensuring the continued development and documentation of India's heritage alongside contemporary scientific endeavors.
