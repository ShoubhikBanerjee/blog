# Silicon Valley Leaders Adopt Trump's "Super Intelligence" Rebrand

President Donald Trump announced his plans to rebrand artificial intelligence as “super intelligence,” prompting a swift response from several tech executives.

## 🔍 Overview
- Trump declared that the White House considers anyone using the term “Artificial Intelligence” instead of the new term “Super Intelligence” *THE ENEMY*.
- The broader population is still using AI, but a number of Silicon Valley’s most powerful figures are falling in line with Trump’s rebrand.

## 🤝 Silicon Valley Alignment
- *Musk, Benioff, Huang, and now Nadella have all capitulated to Trump’s ‘super intelligence’ rebrand.*
- During Trump’s second term, Silicon Valley has repeatedly tried to ingratiate itself with the administration, after having a much more contentious relationship during his first term.

## 🏢 Corporate Renames & Commitments
| Leader | Company | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Elon Musk | SpaceX | Promised to rename SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI |
| Marc Benioff | Salesforce | Announced rename of AIForce to SIForce |
| Jensen Huang | Nvidia | Attended summit where executive order was announced; committed $1 billion to scientific advancement that includes “super intelligence research” |
| Satya Nadella | Microsoft | Attended same AI summit as Huang; in a post calling for an “emergency brake” for AI, repeatedly referred to the technology as “super intelligence” |

## 📜 Executive Order & Government Directive
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang attended the summit where the executive order requiring government agencies to use the term “super intelligence” was announced.
- Huang also stated he believes the danger posed by AI is overblown.

## 💭 Why It Matters
- The coordinated rename effort shows a notable shift toward aligning corporate language with the administration’s terminology.
- Large financial commitments (e.g., Nvidia’s $1 billion) are being framed as support for “super intelligence research.”
- High‑profile leaders publicly endorsing the term may influence public and governmental perception of AI.
