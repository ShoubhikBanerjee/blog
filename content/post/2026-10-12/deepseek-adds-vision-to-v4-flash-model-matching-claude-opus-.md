# DeepSeek adds vision to V4‑Flash model, matching Claude Opus 4.8 on multimodal tasks

DeepSeek has released an experimental vision extension for its V4‑Flash large language model, giving the model image‑understanding capabilities while keeping the same pricing.

## 🔍 Overview
- The new vision model is built on top of the existing V4‑Flash architecture.
- It is positioned as a direct competitor to Anthropic’s Claude Opus 4.8 on tasks that require both language and visual reasoning.

## 🧩 How it works
- Image understanding is **bolted onto** V4‑Flash, meaning the core model remains unchanged while an additional perception layer processes visual inputs.
- The integration does **not increase the per‑request cost**, keeping the price identical to the text‑only V4‑Flash offering.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Model**: V4‑Flash with experimental vision extension.
- **Capability**: Handles multimodal agent tasks that involve both text and images.
- **Pricing**: Same as the original V4‑Flash model.
- **Performance comparison**: Closing the gap with Claude Opus 4.8 on multimodal benchmarks.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Providing vision at no extra cost lowers the barrier for developers building agents that need to interpret images alongside language.
- Matching the performance of a leading multimodal model (Claude Opus 4.8) signals rapid convergence in the capabilities of open‑source and commercial LLM providers.
