# India Meteorological Department Issues Extended Weather Forecast for Oct 8‑21, 2026

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) released a new press statement announcing the current weather status and an extended range forecast covering the period 8 October to 21 October 2026.

## 📅 Overview
- **Press release date:** 12 October 2026
- **Document dated:** 08 October 2026
- **Forecast period:** 08 Oct 2026 – 21 Oct 2026
- **Rainfall observation:** Recorded from 08:30 hrs on 11 Oct 2026 to 08:30 hrs on 12 Oct 2026

## 📊 Key Details
- The release provides a two‑week outlook for temperature, precipitation, and other meteorological parameters for the Indian sub‑continent.
- It is issued by the Office of the Director General of Meteorology, IMD, located at Mausam Bhawan, Lodhi Road, New Delhi 110003.
