# CJP Leaders Released After Detention; CJI Declines Rahul Gandhi Meeting on Vote Chori Claims

The Cockroach Janata Party (CJP) saw three of its leaders released on 12 October 2026 after being produced before an Executive Magistrate. On the same day, Chief Justice of India Surya Kant publicly declined to meet Rahul Gandhi regarding the “vote chori” allegations, citing sub‑judice matters and the limits of judicial intervention in administrative affairs.

## 🔍 Overview
- Over 200 protesters were detained as they marched towards Jantar Mantar.
- Leaders Abhijeet Dipke, Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Das were freed after a magistrate’s hearing.
- The Supreme Court was said to be “open for all citizens, 24x7 where fundamental rights are violated.”

## ⚖️ Judicial Response
> "CJI Surya Kant declines to meet Rahul Gandhi on 'vote chori' allegations, says similar matters are sub‑judice on judicial side in Supreme Court."
> 
> "CJI asks Rahul Gandhi to file petition on judicial side and that may be taken up on Tuesday, say sources."
> 
> "In response to Rahul Gandhi's request for meeting, CJI says no orders can be passed on administrative side: Sources."
> 
> "CJI Surya Kant has said Supreme Court cannot interfere with functions of executive on administrative side: Sources."

## 🚔 Law Enforcement Actions
- Detentions continued: "Even today, several colleagues from CJP have been detained and subjected to physical assault."
- The entire SFI team was detained.
- Delhi Traffic Police issued an advisory for possible diversions on 12 October 2026.

## 🗣️ Statements from CJP
- Ratna Singh, head of legal affairs, confirmed the release on X and wrote the condition: "Condition - no breach of peace for one year."
- Advocate Soutik Banerjee said, "All three (Abhijeet Dipke, Ashutosh Ranka, and Saurav Das) have been released. They can go from here."
- Abhijeet Dipke alleged that Delhi Police denied phone and lawyer access and added, "No bond signed to stop future protests; will hit streets again."
- After release, Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka asked, "Why are you protecting Gyanesh Kumar?"
- Ashutosh Ranka warned, "Delhi Police can give us date for Jantar Mantar protest in 24 hours, else CJP will give another protest call."
- The party’s spokesperson praised four former chief ministers for joining street protests.

## 📢 Upcoming Actions
> "Tomorrow, 12th October, India marches peacefully as one to the Supreme Court. Students, civil society organisations, Opposition parties: Please join us to defend our democracy - Rahul Gandhi"

> "RaGa vows march to SC over vote chori, SIR and Gyanesh Kumar's resignation"

## 🛣️ Traffic Impact
- Delhi Traffic Police advised that traffic may be impacted on several stretches starting 06:00 AM on 12 October 2026, with diversions enforced as the law‑and‑order situation evolves.

*The situation remains fluid, with further protests and legal actions expected.*
