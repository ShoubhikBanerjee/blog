# Stanford Live Celebrates 250 Years of American Voices

Stanford Live launches a season that honors the United States’ 250th anniversary by featuring artists whose stories reflect the nation’s layered, diverse narrative.

## 🎭 Artistic Focus
- Many of the featured composers and performers arrived in the United States from elsewhere, became citizens, and chose to build their new creative home here.
- In illuminating these artists, we endeavor to honor the layered, diverse, and continually unfolding American story.
