# Structured and Hierarchical Insight with Privacy (SHIP) Improves Private Data Analysis

Extracting insights from textual data while protecting user privacy is increasingly important, and a new method called Structured and Hierarchical Insight with Privacy (SHIP) advances this goal.

## 🔍 Overview
- Existing methods produce private insights in diverse forms for different analytical purposes, making their utility difficult to assess and compare.
- This work formulates differentially private (DP) insights as reusable representations for answering analytical queries, providing a common basis for utility assessment.

## 🧩 How it works
- Answering analytical queries requires fine‑grained details and cross‑attribute statistics that existing representations may omit.
- SHIP automatically maps queries to attributes, builds a hierarchical hybrid taxonomy, and releases a DP synthetic table for further analysis.

**Key capabilities**
- Automatic query‑to‑attribute mapping
- Hierarchical hybrid taxonomy construction
- Generation of a differentially private synthetic table

## ⚙️ Evaluation
- An evaluation protocol is developed, focusing on question‑answering performance and structured fidelity.
- Experiments show SHIP’s strong performance on both measures relative to existing private insight baselines.
- The study also identifies queries that remain challenging for structured representations.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Protecting privacy while extracting insights is a growing priority.
- By providing reusable, comparable DP representations, SHIP helps assess utility across methods and improves the quality of privacy‑preserving analysis.
