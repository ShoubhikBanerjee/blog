# Bluesky expands Attie AI tool to create lightweight social apps

Bluesky’s Attie AI tool, which launched earlier this year as a way to build your own feeds with AI, now lets users create small social apps – from games to polls – and publish them as individual websites.

## 🔍 Overview
- Attie was introduced as a feed‑building AI assistant and is being broadened to support “small” social apps such as games, polls, and dashboards that can be shared with others as their own websites.
- The tool is positioned by Bluesky as a way to do “social vibe coding or vibe coding for your community.”

## 🧩 How it works
- Users create an app simply by typing a natural‑language prompt.
- The AI generates the functional app in a matter of minutes. For example, "A simple poll I made about Pokémon Red and Pokémon Blue took a few minutes to generate."

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Supported app types**: games, polls, dashboards, quizzes, idle clickers, and more.
- **Examples highlighted on the Attie site**:
  
  | Example | Description |
  |---|---|
  | Flappy Butterfly | Flappy Bird‑style clone |
  | Drum machine | Simple online drum sequencer |
  | Quiz | Interactive quiz app |
  | Celery Man idle clicker | Idle clicker about a celery man |
  
- Each example lives on a custom `attie.site` link with attribution for the creator displayed at the bottom of the page.
- The apps are described as “pretty lightweight novelties, though I liked the drum machine.”

## 🚀 Availability
- Attie apps can be shared instantly as standalone web pages on the `attie.site` domain.
- The tool is currently live and can be accessed directly through Bluesky’s platform.

## 💡 Why it matters
- By allowing anyone to generate functional mini‑apps with a prompt, Attie lowers the barrier to creating community‑specific tools.
- Bluesky CEO Toni Schneider framed the vision as “social vibe coding or vibe coding for your community,” emphasizing a shift toward user‑driven, AI‑assisted social experiences.
