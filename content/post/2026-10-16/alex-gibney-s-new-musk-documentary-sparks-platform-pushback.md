# Alex Gibney’s New Musk Documentary Sparks Platform Pushback

The latest Alex Gibney documentary about Elon Musk is now screening in select theaters. The film chronicles Musk’s past at xAI and SpaceX and asks why the public allowed him to amass so much power.

## 🎬 Film Details
- The documentary acts as both a chronicle of the xAI/SpaceX head’s past and an inquiry into his accumulation of power across governments worldwide.
- Production began after Musk had already become a prominent figure in multiple technology sectors.

## ⚖️ Controversies & Platform Actions
- Musk’s response has been stark: X blocked the feature from being advertised on the platform, and Musk, a self‑proclaimed “free speech absolutist,” threatened legal action against Gibney for producing a “hit piece.”
- Advertisements for the film were initially missing from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.
- Meta subsidiaries and YouTube later reversed their decisions, but TikTok has kept the ads blocked.
- TikTok’s stance is notable given its indirect connections to the Trump administration via Skydance Corporation CEO David Ellison.
- David Ellison’s father, Oracle co‑founder Larry Ellison, a longtime Donald Trump supporter, led the consortium of investors that purchased TikTok’s US business late last year.
- Ellison has reportedly promised Gibney that Musk will air on HBO (now part of Skydance) sometime next January.

## 📅 Release & Reception
- The documentary is currently screening in select theaters.
- It has faced an unusual amount of outside interference in the weeks leading up to its release.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- The film highlights concerns about how much influence a single tech leader can wield over governments and the public narrative.
- Musk’s aggressive pushback illustrates the tension between high‑profile documentaries and the subjects they profile, especially when those subjects control major platforms.
