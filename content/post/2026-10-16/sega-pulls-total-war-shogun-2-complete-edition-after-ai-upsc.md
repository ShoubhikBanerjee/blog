# Sega pulls Total War: Shogun 2 – Complete Edition after AI‑upscaled launch issues

Sega is removing **Total War: Shogun 2 — Complete Edition** from storefronts following a disastrous launch where users reported bugs and AI‑generated upscaling problems.

## 🔍 Overview
- The remastered edition launched on Tuesday, bundling the original 2011 strategy game and all DLC.
- Shortly after release, users complained about bugs and upscaling that appears to have been done by AI.
- Sega announced the game is being removed from sale.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- Promised upgrades included:
  - “Improved visuals”
  - “1440p and 4K UI scaling”
  - Modernized controls
- Reported problems:
  - Numerous bugs
  - Upscaling quality issues suspected to be AI‑generated
  - An “Overwhelmingly Negative” rating on Steam, where the title has already been pulled
  - Reddit posts showing artwork that users believe AI messed up

## 📢 Response
- Sega and developer Creative Assembly wrote:
  > “We’re aware that Total War: Shogun 2 – Complete Edition has fallen short of both your expectations and our own standards.”
  > “We’ve seen your feedback, comments, and reviews.\nWe agree that the current experience is not where it needs to be.”
- The companies added they will:
  > “reinstate the original game,”
  > and existing owners will “still be able to access” Complete Edition.
- They also said they are “working closely with our development partner to identify the causes of these problems, deliver improvements, and ensure Shogun 2 - Complete Edition meets the quality standards players expect from Total War.”

## 🚀 Availability
- The title has been removed from Steam and other storefronts.
- The original game will be reinstated.
- Players who already own the Complete Edition will retain access.

## 👥 Development
- Developers listed: Sega, Creative Assembly, and Mataboo (shown as a developer on the Steam page).
