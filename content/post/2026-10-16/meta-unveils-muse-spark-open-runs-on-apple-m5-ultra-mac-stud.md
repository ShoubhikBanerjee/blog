# Meta unveils Muse Spark Open, runs on Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios

Meta announced the Muse Spark Open AI model today, calling it the “most capable US open-weight model to date.”

## 🔍 Overview
- The company also says it’s the “first model in its class” that can “run on your desktop using two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios with the latest MLX framework.”
- Meta and Apple, typically rivals, worked together on the launch and are publicly supporting each other.

## 🧩 How it works
- The new model is a “natively multimodal model” with **816 billion total parameters**.
- It “excels at long‑horizon tasks, complex software engineering, and multi‑step reasoning.”
- Apple’s MLX is described as an “array framework” for ML tasks on Apple Silicon and is used to run Muse Spark Open on the Mac Studios.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Model name:** Muse Spark Open
- **Weight release:** Today (weights releasing today)
- **Hardware requirement:** Two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios
- **Software framework:** MLX (Apple’s array framework for ML on Apple Silicon)
- **Capabilities:** Multimodal processing, long‑horizon reasoning, software‑engineering assistance, multi‑step reasoning

## 🚀 Availability
- “Muse Spark Open -- weights releasing today -- runs great on new Mac Studios thanks to work with @johnternus and Apple,” Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg said on X.
- “Congrats to the team!” new Apple CEO John Ternus replied.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang told Axios that the companies have been collaborating for several months, noting the partnership as a sign of “mommy and daddy playing nice again.”
- Apple published a technical post about MLX, highlighting its role in enabling Muse Spark Open and two other open models on Apple Silicon.
