# AI‑altered White House renovation photo posted by Donald Trump flagged by Google detector

President Donald Trump posted a series of photos on Truth Social showing off renovations to the White House — but one of them appears to be modified with AI.

## 🔍 Overview
- The post includes a “before and after” photo of a freshly painted White House column.
- The image was shared to brag about White House restoration work.

## 🧩 Detection
- Google’s AI detector flagged the picture as “made or edited with OpenAI.”
- The Verge confirmed the photo is flagged as AI‑generated when uploaded to Google’s SynthID Detector, which scans for an invisible watermark indicating it was “created or edited” with AI.
- The SynthID watermark was not detected on other pictures Trump posted, which show the White House façade and heliport.

## ⚙️ Visual anomalies
- Reddit users noted an uneven number of steps on both sides of the structure.
- The number of steps does not match the ones in the “before” photo.
- The before and after photos appear nearly identical in angle and detail; the reflection in the windows is nearly the same, and the protective cardboard at the bottom of the column seems to be in the same spot as well.
- The “newly‑painted” column looks unnaturally smooth.

## 📚 Context
- Real pictures show people painting the front of the White House in June, so it’s possible the column actually did get a fresh coat of paint — the White House didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.
- Trump is no stranger to AI tools, as he often posts AI‑generated caricatures of himself.
- The White House has previously posted racist AI memes and at least one photo deceptively edited to make a protester appear distraught.
