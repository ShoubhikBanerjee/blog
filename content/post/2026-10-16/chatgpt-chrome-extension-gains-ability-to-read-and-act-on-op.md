# ChatGPT Chrome Extension Gains Ability to Read and Act on Open Tabs

OpenAI has refreshed its ChatGPT Chrome extension, letting the assistant look at the tabs you already have open and use that context for a variety of tasks.

## 🔍 Overview
- The previous ChatGPT Atlas browser is gone, but the Chrome extension now lets ChatGPT **view your browser tabs** when you tag Chrome in the web or desktop app.
- OpenAI shared a demo video where a user asks ChatGPT to compare three open tabs that show different sofas.

## 🧩 How it Works
- With the extension installed, ChatGPT can **pull information from your open tabs** or appear in a sidebar next to the page you’re viewing.
- The sidebar provides “the full power of ChatGPT” and keeps your entire conversation history in Chat or Work without leaving the page.
- ChatGPT Work can **control Chrome tabs** for actions such as sending messages, filling out forms, or downloading files, similar to Google’s Gemini‑powered “auto browse” feature.

## ⚙️ Key Features
- **Tab peeking** – ChatGPT can see the titles and contents of the tabs you have open.
- **Cross‑tab comparison** – It can compare items across multiple tabs (e.g., sofas, contractor services).
- **Data extraction** – Pull vendor details, pricing, key features, and source links from open tabs into a comparison sheet.
- **Form assistance** – Fill out registration or appointment forms that are open in Chrome.
- **Email drafting** – Draft emails based on the tab that best fits a described need.
- **Calendar integration** – Add appointments to work and personal calendars.
- **Tab control** – Send messages, download files, and organize or group tabs directly from the AI.

## 📋 Sample Queries
| Sample Query | What ChatGPT Does |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Use the details in this event brief to fill out the registration form, and stop before submitting. | Extracts information from the brief, populates the open registration form, pauses before final submission. |
| Put the vendors in these tabs into my comparison sheet, with pricing, key features, and source links. | Reads vendor data from open tabs and populates a spreadsheet with the requested fields. |
| Look at all the tabs I have open and compare the contractor services. | Reviews each open tab, extracts service details, and generates a side‑by‑side comparison. |
| Then draft an email to the one that fits my home renovation. | Crafts an email addressed to the selected contractor based on the comparison. |
| Fill out the form I have open in @chrome ahead of seeing my new dentist. | Completes the dental appointment form using the information provided. |
| Also, can you add the appointment to my work and personal calendar. | Creates calendar events in both work and personal calendars for the appointment. |

## 🚀 Availability
- The upgraded capabilities are available to anyone who installs the ChatGPT Chrome extension. No additional software is required beyond the extension itself.
