# SimCity Creator Will Wright Announces AI-Powered Memory Game MyMems

Will Wright, the creator of SimCity and The Sims, is developing a new mobile game called MyMems that uses artificial intelligence to turn personal memories into a social simulation. The title is designed to build a "toy world" from the player's autobiography, allowing users to create private or shared worlds that reflect their personal experiences.

## 🧩 How It Works
MyMems functions as a "cozy world builder" with a deep social simulation underneath, according to Virginia McArthur, executive producer and GM at Gallium Studios. Players interact with the game by adding "memories," such as speaking a short story to their phone, which are then generated into objects that can be placed on the game’s map.

The game includes AI-powered avatars that represent people in the player's life. As the player adds more elements, the world evolves into what Wright describes as a "map of your psyche," similar to the concept in the film Inside Out. The developers note that by having the player author content bit by bit, the experience avoids feeling like a cheat, encouraging a style reminiscent of *Scribblenauts* where AI generates specific elements like a high school.

## ⚙️ Key Details
*   **Developer:** Gallium Studios.
*   **AI Technology:** The game uses the "Behavior Engine" from Artificial Agency as a foundational technology.
*   **Game Genre:** Described as a "social simulation" with worlds built from personal memories.
*   **Player Reception:** Internal testing showed players, including Maitz's daughter who dislikes AI, became "addicted" to a test app featuring a character creator.

## 💡 Why It Matters
The development of MyMems occurs in a landscape where AI integration in gaming is met with mixed reactions. Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick stated that generative AI has "zero part" in what Rockstar Games is building for GTA VI. Wright notes that his three sons are "pretty negative" on AI, though he views the technology as an "amazing tool." Gallium Studios remains concerned about negative responses to generative AI but believes that letting players author the content mitigates resistance. MyMems will join other social simulations like Krafton’s *Inzoi* and Nintendo’s *Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream*.

## 🚀 Availability
MyMems is set to launch in 2027.
