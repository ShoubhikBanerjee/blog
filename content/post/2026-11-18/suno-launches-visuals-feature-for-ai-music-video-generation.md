# Suno Launches Visuals Feature for AI Music Video Generation

Suno has introduced image and video generation capabilities in its latest effort to expand beyond its role as an AI music platform. The new feature, named “Visuals,” allows users to transform Suno songs into AI-generated video clips that automatically lipsync to lyrics, or create images via prompt descriptions that can serve as thumbnails and album artwork.

## 🧩 How it works
The Visuals feature generates images and 15-second videos that automatically sync with Suno songs. From the interface, users can select which section of the track to use, up to a maximum of 15 seconds, and select a reference image to build the generated video clip. 

There are three options available for the starting image reference:
*   Selecting one of the original character presets provided by Suno.
*   Uploading a user's own photo or artwork.
*   Generating the image directly on Suno using descriptive prompts.

For video generation, users can select whether the reference image is used as the start or end frame, making it easier to edit the 15-second clips together. There is a toggle to automatically lipsync vocals to the generated video, along with tools for adjusting aspect ratio and clip length. The image generation capabilities also allow users to select specific aspect ratios, optimizing results for landscape or portrait modes and online platforms.

## ⚙️ Key details
Visuals includes a dedicated video editing timeline for assembling clips into longer videos and adding customizable song lyric text. The H3 Move AI model, based on H3 from MiniMax, powers this feature. 

Suno states that the AI has been “optimized for our community including adding additional protections and safeguards,” which include a “suite of new text and visual filters” designed to block the use or generation of the likeness of specific artists, public figures, copyrighted works, and IP. 

The feature uses its own “Visuals credits” to generate videos and images. These credits are distinct from the song credits used to make AI music, meaning users will have additional expenses to pay in order to use this new feature.

## 🚀 Availability
Visuals is available on web, iOS, and Android platforms. The exact layout differs across Suno’s desktop and mobile interfaces, but users will find a new Visuals tab under Create mode on each version. Web users can also access Visuals by selecting the three-dot menu on a song in their library.

## 💡 Context and reception
The launch of Visuals follows Suno introducing a Speech feature in October for generating spoken words and voiceovers. In the YouTube video for the Visuals launch, one user commented “FIX AUDIO BEFORE ADDING CRAP NOBODY ASKED FOR,” a sentiment that other commenters are echoing.
