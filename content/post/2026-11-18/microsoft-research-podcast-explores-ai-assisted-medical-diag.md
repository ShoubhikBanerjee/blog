# Microsoft Research Podcast Explores AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis

In a recent Microsoft Research podcast, experts discussed how AI can transform medicine, focusing on AI‑assisted medical diagnosis.

## 🔍 Overview

- The group explores **AI‑assisted medical diagnosis**.
- The group also looks at the untapped potential of **leveraging AI in medical imaging**.
- Whole‑patient models and **virtual cells** are examined.
- The role of **biological foundation models in scientific discovery** is highlighted.

## ⚙️ Key Details

- **Doug Burger** hosts the podcast.
- **Ava Amini** (Principal Research Manager, Microsoft Research) is a co‑lead of Project Ex Vivo, a collaboration with the Broad Institute targeting cell states in cancer.
- **Eric Horvitz** (Microsoft Chief Scientific Officer) is a PhD and an MD and a legend in machine learning.
- **Eric Topol** (Executive Vice President, Scripps Research) is a physician‑scientist with over 1,300 peer‑reviewed papers.

## 💡 Why It Matters

- The episode delivers a research‑grounded conversation of what AI can do today in the health space.
- It discusses why promising research can be slow to reach patients.
- It explores how AI could guide experiments and drug discovery.
- It stresses that better biological data and real‑world validation remain essential.

## 📚 Context

- **AlphaFold** is noted as a breakthrough that was the tip of the iceberg.
- **Models are helping us to discover structures and create abstractions that are really useful for moving forward the field of medicine.**
