# Nvidia’s Vera Rubin AI Chips Drive Record Data Center Growth and Frontier Model Adoption

# 🔍 Overview

Nvidia’s **Vera Rubin NVL72** AI computing system has generated **$20 billion in revenue** in its first quarter of production, marking the fastest product ramp in the company’s history. The platform’s deployment by major tech firms—including CoreWeave, Google, Microsoft, and Oracle—has accelerated adoption of frontier AI models, with companies like OpenAI and Anthropic expanding access to its capacity through cloud providers. Nvidia’s revenue surged **110% year-over-year** in Q3, while AMD reported record growth in data center sales, driven by high-end AI workloads.


# 🧩 How it works

The Vera Rubin NVL72 is designed for **both training and inference** of large AI models, including those used by AI agents. Nvidia’s latest earnings call highlighted its positioning for **frontier labs**, with expectations that these models will account for **~15% of its data center revenue** this fiscal year. The system integrates with Nvidia’s existing ecosystem, including cloud partnerships and AMD’s Helios high-end GPUs for similar workloads.


# ⚙️ Key details

- **Nvidia’s Vera Rubin NVL72**:
  - **Fastest product ramp** in company history.
  - Generated **$20 billion in revenue** in first quarter of production.
  - Deployed by CoreWeave, Google, Microsoft, Oracle, and Meta.

- **Frontier AI Model Adoption**:
  - OpenAI’s **Astra and Sol models** and Anthropic’s **Opus and Fable models** drive **60% of token usage** among businesses (Ramp data, early November).
  - Token prices fell **~60% since May**, spurring increased usage.

- **Nvidia’s Financial Growth**:
  - Revenue growth forecast raised to **75%** (from 70%) for next fiscal year.
  - Data center revenue rose **110%** YoY to **$107.6 billion** (Q3 2023).

- **AMD’s Helios Systems**:
  - Uses **MI450 GPUs** for high-end AI workloads.
  - OpenAI and Anthropic among first customers.


# 🚀 Availability

- **Vera Rubin NVL72**: Deployed at CoreWeave, Google, Microsoft, Oracle, and Meta.
- **AMD Helios**: Available to OpenAI and Anthropic via cloud providers.
- **Nvidia’s Cloud Partnerships**: Expanded access to Rubin capacity for frontier labs.


# 💡 Why it matters

The rapid adoption of **frontier AI models**—driven by Nvidia’s Vera Rubin NVL72 and AMD’s Helios systems—has reshaped data center spending. With **token usage surging** and GPU rental prices rising, companies are prioritizing high-performance AI infrastructure. Nvidia’s dominance in AI computing is further cemented as major labs adopt its chips, while AMD competes with its own high-end offerings. This shift underscores the critical role of specialized hardware in scaling AI innovation.
