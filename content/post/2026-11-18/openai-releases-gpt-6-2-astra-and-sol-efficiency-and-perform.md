# OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol: Efficiency and Performance Updates

# 🔍 Overview
OpenAI today shipped GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, two new models, ahead of Anthropic’s expected first trading day. These models are designed to balance intelligence and cost efficiency, with Astra and Sol deployed across ChatGPT Work, Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Edu, and via the API.

## 🧩 How it works
- **Astra and Sol** are part of the GPT-6.2 family, offering improved efficiency and performance metrics.
- **Astra** has a pricing structure of $8 input, $0.40 cached input, and $40 output.
- **Sol** maintains its predecessor’s pricing at $2 input, $0.10 cached input, and $10 output.
- Both models support 1M context and a 128K output limit. Astra is rated High for AI Self-Improvement, a first for OpenAI’s externally released models.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Performance Benchmarks:**
  - Astra scores 62 on the Intelligence Index, just behind Fable 5.2 at 65.
  - Astra leads in cost efficiency, using less than a third of the cost per task compared to its predecessor and 5.5 points ahead of Fable 5.2 at about 5% cost.
  - On GDPval v2, Astra used 22% fewer output tokens than its predecessor.
  - On AA-3D, Astra scored 1,318, ahead of Fable at 1,271 and Sol at 1,259.

- **Cost Efficiency:**
  - Sol ties September’s Astra at about 15% of the cost per task.
  - Astra’s pricing per task on Perplexity’s WANDR is approximately $12.54, while Sol is about $2.65.

- **Special Features:**
  - Enterprise configuration adds a legal search index.
  - Astra’s RSI-Index rose from 27.07% to 29.01%.
  - Epoch verified seven solutions in Lean out of 68 problems, a significant improvement over previous bests.

## 🚀 Availability
- **Deployment:**
  - Models are rolling out to ChatGPT Work, Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Edu, and developers via the API.
  - Both Astra (gpt-6.2-astra) and Sol are available through the API.

## 💡 Why it matters
- **Efficiency vs. Intelligence:**
  - While Anthropic’s models are still considered the best in raw intelligence, OpenAI’s models excel in intelligence per dollar.
  - Some criticisms regarding RL artifacts and launch quality are noted but not dismissed entirely.

- **Practical Use:**
  - Despite some issues, users find value in these models for automating work.
  - Microsoft’s concurrent releases of its own models and enhancements (e.g., MAI Code 2, Decision model multimodal upgrade) highlight competitive developments.

- **Comparative Strengths:**
  - OpenAI’s models show notable improvements in specific benchmarks and cost efficiency, making them competitive in practical applications.
