# Google rebrands Spark AI as Tasks, making it free and expanding availability

Google is rebranding its Spark AI agent as “Tasks” and making it free to use. The update, powered by Google’s new Gemini 4 Hydrogen AI model, brings the agentic capabilities originally introduced in May to a much broader audience.

## 🔍 Overview

Spark AI was first introduced in May but was previously restricted to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. The transition to “Tasks” in Gemini makes these capabilities available to all users in the US, Canada, Japan, Korea, Australia, and India.

## 🧩 How it works

Users can access Tasks by opening Gemini and changing the toggle on the homepage from “Chat” to “Tasks.” The agent operates within a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to the user’s account, ensuring it is never shared with other users. Users can provide instructions to Gemini while it completes tasks, and the AI will stop and request permission before executing more sensitive actions, such as purchasing an item.

## ⚙️ Key details

The feature allows the AI agent to perform various tasks, including:

| Capability | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| Planning | Create meal plans |
| Management | Manage your calendar |
| Research | Dig through emails to find recurring expenses |
| Communication | Call businesses on your behalf |
| Transactions | Purchase items with Google Pay |
| Access | Sign into accounts that support Google Password Manager |

US users receive access to additional features, such as having the AI agent call businesses to compare product pricing and check inventory. They can also assign Gemini tasks by emailing `gemini@google.com` or texting it.

## 🚀 Availability

The launch brings Spark’s capabilities to users in the US, Canada, Japan, Korea, Australia, and India. Tasks in Gemini joins a broader category of agentic AI features that have emerged over the past year, alongside competitors like Claude Duet, OpenAI’s Dots, and Meta’s Muse.
