# ATLAS-Finance Benchmark Tests AI Agents in Complex Banking Tasks

# 🔍 Overview

Researchers introduced **ATLAS-Finance**, a benchmark designed to evaluate how well frontier AI agents can perform realistic banking tasks. These tasks mirror the challenges of professional work—where information is fragmented across emails, files, and tools—and require agents to reconcile conflicting data and build financial models. The benchmark assesses 100 investment-banking tasks across 13 simulated firms, developed by experienced bankers based on real-world workflows.


# 🧩 How it works

- **Task Design**: Each task mimics the complexity of real banking assignments, taking **15–30 hours** for human professionals to complete.
- **Data Sources**: Agents must search **emails, chats, calendars, notes, and files** to identify requirements and gather information.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Deliverables are judged by expert rubrics covering **financial rationale, spreadsheet construction, completeness, and formatting** (median: **124.5 criteria**).
- **Current Performance**: Despite partial task completion, **no frontier AI agent** (including Claude Opus and Fable) passes **fewer than 11%** of tasks.


# ⚙️ Key details

- **Benchmark Scope**: 100 end-to-end investment-banking tasks across 13 simulated firms.
- **Agent Models Tested**: Claude Opus and Fable (performance metrics not explicitly detailed beyond passing <11% of tasks).
- **Evaluation Method**: Expert-authored rubrics with **124.5+ criteria** per task.
