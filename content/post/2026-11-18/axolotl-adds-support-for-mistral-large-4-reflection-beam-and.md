# Axolotl Adds Support for Mistral Large 4, Reflection Beam, and Multiple New Models with Advanced Training Enhancements

Axolotl has expanded its support for large language models and introduced several new training optimizations, including native integration for Mistral Large 4, Reflection Beam, and five additional models. These updates enhance multi-expert parallelism, quantization workflows, and deployment flexibility through new GGUF export capabilities and improved FSDP2 handling.
