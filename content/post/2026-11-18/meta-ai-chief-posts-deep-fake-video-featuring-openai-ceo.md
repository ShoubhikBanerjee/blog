# Meta AI Chief Posts Deep‑Fake Video Featuring OpenAI CEO

Meta’s chief AI officer, Alexandr Wang, posted an AI‑generated clip on Instagram showing himself, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, and Muse’s mascot Jolly in a four‑way video call.

## 🔍 Overview
- Wang’s clip was released on Instagram.
- The video shows a four‑way call between Wang, Zuckerberg, Altman, and Jolly.
- Altman is dressed as Jolly, wearing a furry hooded suit and face paint.
- Caption reads: “When the homies meet about openai product strategy,” referencing the AI agent race.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The clip is labeled as AI content in accordance with Meta’s AI policy.
- Meta requires AI‑generated videos depicting people to be labeled as AI; static images have no such requirement.
- Wang’s post complies with this labeling rule.

## 👇 Context
- Instagram has faced criticism for allowing AI‑generated images that lack disclosure, such as an AI image of actress Mia Goth shared by a fan account with a blue verification badge.
- Meta previously removed accounts that posed as others and used their likenesses.
- OpenAI’s Sora app made Sam Altman’s likeness available for AI content generation, leading to quickly flooded fake Altman videos.

## 🪄 Commentary
- Wang has heavily promoted Muse on social media, tweeting hundreds of times and referring to traditional marketers as “corposlops.”
- A major tech executive posting a deep‑fake of a competitor is notable, especially on a platform that has been criticized for its handling of AI‑generated content.

## 📈 Implications
- The incident highlights the differing platform policies on labeling AI‑generated media.
- It underscores the need for consistent disclosure requirements across video and image content.
