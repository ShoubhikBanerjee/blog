# Topological Summaries of BERT Attention Offer No Advantage in Linguistic Classification

Researchers evaluated whether topological summaries of Transformer attention could serve as sentence representations for linguistic acceptability classification.

## 🔍 Overview
- Topological summaries of Transformer attention provide sentence representations for linguistic acceptability classification.

## 🧩 How it works
- The zero‑dimensional persistent‑homology ($H_0$) representation summarizes minimum‑spanning‑tree merge values from 144 symmetrized BERT attention graphs into 1,440 features.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Evaluation used 8,517 downstream training sentences and 521 in‑domain and 497 out‑of‑domain CoLA validation sentences after data‑integrity filtering.  
- Out‑of‑domain Matthews correlation coefficients: 0.438 for the topological representation, 0.508 for attention statistics, 0.545 for BERT.  
- Attention‑feature results are aggregate measurements; BERT predictions are rescored and length controls are refitted from the accompanying records.  
- The representation retains token‑count information and discards a class of directional attention differences.  
- Source resampling of BERT predictions demonstrates sensitivity of uncertainty estimates to the sampling unit.

## 🔬 Findings
- The results do not demonstrate a predictive advantage for the fixed topological representation over simpler attention summaries.  
- The study identifies representation properties and evaluation controls relevant to subsequent studies of attention topology.
