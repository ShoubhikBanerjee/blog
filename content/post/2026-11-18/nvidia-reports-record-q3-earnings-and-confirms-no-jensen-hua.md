# Nvidia reports record Q3 earnings and confirms no Jensen Huang return

Nvidia has announced its Q3 earnings, revealing a significant increase in revenue and profit, while confirming that former CEO Jensen Huang will not be reinstated.

## 🔍 Overview

- Nvidia's Q3 revenue has more than doubled, reaching $115.8 billion, up 103 percent from the same period last year.
- Data center revenues alone reached $107.6 billion, up 110 percent from last year.
- The company reported a record profit of $65.3 billion.

## ⚙️ Key details

- Nvidia's board chair Harvey Jones stated that the board does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO.
- The board also decided not to address or take questions on Jensen Huang's resignation and the ongoing investigation during the earnings call.
- Nvidia's interim CEO Colette Kress highlighted the company's momentum and innovation, stating that Nvidia is an exceptional technology leader.
- The company expects revenues of $130 billion in the fourth quarter.

## 💡 Why it matters

- Nvidia's significant increase in revenue and profit indicates strong performance and market demand for its products.
- The company's focus on innovation and technology leadership is crucial in the tech industry.
- The decision not to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO and the ongoing investigation into his resignation are significant developments for the company.
