# Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO amid scandal involving alleged Chinese intelligence assets

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has resigned following reports of controversies surrounding his affairs and business dealings, including allegations involving a person identified as an alleged Chinese intelligence asset.

## 🔍 Overview

- Jensen Huang, the only CEO Nvidia has ever had, resigned Thursday morning.
- Reports indicate the resignation follows investigations into potential chip diversion and personal associations that threatened the company.
- The US intelligence community reportedly believes an individual named Alice Huang may be an asset of Chinese intelligence services.

## ⚙️ Key details

- Investigations focus on companies like Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer that operated out of non-existent or suspicious addresses in Malaysia and Singapore.
- Allegations suggest Megaspeed and other entities may have served as conduits for Chinese companies, such as DeepSeek, to access restricted Nvidia chips.
- The Financial Times reports that Alice Huang is barred from Singapore for providing inaccurate information to police.
- US export controls currently restrict the sale of high-end Nvidia chips to China.
- Nvidia reportedly intervened in a 2025 Commerce Department investigation into Megaspeed, which was suspected of smuggling chips.

## 📊 Companies and entities involved

| Company | Status / Allegation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Megaspeed | Largest Southeast Asian buyer; suspected of smuggling chips |
| Swiftdata | Owns Megaspeed; listed a business address belonging to an interior design firm |
| OBON | Thailand-based company allegedly used as a passthrough for $2.5 billion in chip purchases |
| New Matrix | Approved buyer of Nvidia chips despite ties to blacklisted firms and Chinese military contractors |

## 💡 Why it matters

- The scandal has implications for US export controls and national security, leading to scrutiny of Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick by US lawmakers.
- Observers note that Jensen Huang maintained a close relationship with Donald Trump, raising questions about potential influence on the policymaking process.
- China continues to push for a parallel AI chip supply chain through domestic manufacturers like Huawei to reduce reliance on Nvidia and bypass US policy actions.
