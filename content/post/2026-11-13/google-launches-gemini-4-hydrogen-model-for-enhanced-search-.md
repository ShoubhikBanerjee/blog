# Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen Model for Enhanced Search Performance

Google has released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a new model designed to provide more helpful answers to complex queries, including multi-part questions, highly specific requests, and those containing hard-to-understand typos. According to Google, the model can break down questions and address each individual part in its responses. Robby Stein, VP of product at Google Search, says the update is now the default model in AI Mode and is being used for difficult questions in AI Overviews.

## 🔍 Overview
Google says the new model delivers significant leaps in both speed and intelligence compared to Gemini Flash 3.8. Stein describes Gemini 4 Hydrogen as "the biggest model upgrade to Search yet." The model's performance reportedly matches or outperforms competing models in its class, such as Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Terra.

## 🧩 How it works
To improve information retrieval and language understanding, the model employs several new processing techniques:
* **Simultaneous Queries:** The model conducts simultaneous queries across all relevant subtopics to provide a wider range of links in its responses.
* **Improved Language Understanding:** Understanding has been improved across all supported AI Mode languages.
* **Breakdown Capability:** The model addresses complex questions by breaking them down and responding to each component.

## ⚙️ Key details
Responses from Gemini 4 Hydrogen now feature a broader range of visuals, including images, comparison tables, graphs, and maps. In one example provided by Stein, the AI generated an interactive animated driving simulation to explain how phones locate specific highway lanes in areas without cell service.

Users are also provided with expanded thinking level options:
* Low
* Medium
* High
* xhigh (New)

## 🚀 Availability
Starting today, Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available in the following products and services:
* Google Search and the Gemini app
* All tiers of Google AI Studio and the Gemini API
* Gemini for business
* Google Antigravity (available for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers)
