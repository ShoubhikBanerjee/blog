# Anthropic releases Claude Duet, a consumer‑friendly AI work assistant

Claude Duet is Anthropic’s newest AI agent aimed at consumers who want help with work without writing code. The author tested it for a week while juggling a busy travel and work schedule.

## 🔍 Overview
- Claude Duet is positioned as a consumer‑friendly, work‑focused AI agent.
- It follows earlier Anthropic agents: Claude Code (a coding agent) → Claude Cowork (more consumer‑friendly) → Claude Duet (most consumer‑friendly and work‑oriented).
- Other recent agents mentioned in the landscape are Instinct’s text‑based assistant, Meta’s Muse, and OpenAI’s Dots.

## ⚙️ How it works
- Users connect productivity and chat services (email, calendar, Google Docs, Slack). Duet then scans the data and surfaces suggestions as swipeable cards, similar to a “to‑do list Tinder.”
- Each morning Duet greets the user, highlights key items (e.g., packing for a video shoot), and proposes to‑dos with offers to draft messages or add agenda items.
- By accessing a biweekly staff‑meeting summary email and Slack, Duet linked a meeting comment to a direct Slack message.
- Access to a shared Google Doc let Duet identify the user’s manager for weekly 1:1 meetings.

## 📋 Key Details
- **Design** – Cheery, minimalist Claude design; app described as consumer‑friendly and approachable.
- **Platform** – Available as a standalone app, now fully supports the iPhone Duo.
- **Interaction style** – Swipe left/right on suggestion cards; drafted messages are tagged “sent with Claude.”
- **Personalization** – Mirrors the user’s writing habits (e.g., all‑lowercase style) and occasionally adds playful asides.
- **Data usage** – Operates on data the user opts‑in to share; Anthropic notes ongoing testing of its judgment about when to message.

## 🚀 Availability
- Claude Duet is a “research preview.”
- Access limited to Pro, Max, and Ultra subscribers in the United States, with a waitlist for additional users.
- Intended for “professionals and small business owners,” not for casual tasks like ordering food.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Compared to Meta’s Muse and OpenAI’s Dots, Duet is described as “even more focused on work.”
- It pushes the idea that AI agents can be useful for non‑programmers by handling everyday professional workflows.
- The swipe‑card interface and daily brief aim to make AI assistance feel like a natural extension of existing productivity tools.
