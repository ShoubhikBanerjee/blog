# Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen as New Free Tier and Search Model

On 12th November 2026, Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the lowest cost member of the Gemini 4 family. Designed as a high-efficiency execution engine, the model is intended for everyday work and to power AI features within Google Search.

## ⚙️ Key details

Google describes Hydrogen's position in their model lineup as a tool for fast, disciplined execution. 

* **Model Roles**: While teams use Carbon and Argon for deep architectural judgment and designing systems, Hydrogen is built for writing code, running tests, and verifying changes.
* **Workflow Integration**: Hydrogen can stand alone for everyday tasks or pair with Carbon and Argon in multi-model workflows.
* **Capabilities**: In a comparison of image generation, there is a stark difference in pelican quality for Hydrogen compared to its siblings.

| Model | Primary Function | Performance Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gemini 4 Hydrogen | Everyday work, code execution, free tier | Intended for fast, disciplined execution |
| Gemini 4 Carbon | Architectural judgment, untangling bugs | Frontier model |
| Gemini 4 Argon | Architectural judgment, system design | Faster responses and lower token spend than Carbon |

![Comparison of pelican generation across Gemini 4 models](https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026/gemini-4-pelicans.webp)

## 💰 Pricing

The pricing for the Gemini 4 family is scheduled to change significantly in the coming month:

* **Current Rates**: Hydrogen is currently priced at a 25% discount compared to Carbon and Argon. Carbon and Argon are currently the same price.
* **Scheduled Increases**: On December 1st, the price of Carbon and Argon is scheduled to double.
* **Future Discount**: After December 1st, the Hydrogen price will represent a 75% discount relative to its more capable peers.
* **Market Comparison**: At 15x the cost of Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna (which are both $0.10/$0.50), Hydrogen remains considerably more expensive than competing low-cost models.

## 🚀 Availability

Hydrogen is available immediately as the new model for Google's free tier. It is also the specific model designated to power AI features in Google Search.
