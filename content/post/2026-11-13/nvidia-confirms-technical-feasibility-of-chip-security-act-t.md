# Nvidia confirms technical feasibility of Chip Security Act tracking measures

![Foster and Huang](https://wp.technologyreview.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/foster-huang2a.jpg)

Nvidia representatives have privately informed Representative Bill Foster that the company could technically implement anti-smuggling geotracking measures for advanced AI chips, despite public opposition from the Semiconductor Industry Association (SIA).

## 🔍 Overview

The Chip Security Act, introduced in May 2025, aims to prevent the smuggling of advanced AI chips to China. The bill is currently under consideration for inclusion in the Senate’s National Defense Authorization Act. Discussions surrounding the bill have intensified following reports of an alleged affair between Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Alice Huang, who runs a firm currently under investigation for smuggling advanced GPUs.

## 🧩 How it works

The proposed legislation outlines several mechanisms for tracking chip locations:

*   Inspections of facilities.
*   Attestations provided by companies.
*   Ping-based location mechanisms, where GPUs send a signal of their location when coming online.

While the Center for Security and Emerging Technology identified the ping-based technique as the most cost-effective option, current versions of the bill would only allow investigators to identify chip locations, not disable smuggled units. Representative Foster plans to introduce further legislation requiring licenses to operate advanced GPUs, creating a workaround where a valid license must be verified before a chip can connect.

## ⚙️ Key details

| Entity | Stance / Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Semiconductor Industry Association | Publicly opposed the bill, citing it as “potentially infeasible.” |
| Nvidia Representatives | Privately told Foster that the company could implement tracking measures. |
| Nvidia Fleet Intelligence | A tool that monitors chip performance across data centers and can indicate which country chips are in. |
| Megaspeed | A Singaporean firm under investigation for smuggling GPUs to China. |
