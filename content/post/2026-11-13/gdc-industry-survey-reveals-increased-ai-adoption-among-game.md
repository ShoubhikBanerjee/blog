# GDC Industry Survey Reveals Increased AI Adoption Among Game Developers

The GDC Festival of Gaming’s latest annual industry survey indicates a rise in the use of artificial intelligence within the game development sector. Currently, 40 percent of developers report using AI as part of their job, up from 36 percent in 2025.

## 🔍 Overview
Usage statistics vary significantly by development sector:
* Mobile-only studio workers: 70 percent
* Free-to-play game developers: 58 percent

## ⚙️ Key details
Developers utilize AI across several functional areas:

| Use Case | Usage Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Research and brainstorming | 73% |
| Data analysis and summarization | 62% |
| Code generation and debugging | 56% |
| Concept art or visual ideation | 22% |
| Production art or final game assets | 8% |

## 💡 Why it matters
Industry stakeholders are positioning AI as a central component of future production. Xbox CEO Asha Sharma stated that AI could enable small teams to build worlds that previously required hundreds of developers and hundreds of millions of dollars. In practical application, developers like House House have used LLMs sparingly to assist with technical challenges. Additionally, AI is increasingly being applied to reverse-engineer retro games. Alongside these trends, the survey reported that 34 percent of respondents have experienced layoffs in the past two years.
