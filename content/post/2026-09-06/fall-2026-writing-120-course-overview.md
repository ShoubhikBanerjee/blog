# Fall 2026 Writing 120 Course Overview

The Writing 120 course lineup for Fall 2026 was released, featuring multiple sections and thematic pairings.
## 🔍 Overview
- Course title: Writing 120 Constellation Pairings
- Semester: Fall 2026
- Emphasis on short responses, longer researched papers, and thematic projects.
## 🗓️ Schedule
- Section 1: TUTH 8:30AM-9:45AM / TUTH 10:05AM-11:20AM (Instructor Hannah Davis)
- Section 2: MW 3:05PM-4:20PM / MW 4:40PM-5:55PM (Instructor Rhiannon Scharnhorst)
- Section 3: MW 1:25PM-2:40PM / MW 11:45AM-1:00PM (Instructor Charlotte Asmuth)
## 👩‍🏫 Instructors
- Hannah Davis
- Rhiannon Scharnhorst
- Charlotte Asmuth
## 📝 Assignments
- Regular short‑response writing tasks
- Two major writing projects: a Slang Analysis (≈1,200‑2,000 words) and a Research Project (≈2,500‑3,500 words)
- Collaborative research essay on feminist food history
- Student‑designed library exhibit titled “We Are What We Eat.”
## 📚 Course Themes
- Constellation: “What does it mean to be human?”
- Constellation: “What is the purpose of civil discourse in democracy?”
- Theme “We Are What We Eat?” referencing Mark Menjivar’s photo essay.
