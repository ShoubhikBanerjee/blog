# Astra AI demonstrates detailed 3D rendering, including pelican on bicycle

A new generative AI model called Astra showed an ability to produce highly detailed 3D models, as highlighted in a recent video.

## 🔍 Overview
- A familiar creature appears at 1m59s in the video.
- Astra is noted for more attention to detail, better understanding of the user's prompt, and the ability to build more sophisticated outputs.

## 🎨 Capabilities
- Excels at building 3D models.
- Has produced renderings of gardens, shipyards, animals, cityscapes, and even Dyson spheres.
- Can generate whimsical scenes such as a pelican wearing a red neckerchief while riding a bicycle.
