# Apple HomeView Review: First Smart Display Tested

Jen Tuohy, The Verge’s senior smart home reviewer, has tested every smart display Amazon and Google have ever made. For the past week, she has been testing Apple’s first: The $299 HomeView.

## 🔍 Overview
The HomeView is available in two variants: tabletop and wall-mounted.

## ⚙️ Key Details
*   **Price:** $299
*   **Capabilities:** Cooking, smart home control, calls, and Siri AI capabilities.
*   **Test Locations:** Kitchen counter, bedside table, office, coffee table, and entryway.

## 👥 User Feedback
*   **Tech-averse husband:** A big fan.
*   **Teenage daughter:** Less so.
