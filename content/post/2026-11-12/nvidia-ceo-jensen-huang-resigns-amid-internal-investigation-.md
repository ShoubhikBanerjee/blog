# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Amid Internal Investigation Into Extramarital Affairs

Jensen Huang has resigned as the CEO, president, and board member of Nvidia, the company he co-founded in 1993. The resignation follows a board investigation into two reported extramarital affairs, including one with a direct report and another with an alleged surveillance operative. CFO Colette Kress has been appointed as interim CEO while the board conducts a search for a permanent successor.

## 🔍 Overview
Following a board meeting on Wednesday night, the company accepted Huang’s resignation effective immediately. This leadership change introduces a new corporate structure and temporary management team:

*   **Interim CEO:** Colette Kress, who has served as Nvidia’s CFO for over 13 years.
*   **Board Chair:** Harvey Jones has been appointed as the first-ever Chair of the Board in NVIDIA's history.
*   **Succession Plan:** The board is initiating a search for a permanent CEO, evaluating both internal and external candidates.

## ⚙️ Key details
The board’s investigation focused on two specific relationships involving Huang. One relationship was with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s VP of healthcare, who reported directly to the CEO. The second involved Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based cloud provider Megaspeed. 

According to reports, Alice Huang was allegedly a surveillance operative passing information regarding advanced AI chips to China’s Ministry of State Security. While Huang admitted to being unfaithful and apologized to his family and employees, concerns have been raised regarding whether these relationships influenced business decisions or government export controls.

## 💡 Why it matters
Nvidia is one of the most consequential technology companies in the world, serving as the primary provider of chips fueling AI advancement. The company maintains an investment portfolio worth approximately $99 billion and holds significant influence over the global economy. Under Huang's 33-year leadership, Nvidia defined the modern GPU and pioneered the platform that powers the current AI revolution. The company will host a meeting for employees to discuss the transition on November 18 at 9am PT.
