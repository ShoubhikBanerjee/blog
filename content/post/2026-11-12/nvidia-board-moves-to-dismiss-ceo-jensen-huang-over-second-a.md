# Nvidia board moves to dismiss CEO Jensen Huang over second alleged affair

Nvidia's board is preparing to dismiss CEO Jensen Huang following reports of a second alleged extramarital affair. This development comes days after Huang admitted to a separate affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy.

## 🔍 Overview
Nvidia's board will meet to decide Huang's future at the company, with reports indicating the board may not wait for the results of an ongoing investigation by external counsel. The company's stock fell more than 7% in after-hours trading following the news.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Second alleged affair**: With Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's vice president of healthcare, according to *The New York Times*.
- **Hotel incident**: Huang and Powell were allegedly captured together at a San Jose hotel during Nvidia's GTC conference in March (sources: *The Times*).
- **Prior affair**: Huang previously admitted to an extramarital relationship with Alice Huang, an alleged spy with ties to Megaspeed.
- **Crisis response**: Nvidia retained former US attorney Joon H. Kim's firm for an investigation. Huang separately hired crisis communications firm Heller in 2025.
- **Stock impact**: Shares dropped over 7% in after-hours trading Wednesday.

## 💡 Why It Matters
The potential dismissal of Huang, a high-profile tech leader, raises questions about Nvidia's leadership stability and corporate governance. The situation has already impacted investor confidence, as evidenced by the stock decline. The board's urgency suggests concerns about reputational and operational risks.
