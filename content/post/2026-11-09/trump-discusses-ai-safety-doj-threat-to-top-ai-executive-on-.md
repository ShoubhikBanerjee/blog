# Trump Discusses AI Safety, DOJ Threat to Top AI Executive on 60 Minutes

In a recent 60 Minutes interview, former President Donald Trump addressed AI safety concerns, claimed the Department of Justice warned a leading AI executive, and shared his view on international AI competition.

## 🔍 Overview
- Trump said the risk from AI “is not nearly as real as it– as they talk, and I think the risk can be managed, and it’s managed.”
- He asserted that the DOJ is handling AI‑related risk, noting “we had a situation where somebody was reported to us to be doing something bad with AI. We called them very strongly and said, ‘Get ready to go to jail.’"
- Trump added an off‑hand remark, “Please give your wife a kiss good night.”
- He refused to name the company or person, describing the individual as “the leader of an AI company, ‘One of the big ones.’”
- According to Trump, after the DOJ warning “this person totally changed everything.”
- When asked about working with Democrats on AI safety, he said he would “absolutely” do so, noting he “doesn't mind guardrails, but I don’t want to put us in a deep second position.”
- Trump claimed the United States is “leading number one” in AI safety, while asserting that “China has no guardrails, by the way. They have the guardrails that whatever President Xi says goes.”
- He qualified his own stance: “With me, it’s pretty strong, but it’s not quite that strong.”

## 🗣️ Trump’s Remarks on AI Risk
- “I think that the risk is not nearly as real as it– as they talk, and I think the risk can be managed, and it’s managed.”

## ⚖️ DOJ Involvement Claim
- “We had a situation where somebody was reported to us to be doing something bad with AI.
  We called them very strongly and said, ‘Get ready to go to jail.’"

## 🌐 International Perspective
- Trump referred to China’s President Xi as being “all in to SI, as I call it, because it really is super intelligence. It’s a much better name, I think, because it’s not artificial.”
- He contrasted U.S. guardrails with China’s: “And China has no guardrails, by the way. They have the guardrails that whatever President Xi says goes.”

## 🤝 Political Cooperation
- In response to a question about working with Democrats, Trump said he would “absolutely” cooperate on AI safety and added, “I don’t mind guardrails, but I don’t want to put us in a deep second position.”
- He reiterated U.S. leadership: “We’re leading number one.”
