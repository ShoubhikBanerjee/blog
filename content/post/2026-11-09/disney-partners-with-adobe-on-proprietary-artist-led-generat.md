# Disney partners with Adobe on proprietary artist‑led generative AI models

Disney announced a multiyear partnership with Adobe at the MAX conference to co‑develop proprietary, artist‑led generative AI models for its production pipeline.

## 🔍 Overview
- The collaboration will give Disney early access to Adobe’s latest AI technologies for theme parks, cruise line, and ESPN content.
- Both companies will explore emerging technologies, including responsibly developed AI, per Adobe’s press release.

## 🤝 Partnership Details
- Adobe will provide Disney with early access to AI tools across resorts, cruise ships, and ESPN graphics.
- Disney’s Imagineering R&D division is already using Adobe Firefly for theme‑park development, building on an existing relationship.
- Adobe will help expand internal creative workflows at Walt Disney Studios and identify AI uses for ESPN content and fan engagement.

## 🧩 AI Model Details
- Using Adobe Firefly Foundry, Disney will work directly with Adobe to create generative AI models tailored to Disney properties.
- The models are described as human‑centered and artist‑led, trained on Disney‑specific world knowledge such as character attributes, environments, and visual styles.
- Outputs are intended to be commercially safe, production‑ready, and to accelerate creative work.

## 📈 Why it matters
- This partnership follows Disney’s earlier generative‑AI engagements, including a deal with OpenAI and involvement with Character.AI’s bankruptcy.
- Bloomberg reported Disney is planning staff reductions and targeting 20 % annual cost savings, marking its fourth round of cuts in 2026, underscoring a push for efficiency that AI tools may support.
