# AI system from Physical Superintelligence proves Aaronson‑Ambainis quantum conjecture

Physical Superintelligence, an AI science startup, announced that its internal AI system produced a proof of the Aaronson‑Ambainis quantum computing conjecture.

## 🔍 Overview
- The conjecture, formulated by Scott Aaronson and Andris Ambainis, has been open since 2008 and concerns when quantum computers can be exponentially faster than ordinary ones.
- Physical Superintelligence (PSI) developed a research system that runs a non‑public Anthropic model.
- During the third day of a run, the system generated a complete proof of the conjecture, which was accepted by the Lean proof assistant.
- The system was then tasked with related open problems, improving the result twice, with Lean accepting the final version.

## 🧩 How it works
- PSI’s internal system leverages an Anthropic language model to explore mathematical reasoning.
- The model searches for a sequence of logical steps that constitute a proof.
- Each candidate proof is fed to Lean, which checks every inference mechanically.
- Acceptance by Lean confirms the proof’s correctness.

## 💡 Why it matters
> "we’re in the early stages of the singularity, defined as the period when the rate of technological and scientific progress exceeds the rate at which we humans can parse out and make sense of it."

> "Research like this is a taste of what that means; mostly automated synthetic researchers extending the frontier of knowledge, spitting out results which need to then be understood by humans."

These statements highlight the significance of AI‑generated proofs as an example of automated synthetic researchers extending the frontier of knowledge.
