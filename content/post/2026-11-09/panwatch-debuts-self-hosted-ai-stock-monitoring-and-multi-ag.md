# PanWatch Debuts Self-Hosted AI Stock Monitoring and Multi-Agent Research Platform

PanWatch is a self-hosted AI stock monitoring platform designed to help users research ideas and manage portfolios across A-shares, Hong Kong, and U.S. markets. The service enables users to host the platform using OpenAI-compatible providers or local models through Ollama.

## 🔍 Overview
Operated by a registered Hong Kong company, the platform provides full-capability service with stable availability. Key features include:
- Portfolio tracking and multi-account overviews.
- Deep stock analysis with technical indicators including MACD, RSI, and KDJ.
- Opportunity discovery through AI-scored ideas.
- Paper trading with equity curve and performance tracking.
- Real-time price alerts with combined AND/OR logic conditions.

## 🧩 How it works
The core of the platform is the TradingAgents research team, which follows a structured workflow to analyze portfolio holdings:
- **Analysis**: A nine-agent team researches holdings and conducts bull/bear debates and risk reviews.
- **Decision**: Within 3–5 minutes, the system generates a portfolio manager decision memo and a complete reasoning trail.
- **Automation**: Users can run pre-market, intraday, and closing workflows based on configured schedules.
- **Delivery**: Reports and alerts are delivered via Telegram, WeCom, DingTalk, Feishu, Bark, or webhooks.

## ⚙️ Key details
The platform supports a broad range of AI capabilities across different media types:

| Capability | Supported Models/Tools |
| :--- | :--- |
| Language & Coding | GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM |
| Image Models | GPT-Image, Gemini, Grok |
| Video Models | Seedance, Veo, MiniMax H3, Kling, Grok |
| Speech | Whisper, TTS |

Configuration options for alerts include cooldowns, daily limits, expiration settings, and specific notification channels.

## 🚀 Availability
PanWatch is an installable PWA that can be added to mobile home screens. The interface supports both English and Simplified Chinese. New users are provided with trial credits upon registration to test model capabilities, and live customer support is available on-site. The service offers invoices and maintains full model intelligence without reductions.
