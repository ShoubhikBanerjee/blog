# Nvidia launches independent probe into CEO Jensen Huang’s ties to alleged Chinese spy

Nvidia announced that its board has opened an independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang’s relationship with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, who U.S. officials say was recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security to spy on the company.

## 🔍 Overview
- Nvidia is investigating Jensen Huang’s relationship with a Chinese spy.
- The investigation follows reports that Huang had a “close relationship” – later admitted as romantic in a company memo – with the Megaspeed executive.
- Megaspeed is a customer of Nvidia and is under a Commerce Department probe for allegedly smuggling AI chips to China.

## 🛡️ National security context
- Nvidia’s AI chips are viewed as crucial to the United States maintaining technological supremacy.
- Both the Biden and Trump administrations have restricted Nvidia’s chip sales to China.
- President Trump has loosened some restrictions but still bars sales of Nvidia’s most powerful chips to Chinese firms; the Commerce Department must approve any individual firm that handles those chips.

## 📋 Board response
- The NVIDIA Board is initiating an independent investigation in light of recent reporting that Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort.
- A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside independent counsel, will oversee the investigation.
- The Board says it prioritizes U.S. national and economic security, which it deems paramount to the interests of NVIDIA and its shareholders.
- While the Board has no reason to believe that national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised, the inquiry will examine all available information.

## 📧 Corporate statements
- Jensen Huang fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it.
- The Board has confidence in Jensen and his leadership team and will provide further updates as appropriate.
- The New York Times reported that Huang later admitted the relationship was romantic in a memo to the company.
- U.S. officials believe Alice Huang was feeding information directly back to Beijing.
- There is concern about whether any insights Jensen Huang may have had about U.S. administration deliberations on AI were shared with Alice Huang and ultimately reached Beijing.
