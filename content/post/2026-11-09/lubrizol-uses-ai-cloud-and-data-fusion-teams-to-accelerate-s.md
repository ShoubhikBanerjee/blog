# Lubrizol Uses AI, Cloud and Data Fusion Teams to Accelerate Sustainable Material Development

Lubrizol is accelerating the development of sustainable materials by linking AI, advanced modeling, and cloud‑based data platforms with its scientists’ expertise.

## 🔍 Overview
Connected data, AI, and advanced modeling are helping scientists accelerate research and develop more sustainable materials. The primary challenge for companies is connecting new technologies to reliable data, scientific expertise, and the practical realities of bringing products to market.

## 🏭 Digital Foundation
- Girish Rao, vice president of information technology at Lubrizol, says the company is building a digital foundation that connects business processes, modern data platforms, and cloud infrastructure to support its R&D teams.
- The goal is to combine decades of accumulated technical data with the deep expertise of chemists and engineers who understand customer needs and physical constraints.
- Cloud infrastructure gives the company flexibility to access computing resources and modern tools without making the substantial investments required to build and maintain its own data centers.

## 🤝 Fusion Teams
- Scientists, data scientists, and IT teams approach problems from different perspectives, prioritizing experimental validity, model performance, and platform security and scalability, respectively.
- By bringing these groups together in “fusion teams,” Lubrizol aligns them around shared objectives, starting with small pilots to build trust.
- “Once the trust is built between the team members and they know what value they're about to create," Rao says, "things go a lot better."

## 📊 Modeling & Sustainability
- Data scientists can help identify patterns, test new hypotheses, and model possibilities before researchers conduct physical experiments.
- Digital modeling can help researchers evaluate more formulations, explore materials such as starch and corn‑based alternatives, and assess trade‑offs across a product's life cycle.
- Scaling production while meeting performance expectations, regulatory requirements, and financial constraints is a tightrope walk, but AI's ability to model multiple variables could help teams navigate those competing demands.

## 🚀 Future Outlook
- Rao expects AI, automation, advanced modeling, digital twins, and connected data to converge as laboratories become more intelligent and manufacturing plants more efficient.
- "It's not just a single technology. I think it's the combination of all of these, and it'll be the ability to connect all these processes that are going to generate tremendous amounts of data," he notes.
- The opportunity lies in bringing these capabilities together responsibly and at scale.

| Data Scientists | Model performance                               |
| IT Teams      | Platform security and scalability               |
