# Apple pushes Siri toward agentic AI with new app integration and fine‑grained controls

Apple is updating Siri to become more agentic, adding fine‑grained control over its own apps and integration with third‑party services.

## 🔍 Overview
- Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports that CEO John Ternus is pressuring AI czar Craig Federighi to accelerate the Siri roadmap.
- Federighi has delivered a functional version of Siri AI on an incredibly short timeline, but the company acknowledges it is not keeping pace with rivals.
- Apple is racing to make Siri more agentic and to enable ongoing tasks rather than single‑step commands.

## ⚙️ Upcoming Features
- **Fine‑grained control of first‑party apps** – Siri will be able to rotate and crop images in Photos and organize Safari tabs. *Expected with iOS 27.3.*
- **Third‑party app integration** – Siri will place orders through Grubhub, book reservations through OpenTable, and similar actions. *Expected in the Spring following iOS 27.3.*

## 📅 Timeline
| Feature | Expected Release |
|---|---|
| First‑party app control (Photos, Safari) | iOS 27.3 |
| Third‑party app integration (Grubhub, OpenTable) | Spring after iOS 27.3 |

## 💡 Why it matters
- Competitors such as OpenAI’s Dots, Meta’s Muse, and Grok can keep track of goals and continue pursuing objectives without the user initiating every step.
- Apple aims to give Siri similar agentic capabilities, allowing it to perform ongoing tasks and manage objectives, a direction the company may reach as early as next year.
