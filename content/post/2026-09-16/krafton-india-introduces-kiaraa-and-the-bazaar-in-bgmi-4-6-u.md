# KRAFTON India Introduces Kiaraa and The Bazaar in BGMI 4.6 Update

KRAFTON India's in-house art team has developed the BGMI 4.6 update, which introduces "Kiaraa," the battle royale's first fully original, India-focused character IP and a dedicated content ecosystem.

## 🚀 Availability

*   **BGMI 4.6 Version Update:** Deploys September 16, 2026.
*   **Kiaraa and The Bazaar Content:** Goes live October 8, 2026.
*   **India-Specific Prize Path:** Runs from October 22 to November 15, 2026.

## ⚙️ Key Details

The initial September 16 patch includes:
*   Seasonal rank resets
*   Performance optimizations
*   "Midnight Hunters" supernatural theme mode

Kiaraa is a lore-driven character designed exclusively for Indian players. Her design features royal blue tones, golden armor accents, and peacock feather iconography.

## 🧩 How it works

Coinciding with Kiaraa's arrival is "The Bazaar," a permanent courtyard-style market POI on the Erangel map. It features festive visual architecture, multi-tiered rooftops, narrow alleys, and four peacock-shaped lamps.

The Bazaar hosts "Kiaraa’s Test," a cooperative boss encounter:
1.  **Activation:** Squads track four blue energy indicators and interact with the four peacock-shaped lamps.
2.  **Engagement:** A glowing energy dome locks the zone, and Kiaraa enters wielding a divine bow. She utilizes evasive dashes and an "Arrow Rain" area-of-effect attack.
3.  **Rewards:** Defeating the boss breaks the seal and drops Level 3 armor, crate-exclusive weaponry, rare attachments, and specialized utility consumables.
4.  **Risk:** Activating the fight alerts surrounding squads on the minimap.

## 💡 Why it matters

| Item | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
