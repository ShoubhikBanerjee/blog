# Survey Finds Majority of Likely Voters Oppose AI Data Center Construction

A survey of 1,503 likely voters conducted in early September reveals that 61 percent of respondents oppose the construction of data centers used to power AI technology, while 14 percent say they strongly support it.

## ⚙️ Key details

Opposition to AI data centers varies by political affiliation and voter demographic:

* **Donald Trump voters:** Split nearly evenly with 49 percent support and 45 percent oppose.
* **Kamala Harris voters:** 74 percent lean oppose.
* **Non-voters:** 67 percent lean oppose.
* **Age demographics:** AI and data centers registered at less than 1 percent for most demographics, except for the 18–29 group, where 3 percent selected this issue over other popular responses such as inflation, foreign policy, and the economy.

Among those who oppose the construction of data centers, 56 percent support limits, while 38 percent prefer a total ban.

## 💡 Why it matters

Respondents cited several specific reasons for their opposition:

| Reason for Opposition | Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Environment/water usage | 32% |
| Impact on local communities | 21% |
| General distrust or dislike of AI | 18% |
| Regulation concerns | 10% |
| The economy | 9% |

Regarding trust in handling the issue, 40 percent of 554 respondents favored Democrats and 42 percent favored Republicans, while 18 percent refused or did not know. This follows previous polling, including a March NBC News poll where AI polled lower than ICE, and a May Gallup poll showing seven in ten Americans opposed local construction of AI data centers.
