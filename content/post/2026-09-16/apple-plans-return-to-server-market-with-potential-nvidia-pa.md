# Apple Plans Return to Server Market With Potential Nvidia Partnership

Apple is planning to re-enter the server market, potentially partnering with Nvidia to develop the new hardware.

## 🔍 Overview
Apple previously retired its Xserve line in 2011 and has since left enterprise machines to other manufacturers. This new move follows the popularity of the Mac Mini and Mac Studio among AI developers, which has resulted in shortages.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Hardware:** The servers may run on two or four M8 Ultra chips, depending on the model.
* **Technology:** The servers could incorporate Nvidia’s NVLink Fusion technology, which allows multiple chips to connect and operate as one.
* **Existing Relationship:** Apple recently used Nvidia chips to power its revamped Siri servers.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Estimated Debut:** The product is unlikely to debut until 2029.
* **Chip Timeline:** The M8 line is considered a ways out, as Apple only announced the M6 in August.
