# Novo and Anthropic Collaborate to Advance Drug Discovery with Claude

Novo and Anthropic are collaborating to use Claude to advance the process of drug discovery.

## ⚙️ Key details

*   **Collaboration:** Novo and Anthropic will work together to advance drug discovery using Claude.
*   **Data Sharing:** Novo will share clinical and real-world data, including:
    *   Wegovy® pill
    *   Cardiometabolic pipeline progress regarding next-generation amylin treatments (to be shared at EASD 2026)

## 🔍 Overview

Novo Nordisk is entering a new chapter supported by an updated corporate culture and the 'Novo' rebrand to bring better health to more people.
