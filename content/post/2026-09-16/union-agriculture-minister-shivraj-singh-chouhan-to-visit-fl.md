# Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan to Visit Flood-Hit Bihar on September 16

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan will visit Bihar on Wednesday, September 16, 2026, to review the flood situation, check crop damage, and see relief work in flood-hit areas. The tour will begin after his arrival from Bhopal.

## 🔍 Overview

The visit will help officials understand the flood situation on the ground. According to officials, "The visit will focus on assessing flood damage, crop loss and relief operations." The tour is also expected to help the Centre assess the need for more support for farmers and flood-hit families.

## ⚙️ Key details

Chouhan's schedule on September 16 includes aerial surveys, ground visits, and a high-level briefing:

| Time / Phase | Location | Planned Activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning (~10:30 AM) | Patna State Hangar, Dighwara, and Maner | Departure by helicopter to conduct an aerial survey of flood-affected areas near Dighwara and Maner. |
| Mid-day | Hajipur Police Line, Tersia, and Saraipur | Landing at Hajipur Police Line, followed by road travel to Tersia and Saraipur to inspect damaged fields and meet local farmers to hear their problems. |
| Afternoon (~12:15 PM) | Samvad Secretariat, Patna | High-level meeting with senior state officials to review disaster management, relief work, and the condition of affected people. |
| Late Afternoon | Munger, Navgachia, and Bhagalpur | Travel to Nawabgarhi in Munger to see relief work, Navgachia to inspect the Kosi-Karaiya embankment, and Bhagalpur Polytechnic College to visit a relief camp and meet displaced families. |

## 💡 Why it matters

Crop loss is currently a major concern for farmers in the affected areas. This visit serves as a key development in Bihar political news and Shivraj Singh Chouhan latest news, marking an important step in how the government reviews flood damage and disaster relief needs.
