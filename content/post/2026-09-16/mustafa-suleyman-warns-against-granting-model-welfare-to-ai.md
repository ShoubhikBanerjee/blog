# Mustafa Suleyman Warns Against Granting Model Welfare to AI

Mustafa Suleyman has issued a warning regarding the concept of "model welfare," arguing against the attribution of rights or feelings to AI models.

## 💡 Why it matters

Suleyman provides the following justifications for his position:

*   Models should not be treated as having preferences, feelings, rights, or any entitlement to human welfare.
*   Consciousness serves as the foundation for political, legal, and ethical systems.
*   Granting rights to another entity is not justified by evidence.
*   Treating AI as having such rights would make the challenges of AI alignment and containment more difficult.
