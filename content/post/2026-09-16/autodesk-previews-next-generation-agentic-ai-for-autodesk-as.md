# Autodesk Previews Next-Generation Agentic AI for Autodesk Assistant

At Autodesk University 2026, Autodesk previewed new agentic AI capabilities designed to shape the next generation of Autodesk Assistant. This updated experience extends agentic AI across products, projects, and teams rather than keeping it confined within a single tool, drawing on connected project data to understand the broader project context behind a user's questions.

## 🔍 Overview

Instead of requiring professionals to know which specific Autodesk product or AI capability to select, the next-generation experience starts with what the user is trying to accomplish. It brings forward relevant information and capabilities while keeping professionals in control. This experience connects project intelligence with the right Autodesk capabilities across teams, products, and projects.

Autodesk's three industry clouds provide the specialized context needed for real-world projects:

| Industry Cloud | Industry Focus | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Autodesk Forma | Architecture, Engineering, and Construction | Connects data and workflows |
| Autodesk Fusion | Design and Manufacturing | Connects design and manufacturing |
| Autodesk Flow | Media and Entertainment | Connects media and entertainment |

## 🧩 How it works

Unlike general-purpose AI, Autodesk's Design and Make AI grounds its reasoning in geometry, constraints, physics, processes, and project history to ensure real-world viability. It is designed to:

*   **Understand Specialized Context:** The system accounts for real-world project elements such as buildings, infrastructure, products, manufacturing processes, characters, scenes, schedules, specifications, and their relationships.
*   **Analyze Downstream Impacts:** For example, when a structural change is made to a building, the next-generation Assistant can identify impacts on fabrication, schedule, cost, construction sequencing, and operations, and surface the precise tools needed to evaluate options.
*   **Prioritize Urgent Issues:** The system is built around understanding what needs attention, utilizing features like "Personal Pulse" to surface prioritized issues and insights across projects.
*   **Integrate Third-Party Tools:** Teams can bring their own tools and intelligence into the experience alongside Autodesk's capabilities.

## 🚀 Availability

*   **Current Availability:** Autodesk Assistant is generally available today across many Autodesk products, including Fusion, Forma, and Flow, helping customers inside their existing tools.
*   **Future Roadmap:** The next-generation experience is designed to connect project intelligence across workflows, with a standalone experience planned for release in 2027.

## 💡 Why it matters

Design and manufacturing environments require exact outcomes, where approximate answers are insufficient for real-world implementation.

"The constraint in our industries has never been about ideas. It's capacity," said Andrew Anagnost, president and CEO of Autodesk. "And for Design and Make professionals, 'probably right' is wrong because their work has consequences in the real world. Our three industry clouds, Forma, Fusion, and Flow, connect the people, data and workflows that make industries work. Autodesk Assistant is how we bring intelligence to customers today, and the next generation will extend that experience across products, projects and teams."

"Project teams don't work in one tool or one part of a project, and the next generation of Autodesk Assistant reflects that," said Marius Jablonskis, Digital Transformation Leader at Norconsult. "The potential is to bring the right project context into the workflow so teams can understand what needs attention and act on it more easily. We also see real value in being able to bring our own tools and intelligence into the experience alongside Autodesk's."
