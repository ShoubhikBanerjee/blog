# Google Launches Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking

Google has launched Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, which are conversational models designed to talk, think, and handle tasks in the background without breaking flow.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Language Support:** 97 languages.
* **Capabilities:** Async tool calls while speaking.
* **Performance:** Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking (High) ranks #1 on its speech-to-speech index at 82.6 (surpassing GPT-Live-1 Astra at 81.5) and #1 on Tau Voice at 68.6%.

## 🚀 Availability

* **Access:** Available via Gemini API and AI Studio.
* **Partner Support:** Supported through Vercel, LangChain, Pipecat, and LiveKit.

## 💡 Pricing

| Model Variant | Input Audio Cost |
| :--- | :--- |
| Standard 3.8 Live | $0.84/hour |
| Extended Thinking High | $3.50/hour |
