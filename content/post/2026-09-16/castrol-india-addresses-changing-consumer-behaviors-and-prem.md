# Castrol India Addresses Changing Consumer Behaviors and Premiumisation in Lubricants

Castrol India is adapting its marketing and product strategy to align with changing consumer psychology and the increasing sophistication of vehicles in the automotive space.

## 🔍 Overview
Kaushik Vedula, Vice President – Marketing, Castrol India, has highlighted the evolving role of marketing in the lubricant category, focusing on data, emotional storytelling, and the influence of creators and communities on consumer trust to keep a legacy brand relevant.

## ⚙️ Key Details
Consumer behavior regarding lubricant selection has shifted from workshop-led decisions to independent research:

* **Research Habits**: Consumers now conduct due diligence by watching reviews, comparing products online, and speaking to fellow vehicle owners.
* **Touchpoints**: First interactions with the brand often happen online, where first-time vehicle owners expect education before purchasing.
* **Influence**: While mechanics remain one of the most trusted voices, their recommendations now sit alongside influences from creators and enthusiast communities.

## 💡 Why it Matters
Changes in the automotive market have led to a trend of premiumisation:

* **Vehicle Sophistication**: Vehicles have become more expensive and sophisticated, evidenced by the growth of SUVs and higher-cc bikes.
* **Product Expansion**: Castrol has expanded its full synthetic portfolio to reflect this premiumisation and increasing consumer awareness of synthetic grades.
* **Core Expectations**: Despite changing habits, the fundamental requirements for the category remain protection, performance, and reliability.
