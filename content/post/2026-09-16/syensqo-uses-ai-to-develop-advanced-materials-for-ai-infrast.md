# Syensqo Uses AI to Develop Advanced Materials for AI Infrastructure

Syensqo is utilizing AI agents to accelerate the development of advanced materials as semiconductors and data centers approach their physical limits.

## 🔍 Overview
According to Mike Finelli, chief technology and innovation officer and chief North America officer at Syensqo, the AI boom has become a materials challenge. As AI pushes infrastructure toward physical limits regarding reliability, electrical efficiency, thermal management, and performance, advanced materials are becoming critical to sustainability and efficiency. Finelli contends that these materials are "actually increasingly defining what's going to be possible."

## ⚙️ Key details
Syensqo is developing several specific solutions to meet emerging demands:

| Material Focus | Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| High-voltage data center architectures | Materials for emerging energy-density and voltage demands |
| Semiconductor manufacturing | Advanced sealing materials |
| Thermal management | Fluids for direct immersion cooling |

Finelli notes that materials developed for electric vehicles can assist in addressing the higher voltage and energy-density demands appearing in data centers.

## 🧩 How it works
Syensqo is employing AI agents to streamline the research process:
* **Digital Synthesis**: AI agents digitally synthesize millions of potential molecular combinations.
* **Prediction**: The system predicts sustainability characteristics and performance.
* **Refinement**: Potential combinations are narrowed to a small group for laboratory testing.

This approach allows scientists to go "broader, deeper, and faster" while focusing on complex engineering problems. Additionally, sustainability is considered at the start of the research process to remove the trade-off between performance and environmental impact.

## 💡 Why it matters
Finelli envisions a reinforcing cycle where AI helps develop materials that improve AI infrastructure, which then enables better AI to further accelerate materials discovery. This "accelerated materials, innovative cycle of materials innovation" addresses the pressure on the advanced material sector to keep pace as AI evolves.
