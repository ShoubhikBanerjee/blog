# TAISE Framework Repurposes AI Weather Models for Catastrophe Risk Modeling

A new study proposes the TAISE framework, which utilizes AI weather forecasting models to generate extreme weather sequences for catastrophe (CAT) risk modeling.

## 🧩 How it works

* **Model Repurposing**: The framework repurposes AI weather forecasting models to produce coherent extreme weather sequences.
* **Self-Iterative Generation**: TAISE uses self-iterative generation to produce continuous global atmospheric fields, allowing extreme events to emerge.

## 💡 Why it matters

* **Cost Reduction**: A proof-of-concept experiment showed an order-of-magnitude reduction in computational cost compared to conventional methods.
* **Improved Continuity**: The framework captures temporal continuity and cross-regional correlations, which are absent in snapshot-based approaches.
* **Modernization**: Traditional CAT risk models have relied on costly manual construction for weather scenarios, an approach largely unchanged since the 1990s.
