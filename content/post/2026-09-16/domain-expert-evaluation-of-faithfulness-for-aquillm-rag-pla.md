# Domain-Expert Evaluation of Faithfulness for AquiLLM RAG Platform

A new paper presents a domain-expert evaluation of faithfulness for AquiLLM, an open-weight, offline RAG-LLM platform developed to support scientific research groups in preserving and using formal and tacit knowledge.

## 🔍 Overview

Faithfulness in the context of AquiLLM is defined as the extent to which generated responses remain grounded in retrieved scientific context without omissions or unsupported claims.

## ⚙️ Key details

Researchers conducted an astronomy case study to evaluate the platform across scientific analysis and retrieval tasks. The results indicate:

*   **High Reliability:** Most reliable on explicit retrieval-oriented questions grounded in the RAG collection.
*   **Performance Degradation:** Faithfulness decreases for queries that require ambiguity resolution or synthesis.
