# TypeSafe Launches Jev for Structured AI Decisions

TypeSafe has launched Jev, a new model specifically developed to provide fast, structured AI decisions.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Purpose**: Designed to return predefined, typed answers instead of code or chat.
* **Role**: Defined as a much narrower role than that of a general-purpose language model.
* **Benefits**: TypeSafe states the model can reduce cost and latency.
