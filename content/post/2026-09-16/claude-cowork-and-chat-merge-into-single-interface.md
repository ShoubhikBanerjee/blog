# Claude Cowork and Chat Merge Into Single Interface

Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude starting today.

## 🧩 How it works
* Users can provide quick questions or hand over reports.
* Claude continues working even after the user has closed their laptop.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Plans:** Rolling out to Pro and Max plans first.
* **Platforms:** Available in the Claude app on web, desktop, and mobile.
* **Timeline:** Rolling out over the coming weeks to new and existing users on these plans.
