# Syensqo Uses AI Agents to Develop Advanced Materials for AI Infrastructure

Syensqo, a global leader in specialty materials, is employing AI agents to accelerate the discovery and development of materials designed to support semiconductor and data center infrastructure.

## 💡 Why it matters
According to Mike Finelli, chief technology and innovation officer and chief North America officer at Syensqo, the AI boom is becoming a materials challenge. He states that AI is pushing semiconductors and data centers toward physical limits regarding:
* Performance
* Thermal management
* Electrical efficiency
* Reliability

## 🧩 How it works
Syensqo uses AI agents to navigate the "enormous universe of possible molecules" through the following process:
* Digitally synthesizing millions of potential molecular combinations.
* Predicting the sustainability and performance characteristics of these combinations.
* Narrowing the results to a smaller group for laboratory testing.

## ⚙️ Key details
Syensqo is developing various solutions to meet accumulating requirements for purity, chemical resistance, plasma resistance, high temperature, electrical performance, and long-term stability. These include:

| Material Application | Purpose/Source |
| :--- | :--- |
| High-voltage data center architectures | Specialized materials development |
| Semiconductor manufacturing | Advanced sealing materials |
| Direct immersion cooling | Thermal-management fluids |
| Data center energy-density/voltage | Materials developed for electric vehicles |

Finelli suggests a reinforcing cycle where AI helps develop materials that improve AI infrastructure, which then enables better AI to further accelerate materials discovery. This approach allows scientists to go "broader, deeper, and faster" while spending more time on complex engineering problems.
