# Mastering Prompt Engineering for AI Features in Amazon Quick

Amazon Quick is providing guidance on prompt engineering to improve the accuracy and reliability of its AI-powered features. This information is being released as a two-part series, with the first part focusing on universal principles and reusable frameworks applicable across all Quick components.

## 💡 Why it matters

The way prompts are structured directly shapes the quality of output when querying data through conversational analytics, authoring automation flows, or building custom agents. Specificity eliminates assumptions the AI would otherwise make. For example, replacing a vague request to "analyze customer data" with a detailed request defining metrics, timeframes, scope, and decision context transforms generic summaries into actionable intelligence.

## 🧩 How it works

To produce consistent, high-quality results, the following foundational principles are recommended:

*   **Be Specific**: Define metrics, timeframes, scope, analysis types, and decision contexts.
*   **Provide Business Context**: Include information such as who will use the output and what decisions it informs to calibrate the AI's response.
*   **Few-Shot Learning**: Show the AI concrete models of the desired output format rather than describing requirements in abstract terms.
*   **Shared Vocabulary**: Developing a shared prompt vocabulary allows successful framing patterns to become reusable assets across a team.

## ⚙️ Key details

For sophisticated enterprise interactions, Amazon Quick utilizes structured frameworks to ensure completeness and consistency:

| Framework | Application | Focus Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CRISPE** | Complex requests | Comprehensive template across Quick components |
| **RADAR** | Knowledge retrieval | Retrieval strategy, Analysis approach, Document targeting, Answer formation, and Reasoning transparency |
| **ARCHITECT** | Custom agents | Agent identity, Response parameters, Context/knowledge, Special cases, Interaction patterns, Tool/action usage, Ethical guidelines, Improvement, and Validation |

These frameworks support various Quick capabilities, including:

*   **Amazon Quick Research**: Draws from Quick Index, 200+ news outlets, and premium datasets (S&P Global, FactSet, IDC, US Patent data, and PubMed) to deliver structured reports with citations.
*   **Amazon Quick Flows**: Transforms plain-language descriptions into automated workflows supporting conditional branching, repeat loops, and user inputs.
*   **Amazon Quick Sight**: Enables conversational queries based on business questions, metrics, dimensions, time periods, visualization types, and additional analysis.
