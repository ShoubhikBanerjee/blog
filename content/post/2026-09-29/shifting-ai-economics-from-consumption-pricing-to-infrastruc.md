# Shifting AI Economics from Consumption Pricing to Infrastructure Ownership

As AI transitions from experimental pilots to production portfolios, enterprises are evaluating whether to continue using consumption-based pricing or treat AI infrastructure as a productive asset.

## 🔍 Overview
AI is moving into production portfolios comprising assistants, agentic applications, and retrieval-and-knowledge systems. According to Deloitte’s 2026 State of AI in the Enterprise, worker access to AI rose 5% in 2025, and the number of companies with at least 40% of their AI projects in production is expected to double within six months.

## ⚙️ Key details
While consumption pricing provides flexibility and limits commitment, it can create variable monthly costs that are difficult to forecast as workloads and model requirements change. Organizations face a "crossover point" where owning capacity becomes more economical than buying it one request at a time.

Factors influencing this crossover point include:
* The models being used
* Balance of input and output tokens
* Performance requirements
* System design
* Energy costs
* The required operating model

## 🧩 How it works
Different AI workloads create distinct cost profiles:

| Workload Type | Cost Profile Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- |
| Retrieval-heavy knowledge systems | May process far more context for every interaction compared to simple assistants |
| Agentic workflows | May involve repeated reasoning, retrieval, model calls, and tool use for a single business task |

When multiple workloads share infrastructure, enterprises can spread fixed costs across more productive use to improve the economics of ownership.

## 💡 Why it matters
Moving toward owned capacity allows a business to manage AI as a strategic infrastructure investment rather than a fluctuating monthly spend. However, this requires an operating model to ensure capacity creates value by:
* Bringing users and workloads on board quickly
* Governing AI use
* Reviewing utilization
* Identifying underutilized capacity
* Continually identifying high-value use cases
