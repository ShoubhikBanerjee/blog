# Walmart Denies Use of Personalized Pricing with Digital Shelf Labels

Walmart has stated it will not use personal information or time of day to change product prices, following reports from The Wall Street Journal.

## ⚙️ Key details
In a letter to customers, CEO John Furner clarified the purpose and limitations of the company's pricing strategies:

* **Digital Shelf Labels:** The rollout of these labels in all US stores by the end of the year is intended to save store associates time, not to dynamically change prices.
* **Pricing Constraints:** Prices will not be changed based on:
    * Personal income
    * Shopping history
    * Urgency
    * Perceived ability to pay
    * Time of day (e.g., a hot afternoon)
    * Sudden rushes for an item
* **AI Integration:** Walmart will not use conversations with its Sparky AI assistant to change prices.

## 💡 Why it matters
The company's reassurance comes amid increasing regulatory scrutiny:

* **Federal Level:** The Federal Trade Commission is developing a policy to clamp down on personalized pricing.
* **State Level:** 
    * New York requires retailers to disclose the use of algorithmic pricing.
    * New Jersey, Connecticut, and Maryland have moved to ban the use of personal data to change store prices.
