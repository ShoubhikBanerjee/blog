# Anthropic Releases Claude Sonnet 5.5 Model

Anthropic has released a new model called Sonnet 5.5.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Performance:** Runs 30%+ faster and costs up to 30% less for most work.
* **Pricing:** Priced the same as Sonnet 5.
* **Benchmarks:** Appears to beat Sonnet 5 on every benchmark.
* **Capabilities:** Appears almost as good as Opus 5.5 on some coding tasks, including viral 3D animation tricks.

## 🧩 Technical Observations
Sonnet 5.5 suffered from a bug also seen in Opus 5.5: when using "max" thinking effort, the model thought for 128,000 tokens (costing $1.28) before running out of tokens and failing to produce an SVG. Conversely, using thinking effort "xhigh" produced a pelican in 41 seconds at a cost of 5.74 cents.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Free Tier:** Sonnet 5.5 is now the model used for the free tier on claude.ai.
* **Upcoming:** Haiku 5.5 will be available "in the coming weeks".

## 💡 Why it matters
OpenAI's ChatGPT free tier uses Luna 5.6, which means Anthropic currently have a much more capable free offering.
