# Anthropic Reports First Discovery From New AI-Powered Molecular Biology Lab

Anthropic has announced that its recently launched molecular biology lab has made its first discovery using a system of Claude agents.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **System Composition**: 950 agents.
* **Process**: Claude agents read and conjecture about hard biology problems, while human scientists run experiments based on those reports.
* **Discovery**: After 21 hours, the system flagged a repeating pattern surrounding a known enzyme that Anthropic stated had not been catalogued before.

## 💡 Why it matters
Opinions on the discovery are divided among experts:
* **Support**: One perspective notes that whittling 200,000 candidates down to a few worth exploring is "legitimate scientific work."
* **Critique**: Biologist Lucas Harrington stated AI companies should "set the bar high now" so that the discovery of a fundamentally new biological mechanism is appreciated. Additionally, a post endorsed by the chair and CEO of Eli Lilly noted that finding clusters of genes and repeats is often the "easy part," while the hard part is "figuring out what the system actually does."
* **Controversy**: Mario Rodríguez Mestre, a biologist at the University of Copenhagen, stated his team had already discovered this pattern. While Anthropic denies it, Mestre questioned if the team learned from his conversations with Claude and said he is stopping all use of the tool.
