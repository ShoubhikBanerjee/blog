# Microsoft Research Introduces Quine Multimodal World Model for Biology

Microsoft Research has introduced Quine, a research effort to create a multimodal world model of biology and an interactive harness that connects models, scientific tools, literature, and researchers.

## 🔍 Overview
Quine is designed to work across various biological boundaries, reflecting a vision for a discovery system that evolves through scientific use. It consists of two primary components:
* **A world model of biology**: A system capable of representing the state of a biological system, predicting how that state evolves in response to interventions, and reasoning over the consequences of those interventions multiple steps into the future.
* **An interactive harness**: A system that connects the world model to orchestration and reasoning models, scientific tools, literature, and research teams.

## 🧩 How it works
The core world model learns shared representations across biological modalities and scales, including:
* Sequence
* Structure
* Function
* Cellular state
* Imaging data

By training across these modalities jointly, Quine can use evidence from one modality to inform predictions in another, capturing relationships that might be lost when separate single-domain specialist models are used. This integration allows researchers to generate predictions across modalities and reason about the consequences of biological interventions before wet-lab testing.

## ⚙️ Key details
Microsoft Research has applied Quine in collaboration with researchers at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard to study pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). Through this work, they have used the system to prioritize compounds predicted to drive therapeutic tumor-state shifts and validated several top-ranked candidates across multiple wet-lab assays.

Quine is experimental research technology intended only for research, not for clinical or medical use. Its outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and require review by qualified researchers and appropriate scientific and experimental validation.

## 🚀 Availability
* **Quine Fellows program**: A cohort of scientists will be given access to the system to accelerate their research and provide scientific feedback.
* **Future Access**: As the technology matures, Microsoft expects to expand access through products such as Microsoft Discovery.
