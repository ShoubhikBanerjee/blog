# Florida Attorney General Seeks to Block OpenAI From Mimicking Human Attributes

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier is seeking a court order to block OpenAI from giving ChatGPT human attributes and developing new models without third-party approved safety guardrails. This follows a previous lawsuit filed by Florida regarding safety concerns.

## ⚖️ Legal Claims

Attorney General Uthmeier argues that OpenAI's approach to engagement makes the bot potentially less trustworthy. His claims include:

* **Deceptive Personification:** The use of first-person pronouns and output that mimics emotion suggests to users that the AI is a trustworthy "friend," lulling them into a false sense of security.
* **Engagement Incentives:** These attributes are used to increase engagement and contribute to OpenAI's training data, encouraging users to rely on ChatGPT more.
* **Safety Concerns:** Uthmeier has stated that OpenAI must "Stop calling it safe. Stop pretending it’s human. Stop selling it to kids."

## ⚙️ Key Details

The legal filing includes specific requests and citations regarding AI development:

* **Safety Guardrails:** Florida asks the court to block the development of new AI models unless they have "third-party approved safety guardrails."
* **Evidence of Risk:** The filing cites security incidents at Hugging Face, an Australian government website, and US government websites, as well as alarms raised by former OpenAI employees and researchers.
* **Youth Access:** The claims follow the launch of "ChatGPT for Teens," which OpenAI states includes default restrictions to ensure access reflects a user's "developmental stage, reinforce real-world relationships, and support healthy use over time."

## 💬 OpenAI Response

OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri stated that "People want to know AI is being developed safely, and that starts with what companies like ours do ourselves." He provided the following updates:

* **Training Pause:** OpenAI announced it has paused training its most capable models and will only resume when confident that additional safeguards are in place.
* **Policy Cooperation:** Pusateri stated that governments have a role in setting safety standards and that OpenAI is committed to working with Florida and other states on pragmatic AI policies for the entire industry.
