# Microsoft Research Asia Opens First Southeast Asia Lab in Singapore

On July 24, 2025, Microsoft Research Asia – Singapore (MSRA – Singapore) opened as the first Microsoft research lab in Southeast Asia, joining existing MSRA locations in Beijing, Shanghai, Vancouver, and Tokyo.

## 🔍 Overview
The lab's work is organized around four primary pillars:
* Next-generation AI models and agentic systems
* Domain-specific AI for real-world impact
* AI-native research practices
* Ecosystem and talent development

## ⚙️ Key details
MSRA – Singapore is collaborating with various sectors and government entities to address specific operational challenges:

* **Healthcare:** Deploying research on multimodal healthcare AI and self-evolving diagnostic agents to support clinical decision-making, risk assessment, disease diagnosis, and personalized care.
* **Industry:** Working with organizations in education, technology, and financial services.
* **Government:** Engaging with the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Trade and Industry, and the Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB).

### Partnerships and Events
* **EDB:** Acted as a foundational partner, supporting the lab's establishment and joint PhD training via the Industrial Postgraduate Programme (IPP). In February 2026, EDB co-hosted an Executive Roundtable on AI in Logistics and Transportation.
* **IMDA:** Co-hosted the July 2025 "Microsoft Research Asia Singapore Day — Advancing Industrial AI" event.

## 💡 Why it matters
The lab aligns with Singapore's National AI Strategy 2.0 and strengthens ties with the local academic community. While MSRA has supported over 75 projects with Singapore institutions since 2004, the new lab initiated nine new projects in its first year with Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and National University of Singapore (NUS).

### Academic Collaborations
A five-year Framework Research Agreement with NUS announced in 2025 establishes shared priorities in data-intensive computing, spatial intelligence, societal AI, and healthcare.

| Researcher(s) | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mike Shou (NUS) and Xinxing Xu (MSRA) | Unified multimodal AI for healthcare |
| Yang You (NUS) and Furu Wei (MSRA) | Systems foundations for diffusion-based language models |
| Jialin Li (NUS) and MSRA | Verifiable machine learning systems and rigorous distributed agreement in multi-agent systems |

Additionally, the NUS–Microsoft Research Asia AI Summer School has reached more than 300 students since launching in 2025.
