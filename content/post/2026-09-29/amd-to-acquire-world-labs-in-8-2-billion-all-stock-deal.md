# AMD to Acquire World Labs in $8.2 Billion All-Stock Deal

AMD has announced an all-stock acquisition of World Labs, an AI research lab co-founded by Dr. Fei-Fei Li, for approximately $8.2 billion.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Deal Value:** Approximately $8.2 billion
- **Deal Structure:** All-stock
- **Expected Closing:** By the end of the year

## 🔍 Overview
World Labs launched in 2024 and reached a valuation of $1 billion within a few months. The company developed the following product:

| Product | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| Marble | A world generation model that allows users to create interactive 3D worlds from a prompt |

## 💡 Why it matters
AMD stated the acquisition will strengthen its "ability to develop AI hardware, software and systems around the needs of emerging models and applications."

Following the acquisition:
- Dr. Fei-Fei Li will become EVP and chief scientist at AMD, reporting to CEO Lisa Su.
- The World Labs team will "continue to focus on advancing AI model research."

This acquisition follows other significant AI deals, including Nvidia's announcement earlier this month that it would acquire Hugging Face for nearly $13 billion.
