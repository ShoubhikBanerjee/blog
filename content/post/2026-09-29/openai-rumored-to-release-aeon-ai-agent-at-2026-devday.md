# OpenAI Rumored to Release Aeon AI Agent at 2026 DevDay

OpenAI is rumored to announce a new continuously running, consumer-facing AI agent called Aeon during its upcoming 2026 DevDay event. This move follows a period where OpenAI's visible progress in the agentic AI space has lagged behind competitors.

## 🔍 Overview
AI agents are platforms designed to perform practical tasks over time. Current agents in the industry are being used for:
* Booking activities and organizing group trips
* Paying bills and canceling subscriptions
* Setting up DMV appointments
* Filling out paperwork for doctor visits

## ⚙️ Key details
Aeon is expected to be underpinned by GPT-6 Astra, a frontier model that OpenAI president Greg Brockman stated contributes to the feeling that "we are now in the AGI era." This foundation could make Aeon more powerful in several areas:
* Software engineering
* Scientific research
* Computer use
* Cybersecurity tasks

To assist in development, OpenAI currently employs Peter Steinberger, the creator of OpenClaw.

## 💡 Why it matters
OpenAI enters a crowded market of 24/7 assistants. To succeed, Aeon would need to combine the utility of OpenClaw, the ease of setup of Muse, and the chat simplicity of Instinct, while addressing security risks that have affected previous agents.

| Agent | Notable Attributes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Muse (Meta) | Topped App Store charts; 600,000 daily US active users; integrates with Meta services |
| Gemini Spark (Google) | 24/7 consumer agent; over 30 external partners including Uber, Spotify, and Dropbox |
| Instinct | Known for ease of use; accessible via iMessage and other chat apps |
| OpenClaw | Transitioned agents from proof of concept to practical success |

## 🚀 Availability
While Aeon is the rumored focus for Tuesday's event, other potential announcements include:
* Updates on AI hardware devices developed with Jony Ive
* A hardware update
* An advancement of GPT-6, such as Astra 6.1
