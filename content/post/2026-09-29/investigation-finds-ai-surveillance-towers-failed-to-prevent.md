# Investigation Finds AI Surveillance Towers Failed to Prevent Border Deaths

An investigation by MIT Technology Review has documented that over a thousand people died in areas monitored by US border surveillance towers, including those equipped with AI technology.

## 🔍 Overview
Over the last 25 years, the US has invested billions of dollars into a "virtual wall" of surveillance towers along the southern border. These towers were intended to:
* Detect and apprehend border crossers
* Save lives

## ⚙️ Key details
Despite the investment, the MIT Technology Review investigation found that over a thousand people moved through these watched areas without being apprehended or reached, ultimately resulting in their deaths. Some of these individuals were under the watch of AI-powered towers designed to spot people automatically.
