# Zimmer Biomet Partners with NAACP to Support Diversity and Equality

Zimmer Biomet has partnered with the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP) to end racial injustice and advance diversity and equality.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Zimmer Biomet is committing $2 million over the next three years through the Foundation to support partnered efforts with the NAACP.

## 🔍 Overview
- Team Members: ~17,000
- Global Offices: 30+
- Product Reach: 100+ Countries
