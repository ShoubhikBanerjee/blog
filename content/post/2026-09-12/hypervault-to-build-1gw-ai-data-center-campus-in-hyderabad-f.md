# HyperVault to Build 1GW AI Data Center Campus in Hyderabad for TCS

HyperVault, the AI infrastructure subsidiary of Tata Consultancy Services, has secured 264 acres of land in Hyderabad to build a campus with a capacity of up to 1GW. Deepesh Kiran Nanda, CEO and MD of HyperVault’s AI Data Center, outlined the campus as a facility designed for frontier AI and hyperscale compute.

## 🔍 Overview
The move positions the new planned data center site as one of India’s largest concentrations of AI compute. The campus is intended for frontier AI companies and hyperscalers, providing the specialized infrastructure required for high-density compute requirements.

## ⚙️ Key Details
| Attribute | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| Location | Hyderabad, India |
| Land Size | 264 acres |
| Maximum Capacity | 1GW |
| Infrastructure Focus | High-density GPU deployments |
| Parent Company | Tata Consultancy Services |

## 🧩 Capabilities
The Hyderabad campus will support various advanced computing workloads to meet the needs of the AI industry:
* High-density GPU deployments.
* Frontier AI training.
* AI inference.
* Advanced computing workloads.
* Hyperscale compute operations.
