# Capcom outlines AI-assisted evolution of RE Engine at RE: 2026

Capcom’s RE: 2026 Open Conference featured a presentation by programmer Satoshi Ishida titled “The Outlook and Future of the REX Project, Further Evolving the RE Engine for the Next Generation.” In the talk the studio explained its plan to bring AI into its development pipelines.

## 🔍 Overview
- Capcom laid out its plans to embrace AI in ‘development workflows.’
- The presentation highlighted a “future where we create games together with AI.”

## ⚙️ Challenges in large‑scale game development
- Ishida noted the challenges facing studios producing games at the scale of Resident Evil, where even simple tasks can become extremely time‑consuming.

## 🤖 Solution: AI integration
- The solution Ishida said is “successfully integrating AI technology into development workflows.”
- Capcom is preparing for a ‘future where we create games together with AI.’

## 📈 Future roadmap
- Ishida laid out a plan to turn RE Engine slowly, and incrementally, into an “AI‑generation game engine.”
- The roadmap builds toward “a future where we create games together with AI,” according to IGN’s translation.

## 📢 Current stance on AI‑generated assets
- Capcom has said previously that it will not use AI‑generated assets in its games, and instead would focus on using the tech to make development more efficient.
- Ishida’s comments seem to line up with that stance while leaving the door open to more extensive use.
