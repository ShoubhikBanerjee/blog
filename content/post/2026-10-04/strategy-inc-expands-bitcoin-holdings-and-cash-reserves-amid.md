# Strategy Inc. expands Bitcoin holdings and cash reserves amid market volatility

Strategy Inc. (NASDAQ: MSTR) has been in the spotlight as its stock volatility reflects the interplay between Bitcoin price movements, analyst targets, and corporate treasury actions.

## 📈 Market Activity
- Bitcoin climbed above $86,000‑$87,000, boosting Strategy as a leveraged Bitcoin proxy.
- Investors bought 584,635 call options, about 21% above average daily call volume, indicating positioning for further upside while also raising short‑term volatility risk.
- Despite the rally, the stock later decreased as investors took profits after a sharp monthly advance.

## 💰 Treasury Moves
- Strategy disclosed the purchase of 1,665 Bitcoin for roughly $143 million, bringing total holdings to **847,666 BTC**.
- The company repurchased about $152 million of its STRC preferred stock, signaling ongoing capital‑management activity.
- It holds **$5.02 billion** in cash reserves to cover preferred dividends and debt interest.
- A transfer of 3,568 BTC (≈ $300 million) between wallets was recorded; the move was not identified as a sale but raised questions about custody, financing, or treasury‑management activity.

## 📊 Analyst Outlook
- Citigroup raised its MSTR price target to **$240**, citing a higher Bitcoin forecast of **$113,000** and improved valuation assumptions.
- Bitcoin Standard author Saifedean Ammous noted that rival Bitcoin‑treasury companies may struggle to match Strategy’s scale, cash reserves, and funding model.

## ⚠️ Risks & Index Exposure
- A proposed MSCI “non‑operating company” rule could remove Strategy from major indexes, potentially triggering up to **$2.8 billion** in MSCI‑linked forced selling.
- The company’s dependence on Bitcoin prices, preferred‑dividend obligations, and financing conditions makes the stock highly sensitive to crypto reversals.
