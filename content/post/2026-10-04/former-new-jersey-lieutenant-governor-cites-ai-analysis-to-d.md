# Former New Jersey Lieutenant Governor Cites AI Analysis to Dispute Harassment Investigation Findings

Former New Jersey Lieutenant Governor Dale Caldwell is using outputs from artificial intelligence to challenge the results of an investigation that led to his resignation. Following his departure on September 25th, which occurred after an investigation found he had sexually harassed a staffer and violated ethics rules, Caldwell has claimed that multiple AI agents support his assertion of innocence.

## 🔍 Overview

* Dale Caldwell resigned on September 25th following findings of sexual harassment and repeated ethics violations.
* The former lieutenant governor has engaged in media appearances, including an interview on NJ PBS with host Rob Nelson, to clear his name.
* Caldwell argues that he is being unfairly targeted and asserts that multiple AI platforms back his position.

## 🧩 How it works

Caldwell reported using various AI tools to evaluate the investigation's findings:

* He processed the report through multiple AI platforms, a process he described as having "AIed" the document.
* During his NJ PBS interview, he stated that he submitted the report to AI 59 times.
* For each submission, he asked the AI, "What would your findings be?"

## ⚙️ Key details

* Caldwell claims that in every instance, the AIs failed to find that sexual harassment occurred.
* Observations regarding the behavior of AI chatbots suggest they are notoriously sycophantic and frequently provide users with the information they want to hear.
* It is noted as highly unlikely that Caldwell would have wanted the AI to confirm findings that he engaged in sexual harassment.
