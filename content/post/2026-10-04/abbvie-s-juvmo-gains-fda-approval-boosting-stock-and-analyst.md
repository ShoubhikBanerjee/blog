# AbbVie’s Juvmo Gains FDA Approval, Boosting Stock and Analyst Sentiment

AbbVie (NYSE: ABBV) announced FDA approval for its new Parkinson’s disease pill, Juvmo, with a commercial launch slated for October 2026. The approval has sparked positive moves in the stock and analyst outlook.

## 🔍 Overview
- Juvmo expands AbbVie’s neuroscience portfolio beyond its immunology franchise.
- The drug is positioned as a new growth source for the company.

## 📈 Stock Performance
- Shares have risen **25.4% over six months**.
- Trading near a 12‑month high and above both the 50‑day and 200‑day moving averages.
- Analysts raised the consensus price target **10.06%** to MX$5,190.34.
- One analysis suggests AbbVie could be **41% undervalued** after the Juvmo approval (valuation models are subjective).

## 💊 Pipeline Highlights
- **RINVOQ**: Long‑term data show continued improvement in facial and total‑body repigmentation for non‑segmental vitiligo patients through week 76.
- **Venetoclax AML Study**: Ongoing Greek study provides incremental development but no definitive efficacy or regulatory catalyst.

## 📅 Near‑Term Catalysts
- AbbVie will report Q3 2026 results **before the market opens on October 30**, offering a platform to assess Skyrizi, RINVOQ growth, post‑Humira transition, and management outlook.

## ⚖️ Risks & Negative Sentiment
- Participation in a U.S. government pilot for a new prescription‑drug rebate system may create uncertainty around net drug prices and margins.
- Elevated valuation, Humira revenue erosion, and competition remain concerns that could limit upside despite newer product traction.
