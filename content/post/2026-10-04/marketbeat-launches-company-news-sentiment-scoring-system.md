# MarketBeat launches company news sentiment scoring system

MarketBeat has introduced a sentiment scoring system that assigns a numeric rating to each news headline and aggregates those ratings into company‑level sentiment scores.

## 🔍 Overview
- Each headline receives a score ranging from **2 (good news)** to **-2 (bad news)**.
- Scores are averaged over the most recent **7 days** to produce a **company news sentiment score**.
- The system tracks headlines from **hundreds of news outlets** and tags them by company.

## ⚙️ How it works
- Headlines are collected in real time from more than one hundred trusted media outlets.
- A numeric score is assigned to each headline according to its perceived tone.
- The platform calculates the **average sentiment** for each company based on the scores from the last seven days.
- A separate chart compares the **number of articles** about a company in the last seven days with the **average weekly article count**.

## 📈 Why it matters
- The sentiment scores help investors identify companies receiving **positive press** versus those under **negative coverage**.
- By showing article volume trends, the tool highlights changes in media attention that may precede market movements.

## 🚀 Availability
- The sentiment data is available through MarketBeat’s platform, which also offers a **free daily newsletter** for the latest news and ratings on companies and their competitors.
- Users can subscribe via the website or receive **SMS alerts** (available in multiple countries) that include stock alerts, news stories, and partner offers.
