# Sightlogix Launches NS12 and TC12 Megapixel Thermal Cameras for Large Scale Perimeter Security

Sightlogix has announced the Sightsensor NS12 and TC12, which are among the first fixed thermal cameras on the market to feature a 1280×1024 imager. These devices are designed to protect large sites and critical infrastructure perimeters using high-resolution thermal imaging and edge-based AI classification.

## 🔍 Overview
The higher resolution of the NS12 and TC12 allows each camera to protect approximately 2.5 times the area of a traditional 640×480 system. By increasing coverage per device, the system reduces the total infrastructure cost for wide-area sites.

*   **Target Applications:** Utility substations, data centres, airports, ports, and wide-area campuses.
*   **Infrastructure Impact:** Fewer cameras require fewer poles and fewer cable runs.
*   **Detection Range:** Both cameras deliver AI classification across the full inbound detection range.

## 🧩 How it works
The cameras are powered by the Sightlogix Gen7 edge processing platform, which includes a dedicated AI core. This platform delivers 20× the AI throughput of prior generations, providing the necessary processing headroom for megapixel thermal AI detection.

| Feature | Capability |
| :--- | :--- |
| Thermal Resolution | 1280×1024 imager |
| AI Throughput | 20× increase over previous generations |
| TC12 Dual-Stream | Processes 1280×1024 thermal and 4K visible streams simultaneously |
| On-Device Validation | Real-time cross-validation between streams with no cloud dependency |

On the TC12 model, the Gen7 platform runs independent AI models on both the thermal and visible streams simultaneously to cross-validate detections entirely on-device.

## 💡 Why it matters
The system is engineered to address the specific scale of critical infrastructure. “Critical infrastructure perimeters don’t fit in a small box. The NS12 and TC12 were built for exactly that challenge — protecting more perimeter with fewer devices, with the AI classification performance and trusted alerts that operators can rely on,” commented Jim Hahn, CEO, Sightlogix. The company indicates these products are the result of more than 20 years of engineering for this application.
