# Ukrainian Robot Offensive Operation Vivaldi Reverses Russian Gains in Donbas

Ukraine has launched "Operation Vivaldi," a robotic counteroffensive in the eastern Donbas region that has reportedly reversed more than a year of Russian territorial gains. According to security experts, the operation has dealt a strategic blow to Russian territorial ambitions, particularly around Lyman in northern Donetsk Oblast.

## 🔍 Overview

Operation Vivaldi represents a shift in modern warfare by systematically replacing human personnel with robotic systems. Since starting in May, the offensive has achieved several milestones:

*   **Territory Retaken:** A total of 176 square kilometers have been liberated, including 51 square kilometers during the third phase.
*   **Deployment:** Ukraine's Third Army Corps deployed large bomber drones and remote-controlled robots behind enemy lines.
*   **Enemy Losses:** The latest phase of fighting resulted in over 2,000 Russian losses and more than 250 soldiers taken prisoner.
*   **Tactical Success:** The operation has moved Ukrainian forces into better defendable positions, making it harder for Russian forces to encircle target cities.

## 🧩 How it works

Security experts and defense advisors have detailed a specific multi-step approach used during the Lyman offensive to catch Russian forces off guard:

| System/Entity | Role in Operation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Large bomber drones | Used to secure air control and drop other robotic platforms behind enemy lines |
| Unmanned ground vehicles | Remotely controlled platforms that attack troops from behind to minimize personnel risk |
| Third Army Corps | The primary military unit carrying out the counteroffensive operations |
| Remote-controlled robots | Deployed to replace human warriors in the most dangerous frontline tasks |

## ⚙️ Key details

Patrick Bolder, a defense expert at the Hague Center for Strategic Studies (HCSS), noted that Ukrainian forces masterly secured control of the air in the Lyman area, denying Russia the use of its own drones. This allowed Ukraine to deploy their own drones to deliver ground robotic systems behind enemy lines. 

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has stated that replacing warriors with ground robotic systems is a "particularly promising area" for the country's defense. The goal is to scale up these capabilities so that drones, rather than soldiers, carry out the most dangerous tasks at the front.

## 💡 Why it matters

Serhii Kuzan, chairman of the Ukrainian Security and Cooperation Center, stated that the use of these systems has changed the logic of combat. Where hundreds of soldiers were previously required, remotely controlled platforms are increasingly being used to achieve the same objectives with minimal personnel losses. 

While security experts clarify that the offensive is not yet a decisive breakthrough, it sends a powerful message to both Moscow and international partners regarding the innovative capabilities of the Ukrainian military. The operation has forced Russian forces to focus on defending positions rather than meeting their political leadership's objectives of capturing the entire Donetsk region.
