# White House Launches Super Intelligence Force AI Task Force Led by Jay Clayton

The White House has established a new artificial intelligence task force, named the "Super Intelligence Force," to evaluate the risks and opportunities of the rapidly advancing technology. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton has been appointed to lead the panel and deliver federal oversight recommendations within 120 days.

## 🔍 Overview
The creation of the task force follows a Tuesday meeting between President Donald Trump and prominent technology executives, including OpenAI President Greg Brockman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. During the meeting, President Trump secured a "morally binding" agreement from the heads of major US tech companies to implement internal safety monitoring and independent external reviews of their AI systems.

## ⚙️ Key Details
The "Super Intelligence Force" will evaluate how existing laws and potential congressional actions can address emerging AI threats. Key elements of the task force's mandate include:
* **Oversight Recommendations:** Delivering recommendations on federal oversight within a 120-day window.
* **Protecting Innovation:** Examining how existing laws might address emerging threats without imposing regulatory burdens that hinder competition or stifle innovation.
* **Industry Collaboration:** Collaborating directly with industry leaders to identify system vulnerabilities.
* **Voluntary Standards:** Developing response strategies for advanced AI threats while maintaining American technological leadership through voluntary standards rather than mandatory federal restrictions.

## 🛡️ Incident Reporting Review
The task force is also charged with reviewing and improving security protocols. The panel will:
* Review current protocols for reporting breaches, hacks, and model jailbreaks to federal authorities.
* Recommend improvements to these reporting procedures under existing legal frameworks.
