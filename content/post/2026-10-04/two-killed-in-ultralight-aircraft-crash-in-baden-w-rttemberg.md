# Two killed in ultralight aircraft crash in Baden‑Württemberg

A two‑seater ultralight aircraft crashed into a vineyard in the southwestern German state of Baden‑Württemberg on Sunday, killing both occupants.

## 📍 Overview
- The aircraft was approaching a nearby airfield shortly after noon and aborted its landing for unknown reasons.
- It clipped a fence, collided with a tree, and crashed into a vineyard near the town of Gundelsheim, where it caught fire.
- Both people on board were found dead in the burned‑out aircraft.

## 🕵️ Investigation
- Police reported no indications that anyone else was on board.
- Authorities are examining whether pilot error or a technical fault caused the crash.
