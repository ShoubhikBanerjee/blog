# EPAM Systems Seeks Lead AI/ML Consultant for AWS Agentic AI

EPAM Systems is hiring a Lead AI/ML Consultant to lead the AI/ML workstream for an ADP account engagement, focusing on architecting and building agentic AI and generative AI solutions using AWS services.

## 🔍 Overview
This role is responsible for architecting and building multi-agent AI systems and generative AI solutions on AWS, partnering with Technical Business Analysts to deliver secure, scalable capabilities.

## 🧩 How it works
The consultant will build solutions using the following approaches and tools:
- **Agentic AI Systems:** Architect multi-agent systems covering orchestration patterns, agent-to-agent delegation, tool/function calling, and memory management
- **Amazon Bedrock:** Develop solutions using Amazon Bedrock Agents, Knowledge Bases, Guardrails, and Flows
- **Agentic Frameworks:** Deploy using LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, or comparable tools
- **RAG Systems:** Architect Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems with vector stores like OpenSearch, Kendra, and Pinecone
- **Prompt Engineering:** Create and refine prompts for LLM use cases, including evaluation workflows and hallucination reduction
- **LLM Integration:** Integrate with providers such as Claude, Amazon Nova, and Llama through Amazon Bedrock
- **Serverless Components:** Connect with Lambda, Step Functions, API Gateway, S3, and DynamoDB
- **Security & Compliance:** Establish guardrails, PII handling protocols, and enterprise compliance measures
- **Cost Optimization:** Architect token-efficient designs and apply cost governance strategies

## ⚙️ Key requirements
| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Minimum experience | 5 years of relevant experience |
| Leadership experience | At least 1 year leading and managing teams |
| Agentic workflows | Background in building multi-agent workflows for orchestration, delegation, and autonomous task execution |
| Amazon Bedrock | Direct experience developing and deploying with Bedrock agents, knowledge bases, and guardrails |
| LangChain/LangGraph | Applied experience constructing and coordinating LLM-powered applications |
| RAG systems | Experience architecting systems to anchor LLM outputs in accurate data |
| Python | Advanced skills for building AI/ML applications, integrating models, and automation |
| Communication | English skills at B2 level or higher |
