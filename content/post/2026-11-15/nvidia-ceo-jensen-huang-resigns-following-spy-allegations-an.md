# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Following Spy Allegations and Board Investigation

Jensen Huang has resigned as CEO of Nvidia following reports of a Chinese spy plot and the disclosure of multiple extramarital affairs. The resignation, announced on November 12, concludes a series of developments involving national security concerns, board investigations, and a significant decline in the company's stock value.

## 🔍 Overview
According to The New York Times, Jensen Huang was targeted by a Chinese spy plot involving Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based data center company Megaspeed. While the CEO and company spokespeople characterized the relationship as normal, reports on November 6 indicated that China’s Ministry of State Security specifically recruited Alice Huang to spy on the executive. U.S. officials suspected she was passing information regarding Nvidia’s hardware designs or White House deliberations over trade restrictions directly to Beijing. Megaspeed had previously been investigated by the U.S. Commerce Department for allegedly smuggling advanced AI chips into China.

## ⚙️ Key Details
The resignation followed several internal disclosures and reports regarding the CEO's conduct:

*   **Admitted Affairs:** Jensen Huang admitted to an extramarital affair that raised national security concerns. A second affair was subsequently reported with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of health care and a direct report.
*   **Misleading Leadership:** According to Reuters, Huang misled general counsel Tim Teter and chief marketing officer Alison Wagonfeld about the nature of his relationship with Alice Huang.
*   **Internal Reactions:** Bloomberg reported that before resigning, Huang appeared "broken and shattered," telling associates, "I’m a piece of shit."
*   **Corporate Structure:** Huang managed the company with an unusual structure of approximately 60 direct reports and had no established succession plan prior to his departure.

## 📊 Corporate Impact
Following these developments, Nvidia’s stock price fell from an all-time high of $239.24 on October 6 to $162.85 by November 13. The company is currently facing a shareholder lawsuit claiming it deceived investors. Despite the leadership transition, Nvidia continues to manage significant capital, including $99 billion in AI lab investments and plans for $500 billion in data center infrastructure financing. 

## 💡 Why it matters
The appointment of Harvey Jones as the company's first board chair signals a shift in Nvidia’s corporate governance. Interim CEO Colette Kress described the transition as an "extraordinarily difficult period" for the company. The board has initiated a search for a permanent successor, evaluating both internal and external candidates to lead the company forward.
