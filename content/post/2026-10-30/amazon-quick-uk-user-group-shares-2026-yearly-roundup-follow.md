# Amazon Quick UK User Group Shares 2026 Yearly Roundup Following Three Meetups

The Amazon Quick User Group – United Kingdom has released its 2026 yearly roundup, highlighting the progress and momentum across all three quarterly meetups held since the community's launch.

## 🔍 Overview
The UK Amazon Quick User Group is a local community of Amazon Quick users in the United Kingdom who meet quarterly to exchange knowledge, share best practices, learn through hands-on sessions, and discuss their experiences. All three events took place at the Amazon UK Corporate Office in Shoreditch, London, drawing over 150 attendees with focus centered on agentic AI and Amazon Quick.

## ⚙️ Key details
The group's quarterly gatherings evolved across each session:

- **Q1**: Focused on introductions and product demonstrations for the community's inaugural gathering.
- **Q2**: Included an icebreaker quiz alongside a focused hands-on workshop covering Amazon Quick Flows and automation.
- **Q3**: Featured a specialist panel, a group quiz, and a dedicated networking session at the end.

At each meetup, participants engaged in a 75-minute hands-on workshop starting around 9:45 AM, working inside provisioned sandbox accounts to test tools and features.

## 🛠️ Tools and capabilities
Attendees participated in hands-on workshop sessions testing several tools and features:

- **Amazon Quick Chat Agents**: Created custom chat agents without writing code to connect to tools, access data, and take actions on behalf of users.
- **Amazon Quick Desktop**: Ran directly on attendees' laptops, connecting to local files and using a connector to access Outlook.
- **Amazon Quick Apps**: Converted plain-language descriptions into functional web applications.
- **Amazon Quick Flows & Automate**: Automated processes across workflows.
- **Amazon Quick Research**: Reduced the time required to complete research tasks.
