# OpenAI releases hundreds of AI‑generated math results, Sam Altman acknowledges impact

Sam Altman told the Cheeky Pint podcast that humanity "deserves fundamental knowledge" and that the world should not be left waiting on major scientific discoveries. His comments come as OpenAI pushes out a flood of AI‑generated mathematical results, many of which researchers say are hard to follow.

## 🔍 Overview
- OpenAI has released **hundreds of mathematical results** this month.
- The results span topics such as **Yang‑Mills theory** and the **Navier‑Stokes equations**, both famous problems in physics and mathematics.
- Altman described the effort as a deliberate probe of the limits of OpenAI’s models.

## 📊 Scale of the effort
- **Internal Slack channel**: "Model Name‑Hold My Beer" where employees "were just throwing that at the hardest problems they could."
- **Budget**: OpenAI has spent "in the order of hundreds of millions of dollars" on mathematical problems, including around **$10 million** ("maybe a little more") on its Navier‑Stokes solution.
- **Future output**: When asked if more than the released hundreds of proofs exist, Altman replied: *"We do. Of course."*

## 🧾 Transparency & Communication
- Most releases come with only brief write‑ups that researchers describe as “poorly written and hard to follow.”
- Details for the claimed **Yang‑Mills** result have **not been released publicly**.
- OpenAI says the limited disclosure is based on recommendations from a **hand‑picked group of expert reviewers**.

## 💬 Altman's Statements
- "I think, on net, the world deserves to understand how the universe works."
- "I feel unequivocal on this point: Humanity deserves fundamental knowledge, and we should not be sitting on a major fundamental science discovery."
- "The world deserves that."
- "But I do have empathy for the method."
- "We got out of control for a short period of time, but that was deliberate and necessary to probe the limits of its AI models."

## ⚙️ Future Outlook
- Altman confirmed that OpenAI **continues to generate additional proofs** beyond those already released.
- The company’s aggressive internal experimentation suggests further large‑scale mathematical work will be pursued, despite current criticism over clarity and communication.
