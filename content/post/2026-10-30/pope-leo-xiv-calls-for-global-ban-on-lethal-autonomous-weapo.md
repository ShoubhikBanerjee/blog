# Pope Leo XIV Calls for Global Ban on Lethal Autonomous Weapons

In a Friday papal address, Pope Leo XIV urged United Nations member states to take concrete steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons and called for the disarmament of AI systems.

## 📜 Overview
- Pope Leo XIV says AI systems should be “disarmed,” adding that humans “have the responsibility to guide, change and, when necessary, reject its applications.”
- He repeatedly stresses that “Machines are tools, not persons.”
- The address reflects many of the ideas Pope Leo laid out in his first major papal document in May, which highlighted “safeguarding the human person in the time of artificial intelligence.”

## 🛡️ Call for Disarmament
- He encourages UN member states to “take concrete steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons.”
- He calls for the disarmament of AI, warning against granting AI systems rights or legal personhood.
- He reminds that “Decisions affecting human life, freedom, and the use of force must always remain subject to effective, informed, and responsible human oversight.”

## 📖 Moral Reasoning
- Pope Leo argues that machines must remain tools and that ultimate authority over life‑and‑death decisions must stay with humans.
- He cites the 2024 statement by Pope Francis, who said, “No machine should ever choose to take the life of a human being.”

## 🌐 International Implications
- He calls on international institutions to safeguard the common good from AI‑related risks.
- He urges the adoption of “shared standards, independent oversight and clear mechanisms of accountability so that decisions concerning the development and use of AI can be examined, challenged when necessary, and accompanied by appropriate remedies for any harm caused.”
