# AWS re:Invent 2026 to Showcase AWS Continuum and Security for AI Agents

The AWS Security, Identity, and Governance team has announced the security session lineup for AWS re:Invent in Las Vegas, running from November 30 to December 4, 2026. The event will showcase new developments in AWS Continuum, a service designed to automate security workflows, alongside sessions on governance and authentication for autonomous AI agents.

## 🔍 Overview
AWS re:Invent 2026 features nearly 120 security sessions across breakouts, chalk talks, workshops, code talks, builders' sessions, and lightning talks. Security sessions begin Monday morning and run through Friday, with Tuesday and Wednesday sessions distributed across the Wynn, MGM Grand, and Caesars Palace. On Thursday, December 3, the Wynn venue hosts a full day of advanced security sessions with 78 sessions from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, the majority of which are at the 300 level or above. This includes 12 workshops, 11 builders' sessions, and 22 chalk talks.

## 🎤 Keynotes and Featured Speakers
Several key leadership presentations are scheduled for the event:
- **Chet Kapoor** (AWS VP of Security, Search, and Observability) presents "The Future of Security" at 11:00 AM.
- **Amy Herzog** (AWS Chief Information Security Officer) presents "The Old Security Playbook Is Dead" at 1:00 PM.
- **Eric Brandwine** (VP/Distinguished Engineer, AWS Security) presents "Operationalizing security for the future with best practices today" at 3:00 PM.

The Thursday keynote runs from 8:30 to 10:00 AM at The Venetian and is also streamed online.
