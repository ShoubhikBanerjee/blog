# Amazon Polly Adds Voice Cloning Feature

Amazon Polly now offers a gated release of voice cloning, letting users generate a synthetic voice from a few minutes of recordings.

## 🔍 Overview
- Voice cloning in Amazon Polly is now generally available as a gated release.

## 🧩 How it works
- Provide a few minutes of audio recordings from a speaker who has agreed to have their voice cloned.
- Amazon Polly builds a cloned voice in that speaker’s voice.
- The first few seconds are a real recording and then the cloned voice takes over with new words.
- Example:
  "Hello, I’m Trevor, from Science."
  Cloned voice: "That first sentence was my real voice. This is my cloned voice, created from a few minutes of my recordings. It can read any text you give it."

## ⚙️ Key Details
- The cloned voices run on the Amazon Polly generative engine.
- They deliver similar naturalness and accuracy as the rest of the generative voices.
- They are automatically encrypted and access‑controlled: only you can access your cloned voices.
- Building a cloned voice is free; usage is charged on a pay‑as‑you‑go basis.
- Amazon Polly voice cloning supports seven languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, and Hindi.
- Each cloned voice speaks the language it was recorded in.

## 🚀 Availability
- Available in all AWS Regions supported by the Amazon Polly generative engine, including the AWS GovCloud (US) Region.
- Access is already enabled for select Amazon Polly customers.
- Others can reach out to their AWS account team or apply for access.
- You can send recordings directly in the CreateVoice request or upload them on the console.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Enables a consistent on‑brand voice across IVR, mobile apps, in‑store announcements, advertising, and connected devices.
- Allows on‑demand generation of audio without new studio sessions.
- Facilitates creation of audiobooks, courses, podcasts, game characters, and videos with multiple custom voices.
- Maintains a recognizable voice identity across a content library.
- Provides a natural, human‑sounding voice for reading experiences, assistive tools, and personalized interactions.
- You confirm the speaker’s consent each time you create a voice.
