# SBC Medical Group Exposes Data of 1.6 Million Individuals

SBC Medical Group disclosed in a U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filing that two separate cybersecurity incidents resulted in the unauthorized exposure of sensitive customer and employee information, affecting a total of 1.6 million people.

## ⚠️ The Breach
The company, a clinic management firm primarily operating in Japan with a limited U.S. presence and Nasdaq listing (SBC), revealed that the incidents impacted its Japan-based businesses. The breach was identified on November 16, 2026, when unauthorized access occurred to certain databases within a website system managed by the company’s Japanese subsidiary.

The data affected by this database incident included:
- Basic personal information belonging to 1,624,000 people.
- 255,416 reservation requests.
- 217,769 inquiries submitted to SBC websites.
- Thousands of treatment description records, image filenames, and customer reviews.
- Approximately 20,000 employee account records containing names, IDs, and passwords.
- Bank account and tax information for a subset of 459 employees.

## 🏥 The Tribeau Compromise
Simultaneously on November 16, SBC was notified of a second incident involving unauthorized access to clinic administrator accounts on Tribeau, a third-party booking platform. This breach affected reservation information for 12,641 people and accessed treatment names, dates, and fees for 6,804 patients who received treatment at 56 clinics using the service.

## 📊 Current Status
SBC confirmed that the attackers extracted customer information but stated that they have not confirmed unauthorized access to electronic medical records or information from the mySBC reservation site. The method of intrusion and full scope remain under investigation. As of the November 18 filing, the company had not seen evidence of misuse of the exposed data and could not estimate the financial impact of the incidents.
