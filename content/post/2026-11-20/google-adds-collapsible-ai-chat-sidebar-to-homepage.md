# Google adds collapsible AI chat sidebar to homepage

Google is updating its homepage to include AI searches by adding a collapsible sidebar that allows users to view and continue their AI chats. This change introduces a new way to access recent AI Mode activities directly from the Google homepage, moving beyond the traditional single search bar.

## 🔍 Overview
The update integrates AI chat functionality directly into the Google homepage experience. Previously, users accessed AI features through the AI Mode button in the search bar, which opened a chatbot-like search experience. The new interface adds a persistent sidebar to manage these interactions.

## 🧩 How it works
Users can open the sidebar by clicking a gray bar that appears on the left side of the screen. From there, the interface displays a list of recent searches made using AI Mode. The sidebar provides tools to manage these conversations and initiate new interactions.

## ⚙️ Key details
The new sidebar includes the following features:
*   View a list of recent searches made using AI Mode
*   Start a new thread
*   Search existing threads
*   Connect a Gemini Notebook

This update follows other recent changes to Google's search box, which in May was updated to accommodate longer queries and include AI-powered autocomplete. Last year, Google brought its AI Mode button to the search bar.

## 🚀 Availability
The new homepage layout is rolling out globally on desktop. 

## 💡 Why it matters
Robby Stein, Google’s vice president of product, stated that the option is being rolled out because “people are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done.” Stein added that “in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web.”
