# Amazon launches Selling Partner plugin for AWS Quick

At Amazon Accelerate on September 23, 2026, Amazon announced the Selling Partner plugin and launched it in beta in AWS Quick. The tool brings Amazon sellers' sales data, inventory signals, and marketplace intelligence together so questions become answers and answers become actions.

## 🧩 How it works

- **Contextual Understanding**: Quick understands the seller's full business context, building a living understanding of catalog structure, margin thresholds, seasonality, and competitive position without the seller needing to re-explain these details in every conversation.
- **Cross-Tool Connectivity**: It connects to where the seller already works—including storefronts on other channels, email inboxes, accounting systems, spreadsheets, and supplier portals—pulling context from across the whole operation.
- **Decision Synthesis**: Quick watches for change, flags what matters, builds the analysis, and presents the decision with the evidence behind it. For example, it can audit ASINs for Q4 and return ranked recommendations based on real margin data, historical conversion, and inventory availability.
- **Conversational Agents**: Sellers can describe a task in plain language, and Quick turns the description into a working agent that the seller refines in conversation. An example use case involves staging a new product to find the lowest total landed cost, weighing fulfillment options per SKU on inbound cost, storage, and speed to sellable.

## ⚙️ Key details

- **Plugin Installation**: The Selling Partner plugin installs like an app with no coding required. After one sign-in with a Seller Central account, it provides conversational access to product listings, inventory, and sales data pulled live rather than exported and reassembled by hand.
- **Amazon Ads Integration**: The plugin pairs with Amazon Ads MCP, placing campaign performance and spend next to selling data.
- **Granular Permissions**: Sellers grant access per data category (None, View, or Edit) and review an agreement before authorizing. Access is not all-or-nothing and can be narrowed or revoked later.
- **Data Privacy**: Quick runs on AWS infrastructure, the same system Amazon uses for its marketplace. It does not use seller data to train models. Seller data stays in the seller's environment, and the seller's chosen AWS Region governs where data is stored.

## 🚀 Availability

- **Beta Status**: The plugin is currently in beta for US stores.
- **Promotional Offer**: Every primary selling account holder worldwide can claim a free 12-month subscription to AWS Quick Plus, including access for 10 additional delegated users.
- **Deadline**: Sign up must occur by December 31, 2026.
