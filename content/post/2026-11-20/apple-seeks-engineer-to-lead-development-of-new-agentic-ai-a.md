# Apple seeks engineer to lead development of new agentic AI assistant

Apple is working on an AI agent capable of performing a range of tasks, including browsing the web, working across apps, writing code, and calling services. This development is being pursued by Apple, which is looking for an engineer to lead the creation of an agentic AI assistant, according to a job listing spotted by Bloomberg.

## 🔍 Overview
Apple’s initiative puts the company in a position to compete with other agentic AI offerings. The new agent is designed to operate fluidly between various digital tools, such as a browser, a document, a terminal, an editor, and a service. The goal is to build agents that plan and carry out multi-step tasks across apps, browsers, command lines, code, and APIs, while coordinating specialized sub-agents.

## 🧩 How it works
The job listing details that the engineer will work closely with teams across Siri, UI Frameworks, and Apple Foundation Models divisions. The system is intended to coordinate specialized sub-agents to complete complex tasks. 

## ⚙️ Key details
- The AI agent will be able to browse the web, work across apps, and call services.
- The role involves planning and carrying out multi-step tasks across apps, browsers, command lines, code, and APIs.
- The agent will coordinate specialized sub-agents along the way.
- Apple has already rolled out its AI-upgraded Siri, which allows users to ask questions about what is on their screen and perform actions across apps.
- Reports suggest Apple could add the ability to rotate and crop images in Photos, or organize Safari tabs.

## 🚀 Availability
Apple is rolling out an upcoming iOS 27.3 update that gives developers the ability to integrate Siri AI into all apps. This feature is currently part of a Developer Preview and will eventually allow Siri to perform tasks across a wider range of apps.
