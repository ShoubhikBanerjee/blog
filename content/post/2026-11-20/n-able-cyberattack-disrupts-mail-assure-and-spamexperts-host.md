# N-able cyberattack disrupts Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services

N-able reported a cyberattack that breached a server in its Hosted Cloud environment, leading to a worldwide outage of the Mail Assume and SpamExperts services.

## 🔍 Overview
- Attackers breached one of N-able's servers, causing a major outage of Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services worldwide.
- The outage initially was attributed to a "combination of platform configuration and service dependencies."
- N-able later confirmed unauthorized access to a server in the Hosted Cloud environment.

## 📅 Timeline
- On Tuesday, N-able described the disruption as a configuration and dependency issue affecting the Americas, Asia‑Pacific, and Europe.
- The following day, UI and API access were disabled and unauthorized server access was confirmed.
- An email alert was sent to customers after the attack was detected.

## ⚙️ Impact
- Email flow and spam filtering continued to operate; N-able said there is no evidence that attackers accessed customer data or mailboxes.
- Customers could not sign in to the control panel, view or release quarantined messages, review logs, or modify domain configurations until services are restored.
- The management portal (dashboard) and supporting services for quarantine, logging, and mailbox synchronization were taken down.

## 🛡️ Response
- N-able disabled UI and API access, took down the management portal, and began mitigation steps.
- The company is hardening services to reduce the risk of recurrence.
- Investigation is ongoing, with no current evidence of compromised customer data.
- N-able clarified that the incident is unrelated to a separate N‑central outage caused by more than 30 zero‑day flaws.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Highlights the risk of unauthorized server access even when core email delivery remains functional.
- Demonstrates the importance of rapid customer communication and service isolation during a security incident.
- Shows that a single breached server can impact globally distributed cloud email security services.
