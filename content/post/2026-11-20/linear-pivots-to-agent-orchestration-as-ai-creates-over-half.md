# Linear pivots to agent orchestration as AI creates over half of its issues

Linear, an issue tracker founded to replace legacy tools, has pivoted toward becoming a product development system built around agent orchestration. This shift coincided with CEO Karri Saarinen publishing a post titled "Issue tracking is dead," a sentiment with which most of the company's customers agreed. Today, more than half of the issues created in Linear originate from AI agents, marking a significant change in how the product is used.

## 🧩 The Shift to Agent Orchestration
When Cristina joined Linear in 2023, the company had about 40 people and one salesperson. Three weeks after joining, she crossed "move upmarket to enterprise" off the company goals. Instead of chasing enterprise deals, Linear focused on its core user base and later built infrastructure to support autonomous agents.

*   **MCP Server**: Linear shipped its Model Context Protocol (MCP) server in May 2025, which Cristina says was basically the first in its category.
*   **Agent Platform**: The company built a platform for building agents into Linear, accompanied by a new set of APIs.
*   **Integrations**: Linear launched integrations with Codex, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot.
*   **Linear Agent**: The company released its own specific agent solution.

These developments were not part of a pre-planned enterprise roadmap; instead, agents happened, and Linear built the necessary infrastructure to support them.

## ⚙️ Key Details
*   **Issue Volume**: Agents created 3% of Linear's work a year ago; now it is more than half.
*   **Pricing Model**: Until June, Linear operated on entirely seat-based pricing.
*   **Sales Strategy**: Linear has never hired a single SDR. Companies are signing one-year deals and paid pilots rather than three-year contracts.
*   **Financials**: Linear passed $100 million in ARR this year with more than 40,000 paying customers and 177% net revenue retention.
*   **Valuation**: In August, employees sold $99 million in stock at a $2.5 billion valuation.

## 💡 Why It Matters
Pre-AI companies like Ramp and Coinbase were building internal agents and needed an API to plug them into Linear. Most frontier AI labs run on Linear and wanted it to move faster. Companies have carved out budgets for AI tools that did not exist before, allowing products that fit within these budgets to skip the usual fight for spend. Cristina notes that Linear is lucky it is small enough to cannibalize itself, allowing the shift to agent-driven workflows without the drag of legacy enterprise commitments.
