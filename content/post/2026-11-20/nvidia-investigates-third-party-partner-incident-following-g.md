# NVIDIA Investigates Third-Party Partner Incident Following Gentlemen Ransomware Claims

NVIDIA is investigating claims of a security breach after the Gentlemen ransomware group added the company to its dark web data leak site, though no proof of the breach has been shared yet.

![NVIDIA Gentlemen Leak Site](https://www.bleepstatic.com/images/news/u/1109292/2026/NVIDIA-gentlemen-dls.jpeg)

## 🔍 Overview
- The Gentlemen ransomware group listed NVIDIA on its dark web data leak site and threatened to release data with a countdown ending in nine days on November 28.
- NVIDIA stated it has not found evidence that its systems were compromised, but is looking into a separate incident that may have affected a third-party partner.
- An NVIDIA spokesperson told BleepingComputer: "We have found no indication that NVIDIA systems or networks were affected. We continue to monitor the situation."

## ⚙️ Key details
- The Gentlemen surfaced last year as a closed ransomware group before switching to a Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) model.
- The group offers affiliates up to a 90% cut of ransom proceeds.
- It operates as a double-extortion group that exfiltrates sensitive data before encrypting systems and threatening to leak the information.
- Since emerging, The Gentlemen has claimed more than 1,100 victims across manufacturing, healthcare, transportation, energy, education, and financial services sectors.
