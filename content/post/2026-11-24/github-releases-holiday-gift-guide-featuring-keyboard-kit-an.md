# GitHub releases holiday gift guide featuring keyboard kit and merch

GitHub has released a holiday gift guide for developers, featuring items from the GitHub Shop alongside a Black Friday discount. The guide is aimed at developers who may not have shared their wish lists, offering a selection of tech gear and merchandise.

## ⌨️ Hardware and Building
The GitHub x Omnitype Bauer Lite Keyboard Kit was featured at GitHub Universe in the Makerspace. Each GitHub-exclusive kit includes:
* A hot-swap PCB with RGB underglow
* Omnitype Hawk linear switches
* Dye-sublimated PBT keycaps
* Essential hardware and tools for a hands-on custom build

The kits arrive in randomly selected color combinations. For those interested in building, a step-by-step guide is available for beginners, and a new GitHub jigsaw puzzle is also offered for a different variety of building.

## 💡 Desk Accessories
To brighten up a desk, the guide suggests colorful desk mats, a selection of mugs and tumblers, and two specific items for coding decisions:
* **Copilot Amazeball**: Sit on your desk; shake it to get an answer to burning questions, such as whether to ship on a Friday.
* **Mona Octobobble**: A tiny teammate that nods along to ideas.

For comfort while agents work, the guide recommends a sleep mask and a cozy blanket for mid-afternoon naps.

## 👟 Travel and Apparel
Gifts for digital nomads include tech organizers, work totes, and skateboards. Apparel options include:
* **Invertocat hoodie**: A classic popular item.
* **Mona Varsity jacket**: For cold weather.
* **Cabana shirt and shorts co-ord**: For warm weather.

## 🧸 Items for All Ages
The guide includes items for various age groups:
* **Invertocat onesie**: For brand-new babies.
* **Mona bestie bottle and Mona plush**: For tweens or younger fans.

## 🚚 Shipping and Deals
A special Black Friday deal offers free standard shipping through December 1, 2026. Shoppers can use the code `FREESHIPBLOG26`. To ensure gifts arrive by December 22, customers should check holiday shipping deadlines.
