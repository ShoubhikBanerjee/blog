# Jensen Huang Reinstated as Nvidia CEO Following Trump Administration Involvement

Barely a week after insisting they would not reinstate him, Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, president, and board director, following heavy involvement from the Trump administration. 

![Jensen Huang](https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/07/STKP210_JENSEN_HUANG_A.jpg?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0%2C0%2C100%2C100&w=2400)

## 🔍 Overview

Nvidia’s board accepted Jensen Huang’s resignation after reports that Huang had an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. Following pressure and direct involvement from President Donald Trump and the White House, the board reversed its position and unanimously voted to bring Huang back. 

## ⚙️ Key details

- President Trump asked Vice President JD Vance to act as a liaison between Huang and Nvidia’s board to provide reassurances regarding national security.
- Trump reportedly dialed into Nvidia’s Sunday night board meeting to make the case for Huang's return.
- Nvidia's board received input from senior levels of the US government confirming that Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk.
- Huang admitted to an affair with Alice Huang, a former executive at Nvidia customer Megaspeed who was reportedly believed by US intelligence officers to be a potential Chinese spy.
- Additional reports emerged of affairs with a direct report and a second employee, and Huang reportedly lied to the Nvidia board about his infidelity.
- Megaspeed has been investigated for a broader attempt to illegally funnel advanced Nvidia chips to China, and Huang is scheduled to testify before Congress on December 1st.

## 💡 Why it matters

News of Huang’s reappointment shocked experts, analysts, and investors, causing a surge in NASDAQ-100 futures that hit a circuit-breaker limit and halted trading due to volatility. Meanwhile, the Nvidia board stated that its independent investigation into foreign intelligence will proceed to conclusion, and announced plans to form a new National Security and Export Control Committee alongside hiring a chief compliance officer.
