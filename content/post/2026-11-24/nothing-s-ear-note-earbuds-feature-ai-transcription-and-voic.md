# Nothing's Ear Note Earbuds Feature AI Transcription and Voice Control

Nothing has revealed the Ear Note, a new set of AI-enabled earbuds that record and transcribe conversations. The device is set to launch on December 8th, following demonstrations by CEO Carl Pei. While the hardware is confirmed, a final price has not yet been determined.

## 🔍 Overview
The Ear Note is built on the premise of making an existing, trusted device intelligent rather than introducing a completely new form factor. As described by Pei, this approach allows users to leverage hardware they already wear. The Ear Note is part of a broader strategy to build a hardware context layer for AI.

## 🧩 How it works
The system relies on a combination of the earbuds and their charging case to capture audio data:
- The charging case is equipped with microphones that can record and transcribe conversations.
- A connected app manages the recordings, transcriptions, and AI-generated summaries.
- A "Talk" button on the case allows users to record themselves, a feature previously seen in the Ear 3.

## ⚙️ Key details
The Ear Note supports interactions with various AI agents, including a basic voice-control system developed in-house by Nothing. This allows users to engage in back-and-forth conversations. The company also noted that the feature builds on earlier introductions, such as the Audio Snapshot recording feature found in the Ear 3A.

| Product | Feature |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ear Note | AI transcription and AI agent interactions |
| Ear Note | Microphone-equipped charging case |
| Ear 3 | "Talk" button for recording |
| Ear 3A | Audio Snapshot recording feature |

## 🚀 Availability
Nothing has scheduled the launch for December 8th. The company has not settled on a price ahead of the release. Additionally, rumors of delayed AI smartwatch and smart glasses projects were countered by a commitment to a more aggressive smartphone release schedule next year.
