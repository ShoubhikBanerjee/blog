# European Commission confirms data breach via AI-assisted attack

The European Commission has confirmed that attackers breached its EU Academy online learning hub and stole the personal data of an undisclosed number of users. This incident follows a disclosure that hackers breached the Commission's systems on October 22 using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework.

## 🔍 Overview
*   The attack was conducted using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework.
*   The breach affected the EU Academy platform, which is used to learn about EU policies and follow EU online courses.
*   The incident caused service disruptions that also affected the internal Sysper HR platform.
*   The Commission stated it has not detected evidence of a data breach affecting Commission staff as a whole.
*   No ransom demands have been received, and the attack has not yet been attributed to a specific actor.

## 🚨 Key details
*   Stolen data included users' personal information, such as names and email addresses.
*   As a protective measure, the Commission temporarily took down several applications, most of which have since been restored.
*   Public access to the EU Academy platform has been disabled.
*   Users on the platform received a message warning them to be suspicious of unsolicited emails or messages that may be part of potential phishing attacks.
*   The investigation into the incident is still ongoing.

## ⚙️ Broader context
*   In February, the Commission disclosed a data breach after discovering on January 30 that a mobile device management platform used for staff devices had been compromised.
*   On March 27, the Commission disclosed an additional incident following a breach of its Amazon cloud environment.
*   The EU's Cybersecurity Service (CERT-EU) attributed the March cloud hack to the TeamPCP threat group, noting it exposed the data of at least 29 other Union entities.
*   These incidents follow the Commission's January 20 proposal for new cybersecurity legislation aimed at strengthening defenses against cybercrime groups and state-backed actors.
