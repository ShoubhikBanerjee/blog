# Recent cyber incidents not linked to AI

Microsoft, the FAA, banks, United Airlines, the NSA—it’s been a really bad week in cyberspace.  

## 🔍 Overview
- *But, amazing as it might seem, these are not related.*
- *As far as we know, they’re not the result of cyberattacks.*
- *They’re not even related to AI.*

## ⚙️ Infrastructure Challenges
- *Our computerized infrastructure is vulnerable and fragile.*
- *Much of it is old, and has been incrementally patched and upgraded.*
- *A surprising amount of it is maintained by hobbyists, without any recompense.*
- *And more and more of it is centralized: the same systems affecting many different aspects of our lives and society.*
- *In many cases, it just barely works.*
- *And when it fails, it tends to fail badly.*

## 💡 Economic Drivers
- *There are a lot of technological reasons why this is true, but they are all downstream of the economic ones.*
- *The technologies we rely on are built and maintained within our economic system—and that’s where the core problem lies.*
- *Security, safety, resilience, redundancy, recoverability—these are all essential parts of reliable technology, but they are all costs.*
- *More importantly, they are inefficient: costs that feel wasteful until something bad happens.*
- *Modern shareholder capitalism, and private equity in particular, squeeze all inefficiencies out of a company in pursuit of near‑term profits.*
- *Competition, with multiple companies competing to do the same thing, is another inefficiency.*
- *Monopolization removes that in pursuit of near‑term profits.*

## 🔧 Risks and Failures
- *The result is brittle systems, single points of failure.*
- *They’re more susceptible to cyberattack—both human and AI‑fueled—and more susceptible to random mistakes.*
- *The more we allow unregulated capitalism to privatize gains while socializing risks, the more we should expect these sorts of failures.*

## 🔄 The Path Forward
- *Fixing this is technical, but it’s political first.*
- *It requires changing incentives, and that involves regulation and control.*
