# MIT CSAIL Introduces RLM for Interpretable, Adaptive AI Workflows

Researchers at MIT CSAIL have released predict-rlm, a production-focused runtime that lets language models execute complex, adaptive workflows while maintaining full interpretability. Built on the Recursive Language Models paper by Alex L. Zhang, Tim Kranca, and Omar Khattab, the system enables models to call sub-LMs with DSPy signatures, inspect files, manage state, and handle large contexts through code — all without context rot or loss of control flow visibility.
