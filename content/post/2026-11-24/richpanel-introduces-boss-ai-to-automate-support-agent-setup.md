# Richpanel Introduces Boss AI to Automate Support Agent Setup

Richpanel, a company helping firms automate 50% of support interactions within 30 days, has introduced a new AI system known as Boss AI. This development allows the company to handle the complex setup of its customer support agents without requiring forward-deployed engineers at every client site.

## 🔍 Overview

The introduction of Boss AI addresses a specific operational gap in deploying AI support agents. Typically, setting up these agents requires a forward-deployed engineer to connect the system to a customer's infrastructure and define its workflows. Richpanel identified that many companies lack the engineering resources to handle this process, leading to the creation of an AI manager that handles agent setup internally.

## 🧩 How it works

Boss AI functions as an AI manager responsible for building the tools and procedures required for Richpanel's support agents to operate. Its primary duties include:

- Writing procedures for agent actions.
- Defining how frontline support agents, copilots, and quality-assurance agents call the necessary tools.
- Handling agent setup so that human engineers are not required for every deployment.

To execute these tasks, Richpanel utilizes a specific model architecture. The company credits OpenAI for making models available that can handle long context and longer tasks while remaining cost-efficient, allowing for deeper reasoning in the preparation phase and more economical models for execution.

## ⚙️ Key details

Richpanel employs different OpenAI models for different roles within its ecosystem, prioritizing accuracy for setup tasks and cost-efficiency for ongoing interactions. The model allocation is detailed below:

| Model / System | Role / Function | 
| :--- | :--- |
| GPT‑6 Astra | Used for Boss AI (agent setup) |
| GPT‑6.1 Sol | Used for Boss AI (agent setup) |
| GPT‑6 Luna | Used for conversations (frontline agents) |

According to Richpanel CEO Amit RG, the "intelligence is no longer the bottleneck," but rather everything surrounding it. This includes ensuring agents have access to the right tools and instructions, as human support agents often rely on 20 to 30 different tools and unwritten procedures to serve customers. 

## 🚀 Availability

As of October 2026, Richpanel has completed approximately 300 AI customer-support deployments over the previous six months. The company guarantees a 50% automation rate within the first 30 days of deployment or offers a money-back refund. In all 300 deployments, the company met this automation commitment and noted that the AI is beating human support agents regarding customer satisfaction.

## 💡 Why it matters

This development shifts the barrier to entry for AI support automation from requiring specialized engineering staff to a self-service model. By using AI to manage the "tools and procedures" side of deployment, Richpanel can scale its implementation to companies that do not have engineers to spare, focusing on a balance of cost and intelligence for model selection.
