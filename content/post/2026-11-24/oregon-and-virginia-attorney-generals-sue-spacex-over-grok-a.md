# Oregon and Virginia Attorney Generals Sue SpaceX Over Grok AI Safety Concerns

Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield and Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones have filed separate lawsuits against SpaceX, alleging the company violated state consumer protection laws. The complaints accuse SpaceX of falsely promoting the safety of its X platform and the Grok AI tool, claiming the company ignored industry-standard safeguards.

## 🔍 Overview
The lawsuits focus on Grok Imagine, a tool used to generate images and videos. State officials allege that SpaceX prioritized profit over user safety by maintaining weak safeguards, with Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones describing these protections as "not a failure but a feature."

## ⚙️ Key Details
Legal complaints highlight specific timeline events regarding the platform's AI features:

* August 2025: xAI launched a "spicy" mode for Grok Imagine, which generated sexualized content and nude deepfakes.
* January 2026: A new feature allowed users to edit images without original poster consent, leading to an increase in nonconsensual, sexualized deepfakes of adults and minors.
* xAI's response: The company implemented paywalls and blocked specific requests involving swimwear or explicit scenarios. However, testing by The Verge indicated these updates did not prevent the generation of sexualized deepfakes.

## 🛡️ Company Response
The X Safety team stated that they take measures to address users who generate sexual content involving minors. Their response included the following claims:

* Users who generate prohibited material are deliberately violating Terms of Service and circumventing safeguards.
* The company utilizes sophisticated classifiers to screen prompts and inspect generated outputs.
* Protection mechanisms are continuously tested and adjusted to address changing user tactics.
