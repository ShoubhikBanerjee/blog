# Law enforcement disrupts 57 illegal IPTV services across 11 countries

Law enforcement authorities from 11 countries have disrupted 57 illegal streaming services that operated hundreds of websites and drew over 7.3 million visits each year. The joint operation targeted a criminal market where unauthorized access to premium television channels, live sports, and films is sold via illegal IPTV services, often using cryptocurrency for subscription payments.

## 📋 Operation details

According to a Tuesday press release from Europol, the operation resulted in the following:

- **57** illegal IPTV services were disrupted.
- **321** websites illegally offering copyrighted content were identified.
- Combined annual traffic of the targeted sites exceeded **7.3 million** visits.
- Investigators traced over **€26 million** in cryptocurrency transactions, with further analysis ongoing.

The investigation involved tracing links between illegal IPTV services, crypto wallets, financial transactions, and the individuals controlling the services. These operators used wallets to receive and transfer payments to move money between countries and obscure the origin of their profits.

## 🤝 Partners involved

The operation was a joint action involving Europol, the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO), INTERPOL, and the European Audiovisual Anti-Piracy Alliance (AAPA). To trace cryptocurrency payments and identify financial networks, investigators partnered with crypto industry experts from Binance, Coinbase, and Chainalysis.

## 📉 Impact on legitimate providers

According to the AAPA, legitimate service providers lose around **€4.83 billion** annually as roughly **60 million** people in Europe use illegal platforms for streaming content. These illegal services typically charge a small monthly fee for access to content that has not been licensed by copyright owners.

## 🔄 Related enforcement actions

This operation builds on previous international anti-piracy efforts:

| Date | Operation | Key Outcomes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Summer 2024 | Operation KRATOS | Shut down a massive illegal streaming network with over 22 million users worldwide. |
| June (Year implied by sequence) | Operation KRATOS 2 | Removed more than 27,000 illegal streaming URLs, shut down nine organized crime groups, and led to 29 arrests. Coordinated by Bulgaria with Europol support. |
| January (Year implied by sequence) | Operation Switch Off | Coordinated by Europol, Eurojust, and Interpol; resulted in the seizure of three industrial-scale illegal IPTV services. |
| May (Year implied by sequence) | Italian Authorities Action | Dismantled the CINEMAGOAL piracy platform, which provided illegal access to streaming platforms including Netflix, Spotify, and Disney+. |

These actions fall under the purview of the Intellectual Property Crime Coordinated Coalition (IPC3), created in 2016 by Europol and EUIPO to target counterfeiting and online/offline piracy.
