# Google Gemini 4 Argon Model Reaches General Availability with Benchmarks

Google announced that Gemini 4 Argon is now generally available (GA) for coding, knowledge work, enterprise workflows and cyber‑defense.

## 🔍 Overview
- Gemini 4 Argon went GA today, after a title story was published two weeks earlier.
- Independent benchmarks such as CursorBench confirm the claims made in the original announcement.
- The rollout supersedes an earlier claim that access was limited to cybersecurity defenders.

## ⚙️ Availability
- Access is provided through the paid Gemini API and Gemini platform.
- Included in Google AI Pro and Ultra within the Gemini app.
- Available via AI Mode in Google Search.
- Ultra model is also accessible in the Antigravity environment.
- Argon is integrated into third‑party coding tools: Cursor, Devin Desktop/CLI, and GitHub Copilot.
- Antigravity’s coding interface is entering the Gemini macOS app.
- Gemini now supports connections to more than 40 applications, including Instacart, OpenTable, Adobe and Shopify.

## 💰 Pricing
- Introductory API rates: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens.
- Cached input tokens receive a 95 % discount.
- Artificial Analysis reports that rates will double to $4/$20 after November 30.
- Under introductory pricing, Artificial Analysis estimates a cost of $1.99 per task, about 60 % of GPT‑6 Astra’s cost per task.

## 📊 Benchmarks & Performance
- **CursorBench**: Argon scores 52.8 % at default effort, matching Opus 5.5.
- **FrontierCode 1.1**: Cognition reports a 60.5 % score; a 75 % Devin discount is available through October 27.
- **Roboflow Evaluation**:
  - Text localization/recognition: 84.7 % (Argon) vs 76.8 % (Astra).
  - Object detection: 85.1 % (Argon) vs 83.6 % (Astra).
  - Cost and latency are roughly four times lower, with 19.6 s per image vs 29.7 s for Astra.
- **Arena Ranking**: Argon High places fifth behind Opus 5.5, GPT‑6.1 Sol, Fable 5.1 and GPT‑6 Astra at maximum effort.
- **Artificial Analysis**: Argon shows substantial agentic gains and a 15 % AA‑Omniscience hallucination rate, the lowest among models scoring ≥45 on the Intelligence Index.
- **DeepMind Robotics Test**: Argon derived a cornhole simulation from human video, performed system identification, refined throws on Apptronik’s Apollo robot, and achieved seven consecutive successful throws.
- **LlamaIndex Findings**: Argon, Muse Spark and Wonder perform strongly on hard‑document OCR, especially table understanding, chart reading‑order and formatting recognition.

## 🚀 Use Cases
- Google reports Argon helped separate core webpage handling from a six‑million‑line legacy Chromium folder, completing months of work in weeks and eliminating a category of browser crashes.
- Code generation shows a 35 % increase in agentic submissions and a nearly one‑third reduction in rollbacks.
- The model is being used for OCR tasks, object detection, and robotic control simulations.
- Integration with over 40 productivity apps expands its reach in enterprise workflows.
