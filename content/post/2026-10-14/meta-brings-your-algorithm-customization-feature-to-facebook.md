# Meta brings Your Algorithm customization feature to Facebook

Meta is introducing the "Your Algorithm" feature to Facebook, allowing users to directly manage and customize the content they see in their feed and Facebook Reels. This toolset is part of a broader implementation of AI-powered features designed to provide users with more control over their algorithmic recommendations.

## 🧩 How it works
* Users can access the feature via the three dots menu on individual posts.
* You can select "interested" or "not interested" to adjust recommendations.
* The system may ask follow-up questions to gain more detail on user preferences.
* You can describe in your own words what content you want to see more or less of.

## ⚙️ Key details
* Recommendations respond in real time, adapting within the same session to reflect user input.
* Meta is increasing the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) in ranking systems to interpret user intent and natural language.
* The feature has previously launched on Instagram and as "Dear Algo" on Threads.

## 💡 Why it matters
Meta states that using LLMs allows the algorithm to incorporate interests, context, and intent behind natural language, effectively creating an algorithm that users can talk to.
