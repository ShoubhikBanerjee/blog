# Square launches Square Kiosk with conversational AI ordering interface

Square announced a new hardware product that blends a large touchscreen kiosk with a conversational AI assistant for ordering.

## 🔍 Overview
- The device is called the **Square Kiosk**.
- It is a big kiosk with a 21.5‑inch touchscreen and a pill‑shaped form factor.
- An “agentic, conversational voice layer” lets customers talk to the AI as they would to staff.

## 🧩 How it works
- Presence detection greets customers as they approach the kiosk.
- While speaking with the AI agent, users can:
  - Ask questions about the menu.
  - Request modifications and substitutions.
  - Receive real‑time responses that fully understand the seller’s menu.
- When ordering is complete, payment can be made via a contactless payment reader or a card slot.

## ⚙️ Key details
- **Screen size:** 21.5 inches.
- **Form factor:** Large pill shape.
- **AI capabilities:** Real‑time listening, menu comprehension, natural‑language ordering.
- **Payment options:** Contactless reader and card slot.

## 🚀 Availability
- The Square Kiosk is available **starting today**.
- Price: **$1,999** per device for businesses with a Square Plus or Premium subscription.
- Ongoing software fee: **$99 per device per month**.
