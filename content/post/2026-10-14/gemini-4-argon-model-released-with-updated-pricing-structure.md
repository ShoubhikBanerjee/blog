# Gemini 4 Argon model released with updated pricing structure

Support has been added to llm-gemini for the new Gemini 4 Argon model, which is now available via the Gemini API and for customers of the $100/month subscription plan.

## ⚙️ Key details

Argon exhibits strong performance in SVG generation, a capability noted in previous Gemini models. Testing shows the model can produce detailed illustrations, such as the "high" pelican SVG, at a cost of approximately 17.166 cents.

## 💰 Pricing

Pricing is set to increase on December 1st. Current and future rates are as follows:

| Tier | Input (per million) | Output (per million) | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Current Launch | $2 | $10 | Available |
| Post-December 1st | $4 | $20 | Scheduled |

## 💡 Why it matters

While raw token pricing is one metric, the model's efficiency in an agentic harness is considered more significant. Artificial Analysis reports that Argon costs $2,407 to run their benchmark, placing it close to GLM-5.3 and at a lower cost than current models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta.
