# Android adds Gemini‑AI‑powered enhanced protection to Google Messages

Android is introducing new scam defense features that aim to make it easier for users to evade mobile fraud, including bolstered protection in Google Messages and additional safeguarding tools.

## 🔍 Overview
- Android is using AI to suss out scammers in Google Messages.
- A new “enhanced protection” setting will roll out in the coming weeks and is opt‑in.

## 🧩 How it works
- The enhanced protection mode taps into cloud‑based Gemini AI models to quickly evaluate a potential scammer’s intentions in conversations with an unrecognized sender.
- "Scammers often disguise their true motives behind slow‑burn conversations," Google said.
- "This secure cloud analysis augments and extends our existing on‑device detection, allowing us to identify novel and sophisticated attacks, actively uncovering and stopping abusers who try to hide their tracks."
- User data is encrypted during this AI analysis and processed in a secure dedicated space that Google (and anyone else besides the user) can’t access.
- The mode also uses on‑device Gemini Nano AI to inspect real‑time web signals in unfamiliar URLs before they are opened to determine if the links are malicious.

## ⚙️ New security features
| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Call‑forwarding block | Blocks apps from enabling call forwarding and notifies the user when someone attempts to do so. |
| Screen‑share privacy | Automatically conceals sensitive information while screen‑sharing in Chrome and Android WebView. |
| Enhanced protection | Opt‑in mode that uses Gemini AI to assess intentions of unrecognized senders and scans URLs with Gemini Nano. |

## 🌐 Expansion and rollout
- The scam detection feature for chat notifications that Google introduced on Pixel phones last year is expanding to devices from Honor, Motorola, Oppo, OnePlus, Realme, Tecno, Vivo, Xiaomi, and more.
- Google says it plans to bring it to “hundreds of millions of additional users later this year.”
