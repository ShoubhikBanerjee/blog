# Fastenal Expected to Report Q3 2026 Earnings of $0.3392 Per Share

Fastenal (NASDAQ:FAST) is slated to release its third‑quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 before the market opens. The earnings conference call is scheduled for 10:00 AM ET.

## 🔍 Overview
- Analysts expect earnings of **$0.3392 per share**.
- Consensus revenue estimate is **$2.44 billion** for the quarter.
- In the same quarter a year earlier Fastenal posted **$0.29 per share** on **$2.13 billion** of revenue.

## 📊 Earnings Forecast
- **Expected EPS:** $0.3392
- **Expected Revenue:** $2.44 billion
- **Previous Q3 (2025) EPS:** $0.29
- **Previous Q3 (2025) Revenue:** $2.13 billion

## 🏢 Company Profile
- **Founded:** 1967 by Bob Kierlin
- **Headquarters:** Winona, Minnesota
- **Business:** Distributor of industrial and construction supplies serving businesses, contractors, manufacturers, and other organizations.
- **Product offerings:**
  - Fasteners (bolts, nuts, screws)
  - Safety equipment
  - Tools and cutting tools
  - Material‑handling products
  - Electrical and plumbing supplies
  - Maintenance and repair items
- **Sales channels:**
  - Network of branch locations
  - On‑site customer facilities
  - Industrial vending machines
  - Digital platforms
- **Services:** Help customers manage procurement, inventory, and product availability, with onsite support and automated dispensing solutions used in manufacturing and other industrial environments.
- **Geography:** Serves customers across North America and operates in select markets in Europe, Asia, and other international regions.
- **Ticker:** FAST on the Nasdaq Stock Market.
