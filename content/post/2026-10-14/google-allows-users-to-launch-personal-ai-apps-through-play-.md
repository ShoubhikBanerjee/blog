# Google allows users to launch personal AI apps through Play Store

Google has announced a new feature in the US that allows users to launch personal, vibe-coded apps directly through the Google Play Store.

## 🧩 How it works
* Apps are launched using a standard Google account.
* The feature is separate from the public store catalog, keeping the apps secure and private.
* Users will soon have the ability to share these apps with a select group of people.

## ⚙️ Key details
* Google first introduced the ability to vibe-code personal Android apps in May.
* If users wish to share their app with the world, they can transition to a professional developer account.
* Professional accounts are subject to the same Trust & Safety reviews and quality standards as existing professional developers, according to Sam Bright, Google’s vice president and general manager of Google Play.

## 🚀 Availability
* This feature is currently available in the US.
