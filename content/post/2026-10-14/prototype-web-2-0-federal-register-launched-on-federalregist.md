# Prototype Web 2.0 Federal Register Launched on FederalRegister.gov

A new prototype "Web 2.0" version of the daily Federal Register is now available on FederalRegister.gov. The site provides XML renditions of published documents and links to the official PDF versions, but it does **not** replace the official print or electronic editions.

## 🔍 Overview
- The prototype displays a “Web 2.0” version of the daily Federal Register.
- It is an **unofficial informational resource** until the Administrative Committee of the Federal Register (ACFR) grants it official legal status.
- The partnership between the Office of the Federal Register (OFR) and the Government Publishing Office (GPO) aims to present accurate regulatory information and eventually establish the XML‑based Federal Register as an ACFR‑sanctioned publication.

## 🛠️ How It Works
- Each document on the site is an **XML rendition** of a published Federal Register document.
- Every XML entry includes a **link to the corresponding official PDF** on govinfo.gov.
- A navigational table of contents is generated from headings within the legal text; repeated headings create internal navigation links but have **no substantive legal effect**.
- The site pulls supplemental context from Regulations.gov, which **is not part of the official Federal Register document**.
- Search functions: pressing **Enter** in the search box or selecting a suggestion takes users directly to the relevant content.

## ⚖️ Legal Status
- The prototype **does not replace** the official print version or the official electronic version on govinfo.gov.
- Until ACFR grants official status, the XML rendition **does not provide legal notice** to the public or judicial notice to the courts.
- Only official editions provide legal notice of publication under **44 U.S.C. §1503 & §1507**.
- Legal researchers are advised to **verify results against an official edition** of the Federal Register.
- For complete official information, users should visit **About the Federal Register on NARA's archives.gov**.

## 📚 Access & Usage
- Use the **PDF linked in the document sidebar** for the official electronic format.
- The site is a **prototype and informational only**; comments are no longer being accepted.

## 💡 Why It Matters
- The XML‑based prototype offers a more searchable, web‑friendly format while preserving links to the legally recognized PDF versions.
- If granted official status, the XML edition could become a **sanctioned source of legal notice**, streamlining public access to regulatory information.
