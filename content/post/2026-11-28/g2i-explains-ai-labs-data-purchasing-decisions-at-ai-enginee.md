# G2i explains AI labs' data purchasing decisions at AI Engineer CODE 2026

At AI Engineer CODE 2026, G2i founder Gabe Greenberg discussed how frontier AI labs decide whether to pay more for exclusive training data or take a discount on data another lab can also buy, and when it makes sense to build a data operation in‑house.

## 🔍 Overview
- Labs can pay more for training data they keep exclusive, or take a discount on data that other labs can also buy.
- Small pilot orders often turn into larger engagements.
- Decision factors were explained by Gabe Greenberg during the event recorded in San Francisco.

## 🧩 How labs choose data
- Evaluate the cost of exclusivity versus shared‑discount models.
- Start with pilot orders to test value before scaling up.
- Consider building an internal data operation when long‑term needs justify it.

## ⚙️ Task design process
- A good training task should sit just ahead of what a model can do today.
- Senior engineers design the task, choose the starting data, and build a verifier that can score the result.
- Gabe explained these steps as part of G2i’s workflow.

## 🤝 G2i’s role
- G2i builds AI training data and reinforcement learning environments with senior engineers.
- ORC is the multi‑agent coding platform Gabe’s team uses internally.
- G2i co‑organized AI Engineer CODE 2026 and sponsored the episode.
