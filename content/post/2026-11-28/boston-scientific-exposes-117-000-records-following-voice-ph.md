# Boston Scientific Exposes 117,000 Records Following Voice Phishing Attack

Boston Scientific, a Massachusetts-based medical device manufacturer, is sending data breach notices to individuals after a cyberattack exposed personal and medical information belonging to 117,789 people in the United States. The incident marks the second cybersecurity event for the company this year.

## 🔍 Overview

The attack exposed cloud-based applications and cloud-hosted data between May 7 and May 1, 2025. Boston Scientific became aware of the unauthorized access on May 12. A subsequent investigation determined that a data breach occurred on November 5. The exposed information included personal details and medical information for individuals in multiple U.S. states.

## 🧩 How it works

According to notification samples shared with U.S. authorities, the hacker gained access to the company's cloud infrastructure using social engineering in a voice phishing (vishing) attack.

## ⚙️ Key details

- **Data Exposed:** Personal details and medical information.
- **Data Not Exposed:** The notification specifies that patient medical records, data from remote monitoring systems, connected medical devices, and clinical case management or workflow applications were not exposed.
- **Scope:** The incident impacted 117,789 individuals according to the Texas Attorney General breach portal.
- **Business Impact:** The company stated there was no disruption to manufacturing, ordering, distribution, or service to patients and healthcare providers, and no impact on operations, performance, remote monitoring, or the safety of its products.

## 📊 Incident Comparison

This breach is distinct from a previous cyberattack disclosed on August 25, which caused network outages and global operational disruptions. A Boston Scientific spokesperson confirmed the most recent incident is unrelated to the August 2026 intrusion; a CrowdStrike investigation into that earlier attack found no evidence that attackers compromised cloud applications.

## 💡 Why it matters

As one of the world's largest medical device manufacturers with more than 59,000 employees, 13 manufacturing facilities, and a presence in 127 countries, Boston Scientific's data security is critical. The company contained the incident, investigated with outside cybersecurity experts, and notified law enforcement. However, the company is not offering free credit monitoring and instead recommends impacted individuals use free credit reports, place fraud alerts, and consider credit freezes.
