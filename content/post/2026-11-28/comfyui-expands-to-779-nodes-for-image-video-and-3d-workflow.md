# ComfyUI expands to 779 nodes for image, video, and 3D workflows

ComfyUI has grown to include 779 nodes, expanding its capabilities to handle image, video, 3D, and language models along with the plumbing between them. The project has maintained an MIT license since 2023 and has accumulated over a million downloads. Its architecture is designed so that nothing is installed or fetched unless explicitly requested.

## 🧩 Core Capabilities

The node library supports a wide range of processing tasks, including:

*   Tiled and affine samplers, noise shaping, shift and schedule control, model quantizing, and LoRA stacks.
*   Crop-and-restore edits, edit regions, and specific model integrations like MiniMax H3 Image, Fibo 2.0, and BluePixel 4K.
*   Raw-style develop, film stocks, darkroom effects, and looks baked into LUTs.
*   Levels, LUTs, ACES and colour spaces, linear light, HDR video, and scopes and charts.
*   Skin, face, and subject masks, including masks drawn directly on the picture and subjects followed through a clip.
*   Filters and optics, layer stacks, layered PSD and TIFF files, and ControlNet preprocessors.

## 🎬 Video and 3D Features

Recent developments extend ComfyUI's utility into complex video production and 3D generation:

*   **Video Production**: The Prompt Timeline allows long videos to be built a segment at a time. Features include upscaling, sparse attention, and Pika H3 Flex integration. The system supports video with sound, video super-resolution of any length, edits, transitions, titles, restored footage, face restoration through a shot, audio mixing, and beats.
*   **3D Generation**: Capabilities include scenes, generated models, characters, meshes, blockouts, point cloud worlds, and Video Blueprint.

## 📚 Text and Logic Tools

The platform also includes utilities for prompt engineering and data handling:

*   Prompt terminology and a style library.
*   A system prompt library.
*   Support for text, lists, and dictionaries.

## 🚀 Availability and Resources

*   **License**: MIT (since 2023).
*   **Downloads**: Over a million.
*   **Examples**: 144 example workflows are provided.
*   **Installation Model**: Nothing installed, nothing fetched unless asked.
