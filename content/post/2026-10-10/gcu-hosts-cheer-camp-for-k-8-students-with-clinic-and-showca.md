# GCU Hosts Cheer Camp for K‑8 Students with Clinic and Showcase

Grand Canyon University (GCU) hosted a Cheer Camp for K‑8th graders, featuring a clinic and a showcase that welcomed parents.

## 🎉 Overview
- Cheer camp designed for kindergarten through 8th‑grade students.
- Included a clinic and a showcase.
- Parents were invited to attend.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Who Can Attend
- Students in grades K‑8.
- Their families and parents.

## ⚙️ Clinic Activities
- Instruction led by the GCU Dance Team and GCU Cheerleaders.
- Turns, kicks, and leaps.
- Game day chants.
- Tumbling and stunting.
- Parade‑style dance motions.

## 📅 Event Flow
- Clinic instruction took place first.
- Following this there will be a spirit activity.
- The event concluded with a showcase open to parents.
