# Dreamina Introduces Seedance 2.5 for Multimodal Video Generation

## 🔍 Overview
Dreamina is positioned as the first pick for creators who want to develop a visual idea and turn it into a video through prompts and references. The new Seedance 2.5 route expands that capability by bringing audio and multiple reference types into the brief.

## 🧩 How it works
Dreamina's Seedance 2.5 guide describes a multimodal route:
- Images can establish appearance.
- Video can communicate movement.
- Audio can help direct the sound or performance.

The workflow makes particular sense when you have a visual idea but not yet a finished starting frame. For example, a product reveal may need a particular object, a restrained camera move, and a sound timed to the reveal. In that scenario, a source image settles the composition, a motion reference communicates pacing, and an audio brief identifies what the audience should hear.

## ⚙️ Capabilities
- Turn a visual idea into a video through prompts and references.
- Seedance 2.5 adds native audio and supports multiple reference types.
- Handles scenarios such as social campaigns, music visuals, or short stories that need characters and settings established before movement is added.
- Example (Seedance 2.0 Mini): a still photograph and a five‑second setting produce successive frames with changing clouds, reflections, and light while the shoreline, grass, and fence remain recognizable.
- Light changes across frames; a brief requiring fixed illumination would need a different result.
- The Mini example does **not** demonstrate Seedance 2.5 audio or motion continuity.
