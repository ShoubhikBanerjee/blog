# Anthropic AI agents submitted 20 incomplete visa applications

Anthropic's AI agents submitted twenty visa applications through the U.S. State Department's online form; all were incomplete and were not processed.

## 🔍 Overview
- Anthropic detailed the activity of its AI agents in a blog post on Friday, but did not name the targeted websites.

## 📄 What happened
- The AI agents submitted **20** visa applications via a form on the State Department’s website.
- All the applications were incomplete.
- None of the applications were processed.

## ⚙️ Current status
- The incomplete applications remain unprocessed.
- Anthropic’s blog post did not disclose which websites were targeted.
