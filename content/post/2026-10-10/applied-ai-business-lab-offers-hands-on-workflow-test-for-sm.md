# Applied AI Business Lab Offers Hands‑On Workflow Test for Small Businesses

## 🔍 Overview
- Dive into hands‑on AI strategies and real workflows to crush your 2026 biz goals in person!
- The challenge for most businesses is no longer finding another AI app.
- Instead of collecting AI tools, start deciding where AI actually belongs in your business.

## 🛠️ Workshop Design
- **Applied AI Business Lab** is a focused three‑hour working session for business owners, operators, consultants, and team leads who want to move from general AI interest to a practical business experiment.
- This is not an AI trends conference.
- No coding lectures, technical architecture sessions, or software sales presentations.
- No laptop is required.
- The session is intentionally designed around business logic and workflow planning rather than software configuration.
- The focus is practical workflow evaluation and responsible implementation planning.

## 📋 Core Activities
- Examine practical workflow patterns.
- Compare ideas with other business operators.
- Stress‑test potential use cases.
- Build a small 30‑day pilot you can evaluate inside your own business.
- See a live AI agent built from scratch.
- Discuss governance practices that help organizations encourage productive AI use while protecting company, customer, and confidential information.
- Examine how AI‑powered cyber threats are evolving and what businesses can do to better recognize and defend against them.
- Ask critical questions:
  - Which task is actually worth improving?
  - What part of the process can AI assist with?
  - How will you know if the workflow is helping?
  - What still requires human judgment?

## 🎯 Expected Outcomes
- One workflow, one test, clearer evidence.
- Practical ways to apply AI to your small business.
- Ideas you can evaluate within your own organization.
- A clearer understanding of how to approach AI thoughtfully and securely.
- Determination of whether a specific workflow is worth continuing (the goal is not to prove that AI works).

## ⚖️ Governance & Security
- Practical guidelines for how AI tools should be used in the workplace.
- Governance practices that protect company, customer, and confidential information.
- Awareness of AI‑enabled phishing, impersonation, and social engineering attacks.
- Risks to be aware of before connecting an agent to your data and applications.
