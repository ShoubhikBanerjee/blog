# New Open LLM Leaderboard Consolidates Model Launches, Benchmarks, and Pricing

A dedicated leaderboard page now aggregates weekly releases, pricing changes, benchmark results, and licensing details for both closed‑frontier and open‑weight large language models.

## 🔍 Overview
- Large language models (LLMs) are moving faster than any other part of the AI stack, with new frontier releases, open‑weight drops and benchmark shake‑ups landing almost weekly.
- An LLM is an AI system trained on vast amounts of text to understand and generate human language.
- Frontier models use hundreds of billions of parameters to handle writing, coding, reasoning and analysis.
- Frontier closed models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google lead general benchmarks, while open‑weight models such as Llama, Qwen and GLM offer more control and lower cost for self‑hosted deployments.

## 🗂️ What the leaderboard tracks
- Model launches from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, Mistral, Alibaba and Zhipu.
- Changes to pricing and context windows.
- Independent benchmark results.
- Licensing and export‑control decisions that determine which models teams can actually deploy.
- Both the closed frontier labs and the open‑weight ecosystem, because the gap between them now shifts month to month.

## 📊 Rankings and data
The page is built for high‑intent searches like “open LLM leaderboard” and “open source LLM leaderboard”, providing a dedicated ranking and comparison surface. Rankings use a live composite score, then surface benchmark columns, pricing, context window, and release recency.

## 🧩 Why it matters
- Open‑weight models are AI models whose trained weights are publicly released, so anyone can download, run and fine‑tune them.
- Unlike fully open‑source models, the training data and code are often not published.
- Providing a single feed of launches, pricing, benchmarks, and licensing helps teams quickly compare and decide which models they can actually deploy.
