# Guide to Scoping AI‑Powered Workflow Automation and Expected ROI

A new guide explains how to properly scope AI‑powered workflow automation projects, showing real costs, timelines, and ROI for common business processes.

## 🔍 Overview
- Most businesses know they should automate.
- McKinsey estimates that roughly 60–70% of current work activities are technically automatable with existing technology.
- The majority of automation projects stall before they reach production, and the ones that do ship often automate the wrong process first.
- The problem is rarely the technology; it is that most companies skip the scoping work – they pick a tool, then look for a process to fit it.

## 🛠️ Scoping Approach
- This guide reverses the usual order: first identify which workflows to automate, then choose the appropriate tool.
- It walks through the exact process of identifying workflows, scoping the work, estimating costs, and describing deployment with real numbers and timelines – not vendor marketing.

## 🤖 RPA vs AI Automation
- **RPA (Robotic Process Automation)** follows deterministic rules – e.g., *if field A contains X, copy it to field B, click the submit button, wait for the confirmation screen*.
- RPA is essentially screen‑scraping and keystroke replay dressed in better packaging.
- **AI automation** handles judgment – e.g., *classify this email as a complaint or a feature request, extract line items from an invoice regardless of format, generate a personalised response based on the customer's history*.
- The honest answer for most companies: you need both.
  - RPA handles the plumbing – moving data between systems that refuse to talk to each other.
  - AI process automation handles the judgment – classification, extraction, and decision‑making steps that previously required a human brain.
- Choosing the wrong approach wastes money: using an LLM to copy a value from one field to another is like hiring a surgeon to apply a bandage.

## 📈 High‑ROI Use Cases
| Use Case | Manual Cost / Time | AI‑Assisted Cost / Time | Typical ROI (Cost / Time Reduction) | PoC Timeline |
|----------|--------------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|-------------|
| Finance – invoice processing | $12–$15 per invoice | $2–$4 per invoice (OCR + LLM pipeline extracts data, matches POs, routes exceptions) | 60–75% cost reduction within 6 months | 4–6 weeks |
| Support – ticket triage | 30–40% of team time spent on classification | AI agent classifies intent, extracts account data, drafts initial response, routes complex tickets with context | 40–50% reduction in first‑response time, 25–35% reduction in ticket‑handling cost | 3–5 weeks (with a good training dataset) |
| Legal/Compliance – contract review | 45–90 minutes per 30‑page contract | RAG‑based extraction pulls required fields in under a minute, flags anomalies, surfaces only sections needing human review | 70–85% time reduction per document | 5–8 weeks (depends on document variety) |

## 💸 Cost & Timeline Details
- Average cost to process one invoice manually is **$12–$15**; with AI‑assisted extraction and matching it drops to **$2–$4**.
- Implementation timelines are based on actual PoC projects on customer data:
  - Invoice automation PoC: **4–6 weeks**.
  - Ticket‑classification AI PoC: **3–5 weeks**.
  - Contract‑extraction RAG PoC: **5–8 weeks**.
- The guide provides real numbers and real timelines, not vendor marketing claims.

## 🚀 Getting Started
- Begin by cataloguing high‑volume, high‑labour‑cost, high‑error‑rate processes.
- Determine whether the step is deterministic (RPA) or requires judgment (AI).
- Scope a small PoC using the timelines above, measure cost reduction, and iterate.
- Combine RPA plumbing with AI judgment to achieve the highest ROI.
