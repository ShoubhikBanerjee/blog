# MarketBeat Updates News Sentiment Scoring System for BioMarin

The latest update from MarketBeat adds new analytics to its news‑sentiment tracking, showing how BioMarin Pharmaceutical (BMRN) is covered in the press this week.

## 🔍 Overview
- **Extended trading** is defined as trading on electronic markets outside regular hours.
- A **fair market value extended hours price** is supplied by Massive.
- MarketBeat tracks headlines from **hundreds of trusted media outlets** and tags each by company.

## 🧩 How It Works
- Each headline receives a **score from 2 (good news) to -2 (bad news)**.
- The **company news sentiment score** reflects the **average sentiment of articles** about the company over the most recent **7 days**.
- A separate **average healthcare news sentiment** is also calculated.
