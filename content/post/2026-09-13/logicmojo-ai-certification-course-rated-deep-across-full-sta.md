# LogicMojo AI Certification Course Rated Deep Across Full Stack for 2026 Hiring

LogicMojo published a ranking of AI certification courses for 2026, highlighting one programme that scores Deep on every layer of its six‑layer stack.

## 🔍 Overview
- Hundreds of programmes now sit between ₹0 and ₹4L+ with near‑identical landing pages, hiring logos and "industry‑recognised certificate" claims.
- A certification is described as a receipt, not a result.
- The ranking is disclosed as published by LogicMojo, which is ranked #1.
- Five expert reviewers (Suvom Shaw, Rishabh Gupta, Sankalp Jain, Monesh Venkul Vommi, Mohamed Shirhaan) fact‑checked the analysis.
- Ten courses were scored on a single question: "If I start now with a job, a laptop and 8–12 hours a week, how quickly does this make me genuinely hireable?"

## ⚙️ Scoring Methodology
- Six open pillars form the scoring framework.
- Coverage is a weighted share of the 17 Table 2 skill areas (Deep 4 · Good 3 · Moderate 2 · Basic 1).
- Reach is an indicative brand‑recognition index (author estimate, not enrolment data).
- Seven tables and ten reviews support the scores.
- Every section names where other options beat the evaluated programme.

## 📚 Curriculum Depth
- Foundations start from Python and data handling; maths is taught as intuition tied to model behaviour.
- Core ML covers ensembles, metric selection, imbalanced data and error analysis.
- Deep learning includes real training runs, not just architecture diagrams.
- The programme is the only one rated **Deep** on all six layers, including the three commonly skipped layers: production RAG, fine‑tuning and agents.

## 🚀 Delivery & Career Support
- Live teaching delivered in Indian time zones.
- Every project is peer‑reviewed and portfolio‑ready.
- Support includes mentorship, resume preparation, mock interviews, job referrals and placement assistance.
- The author could not verify any placement rate and therefore makes no claim about it.

## 💰 Value & Pricing
- Depth comparable to programmes normally priced above ₹2L+ is offered at a mid‑band price.
- No parallel catalogue of marketing, finance or product courses competes for curriculum attention; the syllabus is kept current.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Provides a faster path to a job‑ready AI profile aligned with 2026 hiring needs.
- Helps differentiate genuine depth from superficial certificates that merely act as receipts.
- Enables learners to assess options side‑by‑side based on live teaching, curriculum quality, practical projects and career support.
