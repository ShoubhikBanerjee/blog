# Australian Government Sets $100 Million Penalty for Big Tech Digital Duty of Care

The Australian government has established a $100 million penalty for big tech companies that breach their digital duty of care.

## ⚙️ Key details

* **Penalty Amount:** $100 million for breaches of digital duty of care.
* **Legal Basis:** Communications Minister Anika Wells stated that the question of fines was "drafted around Australian consumer law."

## 💡 Why it matters

Communications Minister Anika Wells has expressed skepticism regarding the effectiveness of financial penalties for the world's wealthiest corporations:

* Wells stated, "No particular figure that we mandate in Australia is going to be enough for the world’s richest companies."
* Wells noted that "there is no fine large enough to curb the behaviour of big tech companies."
* As an example of the scale of corporate payments, Wells said, “Meta just unblushingly paid $US18 billion ($25 billion) in a settlement a few weeks ago.”
