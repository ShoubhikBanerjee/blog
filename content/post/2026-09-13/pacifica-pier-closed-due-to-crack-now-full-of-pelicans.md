# Pacifica Pier Closed Due to Crack, Now Full of Pelicans

The Pacifica Pier shut down at the start of June after a crack in the concrete walkway made access to the pier unsafe.

## 🔍 Overview
The pier has been closed since the beginning of June.

## ⚙️ Key Details
*   **Cause:** A crack in the concrete walkway.
*   **Impact:** Access to the pier is unsafe.
*   **Timeline:** Shutdown occurred at the start of June.

## 📸 Current Status
It has since been entirely taken over by pelicans!
