# Meta Launches Muse AI Assistant Designed to Watch Learn and Act

Meta has launched Muse, a new AI assistant designed to watch, learn, and act on your behalf. The company promises a future where artificial intelligence helps manage everything from shopping and bookings to emails and daily tasks.

## ⚙️ Key Details

* **Watch, Learn, and Act**: Muse is built to watch, learn, and take actions on behalf of the user.
* **Task Management**: Meta promises the assistant will help manage daily tasks, emails, shopping, and bookings.
* **Wearable Integration**: The assistant is designed to remain with users at all times via wearable devices.

## 💡 Why It Matters

The launch of Muse has initiated discussions regarding whether the assistant is a helpful digital companion or a step forward in Big Tech's push into personal lives. On the program AI Decoded, presenter Christian Fraser, AI technology critic Ed Zitron, and AI consumer tech expert Ben Hatton explored the implications of the technology, including:
* Whether Muse is a breakthrough in personal AI or a gateway to unprecedented data collection.
* What users may be giving up in exchange for convenience.
* Whether the technology risks giving Meta unprecedented influence over everyday decisions.
* Whether Muse will become an indispensable assistant or Meta's "Trojan horse."
