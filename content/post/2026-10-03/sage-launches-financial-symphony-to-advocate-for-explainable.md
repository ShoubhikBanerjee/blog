# Sage Launches Financial Symphony to Advocate for Explainable AI in Finance

Sage has launched Financial Symphony, a classical electro-acoustic composition generated from real, anonymised business data. The project serves as a tangible expression of the company's "Glass Box" approach to artificial intelligence, highlighting the necessity of explainable AI outputs in the financial and business sectors.

## 🔍 Overview

The premise of Sage's campaign is that AI outputs used in business and finance should be inspectable, traceable, and explainable, rather than accepted as the results of an opaque black box. The company aims to make a serious point about AI in finance: an answer is not enough if a finance professional cannot understand how the technology reached it.

## ⚙️ How it works

The Financial Symphony project translates data into a musical performance using the following elements:
* **Collaboration**: Developed alongside data sonification specialist Loud Numbers.
* **Performance**: Played by a live string quartet.
* **Data translation**: Translates changes in financial metrics into pitch, tempo, and volume.
* **Input**: Generated using real, anonymised business data.

## 💡 Why it matters

Sage's research highlights a significant trust gap and administrative burden regarding unexplained AI tools in the industry:
* **Decision rejection**: Sage's research found that 71% of finance leaders reject AI-driven decisions they cannot explain.
* **Time spent validating**: Finance professionals spend more than 12 hours a week reconstructing logic, validating figures, and defending AI-generated conclusions.
