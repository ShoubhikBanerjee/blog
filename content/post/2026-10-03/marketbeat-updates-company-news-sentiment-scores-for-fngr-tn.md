# MarketBeat updates company news sentiment scores for FNGR, TNON, ICFI, TXNM

MarketBeat has refreshed its company news sentiment metrics, showing the latest seven‑day averages for four publicly traded firms.

## 🔍 Overview
- MarketBeat tracks news headlines from hundreds of news outlets and tags them by company.
- A sentiment score is assigned to each headline on a scale from -2 (bad news) to +2 (good news).
- Company news sentiment scores represent the average of these headline scores over the most recent 7 days.
- The system is used to identify companies that are receiving positive press.

## ⚙️ How it works
- **Headline scoring**: Each headline receives a score ranging from 2 (good news) to -2 (bad news).
- **Aggregation**: Scores are averaged across all articles about a company in the last seven days.
- **Comparison**: A chart compares the number of articles about the company in the last seven days with the average number of articles in a typical week.
- **Media coverage**: MarketBeat tracks news from more than one hundred trusted media outlets in real time (most recent ICF International headline dated 10/2/2026).
