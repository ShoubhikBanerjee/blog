# Shivon Zilis Announces Breakup with Elon Musk Amid AI Portfolio Testimony

Shivon Zilis used X to announce that she and Elon Musk, the father of her four children, have broken up. The post was a quote‑tweet of a *Big Tech Alert* notice that Musk had stopped following her, and she added her own reflection on the sudden change.

## 🔍 Overview
- Zilis posted the breakup on X, quoting *Big Tech Alert* that Elon Musk no longer follows her.
- She wrote, "It's hard to go from in love to let go in a week with no warning, but that's just how it is sometimes."
- The announcement comes after a high‑profile trial in which Zilis testified about her work across Musk’s AI interests.

## 🤖 Role in Musk’s AI Portfolio
- Zilis told the court she worked **across Musk’s entire AI portfolio: Tesla, Neuralink, and OpenAI**.
- She described her workload as **80‑to‑100‑hour weeks**, saying she was there "to go find bottlenecks and solve them."
- She served on **OpenAI’s board** while giving birth to twins, keeping the parentage of the children secret at the time.
- Zilis only informed coworkers that the children’s father was a competitor **in 2022**, after Business Insider reported court documents naming Musk.

## ⚖️ Legal Context
- During the trial, Zilis **denied under oath** that she was Musk’s chief of staff.
- Musk **testified** that she was his chief of staff, a remark that drew loud laughter from the courtroom gallery.
- The lawsuit involved Musk suing Sam Altman over Musk’s donations to OpenAI, with Zilis’s testimony focused on her AI‑related work for Musk.

## ❤️ Personal Statements
- Zilis praised Musk in her breakup post: "I feel happy that I got to show Elon how it feels to be deeply understood, nurtured, and peacefully loved with stability and gentleness."
- She added, "Given the tumultuous nature of his early life … I wanted him to feel the deepest and most stable form of love this world can provide."
- She concluded, "I've loved him more than life itself and he's taught me 100 lifetimes of knowledge… we created four beautiful little loves of my life."
- Recently, Zilis recommended **Maye Musk’s memoir**, calling the mother’s resilience a “beacon of resilience to draw inspiration from.”

## 📚 Related Personal Dynamics
- Elon Musk is known to have **14 children**.
- Another mother of his children, **Ashley St. Clair**, rejected a **$40 million NDA** and is suing Musk over his chatbot **Grok**, which allegedly created deep‑fake nudes of her.
- Former partner **Grimes** described pregnancy as "sacrificing your body and your freedom" and sued Musk to establish a parental relationship, even posting (and later deleting) a tweet asking Shivon to unblock her and demanding a response from Musk’s biographer.
