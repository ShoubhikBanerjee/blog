# Apple to Tighten Mac Full Disk Access Controls Amid AI Agent Risks

Apple has announced plans to tighten the way apps are granted Full Disk Access on macOS, citing growing risks as AI agents become more capable and autonomous. The company warned that this broad permission can expose highly sensitive data and bypasses the privacy controls normally used to protect user data.

## 🔍 Overview

Full Disk Access is one of the broadest permissions available in macOS. While it is intended for software like backup tools, it allows apps to reach data that is otherwise protected behind separate privacy controls. Apple reports that some developers are currently using this permission in ways that could put users at risk by exposing several types of data:

*   Personal files
*   Email messages
*   Messages
*   Browsing history
*   Sensitive data across large parts of a system

## ⚙️ Key Details

Apple directly linked the upcoming changes to the rise of agentic AI software. Unlike traditional apps that might need access for a single function, AI agents can carry out tasks across multiple apps, files, and services. 

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Permission Level | One of the broadest in macOS; bypasses standard privacy controls. |
| Original Intent | Designed for utilities such as backup software. |
| Current Concern | AI agents may expose significantly more personal info than traditional apps. |
| Privacy Stance | Positioned as a central part of Apple's on-device and cloud-based AI processing. |

## 🧩 How it Works

The company intends to introduce additional controls that require more explicit user action before an app can receive Full Disk Access. The goal of these protections is to ensure users clearly understand the risks involved and make a deliberate, informed decision before granting such broad access to their data.

## 🚀 Availability

Apple has not announced exactly what the new Full Disk Access controls will look like or when they will arrive. The company stated only that the additional protections will be introduced going forward as it continues to expand artificial intelligence across the Mac through Apple Intelligence and Siri AI.
