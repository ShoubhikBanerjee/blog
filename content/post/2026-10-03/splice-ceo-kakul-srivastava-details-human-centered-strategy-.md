# Splice CEO Kakul Srivastava Details Human-Centered Strategy for Generative AI Music

Splice CEO Kakul Srivastava is carefully navigating the integration of generative AI into the music production platform by focusing on tools that keep human creators at the center of the process.

## 🔍 Overview
Splice is a sample platform that countless producers rely on for one-shots and melodic loops. Audio samples pulled from the service have been used in major hit songs, including Lisa’s “Money” and “Espresso” by Sabrina Carpenter. Under the leadership of CEO Kakul Srivastava, the company has expanded its footprint and overseen new developments in generative AI while maintaining a strict focus on human-centric technology.

## ⚙️ Key details
* **Human-Centered AI:** Splice is focusing on AI products that keep human creators at the center of the creative process. On an episode of Decoder, Srivastava spoke out against "push-button, get-song" generative AI models like those popularized by Suno.
* **Strategic Acquisitions:** Splice has expanded its presence under Srivastava's leadership, which includes acquiring Spitfire Audio and directing the company's initiatives in generative AI.
* **Executive History:** Before joining Splice, Srivastava served in executive roles at Flickr, Yahoo, GitHub, and Adobe. She was also on the early team at Tiny Speck, the company that later became Slack.
* **Remote Work Culture:** Splice operates as a remote-first organization, meaning the company does not have a traditional physical hallway.
* **Views on Communication:** Srivastava has noted that AI emails are "killing conversations," stating a preference for a well-written document and a real conversation instead.

## 💡 Why it matters
As AI music tools become more controversial, Splice is deliberately avoiding fully automated music generation. Instead, the company is threading the line by developing tools that support and collaborate with human producers rather than replacing them.
