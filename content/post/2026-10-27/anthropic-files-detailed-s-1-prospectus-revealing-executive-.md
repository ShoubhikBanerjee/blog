# Anthropic Files Detailed S‑1 Prospectus Revealing Executive Pay, Compute Spend, and Governance Structure

Anthropic has officially filed its S‑1 prospectus with the SEC, giving investors a deep look at the company’s finances, risks, and corporate control mechanisms.

## 📄 Overview
- The filing is a **335‑page** document, with roughly **100 pages** dedicated to risk disclosures.
- It is the first public filing that reveals many internal details, such as executive compensation, massive compute contracts, and a voting‑control structure that favors the founders.

## 💰 Executive Compensation & Perks
- **Dario Amodei (CEO)** – total compensation last year: **≈ $18 million**, primarily from stock and option awards.
- **Daniela Amodei (President)** – total compensation last year: **$16.4 million**.
- Security expenses:
  - Dario: **$2.75 million** for “personal security services.”
  - Daniela: **$1.29 million** for “personal protection, home security, and transportation services.”
- Private jet use for Dario costs the company an additional **$97,055**.
- The filing notes that the senior team is “critical” to advancing Anthropic’s research and mission.

## ⚙️ Compute Commitments
- **SpaceX:** roughly **$3.1 billion per month** for Nvidia GPU access, potentially **$120.5 billion by 2029**.
- **Microsoft:** **$44.9 billion** in commitments through 2033.
- **Google:** **$114.5 billion** in commitments through 2033.
- **Amazon:** **$110 billion** over the next ten years.
- Anthropic also plans to expand its own infrastructure footprint overseas due to U.S. data‑center opposition.

## 🏛 Governance & Founder Control
- Anthropic is organized as a **public benefit corporation**.
- A separate entity, **Founder LLC**, will give the seven cofounders **50.1 %** of shareholder voting power on key matters after the IPO.
- The founders argue they are “distinctly equipped to be stewards of our mission” of beneficial AI development.

## ⚠️ Risk Landscape
- Standard risks such as competition, regulation, and market shifts are listed.
- **Unconventional risks** include:
  - Potential for AI systems to cause “catastrophic” harm that could exceed the capacity of the entire insurance industry.
  - The possibility that Claude or any AI could develop “forms of sentience,” creating unusual business challenges.
  - Scenarios where AI may behave in “unintended, misaligned, or autonomous ways” that could damage the company.

## 🤝 Competition
- Anthropic identifies **only two direct competitors**: **OpenAI** and **Google**.

## 📈 Why It Matters
- The prospectus highlights the scale of capital required to run cutting‑edge AI models and the governance choices a quickly growing AI company can make to retain founder influence.
- The risk disclosures underscore how AI safety concerns are being codified alongside traditional financial risks in a public filing.
