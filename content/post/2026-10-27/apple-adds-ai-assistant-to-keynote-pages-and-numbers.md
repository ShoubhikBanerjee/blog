# Apple adds AI assistant to Keynote, Pages, and Numbers

Apple is rolling out an AI assistant across its iWork productivity suite—Keynote, Pages, and Numbers. The assistant, built on models from Apple and OpenAI, can understand a document’s context and help with design, writing, and data tasks.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI assistant integrated into Keynote, Pages, and Numbers.
- Performs design, animation, and slide refinement in Keynote.
- Drafts, restructures, and tweaks written content in Pages.
- Calculates, visualizes, organizes data, suggests chart types, and builds custom tables in Numbers.

## 🧩 How it works
- Powered by AI models from Apple and OpenAI.
- Uses the Model Context Protocol on macOS 27 or later, which enables third‑party AI agents to plug into the apps.
- Understands the context of the open document to generate relevant suggestions.

## ⚙️ Key details
| App | Capabilities |
|-----|--------------|
| Keynote | Design, animate, and refine slides |
| Pages | Draft, restructure, and tweak written content |
| Numbers | Calculate, visualize, organize data; suggest chart types; build custom tables |

- The assistant is available in beta for devices that support Apple Intelligence.
- Requires iOS 27, iPadOS 27, or macOS 27 and later.

## 🚀 Availability
- Rolling out in beta to compatible devices.
- Works on iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27 or later.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Brings AI‑driven assistance directly into Apple’s core productivity tools.
- The Model Context Protocol opens the door for third‑party AI agents to extend functionality.
- Aligns with a broader set of AI enhancements across Apple’s creative software lineup.
