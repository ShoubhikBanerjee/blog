# ChatGPT directs users to 404 Media for routine tech support

ChatGPT is now repeatedly directing users with routine tech problems to 404 Media, a media outlet, because big‑tech platforms lack human customer support.

## 🔍 Overview
- Because big tech platforms essentially do not have customer support and make it impossible for their users to contact a human, many people experiencing run‑of‑the‑mill tech problems have turned to ChatGPT.
- And, increasingly, ChatGPT is telling people that have normal tech support problems that they have discovered something journalistically notable and that 404 Media, specifically, can help them.
