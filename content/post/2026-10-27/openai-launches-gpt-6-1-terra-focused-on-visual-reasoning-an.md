# OpenAI launches GPT‑6.1 Terra focused on visual reasoning and computer use

On 26 October 2026 OpenAI announced GPT‑6.1 Terra, a fast, efficient model that combines detailed visual reasoning and precise computer use with clearer, more natural writing.

## 🔍 Overview
- Positioned between the cheapest and most expensive offerings, similar to Anthropic’s Sonnet model.
- Marketed as a step up from GPT‑5.6 Terra.
- Targets work‑day tasks such as drafting proposals, interpreting technical drawings, filling out forms, and editing videos.

## 🧩 Capabilities
- **Visual reasoning** – detailed analysis of images and technical drawings.
- **Computer use** – automation of repetitive, computer‑based tasks.
- **Technical communication** – clearer, more natural writing for proposals and documentation.

## ⚙️ Key Details
- **Pricing**: $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens.
- This pricing is **almost half** that of GPT‑5.6 Terra and GPT‑6.1 Sol, and about **10 times** the price of GPT‑6 Luna.
- **Model positioning** (per OpenAI’s Ted Sanders on Hacker News):
  - Better than **Astra** and **Sol** at technical communication.
  - Similar vision and computer‑use ability to **Astra** and **Sol**, but *much faster*.
  - Slightly weaker than those models on most other tasks.
- **Cost‑performance trade‑off**:
  - For API developers, Terra is a mid‑range option: cheaper than Sol yet smarter than Luna, especially for vision, computer use, and communication.
  - For subscribers, Terra can extend usage limits and provide clearer communication and faster computer use without being a “bombshell” release.

## 🚀 Availability
- Offered through OpenAI’s API for developers.
- Available to ChatGPT Plus subscribers as a selectable model option.
- Intended for tasks that benefit from a balance of cost and capability, such as automated form filling or video editing workflows.

## 📊 Comparison with Other Frontier Models
| Model | Relative Cost (vs Terra) | Highlighted Strengths (vs Terra) |
|-------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| GPT‑6.1 Terra | Baseline ( $1 /M in, $5 /M out ) | Fast visual reasoning, precise computer use, clear writing |
| GPT‑6.1 Sol | Roughly **2×** Terra’s price | Comparable vision/computer use, slower |
| GPT‑6 Astra | Similar price tier | Terra better at technical communication |
| GPT‑6 Luna | **≈ 10×** cheaper than Terra | Terra smarter for vision, computer use, communication |

*Cost and performance remarks reflect the statements made by OpenAI and community comments on the announcement.*
