# Mozilla adds AI form‑filling and webpage‑watching to Firefox Smart Window

Mozilla is updating Smart Window — Firefox’s AI‑powered browsing experience — with a new feature that can help you fill out online forms based on your open tabs and watch webpages for changes.

## 🔍 Overview
- AI form filling pulls information from the tabs you have open.
- Webpage monitoring lets you specify pages to watch for sales, job listings, or other updates.

## 🧩 How it works
- Click into a form that contains at least four text fields.
- An option **“Fill from sources”** appears, showing all open tabs Firefox can draw from.
- Select or deselect tabs; the browser generates text suggestions based on the content of the chosen pages.
- The suggestions are generated by Google’s **Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite** model, with data from your source pages shared with that model.
- To set up monitoring, type **`/watch`** in the Smart Window chat, describe what you’re looking for, and add the webpages you want to watch.
- Firefox checks the listed pages daily or weekly, but only while the browser is open.
- When a change is detected, you receive a notification via the Tasks button on the toolbar or a desktop notification (if enabled).
- Watching pauses after 60 days of no change and stops completely after 90 days.

## ⚙️ Key details
- Form‑filling currently supports only text fields; support for dropdown menus and PDFs is under development.
- Monitoring frequency can be set to daily or weekly.
- These features are rolling out to the Smart Window beta on desktop in the United States and Canada, with availability in other countries expected in the coming months.

## 💡 Why it matters
- Reduces manual data entry by reusing information already open in the browser.
- Helps users stay informed of updates (sales, job postings, etc.) without constantly revisiting sites.
